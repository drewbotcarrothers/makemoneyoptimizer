# Make Money Optimizer — Monetization Plan

**Brand:** Canadian side-hustle media (makemoneyoptimizer.com)  
**Streams:** (1) free content + ads · (2) affiliates · (3) digital ebooks  
**Principle:** Trust first. No fake earnings. Disclose commercial relationships. Educate before you sell.

This is an operating plan, not tax, legal, or investment advice. Confirm GST/HST, CASL, and advertising rules for your situation (CRA / provincial rules may apply).

---

## North-star model

| Stream | When it pays | Role |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Affiliates** | Can earn from first real traffic | Early cash + product recommendations inside guides |
| **Ads** | Scales with sessions | Passive layer on free content once inventory is worth it |
| **Ebooks** | Needs trust + email | Highest-margin owned product; compounds with list |

**Recommended sequence**

1. **Now (0–3 months):** Affiliate stack + disclosure hygiene + email capture  
2. **Next (as traffic grows):** AdSense (or Journey) with sparse placements  
3. **Product (1–2 months parallel):** One focused ebook/lead magnet → paid playbook  
4. **Later:** Premium ad network upgrade; ebook ladder / bundle

Do not wait for “perfect traffic” before affiliates and an email list — those are the retention and conversion engine ads alone won’t give you.

## 1. Affiliates

### Goals
- Every major side-hustle guide has **0–3** relevant, disclosed offers (tools, platforms, bank/fintech, marketplaces).
- Never invent results; prefer “we recommend X because…” with honest trade-offs.
- Track links (UTM + network dashboards).

### Disclosure (non-negotiable)
- Keep `/affiliate-disclosure` accurate and linked in footer (already on site).
- Label affiliate links in-copy (“may earn a commission”).
- Update Privacy/Terms before collecting emails or using tracking cookies beyond essentials.

### Priority programs (Canada-relevant)

| Tier | Program | Fit for MMO | Notes |
|------|---------|-------------|--------|
| **A — apply early** | [Shopify Affiliates](https://www.shopify.com/ca/affiliates) | Ecommerce / online store hustles | Up to ~US$150/referral (CA merchants in high-payout region); Impact; needs live site + relevant content |
| **A** | [Amazon Associates (Canada)](https://affiliate-program.amazon.ca/) | Gear, books, tools for hustles | Easy product inserts; lower commission; high intent on “what you need” lists |
| **A** | [KOHO Affiliates](https://www.koho.ca/affiliate/) | Banking / cash-flow for freelancers | CA fintech; no minimum payout marketed |
| **B — finance stack** | [Qtrade Affiliates](https://qtrade.ca/en/investor/about/why-qtrade/affiliateprogram.html) (via Fintel Connect etc.) | Investing side of “make money” | Often CPA-style funded accounts; audience must match |
| **B** | [Ratehub partners](https://www.ratehub.ca/affiliate-program) | Mortgages / cards / calculators later | Widgets; stronger when you cover personal finance adjacent to hustles |
| **B** | Software / creator tools | Canva, Notion, hosting, email tools, freelancing platforms | Prefer Impact, PartnerStack, or official creator programs with CA payouts |
| **C — careful** | Lending / high-risk credit offers | Only if content is clearly educational and compliant | Strict disclosure; avoid anything that looks predatory |

**Content → offer map (examples)**

| Content cluster | Natural affiliates |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Freelance writing / VA | Invoicing, accounting (Wave/Xero-class), portfolio hosts, job boards |
| Reselling | Shipping supplies (Amazon.ca), marketplace sellers’ tools, label printers |
| Gig / delivery | Tax apps, vehicle maintenance, phone mounts — not “get rich driving” |
| Tutoring / online teaching | Scheduling, video tools, payment processors |
| “Start an online store” | Shopify + payment + email marketing stack |

### Affiliate operating rules
1. Max **one primary** and **one secondary** offer per article unless it’s a roundup.
2. Editorial first: sections “Who it fits / costs / risks” stay non-sponsored.
3. Quarterly review: kill underperforming or trust-damaging offers.
4. Maintain a private sheet: program, cookie window, payout, status, articles using it.

### 30-day affiliate checklist
- [ ] Site live on custom domain (helps approvals)
- [ ] Apply Shopify + Amazon.ca + KOHO (or equivalent fintech)
- [ ] Expand disclosure page with list of partner types (not every URL required)
- [ ] Add affiliate fields to article frontmatter later (`affiliateTags`, `primaryOffer`) if useful
- [ ] Ship 2–3 “stack” posts designed for conversions (e.g. “Freelance toolkit for Canadians”)

## 2. Ads (free content + display)

### Philosophy
- Fewer units, better UX → better retention and better chance at premium networks later.
- Site already has `AdSlot` placeholders — wire one network at a time; don’t stack competitors.

### Ladder

| Stage | Approx. traffic / signal | Network | Action |
|-------|---------------------------|---------|--------|
| **0** | Pre-launch / low traffic | None | Keep placeholders empty; protect CWV |
| **1** | Any approved site | **Google AdSense** | Apply once you have original, policy-safe pages live |
| **1b** | ~1,000+ sessions/mo | **Journey by Mediavine** (on-ramp) | Stronger than AdSense for many publishers; check current reqs |
| **2** | Stronger revenue (~US$5k+/yr ad revenue for full Mediavine as of recent guidance) or Raptive-level traffic | **Mediavine** / **Raptive** | Upgrade when eligible; verify live requirements before applying |

> Thresholds change. Re-check Mediavine Journey, Mediavine, and Raptive application pages before applying. Older “50k sessions / 100k pageviews” lore is not always current.

### Placement rules (site already oriented this way)
- Home: below hero only  
- Hubs: one unit  
- Articles: top **or** mid — prefer mid after intro; avoid stacking above the fold  
- Never interrupt the first screen of an article with multiple ads  
- No fake “continue reading” walls  

### Compliance before ads go live
- [ ] Privacy Policy updated (cookies, ad partners, US/CA users)
- [ ] Cookie consent approach decided (especially if EU traffic appears)
- [ ] AdSense / network policy review (no prohibited claims in content)
- [ ] GA4 (or equivalent) installed for session/pageview proof

### Ads + affiliates coexistence
- Ads monetize attention; affiliates monetize **intent**. Prefer affiliates in comparison/tool sections; ads in residual inventory.
- If a premium network restricts certain affiliate categories, follow their rules.

## 3. Digital ebooks / products

### Product ladder (start narrow)

| Product | Price band (CAD, guide) | Purpose |
|---------|-------------------------|---------|
| **Free lead magnet** | $0 | Email growth — e.g. “Side Hustle Starter Checklist (Canada)” PDF |
| **Ebook 1 — flagship** | $19–$39 | Depth: pick one cluster (e.g. Freelance Starter for Canadians, or Reselling Playbook) |
| **Ebook 2 / pack** | $29–$79 | Expansion or bundle once Ebook 1 sells |
| **Templates** | $9–$19 | Invoices, pitch emails, tracking sheets — high margin, fast to ship |

**v1 recommendation:** Free checklist → paid **“Canadian Side Hustle Starter Playbook”** (one PDF, 25–40 pages, practical worksheets). Tie CTAs from articles + newsletter.

### Checkout platform (Canada-friendly)

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict for MMO |
|--------|------|------|------------------|
| **Gumroad** | Fast, creator-native, MoR tax handling (verify current coverage) | Higher fees | **Good default to launch** |
| **Lemon Squeezy / Stripe MoR path** | Strong MoR story, polished checkout | Platform in transition; confirm CA payouts | Strong alternative |
| **Stripe Payment Links** on-site | Lowest fees | **You** handle GST/HST registration & remittance | Better once volume + bookkeeping are ready |

**Recommendation:** Launch ebook #1 on **Gumroad** (or Lemon Squeezy if you prefer that MoR), embed/buy buttons on `/guides` or a future `/resources` page. Revisit Stripe-native checkout when sales justify tax ops.

### Email (required for ebook economics)
- Wire the existing `Newsletter` stub to **Buttondown**, **ConvertKit/Kit**, or **Beehiiv**.
- CASL: express consent, clear sender, unsubscribe.
- Sequence: welcome → 3–5 free tips → soft pitch to playbook.

### Ebook content rules
- Same trust bar as articles: no guaranteed income  
- Include Canadian notes (GST/HST on self-employment at a high level, record-keeping — with “talk to a professional” framing)  
- Upsell from free articles via related CTAs, not pop-up spam

## 4. 90-day roadmap

### Days 1–14 — Foundation
- Domain live + HTTPS  
- GA4 + Search Console  
- Apply: Shopify, Amazon.ca, KOHO (or chosen fintech)  
- Finalize Privacy + Affiliate Disclosure for real partners  
- Choose email provider; wire newsletter stub  
- Outline Ebook 1 + free checklist  

### Days 15–45 — Earn early
- Publish 4–6 SEO articles with natural affiliate inserts  
- Ship free checklist → grow list  
- Soft-launch Ebook 1 to email list  
- Apply AdSense when content volume + originality look solid  

### Days 46–90 — Compound
- Double down on top 2 traffic/affiliate articles  
- Add 1 “commercial” roundup per month (tool stacks)  
- Review ad RPM vs UX; prepare Journey/Mediavine packet if sessions support it  
- Iterate ebook from buyer feedback; add template upsell

## 7. What not to do

- Guarantee income or show fabricated screenshots of earnings  
- Stuff every paragraph with affiliate links  
- Enable heavy ad stacks before content quality and speed are solid  
- Sell an ebook before you have 3–5 strong free articles that prove the angle  
- Ignore CASL / disclosure because “everyone else does”
