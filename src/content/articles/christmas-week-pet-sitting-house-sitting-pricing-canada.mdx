Christmas week is the busiest stretch of the year for Canadian pet sitters and house sitters, so the money is made in pricing and calendar control, not in finding clients. Set a holiday rate in November, publish the dates it covers, take a deposit to hold each night, and stop accepting overnights once the calendar is full. On Rover, the Canadian holiday period for Christmas, Boxing Day and New Year's Day is December 24, 2026 to January 3, 2027, and Rover says sitters generally price it 10% to 20% above their standard rate. Independent sitters in Calgary, Vancouver and the Toronto area publish flat holiday surcharges of $10 to $25 a night instead.

This guide covers holiday-week pricing and booking only. ## When is the Christmas holiday period for pet sitters in 2026?

On Rover Canada, the Christmas, Boxing Day and New Year's Day holiday period is December 24, 2026 to January 3, 2027; for 2027 it is December 23, 2027 to January 2, 2028 ([Rover holiday rates](https://support-ca.rover.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360037020572-What-are-holiday-rates-and-how-do-I-manage-them)). Independent businesses pick their own windows, and they vary a lot: Woofur's runs December 17 to January 4, PAWS Pro covers December through the first week of January, and Infinite Pets charges only on four named days.

## Which holiday pricing model should you use?

There are three published models. Pick one and apply it the same way to every client.

| Model | How it works | Published example | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whole-booking holiday rate | Every night of a booking that touches the window is charged at the holiday rate | Rover: holiday rate applies to the first dog for the entire booking | Sitters who want long bookings to pay fully |
| Flat surcharge on named days | A fixed amount added only on listed dates | Infinite Pets +$25/day on Dec 24, 25, 31, Jan 1; Pets Inc +$10/night | Simple quotes, repeat clients |
| Flat surcharge across a season | Fixed amount added every night in a long window | Woofur +$10/night/dog, Dec 17 to Jan 4; PAWS Pro +$10/night/dog | Sitters booked solid for weeks |
| No surcharge, shorter visits | Normal price, but shorter holiday-day visits | Mad Catter: 15-minute visits Dec 24, 25, Jan 1 morning | Drop-in sitters protecting their own holiday time |

On Rover, if you leave the holiday rate blank, Rover uses your normal service rate. Rover also lets you set a custom price for one client by editing the booking request, and says you can apply holiday rates to other demand spikes it doesn't list.

## How do you calculate a Christmas week pet sitting quote?

Price the standard night first, apply your holiday model, then add pets, extra visits and tax.

```text
Holiday night (percentage model) = standard night × (1 + holiday %)
Holiday night (flat model)       = standard night + surcharge on surcharge dates only
Booking total = Σ nightly prices + extra pets × nights + add-on visits
Deposit       = deposit % × booking total
Client pays   = booking total × (1 + GST/HST rate)   (only if you're registered)
Your take on Rover = booking total × 0.80   (Rover keeps a 20% service fee)
```

**Set the standard night from the market, not from an hourly wage.** For an in-home overnight, Rover's Toronto range is $40 to $70; independent sitters who include a midday visit or medications charge $95 to $120 (Artemis standard and premium, Infinite Pets). Decide what your night includes, match it to the closest published package, and price near it.

Rover's fee is 20% of each booking, so you keep 80% ([Rover service fees](https://support-ca.rover.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360037446911-What-are-the-service-fees)). A direct client paying the same price leaves you 25% more money than a Rover booking ($1.00 ÷ $0.80 = 1.25).

## What do worked Christmas week quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** The standard rates and bookings below are assumptions chosen inside the published ranges above. Holiday rules, deposit terms and tax rates are the sourced ones. Tax lines apply only if you're registered for GST/HST.

### Quote 1: two dogs, 4-night house sit, Toronto, percentage model

- Booking: December 23 to 27 (nights of Dec 23, 24, 25, 26). It touches Rover's window, so all 4 nights take the holiday rate.
- Standard night $65 (between Rover's $60.43 Toronto average and its $70 top). Holiday rate at 20%: $65 × 1.20 = **$78**.
- First dog: 4 × $78 = $312. Second dog at an assumed $20 a night: 4 × $20 = $80. Total **$392**.
- 25% deposit (Artemis's published rate): $392 × 0.25 = **$98**.
- With 13% HST: $392 × 1.13 = $442.96.
- Same booking on Rover with no HST collected by you: $392 × 0.80 = $313.60 to you.

### Quote 2: two cats, 11-night live-in, Calgary, flat surcharge on named days

- Booking: nights of December 22 through January 1, which is 11 nights.
- Standard night $75 (between Artemis's $70 basic and Infinite Pets' $80 cat rate). 11 × $75 = $825.
- Surcharge of $25 on the four named days Infinite Pets uses (Dec 24, 25, 31, Jan 1): 4 × $25 = $100.
- Total **$925**; with 5% GST, $925 × 1.05 = $971.25.
- Comparison: a 20% whole-booking rate would be 11 × $90 = $990, so the named-day model is $65 cheaper for the client and easier to explain.

### Quote 3: one cat, twice-daily drop-ins, Vancouver, per-visit surcharge

- Booking: December 24 to 28, 2 visits a day = 10 visits at $30 (Mad Catter's and Pets Inc's 30-minute starting price): $300.
- Holiday surcharge of $5 per visit on Dec 24, 25 and 26, as Pets Inc and Catcouver charge: 6 visits × $5 = $30.
- Total **$330**; with 5% GST, $330 × 1.05 = $346.50.
- 50% non-refundable deposit (Catcouver and Pets Inc): $330 × 0.50 = **$165**.

### Quote 4: one dog, 6-night house sit that straddles New Year's, Ottawa

- Booking: December 30 to January 5, 6 nights, the same dates as Rover's own example. Under Rover's rule, all 6 nights take the holiday rate even though January 4 and 5 fall outside the window.
- Standard night $75 (below Dreamline Canine's $85). Holiday rate at 15%: $75 × 1.15 = **$86.25**.
- Total: 6 × $86.25 = **$517.50**. With 13% HST: $517.50 × 1.13 = $584.78.
- On Rover: $517.50 × 0.80 = $414 to you.

### What one Christmas week can earn

**Illustrative.** One sitter takes overnights only, one home at a time. From December 23 to January 3 that's 11 nights; at the $78 holiday night from Quote 1 that's 11 × $78 = **$858**. Adding two nearby cat clients at 2 visits a day for 5 days at $35 a visit ($30 + $5 surcharge) adds 2 × 10 × $35 = $700, for **$1,558** before tax and expenses. Drop-ins only work if they sit within a short drive of the house you're sleeping at.

## How do you take holiday bookings without overbooking?

Treat each night as one slot, fill it in order of priority, and hold it only when money arrives.

1. **Block your own plans first.** Mark the nights you won't work.
2. **Set a cap.** One overnight house sit at a time; drop-ins only near that home.
3. **Offer dates to repeat clients first,** then open remaining nights to new clients.
4. **Hold dates with a deposit.** Artemis takes a 25% non-refundable deposit and charges the full balance for cancellations inside 14 days. Catcouver and Pets Inc take 50% non-refundable deposits, and Catcouver requires full payment for winter holiday bookings (December 21 to January 3) made between November 24 and December 31.
5. **Charge for late bookings.** PAWS Pro adds $10 a night when a request comes in within 7 days of drop-off.
6. **Do meet-and-greets before December 20** so the week is for sitting, not intros. Rover's Toronto data shows clients lock in house sitting about 15 days ahead on average, and earlier for popular holidays.
7. **Plan for storms.** Agree in advance what happens if roads close and who your local backup is.

## How do you raise your holiday rates next year?

Holiday rates are the easiest prices to raise because demand is already there.

- **If your window filled by mid-November,** move from the bottom of Rover's 10% to 20% range to the top, or from a $10 to a $20 flat surcharge (the gap between Pets Inc and Artemis).
- **Widen the window** if you were turning people away on the shoulder dates; Woofur's window starts December 17.
- **Switch to a whole-booking rule** if long bookings that touch one holiday night are filling your calendar.
- **Add a late-booking charge** like PAWS Pro's $10 a night within 7 days.
- **Move to full payment** for late holiday bookings, as Catcouver does after November 23.
- **Announce it with the November opening message,** not after requests arrive. For the scripts, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What holiday-week hazards should sitters watch for?

Holiday homes add hazards that aren't there the rest of the year. Health Canada advises keeping breakable ornaments with small parts away from young children and pets, keeping burning candles out of reach of pets and away from flammable materials, and placing trees away from heat sources ([holiday safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/home-safety/tips-holiday-safety.html)). Ask owners to unplug decorative lights while away or show you how they want them handled.

## What mistakes do sitters make over Christmas week?

- **Setting holiday rates after requests arrive.** Set them before you open the calendar in November.
- **Double-booking overnight sits.** You can't sleep at two homes.
- **No deposit.** A late cancellation leaves a night you can't refill in December.
- **Vague dates.** Say exactly which nights the holiday rate covers, and whether it applies to the whole booking.
- **Pricing a medication-heavy sit like a basic one.** Artemis charges $70 for basic and $120 for premium with medications and a midday visit.
- **No local backup.** Have a vet and an in-town emergency contact for every sit.

## Which tax rules apply to holiday pet sitting income in Canada?

Sitting income is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both shares of CPP contributions, 11.9% on net self-employment earnings above the $3,500 basic exemption in 2026 ([CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html)). GST/HST registration is required once taxable sales from all your businesses pass $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters ([when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). Once registered, charge the rate for the client's province: 5% GST in Alberta and B.C., 13% HST in Ontario ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)). All the business prices in this guide are before tax. See [RRSP-season money tips](/side-hustles/rrsp-season-side-hustle-money-tips-canada/) for what to do with holiday earnings.
