Decide how many overnight sits and drop-ins you can physically cover between December 24, 2026 and January 3, 2027, set a holiday rate before requests arrive, and take a deposit to hold dates. On Rover, the Canadian holiday period for Christmas, Boxing Day, and New Year's Day is December 24 to January 3, and a booking that overlaps it even partly uses your holiday rate for the first dog for the whole booking. ## When is the Christmas holiday period for pet sitters in 2026?

On Rover Canada, the Christmas, Boxing Day, and New Year's Day holiday period is December 24, 2026 to January 3, 2027; for 2027 it is December 23, 2027 to January 2, 2028 ([Rover holiday rates](https://support-ca.rover.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360037020572-What-are-holiday-rates-and-how-do-I-manage-them)). If you work with direct clients, you can define your own holiday dates, but using the same window keeps quotes consistent.

| Date (2026–27) | Day | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thursday, December 24 | Christmas Eve | Start of Rover's holiday period |
| Friday, December 25 | Christmas Day | Inside the period |
| Saturday, December 26 | Boxing Day | Inside the period |
| Friday, January 1 | New Year's Day | Inside the period |
| Sunday, January 3 | Last day | End of Rover's holiday period |

## How do holiday rates work on Rover?

Rover lets you set a separate holiday rate. If a booking includes any date in a holiday period, the holiday rate applies to the first dog in the booking for the entire booking. Rover's example is a December 30 to January 5 house sit for two dogs, which partly overlaps the holiday period, so the holiday rate applies to the first dog for all six nights.

- **Leaving it blank:** if you do not set a holiday rate, Rover uses your normal service rate.
- **Custom prices:** you can set a custom price for a specific client by editing the booking request.
- **Other holidays:** Rover says you may also apply holiday rates when you see demand spikes it does not list.

## How should you price Christmas week as an independent sitter?

Set a clear holiday rate, publish the dates it covers, and apply it to every night of a booking that touches those dates, as Rover does. The amounts below are illustrative only, not market rates.

### Illustrative example

Assume a standard overnight house-sitting rate of $60 a night and a 20% holiday premium, the top of Rover's 10% to 20% range. Your standard rate and the booking are assumptions.

- **Holiday nightly rate:** $60 × 1.20 = $72.
- **A December 30 to January 5 sit (6 nights) that overlaps the holiday period:** 6 × $72 = $432, versus 6 × $60 = $360 at standard rates, a $72 difference.
- **A 25% deposit to hold the dates:** 25% × $432 = $108.

For direct clients, put the holiday dates, the nightly rate, the deposit, and your cancellation terms in writing.

## How do you take bookings without overbooking Christmas week?

Treat each night as a single slot for overnight sits, and only add drop-ins you can reach within your travel time.

1. **Block your own plans first.** Mark the nights you will not work.
2. **Set a cap.** One overnight house sit at a time; drop-ins only near that home.
3. **Offer dates to repeat clients first,** then open remaining nights.
4. **Hold dates with a deposit,** and confirm with a written plan: keys, feeding, medications, vet contact, and an emergency contact who is in town.
5. **Do meet-and-greets before December 20** so the week is for sitting, not intros.
6. **Plan for storms.** Agree in advance what happens if roads close.

## What holiday-week hazards should sitters watch for?

Holiday homes add hazards that are not there the rest of the year. Health Canada advises keeping breakable ornaments with small parts away from young children and pets, keeping burning candles out of reach of pets and away from flammable materials, and placing trees away from heat sources ([holiday safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/home-safety/tips-holiday-safety.html)). Ask owners to unplug decorative lights while away or show you how they want them handled.

## What mistakes do sitters make over Christmas week?

The common mistakes are pricing too late and accepting overlapping overnights.

- **Setting holiday rates after requests arrive.** Set them in November.
- **Double-booking overnight sits.** You cannot sleep at two homes.
- **No deposit.** Late cancellations leave nights unfilled.
- **Vague dates.** Say which dates the holiday rate covers.
- **No local backup.** Have a vet and an emergency contact for every sit.

## Which tax rules apply to holiday pet sitting income in Canada?

Sitting income is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both shares of CPP contributions, 11.9% on net self-employment earnings above the $3,500 basic exemption in 2026 ([CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html)). GST/HST registration is generally required once taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters ([when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)).
