<QuickAnswer>

- **What pros charge:** Rinkbuild, a Toronto rink company, lists $1,800 to $2,500 for a standard 24×30 or 24×48 installed rink, and weekly maintenance at about $100 (20×30) to $200 (40×60) per visit ([Rinkbuild](https://rinkbuild.com/backyard-ice-rink-cost-toronto/)).
- **What materials cost:** Rinkbuild puts DIY materials at roughly $900 to $1,500; RinkMaster lists tube kits from $339.99 to $639.99 ([RinkMaster](https://www.rinkmaster.com/pages/backyard-ice-rink-cost)).
- **Water belongs to the homeowner:** on Guelph's 2026 rates, RinkMaster estimates about $37 to fill a 20×40 rink and about $35 for a season of floods. The client's tap, the client's bill.
- **The realistic side hustle:** setup, snow clearing, and evening floods for neighbours, not full builds, until you have done your own rink for a season.

</QuickAnswer>

A backyard rink is a short, weather-bound season: frames go in during October and November, ice usually forms by mid-December in the GTA, and skating runs to late February or early March, according to [Rinkbuild](https://rinkbuild.com/backyard-ice-rink-cost-toronto/). That makes rink care a natural add-on for anyone already doing snow removal. Prices below are as published on the companies' pages, read October 15, 2026.

## What does the homeowner pay for materials and water?

RinkMaster's [cost page](https://www.rinkmaster.com/pages/backyard-ice-rink-cost) lists tube kits with liner and frame from $339.99 (15×20) to $639.99 (25×40), patch tape from $14.99, and a FloodMaster resurfacer at $329.99. On a lumber build, a 20×40 rink has 120 feet of perimeter, about ten 12-foot 2×10s before stakes. For water, RinkMaster uses Guelph's 2026 water and wastewater charges ($2.63 and $2.33 per cubic metre) to estimate 7,550 litres and about $37 for a first fill at 4 inches, and about 240 litres, a little over $1, per thin flood.

The homeowner buys the kit and pays the water. Your service is the labour and the know-how.

## What can a side hustler realistically offer?

- **Setup help:** levelling, framing and liner fitting on a kit the client already bought. Rinkbuild says slope, liner fitting and frame alignment are what trip up first-timers.
- **Snow clearing on the ice:** Rinkbuild says it takes 10 to 50 minutes with a shovel per snowfall and is the most important upkeep task.
- **Evening floods:** thin floods with the client's hose after clearing.
- **Spring takedown:** drain, dry and store the liner and frame.

Avoid refrigerated systems, platforms over steep slopes, and anything near a foundation. Rinkbuild describes a client blowout that sent water into a basement; a mistake here is expensive and may not be covered by a basic policy. See [side hustle insurance](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

## Illustrative example: one winter of rink care for three neighbours

Teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote or a forecast. Suppose you care for three neighbours' 20×30 rinks for 10 weeks (Rinkbuild cites 10 to 12 weeks of usable ice in a normal Toronto season):

| Line | Assumption | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup help, 3 rinks | $150 each (assumed, well under a pro install) | $450 |
| Weekly clear-and-flood visit | $40 per visit (assumed, under Rinkbuild's ~$100 shave-and-torch) × 3 rinks × 10 weeks | $1,200 |
| Spring takedown, 3 rinks | $60 each (assumed) | $180 |
| **Gross** | | **$1,830** |
| Your costs | Shovel, ice scraper, cold-rated gloves (assumed $120); clients supply water and hose | −$120 |
| **Before tax** | | **$1,710** |
| Tax set-aside | 25% (assumed) | −$427.50 |
| **Kept** | | **$1,282.50** |

Arithmetic: $450 + $1,200 + $180 = $1,830; $1,830 − $120 = $1,710; 25% of $1,710 is $427.50. Warm spells cancel visits, so agree in writing whether a missed week is billed.

## What checklist can you copy for each visit?

1. Check the forecast; skip floods above freezing.
2. Clear all snow off the ice before it packs down.
3. Look for liner leaks along the boards; patch while dry.
4. Flood thin, from the far end toward the hose.
5. Drain the client's hose so it does not freeze solid.
6. Text the client a photo and the next visit date.

## Which tax rules apply?

Income goes on Form T2125 with your other self-employment income. GST/HST registration applies once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single quarter or over four consecutive quarters.
