For a Canadian newsletter side hustle, Substack is the simplest start: publishing is free at any list size, and if you turn on paid subscriptions Substack keeps 10% plus Stripe's processing fees. beehiiv has a free plan up to 2,500 subscribers and paid plans from 49 USD a month billed yearly, and it takes 0% of paid subscription revenue. Kit is free up to 10,000 subscribers, with paid plans from 33 USD a month billed yearly, and charges 3.5% plus 30 cents on digital products and subscriptions, including card processing. Substack costs nothing until you earn; beehiiv and Kit can cost less once paid revenue is large enough to outweigh the monthly plan.

## How do Substack, beehiiv, and Kit compare on cost?

Substack charges a share of revenue; beehiiv and Kit charge monthly plans with lower or no revenue share. All three priced in USD on October 4, 2026; conversions use the Bank of Canada [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4246 CAD per USD for October 2, 2026, and are approximate.

| Factor | Substack | beehiiv | Kit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free plan | Free at any list size ([cost](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037607131-How-much-does-Substack-cost)) | Up to 2,500 subscribers ([pricing](https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing)) | Up to 10,000 subscribers ([pricing](https://kit.com/pricing)) |
| First paid plan | None needed | Lite: 49 USD/mo billed yearly (about $69.81 CAD) | Creator: 33 USD/mo billed yearly, 390 USD a year, priced at 1,000 subscribers |
| Next plan | None | Pro: 95 USD/mo billed yearly | Pro: 66 USD/mo billed yearly, 790 USD a year |
| Platform cut of paid subscriptions | 10% | 0% (paid subscriptions from Lite up) | 3.5% + 30¢, inclusive of card processing |
| Card processing | Stripe: 2.9% + 0.30 USD for US cards, plus 0.7% Stripe Billing on recurring payments | Stripe's standard processing fees | Included in the 3.5% + 30¢ |
| Other notes | Optional custom domain, one-time 50 USD | Ad network and recommendations listed | 14-day trial on paid plans; Paid Recommendations fee 23.5% |

Kit's paid plan prices rise with list size; the figures above are its quoted prices for 1,000 subscribers.

## Which fees matter when you start charging readers?

Once you have paid subscribers, compare the platform cut plus card processing against the monthly plan.

- **Substack:** no monthly cost, but 10% of each paid subscription payment plus Stripe fees. Readers pay the price you set, and Substack does not add a fee to web payments.
- **beehiiv:** you need Lite or higher for paid subscriptions. beehiiv says it takes 0% of paid subscription and digital product revenue, so you pay only Stripe's processing fees on top of the plan.
- **Kit:** the 3.5% plus 30 cents covers card processing, so there is no separate Stripe line.

### Illustrative example

Assume 100 paid subscribers at 7 USD a month, so 700 USD a month in gross revenue, paid as 100 card payments. The price, subscriber count, and Stripe rate applying to every payment are assumptions.

- **Substack:** 10% × 700 = 70. Stripe: 2.9% × 700 = 20.30, plus 100 × 0.30 = 30, plus 0.7% × 700 = 4.90. Total fees 125.20 USD, keeping 574.80 USD (about $818.86 CAD).
- **beehiiv Lite:** plan 49. Stripe: 20.30 + 30 = 50.30 (any Stripe Billing fee is extra). Total 99.30 USD, keeping 600.70 USD (about $855.76 CAD).
- **Kit Creator:** plan 33. Fees: 3.5% × 700 = 24.50, plus 100 × 0.30 = 30. Total 87.50 USD, keeping 612.50 USD (about $872.57 CAD).

At lower revenue Substack's lack of a monthly fee wins. Note that Kit's plan price rises with list size, and this example ignores free readers.

## What mistakes do Canadian newsletter writers make?

The common mistakes are ignoring consent rules and comparing plan prices without revenue share.

- **Importing a contact list without consent.** Canada's [anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) governs commercial electronic messages; use opt-in sign-ups and include an unsubscribe link.
- **Forgetting currency.** All three price in USD; a USD plan costs more in CAD.
- **Paying for a plan before you have readers.** Every platform has a free starting point.
- **Ignoring Stripe Billing.** Substack's help page lists 0.7% for recurring payments on top of card fees.
- **Moving platforms without exporting.** Export your subscriber list regularly.

## Which tax rules apply to newsletter income in Canada?

Paid subscriptions and sponsorships are generally business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), converted to Canadian dollars, and platform plans and processing fees used for the business are generally deductible. Once your taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters, you generally must [register for GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html); see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). For where to keep payouts, see [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).
