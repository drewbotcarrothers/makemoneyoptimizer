## Which pricing models work for freelance bookkeeping?

Sell a monthly retainer by transaction volume, a fixed-price catch-up after a paid review, and priced add-ons. Keep hourly for clean-up with unclear scope.

| Model | How it works | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Retainer by volume | Tiered monthly fee by transactions and accounts | 786 Venture CPA: $149 / $649 / $1,099 |
| Per-employee payroll | Fixed fee plus a charge per extra employee | Babii: +$30 / $25 / $20 per employee |
| Catch-up per month of backlog | Rate × months behind, quoted after a review | Frankly Financial: $150–$400 per month |
| Catch-up fixed project | One price for the whole backlog | Profitick: $1,500–$5,000 for 6–12 months |
| Hourly | Clean-up and advisory | 786 Venture CPA: $100/hour catch-up |

Define a "transaction" in the engagement letter, review the tier every quarter, and move the client up when volume passes the limit. Count myAccount tells clients before it changes their tier; do the same. Keep work that needs a professional designation, such as assurance engagements, out of scope unless you hold one.

## How do you calculate a freelance bookkeeping rate?

Build an hourly rate from target pay, CPP, overhead and the share of hours you can bill. Then time a client's month and turn the hours into a tier.

```text
Hourly rate   = (target pay + CPP + software, training, insurance per hour)
                ÷ share of hours billable
Monthly fee   = measured hours per month × hourly rate, rounded to a tier
Catch-up      = months behind × hours per month of backlog × hourly rate
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $38/hour (between the $28.02 median and $45.07 high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $4.52 |
| Software, training, insurance per hour | $3.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 70% |
| **Rate** | ($38 + $4.52 + $3) ÷ 0.70 = $65.03, **round to $65** |

$65 sits inside Maxpro Financials' $45 to $80 Toronto range and below 786 Venture CPA's $100 catch-up rate.

**Illustrative price sheet** at $65 an hour, before GST/HST:

| Service | Assumed hours | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter: up to 50 transactions, 1–2 accounts | 1.5 h/month | $100/month |
| Standard: up to 150 transactions, up to 4 accounts | 3 h/month | $195/month |
| Plus: up to 300 transactions, up to 6 accounts | 5.5 h/month | $360/month |
| Catch-up, per month of backlog (standard volume) | 2.5 h | $165 |
| Payroll run, up to 5 employees | 0.75 h | $50 per run |
| GST/HST return preparation | 1 h | $65 per return |
| Year-end package for the client's accountant | 3 h | $195 |
| Review month (paid trial) | 3 h | $195 |

Check: 1.5 × $65 = $97.50 → $100; 3 × $65 = $195; 5.5 × $65 = $357.50 → $360; 2.5 × $65 = $162.50 → $165; 0.75 × $65 = $48.75 → $50.

## How should you write a bookkeeping quote and invoice?

Quote after a paid review month, list what counts as a transaction, and name everything outside the fee.

```text
BOOKKEEPING ENGAGEMENT QUOTE — [client]          Date: [date]
Tier: Standard — up to 150 transactions/month, up to 4 accounts
  A transaction = one bank, card or payment-processor line.
  A transfer between your own accounts counts as two.
Monthly: categorise, reconcile all accounts, P&L and balance sheet
  by the 15th of the following month ................. $195/month
Catch-up: Jan–Dec 2025, 12 months × $165 ............... $1,980
  50% deposit ($990) before work starts; balance on delivery
Add-ons (only if requested): payroll $50/run · GST/HST return $65 ·
  year-end package for your accountant $195
Not included: tax returns, CRA representation, audit or review
  engagements, work needing a professional designation
Tier review: quarterly. If volume passes 150 for two months running,
  Plus tier ($360) applies from the next month, with notice.
Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ____]. Net 15 days.
```

```text
INVOICE #2026-031   Date: [date]
Monthly bookkeeping, Standard tier (Sept) ....... $195.00
Payroll runs, 2 × $50 ........................... $100.00
Subtotal ........................................ $295.00
HST 13% (registered only) ....................... $38.35

e-Transfer to [email] or pay online by [date].
```

## How do you raise freelance bookkeeping prices?

Raise prices when volume moves up a tier, when your timings run long, and at an annual review written into the engagement letter.

1. **Enforce tiers.** Babii and Count myAccount move clients up when volume grows; so should you.
2. **Charge per employee.** Babii's per-employee payroll charge scales with the client's team.
3. **Price filings separately.** Babii includes GST/HST filing only from its $600 tier.
4. **Charge for the review.** Catch-up firms review before quoting; a paid review month protects your rate.
5. **Move toward market.** If your Standard tier is $195 and Toronto firms start at $300, there's room once your calendar fills.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], your bookkeeping fee moves from $[195] to $[215] a month
from [January 1], per our annual review. Your volume and service stay
the same. Reply with any questions. — [Your name]
```

New clients get the new prices first.

## What freelance bookkeeping pricing mistakes are common?

- **Fixing a retainer before seeing a month of data.** Charge for the review month.
- **No definition of a transaction.** Volume creeps up and the fee doesn't.
- **Quoting catch-up blind.** Missing statements stretch the job.
- **Free payroll and filings.** Babii and 786 Venture CPA price these by tier or employee.
- **Matching offshore rates.** Canadian clients pay for knowledge of GST/HST and CRA deadlines.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect freelance bookkeeping prices?

Freelance bookkeeping income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, counting all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, which for most services generally follows the client's address, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers voluntary registration; business clients who are registered can generally claim input tax credits for the GST/HST you charge. Set aside tax from each payment with the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
