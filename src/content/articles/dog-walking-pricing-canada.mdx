Canadian dog walking companies publish $17 to $39 for a 30-minute solo walk in 2026, and $25 to $37 for a one-hour group walk. Toronto, Ottawa and Calgary sit mostly at $25 to $35 for 30 minutes, and Montreal and Halifax at $20 to $27. Rover's Toronto walkers average $20.73. A side hustler can price around $23 for 30 minutes and $25 per dog for a group walk, and charge separately for extra dogs, weekends and holidays.

A self-employed walker pays both halves of CPP, travel between homes, bags and insurance, and isn't paid for messages or meet-and-greets. The per-walk price has to cover all of that.

## Which pricing models work for dog walking?

Price solo walks by length, group walks per dog, and give regular clients a frequency rate. Charge for every add-on the companies above list separately.

| Model | Use it for | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per solo walk by length | Reactive dogs, puppies, seniors | Waggr: $26 / $36 / $47 for 30 / 45 / 60 minutes |
| Group walk per dog | Social dogs on a dense route | All Paws In: $27 per dog, $15 for a second dog |
| Frequency rate | Commuters booking 3 to 5 walks a week | A Bark Above: $30 at 1 a week, $29 at 2, $27 at 3 to 5 |
| Monthly volume discount | Clients who book 20+ services a month | Utopianimal: 5% at 4 to 7 services up to 15% at 20+ |
| Drop-in / potty break | Puppies and short absences | All Paws In $15; HFX Dog $15; United Pets $20 |
| Surcharges | Weekends, holidays, early or late slots | A Bark Above +$10; Artemis holiday surcharge $20; Dog Jogs weekend +$10 |
| Travel fee | Clients outside your core area | Eva & Zoe $0.70/km; A Bark Above 5% fuel surcharge |

Group size can be a city rule. Toronto's [commercial dog walker permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) covers walking four to six dogs commercially, requires $2,000,000 in commercial general liability insurance, and costs $305.99 for 2026 (tax not included). That fee belongs in your group price once you walk four or more dogs.

## How do you calculate a dog walking rate?

Build a loaded hourly cost, then charge each walk for its walk time plus the travel and messaging time it causes.

```text
Hourly cost   = target pay + CPP (11.9% of pay) + gear, bags, fuel and insurance per hour
Solo walk     = (walk minutes + travel/admin minutes) ÷ 60 × hourly cost
Group walk    = (walk minutes + pickup/drop-off minutes) ÷ 60 × hourly cost ÷ dogs
                (break-even per dog; price above it)
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $25.00/hour (between Job Bank's $18.00 median and $25.50 high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $2.98 |
| Bags, treats, fuel, insurance per hour | $2.00 |
| **Hourly cost** | $25.00 + $2.98 + $2.00 = **$29.98, round to $30** |
| 30-minute walk + 15 minutes travel and messages | 0.75 h × $30 = $22.50, **price $23** |
| 60-minute walk + 15 minutes | 1.25 h × $30 = $37.50, **price $38** |
| 15-minute drop-in + 15 minutes | 0.5 h × $30 = **$15** |
| 60-minute group, 3 dogs, 30 minutes of pickups | 1.5 h × $30 = $45, or $15 per dog break-even; **price $25 per dog** ($75 for 1.5 hours = $50/hour) |

$23 for 30 minutes sits above Rover's $20.73 Toronto average and below Waggr's $26 and Dog Friends' $30. $25 per dog for a group walk sits under All Paws In's $27 and Mutts Ottawa's $30.

## What do worked dog walking quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** These use the rate build above and assumed walk times. Market comparisons are published.

### Quote 1: one dog, 5 × 30-minute walks a week, Toronto

- 5 × $23 = $115, less 5% for a fixed weekly booking = $109.25, quote **$110 a week**.
- With 13% HST once registered: $110 × 1.13 = $124.30.
- Market check: 5 Rover walks at the $20.73 average are $103.65; Dog Friends charges $28 a walk at 4+ a week.
- Annual value: 50 weeks × $110 = $5,500 from one dog.

### Quote 2: two dogs from one home, group walk 3 days a week, Calgary

- First dog $25 + second dog $15 = $40 per walk; 3 × $40 = **$120 a week before 5% GST** ($126 with GST if registered).
- Market check: All Paws In charges $27 + $15 = $42 per walk for the same two dogs.

### Quote 3: reactive dog, 2 × 60-minute private walks a week, Ottawa

- 60-minute solo price $38 + $5 for a reactive dog that can't join a group = $43; 2 × $43 = **$86 a week** ($97.18 with 13% HST).
- Market check: Walk With Me Ottawa and Dreamline Canine charge $45 plus HST for 60 minutes; Mutts Ottawa charges $35.

### Quote 4: holiday walk

- Christmas Day 30-minute walk: $23 + $10 holiday surcharge = **$33**. A Bark Above adds $10 for weekends and holidays; Artemis adds $20 on holidays.

### What a weekday route earns

**Illustrative.** One midday group of 3 dogs at $25 each, plus four 30-minute solo walks at $23, five days a week:

- Group: 3 × $25 × 5 = $375. Solo: 4 × $23 × 5 = $460. Total: **$835 a week**.
- Time: 1.5 hours for the group + 4 × 0.75 hours for solos = 4.5 hours a day, 22.5 hours a week. $835 ÷ 22.5 = $37.11 an hour.
- Over 48 weeks that's $40,080, past the $30,000 GST/HST threshold, so plan to register and add tax.

## How should you write a dog walking quote and service agreement?

Meet the dog first, then send a written quote that lists the walk, the schedule, the add-ons, and the rules for keys, weather and cancellations. [Your first dog walking clients](/side-hustles/dog-walking-first-clients-canada/) covers the meet-and-greet.

```text
DOG WALKING QUOTE — [client name, address]   Date: [date]
Dog: [name], [breed], [age]; on-leash only; pulls toward squirrels
Service: 30-minute solo walk, Monday–Friday, between 11:30 and 1:30
Price: $110 per week (5 × $23, less 5% for a fixed weekly booking)
Included: walk, water refill, paw wipe, photo update by text
Extra: second dog +$10/walk; weekend walk +$8; holidays +$10
Keys: lockbox [code by text]; I'll return keys if service ends
Weather: walks go ahead in rain and snow; shortened below -25°C wind chill,
  same price
Cancellation: 24 hours' notice, or the walk is charged
Emergency vet: [clinic], owner pre-authorizes up to $[amount]
Prices before GST/HST [or: includes HST at 13%, # ______]
```

## How do you raise dog walking prices?

Raise prices once a year with a month's written notice, raise new-client prices first, and use the structures companies already publish.

1. **Charge less per walk for more walks, not less overall.** A Bark Above charges $30 for one walk a week and $27 at three to five. Douglas Fur Pets in Vancouver adds $5 to a [once-a-week group booking](https://douglasfurpets.com/vancouver-group-walks/). Pricing low-frequency clients higher fills your route with regulars.
2. **Price every extra dog.** Walk the Dog YYC adds $10 per extra dog on a 30-minute walk; Rover walkers in Toronto average +$12.74.
3. **Add weekend, holiday and early or late surcharges.** Waggr charges extra before 8 a.m. and after 8 p.m.
4. **Charge for distance.** Eva & Zoe bills $0.70/km for travel; A Bark Above adds a 5% fuel surcharge.
5. **Pass on card fees.** Walk the Dog YYC adds 5% on card payments and takes e-Transfer free.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], thank you for a great year with [dog]. Starting [date, 30+ days out],
30-minute walks will be $[24] (from $[23]); your weekly rate becomes $[115].
Holiday and extra-dog prices stay the same. — [Your name]
```

A waiting list, or a far-away client costing you 20 minutes a day, is the signal to raise prices or replace that slot with a closer dog. [Planning dog walking routes](/side-hustles/dog-walking-routes-canada/) shows how to tighten a route.

## What dog walking pricing mistakes are common?

- **Pricing at the app average after leaving the app.** Rover's $20.73 average is before its 20% fee; a direct client paying $23 keeps more money with you.
- **Pricing a reactive dog like a group dog.** A dog that can't join a group takes a solo slot.
- **Free second dogs.** Every company above charges for them.
- **No cancellation window.** HFX Dog and A Bark Above both use 24 hours.
- **Ignoring travel.** A 30-minute walk with 15 minutes of travel is 45 minutes of your time.

For the insurance, permit and agreement mistakes walkers make most, see the [dog walking mistakes guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/#common-dog-walking-mistakes). ## How do GST/HST and income tax affect dog walking prices?

Dog walking income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. Register for GST/HST once taxable sales from all your businesses pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). Then add the rate for your province ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)): most price lists above are before 5% GST in Alberta and B.C., 13% HST in Ontario and 15% HST in Nova Scotia. HFX Dog's $20 private walk is $23 with HST. Prepaid weekly packages are income when you earn them; keep a record of each payment.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

See [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) and the [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

Company prices were on each company's page on October 9, 2026, and many are "from" or starting prices that change with the dog, the area and the schedule. Rover's averages come from its own walkers' rates in September 2025. Job Bank wages are employee pay from 2023–2024. The rate build, quotes, route and price sheet use an assumed $30 hourly cost and assumed travel times, so time your own route for a week before you publish prices.
