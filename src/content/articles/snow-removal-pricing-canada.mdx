## Where should a side hustle price itself against those companies?

Price 10% to 25% under the established companies in your city, but don't go below the hourly floor you work out in the next section. Those companies carry trucks, blowers, insurance and staff. You compete on being close by, quick after a storm and flexible.

| Your setup | Where to price | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shovel only, walking route, small driveways | Low end of HomeStars' $30 to $90 per visit; seasonal 20% to 25% under local companies | Slower on big storms, no plough |
| Cordless or two-stage blower, car | 10% to 15% under local companies | Close to company speed on a dense route |
| Blower, insurance, written contract, salt included | Match the local company list | Same product, closer to the client |

In 2019, a 14-year-old in Winnipeg told the *Winnipeg Free Press* he usually charged about $10 to $15 a property ([Winnipeg Free Press](https://www.winnipegfreepress.com/business/2019/11/08/no-business-like-snow-business)). That's a youth neighbourhood rate from seven winters ago. Adults pricing in 2026 should start from the table above.

## What formula should you use to price snow removal?

Use minutes, not square feet. Time the driveway once, add travel, then apply your hourly target and materials. A seasonal price is about 8 to 10 times that visit price, checked against a heavy winter, with a cap.

```text
Per-visit price = (minutes on site + travel minutes) ÷ 60 × hourly target + salt per visit
Seasonal price  = per-visit price × 8 to 10
Heavy-year test = seasonal price ÷ (heavy-winter visits × hours per visit) ≥ hourly floor
Visit cap       = expected visits + 20% to 30%, then the per-visit rate
Monthly price   = seasonal price ÷ number of billing months
```

**Choosing the hourly target.** The Job Bank median for landscaping and grounds labourers is $20.75 an hour ([Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/20227)). As a self-employed operator you also pay your own CPP (11.9% on net earnings), gear, fuel, insurance and the hours you spend selling and invoicing. The illustrative examples below use a $50 target, about 2.4 times that wage, to cover those costs.

**Why 8 to 10 visits.** The published lists price a season at roughly that many one-time visits: BlueGuard's $499 season is 10.2 of its $49 visits, Blizzard Wizard's $495 is 8.3 of its $60 visits, and Km Seasonal's $550 is 7.3 of its $75 visits. Clients see a season as a discount, and you get paid in a mild winter.

**Expected visits.** Use your city's operators as a guide. Ottawa contracts often cap at 250 cm of total snowfall ([Snowforge](https://snowforgeottawa.ca/pricing)) or 275 cm ([BlueGuard](https://snowremoval.blueguard.ca/snow-removal-cost-ottawa)). Bulger Brothers counts 25 to 35 significant snowfalls in a typical Winnipeg winter. A Quebec pricing guide from [Sendwork](https://blog-fr.sendwork.com/deneigement-residentiel-quebec/) counts 15 to 22 notable snowfalls a winter in Montreal. A 1 cm trigger produces many more visits than a 5 cm one.

### Quote 1: single driveway in Ottawa, per visit

- Timed clear: 20 minutes for the driveway, walk and steps, plus 5 minutes walking to the next house = 25 minutes.
- Labour: 25 ÷ 60 × $50 = $20.83. Salt: $2. Cost-based price: $22.83.
- Market check: BlueGuard charges $49 per visit, and HomeStars' national range starts at $30.
- **Quote: $40 per visit, before tax.** That's under BlueGuard and well above the cost floor, so heavy storms still pay.

### Quote 2: the same driveway as a seasonal contract with a cap

Seasonal price: $40 × 10 = $400, below Stittsville's $418.50 detached price and BlueGuard's $499. Expected visits: 20 (assumed). Cap: 25 visits, then $35 each.

| Light | 12 | $400 | 5.0 | $80.00 |

### Quote 3: double driveway in Edmonton, monthly

- Timed clear: 35 minutes plus 5 minutes travel = 40 minutes. Labour: 40 ÷ 60 × $50 = $33.33, plus $3 salt = $36.33 per visit.
- A 1 cm trigger, like Serene's and Helpful Guys', means frequent visits. Assume 6 visits a month: 6 × $36.33 = $217.98.
- Market check: Serene charges $249 a month for a double driveway, and Helpful Guys $299.
- **Quote: $220 a month from November to March (5 × $220 = $1,100), plus GST if registered.** Add $25 a month for a corner lot, under Serene's $30 add-on.

### What a 10-client route earns

**Illustrative.** Ten seasonal clients at $400 bring in $4,000 before tax. At 20 visits of 25 minutes each, that's 10 × 20 × 25 ÷ 60 = 83.3 hours, or $48 an hour before gear and salt. Deposits arrive in October and November, before the work.

## How do per-visit, seasonal and monthly pricing compare?

Seasonal contracts give you money up front and a dense route. Per-visit pricing pays more per clear but disappears in a mild winter. Monthly billing is a seasonal contract paid in instalments, and the published lists show monthly totals usually run higher than prepay.

| | Per visit | Seasonal prepay | Monthly | Pay-per-service pass |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example | BlueGuard $49 | Blizzard Wizard $495 (1-car) | Blizzard Wizard $95 × 6 = $570 | Snoweco from $35.99 |
| When money arrives | After each clear | Fall, often with a deposit | First of each month | After each clear, card on file |
| Mild winter | You earn less | You keep the fee | You keep the fee | You earn less |
| Heavy winter | You earn more if you can keep up | You need a cap | You need a cap | You earn more |
| Best for | Starting out, irregular clients | Dense walking routes | Clients who won't prepay | Card billing with automation |

Payment terms to copy from the published lists: Stittsville takes half when the client signs and the rest by January 1. Déneigement Montréal takes a 25% deposit. Ice Snow Removal allows a second instalment on February 1. Blizzard Wizard bills six monthly payments.

## How do you raise snow removal prices?

The published lists show four ways to charge more without a fight: early-bird pricing, response-time tiers, tighter triggers and add-ons. Each gives clients a reason to pay more, rather than simply raising the price.

1. **Early-bird discount with a deadline.** Blizzard Wizard's $550 standard price drops to $495 prepaid until October 31, 2026, a 10% discount. Monster's early-bird pricing ends October 15. Set next year's standard price higher and give returning clients the early-bird price.
2. **Response-time tiers.** Blizzard Wizard charges $540 instead of $495 for a 4–6 hour window instead of 6–10 hours, about 9% more.
3. **Trigger tiers.** Snowforge's 2.5 cm "Ultra" plan costs $1,149 for a single driveway against $649 at 5 cm, 77% more for clients who want bare pavement.
4. **Add-ons.** Snowforge charges $199 a season for a front walkway and $99 for steps. Stittsville charges $150 for hand-shovelling the door, steps and porch. Serene adds $30 a month each for a corner lot, a detached garage or ice melt.

A renewal note you can send in September:

```text
Hi [Name],
Thanks for having me clear your driveway last winter. 2026–27 pricing:
- Seasonal (Nov 1 to Mar 31, 5 cm trigger, up to 25 visits): $[new price]
- Returning-client price if you confirm by October 31: $[last year's price]
- Optional: walkway and steps, $[add-on] for the season
Half is due when you sign and the rest by January 1. Reply to keep your spot.
[Your name, phone]
```

Raise prices in September, before the first snowfall, and keep a returning-client price for the first year. More scripts are in the [guide to raising side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## How do you size a driveway for the quote?

Measure the driveway and time one clear, then add steps, the sidewalk if your city puts it on the owner, salt and the plough windrow. Square metres describe the property. Minutes set the price. The [price sheet by driveway size](/side-hustles/snow-removal-price-sheet-driveway-size-canada/) turns your timings into tiers.

1. **Measure.** Driveway length and width, front walk, number of steps, public sidewalk length, gravel or paved, slope, and where snow can legally go. Gravel changes the tool; see the [tools guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-tools-canada/).
2. **Time one clear.** Include walking back and stacking snow.
3. **Price the windrow.** The ridge a city plough leaves across the driveway is a second visit unless you include it. BlueGuard's seasonal plan includes the windrow, and Nova Snow includes street plough re-clearing on every plan.
4. **Separate salt.** Scrape first, then melt. Before you set a salt line, <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="compare ice melt bag sizes on Amazon.ca" search="ice melt" /> and check your hardware store.
5. **Check the sidewalk rule.** Calgary owners must clear public sidewalks to bare surface within 24 hours, with fines of $250, $500 and $750 ([City of Calgary](https://www.calgary.ca/bylaws/snow-ice.html)). Toronto clears about 98% of sidewalks once 2 cm falls and fines $500 plus a $110 victim surcharge for pushing snow onto sidewalks or roads ([City of Toronto](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/streets-parking-transportation/road-maintenance/winter-maintenance/clearing-snow-and-ice-from-your-property/)). Ottawa's [roads by-law](https://ottawa.ca/en/living-ottawa/laws-licences-and-permits/laws/laws-z/use-and-care-roads-law-no-2003-498) bans depositing snow on a road.
6. **Write the quote before the snow.** The full list of terms is in the [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/).

## What should you leave out of a snow removal price?

## Which tax rules apply to snow removal pricing in Canada?

Snow removal income is self-employment income, including a seasonal lump sum, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people outside Quebec pay both halves of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution.
