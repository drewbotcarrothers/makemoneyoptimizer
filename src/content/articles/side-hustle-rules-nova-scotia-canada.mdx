In Nova Scotia, a side hustle registers a sole proprietorship name with the Registry of Joint Stock Companies only when it uses a business name ($68.55 to register after a name reservation), charges 14% HST only after passing the $30,000 small-supplier test, and checks Halifax or its own municipality for activity licences such as vendor licences for selling on the street. Employees, including event and seasonal staff, are covered by a $17.00 minimum wage from October 1, 2026. This guide suits Nova Scotians starting a service, selling crafts or food, or picking up gig work.

It is part of the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) set. For depth, read [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/), [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), and [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

## Do you need to register a side hustle in Nova Scotia?

Only if you trade under a business name. Nova Scotia's [sole proprietorship page](https://novascotia.ca/register-sole-proprietorship) describes reserving a name, which the Registry of Joint Stock Companies must approve, and lists $68.55 to register and $68.55 to renew. The CRA lists Nova Scotia among the provinces where a business number is issued automatically when you register ([When you need a BN](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html)).

### Step 1: Choose your name

Trading as "Sam MacDonald" keeps you out of the registry; "South Shore Snow Clearing" needs a reservation and registration.

### Step 2: Reserve, then register

Wait for the name approval, then file the registration and keep the documents for your bank.

### Step 3: Diary the renewal

The renewal fee matches the registration fee, so treat it as an annual cost when you price your service.

## When do Nova Scotia side hustlers charge HST?

After passing $30,000 in taxable sales. The CRA's [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) notes that the HST rate in Nova Scotia decreased to 14% on April 1, 2025. You do not have to register until worldwide taxable sales exceed $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). The CRA requires [taxi and ride-sharing drivers](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) to register from the start.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Halifax cleaner bills $2,000 a month: $24,000 over four quarters, under $30,000, so no HST. At $2,700 a month ($32,400) registration applies, and a $120 clean becomes $120 plus $16.80 HST (14%), or $136.80. Old price lists that assume 15% need updating.

## Which licences do side hustles need in Nova Scotia?

Mostly activity-specific municipal ones. Halifax's [licences page](https://www.halifax.ca/business/licences) covers vendor licences, including artisan and food vending licences, for selling food or handmade crafts in the municipality, as well as sidewalk café licences. A home-based online or service business is not on that list, but check your municipality's planning rules and search [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/) by location and business type.

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in Nova Scotia?

Not a platform-specific one. Nova Scotia's [minimum wage page](https://novascotia.ca/lae/employmentrights/minimumwage.asp) says employers must pay employees at least $17.00 an hour from October 1, 2026, with a three-hour minimum when an employee is called in outside regular hours. That covers employees such as event staff, not independent contractors on delivery apps. Ontario and B.C. are the provinces with app-worker minimums; compare [side hustle rules in Ontario](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-ontario-canada/).

## What are the common Nova Scotia side hustle mistakes?

- **Using the old 15% HST rate.** It has been 14% since April 1, 2025.
- **Charging HST before registering.** Only registered businesses add HST.
- **Selling at street events without a vendor licence.** Halifax licenses vendors and artisans.
- **Forgetting the renewal fee.** Budget the $68.55 renewal.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in Nova Scotia?

Report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net earnings, and Nova Scotia income tax is calculated on the same return.
