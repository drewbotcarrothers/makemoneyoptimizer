## How should you choose pressure washing tools in Canada?

Choose pressure washing tools in Canada by the surfaces you will actually sell, the power you can safely reach, and where the water will go. Nothing on this page was hands-on tested by this site. These are selection criteria, so you can compare whatever is on your local shelf.

| Criterion | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surfaces you will sell | Concrete driveways and walks first; decks and siding only once you know the nozzle and distance | A narrow tip on soft wood or siding is how a paid job becomes a damage claim |
| Power source | Electric units need an outlet. [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/garages/wash_clean.html) says to use a GFCI-protected outlet and keep extension cords out of water | A cord lying in a puddle is the hazard, not a detail |
| Nozzles included | A wide fan tip in the box, or a set you can add | You can always move closer. You cannot undo an etched line |
| Weight and storage | Can you lift it into the car, and store it above freezing if the manual asks? | Canadian winters crack pumps that were left with water inside. Follow the manual |
| Runoff plan | Can you route water away from a catch basin or contain it? | [Toronto](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says outdoor washwater must not reach a catch basin |

## When should you upgrade pressure washing tools in Canada?

Upgrade pressure washing tools in Canada when paid jobs show a specific bottleneck: driveways take too long, the hose is too short, or electric power is not available where the work is. Buying the commercial machine first turns a side hustle into a payment.

| Upgrade item | Typical range (check current price) | Buy it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surface cleaner attachment | Often about $80–$250 | Flat concrete is most of your bookings and wand streaks are slowing you down |
| Extension cord rated for the machine | About $25–$60 | The manual allows one and outlets are far from the work. A household lamp cord is the wrong tool |
| Longer hose and quick-connect fittings | Check current price | You lose time moving the machine on every job |
| Gas consumer or commercial unit | Often a four-figure shelf price | Electric cannot finish the route, and you have a legal place to fuel and store it |
| Water recovery or containment gear | Whatever your city's method requires | You want jobs where the water would otherwise reach a grate |

Gas units change the safety picture. [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/garages/wash_clean.html) says to fill or drain a fuel tank outdoors away from ignition sources, and never to run gasoline-powered equipment in an enclosed space or a building because carbon monoxide poisoning may occur.

## Which pressure washing safety gear matters most in Canada?

The pressure washing safety gear that matters most in Canada is eye protection, waterproof boots and clothing, and a GFCI outlet for electric units. [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/garages/wash_clean.html) says never to direct the spray toward any person or your own body, because the water pressure may break or enter the skin and cause severe injury, and to expect kickback from the spray gun.

Stay on the ground. Second-storey siding and gutters put you on a ladder with a kicking wand, which is a different risk. If you ever do need a ladder for a different task, [CCOHS on portable ladders](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) says falls from portable ladders are a major source of serious injury and not to carry objects in your hands while climbing.

## How does runoff change which pressure washing tools you buy in Canada?

Runoff changes the tool list because the City of Toronto says power washing with water alone still dislodges or dissolves contaminants, and that wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach any catch basin. Toronto's options are to let the water evaporate, re-route it away from a catch basin or contain it, dispose of it in an indoor drain, or have it hauled off-site by an approved hauler.

Toronto also says both the person hiring and the mobile wash business are responsible for wastewater leaving the property. Other cities use their own bylaws, so confirm locally. In practice that means a few cheap containment items (absorbent socks or a berm, a wet vacuum if you will collect water) can matter more than extra pressure. Check the current price and your city's wording before you buy.

## What pressure washing tool mistakes are common in Canada?

Common pressure washing tool mistakes in Canada are buying a gas commercial unit before any bookings, using a narrow tip on wood or siding, running a cord through standing water, and ignoring where the water drains. Leaving water in the pump over a Canadian winter is the quiet one; follow the storage section of the manual.

## Which tax rules apply to pressure washing tools in Canada?

Pressure washing income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people make the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net business income. Cleaner and small parts are ordinary expenses. A machine that lasts years is depreciable property, and the CRA says you [cannot deduct the full cost](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) in the year you buy it; you claim capital cost allowance over several years.

GST/HST registration is required once your worldwide taxable supplies, including your associates', exceed [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters.
