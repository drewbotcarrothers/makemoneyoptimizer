Getting your first snow removal clients in Canada means asking people who already know you, in the fall, with a written offer and a deposit, before you buy a machine for a route you do not have. Snow removal is paid clearing of nearby driveways and walks. The first clients suit someone who can walk a few blocks before work and can say what is included.

The work itself is the [snow removal side hustle guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/). The number you say at the door comes from [how to price snow removal in Canada](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/), not from a guess that wins the conversation.

## Who should you ask first for snow removal in Canada?

Ask lawn clients and near neighbours first for snow removal in Canada, because they already know whether you show up. A city-wide ad is a later step. Five households on a route you can finish beat twenty names across town.

If you cut grass on the street, the summer half is [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/). Mention winter while you are already trusted. People who hire you for a gate or a railing may be the same doors. That work has its own limits in the [handyman guide](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/). Do not trade a snow contract for electrical work.

## When should you look for snow removal clients in Canada?

Look for snow removal clients in Canada in October and early November in much of the country, before the first storm, when owners are buying a plan rather than a shovel at dusk. A mild coastal street and a Prairie street do not share a calendar. Ask before you need the money from the first snowfall.

The week the forecast turns is when everyone else is knocking. Your note should already be on the fridge. If you wait for snow to prove the service, you are competing with panic, and panic buys the cheapest unclear promise.

## What should the first snow removal offer in Canada say?

The first snow removal offer in Canada should name the address, the surfaces, the trigger depth, the done-by time, the season dates, the price, salt, the windrow, and how either person can cancel. A sentence that you do not clear roofs belongs on the page. The line-by-line version is the [contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/), which is a working note, not legal advice.

Say whether the city sidewalk is included. Sidewalk duty is municipal and can change. Read the city's winter page and quote only what you will actually do. Confirm the trigger by text when they agree, so there is a record besides memory.

Do not offer a free month that becomes a free winter. A paid first storm, at the written price, teaches both of you whether the timing works. If the property is unsafe, decline it. An aggressive dog, black ice you cannot manage, or a driveway you cannot exit is not a client to collect.

## How do you list snow removal in Canada without publishing your home?

List snow removal in Canada as a service-area business and keep your home address off the public listing. You clear snow at the client's property. You do not need strangers visiting your hallway.

Google's [policy overview](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13762416?hl=en) says a Business Profile is for a business that makes in-person contact with customers during its stated hours. Snow removal does that when you are at the driveway. State hours you can explain. Do not put the profile on a 24-hour clock you will not keep. Google's [representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) say to use your real-world name, an accurate address or service area, and the fewest categories that match the core work. If you do not serve customers at your address, those guidelines say a service-area business should hide the address. Google's [service-area page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9157481?hl=en) says a service-area business can have one profile for the area it serves, and that you remove the address if customers are not served there. Set the area to the streets you will actually clear.

## How do you take the first yes for snow removal in Canada?

Take the first yes for snow removal in Canada only after an insurance quote describes the work, and take a deposit or the first instalment into an account you will not spend as wages. Two signed neighbours are a route. Twenty unsigned maybes are not.

### Step 1: Get the insurance quote with the real job described

Say residential driveways and walks, whether you use a blower, whether you apply ice melt, and that you do not clear roofs. Ask whether a home policy covers the business activity before you rely on it. This is not insurance advice. A seasonal cheque without liability coverage is a bet against someone's broken wrist.

### Step 2: Use the price you already calculated

Read the quote aloud: trigger, surfaces, windrow, salt, done-by time, and the heavy-winter picture. If you discount at the door, write the new number and live with it. Do not invent a cheaper tier that includes the same work.

### Step 3: Collect a deposit you can point to

A seasonal client should pay something when they agree, on the schedule in the note. Per-visit clients can pay after each clear if the note says so. Either way, keep the transfer.

### Step 4: Text the trigger back

Repeat the depth, the done-by time, and the first date. Ask them to reply yes. That message is your record when a flurry arrives and someone expected a full clear.

### Step 5: Stop at the houses you can finish

Add the next client after a real storm, not during it. The last house on an overcrowded morning gets the worst job and the first cancellation.

## Which tax rules apply to the first snow removal clients in Canada?

GST/HST for snow removal follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. A deposit in November is still income to track. Keep the agreements and the transfers. If you are registered, charge the rate for the province where the driveway sits and confirm it with the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).
