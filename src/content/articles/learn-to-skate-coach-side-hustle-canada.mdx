A learn-to-skate coaching side hustle in Canada usually means coaching Skate Canada's CanSkate program at a local club on weekday evenings and weekends through the winter. To start, Skate Canada requires you to be at least 15, a club registrant, past a complete Star 5 assessment, and through two eLearning modules and a 2-day in-person course. Section course fees were $385 in Saskatchewan and $475 in Ontario, and Job Bank lists skating coaches at a $25.00 national median wage. Figures were read on Skate Canada's, the sections' and Job Bank's pages on October 19, 2026.

<QuickAnswer>

- **Prerequisites:** age 15+, a current Skate Canada club registrant, at least one complete Star 5 assessment, and the Coaching Foundations and CanSkate Foundations eLearning ([Skate Canada](https://info.skatecanada.ca/index.php/en-ca/procedures/342-national-coaching-certification-program.html)).
- **Course fees:** CanSkate courses are $475 in Ontario ([Skate Ontario](https://skateontario.org/coaches/courses-and-seminars/club-coach-canskate-courses/)) and $385 in Saskatchewan ([Skate Sask](https://skatesask.com/events/canskate-coach-course)); fees vary by section.
- **2026-27 coach fees:** coach fee $125.00, Safe Sport fee $5.00, and Coach Insurance Program $74.50 plus tax for coaches under 65 ([Skate Canada](https://noticeboard.skatecanada.ca/2026/06/23/registration-information-for-2026-2027/)).
- **Pay benchmark:** Job Bank lists figure skating coaches at a $25.00 national median, $16.00 low and $43.27 high; the B.C. median is $30.00 ([Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/5986/ca)).
- **Time limit:** coaches have three years from the in-training course to certify as a CanSkate coach.

</QuickAnswer>

## How do you become a CanSkate coach?

You become a CanSkate coach by meeting Skate Canada's prerequisites, attending a 2-day in-person course run by your provincial section, and then completing the trained and certified steps within three years. Skate Canada's [NCCP page](https://info.skatecanada.ca/index.php/en-ca/procedures/342-national-coaching-certification-program.html) sets out three statuses:

| Status | What it takes | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In-training | Prerequisites + 2-day section course | Coach only the CanSkate program in Skate Canada clubs and schools, as a registered coach in good standing |
| Trained | The 2-day course plus Making Ethical Decisions (multi-sport, Locker) and Teaching and Learning (multi-sport) | Same program, trained status |
| Certified | Evaluation portfolio: mentorship interview, coaching philosophy and goals, skater development philosophy, an Emergency Action Plan, one technical video lesson | Certified CanSkate coach |

Skate Canada says the CanSkate course fee varies by section and that course frequency depends on demand. Skate Ontario's listed 2026 courses were marked complete when read, and it says courses are being planned for 2027. Skate Saskatchewan's fall 2026 course in Regina cost $385, with online registration closing September 8, 2026, and the eLearning had to be finished at least one week before the course.

## What does it cost to start coaching?

Starting costs the section course, the annual Skate Canada coach fees and insurance, and your skates and club registration if you do not already skate. Skate Canada's [2026-27 fee notice](https://noticeboard.skatecanada.ca/2026/06/23/registration-information-for-2026-2027/) lists:

| Fee (effective August 1, 2026) | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Coach fee | $125.00 |
| Safe Sport fee (coaches) | $5.00 |
| Coach Insurance Program, under 65 (liability, accident and weekly indemnity) | $74.50 + tax |
| Coach Insurance Program, 65 to under 75 | $65.05 + tax |
| Coach Insurance Program, 75+ (liability only) | $60.55 + tax |
| Skate Canada registrant fee (skaters) | $30.00 + $5.00 Safe Sport + $0.70 injury benefit |
| Section registrant fee | $25.00 (PE, MB) to $42.50 (SK); Ontario $30.00 |

## How much do skating coaches earn?

Skating coaches in Canada earn a $25.00 median hourly wage, according to Job Bank's [figure skating coach page](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/5986/ca), updated November 19, 2025 (reference period 2023-2024; the occupation group is all coaches, NOC 53201):

| Region | Low | Median | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canada | $16.00 | $25.00 | $43.27 |
| British Columbia | $18.50 | $30.00 | $42.31 |
| Alberta | $18.00 | $25.00 | $45.00 |
| Ontario | $18.00 | $25.00 | $45.00 |
| Quebec | $18.19 | $26.06 | $38.34 |
| Saskatchewan | $17.34 | $26.48 | $40.34 |
| Nova Scotia | $19.68 | $28.17 | $37.25 |

A first-year in-training CanSkate coach is more likely near the low column than the median. Clubs set their own pay or session rates; ask before you take the course.

## Illustrative example: when does the first season cover the fees?

This is an illustrative example. Course and coach fees are sourced; the hourly rate and hours are assumptions.

- Sourced up-front costs (Ontario): course $475 + coach fee $125 + Safe Sport $5 + insurance $74.50 = $679.50 before tax on the insurance.
- Assumed rate: $20 an hour, between Job Bank's $16.00 low and $25.00 median.
- Hours to cover the costs: $679.50 ÷ $20 = 34 hours.
- Assumed schedule: 2 club sessions of 1.5 hours a week = 3 hours a week. 34 ÷ 3 = 11.3 weeks.

| Hours a week (assumed) | Weeks to cover $679.50 at $20 | Weeks at $25 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 11.3 | 9.1 |
| 6 | 5.7 | 4.5 |
| 9 | 3.8 | 3.0 |

Over an assumed 20-week season at 6 hours a week and $20: 20 × 6 × $20 = $2,400 gross, or $2,400 − $679.50 = $1,720.50 after first-year costs, before tax. In later seasons only the annual coach fees and insurance repeat (about $204.50 plus tax).

## What does a first season plan look like?

A first season plan finishes the eLearning in the fall, takes the section course as soon as one runs, and asks a club for CanSkate sessions.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| September to October | Confirm you have a complete Star 5; complete Part A and Part B eLearning |
| October | Ask your section when the next CanSkate course runs; ask a local club about coaching sessions |
| Course weekend | Attend the 2-day course for in-training status |
| November to March | Coach CanSkate sessions; book the two multi-sport courses for trained status |
| Spring | Start the evaluation portfolio with a mentor coach |

## What mistakes do new CanSkate coaches make?

- **Starting the course before you qualify.** You need to be 15 or older, a current Skate Canada club registrant, with at least one complete Star 5 assessment and the eLearning modules done.
- **Forgetting yearly fees.** The 2026–27 coach fee is $125.00, plus a $5.00 Safe Sport fee and the $74.50 Coach Insurance Program for coaches under 65.
- **Budgeting the wrong course fee.** CanSkate courses are $475 in Ontario and $385 in Saskatchewan.
- **Expecting the top rate.** Job Bank's national median is $25.00, with a low of $16.00.

## Which tax rules apply to skating coach pay in Canada?

If a club pays you as an employee, the pay is employment income on a T4. If you invoice the club or families as a self-employed coach, the income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and the coach fees, insurance and course costs are expenses to discuss with a tax professional. GST/HST registration follows the $30,000 small-supplier test on the CRA's [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) page. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
