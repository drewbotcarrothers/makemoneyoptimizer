## Where does bin wash water have to go in Canada?

Bin wash water has to go where your city's sewer bylaw allows, and Toronto has written it in plain language. The City of Toronto's page on [washing cars and outdoor surfaces](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach catch basins, because soaps, cleaning products and contaminants violate storm-sewer limits. It says to contain the water or dispose of it in an indoor drain, which may require treatment to meet sanitary-sewer limits, and that the person hiring and the mobile wash business are both responsible.

Bin sludge is food residue, which is exactly what reaches those limits. That's why the contained kit exists: the wet/dry vac and a lidded bucket let you carry the water to a drain you're allowed to use. Other cities write their own bylaws. Read yours before you quote. If you can't name the drain, don't take the cart.

## Do you need insurance or a licence for bin cleaning in Canada?

Insurance isn't legally required for most sole proprietors, but a single slip on a client's driveway or a damaged cart can cost more than a season of revenue. Zensurance's [general liability page](https://www.zensurance.com/general-liability-insurance) says a basic commercial general liability policy for a small business starts around $450 a year for a $2 million limit. That's a starting estimate, not a quote. Describe residential carts, wash water and any pressure washer when you ask. Compare a direct online quote with a broker in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

Licensing is municipal. Some cities licence home-based or mobile service businesses; search your city's site. If you trade under a name that isn't your own, most provinces require a business name registration; in Ontario that's $60 online, per the province's [fee page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/cost-time-required-to-register-change-search-for-business-name-corporation-not-for-profit). ## Which tax rules apply to a bin cleaning budget in Canada?

Bin cleaning income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both portions of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive quarters. Add every side hustle to the same total. Once registered, charge the provincial rate on the CRA [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) page; Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of April 1, 2025.

Cleaner you use up is a current expense. A pressure washer or wet/dry vac that lasts for years is usually [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. If you're registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html). See [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) and the [tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/).
