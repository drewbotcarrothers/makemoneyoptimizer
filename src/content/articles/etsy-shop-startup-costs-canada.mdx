## How much does an Etsy shop cost to start in Canada?

An Etsy shop in Canada starts at about $15–$60 when the first items use materials you own, about $60–$250 when you buy a scale, mailers, and a first small batch, and about $250–$700 with a fuller batch and better light. Etsy's set-up fee is shown at checkout during shop creation, so confirm it on screen before you count it. The ranges are not quotes.

| How you start | First-month cash, about | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $15–$60 | The set-up fee shown to you, five to ten listings at 0.20 USD each, a small pack of mailers, and materials you already own |
| Typical | $60–$250 | The lean column plus a kitchen or postal scale, tissue and tape, and materials for a first batch of five to ten items |
| Fuller | $250–$700 | A larger batch, a daylight lamp or light box, branded labels, and an optional paid subscription you chose on purpose |

Fees on a sale come out of that sale. They are not in the first-month column until something sells.

## Who should use an Etsy startup budget in Canada?

An Etsy startup budget in Canada fits people who already make, design, or curate something that meets Etsy's rules and can be shipped from a place they control. The lean column fits a maker with supplies in a drawer. It is a poor fit if the plan depends on buying finished goods to relist as handmade, or on a holiday batch that does not exist yet.

If your product is a file instead of a parcel, the budget looks more like [printables startup costs](/side-hustles/printables-startup-costs-canada/). If you want your own checkout later, compare [Shopify store startup costs](/side-hustles/shopify-store-startup-costs-canada/).

## What are the one-time costs for an Etsy shop in Canada?

One-time Etsy costs in Canada are the set-up fee, a way to weigh parcels, and enough packaging for the first few orders. Materials feel like startup, but they repeat every time you restock.

| Item | Typical CAD | Timing | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Etsy set-up fee | Shown during shop set-up | Once, when you open | Etsy says the cost varies by location and the fee is non-refundable. Write down the amount you saw. |
| Phone you already own | $0 | Already owned | Daylight and the real object. A borrowed lifestyle photo misleads. |
| Kitchen or postal scale | $15–$35 | Before you set shipping | A guessed weight becomes a postage adjustment. Retail range to verify locally. |
| Mailers, tissue, tape | $8–$25 for a small pack | Before the first shipped order | Enough for a few orders, not a case. |
| Daylight lamp or light box | $25–$70 | Optional | Only when window light is not enough. |
| Labels or stickers with your shop name | $10–$40 | Optional | Nice, not required for a first sale. |

Monthly Etsy costs in Canada are listing renewals, materials you restock, and any subscription you choose. Per-sale costs are the transaction fee, payment processing, and the Canada regulatory operating fee, all taken from the sale.

## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for an Etsy shop in Canada?

Etsy break-even in Canada is the sale price minus Etsy's percentage fees, the processing flat fee, the listing fee, materials, and packaging. The sketch below is a method, not an average Etsy margin.

### Step 1: Price materials per item, not per bag

A $20 bag of beads that makes ten items is $2 an item. Write that number on the listing draft.

### Step 2: Add the fee lines before you set the price

Transaction 6.5%, processing 3% + 0.25 CAD for a domestic order, and the 0.50% regulatory fee. Shipping you charge is in the base for all three.

### Step 3: Include the listing fee for items that do not sell

Ten listings at 0.20 USD each is about $2.85 CAD at the rate above, even if one item sells.

### Step 4: Weigh the packed parcel

A light guess becomes a postage line you pay. If you offer free shipping, the postage is inside your price and the 6.5% applies to it.

### Step 5: Restock only after the first batch sells

Materials for a second batch are a decision after orders, not before.

### Illustrative example

**A teaching sketch, not a quote, a forecast, or a typical Etsy result.**

Suppose a domestic order is $40 CAD with shipping included in that price, which is an assumption. Transaction fee: 0.065 × $40 = $2.60. Processing: 0.03 × $40 + $0.25 = $1.45. Regulatory operating fee: 0.005 × $40 = $0.20. Fees total $4.25, leaving $35.75. Suppose materials are $9, a mailer is $1, and postage you paid is $12. The remainder is $35.75 − $9 − $1 − $12 = $13.75 before the listing fee, your time, and a tax set-aside. Suppose you listed ten items to get this one sale: about $2.85 CAD in listing fees brings the sketch to about $10.90. These totals are assumptions. Do not copy $40.

## Where can Etsy startup spending be lower in Canada?

Etsy startup spending in Canada stays lower when you list a few items you can already make, use window light, and buy packaging for orders you have, not orders you hope for. Turn automatic renewal off on listings you are not ready to pay for again in four months. Skip paid subscriptions and ads until a listing has sold without them.

A free design tool and a phone camera are enough for the first listings. A branded box is not what makes a first sale.

## Where should Etsy spending not be cut in Canada?

Etsy spending in Canada should not skip a scale, honest photos, or materials that match the description. A wrong weight becomes a postage loss. A photo that hides a flaw becomes a case and a refund. A cheaper material than the listing says is a misrepresentation, not a saving.

## Which tax rules apply to an Etsy shop budget in Canada?

Etsy shop income in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on the activity for profit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the Etsy payment account statements, material receipts, and postage receipts. Report the activity on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. An Etsy shop is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Supplies, platform fees, and subscriptions used for the business are generally current expenses. Equipment that lasts for years, such as a computer or a camera, may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion rather than a one-year deduction. If you are registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) to the extent of commercial use, with receipts that show the tax. If you are not registered, you do not charge GST/HST and you do not claim the credit.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made, unless a platform rule you have read says the platform collects. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). For a set-aside habit, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle in Canada](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
