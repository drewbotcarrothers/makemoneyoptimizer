**Quick answer:** a lean Etsy shop in Canada costs about **$37** to open with materials you already own: 25 kraft bubble mailers and a roll of thank-you stickers at **$33.87** with 13% Ontario HST, plus ten listings at 0.20 USD each, about **$2.85**, plus Etsy's one-time set-up fee, which Etsy shows during shop set-up. A standard kit that adds a shipping scale, a light box, tissue paper and a phone tripod costs **$149.07**, and a pro kit with a thermal label printer costs **$402.17**. With a year of listing renewals and an e-commerce insurance policy from about $500, the standard first year is about **$666**. Gear prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026; Etsy fees are from Etsy's help centre the same day. At an assumed $30 item plus $12 shipping, each order leaves $16.57 after Etsy's fees, postage, materials and packaging, so the standard first year pays back after 41 orders.

How to open the shop, write a listing and follow Etsy's rules on what you may sell is in the [Etsy shop side hustle guide](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash: what Etsy charges a Canadian seller, what to buy to pack and ship, what each item cost on a specific date, and how many orders recover it. The first order is covered in [how to make your first Etsy sale in Canada](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-first-sale-canada/).

## How much does an Etsy shop cost to start in Canada?

An Etsy shop costs $36.72 to open on the lean kit, before the set-up fee, and $50.96, $666.16 or $919.26 across the first year for the lean, standard and pro setups. Gear is Amazon.ca on October 9, 2026 with 13% Ontario HST; listing fees use the Bank of Canada's October 8, 2026 rate of 1.4240 CAD per USD.

| Setup | Gear with 13% HST | Gear with 5% GST | Listing fees, first year | Insurance (per year) | First-year cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $33.87 | $31.47 | $17.09 | None for low-risk items under a quote | $50.96 |
| Standard | $149.07 | $138.52 | $17.09 | About $500 | $666.16 |
| Pro | $402.17 | $373.70 | $17.09 | About $500 | $919.26 |

The listing line assumes 20 listings, each renewed three times in the year because a listing expires after four months: 60 × 0.20 USD = 12 USD, or $17.09 CAD. The opening figure of $36.72 is the lean gear plus ten listings at $2.85. Etsy's set-up fee is not in these totals: Etsy says it is a one-time, non-refundable fee that varies by location and is displayed and charged during shop set-up, and it does not publish the Canadian amount on its fees page. Write down what you see on screen and add it.

Alberta and the territories pay 5% GST only on gear; the [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province.

## Who should use an Etsy startup budget in Canada?

This budget fits people who already make, design or curate something that meets Etsy's rules, such as jewellery, prints, candles, knitwear, stickers or vintage finds, and can ship from a place they control. Etsy brings buyers who are already searching, so it suits makers with no audience of their own. It is a poor fit if the plan depends on buying finished goods to relist as handmade, or on a holiday batch that does not exist yet.

If your product is a file instead of a parcel, the budget looks more like [printables startup costs](/side-hustles/printables-startup-costs-canada/). If you can bring your own traffic, compare a monthly plan with Etsy's per-sale fees in [Etsy vs Shopify in Canada](/side-hustles/etsy-vs-shopify-canada/) and [Shopify store startup costs](/side-hustles/shopify-store-startup-costs-canada/).

## What does Etsy charge a Canadian seller?

Etsy charges per listing and per sale, not per month. These are the fees on Etsy's help pages on October 9, 2026, for a seller with a Canadian bank account:

| Fee | Amount | When it is charged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set-up fee | One-time, varies by location, shown during set-up | When you open the shop ([Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy)) |
| Listing | 0.20 USD, about $0.28 CAD | Each listing, each auto-renewal every 4 months, and each extra unit sold in one order |
| Transaction | 6.5% of the order total, including shipping and gift wrap you charge | Each sale |
| Payment processing | 3% + $0.25 CAD for Canadian and U.S. orders; 4% + $0.25 CAD for other international orders | Each sale, on the total including shipping and any tax ([processing fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy)) |
| Regulatory operating fee, Canada | 0.50% of item price plus shipping and gift wrap | Each sale ([regulatory fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee)) |
| Offsite Ads | 15% of an attributed order if you made under $10,000 on Etsy in the past 365 days; 12% at or above; capped at $100 an order | Only when a buyer arrives through an Etsy ad off the site |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% | Only if you list in a currency other than your payment account's |

On a domestic order, the three per-sale fees add to 6.5% + 3% + 0.5% = 10% of the order plus $0.25. Fixed fees such as listings are published in USD and converted at the market rate when they post, so the CAD amount moves with the exchange rate. List in CAD if your bank account is in CAD to avoid the 2.5% conversion fee.

- **Etsy set-up fee.** Shown during shop set-up; non-refundable.

## How much is insurance for an Etsy shop in Canada?

[Zensurance](https://www.zensurance.com/e-commerce-insurance) says a low-risk small e-commerce business can expect a starting cost of approximately $500 a year for a basic policy, with higher-risk or expensive products costing more. The standard and pro totals use $500. Product liability is the cover that matters for makers: it responds to claims that something you made or sold caused injury or damage, such as a candle, a skin product, a child's toy or jewellery that causes a reaction. Food, cosmetics and children's items raise the premium and bring their own product rules. Compare quote routes in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for an Etsy shop in Canada?

Every order has to cover Etsy's three per-sale fees, postage, materials and packaging before anything goes toward the kit.

### Illustrative example: one domestic order

**Sourced inputs:** the 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + $0.25 processing and 0.50% regulatory fee, and the packaging prices above. **Assumed inputs:** a $30 item, $12 shipping charged to the buyer and $12 postage paid, and $8 of materials.

- Order total: $30 + $12 = **$42**
- Transaction fee: 0.065 × $42 = **$2.73**
- Processing: 0.03 × $42 + $0.25 = **$1.51**
- Regulatory fee: 0.005 × $42 = **$0.21**
- Etsy fees: $2.73 + $1.51 + $0.21 = **$4.45**
- Packaging: mailer $19.99 ÷ 25 = $0.80, tissue $9.98 ÷ 60 = $0.17, sticker $9.98 ÷ 1,120 = $0.01
- Left per order: $42 − $4.45 − $12 − $8 − $0.80 − $0.17 − $0.01 = **$16.57**

| Setup | First-year cash | ÷ $16.57 per order | Orders to break even |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $50.96 | 3.08 | 4 |
| Standard | $666.16 | 40.20 | 41 |
| Pro | $919.26 | 55.48 | 56 |

Add the set-up fee shown to you at the top of each line. Insurance is most of the standard and pro totals; without it, the standard kit and a year of listings ($166.16) pay back after 11 orders.

### Illustrative example: the same order through Offsite Ads

If the buyer arrived through an Etsy ad on another site and you made under $10,000 on Etsy in the past year, Etsy adds 15% of the order: 0.15 × $42 = **$6.30**. The same order leaves $16.57 − $6.30 = **$10.27**, and the standard first year needs 666.16 ÷ 10.27 = 64.86, so **65 orders** if every sale came that way. Price with that fee in mind; it applies to the order total including shipping.

## What could change these Etsy numbers?

Your margin moves the break-even more than any gear. At a $20 item with the same $12 shipping and $8 of materials, the order total is $32, Etsy's fees are $3.45 (0.10 × $32 + $0.25), and the order leaves $32 − $3.45 − $12 − $8 − $0.98 = $7.57, so the standard first year needs 88 orders instead of 41. Free shipping moves postage into the item price, where the 10% applies to it the same way. Etsy's listing fee is set in USD, so its CAD cost moves with the exchange rate. Insurance depends on the product. Amazon.ca prices were checked October 9, 2026 and move with sales. This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.

## Where can Etsy startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **List what you can already make.** Ten listings cost $2.85; a hundred listings of untested designs cost $28.48 and renew every four months.
- **Turn off auto-renew on listings that have not sold.** Each renewal is another 0.20 USD.
- **Use window light and your phone.** The light box earns its place when you list many items or work through winter.
- **Buy the 25-pack of mailers, not 500.** Mailer size depends on what actually sells.
- **Skip Etsy Ads and paid tools until listings sell without them.**
- **Print labels at the post office or on paper until you ship several orders a week.**

## Where should Etsy spending not be cut in Canada?

- **A scale.** A guessed weight becomes a postage adjustment you pay.
- **Honest photos and materials.** A photo that hides a flaw becomes a case and a refund; a cheaper material than the listing says is a misrepresentation.
- **Product liability for anything worn, burned, eaten or given to children.** About $500 a year is cheaper than one claim.
- **Separate money.** Etsy deposits are easier to track in their own account. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian option, and others are compared in [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply to an Etsy shop budget in Canada?

Etsy income is business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both shares of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. Etsy fees, materials, packaging, postage and insurance are generally current expenses. A label printer or light box that lasts several years is equipment claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html).

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting all your side hustles. An Etsy shop is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html). Once registered, use your province's rate from the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) and claim [input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) for GST/HST you paid on business purchases. This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.
