## What offer gets a first pressure washing client in Canada?

A first pressure washing client in Canada usually buys one concrete driveway or a short walk, with cleaner only if the label allows it, and with a time window. "Exterior cleaning" is hard to purchase. "This concrete driveway, wide fan, water kept out of the grate" is a purchase.

Say what is outside the first job. Wood decks, house siding, roofs, and "while you are here, the fence" are later quotes, and some of them are the wrong job entirely. A narrow tip is not a feature you advertise on day one.

Toronto's page on washing cars and outdoor surfaces says wastewater from outdoor washing, including power washing with water alone, must not reach a catch basin, and that the hirer and the mobile wash business are both responsible. If you cannot name a legal path for the water, do not sell the visit.

## What should a first pressure washing note say?

A first pressure washing note in Canada should name the surface, the water rule you will follow, the time window, and the price. It should not promise a deck or a house wash.

**Example message.** This is a generic example you can adapt. It is not a note that produced a client.

> Hello. I am booking one concrete driveway wash: a consumer machine, a wide fan, and cleaner only if the bottle allows that surface. I need a tap and a path for the water that does not go to a catch basin. I do not wash wood decks or siding on a first visit. The price and the time window are written down before I start. I can show you the pad I practised on. Would Saturday morning work, or is another day better?

Honour a no. Do not cut the price because the week was quiet. A frozen forecast is a reason to move the date, not a reason to invent a winter special you cannot perform.

## How do you prove pressure washing skill without fake reviews?

You prove pressure washing skill in Canada by washing concrete you own or have permission to mark, and by being able to say where that water went. A before-and-after from a video you did not shoot is not your portfolio.

Write "Practice on my own pad" on the photo if nobody hired you. If the water had nowhere legal to go, you do not have a sample. You have a reason to wait.

Timing matters. Setup, the cord, and the walk back to the tap are part of the visit. If your own pad took two hours, do not promise a stained double driveway in forty minutes.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first pressure washing client look like?

A 14-day plan for a first pressure washing client in Canada should produce a surface you will sell, a practice wash, a sewer answer, and a few offers. A paid yes is a bonus. Silence is a reason to improve the offer, not a reason to buy a commercial rig.

### Days 1 and 2: Name the surface

Write "concrete driveway" or the walk you mean. Read the city's sewer page. List decks, siding, and roofs as outside the first job.

### Days 3 to 5: Practise where you are allowed to

Wash your own pad or one you have permission to mark. Note the water's path. If it would reach a grate, stop and change the method or decline that kind of job. Ask for a liability quote. This is not insurance advice.

### Days 6 to 9: Ask five people

Offer the package you practised. If you email, follow ISED's consent page. Confirm tap, cord, and the grate before you book.

### Days 10 to 14: Do the job or hold the offer

If someone says yes, stop at the surface you named and invoice the same day. If nobody says yes, do not add a deck. Show the practice photo to people you have not already asked.

## What mistakes block a first pressure washing client in Canada?

Mistakes that block a first pressure washing client in Canada are selling a deck first, hiding the water problem, and pricing from a video shot in another climate. One concrete surface is enough.

A commercial machine does not replace a legal drain. Plain water is not automatically allowed in a storm drain. Toronto's page says power washing with water alone can still move contaminants. A purchased email list skips anti-spam consent.

A free "test driveway" with no cap is a gift. Call it a gift if you choose it. Do not describe it as a client, and do not let it include the siding.

## Which tax rules apply to a first pressure washing client in Canada?
