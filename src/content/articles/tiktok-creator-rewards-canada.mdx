## Does TikTok's Creator Rewards Program include Canada?

No, not on the Creator Academy page checked October 6, 2026. The page says the program is currently open to creators in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, and Brazil. A second line on the same page says a rewarded video must be from where the program is available: the United States, Mexico, Brazil, South Korea, Japan, Germany, the United Kingdom, and France. Canada appears in neither list.

Other rules on that page, for people in the countries that are listed:

- Only Personal Accounts qualify. Business Accounts, and accounts associated with political or government organizations, do not.
- The account should have at least 10,000 followers and 100,000 video views in the last 30 days.
- Rewarded videos are original, high-quality, and over one minute long. The page says a video should also reach at least 1,000 views on the For You feed.
- A Duet, a Stitch, or a Photo Mode post is not the rewarded format the page describes.
- Ads and paid promotions are called out as outside the rewarded set.
- Five account violations make the account permanently ineligible. Copied or low-quality content is ineligible.

Those thresholds are the rules for joining a program that does not list Canada. Meeting 10,000 followers in Canada does not put you on the country list. Do not buy a camera to chase a payout the page does not offer you.

TikTok Shop is a separate product. This guide did not open an official TikTok seller page that lists Canada as a Shop market. Check TikTok's own seller centre before you plan a business around in-app checkout. A product link in a video is not the same thing as a Shop tab.

## How do Canadian TikTok creators actually get paid?

They get paid when a person or a company sends money for a defined piece of work, or when a viewer buys something the creator is allowed to sell. The Creator Rewards page is not that payer.

| Path | Who pays | What has to be true |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand deal on your account | The business, under a written note | Fee, usage, and a disclosure a viewer can see |
| UGC | The brand, for a file it posts | A brief and a fee. Followers are not the product. [UGC pricing](/side-hustles/ugc-pricing-canada/) |
| Affiliate | The merchant, if a tracked sale happens | You may say you earn a commission. A tag alone is a weak disclosure |
| Your own product or service | Your customer | A price and a way to pay you in Canada |
| LIVE Gifts | Viewers, through TikTok, only where the feature exists | TikTok's support article requires you to live where LIVE Gifts are available, be 18 or older, and be eligible to go LIVE. Government and political accounts cannot collect Diamonds. This guide did not extract a country list. If the Gift control is missing, the feature is not on your account |

The Competition Bureau's [influencer page](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says a material connection, including pay or a free product, should be disclosed in plain language where the viewer will see it without an extra tap. Linking or tagging a brand, or dropping an affiliate link, is unlikely to be enough on its own.

## What should you ignore when a view count shows up?

Ignore any plan that turns views into a Canadian paycheque through Creator Rewards. The country list is the first filter. The follower and view minimums are the second filter, and they apply in the countries that are listed. A US creator's dashboard screenshot is not a Canadian rate card. TikTok does not publish a Canadian per-view price on the page this guide opened, and this guide will not invent one.

Also ignore gear purchases made to "qualify." A phone you already have is the camera in [TikTok and Reels startup costs](/side-hustles/tiktok-reels-startup-costs-canada/). A tripod is a steadiness tool, in a retail range you should verify locally, not a ticket into a rewards program.

### Illustrative example

Assumed brand fee, not a TikTok rate and not a Creator Rewards estimate. Suppose a local shop pays $120 for two short clips you film on your phone, and the note allows the shop to post them. Suppose you already own the phone. $120 is the fee. If the two clips take 3 hours including travel, the sketch is $120 ÷ 3 = $40 an hour before tax, only inside these assumptions. Views are not part of the $120 unless the note says they are. A second shop that never replies is $0. Creator Rewards adds $0 in this sketch because Canada is not on the published country list. These totals are assumptions, not a quote or a typical brand rate.

## How do you earn from TikTok in Canada without the rewards program?

### Step 1: Read the country list and write down that in-app rewards are not the plan

Open the [Creator Rewards article](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/creator-rewards-program). If Canada is still absent, stop researching RPM screenshots.

### Step 2: Post clips you can explain in one line

The how-to is [TikTok and Reels](/side-hustles/tiktok-reels-side-hustle-canada/). Original means you filmed it. A borrowed clip is the kind of unoriginal content the rewards page says is ineligible even in countries where the program exists, and it is a poor sample for a brand.

### Step 3: Write a one-page note before any paid ask

Who pays, how many clips, where they may be posted, whether the file can run as an ad, the date, and the fee. No note, no filming. "We'll send product" is not a fee unless you have decided the product is the pay and you can say so.

### Step 4: Put the disclosure where it can be seen

Say that you were paid, or that you received the product, in the video or in the first line. The Competition Bureau's page is the standard. A hashtag pile is a poor place for it.

### Step 5: Offer the same skill as UGC if you do not want to post as yourself

[UGC](/side-hustles/ugc-side-hustle-canada/) is the brief where the brand uses the file. You can do that with a phone and a written fee, without 10,000 followers. Price it in [UGC pricing](/side-hustles/ugc-pricing-canada/).

### Step 6: Check LIVE Gifts inside the app, not in a US article

TikTok's [LIVE Gifts article](https://support.tiktok.com/en/live-gifts-wallet/tiktok-live/live-gifts-on-tiktok) says you must live where the feature is available, be 18 or older (19 in South Korea), be eligible to go LIVE, and keep the account in good standing. It does not, on the page this guide could read, print a country list. If Gifts are not offered in your LIVE settings, they are not a Canadian pay plan. Diamonds are not a dollar figure you can invoice.

### Step 7: Sell something you control if you want income that does not depend on a brand's reply

A service, a digital product, or a newsletter. [YouTube](/side-hustles/youtube-side-hustle-canada/) is the longer public video, with its own partner rules. [Affiliate marketing](/side-hustles/affiliate-marketing-side-hustle-canada/) is the tracked-link version, still with a disclosure.

## What mistakes do Canadian creators make about TikTok pay?

- **Buying gear to hit a follower count for Creator Rewards.** The country list fails first.
- **Treating a US fund screenshot as a rate.** The page you need is the country list, and Canada is not on it.
- **Burying a paid disclosure in hashtags.** The Competition Bureau says the viewer should see it without an extra tap.
- **Planning a shop around TikTok Shop without a seller-centre page that names Canada.** This guide did not find that page. Confirm it yourself before you order inventory.
- **Counting LIVE Diamonds as cash.** The support article describes Diamonds and eligibility. It does not publish a Canadian dollar value for a Gift.

## Which tax rules apply to TikTok income in Canada?

When a business pays you, or a product sells, that is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST on your taxable supplies follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Views are not the test. Unpaid practice clips are not income. A free product can be a different question. Ask a tax adviser if a brand sends goods instead of a fee. This guide does not assign a dollar value to a free product.
