## What are the best side hustles for teachers in Canada?

The best side hustles for teachers in Canada either serve learners who aren't in your class or turn your planning skills into products. The groups below follow that split.

### Tutoring students who aren't yours

[Online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/) is the most direct match. Your first step is a clear subject and grade range; the [first tutoring client guide](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-first-client-canada/) covers how to find families outside your school. Exam periods bring a seasonal bump, covered in [exam season tutoring](/side-hustles/exam-season-winter-tutoring-canada/).

### Teaching English or another language online

If you've taught ESL or French immersion, [teaching English online](/side-hustles/teach-english-online-side-hustle-canada/) and [freelance translation](/side-hustles/freelance-translation-side-hustle-canada/) are options. Bilingual teachers should also read [side hustles for bilingual French-English speakers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-bilingual-french-english-speakers-canada/).

### Turning lesson plans into products

[Printables](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/), [Notion templates](/side-hustles/notion-templates-side-hustle-canada/), and [online courses](/side-hustles/online-course-side-hustle-canada/) take time up front and then sell without you being present. Make these on your own time and equipment. Under section 13(3) of the [Copyright Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-13.html), an employer is the first owner of copyright in work an employee makes in the course of employment unless there's an agreement saying otherwise, so materials you made for your classroom may not be yours to sell. Check your collective agreement and board policy. A design tool like <AffiliateLink program="canva" label="Canva" /> is enough for most worksheets.

### Writing for education publishers and blogs

Teachers can write lesson guides, explainer articles, or parent resources. Start with [freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/) and [ebook publishing](/side-hustles/ebook-publishing-side-hustle-canada/) if you'd rather publish your own.

## What conflict-of-interest rules apply to teachers' side hustles in Canada?

The rules come from your employer, your federation, and your provincial regulator, and they vary by province and board. Ontario is a useful example because the guidance is public:

- **ETFO's tutoring guidelines** say members can legally tutor on their own time, but the Ontario Teachers' Federation policy says a teacher must not tutor their own students for pay and should consult a tutored student's regular teacher ([ETFO](https://www.etfo.ca/about-us/member-advice/prs-matters-bulletins/tutoring-and-conflict-of-interest-guidelines-for-members)).
- **ETFO also advises** finding out whether your board has a tutoring policy and following it, keeping private tutoring off school property and outside the instructional day, and not advertising through school connections, board publications, or the board's network.
- **Ontario's misconduct regulation** lists "practising the profession while the member is in a conflict of interest" as professional misconduct ([O. Reg. 437/97](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/970437)).

Outside Ontario, read your own board's policy and your federation's guidance before you advertise. If you're unsure, ask your federation before taking a client.

## How should teachers price tutoring as a side hustle?

Price per session based on your subject, grade level, and local market, then check it against what you can actually deliver in a week. The [tutoring pricing guide](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-pricing-canada/) walks through the options.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not a market rate: a teacher charges an assumed $50 per hour-long session and takes 4 sessions a week for 30 weeks of the school year.

- Sessions: 4 × 30 = 120
- Revenue: 120 × $50 = $6,000
- If an assumed $300 goes to a platform subscription and materials: $6,000 − $300 = $5,700 net before tax

## What mistakes do teachers make with side hustles?

- **Tutoring a current student for pay.** That breaks the OTF policy ETFO cites, and it's a clear conflict.
- **Advertising through school channels.** Use your own networks instead.
- **Selling classroom materials you made on the job.** Copyright may belong to your employer.
- **Over-committing in September and June.** Plan around report cards.
- **Forgetting the tax side.** Side income isn't covered by your T4 deductions.

## How is a teacher's side hustle taxed in Canada?

Self-employment income is reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), alongside your T4 employment income. Because your teaching salary already uses up the lower tax brackets, set aside money from each payment; [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) explains the method. Tutoring, as a service, follows the normal $30,000 GST/HST small-supplier test ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)); see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). Keep receipts as shown in [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or employment advice.
