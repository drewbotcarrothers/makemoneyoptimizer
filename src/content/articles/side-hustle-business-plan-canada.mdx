A side hustle business plan in Canada can fit on one page: what you sell, who buys it, what you charge, what it costs, how many hours you have, how you'll find clients, which rules apply, and what you want to earn. That's enough to make the decisions that matter in the first year. If you later need a formal plan for a loan or grant, the Government of Canada's [planning a business](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business/start/planning.html) page links to templates, including [BDC's free business plan template](https://www.bdc.ca/en/articles-tools/entrepreneur-toolkit/templates-business-guides/business-plan-template).

## What goes in a one-page side hustle business plan in Canada?

Write one or two lines per box. If a box takes a page, the idea isn't clear yet.

### Step 1: Define the offer

"Weekly dog walks, 30 or 60 minutes, in my neighbourhood, weekdays only." A clear offer makes pricing and marketing easier. The start guides, such as [dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) or [freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/), describe typical offers.

### Step 2: Name the customer

Be specific: "busy households within a 15-minute walk" or "small Ontario businesses that need blog posts." That decides where you'll advertise.

### Step 3: Set a starting price

Use [the pricing worksheet](/side-hustles/side-hustle-pricing-worksheet-canada/) to build a price from costs, time and tax, then check it against what similar local or online sellers advertise.

### Step 4: List startup and per-job costs

Startup costs are one-time purchases; per-job costs repeat. The startup costs guides, such as [house cleaning startup costs](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-startup-costs-canada/), have checklists.

### Step 5: Count your hours

Be honest about evenings and weekends. Include travel, messaging and admin, not just billable time.

### Step 6: Pick two client channels

For local work, pairing one online and one offline channel spreads your reach, such as a [Google Business Profile](/side-hustles/google-business-profile-side-hustle-canada/) plus flyers or referrals. Two channels done well beat six done halfway.

### Step 7: Write down the rules that apply

The CRA describes a business as an activity you intend to carry on for profit with evidence to support that intention ([CRA T4002](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4002.html)), so plan to report the income on Form T2125 from the start. Note whether you need a business name registration ([Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business/start/register-with-gov/register-sole-prop-partner.html)), a city licence, or [insurance](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/), and when the $30,000 GST/HST threshold would apply ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)).

### Step 8: Set money targets and a review date

A monthly revenue goal, a percentage to set aside for tax, and a date three months out to check how it's going.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A one-page plan for a lawn care side hustle might read:

- **Offer:** weekly cuts and trimming, May to October, in two neighbourhoods.
- **Customer:** homeowners within a 10-minute drive.
- **Price:** a set price per visit for a standard lot, built with the pricing worksheet.
- **Costs:** mower, trimmer, fuel, a liability policy.
- **Time:** 10 hours a week, Saturday plus two evenings.
- **Clients:** a Google Business Profile and door-hanger flyers.
- **Rules:** trading under own name, so no registration; report on T2125; GST/HST not expected in year one; check the city's licence list.
- **Targets:** 12 regular clients by July; set aside a fixed share of each payment; review on August 1.

## What mistakes do people make with a side hustle plan?

- **Writing a 20-page plan and never starting.** One page, then act.
- **Skipping the hours box.** Time is often the real limit.
- **Leaving out the rules.** Licences, insurance and GST/HST can change your price.
- **No review date.** A plan you never compare with reality isn't doing anything.
- **Copying a template built for a bank loan** when all you need is a decision tool.

## How does a business plan help with side hustle taxes in Canada?

It puts the tax decisions in writing before the money arrives: report on T2125, keep records from day one, set aside a share of every payment, and watch the GST/HST threshold. For the record-keeping side, see [record-keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).
