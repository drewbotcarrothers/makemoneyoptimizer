## How do you turn post-holiday tree disposal into a side hustle?

Offer to take trees down, bag needles, and deliver trees to the municipal drop-off or curb on collection day. Health Canada advises removing a real tree as soon as the holidays are over or once needles start to fall, since dry trees can be a fire hazard, and notes that most municipalities have tree recycling programs ([holiday safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/home-safety/tips-holiday-safety.html)). Check your municipality's collection dates and rules before you advertise.

### Illustrative example

Assume a flat $30 fee per tree pickup, 25 minutes per pickup including driving, and $3 in fuel and bags per pickup. All figures are assumptions.

- **Per pickup:** $30 − $3 = $27 for 25 minutes, about $64.80 an hour before tax.
- **15 pickups over two weekends:** 15 × $27 = $405 for about 6.25 hours.
- **Bookkeeping comparison:** Job Bank lists the median bookkeeper wage in Canada at $28.02 an hour (2023–2024), a reference point for desk-based January work ([Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/12562/ca)).

## What should you do with holiday side hustle money in January?

Set aside tax first, then decide what to save. If you sold through apps or marketplaces in 2026, platforms that facilitate the sale of goods or personal services may report your earnings to the CRA ([reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html)), so match those totals to your records. ## What mistakes do people make with New Year side hustles?

The common mistakes are starting too late for the date that drives demand and spending holiday money that is owed in tax.

- **Starting tutoring in mid-January.** Families book in December.
- **Advertising tree pickup without checking municipal rules.** Collection dates vary.
- **Waiting until February for Valentine's prep.** January is the build month.
- **Spending tax money.** Set aside tax on December income.
- **Ignoring GST/HST thresholds.** Registration is generally required once taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters ([when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)).

## Which tax rules apply to New Year side hustles in Canada?

Side hustle income is generally business income reported on Form T2125, and expenses such as supplies, fuel for business trips, and software are generally deductible. Self-employed people file by June 15, 2027 but owe any balance by April 30, 2027.
