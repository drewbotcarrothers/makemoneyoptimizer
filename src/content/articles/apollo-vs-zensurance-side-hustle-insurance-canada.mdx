## How do APOLLO, Zensurance and a local broker compare?

APOLLO and Zensurance are both online and Canadian; the main difference is instant self-serve (APOLLO) versus a brokered, more customised policy (Zensurance). A local broker trades speed for in-person advice.

## Which is cheapest for side hustle insurance?

There is no universal cheapest option, because every policy is priced to the applicant. Zensurance's advertised starting price is $11 a month (based on a $125-a-year select professional policy, excluding taxes), and APOLLO's published examples range from $22.64 to $63.99 a month for the professions it listed. Get at least two quotes with the same limits and deductible so you compare like for like.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not a quote: a dog walker pays $35 a month for general liability.

- Annual premium: 12 × $35 = $420.
- If the dog walker charges $25 a walk, that premium equals 420 ÷ 25 = about 17 walks a year.
- The premium is generally a deductible business expense.

## What should you ask before buying side hustle insurance?

- **What exactly is covered?** CGL, E&O, product liability and property are different.
- **What are the limits and deductible?** Match what clients or venues require.
- **Does it cover work at clients' homes or at markets?** Ask about events if you sell at craft fairs.
- **Is my equipment covered away from home?** Home policies often exclude it.
- **How do I get a certificate of insurance?** Venues and commercial clients often ask.
