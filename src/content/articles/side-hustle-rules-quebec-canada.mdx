<QuickAnswer>

- **Registraire des entreprises:** register within 60 days of starting only if your business name does not include your family name and given name. You get a Quebec enterprise number (NEQ).
- **GST and QST together:** both are registered with Revenu Québec once worldwide taxable supplies pass $30,000. The QST rate is 9.975% on top of 5% GST.
- **Two tax returns:** you report business income on the federal return (T2125) and on your Quebec return (TP-1) with form TP-80, and pay QPP and QPIP rather than CPP.
- **Minimum wage:** $16.60 an hour since May 1, 2026, for employees; Quebec has no app-worker minimum like Ontario's or B.C.'s.

</QuickAnswer>

In Quebec, a side hustle follows the same basic pattern as the rest of Canada with three local twists: business names are registered with the Registraire des entreprises, sales taxes (GST and QST) are both administered by Revenu Québec, and you file two income tax returns, reporting your business income on form TP-80 with your Quebec return and paying Quebec Pension Plan contributions. If you trade under your own name and sell less than $30,000 a year, there is usually nothing to register. This guide suits anyone in Quebec, including bilingual freelancers, starting a service, selling goods, or working for an app.

It is one of the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) guides. ## Do you need to register a side hustle with the Registraire des entreprises in Quebec?

Only if your business name does not include your family name and given name. Québec.ca says an individual running a sole proprietorship must register [within 60 days of starting](https://www.quebec.ca/entreprises-et-travailleurs-autonomes/demarrer-entreprise/immatriculer-constituer-entreprise/immatriculer-constituer-entreprise-formes-juridiques/personne-physique-entreprise-individuelle) if the name does not include both. (Tobacco sales points and tanning salons register within 30 days regardless.)

### Step 1: Decide on the name

"Marie Tremblay" needs no registration; "Déneigement ABC" does. The page's own example is a snow removal business name.

### Step 2: File the déclaration d'immatriculation

The Registraire assigns a Quebec enterprise number (NEQ) beginning with "22" to you as a person, and it is reused if you close and later restart. Fees are listed on the Registraire's tariff page when you file.

### Step 3: Register with the CRA separately if you need a BN

The CRA lists Quebec among the places where you register for a business number separately ([When you need a BN](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html)). Many side hustlers never need one; see [how to get a CRA business number](/side-hustles/how-to-get-cra-business-number-canada/).

## When do Quebec side hustlers charge GST and QST?

When worldwide taxable supplies pass $30,000. Revenu Québec says you must [register for the GST and QST](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/registering-for-the-gst-and-qst/) if your total worldwide taxable supplies, with your associates', exceed $30,000 in a calendar quarter or in the four preceding quarters. Below that you are a [small supplier](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/consumption-taxes/taxable-zero-rated-or-tax-exempt-goods-and-services/gst-and-qst/other-situations/small-suppliers/) and are not required to register or collect either tax.

The QST rate is 9.975%, charged in addition to the 5% GST ([rate table](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/basic-rules-for-applying-the-gsthst-and-qst/tables-of-gst-and-qst-rates/)). Passenger transport is the exception: Revenu Québec says a [taxi business](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/sector-specific-measures/taxi-business-operators/) must register for both before transporting its first paying passenger, and the CRA requires [commercial ride-sharing drivers](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) to register for GST regardless of sales.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Montreal translator invoices $2,200 a month. Four quarters total $26,400, under $30,000, so no registration is required. If the translator reaches $2,700 a month, four quarters total $32,400 and both registrations apply. A $500 job is then billed as $500 + $25.00 GST + $49.88 QST = $574.88.

## What changes on your income tax return in Quebec?

You file two returns. Federally, you report business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Revenu Québec says self-employed people [enclose form TP-80-V](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/self-employed-people/information-for-the-self-employed/), Business or Professional Income and Expenses, with their Quebec return (TP-1-V), one per business.

Instead of CPP, self-employed Quebecers pay [QPP contributions and QPIP premiums](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/income-tax-return/paying-a-balance-due-or-receiving-a-refund/paying-contributions-and-premiums/contributions-and-premiums-payable-by-a-self-employed-person-or-a-member-of-a-partnership/) on their business income. Budget for those on top of income tax; [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) explains the approach, though its CPP examples use the rest-of-Canada plan.

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in Quebec?

Not a platform-specific one. The [CNESST](https://www.cnesst.gouv.qc.ca/en/working-conditions/wage-and-pay/wages/minimum-wage) lists a general minimum wage of $16.60 an hour, in effect since May 1, 2026, for employees, and $13.30 for employees receiving tips. Those rates cover employees, such as staff hired for events, not independent contractors on delivery apps. Ontario and B.C. are the provinces with app-worker minimums; see [side hustle rules in Ontario](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-ontario-canada/).

## Which permits do side hustles need in Quebec?

It depends on the activity and the municipality; [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/) lists permits by location. Food is the big one: selling food prepared at home requires a MAPAQ permit, inspection and hygiene training, with limits on volume ([Québec.ca](https://www.quebec.ca/agriculture-environnement-et-ressources-naturelles/agriculture/agriculture-urbaine/reglementation/don-vente-aliments-frais-ou-cuisines-a-la-maison)). ## What are the common Quebec side hustle mistakes?

- **Forgetting the provincial return.** TP-80 is separate from T2125.
- **Budgeting CPP instead of QPP.** Quebec self-employed people pay QPP and QPIP.
- **Registering for GST but not QST.** In Quebec both go through Revenu Québec and the thresholds match.
- **Adding a word to your name and not registering.** "Tremblay Traduction" lacks a given name, so it needs registration.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in Quebec?

Federal income tax on T2125, Quebec income tax on TP-80 and TP-1, QPP and QPIP, and GST and QST once you pass $30,000. Keep receipts and an income log for both returns; [record keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to keep.
