Teaching English online from Canada is a booked conversation or skills lesson for an adult learner: pronunciation, workplace phrases, or everyday speaking, on a video call, for a written length and a written price. Teaching English online suits people who can explain without jargon, who will correct a few things well, and who will not advertise a certificate they do not hold.

The money, when language teaching pays, comes from a repeated lesson a learner can put on a calendar. People who do this well sell one kind of hour. People who stall sell "English for anyone, any age, any exam" and then improvise.

| Fact | Detail for teaching English online from Canada |
| --- | --- |
| Useful first lesson | 45 minutes of adult conversation, with a parent absent because the learner is an adult |
| Who it suits | People who can listen, correct a little, and end on time |
| Starter stack | A computer you have, a headset, and a one-page plan |
| TESL Canada | A professional certificate for adult ESL programs. It does not replace provincial K–12 certification. [Certification FAQ](https://tesl.ca/certification/tesl-canada-professional-certification/certification-faq) |
| GST/HST | Small-supplier threshold of $30,000. [When to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) |
| Income record | [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with the T1 |

Help with a school subject, rather than language lessons, is [online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/). A written explanation the learner can keep is [freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/). A public video is [YouTube](/side-hustles/youtube-side-hustle-canada/), which does not replace a booked hour.

## Who does teaching English online from Canada suit?

Teaching English online from Canada suits people who can run a short lesson without filling every silence, and who already speak the language they are teaching clearly enough to model it. Newcomers, workplace English, and conversation practice are concrete offers. Teaching English online is a poor fit if you need income that does not depend on someone showing up, if you will teach children without a parent in the plan, or if you will promise an immigration outcome.

A marketplace can introduce learners and will publish its own fee and payout currency. Read that page before you rely on a cut you heard second-hand. A direct lesson with an adult you can actually schedule has no marketplace cut, and you still need a written price.

## What credentials apply to teaching English in Canada?

Credentials for teaching English in Canada depend on the room, and a private adult conversation is not the same thing as a public-school job. TESL Canada's [certification FAQ](https://tesl.ca/certification/tesl-canada-professional-certification/certification-faq) says TESL Canada professional certification is intended for ESL or EFL instructors teaching in adult learning environments, and that provincial teachers' colleges and provincial regulatory bodies govern the certification of K–12 teachers.

TESL Canada's [professional certification page](https://tesl.ca/certification/tesl-canada-professional-certification/35-tesl-canada-professional-certification.html) says a TESL Canada professional certificate does not replace provincial professional certificates where they exist. Some employers and language schools require a certificate. Do not advertise "TESL certified" or "certified teacher" unless you hold the credential you name.

In Ontario, the [Ontario College of Teachers](https://www.oct.ca/) regulates teaching in the province's publicly funded schools. Other provinces have their own teacher regulators. Read that body before you work inside a school or use a teacher's title. This is not legal advice.

The RCMP's page on [vulnerable sector checks](https://rcmp.ca/en/criminal-records/criminal-record-checks/vulnerable-sector-checks) says there is no federal legislation that requires any organization to conduct those checks, and that the hiring organization makes the request for a position of trust or authority over children or vulnerable persons. A solo tutor may not have that organization. Prefer adult learners for the first offer. If the learner is under 18, a parent books the lesson and is present, and you read the RCMP page and your province's rules before you advertise children's lessons.

| | Adult conversation | K–12 classroom or a language-school job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First month | A plan, a price, a calendar | Read the employer's certificate rule |
| TESL Canada | Optional professional recognition, not a word you invent | May be required by that employer |
| Minors | Not the starter offer | A trust situation. Read the RCMP page |

## How much can teaching English online earn in Canada?

Teaching English online from Canada cannot be promised as an hourly rate, because a 45-minute conversation and a packaged exam course are different products, and a marketplace cut is not yours to guess. What you keep depends on the list below, and this guide does not publish a national teaching rate.

- Whether the learner is a direct client or a marketplace profile
- The marketplace fee, on that platform's page, if you use one
- Cancellations and the notice you wrote down
- Prep time you forgot to count
- No-shows
- Lessons you extended for free
- A promise about an exam or a visa that you cannot keep

Price the lesson. A rate copied from another country ignores your prep and your payout currency.

## How do you start teaching English online in Canada?

Starting English teaching online from Canada means writing one adult lesson and teaching it to a person who can give you a real reaction. The steps below are that sequence.

### Step 1: Name the learner and the hour

Adult conversation, 45 minutes, one topic such as work small talk or a phone call with a landlord. Children, exam guarantees, and immigration advice stay off the page.

### Step 2: Write the plan

A goal, a short dialogue you wrote yourself, three corrections you will actually make, and a last five minutes where the learner speaks more than you do. Do not photocopy a textbook.

### Step 3: Read the credential pages before you write a bio

TESL Canada's FAQ, and your province's teacher regulator if you are tempted to say "teacher." In Ontario, that regulator's public site is the [Ontario College of Teachers](https://www.oct.ca/). Use only words you can support.

### Step 4: Teach the plan to a friend

Record yourself if they agree. Listen for how long you talk. Cut the plan until the learner has room.

### Step 5: Write the agreement

Length, price, how to pay, what happens if someone cancels, and a line that you do not promise exam scores, jobs, or immigration results. If you will store a learner's name and email, read the OPC's [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/) page. Collect less than you think you need.

### Step 6: Offer the lesson to five adults

People who have asked for English practice, a workplace, a community board that allows the post. Say the price. Do not say the first month is free and unlimited.

### Step 7: Run one paid lesson on time

Headset on, plan open, camera where the learner can see your mouth if pronunciation is the point. End at 45 minutes. Send a three-line recap and the invoice.

### Step 8: Note what you will correct next time

One pattern, not twenty. The learner should leave with a sentence they can repeat, not a list of every error.

### Step 9: After four paid lessons, keep the offer or narrow it

If prep is eating the hour, shorten the plan. If a marketplace is how you found them, put that fee next to the $30 assumption and decide if the direct lesson is the better path.

## What should the first week of teaching English online in Canada include?

The first week of teaching English online from Canada should produce a plan, a practice run, and five offers to adults.

- **Day 1:** Write the learner sentence. Read the TESL Canada FAQ so your bio stays accurate.
- **Day 2:** Draft the 45-minute plan in your own words.
- **Day 3:** Teach it to a friend. Time the silence.
- **Day 4:** Write the agreement, including cancellation and the limits on promises.
- **Day 5:** Buy a headset only if the practice call was hard to hear. Otherwise wait.
- **Day 6:** Offer the lesson to five adults.
- **Day 7:** If someone books, confirm the link and the price by text. If the request is for a child, stop and read the RCMP vulnerable-sector page before you say yes.

## What is easy to miss in an online English lesson in Canada?

Online English lessons go wrong when the teacher talks for 40 of the 45 minutes, when the example uses idioms the learner cannot use on Monday, and when the headset is so poor the correction is a guess. Leave space. Use a sentence from the learner's actual week, with details they are willing to share. Test the audio before the paid minute starts.

Ask where the learner needs the English, and do not record them unless they agreed.

## What teaching English online mistakes are common in Canada?

Common mistakes in teaching English online from Canada are advertising a certificate you do not hold, teaching minors with no parent and no reading of the vulnerable-sector rules, and copying a coursebook into the plan. TESL Canada's FAQ is explicit that its certificate is for adult environments and that K–12 certification is provincial. Use the honest sentence.

A marketplace profile with no plan is a wish. The plan comes first. The profile, if you use one, repeats the same adult offer and the platform's actual fee.

Promising a language test score, a visa, or a job is outside a conversation lesson. Leave it out of the agreement and out of the bio.

## Which tax rules apply to teaching English online in Canada?

English-teaching income in Canada is self-employment income, including a deposit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST for this work follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Some educational services are treated differently for GST/HST than a private conversation. Do not assume a side lesson is exempt because a school course might be. Read the CRA pages for your exact supply, or ask a tax adviser, before you tell a learner the hour is tax-free. Keep the agreements and the e-transfers. Report the income on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add tutoring and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Language teaching is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Confirm Quebec and every other province on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside.

A marketplace may pay you from outside Canada. Your record of the payout still belongs on the T2125. Search your city for a business licence if you advertise locally. This guide does not state a licence fee.
