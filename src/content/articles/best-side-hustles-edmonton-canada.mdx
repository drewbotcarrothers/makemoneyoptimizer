## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Edmonton, Canada?

### Step 1: Clear residential sidewalks and driveways

The City's [residential sidewalk page](https://www.edmonton.ca/transportation/on_your_streets/sidewalk-snow-removal) says it doesn't have the resources to clear sidewalks in most residential areas. It expects snow and ice cleared to the cement or asphalt for the full width and length of the sidewalk. [Bylaw 14600](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/documents/Bylaws/C14600.pdf) says a person shall keep any sidewalk next to land they own or occupy clear of all snow and ice. The City notes the bylaw doesn't set a timeframe, so agree a response time with each client, such as by the next morning. Read the [snow removal guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/), [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/), and [snow removal tools](/side-hustles/snow-removal-tools-canada/).

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Edmonton, Canada?

Yes, if you run it from home. The City's [home-based business page](https://www.edmonton.ca/business_economy/licences_permits/home-based-business) says home-based businesses must have a business licence and may need development and building permits. Businesses that are more visible, such as those with customers coming to the home, need a development permit. You can apply for both together. The rules that apply depend on your business licence categories. A trade name is a provincial step, covered in [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you clear sidewalks and driveways for six homes on your block. You'd apply for a home-based business licence, since your gear and records are at home. Your written terms would say what "clear" means: the full width of the sidewalk down to the concrete, matching the City's expectation. They'd also say when you'll arrive after a snowfall. In summer, the same clients might want lawn care.

## What mistakes do Edmonton side hustlers make?

- **Skipping the licence.** Home-based businesses in Edmonton need one.
- **Leaving a narrow path.** The City expects the full sidewalk cleared to the surface.
- **Promising a deadline the bylaw doesn't set.** Agree your own response time and stick to it.
- **Ignoring development permits.** Clients coming to your home can trigger one.

## How are Edmonton side hustles taxed?

Alberta has no provincial sales tax. You report self-employment income on Form T2125 and register for 5% GST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from their first fare.
