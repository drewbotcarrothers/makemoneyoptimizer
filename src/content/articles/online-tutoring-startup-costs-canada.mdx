## How much does online tutoring cost to start in Canada?

Online tutoring in Canada costs $0 with a computer and a free video tool, $39.55 with a USB headset, $167.21 with a webcam, pen tablet and stand, $567.21 with a starting tutor insurance policy, or $1,065.98 with a document camera and a year of invoicing software. Goods and software include 13% Ontario HST; insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate.

| Kit | What's in it | Total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $0 start | Computer you own, Google Meet or Zoom's free plan, or a platform's built-in classroom | $0 |
| Lean | Logitech H390 USB headset ($35.00) | $39.55 with HST |
| Standard | Lean + Logitech Brio 100 1080p webcam ($52.99) + Huion HS64 pen tablet ($39.99) + ivoler aluminum laptop stand ($19.99) | $167.21 with HST |
| Standard with insurance | Standard + professional liability at Zensurance's starting tutor estimate (about $400/year) | about $567.21 |
| Pro | Standard with insurance + INSWAN INS-1 document camera ($129.39) + FreshBooks Lite for 12 months ($26/month, $312) | $1,065.98 |

A police check, if a family or platform asks for one, is extra; see below. The pro kit's software is spread across a year.

## Who should use an online tutoring startup budget in Canada?

An online tutoring budget fits people who can explain one subject to one kind of learner and keep a short note for the parent who wasn't on the call. The lean kit fits a first student on a platform with its own classroom. The standard kit fits math, science or language tutors who need to write as they talk. The pro kit fits someone with several regular families, working through printed worksheets under a document camera.

It's a poor fit if you'll promise grades, if you want to do a student's assignments, or if the budget includes a "get students" course before you've taught a single paid hour.

A defined document instead of a lesson is [freelance writing startup costs](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-startup-costs-canada/). Recorded lessons for an audience are [YouTube startup costs](/side-hustles/youtube-startup-costs-canada/). Admin for a busy tutor is [virtual assistant startup costs](/side-hustles/virtual-assistant-startup-costs-canada/).

### A first-month sketch

**Illustrative example, not a forecast or a survey of tutoring rates.** Suppose two families book you directly at an assumed $40 an hour, once a week each, for four weeks: $320. You bought the standard kit for $167.21. Remainder: $320 − $167.21 = $152.79 before tax. Add 15 minutes of prep and notes per lesson, so 10 hours in the month: about $15.28 an hour in month one, then $32 an hour from month two. A cancelled lesson without a written policy is unpaid. Don't copy $40.

## What are the ongoing costs?

- **Platform commissions,** if you teach through one; trial lessons on Preply pay you nothing.
- **Insurance,** from about $400 a year once you carry it.
- **Software,** such as FreshBooks from $26 a month, if you pay for it.
- **Prep time** is unpaid unless your price includes it.
- **Cancellations.** Write a 24-hour policy before the first booking.

Keep prepaid packs apart from household money. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can hold unearned lessons until they're taught.

## Where can online tutoring startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Use a platform's built-in classroom** or a free video tool.
- **Test the laptop camera and mic** before buying a webcam or headset.
- **Write on paper and hold it up** before buying a tablet or document camera.
- **Skip paid "get students" courses.** The provincial curriculum is the subject.

## Where should online tutoring spending not be cut in Canada?

Don't cut clear audio, a written cancellation policy, or a police check when a family or platform asks for one. Don't record a minor without written consent from a parent. Don't share a student's work or details outside the lesson.

## Which tax rules apply to an online tutoring budget in Canada?

Online tutoring income in Canada is self-employment income, including a prepaid pack, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. A headset and a month of Zoom are generally current expenses. A computer or a tablet that lasts for years is usually [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. GST/HST for online tutoring uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the booking messages, the e-transfers, and the session log. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add writing and any other side hustle to the same pot. Online tutoring is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

If you are registered, GST/HST paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) to the extent of commercial use. You generally need to be a registrant when the tax was paid or became payable, and you need receipts that show the tax. If you are not registered, do not add GST/HST to the session fee and do not claim the credit. Confirm the current rules with the CRA or a tax professional.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Quebec charges GST and QST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) and set the tax aside. A platform payout report does not replace that record.
