## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Victoria, Canada?

### Step 1: Run a regional yard and garden route

[Lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) and garden cleanup are easy to sell door to door and to group into routes. Victoria's [starting a business page](https://www.victoria.ca/building-business/business-hub/starting-business) says mobile businesses may be eligible for the Inter-Municipal Business Licence. It covers the 13 municipalities in the Capital Regional District at no extra cost over the base City licence. [Lawn care pricing](/side-hustles/lawn-care-pricing-canada/) and the [lawn care 30-day plan](/side-hustles/lawn-care-30-day-plan-canada/) show how to quote and build a route.

### Step 2: Add exterior cleaning

[Window cleaning](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) and [pressure washing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-side-hustle-canada/) pair well with yard work on the same visit. Check runoff and water rules in each municipality before washing driveways or decks. [Pressure washing pricing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-pricing-canada/) covers quoting by surface.

### Step 3: Be on call for rare snow

The City's [snow clearing page](https://www.victoria.ca/getting-around/road-maintenance/snow-clearing) says its Streets and Traffic Bylaw requires businesses and residents to clear the sidewalk in front of their property by 10 a.m. after a snowfall. Missing it can mean a $125 fine per ticketed offence. Offer per-visit clearing to existing yard clients, especially those who travel. The [snow removal guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) covers gear basics.

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Victoria, Canada?

Yes. The City says all businesses must obtain a business licence, and home-based businesses must be licensed in the municipality where they're located. If you work across the region, ask for the Inter-Municipal Business Licence. For other Vancouver Island communities, the Inter-Community Business Licence costs $170 on top of the City licence. A business name other than your own is registered with B.C., as covered in [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you live in Victoria and have yard clients in Victoria, Saanich and Oak Bay. You'd get a City of Victoria business licence and ask for the Inter-Municipal option. Then one licence covers all three. Your route would group clients by day to cut driving. Window cleaning would be an add-on, and you'd offer snow clearing only to route clients on the rare snow day.

## What mistakes do Victoria side hustlers make?

- **Working without a licence.** All Victoria businesses need one.
- **Buying separate licences for each municipality.** The Inter-Municipal licence covers the region at no extra cost.
- **Washing debris into storm drains.** Check each municipality's rules first.
- **Promising snow clearing to everyone.** Keep it to a short list you can reach by 10 a.m.

## How are Victoria side hustles taxed?

You report self-employment income on Form T2125 and register for 5% GST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from the first fare. B.C.'s 7% PST is separate. [Bulletin PST 003](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/taxes/sales-taxes/publications/pst-003-small-sellers.pdf) explains the small seller exemption for those with $10,000 or less in eligible sales.
