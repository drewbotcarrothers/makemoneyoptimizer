Private tutors in Canada charge about $25 to $80 an hour in 2026, and certified teachers $60 to $100 or more. Toronto families commonly pay $40 to $60 for standard subjects. Ontario math tutoring runs $40 to $80 with a Toronto average around $56, and Calgary high school math runs $45 to $70. Tutoring companies charge $60 to $100. Employed tutors earn a $20 median. ## Which pricing models work for online tutoring?

Charge per lesson, sell prepaid packages to weekly students, and price small groups per student.

| Model | How it works | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per lesson | 60-minute lesson at a set rate | Ontario independent tutors: $40–$80 |
| Frequency discount | Lower rate for 2+ sessions a week | Winny Math: $40 solo, $35 for 2+ a week |
| Prepaid package | 8 or 10 lessons paid up front | Tutor Doctor: lower rate for pre-purchased hours |
| Small group | Each student pays a lower rate | Winny Math: $25 per student; The Math Guru: about $55.50 |
| Premium subject | Higher rate for senior math, sciences, exam prep | Calgary high school math: $45–$70 |
| Platform | You set a rate; the platform takes a commission | Preply: 33% falling to 18% |

On Preply, the [commission model](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4171383-preply-commission-model) says the trial lesson with each new student carries a 100% commission, and later lessons carry 33% for new tutors, falling to 28% after 20 hours and to as low as 18% with more teaching hours. Preply's own example: a $10 USD rate leaves a new tutor $6.70 USD per regular lesson.

## How do you calculate an online tutoring rate?

Build an hourly rate for your working time, then multiply by the lesson plus its prep and messages. On a platform, gross up for the commission.

```text
Working-hour rate = target pay + CPP + tools per hour
Lesson price      = working-hour rate × (lesson minutes + prep minutes) ÷ 60
Platform price    = what you want to receive ÷ (1 − commission rate)
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $34/hour (between Job Bank's $31.50 national and $35.00 Ontario high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $4.05 |
| Whiteboard app, tablet, subscriptions per hour | $1.00 |
| **Working-hour rate** | $34 + $4.05 + $1 = **$39.05** |

**Illustrative rate sheet** from that working-hour rate:

| Lesson | Assumed prep and messages | Calculation | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Elementary, 60 minutes | 15 min | $39.05 × 75 ÷ 60 = $48.81 | **$50** |
| Grades 9–10, 60 minutes | 25 min | $39.05 × 85 ÷ 60 = $55.32 | **$55** |
| Grades 11–12 math or sciences, 60 minutes | 35 min | $39.05 × 95 ÷ 60 = $61.82 | **$60** |
| 10-lesson package, Grades 9–10 | — | 10 × $55 = $550, less 5% | **$525** |
| Small group of 3, 60 minutes | 35 min | $61.82 ÷ 3 = $20.61, priced up for group admin | **$25 per student** ($75 a lesson) |
| Cancellation under 24 hours | — | — | Full lesson charge |

These sit inside Profify's $40 to $60 Toronto band for standard subjects and below TutorBright's $67.

## How should you write a tutoring rate sheet and invoice?

Give families one page with rates, packages, the cancellation rule and what tax applies.

```text
[YOUR NAME] ONLINE TUTORING — 2026–27 RATES
60-minute lessons on [Zoom / Google Meet] with a shared whiteboard
  Elementary ............................ $50
  Grades 9–10 ........................... $55
  Grades 11–12 math and sciences ........ $60
10-lesson package (Grades 9–10) ......... $525, valid 16 weeks
```

## How do you raise online tutoring prices?

Raise prices at natural breaks, September and January, when your weekly slots are full, or when you add a credential or specialty.

1. **Price by level.** Profify, TutorLyft and TutorOne all show senior and specialty lessons priced higher.
2. **Add groups when you're full.** Three students at $25 pays $75 for one hour.
3. **Use packages, not discounts.** Tutor Doctor lowers the rate only for hours bought up front.
4. **Count credentials.** Certified teachers charge $60 to $100+ in Toronto.
5. **Keep current packages at the old price.** Move families to the new rate when a package ends.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], from September my Grade 11–12 lessons move from $[60] to
$[65], and the 10-lesson package to $[620]. Lessons in [student]'s
current package stay at the old rate. — [Your name]
```

## What online tutoring pricing mistakes are common?

- **Ignoring prep.** Twenty-five minutes of prep turns $55 into $39 a working hour.
- **Forgetting the platform commission.** A new Preply tutor keeps 67% and nothing from trial lessons.
- **No cancellation rule.** Write 24 hours and charge for no-shows.
- **Promising grades.** Describe the work, not the result.
- **Group rates without group limits.** Same grade and level, three students at most.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect online tutoring prices?

Online tutoring income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. Tutoring has its own GST/HST rule: the CRA's [GST/HST Memorandum 20-6, Tutoring and Equivalent Services](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/20-6/tutoring-equivalent-services.html) explains when tutoring individuals in a course that follows an elementary or secondary school curriculum is an exempt supply, which means no GST/HST is charged on it.

Tutoring outside the exemption, such as general coaching, follows the usual [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) and the rates on the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). Read the memorandum before you add tax to a quote. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration, and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to hold back.

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
