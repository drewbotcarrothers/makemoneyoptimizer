- **What it is:** letting someone store belongings in a garage, shed, or covered driveway you control, for a fee.
- **Platforms:** Shared Storage's site says it lists spaces in Ottawa and Toronto. Neighbor's app listing describes the United States. A parking app is not automatically a storage app.

Renting out storage space in Canada means charging a neighbour to keep boxes, seasonal gear, or a vehicle in a garage, shed, or driveway you are allowed to offer. It suits someone who has dry, lockable space and can meet the renter for access. It is a poor fit if the space is damp, if you cannot control who enters, or if you need the garage back on a day's notice for your own car.

A stall that is only for parking, with no stored goods, is [renting out a parking space](/side-hustles/rent-out-parking-space-canada/). Turning the arrangement into a room someone sleeps in is [renting out a room or basement suite](/side-hustles/rent-out-room-basement-suite-canada/), which has tenancy rules storage does not.

## Which storage platforms operate in Canada?

Check the company's own site before you send photos of your garage.

| Shared Storage | Its homepage says it is a peer-to-peer marketplace for unused space and that Ottawa and Toronto are live, with host and renter fees stated on that page. | Read the live fee and insurance text. | Neighbor | The Canadian App Store listing describes hosts across all 50 U.S. states. | Not a confirmed Canadian storage option. |
| Classifieds | Kijiji and Facebook Marketplace are available in Canada as ad sites. | You screen the person, you write the terms, and you handle payment. |

This guide does not repeat Shared Storage's fee percentages. They are on the company's page and can change. Traditional self-storage companies (a Public Storage or a local mini-storage) rent their own units. They are competitors, not a way to list your shed, unless one of them runs a host program you have read.

## What should you decide before you store someone else's goods?

Decide access, banned items, and insurance before you name a price.

**Access.** A lock you control, or two locks, beats handing over the only key to the house. A camera that films your own property is a record if something goes missing. <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="A lock and a simple indoor camera" search="weatherproof padlock" /> are ordinary gear. Tell the renter about a camera. Do not film a neighbour's yard.

**What you will not store.** Fuel, propane, paint thinner, firearms, and anything that smells like a business inventory you did not agree to. A "few boxes" that becomes a mechanic's shop in your garage is a different risk, and it can breach zoning or a condo rule. If the volume looks like a business, say no.

**Insurance.** Ask your home or tenant insurer whether a paying storage renter is covered, and whether their goods are covered if a pipe bursts. Their goods are usually their problem, and your liability if someone is injured on the way to the garage is yours to ask about. Get the answer from the insurer. This is not insurance advice.

**The building.** A condo or strata locker may not be yours to rent. Use the same permission check as [parking rental](/side-hustles/rent-out-parking-space-canada/). A municipal home-occupation rule can apply if the garage becomes a steady storage business. Search your city and [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/) before you take more than one renter.

## How do you rent out a garage or shed in Canada?

### Step 1: Measure and photograph the empty space

Height, width, and whether a car still fits. Damp floors and an unheated shed should be in the ad. Winter in most of Canada freezes liquids and grows mould when the weather warms.

### Step 2: Write a short agreement

Names, what may be stored, the monthly amount, how payment works, notice to end, and that you may refuse hazardous goods. A handshake is how disputes start. Keep a copy with your [records](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 3: Screen the renter

Meet at the space. Look at what they want to move in. Refuse anything you would not want a firefighter to find. Offers that pressure you to pay a "placement company" to find renters belong with the warning signs in [side hustle scams](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/).

### Step 4: Take payment for the period in advance

E-transfer with a note of the month. Do not give unsupervised access before the first payment is in your account.

### Step 5: Walk the space when the agreement ends

Photograph the empty garage the day they leave. Return any deposit you agreed, on the terms you wrote. Do not keep a deposit you never described.

## What can storage rent look like in a year?

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, for the arithmetic only.

Suppose you charge $80 a month for half a dry garage and the renter stays 10 months. Gross is $800. Suppose you buy a lock for $25 and your insurer makes no premium change. Cash left is $775 before tax and before the time you spent meeting them. Suppose instead the insurer adds $120 for the year. Then $800 − $25 − $120 = $655. If the space is only worth it when you ignore the $120, ask the insurer before you advertise. There is no national "going rate" in this guide.

## What mistakes do storage hosts in Canada make?

- **Listing on a U.S. storage app.** Neighbor's own listing describes U.S. states. Confirm Canada on the company's site.
- **Storing goods you have not seen.** A sealed truck is a reason to say no.
- **Sharing the house key.** Access should stop at the garage.
- **Promising climate control you do not have.** An unheated shed is an unheated shed.
- **Calling it passive.** You still coordinate move-in, payment, and the day you need the space back.

## Which tax rules apply to storage rent in Canada?

Renting space and providing only basic services is the pattern the CRA describes as income from property in [Guide T4036](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4036/rental-income.html), reported on Form T776. The [rental or business](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/rental-income/you-have-rental-income-business-income.html) page says extra services increase the chance the activity is a business on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Moving help, packing, and a staffed facility are services. A locked garage and a monthly e-transfer are closer to renting space. GST/HST still depends on whether the supply is taxable and on the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
