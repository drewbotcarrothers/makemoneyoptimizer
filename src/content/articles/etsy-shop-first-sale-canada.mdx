A first Etsy sale in Canada usually comes from a small set of listings for one kind of item that a buyer can search for by name, photographed honestly, priced after Etsy's fees, and shipped on a promised date. Traffic is not guaranteed, so the work in the first two weeks is listings a stranger can trust, not volume.

Etsy charges per listing and per order, so the first sale has a cost you can calculate before it happens. ## What should you list for a first Etsy sale in Canada?

List one kind of item for a first Etsy sale in Canada: a product family you can make again, describe exactly, and ship in a known box. Ten variations of one thing teach you more than ten unrelated items, because each listing answers the same buyer question.

Write titles in the words a buyer would type, such as the item, the material, and the size, rather than a brand name nobody searches for yet. State dimensions, materials, care, and processing time in the description. A buyer who receives what the photos showed is less likely to open a case.

Each listing costs $0.20 USD, about $0.28 CAD at the Bank of Canada rate of 1.4246 on 2 October 2026 ([daily exchange rates](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/)). Etsy's [fees page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) says a listing expires after four months. Ten listings is a small, known cost. A hundred hurried listings is a larger one, with weaker photos.

## Where do Canadians find a first Etsy buyer?

Canadians find a first Etsy buyer mostly through Etsy search, plus people who already know their work and are given a direct link. Etsy also runs Offsite Ads, and its fees page lists a 15% Offsite Ads fee on attributed orders for shops under $10,000 in sales (12% at or above), capped at $100 per order. That fee is a cost of a sale, not a guarantee of one.

Share the shop link with people who asked about your work. Do not post the link into groups whose rules forbid selling, and do not message strangers in bulk. If you send commercial email to people who did not ask for it, Canada's anti-spam rules apply, which is covered in the sibling service guides.

## What should a first Etsy listing say?

A first Etsy listing in Canada should say what the item is, its exact size and material, how long it takes to make and ship, and what happens if it arrives damaged. Plain facts beat adjectives.

**Example listing text.** This is a generic example. It is not a listing that produced a sale.

> Hand-poured soy candle in a 250 mL amber glass jar, unscented. Burn time not stated because it varies with wick trimming and room conditions. Made to order and shipped within 3 business days from Ontario in a padded box. If the jar arrives broken, send a photo within 7 days and I will replace it.

Avoid claims you cannot support, such as "non-toxic" or "best-selling." The Competition Bureau's page on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) covers representations made to the public to promote a product.

## How do you build trust on Etsy without fake reviews?

You build trust on a new Etsy shop with clear photos in daylight, a photo with a common object for scale, a written shop policy, and a fast reply to the first message. Reviews come from real orders. Buying your own items through a friend's account, or trading reviews, is a false signal and a shop risk.

Show flaws. A handmade item with a visible seam should show the seam. Answer questions inside Etsy messages so the record stays on the order.

## How should you price the first Etsy sale in Canada?

Price the first Etsy sale in Canada by adding materials, packaging, the real shipping label, and Etsy's fees, then deciding what is left for your time. This guide does not state a typical Etsy price for any product.

| Path | What the buyer sees | What you watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free shipping built into price | One price | The 6.5% transaction fee still applies to the total |
| Shipping charged separately | Item price plus shipping | Buyers comparing the total with other shops |
| Personalised item | A higher price and longer processing time | Mistakes in names and a no-return policy written clearly |

### Illustrative example

Suppose a buyer in Canada orders a $30 item with $10 shipping, a $40 order, and ignore sales tax for simplicity. Etsy's processing fee is charged on the total including shipping and tax, so tax would raise it slightly.

- Transaction fee: 6.5% × $40 = $2.60
- Payment processing: 3% × $40 + $0.25 = $1.45
- Regulatory operating fee: 0.50% × $40 = $0.20 (assumed here on the same $40 total; confirm the base on Etsy's page)
- Listing fee: $0.28 CAD
- Fees total: $2.60 + $1.45 + $0.20 + $0.28 = $4.53

Suppose materials and packaging are $8 and the label costs $10. What is left is $40 − $4.53 − $8 − $10 = $17.47 before your time and tax. If that sale came through Offsite Ads, add 15% × $40 = $6.00, which leaves $11.47. These figures are assumptions, not a quote, a forecast, or a typical result.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first Etsy sale look like?

A 14-day plan for a first Etsy sale in Canada should produce ten honest listings in one product family, a written policy, and a priced shipping method. A sale inside 14 days is not guaranteed.

### Days 1 and 2: Read the fee pages

Read Etsy's [fees and taxes](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy), [payment processing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy), and [regulatory operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) pages. Put the percentages in a spreadsheet.

### Days 3 to 5: Make and photograph

Make or gather stock for one product family. Photograph each item in daylight, with a scale shot and a flaw shot.

### Days 6 to 9: List and price

Write ten listings. Weigh one packed parcel and price shipping from the real label. Run the fee arithmetic on each price.

### Days 10 to 14: Share and answer

Send the link to people who asked. Answer every message the same day. If nothing sells, rewrite titles in buyer words before adding more listings.

## What mistakes block a first Etsy sale in Canada?

Mistakes that block a first Etsy sale in Canada are pricing before adding fees, vague titles, flattering photos that hide flaws, and long processing times nobody wrote down. Each one either loses the buyer or loses the margin.

For the fee, GST/HST, and product rule mistakes Etsy sellers make most, and how to fix them, see the [Etsy shop mistakes guide](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-side-hustle-canada/#what-mistakes-do-canadian-etsy-sellers-make).

Listing far more items than you can photograph well adds listing fees without adding trust. Forgetting that the transaction fee applies to shipping makes free-shipping prices too low. Claims such as "eco-friendly" without support invite a complaint.

## Which tax rules apply to a first Etsy sale in Canada?

Etsy shop income in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on the activity for profit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the order export, the fee statement, material receipts, and shipping labels. Report the activity on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Selling on Etsy is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made, unless a platform rule you have read says the platform collects. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). For a set-aside habit, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle in Canada](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
