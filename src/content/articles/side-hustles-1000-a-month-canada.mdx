<QuickAnswer>

- **$1,000 is a target you reverse, not a typical result.** Divide it by a price you set, or by a wage floor a government page actually publishes, then subtract costs. This page does not survey what Canadians earn.
- **An Ontario gig floor is not $1,000 in your pocket.** The general minimum wage is $17.95 an hour from October 1, 2026, and it applies to work assignments, not to time spent waiting for an offer.
- **Kilometres eat that floor.** The CRA's 2026 prescribed allowance is 73 cents per kilometre for the first 5,000 km in a province. Self-employed drivers do not deduct that rate. The sketches use it only as a labelled cost stand-in.
- **A price you control reaches the target in fewer hours** than a floor you do not control, if the visits actually book. How to price them is in the hustle's own pricing guide.

</QuickAnswer>

A side hustle reaches $1,000 a month in Canada when price times units, minus the costs you actually pay, covers $1,000. That is arithmetic, not a promise that the units will sell. It suits someone who will write down a rate, a kilometre count, and the hours, and who will stop if the bookings are not there. It does not suit someone who wants a single "best" hustle with a national average attached. This page does not have that average.

How the ideas compare on cash and season, without income ranks, is [best side hustles in Canada](/side-hustles/best-side-hustles-canada/). The gig worksheet that turns one shift into an hourly figure is [your real hourly rate on gig apps](/side-hustles/gig-app-real-hourly-rate-canada/). Pitches that skip this arithmetic are [overhyped side hustles](/side-hustles/overhyped-side-hustles-canada/).

## What does $1,000 a month require from a side hustle in Canada?

$1,000 a month requires enough paid units that the remainder after costs is $1,000. Gross deposits are the wrong number. Tax and Canada Pension Plan contributions come off self-employment net as well. Self-employed people pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution. How much to hold back is [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/). The sketches below stop at costs you can point at. They are not take-home pay.

| Path | Input this page can cite | What is still an assumption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario app work | $17.95/hour general minimum wage on work assignments, tips excluded | How many kilometres you drive per hour, and your waiting time |
| A stated app range | Amazon Flex's "most drivers earn $22–$27 an hour" | Your real block, your kilometres, and time outside the block |
| Employed delivery benchmark | Job Bank median $20.00 an hour for food delivery drivers, 2023–2024 data | Not an app payout, and not your net |
| A service you price | None. You set the visit price | The price, the hours, and whether clients book |

## How many gig hours does $1,000 take in Ontario after a per-km cost?

In Ontario, $1,000 from gig work takes more engaged hours once a per-kilometre cost is subtracted, and the legal floor does not pay you for waiting. Ontario's [digital platform workers page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers) says workers must be paid at least the general minimum wage in the Employment Standards Act, 2000, determined for each work assignment or on a pay period basis, tips excluded. A work assignment generally starts when you accept it. Travel before it starts and after it ends is not part of the assignment. The [minimum wage page](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage) lists the general rate at $17.95 an hour from October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027.

The CRA's [automobile allowance page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/benefits-allowances/automobile/automobile-motor-vehicle-allowances.html) lists the 2026 prescribed rate at 73 cents per kilometre for the first 5,000 kilometres in a province, and 67 cents after that. The territories are 77 cents, then 71 cents. Finance Canada's [2026 announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/01/government-announces-the-2026-automobile-deduction-limits-and-expense-benefit-rates-for-businesses.html) is the same pair of provincial figures. Those rates are for reasonable allowances employers pay employees. The CRA's [motor vehicle expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses.html) page is how a self-employed driver claims actual costs. Do not put 73 cents on a tax return as if it were your deduction. The sketches use it only so the kilometre line has a published number.

### Illustrative example

Teaching sketch. Assumed kilometres. Not a forecast, a quote, an average, or a survey.

**Gross only, at the Ontario floor.** 56 engaged hours × $17.95 = $1,005.20. That is about $1,000 before vehicle costs, before waiting time, and before tax. Tips are excluded from the floor, so tips would be extra if customers leave them.

**Same hours with a 15 km assumption.** 56 × 15 = 840 km. At 73 cents, 840 × $0.73 = $613.20. Remainder: $1,005.20 − $613.20 = $392.00. The floor still "paid" minimum wage on the assignment clock. The kilometre stand-in took most of it.

**Hours to clear $1,000 after that stand-in, still at 15 km per engaged hour.** Each hour nets $17.95 − (15 × $0.73) = $17.95 − $10.95 = $7.00. Then $1,000 ÷ $7.00 = about 143 engaged hours. Check: 143 × $17.95 = $2,566.85. Kilometres are 143 × 15 = 2,145, under 5,000, so 73 cents still applies: 2,145 × $0.73 = $1,565.85. Remainder: $2,566.85 − $1,565.85 = $1,001.00. Waiting time is not inside the 143 hours. A month with that much engaged time is a large side schedule.

**Same target if the car only does 5 km per engaged hour.** Each hour nets $17.95 − (5 × $0.73) = $14.30. Use 70 engaged hours: 70 × $17.95 = $1,256.50. Kilometres are 350. Vehicle stand-in: 350 × $0.73 = $255.50. Remainder: $1,256.50 − $255.50 = $1,001.00. Route density is the difference between these two sketches, not a different app.

Replace 73 cents with the per-kilometre cost from your own fuel, maintenance, and insurance if you have the receipts. A lower personal cost means fewer hours. A higher one means more. British Columbia's engaged-time floor is a different statute. The dollar figures and the per-kilometre allowance are in [when to quit a gig app](/side-hustles/when-to-quit-a-gig-app-canada/) and [food delivery earnings](/side-hustles/food-delivery-pricing-canada/).

## What do published hourly ranges imply for $1,000 in Canada?

Published hourly ranges imply a gross hour count, and they are not net of your car. [Amazon Flex Canada](https://flex.amazon.ca/) says most drivers earn $22–$27 an hour, and that actual earnings depend on location and how long deliveries take. The midpoint of that stated range is $24.50. This sketch uses $24 only as a round number inside the range Amazon printed, not as an average of drivers.

### Illustrative example

Suppose a block pays $24 an hour for the block length Amazon is describing, and you need $1,000 gross. $1,000 ÷ $24 = 41.7 hours of block time, before the drive to the station and before kilometres. Suppose those 41.7 hours include an assumed 20 km per hour: 834 km × $0.73 = $608.82. Gross is about $1,000. Remainder after the stand-in: about $391. The same warning applies: 73 cents is not your tax deduction, and Amazon's range is not a promise for your city.

Job Bank's wage report for [food delivery drivers](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/21666/ca) lists a Canada median of $20.00 an hour, with a low of $15.00 and a high of $29.35, for 2023–2024 data. At the median, $1,000 gross is 50 hours. That row is employed delivery work. An employed driver is not paying for the van the way an app courier is. Do not treat $20 as your gig net.

## How many local visits does $1,000 take if you set the price?

If you set the price, $1,000 takes as many visits as that price produces after supplies. No government page sets a national cleaning or lawn rate. Use the pricing guide for the hustle, then run your own numbers. [House cleaning pricing](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-pricing-canada/), [lawn care pricing](/side-hustles/lawn-care-pricing-canada/), and [dog walking pricing](/side-hustles/dog-walking-pricing-canada/) are the rate-card pages. [Freelance writing pricing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-pricing-canada/) is the same idea for a page you invoice.

### Illustrative example

Suppose you charge an assumed $120 for a recurring clean, the visit takes an assumed 3 hours door to door, and supplies are an assumed $8. Net before tax is $112. Then $1,000 ÷ $112 = 8.9, so 9 visits. Hours: 9 × 3 = 27. Those figures are assumptions so the division is visible. They are not a quote, a city average, or a forecast that nine clients will book. If the real visit is $80 and takes 4 hours, redo the division. Do not print $120 on a flyer because it appeared here.

A freelance hour works the same way. Suppose your rate is an assumed $50 and the work is billable. $1,000 ÷ $50 = 20 hours. Software, revisions, and tax are not in the $50. Twenty hours that nobody booked are zero.

## Which side hustles should you not put on a $1,000 list in Canada?

Do not put a file, a channel, or a survey app on a $1,000 list in Canada unless you can show the sales count. This site does not have a sourced monthly total for printables, templates, YouTube, or beermoney. [Selling digital products](/side-hustles/digital-products-reality-canada/) and [passive income](/side-hustles/passive-income-reality-canada/) explain the hours-before-the-sale shape. [Beermoney apps](/side-hustles/best-beermoney-apps-canada/) are small tasks with their own pay pages. Adding them up to $1,000 is a hope, not a line in the table above.

## What mistakes do people make with a $1,000 side hustle target in Canada?

People divide $1,000 by a gross hourly figure and ignore kilometres, waiting, and tax. They treat an employed median as an app payout. They copy a visit price from a sketch. They also stack three unproven ideas so none of them gets the visits the arithmetic needs. One priced service, or one logged app, is enough to test the month.

## Which tax rules apply when a side hustle nears $1,000 a month in Canada?

$1,000 a month is $12,000 a year if it holds, and it is still business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) if you are carrying on the activity. It is under the [$30,000 GST/HST small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) by itself, and it is not an income-tax exemption. Add your other hustles before you decide you are under the line. Ride-hail is a different GST/HST rule from the first fare. The short version is the [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. Recheck the Ontario minimum wage, the CRA per-kilometre rates, Amazon Flex's stated range, and the Job Bank table when you reuse the numbers. The wage and the allowance are dated 2026. The Job Bank row cited here is 2023–2024 data.
