## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Ottawa, Canada?

### Step 1: Clear driveways without breaking the roads by-law

Ottawa's [Use and Care of Roads By-law](https://ottawa.ca/en/living-ottawa/laws-licences-and-permits/laws/laws-z/use-and-care-roads-law-no-2003-498) says no person shall push, plow, dump or otherwise deposit snow or ice on a road. Your job is to clear driveways, walkways and steps and pile the snow on the client's lot. That matters most after the City plow leaves a windrow at the end of the driveway. Use [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) to set per-visit and seasonal rates, and the [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/) for written terms.

### Step 2: Sell bilingual skills

Ottawa and Gatineau form one region across the provincial border, and bilingual work is in demand for tutoring, editing and admin support. See [side hustles for bilingual French-English speakers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-bilingual-french-english-speakers-canada/), [online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/) and [freelance translation](/side-hustles/freelance-translation-side-hustle-canada/). Work done for Gatineau clients falls under Quebec rules. Quebec has its own sales tax, so check [Revenu Québec's small supplier page](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/consumption-taxes/taxable-zero-rated-or-tax-exempt-goods-and-services/gst-and-qst/other-situations/small-suppliers/) before regular cross-river work.

### Step 3: Plan for Winterlude and winter care work

[Winterlude](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/winterlude.html) runs January 29 to February 15, 2027 in Canada's Capital Region. Visitors and events add short-term demand for [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/), delivery and [winter pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/). Start applying in the fall, because shift work for the festival fills early.

Ottawa is on [TaskRabbit's list of locations](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/locations); see the [TaskRabbit review](/side-hustles/taskrabbit-review-canada/) for fees and how jobs are assigned. - **November to March:** driveway snow clearing and Winterlude shifts.
- **April to May:** spring cleanup, [pressure washing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-side-hustle-canada/) and [window cleaning](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/).
- **June to August:** [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/), [moving help](/side-hustles/moving-help-side-hustle-canada/) and summer events.
- **September to November:** back-to-school tutoring, [leaf cleanup](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/), then holiday lights.

## Can you run a side hustle from home in Ottawa, Canada?

Yes, within the zoning rules. Ottawa's [home-based business provisions](https://documents.ottawa.ca/sites/default/files/zoning_bylaw_part5_section127_en.pdf) (Section 127 of the zoning by-law) say:

- the operator must live in the unit;
- the business can't be a nuisance or a hazard;
- outdoor storage is prohibited;
- the premises can't be used as a dispatching office or supply depot;
- clients can't be served on-site in an apartment, rooming unit or additional dwelling unit;
- businesses that need a City business licence aren't permitted as home-based businesses, except a few listed ones, though their admin and indoor storage can be.

For mobile services such as cleaning or snow clearing, the work happens at the client's property. Your home is only the office. A business name other than your own is registered with Ontario, as covered in [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you live in an apartment and want to tutor French and clear snow. Under Section 127 you couldn't hold tutoring sessions in your apartment, so you'd tutor online or at the student's home. For snow, you'd store your shovel and salt indoors, because outdoor storage for the business is prohibited. On each driveway you'd pile snow on the client's lot, never on the road.

## What mistakes do Ottawa side hustlers make?

- **Throwing snow back onto the road.** The roads by-law prohibits it.
- **Seeing clients in an apartment.** The zoning by-law doesn't allow on-site clients there.
- **Storing gear outside.** Outdoor storage for a home-based business is prohibited.
- **Ignoring Quebec rules for Gatineau work.** Cross-river jobs can bring QST obligations once you register.

## How are Ottawa side hustles taxed?

You report self-employment income on Form T2125 and register for HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from the first fare. Ontario's HST rate is 13%. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/), and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/).
