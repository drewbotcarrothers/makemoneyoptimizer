- **Still a Canadian product:** Rakuten.ca pays confirmed cash back in Canadian dollars by Interac e-Transfer, cheque, or PayPal, once the balance is at least $5.01. Caddle pays Canadians by Interac e-Transfer once cash-back credits reach $20.
- **Not the US app:** Rakuten.com is a separate account that pays US dollars. Balances do not combine.
- **US-only on the company's own page:** Checkout 51's homepage says United States members only. Ibotta says the app's extra functions do not work outside the United States, and a foreign phone number cannot be verified for withdrawal.

Cashback and receipt apps in Canada return a slice of money you were already going to spend, if you remember to start at the app or to photograph the receipt. They suit a household that shops online or buys the same groceries every week. They do not suit someone looking for income without spending. Several apps that US articles still recommend either never launched a Canadian payout or now say the offer is for the United States.

Figures below come from the companies' own pages, checked on October 6, 2026. The wider map is [beermoney apps](/side-hustles/best-beermoney-apps-canada/). Survey panels, which sometimes sit in the same apps, are [survey sites](/side-hustles/survey-sites-canada/).

## What happened to the US receipt apps?

**Checkout 51** still shows up in Canadian app roundups. On October 6, 2026 its public homepage says the offer is available to United States members only, next to a Français (CA) language toggle. This guide did not find a separate Canadian payout page. If you already have a login, check that account rather than assuming the US homepage pays a Canadian e-Transfer.

**Ibotta** tells US service members that additional functions of the normal app do not work outside the United States, and that withdrawal needs a US phone number that can receive calls or texts. A separate help article says a number based outside the US cannot be verified. That is enough to treat Ibotta as not a Canadian cash-back tool ([military bases](https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000424587-Can-I-use-Ibotta-on-a-military-base), [phone verification](https://help.ibotta.com/hc/en-us/articles/225682028-How-do-I-verify-my-phone-number-so-that-I-can-withdraw-my-earnings)).

**Fetch Rewards** did not have an official country page this guide could confirm. Check the app store page for your region before you install it. **Drop** is another name people still search. This guide did not find a current official Canadian payout page for it, so it is not listed as working.

**Rakuten.com** is the American site. Rakuten Canada says the two accounts are separate even if you reuse an email and password. The .com account pays US dollars. The .ca account pays Canadian dollars. The balances cannot be combined. Shopping a US store through Rakuten.ca can also change the cash-back amount, because Rakuten converts USD orders to CAD using the rate on the purchase date ([FAQ](https://www.rakuten.ca/help/most-shoppers-want-to-know)).

## How do you actually get the cash back?

### Step 1: Use Rakuten.ca, not a US link

Create the account on rakuten.ca. Set the payment method to Interac e-Transfer if you want it in your bank, or to a verified PayPal if you prefer. Update the address before a cheque run if you choose the cheque. Rakuten says sender fees on Interac and PayPal are on them.

### Step 2: Start the trip at Rakuten

The store pays Rakuten a commission only when the click is tracked. A purchase you typed straight into the store's site often earns nothing. The FAQ describes statuses: Pending (still in the return window), Scheduled (included in the next payment unless you return the item), Confirmed (in the payable balance), and Ineligible (excluded item, coupon, or cancellation).

### Step 3: Use Caddle only for offers you were going to buy

Caddle says you can shop at any store in Canada, and that the receipt has to show the exact product, which it checks with the description and the UPC. An offer you would not have bought is not "free money." It is a purchase plus a rebate that might be rejected.

### Step 4: Wait for the threshold

Rakuten will not send $3. Confirmed cash back under $5.01 rolls forward, and sign-up or referral bonuses do not count toward that $5.01. Caddle will not send an e-Transfer under $20. A light shopper can sit under either line for more than one quarter. That is the published rule, not a missing payment.

### Step 5: Watch duty on cross-border orders

Rakuten.ca lists some merchants that ship only to the United States, and it says duty on shipments to Canada depends on the merchant. A cash-back rate does not pay the duty, the brokerage fee, or a return to another country. Read the store's shipping note on the Rakuten page before you chase a higher percentage.

## What mistakes do Canadians make with cashback apps?

- **Downloading the US app** because a video said "Rakuten" or "Checkout 51." The country on the store listing matters.
- **Forgetting the click.** No tracked trip, no commission, no cash back.
- **Counting Pending as paid.** Pending can disappear if you return the item.
- **Letting a bonus confuse the minimum.** Rakuten excludes bonus rewards from the $5.01 test.
- **Buying something only for the rebate.** If the rebate is $1 and you did not need the item, you spent money to collect a dollar that may not even clear the payout minimum this quarter.

### Illustrative example

Assumed cash-back amounts, using Rakuten's real $5.01 rule. Suppose three tracked orders confirm at $1.40, $2.10, and $1.20. The sum is $4.70. That is under $5.01, so the May, August, November, or February payment does not include it. One more confirmed $0.50 later makes $5.20, which clears the minimum and can go out on the next scheduled date, still only after those amounts are confirmed rather than pending. This is not a typical shopping month. It only shows the threshold.

## Are cashback rewards taxable in Canada?

This guide does not give a yes or no. A rebate on a personal grocery run is not the same thing as being paid to do a task, and the CRA pages that govern business income do not walk through every loyalty program. Survey points, cash-back, and bank bonuses are collected in [are rewards and bonuses taxable](/side-hustles/are-rewards-and-bonuses-taxable-canada/). Bank sign-up offers, which are not receipt apps, are in [bank account bonuses](/side-hustles/bank-account-sign-up-bonuses-canada/). If you use Rakuten or Caddle as part of a business (you buy stock to resell, and the rebate reduces the cost), keep the statements with your other records and see [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
