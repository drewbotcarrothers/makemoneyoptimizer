The best free bookkeeping tool for most Canadian side hustles is a well-organized spreadsheet or a free app such as Wave Starter or Zoho Books Free. The CRA requires you to keep records of all transactions to support your income and expense claims, but it says it "does not issue record books nor suggests any type of book or set of books" ([CRA business records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html)). Paid software starts to pay for itself when you send many invoices, want bank feeds, or are registered for GST/HST and need tax tracked on every sale.

## Is a spreadsheet enough for side hustle bookkeeping in Canada?

Yes, for many side hustles with a few transactions a week. The CRA asks for records of daily income and expenses, with income records showing the date, amount, and source, and expense receipts showing the date, the seller's name and address, a description, and the vendor's GST/HST number on purchases of $100 or more from a registrant ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html)). A spreadsheet can capture all of that.

A simple layout with three tabs:

**Income tab:** Date · Client or platform · Invoice or payout ID · Description · Amount · GST/HST collected (if registered) · Paid date

**Expenses tab:** Date · Vendor · Description · Category · Amount · GST/HST paid · Business % · Receipt file name

**Mileage tab:** Date · Destination · Purpose · Km (see [tracking mileage for the CRA](/side-hustles/mileage-log-cra-side-hustle-canada/))

Use categories that match the expense lines on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), such as advertising, office expenses, supplies, and telephone and utilities, so the year-end totals copy straight across. A summary tab with a SUMIF per category gives your T2125 numbers in seconds.

## How do you choose a bookkeeping tool for a side hustle?

Match the tool to how you get paid and whether you charge GST/HST.

### Step 1: Count your transactions

Under a few dozen a month, a spreadsheet or a free plan is fine.

### Step 2: Decide whether you need invoicing

If you [invoice clients](/side-hustles/how-to-invoice-clients-canada/) regularly, an app that sends invoices and records payments saves time. Wave Starter and Zoho Books Free both include invoicing.

### Step 3: Check GST/HST handling

If you are registered for GST/HST, pick a tool that tracks tax collected and paid on each line. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 4: Consider bank feeds

Automatic bank import is the biggest time-saver, and it is often a paid feature (for example, Wave lists it under Pro). It works best when your side hustle has its own [business account](/side-hustles/business-bank-account-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 5: Try before paying

All the paid options above list a free trial or free plan. Enter one month of real transactions before deciding.

## What bookkeeping mistakes do Canadian side hustlers make?

Most mistakes are about timing, not tools.

- **Doing it all in April.** Update weekly or monthly.
- **Paying for features you don't use.** Start free and upgrade when a specific need appears.
- **Not saving receipts.** The app records the transaction; the receipt proves it. Keep receipts six years, per [record-keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Mixed accounts.** Bank feeds from a personal account pull in groceries and rent. [Separate the money](/side-hustles/separate-business-personal-money-canada/) first.
- **Missing the promo end date.** Note when introductory pricing ends.
