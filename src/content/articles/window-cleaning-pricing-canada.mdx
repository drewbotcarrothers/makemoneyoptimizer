Pricing window cleaning in Canada means counting panes, timing a real set of them, and quoting either per pane or as one number for the home or storefront. Ground-floor exterior glass and small storefronts are the jobs a new cleaner can price honestly. There is no official Canadian price per window, so this guide does not publish an average. ## Which pricing models work for window cleaning in Canada?

Window cleaning in Canada is usually sold per pane or as a flat price for a house front, a whole ground floor, or a storefront. Hourly billing suits an odd interior job. A route of shops is easier to keep if each one has a standing visit price.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per pane | One price for each pane, inside and outside listed separately | Houses where you can see every window from the sidewalk | French doors and large picture windows that take longer than a small pane |
| Per home or per side | One number for "ground-floor exterior" or "front only" | Repeat houses on one street | A client who hears "the house" and means three storeys |
| Per storefront | One number for the glass on the shop front, on a schedule | A main street you can walk | A price that ignores a filthy first visit |
| Hourly | Rate times hours, with an estimate | Interiors, construction dust, paint specks | Clients who want a single number before you arrive |
| Add-ons | Screens, tracks, sills, interior | After the glass price is clear | Leaving them free because you are already there |

The Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety's page on [portable ladders](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) says falls from portable ladders are a major source of serious injury. A pane you can reach only by climbing is not the same product as a pane you can reach from the ground. If you do not climb, say so in the price. The safety steps are in the [start guide](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do you calculate a window cleaning price in Canada?

Calculate a window cleaning price in Canada from the minutes per pane on glass you have actually cleaned, the walk or drive between jobs, and the pay you need per working hour. Then decide whether the client will understand a pane count or a single home price. Both should come from the same minutes.

### Step 1: Time your own windows

Clean a known number of panes with the scrubber and squeegee. Divide the minutes by the panes. A large pane is not "one" if it takes three times as long. Count it as you will count it for a client, and be consistent.

### Step 2: Separate inside, outside, and height

Exterior ground-floor glass is the base. Interior glass needs someone home and a drop cloth habit. Anything above what you can reach with a pole is either declined or priced as ladder work you are equipped and insured to do.

### Step 3: Add the trip

Four bungalows on one street share the walk. One house across the city does not. Put travel in the price or do not take the isolated job.

### Step 4: Hold back tax and CPP

Both CPP shares come out of a self-employed rate. See the [CPP contributions page](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 5: Write what is not included

Screens, tracks, sills, interior glass, and any pane above the ground floor. A client who wants them can add them at the prices you already wrote.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, an average, or a survey. Suppose your target is $32 for each working hour, including a short walk between nearby houses. Suppose a ground-floor exterior pane takes 4 minutes, including the move to the next pane. One pane is 4 ÷ 60 = 0.067 hours. Labour is 0.067 × $32 = $2.13. Suppose solution and a cloth, spread over the job, add about $0.15. The pane is about $2.28 before you round.

A price of $2.28 is hard to say and leaves nothing for a pane that is painted with pollen. Suppose you round exterior ground-floor panes to $4. Twelve panes are 12 × $4 = $48, and 12 × 4 minutes = 48 minutes of glass. At $32 an hour, 0.8 hours of labour is $25.60, so the $48 also contains time to set down the bucket, talk to the client, and walk to the next house on the same street. If the next house is a 25-minute drive, this $48 does not pay for the drive. Reprice or decline.

Suppose interior glass on the same 12 panes takes longer because of furniture and blinds, an assumed 6 minutes each. Labour is 12 × 0.1 × $32 = $38.40, which you might round to $6 a pane, or $72 for the set. Suppose a small storefront is 8 large panes that you time at 8 minutes each, 64 minutes, or about 1.07 hours. Labour is about $34. You might quote $55 for the front, not 8 × $4, because the panes are not the small ones you timed. Do not copy $4, $48, or $55.

## When should you raise window cleaning prices in Canada?

Raise window cleaning prices in Canada when your good-weather weeks are full, when a house has more panes than you counted, or when travel on that street no longer fits the old number. Tell repeat clients before the next season, which for many routes is spring or fall, and change new-client prices first.

Exterior work is thin in a Canadian winter because water freezes on the glass. Do not "make up" a winter price by taking ladder jobs you priced as ground-floor work. The [start guide](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) explains the season limit.

## What window cleaning pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common window cleaning pricing mistakes in Canada are one price for "the whole house," ignoring travel, and not charging more for interior or height.

- **Counting a picture window as one small pane.** Time it or price it as several.
- **Free screens.** They add minutes. Write an add-on or leave them out.
- **A ladder price you will not climb.** If the pane needs a ladder, the [CCOHS portable ladder page](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) is the safety bar, and the price has to include the slower work. Many beginners should decline.
- **Copying the cheapest note on the block.** You do not know whether it is exterior only.
- **A route of one-off houses.** Group the street. [First clients](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-first-clients-canada/) covers how.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect window cleaning prices in Canada?

Window cleaning income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Add GST/HST only after you register, which is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles. That figure is not an income-tax-free amount. See the [myth guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/).

When registered, use the rate for the province where you clean from the [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) and the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).
