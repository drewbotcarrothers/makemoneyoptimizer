## Which pricing models work for window cleaning?

Quote a flat price per home, built from a per-pane rate, with surcharges for upper storeys and add-ons, and a minimum ticket. Storefronts are priced per visit on a recurring route.

| Model | Use it for | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flat per home by storeys | Most houses | DT Cleaning: exterior $130 / $170 / $210+ |
| Per pane | Building the flat price | DT Cleaning: $3–$8 exterior, $8–$15 both sides |
| Scope tiers | Exterior only vs inside and out | Serv1sland: $149 vs $299 |
| Add-ons | Screens, tracks, hard water | DT Cleaning: screens $5, tracks $7 |
| Recurring plan | Spring and fall, quarterly | Spiffy: 10%–15% off |
| Storefront route | Shops every 1–4 weeks | WonderWash: $50–$150 per visit |
| Minimum ticket | Small jobs | Toronto: $150–$200 at many companies |

Ladder work belongs in the price. The Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety's page on [portable ladders](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) says falls from portable ladders are a major source of serious injury. If a pane needs more than you can reach safely with a pole or a properly set ladder, don't quote it.

## How do you calculate a window cleaning price?

Build an hourly rate, convert it to per-pane prices from your timed minutes, then add storeys, add-ons and a minimum.

```text
Hourly rate    = (target pay + CPP + ladders, poles, insurance, fuel per hour)
                 ÷ share of hours billable
Per-pane price = minutes per pane ÷ 60 × hourly rate
Home price     = panes × per-pane price + upper-storey surcharge + add-ons,
                 never below your minimum
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $30/hour (about 1.5 times the $19.23 Job Bank median) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $3.57 |
| Ladders, poles, insurance, fuel per hour | $5.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 70% |
| **Rate** | ($30 + $3.57 + $5) ÷ 0.70 = **$55.10, round to $55** |
| Exterior pane, 5 minutes | $4.58, **price $5** |
| Inside and out, 10 minutes | $9.17, **price $9** |
| Upper-storey surcharge, 3 extra minutes | $2.75, **price $3 per window** |

$5 and $9 sit inside DT Cleaning's $3–$8 and $8–$15 per-pane ranges.

### Quote 3: bungalow, 14 windows, inside and out with screens, Calgary

### Quote 4: storefront route

- Six shops, four large panes each, both sides, every two weeks. 20 minutes of cleaning plus 10 minutes of travel and parking per stop = 30 minutes × $55 = $27.50, quote **$30 per visit**.

## How should you write a window cleaning quote and invoice?

Count windows from outside before you quote, ask about screens and tracks, and write the storeys and scope down.

```text
WINDOW CLEANING QUOTE — [client, address]   Date: [date]
Home: two-storey, 22 windows (11 main, 11 upper), vinyl sliders
Scope: inside and out, glass + frames + sills; 22 screens washed
22 × $9 inside and out ............................ $198
11 upper-storey windows × $3 ...................... $33
22 screens × $5 ................................... $110
Total ............................................. $340 (quoted)
Spring + fall plan: $306 per visit
Not included: tracks ($7 each), skylights, hard-water removal,
  windows above second storey
Client clears window sills and moves fragile items inside.
Payment: e-Transfer on completion. Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ____]
```

```text
INVOICE #2026-054   Date: [date]
Spring clean, inside and out (plan rate) ....... $306.00
Tracks, 6 × $7 ................................. $42.00
Subtotal ....................................... $348.00
HST 13% (registered only) ...................... $45.24
Total .......................................... $393.24
```

## How do you raise window cleaning prices?

Raise prices each spring, and use the structures companies already publish.

1. **Sell plans, not single visits.** Spiffy's quarterly and spring-and-fall plans trade 10% to 15% off for guaranteed repeat work.
2. **Charge for height.** Every guide above prices two- and three-storey homes higher.
3. **Price screens and tracks.** DT Cleaning charges $5 per screen and $7 per track.
4. **Set a minimum.** Many Toronto companies won't start under $150 to $200.
5. **Raise storefronts toward market.** Small storefronts run $50 to $150 per visit in Calgary.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], spring window cleaning is booking now. Your inside-and-out plan
is $[320] a visit this year (from $[306]). Reply to keep your usual week.
— [Your name]
```

## What window cleaning pricing mistakes are common?

- **No minimum.** A seven-window bungalow at $5 a pane is $35, less than the drive.
- **One price for all storeys.** Upper windows take ladder time and more risk.
- **Free screens and tracks.** They're priced add-ons at DT Cleaning and included only in higher packages at Serv1sland.
- **A ladder price you won't climb.** If it isn't safe, don't quote it.
- **A route of one-off houses.** Group the street. [First clients](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-first-clients-canada/) covers how.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect window cleaning prices?

Window cleaning income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. Register for GST/HST once taxable sales from all your businesses pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters. That figure isn't an income-tax-free amount; see the [myth guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/).

When registered, use the rate for the province where you clean from the [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) and the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). A $340 job is $384.20 with Ontario's 13% HST and $357 with 5% GST in Alberta or B.C. The [tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) and [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) keep this on the service form. Set aside tax from each job with [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
