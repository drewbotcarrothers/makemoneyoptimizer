## How should you rank side hustles in Canada if income figures are not the point?

Rank side hustles in Canada by startup cash, by the kind of effort, and by season. Income comes after those three, from the price you set or the wage on a posting, minus costs. A Job Bank median for an occupation is a wage distribution for employees. It is not a ranking of businesses.

| Lens | Question to ask | What this page will not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Startup cash | What do you buy before the first paid day? | Invent a national "under $100" list that ignores insurance |
| Effort | Is the limit your body, your calendar, a skill, or a screen? | Call a night shift "easy" because the cash to start is low |
| Season | Does the work exist in your month? | Recommend lawn care in January on the Prairies |
| After costs | What is left once supplies, fees, and travel are subtracted? | Publish an unsourced monthly income |

Self-employment income is reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Employee shifts come with a T4. The [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) is the same habit either way once you know which one you are.

## What is a poor fit on a national side hustle list in Canada?

A poor fit on a national list is anything that needs you to be unwell, to borrow for a promotion, or to treat a medical procedure as a job. [Paid plasma donation](/side-hustles/paid-plasma-donation-canada/) is legal to compensate only in some provinces, and you can be deferred. [Bank account sign-up bonuses](/side-hustles/bank-account-sign-up-bonuses-canada/) are conditional credits, not work. This site does not cover casino or sportsbook offers.

Also a poor fit: licensed electrical, gas, or structural work presented as a casual hustle. The [handyman guide](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/) draws that line. Christmas lights stop at plug-in clips. An offer that asks you to pay to start, or to deposit a cheque and send money back, is not a side hustle on this list. ## Which tax rules apply no matter which side hustle you pick in Canada?

The tax rule that applies to every side hustle in Canada is that the income is taxable, either as employment or as business income. Business income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all of your activities added together, with a different rule for taxi and rideshare that the rideshare guide covers.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Employees do not charge GST/HST on wages. Tips still get reported. The [guide to reporting side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/) is the overview. The short answers people ask first are in the [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/). Insurance for ladders, pets, and other people's homes is a broker question before the first paid visit, not a line this page can price.
