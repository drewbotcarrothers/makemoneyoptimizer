## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Calgary, Canada?

### Step 1: Sell snow clearing against the 24-hour rule

Calgary's [snow and ice bylaw page](https://www.calgary.ca/bylaws/snow-ice.html) says owners must clear public sidewalks bordering their property down to the bare surface within 24 hours of a snowfall ending. They must also clear at least 1.5 metres of any public pathway that runs along their property. The City relaxes noise rules during a snowfall and for 48 hours afterward, so you may run a snow blower then. A bare-surface promise within 24 hours is an easy service to explain. Read the [snow removal guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) and [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/), then put the terms in writing with the [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/).

### Step 2: Walk dogs and know the six-dog line

Calgary's [pet permits page](https://www.calgary.ca/pets/permits.html) says that since September 30, 2022, walking more than six dogs off leash at once in an off-leash park needs a professional dog walker permit. The application asks for proof of a year or more of dog-walking work. Most beginners stay well under six. Start with [dog walking pricing](/side-hustles/dog-walking-pricing-canada/) and the [Rover review](/side-hustles/rover-review-canada/).

### Step 3: Plan for Stampede season

The [Calgary Stampede](https://www.calgarystampede.com/upcoming-stampede-dates) runs July 9 to 18 in 2027. Visitors and events bring short-term demand for [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/), delivery and pet sitting while locals travel. Book flexible gigs ahead, because staffing apps and agencies fill shifts early. The [flash staffing apps guide](/side-hustles/flash-staffing-apps-side-hustle-canada/) explains how shift apps work.

## What seasons drive Calgary side hustle income?

- **October to April:** snow clearing, plus [winter car detailing](/side-hustles/winter-car-detailing-salt-removal-canada/) to wash off road grit.
- **May to June:** spring yard cleanup and [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) sign-ups.

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Calgary, Canada?

It depends on the activity, but you still register. The City's [getting started page](https://www.calgary.ca/for-business/getting-started.html) says some businesses need a licence. Its [not-required page](https://www.calgary.ca/for-business/licences/not-required.html) explains that businesses that don't need one still need a Business ID and land use approval. Ask the City which applies before you take payments. A business name other than your own is a provincial trade name, covered in [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you sign up eight homes on two adjacent streets for winter sidewalk and driveway clearing. You'd confirm with the City whether you need a licence or only a Business ID and land use approval. Then you'd write a service promise of bare pavement within 24 hours of snowfall ending, matching the bylaw. You'd also set a cutoff for big storms. In July you'd switch to Stampede shifts and pet sitting for clients who travel.

## What mistakes do Calgary side hustlers make?

- **Assuming no licence means no registration.** Calgary still wants a Business ID and land use approval.
- **Leaving packed snow.** The bylaw says bare surface, not just a shovelled path.
- **Forgetting the pathway.** Properties next to a public pathway must clear at least 1.5 metres of it too.
- **Booking Stampede shifts late.** Shift work for the July peak gets taken early.

## How are Calgary side hustles taxed?

Alberta has no provincial sales tax. You report self-employment income on Form T2125 and register for 5% GST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from the first fare.
