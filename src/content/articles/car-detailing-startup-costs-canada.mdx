## How much does car detailing cost to start in Canada?

Car detailing costs $137.04 to $436.73 to equip in Canada with 13% HST, depending on whether you start with a bucket kit, add a carry vacuum, or add a spot extractor for stained seats. Insurance is the biggest single line, about $450 a year. Prices below were checked on Amazon.ca and homedepot.ca on 9 October 2026 and move often, so treat them as a dated snapshot.

| Kit | Before tax | With 13% HST | With 5% GST only | First year with ~$450 insurance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean bucket kit | $121.27 | $137.04 | $127.33 | $587.04 |
| Standard mobile kit | $266.82 | $301.51 | $280.16 | $751.51 |
| Pro interior kit | $386.49 | $436.73 | $405.81 | $886.73 |

The HST column fits Ontario. The GST column fits Alberta and the territories. Provinces with PST add it on top of GST, and Nova Scotia's HST is 14%. Rows in every kit are cumulative: standard includes everything in lean, and pro includes everything in standard.

The kit assumes you already drive a car that can hold two buckets and a vacuum. A van, a trailer, a water tank and a generator are not in any column. They are the purchases that turn a $300 start into a loan.

## How much is car detailing insurance in Canada?

Car detailing insurance in Canada starts around $450 a year for a basic commercial general liability policy with a $2 million limit, which is the figure [Zensurance publishes](https://www.zensurance.com/general-liability-insurance) for a small business. Its [auto detailing page](https://www.zensurance.com/auto-detailing-insurance) says many detailers carry at least $2 million in liability, and it gives no fixed detailing price, so the $450 is a starting point and your quote may differ.

Three parts of a detailing policy matter for a side hustle:

- **General liability** covers third-party injury and property damage. Zensurance's own example is a cleaning agent that damages a customer's paint.
- **Garage liability** covers damage to a customer's vehicle while it is in your care, custody and control. If you move a client's car or keep it at your place, ask for it by name.
- **Commercial auto** applies when you drive to customers for mobile detailing. Zensurance states that private-passenger car insurance is not designed for vehicles used for business. Tell your auto insurer what you are doing before you rely on a personal policy.

Rates depend on services, experience, location, revenue and claims history. A quote that excludes paint correction or ceramic coating is normal for a new detailer. Leave those services off the menu until the policy names them. Compare routes in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

## How many paid interiors does it take to break even on car detailing?

Car detailing breaks even after about 8 interiors for the standard kit plus a year of insurance, and about 9 for the pro kit, at an assumed $120 per interior with $15 of supplies and fuel per job. At a Toronto shop rate of $150 it drops to 6 and 7.

**Illustrative. The $120 price and $15 per-job cost are assumptions, not quotes or survey results.**

For a market reference, Romeo's in Toronto lists a mobile sedan interior at $150 and Detailing Pro in the GTA lists $160 for a sedan, both drawn from [how much to charge for car detailing in Canada](/side-hustles/car-detailing-pricing-canada/). | Lean kit, no insurance | $137.04 | $105 | 2 (1.3) | 2 (1.0) |
| Lean kit + $450 insurance | $587.04 | $105 | 6 (5.6) | 5 (4.3) |

| Pro kit + $450 insurance | $886.73 | $105 | 9 (8.4) | 7 (6.6) |

Round up, because a partial interior does not pay a bill. The arithmetic is first-year cost ÷ margin per job. Standard: $751.51 ÷ $105 = 7.16, so 8 interiors. Pro: $886.73 ÷ $105 = 8.45, so 9.

The pro kit costs one extra interior at $120 and pays for itself if it lets you sell a stain or pet-hair add-on. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/car-detailing-pricing-canada/) lists published pet-hair add-ons from $30 to $120.

Change the inputs to your own: your price, your kilometres, and how much product a salty March interior uses. Note how many cars each bottle lasts, and replace the $15 with your own number after five jobs.

## What ongoing costs does car detailing have in Canada?

Ongoing car detailing costs in Canada are soap, interior cleaner, towels you retire, fuel to reach driveways, and insurance that does not shrink in a frozen February. Buy product when a bottle is empty, not in bulk on day one. The 1 gallon Gold Class was $68.20 on 9 October 2026 against $18.97 for 1.89 L, which works out to about $18 per litre against about $10 per litre for the smaller bottle on that day, so the bigger jug was not the bargain.

**Water and drains.** Toronto's page on [washing cars and outdoor surfaces](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says nothing other than rain and melted snow should enter a catch basin, that power washing with water alone can still move contaminants, and that both the person hiring and the mobile wash business are responsible. Your city's rule may be worded differently. If you cannot say where the rinse water goes, sell the interior only that day.

**Kilometres.** Log the trips if you plan to claim them. A route of neighbours is cheaper than a route across the city.

**Unpaid scope.** A second vehicle "while you are here," an engine bay, or pet hair that was not on the booking note are how a priced interior becomes an open afternoon. Write the add-ons before the first visit.

## Which tax rules apply to car detailing startup costs in Canada?

Car detailing income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. Soap, towels and cleaners are current expenses. A vacuum or extractor that lasts for years is usually claimed as [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business portion, over time rather than all in the first year.

GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): worldwide taxable supplies of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive quarters, counting every side hustle together. Detailing is not ride-sharing, so the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule does not apply. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST. Once registered, charge the rate where the work happens, using the CRA [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table and the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).

Search your own city for a business licence requirement. A licence is a separate question from the sewer bylaw. If you pay a helper, check your province's workers' compensation rules first.

## Where can car detailing startup spending be lower, and where should it not be cut?

Spend less by selling interiors first, using the vacuum you own, and waiting on the extractor until a booking names stains. Two Home Depot buckets at $4.77 each plus a $14.99 insert do the job of a branded grit-guard bucket.

Do not cut the second bucket, the separate glass towels, before photos of every panel, or the insurance before the first paid exterior. A single gritty bucket is how a paid wash becomes a scratch you have to pay for. Test cleaners on a hidden spot every time.
