In Alberta, the side hustle rules are simpler on tax and stricter at city hall. There is no provincial sales tax, so the only sales tax question is the federal 5% GST and its $30,000 small-supplier test. You register a trade name only if you use a name other than your own. The step people miss is municipal: Calgary and Edmonton both expect home-based businesses to be licensed or registered and to have land-use approval. This guide suits anyone in Alberta starting a service, selling goods, or driving for an app.

It belongs to the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) set. ## Do you need to register a trade name for a side hustle in Alberta?

Only if you do business under a name other than your own. Alberta says a [trade name](https://www.alberta.ca/register-business-name) is used when an individual does business under a name other than their own personal name, and a trade name is also known as a sole proprietorship.

### Step 1: Pick the name and check it

If you want "Bow River Bookkeeping" rather than your legal name, plan for a trade name. Registering does not make the name exclusive, and the business has no separate legal existence, so you stay personally responsible for its debts.

### Step 2: File the Declaration of Trade Name through a registry agent

Alberta says service providers charge a government fee and a service fee to register a business name, so the total varies by agent. Ask for the full price before you file.

### Step 3: Check for a business number

The CRA lists Alberta among the provinces where a BN is issued automatically when you register with the province ([When you need a BN](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html)). See [how to get a CRA business number](/side-hustles/how-to-get-cra-business-number-canada/) if you need one without registering a name.

## Do Alberta side hustlers charge sales tax?

Only GST, and only once registered. The CRA's [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) shows Alberta at 5% GST and 0% PST. You do not have to register for GST until your worldwide taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)).

Rideshare drivers are the exception: the CRA says [taxi and commercial ride-sharing drivers](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) must register even as small suppliers. Food delivery couriers follow the normal test.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Calgary snow shoveller bills $1,500 a month for five months and does $800 a month of lawn work for five months. That is $7,500 plus $4,000, or $11,500 a year, well under $30,000, so no GST registration is required. If they register voluntarily, a $50 driveway visit becomes $52.50 with 5% GST.

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in Alberta?

Not a platform-specific one. Ontario and B.C. have rules for app-based delivery and ride-hail workers, but Alberta's [minimum wage page](https://www.alberta.ca/minimum-wage) lists only employee rates as of October 5, 2026: $15 an hour for most employees, and $13 for students under 18 for the first 28 hours a week while school is in session. Those rates apply to employees, such as event staff hired by an agency, not to independent contractors on delivery apps. That makes it more important to work out your own hourly pay after costs, as shown in [food delivery pay in Canada](/side-hustles/food-delivery-pricing-canada/).

## Which licences do side hustles need in Calgary, Edmonton and elsewhere in Alberta?

Alberta's two big cities both regulate home-based businesses.

- **Calgary:** the City says [business licences are required depending on your activity](https://www.calgary.ca/for-business/getting-started.html), and home-based and mobile businesses are licence types. Businesses that do not need a licence still [obtain land use approval](https://www.calgary.ca/for-business/licences/not-required.html) and register for a Business ID, with no fee for the Business ID when no licence applies.
- **Edmonton:** home-based businesses, including those working at clients' locations with admin done at home, such as cleaning, landscaping and photography, must have a [business licence like any other business](https://www.edmonton.ca/business_economy/licences_permits/home-based-business), and some need a development permit.
- **Elsewhere:** search [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/) for your town.

Two provincial rules are worth knowing. Low-risk home-prepared foods can be sold from home, online, at special events and at farmers' markets with proper labelling ([Alberta](https://www.alberta.ca/low-risk-home-prepared-foods)); see the [home baking guide](/side-hustles/home-baking-side-hustle-canada/). And unlicensed childcare providers may care for up to six children, not including their own ([Alberta childcare](https://www.alberta.ca/understand-albertas-childcare-system-for-parents-and-families)).

## What are the common Alberta side hustle mistakes?

- **Assuming "no PST" means no sales tax.** GST still applies once you pass $30,000.
- **Skipping the city step.** Calgary and Edmonton both expect home-based businesses to be registered or licensed.
- **Expecting a gig minimum.** Alberta has no platform-worker minimum like Ontario or B.C.
- **Thinking a trade name protects you.** Alberta says it grants no ownership of the name and no liability shield; insurance does that job ([side hustle insurance](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/)).

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in Alberta?

You report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net self-employment earnings, and Alberta income tax is calculated on the same return.
