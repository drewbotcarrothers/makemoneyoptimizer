## How much does moving help cost to start in Canada?

Moving help in Canada costs $114.97 for gloves, ratchet straps and a 6-pack of moving blankets, $291.10 adding lifting straps, a dolly 2-pack, knee pads and stretch wrap, $741.10 with a starting liability policy, or $1,161.45 with a hand truck and a year of invoicing software. Goods and software include 13% Ontario HST. Insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate. Boots you already own aren't counted.

## Who should use a moving help startup budget in Canada?

A moving help budget fits people who can lift safely, have a partner for two-person pieces, and will write down what's included before the first box moves. The lean kit fits boxes and a few pieces in a client's rental truck. The standard kit fits whole apartments with sofas and dressers. The pro kit fits regular month-end bookings and a helper to invoice for.

It's a poor fit if the plan is pianos, safes or hot tubs, or if you'll be driving the truck without reading your province's rules and your insurer's answer.

What the client wants gone, not moved, is [junk removal startup costs](/side-hustles/junk-removal-startup-costs-canada/). Furniture assembly at the new address is [odd-job apps startup costs](/side-hustles/odd-jobs-apps-startup-costs-canada/).

## Do you need insurance or a licence for moving help in Canada?

**Insurance.** Zensurance's [moving company insurance page](https://www.zensurance.com/moving-company-insurance) doesn't publish a price; it quotes. Its [home-based business page](https://www.zensurance.com/home-based-business-insurance) said on October 9, 2026 that a small business owner operating from home can anticipate premiums starting at $450 a year for liability with a $2 million limit. Use that as a placeholder and get a quote that names moving labour, stairs and other people's goods. Ask separately whether damage to the items you carry is covered, and whether a platform's guarantee is insurance (Taskrabbit says its Taskprotect isn't). Compare options in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/). This is not insurance advice.

**Ontario's contract rules.** The province's [Hiring a mover](https://www.ontario.ca/page/hiring-mover) page says consumer contracts for moving services must be in writing if they're worth more than $50, and must include the mover's name, address and contact information, a description of the services with an itemized price list, the total, start and end dates, and the payment terms. It lists what a contract can also include: the estimate (by law, the mover can't charge more than 10% above the estimate in the contract unless the client needs extra supplies or services and agrees), the insurer's name and policy number, who's responsible for damage or loss, and the crew size, hours and vehicle size. A contract negotiated in the client's home comes with a 10-day cooling-off period. Other provinces publish their own rules.

**Business licence.** Ontario's page says moving companies aren't licensed by the province, but a city can still require a business licence. Search your city's bylaws.

### A first-month sketch

**A teaching sketch, not a quote, a forecast, or a survey of Canadian moving rates.**

Suppose you buy the lean kit for $114.97 and carry insurance at $450 a year, or $37.50 a month. Over a month-end weekend you and a partner do three labour-only jobs, each four hours at an assumed $35 an hour per helper with an assumed $15 tip, and you split $3.06 of fuel each time.

Your pay per job = (4 × $35) + $15 − $3.06 = $151.94. Three jobs = $455.82. After the month's insurance share: $455.82 − $37.50 = $418.32. After the lean kit: $418.32 − $114.97 = $303.35 before a tax set-aside, for 12 hours of work, or about $25.28 an hour in month one. Do not copy $35.

## What are the ongoing costs?

- **Gloves, blankets and wrap,** which wear out or tear.
- **Insurance,** from about $450 a year, renewed yearly.
- **Fuel and parking** if you drive to the job. Log the kilometres.
- **Your partner's share.** The pillar sketch assumes each helper keeps their own pay.
- **Unpaid scope.** "While you're here, the piano" and a second address that wasn't on the note are how a morning becomes a loss.

## Where can moving help startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Start labour-only.** The client rents the truck.
- **Use the boots you already own,** if they grip on wet stairs.
- **Wait on the dolly and hand truck** until jobs include more than boxes.
- **Use the client's blankets and wrap** when the truck rental includes them, and say so in the note.
- **Skip specialist gear** for pianos and appliances you won't carry.

## Where should moving help spending not be cut in Canada?

Don't skip straps, gloves or the written note. A dresser on an open tailgate without straps is a road hazard. Don't skip insurance before you carry other people's things through their doorways. Don't supply a vehicle until you've read your province's rules and your insurer has answered.

Keep job pay apart from household money. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can hold the day's pay until the tax set-aside is moved.

## Which tax rules apply to a moving help budget in Canada?

Moving help income in Canada is self-employment income, including tips and deposits, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. Gloves, blankets and wrap are generally current expenses. A dolly or hand truck may be a current expense or [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) depending on its cost and life. GST/HST uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add junk removal, handyman and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. If you are registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html). If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST. Once registered, use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) for the province where the supply is made.
