Owners buy the relief of knowing someone is watching the site, so the checklist has to be specific.

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security lists patching and limiting admin privileges among its top actions, which is most of what monthly maintenance is. ## What offer gets a first website maintenance client in Canada?

The offer most likely to get a first website maintenance client in Canada is a monthly care plan with a written checklist: updates to the platform, theme, and plugins after a backup; a restore test each quarter; uptime monitoring; a broken-link check; and a short monthly report. The checklist is the product.

Start with one platform you know, such as WordPress. Say what is out of scope, such as redesigns, new pages, or content writing, and quote those separately.

## Where do Canadians find a first website maintenance client?

Canadians find a first website maintenance client among small businesses they already know, owners whose sites were built by someone who has moved on, and web designers who would rather hand off upkeep. A designer referral partner can send several clients over time.

Public clues that a site needs care include a copyright year several years old, a broken contact form, or a "not secure" warning in the browser. Note what you can see without logging in or scanning the site. Do not run security scans against a site you do not have permission to test.

If you email owners, ISED's [anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies to commercial messages, and its [getting consent](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) page explains consent, identification, and the unsubscribe mechanism. This is not legal advice.

## What should a first website maintenance note say?

A first website maintenance note in Canada should name one visible issue, describe the monthly checklist, and say the owner keeps ownership of every account.

**Example note.** This is a generic example. It is not a note that produced a client.

> Hello. On your website, the contact form returns an error page, and the footer still shows 2021. I offer a monthly care plan for small business sites: a backup before every update, platform and plugin updates, uptime monitoring, and a one-page report each month. You keep ownership of the hosting and domain accounts and add me as a separate user that you can remove at any time. Would you like the full checklist?

## How do you prove maintenance skill without fake testimonials?

You prove website maintenance skill in Canada with a sample monthly report from a test site you own, a written checklist, and a documented restore from backup. A real restore is stronger evidence than a claim that you have never lost a site.

Do not list client logos or testimonials you do not have. A test site you broke and restored on purpose shows the skill directly.

## How should you handle access for a first maintenance client in Canada?

Handle access for a first maintenance client in Canada by having the owner create a separate user for you with only the rights the work needs, and by never asking for their personal password. The Cyber Centre's [top 10 IT security actions](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/top-10-it-security-actions) include enforcing the management of administrative privileges.

Keep credentials in a password manager, turn on two-factor authentication where the platform supports it, and write down how the owner removes your access when the contract ends. Contact-form entries and customer accounts may hold personal information. [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) applies to private-sector organisations handling personal information in commercial activity. Do not download customer data you do not need.

## How should you price the first website maintenance plan in Canada?

Price the first website maintenance plan in Canada as a flat monthly fee for a named checklist and a capped number of small-change minutes, with anything larger quoted separately. This guide does not state a market rate for maintenance.

| Path | What the client buys | What you watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Checklist only | Updates, backups, monitoring, report | Clients assuming content edits are included |
| Checklist plus minutes | The above and a cap on small edits | Unused minutes and whether they roll over |
| One-time cleanup first | Overdue updates and fixes before monthly care | Old plugins that break when updated |

### Illustrative example

Suppose a shop pays an assumed $75 a month for a checklist plan. Suppose updates, checks, and the report take 1.5 hours a month. The sketch is $75 ÷ 1.5 = $50 an hour before tax set-aside. If one month a plugin update breaks a page and the fix takes 1.5 more hours, that month is $75 ÷ 3 = $25 an hour, which is why a one-time cleanup comes first on neglected sites. These figures are assumptions, not a quote, a forecast, or a typical result.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first website maintenance client look like?

A 14-day plan for a first website maintenance client in Canada should produce a test site with a documented restore, a written checklist, a sample report, and a short list of businesses to contact. A signed plan inside 14 days is not guaranteed.

### Days 1 and 2: Read the security basics

Read the Cyber Centre's top 10 actions. Turn them into lines on your checklist.

### Days 3 to 5: Build and break a test site

Set up a test site, back it up, update it, break something, and restore it. Write the steps down.

### Days 6 to 9: Write the offer and sample report

Write the monthly checklist, the scope limits, and a one-page sample report.

### Days 10 to 14: Contact five owners

Contact five businesses you know or that a designer referred, each with one visible issue. Follow up once.

## What mistakes block a first website maintenance client in Canada?

Mistakes that block a first website maintenance client in Canada are updating without a backup, asking for the owner's personal password, and leaving scope vague. Each one risks the site or the relationship.

Running scans on sites you have no permission to test is a problem in itself. Promising the site "can't be hacked" is a claim no one can keep. Emailing scraped lists skips consent.

## Which tax rules apply to a first website maintenance client in Canada?

Website maintenance income in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on the activity for profit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the agreement, monthly invoices, payments, and tool subscription receipts. Report the activity on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Website maintenance is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made, unless a platform rule you have read says the platform collects. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).
