Winter exam season gives Canadian tutors a concentrated burst of demand in late December and January. In British Columbia, the January 2027 graduation assessment session (Grade 10 Numeracy, Grade 10 Literacy, and Grade 12 Literacy) runs January 11 to 22. In Alberta, January diploma exams run January 14 to 28, 2027, including Mathematics 30-1 and 30-2 on January 20, Biology 30 on January 25, Chemistry 30 on January 26, and Physics 30 on January 27. Other provinces and boards set their own semester exams, so check local school calendars. Sell a short package of sessions that ends just before the exam, set the price before December, and note that tutoring in an elementary or secondary curriculum course is generally exempt from GST/HST.

## When are the January 2027 provincial exams?

The two provinces with published January provincial exam schedules covered here are British Columbia and Alberta. Dates are from each province's 2026–27 schedule; provinces can update schedules, so confirm before you sell a package.

| Province | Assessment or exam | January 2027 date(s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BC | Graduation assessments: Grade 10 Literacy, Grade 10 Numeracy, Grade 12 Literacy | January 11–22 session ([BC schedule](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/education/administration/kindergarten-to-grade-12/exams/graduation-assessment-schedule-2026-2027.pdf)) |
| Alberta | English Language Arts 30-1 and 30-2 | Part A January 18; Part B January 21 ([Alberta schedule](https://www.alberta.ca/system/files/custom_downloaded_images/edc-diploma-exam-schedule.pdf)) |
| Alberta | Social Studies 30-1 and 30-2 | Part A January 19; Part B January 22 |
| Alberta | Mathematics 30-1 and 30-2 | January 20 |
| Alberta | Biology 30 / Chemistry 30 / Physics 30 / Science 30 | January 25 / 26 / 27 / 28 |
| Alberta | Français and French Language Arts 30-1 | Partie A January 14; Partie B January 15 |

BC also has sessions in November 2026 (November 2–6), April 2027, and June 2027, and Alberta has a late October to November session and an April session, so the same approach works year-round.

## What should an exam-season tutoring package include?

A package works better than open-ended weekly sessions because the exam date sets the end point.

- **Diagnostic first session:** a past-paper or practice section to find weak topics.
- **Four to six focused sessions** in the three weeks before the exam.
- **A final review** two or three days before, not the night before.
- **Practice materials** from official provincial sources where available.
- **A clear cancellation policy** for snow days and illness.

## How should you price winter exam tutoring?

Price the package, not just the hour, and set it before December so families can book early. The figures below are illustrative only, not market rates; see [online tutoring pricing](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-pricing-canada/) for rate-setting.

### Illustrative example

Assume a $45 hourly rate, a five-session package of one hour each, and one 30-minute diagnostic included free. The rate and package shape are assumptions.

- **Package price:** 5 × $45 = $225.
- **Your hours:** 5.5 hours of sessions plus about 1.5 hours of prep = 7 hours, so about $32 an hour effective.
- **Six students in January:** 6 × $225 = $1,350 before tax and CPP. Six packages means about 42 hours across three weeks, which is the real capacity limit.

## What safety and trust steps apply when tutoring minors?

Families will ask about safety, so have answers ready.

- **Vulnerable sector checks:** the RCMP says the hiring organization must request a vulnerable sector check, and the person being checked only gives consent ([vulnerable sector checks](https://rcmp.ca/en/criminal-records/criminal-record-checks/vulnerable-sector-checks)). As an independent tutor you may not be able to request one for yourself; a tutoring agency or school program may. Ask your local police what checks they offer to individuals.
- **Session setup:** keep online sessions in a shared family space, with a parent able to join.
- **Communication:** message parents rather than students directly for scheduling and payment.

## What mistakes do tutors make in exam season?

The common mistakes are booking too late and overfilling the final week.

- **Opening bookings in January.** Families plan in December.
- **Overbooking the final week.** Everyone wants the last three days.
- **Using the wrong course content.** Alberta 30-1 and 30-2 differ; confirm the exact course.
- **No package end date.** Tie the package to the exam date.
- **Ignoring time zones online.** Confirm local time for each family.

## Which tax rules apply to tutoring income in Canada?

Tutoring income is generally business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed tutors pay both shares of CPP, 11.9% on pensionable earnings above the $3,500 exemption in 2026 ([CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html)). For GST/HST, the CRA's [tutoring info sheet](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/20-6/tutoring-equivalent-services.html) says tutoring or instructing an individual in a course approved for credit by, or following a curriculum designated by, a school authority is exempt; the exemption does not apply to post-secondary courses.
