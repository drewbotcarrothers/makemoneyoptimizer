<QuickAnswer>

- **Formula:** price per job = (take-home per hour ÷ (1 − set-aside rate)) × real hours per job + costs per job.
- **CPP in 2026:** self-employed people pay 11.9% (5.95% twice) on earnings between $3,500 and $74,600, up to $8,460.90 a year (CRA).
- **Wage benchmarks (Job Bank medians):** dog walker $18.00, house cleaner $19.74, lawn care $20.75, handyman $25.00, bookkeeper $28.02, graphic designer $31.25 in Canada.
- **Worked result:** keeping $30 an hour at a 30% set-aside means charging $42.86 an hour; a 2.5-hour handyman job with $12 of supplies is $120.
- **Rules:** advertised prices must be attainable, only sales tax goes on top, and GST/HST registration starts after $30,000.

</QuickAnswer>

A side hustle price starts with the hourly amount you want to keep, grosses it up for the income tax and CPP you'll owe, multiplies by the real hours each job takes and adds the job's costs. Only then do you compare it with the market. Self-employed people pay both halves of CPP, 5.95% each in 2026 on earnings between $3,500 and $74,600 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/canada-pension-plan-cpp/cpp-contribution-rates-maximums-exemptions.html)), and nothing is withheld for you. Job Bank's wage medians give the floor: an employed handyman earns a $25.00 median, a house cleaner $19.74 and a dog walker $18.00. Your price has to beat those after tax, travel and supplies, or the job pays less than employment.

This worksheet is the general method. Hustle-specific guides add published local prices: see [handyman pricing](/side-hustles/handyman-pricing-canada/), [car detailing pricing](/side-hustles/car-detailing-pricing-canada/), [moving help pricing](/side-hustles/moving-help-pricing-canada/), [website maintenance pricing](/side-hustles/website-maintenance-pricing-canada/) and the rest of the pricing guides. For the plan this price sits in, see [the one-page side hustle business plan](/side-hustles/side-hustle-business-plan-canada/).

| Line | What to enter | Example (assumed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Take-home per hour | What you want to keep after tax and CPP | $20 |
| B. Set-aside rate | Your income tax plus CPP estimate | 25% |
| C. Gross hourly | A ÷ (1 − B) | $26.67 |
| D. Hours per job | Billable time plus travel, quoting, admin | 1.5 + 0.5 = 2.0 |
| E. Costs per job | Supplies, fuel, platform fees | $6 |
| F. Price per job | (C × D) + E | $59.33 |
| G. Market check | Job Bank median and published local prices | Compare, then adjust |

## How do you fill in a side hustle pricing worksheet?

Work down the lines once, then revisit them after a month of real jobs.

### Step 1: Line A, the take-home you want

Pick an hourly amount that makes the work worth your evening. Your province's minimum wage is a floor to beat: $17.95 in Ontario from October 1, 2026 ([Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage)) and $18.25 in British Columbia from June 1, 2026 ([BC](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage)). The Job Bank medians below are a better target, because they're what employers pay for the same work.

### Step 2: Line B, the set-aside rate

This covers income tax at your marginal rate plus self-employed CPP. If you have a job, side income is added on top of your salary and taxed at your marginal rate. [How much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) shows how to estimate it; the examples here use 25% to 35%.

### Step 3: Line C, gross it up

Divide A by (1 − B). At a 25% set-aside, keeping $20 means charging $26.67 per hour of work.

### Step 4: Line D, real hours per job

Add travel, setup, quoting, messages and invoicing to the billable time. Leaving this line out quietly lowers your real hourly rate.

### Step 5: Line E, costs per job

Supplies used, fuel, parking, platform or payment fees. Only business costs count, and only the business share of mixed costs ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses.html)). Startup purchases go in the plan, not each job; see your hustle's startup costs guide, such as [lawn care startup costs](/side-hustles/lawn-care-startup-costs-canada/).

### Step 6: Line F, the price

(C × D) + E. Round to a clean number clients understand.

### Step 7: Line G, the market check

Compare with the Job Bank median for the occupation and with published local prices in the hustle's pricing guide. If yours is far higher, look for hours or costs you can cut, or a more specific offer. If it's far lower, you've probably left something out.

## How much does CPP add to a side hustle price?

Self-employed people pay the employee and employer shares of CPP on net self-employment income. The CRA's 2026 figures:

| 2026 CPP figure | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Basic exemption | $3,500 |
| Maximum pensionable earnings | $74,600 |
| Contribution rate, employee and employer each | 5.95% |
| Self-employed rate (both shares) | 11.9% |
| Maximum self-employed contribution | $8,460.90 |

If your job already uses the $3,500 exemption and your total earnings stay under $74,600, every extra $10,000 of side hustle profit carries $1,190 of CPP before income tax. If your job pay is already above $74,600, base CPP is maxed through work. The CRA's page also lists second additional contributions (CPP2) on earnings above the maximum. Source: [CRA CPP rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/canada-pension-plan-cpp/cpp-contribution-rates-maximums-exemptions.html).

## What do worked prices look like?

**Illustrative.** Take-home targets, set-aside rates, hours and costs are assumed. CPP rates, Job Bank wages and Etsy fees are published.

### Example 1: Ontario handyman, 2.5-hour job

- Keep $30 an hour; set aside 30%: $30 ÷ 0.70 = $42.86 an hour.
- 2 hours on site plus 0.5 hours of travel and quoting; $12 of fasteners and caulk.
- Price: $42.86 × 2.5 + $12 = $119.15, quote **$120**.
- Market check: the Ontario handyman median is $24.00, so the gross rate is about 1.8 times the employee wage, which is where it should be once tax, CPP and travel are covered.

### Example 2: BC dog walker, 30-minute walk

- Keep $20 an hour; set aside 25%: $26.67 an hour.
- 30-minute walk plus 15 minutes of travel and messages: 0.75 hours; $1 of bags and treats.
- Price: $26.67 × 0.75 + $1 = **$21 a walk**.
- Market check: the BC dog walker median is $19.50 an hour. Group walks or walks on one street raise your effective rate; see [dog walking pricing](/side-hustles/dog-walking-pricing-canada/).

### Example 3: Ontario bookkeeper, monthly client

- Keep $35 an hour; set aside 35%: $35 ÷ 0.65 = $53.85 an hour.
- 6 hours of bookkeeping plus 1 hour of admin a month; $20 a month of software allocated to this client.
- Price: $53.85 × 7 + $20 = $396.92, quote **$400 a month**, or $452 with 13% HST if registered.
- Market check: the Ontario bookkeeper median is $28.50; [freelance bookkeeping pricing](/side-hustles/freelance-bookkeeping-pricing-canada/) has published firm rates.

### Example 4: Handmade item sold on Etsy

- Keep $20 an hour; set aside 25%: $26.67 an hour. 30 minutes per item: $13.33.
- Materials $8, packaging $1: $22.33 before fees.
- Etsy's fees are 10% of the order plus about $0.53 per order for a Canadian seller, so price = ($22.33 + $0.53) ÷ 0.90 = $25.40, list at **$26**.
- Market check against similar listings; [Etsy shop pricing](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/) has the fee details.

## Which templates turn the worksheet into prices?

The worksheet itself, one per service:

```text
PRICING WORKSHEET — [service]                    Updated: [date]
A. Take-home per hour ...................... $[30.00]
B. Set-aside rate (tax + CPP) .............. [30]%
C. Gross hourly  A ÷ (1 − B) ............... $[42.86]
D. Hours per job (work + travel + admin) ... [2.0] + [0.5] = [2.5]
E. Costs per job (supplies, fuel, fees) .... $[12.00]
F. Price per job  C × D + E ................ $[119.15] → quote $[120]
G. Market check: Job Bank median $[24.00]; local prices $[ ]–$[ ]
Revisit after [10] jobs: actual hours [ ], actual costs $[ ]
```

A written quote built from line F:

```text
QUOTE — [client]                                Date: [date]
[Service], [scope in one line] .................... $[120.00]
Includes: [labour, travel, standard supplies]
Not included: [parts over $X, extra visits]
Price valid [30] days. GST/HST added only if registered.
Final price won't exceed this quote by more than 10% without your
written approval.
```

A rate-increase note when line A or E changes:

```text
Hi [Name], from [date] my rate for [service] moves from $[120] to
$[130]. Supply and fuel costs went up this year. Bookings made
before [date] keep the current price. Thanks for your business.
— [Your name]
```

## How do you raise prices with the worksheet?

1. **Re-run it every quarter.** Replace assumed hours and costs with your real averages; the [quarterly money check-in](/side-hustles/quarterly-side-hustle-money-check-in-canada/) is a good time.
2. **Raise line A as your calendar fills.** A full schedule means your take-home target is too low.
3. **Raise line B when your income rises.** A higher marginal tax rate needs a bigger gross-up.
4. **Cut line D before you cut price.** Grouping jobs by neighbourhood removes travel hours.
5. **Give notice.** The [guide to raising side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/) covers timing for repeat clients.

## What pricing rules apply to side hustles in Canada?

- **All-in pricing.** The Competition Bureau says advertising a price that can't be attained because of mandatory added fees is against the law; only government charges such as sales tax can be added ([Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/drip-pricing)). Build fees into line E.
- **Written estimates in Ontario.** If a written estimate is part of a consumer contract, the final price can't be more than 10% above it unless the client agrees ([Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract)).
- **GST/HST.** Once total taxable sales from all your businesses pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, you must register and charge GST/HST on top ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

## What pricing mistakes do side hustlers make?

- **Pricing at the employee wage.** A $25 handyman rate matches the Job Bank median before tax, CPP and travel, so you earn less than an employee.
- **Leaving out travel and admin time.** Half an hour per job cuts a $42.86 rate to $34.29 on a 2-hour job.
- **Forgetting the CPP gross-up.** It's 11.9% of profit when job pay plus side profit stays under $74,600.
- **Adding a mandatory "service fee" at checkout** instead of building it into the price.
- **Never revisiting the worksheet** after real jobs show the true hours.

## How does your price affect your side hustle taxes?

Your price sets revenue, which goes on Form T2125 with your expenses. Line B keeps the tax money in your account until you file. Accounting software such as <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can store your price list and track actual hours per job, which is what you need to refine lines D and E. For year-end, see [side hustle tax prep before January](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-prep-before-january-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

CPP figures are the CRA's for 2026. Job Bank wages are medians for employees from 2023–2024 data, updated November 19, 2025, so they lag current pay and exclude self-employed people. Minimum wages are Ontario's and BC's for 2026. Take-home targets, set-aside rates, hours and costs in the examples are illustrative; replace them with your own after ten jobs.
