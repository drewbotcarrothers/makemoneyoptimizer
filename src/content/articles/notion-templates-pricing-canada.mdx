## How do you calculate a Notion template price?

```text
Notion net (CAD)   = (price × 0.92 − 0.40) × 0.99 × USD/CAD rate
Gumroad net (CAD)  = (price × 0.90 − 0.50) × USD/CAD rate      (direct link)
Discover net (CAD) = price × 0.70 × USD/CAD rate
Break-even sales   = build hours × hourly value ÷ net per sale
Custom setup       = hours × hourly rate (+ GST/HST if registered)
```

**Place the template on the price ladder.** A single tracker sits near US$5, a working system for one job near US$29, a full business or life system at US$69 to US$129. **Run the CAD net.** The examples use the Bank of Canada rate of [1.4240 on October 8, 2026](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/). **Then count sales.** If break-even needs hundreds of sales, the template is too small or the price too low.

## What do worked Notion template examples look like?

**Illustrative.** Build hours, the C$40 hourly value, the C$75 setup rate and the prices are my assumptions; fees are each platform's published rate.

### Example 1: a US$12 budget tracker on Notion Marketplace

- Fee: 8% × $12 + $0.40 = $1.36, leaving US$10.64; 1% FX leaves US$10.53, about **C$15.00**.
- Build: 15 hours × C$40 = C$600. C$600 ÷ C$15.00 = **40 sales** to break even.

### Example 2: a US$29 freelancer CRM on three channels

- Notion Marketplace: ($29 × 0.92 − $0.40) × 0.99 × 1.4240 = **C$37.05**.
- Gumroad direct link: ($29 × 0.90 − $0.50) × 1.4240 = **C$36.45**. Gumroad Discover: $29 × 0.70 × 1.4240 = **C$28.91**.
- Same anchor as StudioOps' US$29 CRM. At 15 build hours, break-even on Notion is C$600 ÷ C$37.05 = 16.2, so 17 sales.

### Example 3: a three-template bundle

- Singles at US$5, US$12 and US$29 total US$46. Bundle at **US$39** nets about C$50.02 on Notion, against C$58.97 if one buyer bought all three singly (C$5.92 + C$15.00 + C$37.05). The bundle trades C$8.95 for a sale that might not happen otherwise.

### Example 4: custom setup for a Toronto small business, HST-registered

- 10 hours at C$75 = C$750, template included. Anchors: $40 an hour in Montreal, from $650 in Toronto, $175 an hour in Vancouver.
- With 13% HST: C$750 × 1.13 = **C$847.50**. Not registered: C$750, no tax line.

## How do you raise Notion template prices?

- **Launch low, then step up.** Move to the full price after a fixed number of buyers, as in the template, and say so on the listing.
- **Raise with each real addition**: a guide, a video walkthrough, a feature buyers asked for. Existing buyers keep what they bought.
- **Add a premium tier** rather than raising the base: the template alone, and the template plus a setup video or call.
- **Sell setup separately** at consultant rates; the gap between US$29 and $650 is the reason.
- **Change one product at a time** and compare net per week. Scripts are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What Notion template pricing mistakes are common?

Forgetting the USD-to-CAD step, ignoring the fixed per-sale fee on very cheap templates, and giving away setup help. On a US$2 sale, the fixed $0.40 is a fifth of the price before the 8%. Pricing below the free template next to yours signals nothing; pricing above it needs a clear reason on the listing.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect Notion template pricing?

Template sales are self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both [CPP shares](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. GST/HST registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): taxable supplies over $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, across all your businesses.

Notion and Gumroad each say they act as merchant of record and handle sales tax on their sales; the CRA explains platform rules in its [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html). Custom setup you invoice yourself follows the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) once you're registered. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers the steps.
