Pricing Notion templates from Canada means choosing a US-dollar price, because Notion Marketplace sales are in USD, then checking what lands in your account after fees and conversion. Notion charges 8% plus $0.40 per transaction and a further 1% foreign exchange fee on payouts to creators outside the US. Gumroad charges 10% plus $0.50 on sales through your own links and 30% on sales through its Discover marketplace. Your price is then set by how much time the template saves, how complete it is, and how it compares with similar templates. This guide suits creators who will do the fee math before setting a price.

## What fees do Notion Marketplace and Gumroad charge template sellers?

Notion Marketplace charges template sellers 8% plus $0.40 per transaction, according to Notion's [selling on Marketplace help page](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace). [Gumroad's pricing page](https://gumroad.com/pricing) lists 10% plus $0.50 on direct sales and 30% through Discover.

| Rule | Notion Marketplace | Gumroad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction fee | 8% + $0.40 | 10% + $0.50 (your profile or direct links); 30% (Discover marketplace) |
| Currency | All template sales in USD | Gumroad does not name the currency on its pricing page; examples here assume US dollars |
| Foreign exchange | Extra 1% fee to convert payouts for creators outside the US | Depends on payout method |
| Sales tax | Notion is merchant of record and handles regional sales tax or VAT | Gumroad says it has been merchant of record since January 1, 2025 and handles sales tax |
| Payouts | Biweekly, $20 USD minimum, funds held 14 days | Varies by country and method |
| Refunds | Buyers on Marketplace can request a refund within 14 days | Set by Gumroad and your policy |

Both say you remain responsible for your own income tax.

## Which pricing models work for Notion templates in Canada?

Notion template pricing models for Canadian sellers are a free template to build an audience, a single paid template, a bundle, tiers of the same product, and custom setup work priced by the hour.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free template | Listed free to collect emails or reviews | Building an audience | Free forever with no paid next step |
| Single paid template | One price for one template | Focused tools (budget, habit, CRM) | Pricing a tiny tool like a full system |
| Bundle | Several templates at less than their total | Related tools | Bundles that undercut every single sale |
| Tiers | Basic template and a premium version with guides or video | Complex systems | Support load on the premium tier |
| Custom setup | Hourly or fixed-price build for one client | Businesses wanting a tailored workspace | Selling custom work at template prices |

Check comparable templates' prices on the marketplace you use before choosing, rather than relying on a general "average".

## How do you calculate a Notion template price in Canada?

Calculate a Notion template price in Canada by working out your net per sale in Canadian dollars at a few USD prices, then dividing your build time and costs by that net to see how many sales you need.

### Step 1: Count your build time and costs

Hours to build, test, write instructions, and make previews, plus any paid tools.

### Step 2: Work out net per sale

Price minus the platform fee, minus any FX fee, converted to CAD.

### Step 3: Calculate break-even sales

Costs ÷ net per sale. Value your build hours at a rate you choose.

### Step 4: Compare with similar templates

Price relative to depth, polish, and support.

### Step 5: Set aside tax

Sales are self-employment income; see the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a forecast, average, or survey. Conversions use the Bank of Canada rate of 1.4246 for October 2, 2026; the rate applied to your payout may differ.

Suppose you price a template at US$12 on Notion Marketplace. The fee is 8% × $12 + $0.40 = $0.96 + $0.40 = $1.36, leaving US$10.64. The 1% FX fee on the payout is about $0.11, leaving about US$10.53, which is about C$15.01 (10.5336 × 1.4246).

The same template at US$12 through a Gumroad direct link pays 10% × $12 + $0.50 = $1.70 in fees, leaving US$10.30, about C$14.67. Through Gumroad Discover, the 30% fee is $3.60, leaving US$8.40, about C$11.97.

Suppose the template took 15 hours to build and you value your time at C$40 an hour: C$600. At about C$15.01 net per sale, you need 40 sales to recover that time (C$600 ÷ C$15.01 = 39.97).

## When should you raise Notion template prices in Canada?

Raise Notion template prices in Canada when you add features, guides, or video walkthroughs, when reviews show strong value, or when a template sells steadily at its current price. Change one product at a time and compare net revenue per week.

Existing buyers keep what they bought; a price rise only affects new sales.

## What Notion template pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common Notion template pricing mistakes in Canada are forgetting the USD-to-CAD conversion, ignoring the fixed per-sale fee on very cheap templates, and giving away custom setup help for free. Each lowers your real earnings.

On a US$2 sale, the fixed $0.40 is a fifth of the price before the percentage fee. Price support and custom work separately.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect Notion template pricing in Canada?

Template sales in Canada are self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both [CPP shares](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net income. GST/HST registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all your side hustles.

Notion and Gumroad each say they act as merchant of record and handle sales tax on their sales. The CRA explains platform rules in its [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html), and the [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) covers sales you make yourself, such as custom setup work. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration.
