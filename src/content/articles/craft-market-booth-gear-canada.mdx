## What should you look for in craft market booth gear?

| Item | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canopy (outdoor) | Size matching your booth (often 10×10 ft), straight legs, sidewalls | Many outdoor markets require tents to be weighted |
| Weights | Leg weights or sandbags | Wind is the most common tent hazard |
| Table | Folding, height-adjustable if possible | Raises products closer to eye level |
| Tablecloth | Floor-length, fitted, wrinkle-resistant | Hides stock boxes; looks finished |
| Risers and displays | Crates, tiered stands, grid walls | Height draws the eye from the aisle |
| Lighting | Battery LED clip lights or strips | Indoor halls and winter evenings are dim |
| Signage | Banner, price cards, QR code to your shop | Shoppers rarely ask prices |
| Payments | Card reader plus charged phone and backup battery | Many shoppers expect to tap |

## What should Canadian vendors know?

- **Cold outdoor markets.** Layer up, bring a mat to stand on and plan warm-up breaks; CCOHS covers [working in the cold](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/phys_agents/cold/cold_working.html). Batteries drain faster in the cold, so keep the card reader and phone warm.
- **GST/HST.** Market sales count toward the [$30,000 small supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html).
- **Food and cottage goods.** Rules vary by province; see [home baking](/side-hustles/home-baking-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Insurance.** Some markets ask for liability insurance; compare [APOLLO vs Zensurance](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/).
