**Quick answer:** a lean handyman kit in Canada costs about **$230** (20V drill, tape, level, stud finder, bits, glasses, shoe covers and a tool bag, 13% Ontario HST included). A standard kit with a DEWALT 12V drill, an oscillating multi-tool and better detection costs about **$532 plus liability insurance from $450 a year**, and a pro kit that adds moving gear for furniture and TV jobs costs about **$667 plus $700 or more a year for liability and tools cover**. Prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026. At a two-hour job billed at $85 an hour, the lean kit pays back on the second job and the pro kit with insurance on the ninth.

The [handyman side hustle guide](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/) covers which jobs to take and which to refuse. This page is the cash: what to buy, what it cost on a specific date, and how many jobs recover it.

## How much does it cost to start handyman work in Canada?

Handyman work costs $229.98 to start with a lean kit, $982.02 with a standard kit and a starting liability policy, and $1,367.21 with a pro kit and liability plus tools cover. Gear totals are Amazon.ca prices from October 9, 2026, with 13% Ontario HST.

| Kit | Gear before tax | Gear with 13% HST | Insurance (per year) | First-year cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $203.52 | $229.98 | Quote before the first paid job | $229.98 |
| Standard | $470.81 | $532.02 | From $450 | $982.02 |
| Pro | $590.45 | $667.21 | From $700 (liability + tools, Ontario) | $1,367.21 |

At 5% GST (Alberta, the territories), the gear totals are $213.70, $494.35 and $619.97. The [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province's rate.

## Who is a handyman startup budget a fit for in Canada?

This budget fits people who already do small repairs at home, own or can borrow a car, and want evening and weekend jobs: shelves, TV mounts, furniture assembly, caulking, door adjustments and drywall patches. It is a poor fit if the work you want is electrical, gas, plumbing that opens a wall, or structural, because those are licensed trades. In Ontario, the [Electrical Safety Authority](https://esasafe.com/doing-electrical-work/who-can-do-electrical-work-at-my-property/) says only Licensed Electrical Contractors (with some exemptions, such as HVAC installers) are legally authorized to do electrical work in a home, apart from the homeowner or occupant; in Quebec, the [RBQ](https://www.rbq.gouv.qc.ca/en/you-are/citizen/renovation-work/work-you-can-do-yourself) lists what homeowners may do themselves and what needs a licensed contractor.

Outdoor work on the same street is [lawn care startup costs](/side-hustles/lawn-care-startup-costs-canada/) and [snow removal startup costs](/side-hustles/snow-removal-startup-costs-canada/). App-based small jobs are in [odd-jobs apps startup costs](/side-hustles/odd-jobs-apps-startup-costs-canada/).

## How do ongoing costs change handyman break-even in Canada?

**Jobs to break even = first-year cash ÷ revenue per job.** Two published price points work as inputs. [HomeStars' handyman cost guide](https://www.homestars.com/handyman-services/price-guides/handyman-cost) (updated August 21, 2026) lists typical rates of $85 to $110 an hour and small jobs at $130 to $250. [TaskRabbit's Canadian cost guide](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/cost-guides/general-handyman) (updated October 1, 2026) shows an average general-handyman invoice of $98 in Toronto, $96 in Calgary and $64 in Winnipeg.

| Kit | First-year cash | ÷ $170 (2 hours at $85) | ÷ $98 (Toronto average invoice) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $229.98 | 1.35, so 2 jobs | 2.35, so 3 jobs |
| Standard + $450 insurance | $982.02 | 5.78, so 6 jobs | 10.02, so 11 jobs |
| Pro + $700 insurance | $1,367.21 | 8.04, so 9 jobs | 13.95, so 14 jobs |

### Illustrative example: a first month on the standard kit

**Assumed inputs:** eight two-hour jobs at $85 an hour and $12 per job in fuel, anchors, caulk and blades. **Sourced inputs:** the $85 hourly rate (HomeStars' low end) and the standard kit and insurance totals above.

- Revenue: 8 × $170 = **$1,360**
- Variable costs: 8 × $12 = **$96**
- First month: $1,360 − $96 − $982.02 = **$281.98**
- Each later month at the same pace: $1,360 − $96 = **$1,264** before tax and your time

For comparison, Job Bank's [wage data for building handypersons](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/14385/ca) shows a Canadian median of $25.00 an hour for employees. The gap between that and $85 an hour billed is what pays for your tools, insurance, travel and unpaid quoting time. ## Where can a handyman budget in Canada stay smaller?

- **Buy for the jobs you will advertise.** Mounting and assembly need the lean kit; drywall and trim work justify the oscillating tool.
- **Rent rare tools.** A tile saw or a long ladder for a one-off job is a rental, not a purchase.
- **Use free invoicing first.** <AffiliateLink program="wave" label="Wave" /> handles a few invoices a month at no cost; <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> or <AffiliateLink program="jobber" label="Jobber" /> earn their fee once you are booking every week.
- **Stay on one battery platform.** A second brand of cordless tools means a second charger and a second set of batteries.

## Where should a handyman budget in Canada refuse to shrink?

- **Detection before drilling.** A stud finder and the right anchor are what keep a TV on the wall.
- **Eye protection.** $17.99 is the cheapest insurance in the kit.
- **Liability insurance before the first paying client.** A cracked tile or a fallen mount is the typical claim; compare quote routes in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).
- **Licensed-trade limits.** Electrical, gas and wall-opening plumbing stay with licensed contractors, whatever tools you own.

## What mistakes make handyman startup costs higher?

1. **Buying a 100-piece combo kit before the first job.** Most first jobs use a drill, a level and a stud finder.
2. **Pricing per job without a minimum.** A $98 invoice that takes three hours with travel pays less than the employee median.
3. **Skipping the oscillating tool, then turning down drywall work.** At $79.99 it opens a common job category.
4. **Mixing tool money with personal spending.** <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that keeps job income separate.
5. **Assuming home insurance covers paid work.** It typically does not; a commercial policy starts at $450 a year.

## What could change these handyman numbers?

Province, insurance and job mix move these totals most: 13% HST versus 5% GST changes the gear line by up to $47, the published insurance starting points run from $450 to $2,000 a year depending on coverage and scope, and the jobs you advertise decide whether the oscillating tool and moving gear earn their place. Tool prices were checked on October 9, 2026 and move with sales. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

## How do taxes and vehicle claims work for handyman work in Canada?

Handyman income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Consumables such as anchors, caulk and blades are ordinary expenses; drills and other tools are equipment claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html). Keep a log of business kilometres if you claim the business share of your vehicle costs.

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting all your side hustles. Handyman work is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. Self-employed profit also means paying both shares of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).
