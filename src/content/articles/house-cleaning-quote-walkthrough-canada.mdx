To quote a house cleaning job in Canada, gather the basics by message, walk through the home, and write a scope that lists every room and task. Then price the first clean and regular visits separately and send the quote in writing. The walkthrough is where most underpricing happens: a home that sounds small can hide pets, clutter or a neglected oven. This guide is for new cleaners who already have a target hourly rate and need a repeatable way to turn it into quotes.

It builds on [house cleaning pricing](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-pricing-canada/), which shows how to set the hourly target. If you're starting from zero, read the [house cleaning side hustle guide](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) first.

## What should you ask before quoting a house cleaning job in Canada?

### Step 1: Send five intake questions

1. How many bedrooms and bathrooms, and roughly how large is the home?
2. Is this a one-time, move-out, or regular clean, and how often?
3. Any pets, and do they shed?
4. Which areas matter most, and anything you don't want touched?
5. Do you want me to bring supplies, or use yours?

### Step 2: Do the walkthrough

Spend 10 to 15 minutes in the home, or ask for a short video call. Look at the kitchen, bathrooms and floors, because they drive time. Note buildup on the stove, inside the oven and fridge, on shower glass, and on baseboards. Ask where clutter will be before you arrive, since tidying isn't cleaning.

### Step 3: Estimate time room by room

Write a minute estimate for each room, add travel and setup, and compare it with your past jobs and with SNAM Cleaning's published 3 to 3.5 hours for a two-bedroom maintenance clean. First cleans usually take longer than maintenance visits because buildup has to come off once.

## How do you turn a walkthrough into a price in Canada?

### Step 4: Price two numbers

Multiply your total minutes by your target hourly rate from the [pricing guide](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-pricing-canada/), then add supplies. Quote the first deep clean as one price and the recurring visit as another. Don't absorb the deep clean into a weekly rate. Clients understand the difference when you show both.

### Step 5: List exclusions

Write what's not included: inside the oven, inside the fridge, interior windows, walls, laundry, dishes, and biohazards. Offer the common ones as priced add-ons so the client can choose.

### Step 6: Send a written quote

Include the scope checklist, both prices, supplies, cancellation and lockout terms, payment method, and how prices change. In Ontario, the province says that if a written estimate is part of the contract, the [final price can't be more than 10% above it](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) unless the client agrees to a new price and signs a change. Use clear quotes in every province. If a job turns out bigger, stop and agree on a revised price before continuing.

### Illustrative example

Suppose your walkthrough of a two-bedroom apartment adds up to 3 hours for a first clean and 2 hours for maintenance, plus 20 minutes of travel. You'd quote the first clean at about 3 hours 20 minutes of your rate plus supplies, and the regular visit at about 2 hours 20 minutes. Inside the oven and fridge would be listed as add-ons, not quietly included. The client would sign the scope before the first visit. At SNAM's published $50 an hour, that is $50 × 3.33 h ≈ $167 for the first clean and $50 × 2.33 h ≈ $117 for the regular visit, plus supplies; at Maidless's Vancouver list, the same apartment is $317 + GST for a first deep clean.

## What templates can you copy for a cleaning quote?

**Intake message**

```text
Thanks for reaching out! To give you an accurate quote, could you tell me:
1) Bedrooms/bathrooms and rough square footage
2) One-time, move-out, or regular (weekly / every 2 weeks / monthly)?
3) Any pets? Do they shed?
4) Top priorities, and anything I shouldn't touch?
5) Should I bring supplies, or use yours?
A few photos of the kitchen and bathrooms help me quote without surprises.
```

**Walkthrough sheet (minutes per room)**

```text
Room            Standard  First/deep  Notes (buildup, clutter, surfaces)
Kitchen         [  ]      [  ]        oven? fridge? stone counters?
Bathroom 1      [  ]      [  ]        glass shower? hard water?
Bathroom 2      [  ]      [  ]
Bedrooms (x__)  [  ]      [  ]        clutter on floors?
Living/dining   [  ]      [  ]        pet hair?
Floors          [  ]      [  ]        hardwood / tile / carpet
Travel + setup  [  ]      [  ]
TOTAL minutes   [  ]      [  ]   x rate/60 = $____ / $____  + supplies $__
```

**Written quote**

```text
Quote for [client], [address]  Date: [date]  Valid 30 days
Regular clean ([frequency]): $[price]. First (deep) clean: $[price].
Includes: [room-by-room checklist]
Not included (add-ons): inside oven $[x], inside fridge $[x],
interior windows $[x], laundry, dishes, walls, biohazards.
Supplies: [mine/yours]. Payment: [e-transfer] on the day.
Cancellation: 48 hours' notice; lockout or same-day cancel = $[x].
If the job is larger than quoted, I'll stop and agree a new price with you first.
Prices are the full price; GST/HST added only if I'm registered.
```

## What should a Canadian cleaning quote never do?

- **Hide mandatory fees.** The Competition Bureau says promoting a price that [isn't attainable because of fixed fees](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/drip-pricing) is against the law, apart from government charges like sales tax. If you always charge a supply fee, put it in the price.
- **Quote by phone without seeing anything.** Ask for photos or a video at minimum.
- **Promise "everything."** A scope list protects both sides.
- **Leave out cancellation terms.** Lockouts and same-day cancellations cost you a slot.

## What mistakes do new cleaners make when quoting in Canada?

- **Quoting the first clean at the maintenance price.** Buildup makes the first visit longer.
- **Forgetting travel and setup.** They're part of your time.
- **No exclusions list.** Ovens and interior windows become free extras.
- **Never revisiting the quote.** Re-time the home after three visits and adjust if needed.

## How are house cleaning quotes taxed in Canada?

Cleaning income is reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). You register for GST/HST once your taxable sales pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). After that, add the tax as a separate line on the quote. The published prices are companies' own list prices, checked on the dates shown on their pages, and include staff, insurance and supplies a solo beginner may not have. Use them as a ceiling and a sense check, not as your rate. The Ontario 10% estimate rule is from the province's consumer contract page; other provinces have their own consumer protection laws.
