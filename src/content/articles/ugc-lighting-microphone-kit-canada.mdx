<QuickAnswer>

- **Short answer:** start with the phone you have, then add sound before light: a wireless or wired lavalier mic, one soft key light and a stable phone tripod cover most UGC briefs.
- **Lights:** a soft panel or small softbox beats a bright bare bulb; adjustable colour temperature helps match window light.
- **Mics:** lavaliers for talking-to-camera; a small shotgun mic for product demos where a clip-on would show.
- **Prices:** see current price; this guide is about what to look for, not specific models.

</QuickAnswer>

Brands hiring UGC creators mostly want clear sound, even light and a steady frame, so the kit is small. This guide explains what to look for and links to browse on Amazon.ca. ## What should you look for in UGC lighting?

| Feature | What it means | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Softness | How diffused the light is | A panel with a diffuser, softbox or ring light with a diffuser |
| Colour temperature | Warm to cool (measured in kelvin) | Adjustable, so you can match a window |
| CRI | How accurately colours render | A high CRI rating matters for products and skin tones |
| Power | Mains, USB-C or battery | Battery or USB-C for shooting anywhere in the house |
| Mounting | Light stand, desk clamp or tripod thread | A stand tall enough to light from above eye level |

## What should Canadian UGC creators know?

- **Room sound matters as much as the mic.** Soft furnishings reduce echo; shoot away from fridges and furnaces.
- **Protect your hearing when editing.** Keep headphone volume moderate; CCOHS explains how [noise exposure](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/phys_agents/noise/noise_basic.html) adds up.
- **Deductions.** Gear bought for paid UGC work is generally a [business expense](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses.html); larger items may go through [CCA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html). Free products from brands can be taxable income too.
