What you buy is what makes those videos look and sound better: a tripod, a microphone, a light, and possibly a gimbal and editing tools. ## How much does a TikTok and Reels side hustle cost to start in Canada?

A TikTok and Reels side hustle in Canada costs about $58 to start with a tripod and a wired mic, about $330 with a wireless mic, a light and Canva Pro, and about $512 with a ring light and gimbal, or about $1,077 with a year of insurance. All figures use Amazon.ca and vendor prices checked on October 9, 2026, with 13% Ontario HST. None includes a new phone.

| Kit | Cash to start with HST | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $58.38 | Tripod with remote, wired lavalier mic, free editing apps |
| Standard | $329.93 | Tripod, wireless mic, softbox light, Canva Pro for a year |
| Pro | $511.61 | Tripod, wireless mic, ring light, gimbal, Canva Pro for a year |
| Pro with insurance | about $1,076.61 | Pro kit plus about $565 for a year of videography insurance |

## Who should use a TikTok and Reels startup budget in Canada?

This budget suits people who can film a clear point in under a minute, edit quickly, and show up on time for a client shoot. The lean kit suits filming your own test clips or a first client. The standard kit suits regular client work indoors. The pro kit suits filming on location, walking shots and paid shoots where a contract asks for insurance.

It's a poor fit if you need money this week, or if the plan relies on going viral. Paying clients care about clear video, good sound and showing up on schedule, not follower counts.

## What equipment do you need for TikTok and Reels in Canada?

You need a phone that shoots clean vertical video, a way to hold it steady, and decent sound. Light comes next. Prices below were read on Amazon.ca product pages on October 9, 2026.

| Item | Product and price | What it fixes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone | One you already own: $0 | Most recent phones shoot good vertical video |
| Tripod | [EUCOS 62-inch phone tripod with remote](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B09XHZ8F7F), $29.99 | Steady shots and filming yourself |
| Wired mic | [BOYA BY-M1 lavalier](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B00MRMU2HU), $21.67 | Clearer voice than the phone's built-in mic. No batteries |
| Wireless mic | [DJI Mic Mini (1 TX + 1 RX)](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0DDLCLFFX), $59.99 | Sound without a cable, for walking shots and interviews |
| Softbox light | [EMART 16 × 16 in. softbox kit](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0BGPSLDLJ), $59.99 | Soft, even light in small rooms |
| Ring light | [NEEWER 18-inch ring light kit](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B01LXDNNBW), $140.79 | Even face light; includes stand and phone holder |
| Gimbal | [DJI Osmo Mobile 7](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B07FSS4R16), $79.98 | Smooth handheld and walking shots |

**Sound before light.** Viewers will forgive slightly dark video more than muffled speech. If you buy one thing after a tripod, buy a mic. The [UGC lighting and microphone kit guide](/side-hustles/ugc-lighting-microphone-kit-canada/) compares more mics and lights.

**Window light is free.** Filming facing a window in daytime works well. A light matters when you film in the evening or in a client's dim shop.

## What software do you need for TikTok and Reels?

You can start with free software. | --- | --- | --- |

| CapCut Pro | Shown in the app | Check the in-app price before subscribing |
| Canva Free | $0 | Templates, 5 GB of storage |

Buy software only when the free version is slowing you down on paid work.

## What does insurance cost for a Reels side hustle in Canada?

[Zensurance's videographer page](https://www.zensurance.com/videographer-insurance), read October 9, 2026, says videography business insurance starts at around $565 a year for commercial general liability and professional liability with a $1.5 million combined limit. That's an estimate, not a quote.

Most first clients won't ask for insurance. It matters when you film on a client's premises, around customers, or for a business whose contract requires it. Your home or tenant policy may not cover equipment used for paid work. Ask your insurer. This is not insurance advice.

## What are the ongoing costs of a TikTok and Reels side hustle?

Ongoing costs are Canva Pro at $142 a year if you keep it, any paid editing app, insurance if you carry it, and travel to client shoots. Gear lasts for many shoots, so it isn't a monthly cost. Things that don't show on a receipt:

- **Travel.** Driving to a client's shop costs fuel and time. Put travel in the quote.
- **Revisions.** Set the number of revision rounds in writing before the shoot.
- **Raw footage requests.** Decide whether raw files and full usage rights are included or extra. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/tiktok-reels-pricing-canada/) shows how published packages handle this.

Keep client payments separate from everyday money. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account option for that.

## Where can TikTok and Reels startup costs be lower in Canada?

Costs stay lower when you film with the phone you have, use window light, and buy one piece of gear at a time to fix a real problem in your clips. The BOYA wired mic is the cheapest sound upgrade here. A gimbal can wait until a client wants walking shots. Free CapCut and Canva cover most beginner editing.

## Where shouldn't you cut TikTok and Reels costs?

Don't cut sound. Muffled audio is the fastest way to lose a client. Don't skip a simple written agreement covering deliverables, revisions, deadlines, usage rights and payment.

Don't skip disclosure. The Competition Bureau's [influencer marketing guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says paid or gifted endorsements must be clearly disclosed. Claims in a video for a client also have to be true, under the rules on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations). Don't use music you don't have the rights to in client videos.

## Which tax rules apply to a TikTok and Reels budget in Canada?

Reels income in Canada is self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net business income. Software, a mic or tripod used for the work, and insurance premiums are generally current expenses. Gear that lasts for years, such as a gimbal or ring light, may be claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. See [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

You must register for GST/HST once taxable sales pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses. Once registered, charge the client's provincial rate using the CRA's [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) and [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) pages. Registrants can generally recover GST/HST paid on business purchases as [input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html).

This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.
