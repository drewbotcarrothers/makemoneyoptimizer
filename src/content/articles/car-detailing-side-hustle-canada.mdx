Car detailing suits people who can keep a small kit in the car, show up at a written time, and stop when a stain or a sewer rule is outside the package.

The money, when detailing pays, comes from a package a neighbour can understand before you open a door. People who do this well sell one result and photograph the before. People who stall sell "I'll make it look new" and then spend the afternoon on dog hair that was never priced.

## Who does car detailing in Canada suit?

Car detailing in Canada suits people who can work in a driveway, a legal parking spot, or the client's garage, and who will write the package before they touch the paint. A vehicle you already drive, evenings or Saturdays, and a tolerance for crumbs and salt film make the first month practical. Car detailing is a poor fit if you have nowhere legal to discharge wash water, if the only plan is a hose into the street, or if you need the money on a day the temperature will freeze the hose.

Winter salt is a real Canadian job, and it is often an interior and a careful rinse, not a summer foam show. If the city forbids the rinse, sell the interior.

## Where can wash water from car detailing go in Canada?

Wash water from car detailing in Canada has to follow the municipality's sewer rules, because soap, road film, and salt are not rain. In Toronto, the city's page on [washing cars and outdoor surfaces](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says nothing other than rain and melted snow should enter a catch basin, and wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach those grates. The same page says power washing with water alone can still move contaminants, and that both the person hiring and the mobile wash business are responsible for wastewater leaving the property.

Other cities publish their own bylaw. Read that page before the first appointment. A commercial car wash that already discharges to the sanitary system is a different setup from a hose on a driveway. If you cannot name where the bucket will be emptied, do not sell the exterior.

## How much can car detailing earn in Canada?

Car detailing earnings in Canada cannot be promised as an hourly rate, because a salty sedan and a three-row vehicle with pet hair are different jobs. What you keep from car detailing depends on the list below, and this guide does not publish a national detailing rate.

- Whether the package names interior, exterior, or both

## How do you start car detailing in Canada?

Starting car detailing in Canada means choosing one package, reading the city's wash-water page, and practising on your own vehicle before you take a stranger's paint. The steps below are that sequence.

### Step 1: Choose the package

Interior only is the cleaner first offer in a city with a strict sewer bylaw, and in any week the hose will freeze. Exterior is a second product after you can point to where the water goes. Write one sentence: what is included, what is not, and how long you allow.

### Step 2: Read the wash-water rule

Use your city's sewer or environmental page. If you are in Toronto, start with the [washing cars and outdoor surfaces](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) page. If the only available drain is a catch basin, do not sell the rinse.

### Step 3: Write three prices on one page

One sedan interior, one larger interior, and the extra for pet hair or smoke. Add travel beyond a distance you name. A single "cars from $50" line will be read as a promise for a muddy SUV.

### Step 4: Buy the small kit and detail your own car

Wash in the shade if the product label says so. Keep separate towels for paint and for glass. Note how long the interior actually took. That time is your price, not a video's time.

### Step 5: Ask for an insurance quote with the work described

Say mobile detailing, whether you use the client's water, and that you are not repairing paint or electronics. Ask about a home policy before you treat it as coverage. This is not insurance advice.

### Step 6: Photograph a before and an after on your own car

The sample is proof you can finish, not a claim that a client's car will match it. When a client agrees, photograph their car before you start, including stains you will not remove.

### Step 7: Offer the package to five people who already know you

A colleague, a neighbour, a family car. Name the price and the time window. Do not add a free second vehicle.

### Step 8: Run the first paid visit as a drill

Arrive when you said. Mats come out. You stop at the package. You text when you leave. The invoice goes out the same day.

### Step 9: After three cars, keep or cut the offer

If every visit ran long because of pet hair, the extra becomes a line on the next invoice. Honour the written price for the client who already booked. If a driveway has no legal place for water, decline the exterior and offer the interior.

## What should the first week of car detailing in Canada include?

The first week of car detailing in Canada should produce a package, a wash-water answer, one practised car, and five offers. A paid visit is a bonus.

- **Day 1:** Write the package. Read the city's page on outdoor washing.
- **Day 2:** Price three vehicle types. List what you will not do: paint correction, coatings, engine bays, and electrical.
- **Day 3:** Buy the bucket kit. Detail your own car and time it.
- **Day 4:** Insurance quote. Ask about the home policy in the same call.
- **Day 5:** Photographs and a one-page note a client can keep.
- **Day 6:** Talk to five people. Book one time or none. Do not discount the package to fill the silence.
- **Day 7:** If someone booked, confirm water, parking, and the pet-hair line by text. If nobody booked, tighten the photos, not the slogan.

## What car detailing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common car detailing mistakes in Canada are rinsing into a catch basin, selling a coating you have not trained for, and pricing every vehicle as a small sedan. Toronto's wash page is explicit that outdoor wash water must not reach a catch basin. Your city may use different words and the same idea. Read it. Use two towels, test cleaner on a hidden spot, and do not promise an exterior wash in a week the hose will freeze.

Ceramic coatings, paint correction, and engine-bay work are different trades from a bucket. Leave them off the menu until you have practiced on cars you can afford to learn on, and until the insurance quote names them.

No before photo means the scratch you did not cause becomes your scratch in the argument. Grow the menu after three paid interiors, not before the first one.

## Which tax rules apply to car detailing in Canada?

Car detailing income in Canada is self-employment income, including a deposit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST for car detailing follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the notes, the e-transfers, and the receipts for soap and fuel. Report detailing on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add cleaning, pressure washing, and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Car detailing is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the work happens. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Confirm Quebec and every other province on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside.

If you drive to the client, keep a kilometre log for any vehicle claim. Do not claim the family car as 100 percent business because you detailed a sedan. Search your city for a business licence and use that city's fee if there is one. This guide does not state a licence fee from another city. Detailing is not a licensed trade in the way electrical work is, and a municipal licence is still a separate question from a sewer bylaw. If you pay a helper, check that province's workers' compensation rules before you call the person a favour.
