Getting your first dog walking clients in Canada means asking people who already know you, on a street you can walk, with a written visit and a meet-and-greet before you hold the leash alone. Dog walking is paid time with someone else’s dog. It suits a person who can keep the same hour and who will decline a dog they cannot control.

A pack photo is not a client list. Overnight sitting is a different product, covered only after solo walks are calm. The work itself is the [dog walking side hustle guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/). ## Who should you ask first for dog walking in Canada?

Ask neighbours and people who already trust you with an animal first for dog walking in Canada, because the first client is handing you a key or a leash. A city-wide ad is a later step. Two dogs on one walked loop beat ten names across town.

Mention the walks to households you already see at the park, and only if those people know you. Do not collect phone numbers off tags. If you also cut grass or clear snow on the same street, those clients already know whether you show up. The outdoor seasons are [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) and [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/). Do not trade a walk for electrical work. That limit is in the [handyman guide](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/).

Some people also create a profile on a pet-care marketplace. [Rover’s Canadian site](https://www.rover.com/ca/) is one example. This guide does not state Rover’s fee, insurance, or approval time. Read the account screens before you rely on them. An app profile with no meet-and-greet is still a stranger’s dog.

## What should the first dog walking offer in Canada say?

The first dog walking offer in Canada should name the minutes, the number of dogs, the days, the door, what you will not do, and the price. A sentence that the first walk is with the owner belongs on the page. So does a line that you do not do off-leash play unless that park allows it and you have already walked the dog.

Ask, before the paid walk, about vaccines, the vet’s name and number, bite or reactivity history, food and treats, equipment, how to get in, and a backup person. Decline a dog that lunges, guards food, or has no vaccines on record. “He’s friendly” is not a health record. Write the cap on how many dogs you will hold. Start at one.

Holiday overnights are not the first product. If you later add a drop-in, price it as its own visit. The client’s fridge and mail stay alone unless the task list includes them.

## What does an example dog walking note say?

An example dog walking note in Canada is a short paper or message you can adapt. It is not a note that produced a client, and it is not a claim that anyone replied.

**Example flyer.** Leave it where you are allowed to leave paper. Do not put it in a mailbox if postal rules forbid that. Do not print your home address.

> Dog walks on [these streets] only. One dog at a time to start. 30 minutes, on leash, waste picked up. First walk is with you. I need the vet’s name and a backup phone before I take a key. Text [number]. I do not offer overnight sitting yet.

**Example message.** For someone you already know.

> Hello. I am taking two solo dog walks on our block and I have room on weekday lunches. The first time I would walk with you. I do not group unknown dogs. Want the one-page note?

If you email people you do not know, [Canada’s anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) can apply. ISED’s page on [getting consent](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) says a commercial electronic message needs consent, identification, and an unsubscribe path. A note to a neighbour you know is a different posture from a bought list. This is not legal advice.

## How should you price the first dog walks in Canada?

Price the first dog walks in Canada from the minutes, the number of dogs, the walk from your door, weather, and a meet-and-greet you do not pretend was free labour forever. There is no national rate in this guide. A figure from another city ignores ice, a condo fob, and a dog that pulls.

Factors: duration, reactivity, keys and elevators, whether you drive, holiday dates, and a second dog. Two dogs are not double unless you write that. A group walk waits until you know each dog and you have checked the city’s leash rules. Extreme cold is a reason to shorten or cancel, not a reason to discount the written price and stay out longer.

**Illustrative example.** Suppose a 30-minute solo walk plus a 10-minute handoff, and you assume a fee of $28. Travel from your building is $0 in this sketch. Suppose a liability quote you actually received is $240 for the year. Six walks at $28 are $168, which does not cover that quote. The sketch is why you get the quote before you promise a route, and why six walks are not a business yet. Do not copy $28 or $240. The insurance number is an assumption until your insurer prints one. This is not a typical price and not a promise that six neighbours will say yes.

## What does a 14-day plan for first dog walking clients in Canada look like?

A 14-day plan for first dog walking clients in Canada should produce a one-page offer, a liability question asked of your insurer, and a short list of real conversations. A paid walk is a bonus. Silence is a reason to tighten the streets you named, not a reason to leaflet the whole city. This plan does not promise clients.

### Days 1 and 2: Write the offer and the no-list

One dog, a minute count, the streets, and no overnight sitting. Ask your insurer whether paid dog walking is covered. If you will drive dogs, ask the auto insurer too. This is not insurance advice.

### Days 3 to 5: Talk to people who know you

Give the note to neighbours and to anyone who has already seen you handle a dog. Book meet-and-greets. Do not take a key on the first hello.

### Days 6 to 9: Walk with the owner, then one paid solo

If someone says yes, walk with them, write the vet and the backup number, and only then schedule a paid walk at the written price. Log the minutes. If nobody has replied, edit the streets on the note. Do not add a second dog to look busier.

### Days 10 to 14: One listing, still without your address

If you want strangers to find you, publish a service-area profile and a Kijiji ad with the exact address turned off. Keep the same scope. A photo of several unknown dogs is not the ad. If the fortnight produced only conversations, that is the result. Do not buy matching bandanas for a route you do not have.

## Which tax rules apply to the first dog walking clients in Canada?

Dog walking fees in Canada are self-employment income, including an e-transfer, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed walkers pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia’s HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.
