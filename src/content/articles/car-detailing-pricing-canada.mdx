### What do employed detailers earn?

Job Bank's auto detailer wages ([Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/14380/ca), NOC 65311, 2023–2024 data) are $19.23 median nationally ($15.50 low, $27.00 high), $21.00 in Quebec, $20.00 in British Columbia and Alberta, and $19.25 in Ontario. An employee at a shop doesn't buy towels and chemicals, drive to the car, answer messages or pay the employer half of CPP. You do.

## How do you calculate a car detailing rate?

Build an hourly rate from target pay, CPP, supplies and travel, divided by the share of hours you can bill, then time each package on each vehicle size.

```text
Hourly rate   = (target pay + CPP + chemicals, towels, water, fuel, insurance per hour)
                ÷ share of hours billable
Package price = hours for that package and vehicle size × hourly rate
Add-on price  = extra hours × hourly rate (+ materials)
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $28/hour (about 1.5 times the $19.23 Job Bank median) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $3.33 |
| Chemicals, towels, water, fuel, insurance per hour | $7.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 75% |
| **Rate** | ($28 + $3.33 + $7) ÷ 0.75 = **$51.11, round to $50** |

At $50 an hour, a 5-hour full detail is $250, the bottom of Calgary PPF Pros' $250 to $450 sedan range.

Ask for photos of the interior before quoting, note existing damage, and write what's included.

## How do you raise car detailing prices?

Raise prices at the start of the season, and use the structures detailers already publish.

1. **Price by vehicle size.** Romeo's charges $50 more for a large SUV interior than a sedan; size is time.
2. **Add a top tier.** Detailing Pro's Showroom Pro ($240 to $300) sits above its $170 to $230 Showroom package; some clients pick the higher one.
3. **Sell seasonal packages.** The Fine Details built a $499 post-winter package around salt.
4. **Charge for add-ons every time.** Pet hair and salt stains are the most common time sinks.
5. **Turn one-time clients into monthly ones.** A $70 monthly visit is easier work than a neglected full detail.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], spring bookings are open. Full details are $[265] this year
(from $[250]); monthly maintenance stays $[70]. Book before [date] for
last year's price. — [Your name]
```

## What car detailing pricing mistakes are common?

- **Coupon pricing.** Calgary PPF Pros points out a real full detail takes 5 to 8 hours; $99 doesn't cover it.
- **One price for all vehicles.** A 3-row SUV takes much longer than a compact.
- **Free pet hair and salt removal.** They're priced add-ons everywhere.
- **No damage notes.** Photograph scratches and tears before you start.
- **Forgetting water and power.** Mobile detailers need a tap and outlet, or a waterless setup.

Winter work is priced differently; see [winter car detailing and salt removal](/side-hustles/winter-car-detailing-salt-removal-canada/). The [side hustle pricing worksheet](/side-hustles/side-hustle-pricing-worksheet-canada/) walks through the same rate math.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect car detailing prices?

Detailing income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. Register for GST/HST once taxable sales from all your businesses pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). Then add the rate where you work ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)): Detailing Pro's prices are before Ontario's 13% HST, so its $190 sedan full detail is $214.70 with tax. Chemicals, towels and part of your vehicle costs may be deductible; see [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

See [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) and the [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
