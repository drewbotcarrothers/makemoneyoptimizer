For Canadian print-on-demand sellers, the main difference is who prints. Printful runs its own fulfilment centre in Ontario, so domestic orders ship without customs or duty fees, in about 2 days to Ontario and 4 days to most other provinces. Printify is a marketplace of independent print providers, including Canada-based ones, so price and speed depend on the provider you pick. Both are free to start; Printful Growth (24.99 USD a month, free at 12,000 USD a year in sales) cuts product prices by up to 33%, and Printify Premium (from 39 USD a month, or 24.99 USD a month billed yearly) gives up to 20% off all products. Choose Printful for consistency from one Canadian facility, and Printify for provider choice and price shopping.

## How do Printful and Printify compare for Canadian sellers?

Printful is one company printing in its own facilities; Printify routes orders to partner print providers. Prices loaded in USD on October 4, 2026, and are converted at the Bank of Canada [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4246 CAD per USD for October 2, 2026; conversions are approximate.

## What does each platform cost per order?

On both platforms you pay the product cost and shipping when a customer orders, and your margin is your retail price minus those costs. Paid plans only make sense once the discount outweighs the monthly fee.

- **Printful:** product price includes the item and one print design; extras such as additional placements, labels, and premium images cost more. Growth becomes free at 12,000 USD in yearly sales.
- **Printify:** product prices vary by provider. Premium's 20% discount applies across products.
- **Shipping:** Printful's Canadian page highlights domestic shipping from Ontario; with Printify, compare providers' shipping rates and locations.

### Illustrative example

Assume a t-shirt with a 15 USD product cost and 30 monthly orders. The product cost and order count are assumptions; actual prices vary by product and provider.

- **No paid plan:** 30 × 15 = 450 USD in product costs.
- **Printful Growth at a 20% discount on this product (within its "up to 33%"):** 450 × 0.80 = 360 USD, plus 24.99 USD = 384.99 USD, saving about 65 USD (about $92.60 CAD) a month.
- **Printify Premium yearly at a 20% discount:** 360 + 24.99 = 384.99 USD; on monthly billing at 39 USD the total is 399 USD.

At 5 orders a month, 20% of 75 USD is 15 USD, less than either plan's fee, so the free tiers win.

## What mistakes do Canadian print-on-demand sellers make?

The common mistakes are ignoring currency, customs, and sales tax on fulfilment orders.

- **Choosing a US provider for Canadian buyers.** Cross-border orders can add delays and customs; pick Canadian fulfilment where possible.
- **Pricing in CAD from USD costs without converting.** Convert product and shipping costs before setting retail prices.
- **Paying for a plan too early.** The discount must exceed the monthly fee.
- **Forgetting the tax on your fulfilment bill.** Printful charges sales tax on (Printful price + shipping) based on the destination province ([sales tax help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264769445905-How-does-sales-tax-work-for-my-Canadian-orders)).
- **Not adding your GST/HST number.** If you are registered, add it in Printful's dashboard under Billing.

## Which tax rules apply to print-on-demand income in Canada?

Print-on-demand profit is generally business income, and product, shipping, and plan costs are generally deductible. Once your taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters, you generally must [register for GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) and charge it to customers; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). For where to keep store payouts, see [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).
