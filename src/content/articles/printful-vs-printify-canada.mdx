For Canadian print-on-demand sellers, the difference is who prints and what that does to your cost per order. Printful runs its own fulfilment centre in Ontario, so domestic orders ship without customs or duty, in about 2 days to Ontario and 4 days to most other provinces. Printify is a marketplace of independent print providers, so the base price, shipping rate and speed depend on the provider you choose for each product.

## What does a Canadian order cost on each platform?

You pay product cost plus shipping when a customer orders; your margin is retail price minus those, minus your storefront's fees.

### Illustrative example: one tee to a Canadian buyer on Printful

**Assumed input:** you sell the tee for $35 CAD with free shipping. **Sourced inputs:** 11.92 USD product, $11.29 CAD shipping, 1.4240 exchange rate.

- Product: 11.92 × 1.4240 = **$16.97 CAD**
- Shipping: **$11.29 CAD**
- Left before storefront fees and tax: $35 − $16.97 − $11.29 = **$6.74 CAD**

A second shirt in the same order ships for $2.50 more, not $11.29, so bundles and "two for" offers carry most of the profit. Printful also charges Canadian sales tax on the product price plus shipping, based on the destination province ([sales tax help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264769445905-How-does-sales-tax-work-for-my-Canadian-orders)); a GST/HST registrant can enter their number under Billing.

### When does a paid plan pay for itself?

**Assumed input:** a 20% discount on the tee (Printful says "up to 33%", and the exact Growth discount varies by product; Printify Premium's figure is "up to 20%").

| Plan | Fee (USD/month) | Saving per tee | Tees/month to break even |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Printful Growth | 24.99 | 20% × 11.92 = 2.38 | 24.99 ÷ 2.38 = 10.5, so 11 |
| Printify Premium, yearly billing | 24.99 | 20% × 8.77 = 1.75 | 24.99 ÷ 1.75 = 14.2, so 15 |
| Printify Premium, monthly billing | 39.00 | 1.75 | 39 ÷ 1.75 = 22.2, so 23 |

### Illustrative example: 30 tees a month

**Assumed inputs:** 30 tees a month and the same 20% discount.

- **Printful, free plan:** 30 × 11.92 = 357.60 USD. **With Growth:** 30 × 9.54 + 24.99 = 311.07 USD, a 46.53 USD (about $66.26 CAD) monthly saving.
- **Printify, free plan:** 30 × 8.77 = 263.10 USD. **With Premium yearly:** 30 × 7.02 + 24.99 = 235.47 USD, a 27.63 USD saving. **With Premium monthly:** 249.48 USD.

Printify's lower base price wins on product cost at every volume in this example; Printful's case rests on its published Canadian shipping, its single facility and its Growth plan becoming free at 12,000 USD a year in sales.

## What mistakes do Canadian print-on-demand sellers make?

- **Pricing in CAD from USD costs without converting.** An 11.92 USD tee is $16.97 CAD, before shipping.
- **Forgetting shipping in the margin.** $11.29 CAD to ship one Printful shirt inside Canada is two-thirds of the product cost.
- **Choosing a US provider for Canadian buyers on Printify.** Cross-border orders can add delays and customs.
- **Paying for a plan too early.** At a 20% discount, Printful Growth needs 11 tees a month to pay for itself.
- **Skipping samples.** Printful takes 20% off samples on the free plan and 25% on Growth; order one before you list.

## What could change these Printful and Printify numbers?

The exchange rate moves every USD price: at 1.40 CAD per USD the Printful tee is $16.69, at 1.45 it is $17.28. Plan discounts vary by product, and Printify prices vary by provider, so the per-tee savings above use an assumed 20%. Shipping and product prices are from October 9, 2026. ## Which tax rules apply to print-on-demand income in Canada?

Print-on-demand profit is generally business income, and product, shipping and plan costs are generally deductible. Once your taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, you must [register for GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) and charge it to customers; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
