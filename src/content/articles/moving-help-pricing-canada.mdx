Charging for moving help in Canada usually means an hourly rate per mover with a two- or three-hour minimum, plus extras for stairs, long carries, and travel, while the client rents the truck. Job Bank lists employed furniture movers at $16.55 (low), $22.00 (median), and $30.29 (high) an hour nationally, and mover helpers at a $22.00 median. A self-employed helper prices above that employee wage because travel, blankets and straps, unpaid messages, and both halves of CPP come out of the same rate. This guide suits labour-only helpers, not licensed moving companies.

## Which pricing models work for moving help in Canada?

Moving help in Canada is usually priced hourly per mover with a minimum, as a flat price for a single item, or as a fixed price for a small, well-described move. Stairs, long carries, and travel time are add-ons.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly per mover, with minimum | Rate × movers × hours, minimum 2–3 hours | Most apartment moves | Clients who are not packed |
| Single-item flat price | One price for a couch, an appliance, or a dresser | Marketplace pickups | Stairs and tight turns |
| Fixed price for a small move | One number for a described load | Studios and dorms | Scope creep: "just a few more boxes" |
| Add-ons | Stairs per flight, long carry, travel time, assembly | Covering real extra effort | Doing them for free |

Labour-only means the client rents and drives the truck. Transporting goods in your own vehicle changes your insurance questions; check them before offering it.

## How do you calculate a moving help rate in Canada?

Calculate a moving help rate in Canada from a target hourly pay per mover, the share of time lost to travel and messages, wear on blankets and straps, and a minimum that makes the trip worthwhile.

### Step 1: Set a target hourly pay per mover

Read your province's Job Bank row and aim above the employee median once unpaid time is counted.

### Step 2: Count unpaid time

Travel to and from the job, quotes, and messages. If a quarter of your time is unpaid, the billable rate has to cover it.

### Step 3: Set a minimum

A two- or three-hour minimum covers travel and the setup of a move, even if the load is small.

### Step 4: Add equipment wear

Blankets, straps, shrink wrap, and a dolly that eventually breaks. Divide replacement costs over the jobs you expect.

### Step 5: Hold back tax and CPP

Self-employed people pay both CPP shares; see [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose each mover's target is $28 an hour, close to Job Bank's national high of $30.29. Suppose 75% of working time is billable. The billable rate is $28 ÷ 0.75 = $37.33 an hour per mover, which you might round to $37.

Suppose a labour-only one-bedroom move needs two movers for 3 hours, loading and unloading a rented truck. Labour is 2 × 3 × $37 = $222. Add an assumed $10 of shrink wrap and blanket wear: $232 before tax.

If the new apartment is a third-floor walk-up, suppose stairs add 45 minutes for both movers: 2 × 0.75 × $37 = $55.50. A flat "$25 per flight" add-on would charge $75 for three flights, which covers it. Price the stairs before the day, not on the landing.

Say that the client supplies the truck and fuel, and that you do not move pianos, safes, or items you cannot lift safely.

## When should you raise moving help prices in Canada?

Raise moving help prices in Canada when you are booked out on month-end weekends, when your timings show moves running long, or when equipment costs rise. Month-end and summer peaks are the natural times to post a higher weekend rate.

A peak rate for the last and first days of the month, written on the price sheet, is easier to explain than a surprise increase.

## What moving help pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common moving help pricing mistakes in Canada are skipping a minimum, quoting without asking about stairs and elevators, and starting before the client is packed. Each one turns a three-hour job into five unpaid hours.

Ask for floor, elevator booking, parking distance, and a list of large items. Write that time waiting for an unpacked client counts as billable. Never accept payment "after the truck is unloaded" from a stranger without a deposit.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect moving help prices in Canada?

Moving help income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration becomes required once you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, counting all your taxable side hustles.

When registered, charge the rate for the province where the service is supplied, from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) explains voluntary registration.
