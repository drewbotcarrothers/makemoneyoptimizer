It suits people with daytime availability who are comfortable with dogs of different sizes and can walk in any weather.

## What happens in week 1 of a dog walking launch plan in Canada?

Week 1 of a dog walking launch plan in Canada is setup: choose a walkable service area, check municipal rules, get an insurance quote, and assemble a lean kit. No client sees anything yet.

### Days 1 and 2: Choose your service area and hours

Draw a service area you can walk or cycle between visits, and list the hours you can reliably offer, such as weekday middays. Clustering clients close together keeps travel time unpaid time low.

### Days 3 and 4: Check local rules

Search your city's website for dog walker permits and park rules. In Toronto, the City's [commercial dog walker permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) is for walking four to six dogs commercially, requires $2,000,000 of commercial general liability insurance naming the City, and lists a one-year fee of $305.99 from January 1, 2026 (tax not included). Other cities set their own rules, so confirm locally.

### Days 5 to 7: Get an insurance quote and build the kit

Ask a broker for a quote on liability insurance for pet care, describing walks, drop-ins, and how many dogs at once. Build a lean kit: a sturdy leash, a backup slip lead, waste bags, and a treat pouch used only with owner permission. The [startup costs guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-startup-costs-canada/) lists the ranges.

## What happens in week 2 of a dog walking launch plan in Canada?

Week 2 is your offer and outreach: write down what you sell, tell people you already know, and book meet-and-greets with each dog before any paid walk.

### Days 8 to 10: Write your offer and policies

Write one short page: service area, walk length options, solo or small-group walks, how keys are handled, cancellation and weather policy, and how you will be paid. Set your rates using the [pricing guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-pricing-canada/) rather than guessing.

### Days 11 and 12: Tell your network and the neighbourhood

Message friends and neighbours, post in local community groups that allow service posts, and consider a free Google Business Profile; Google says you can [add or claim a Business Profile](https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778?hl=en) at no charge if your business is eligible. If you email people who are not already contacts, read the CASL [consent rules](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) first: commercial electronic messages generally need express or implied consent.

### Days 13 and 14: Book meet-and-greets

Meet each dog and owner before the first walk. Record the vet's name, feeding and treat rules, leash and harness preferences, reactivity triggers, and an emergency contact on a written care sheet the owner confirms.

## What happens in week 3 of a dog walking launch plan in Canada?

Week 3 is first walks. Deliver exactly what you promised, send a short update after each walk, and decide whether to add an app.

### Days 15 to 17: Run your first walks

Start with one dog at a time, even if you plan small groups later. Send the owner a brief note after each walk (time, bathroom breaks, anything unusual).

### Days 18 and 19: Tighten your key and emergency routine

Never label keys with an address. Confirm the owner's emergency plan and save the vet's number in your phone. Review what went wrong in the first walks and fix it now.

### Days 20 and 21: Decide on an app alongside direct clients

Apps can fill gaps while you build direct clients. [Rover Canada](https://www.rover.com/ca/) says pet care providers must pass a background check and that bookings come with the Rover Guarantee; read its terms and fees before joining. The [Rover vs independent dog walking](/side-hustles/rover-vs-independent-dog-walking-canada/) comparison weighs both paths.

## What happens in week 4 of a dog walking launch plan in Canada?

Week 4 turns first walks into a route: offer fixed weekly slots, ask happy clients for reviews and referrals, and review your records.

### Days 22 to 24: Offer recurring slots

Offer each client a fixed weekly time, such as Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at noon. Recurring slots let you plan a route instead of chasing single bookings.

### Days 25 and 26: Ask for reviews and referrals

Ask satisfied owners for a short review and whether a neighbour might need walks. A referral from a neighbour they trust can carry more weight than a flyer.

### Days 27 and 28: Prepare for weather

Check your winter kit (boots that grip, a light or reflective layer) and write a severe-weather policy. Seasonal care is covered in [winter pet sitting and house sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/).

### Days 29 and 30: Review your numbers and plan month two

Total your income and expenses, list your clients and slots, and decide whether to grow, hold, or adjust rates. Keep receipts for leashes, bags, and insurance.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a forecast or average: suppose by day 30 you have three dogs walked three times a week at a rate you set from the pricing guide, called R. Weekly revenue is 3 × 3 × R = 9R. If R were $25, that is $225 a week before insurance, supplies, and tax. Your real rate and client count will differ.

## What dog walking launch mistakes should you avoid in Canada?

Common launch mistakes are walking strangers' dogs without a meet-and-greet, taking group walks before you can handle one dog calmly, skipping the city's permit or insurance rules, and pricing so low that a route never pays. The [dog walking mistakes guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-mistakes-canada/) lists more.

## Which tax rules apply when launching dog walking in Canada?

Dog walking income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. GST/HST registration applies once worldwide taxable supplies, across all your businesses and associates, pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single quarter or over four consecutive quarters. Start a simple income and expense record on day 1. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
