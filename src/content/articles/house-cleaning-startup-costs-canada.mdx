**Quick answer:** a lean house cleaning kit in Canada costs about **$172** (a caddy, microfibre cloths, all-purpose and glass cleaner, a Magic Eraser, scrub brushes, a flat mop, nitrile gloves and shoe covers, 13% Ontario HST included). A standard kit that adds a compact Bissell vacuum and a 2-step stool costs about **$311 plus liability insurance from about $450 a year**, and a pro kit with a Shark cordless stick vacuum, a handheld vacuum and a spin mop costs about **$596 plus insurance**. Prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026. At $140 a biweekly clean, one Calgary company's published price for a two-bedroom home, the standard kit and insurance pay back after six cleans.

How to define the service, find the first homes and run a trial week is in the [house cleaning side hustle guide](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash: what to buy, what it cost on a specific date, and how many cleans recover it.

## How much does house cleaning cost to start in Canada?

House cleaning costs $171.92 to start with a lean kit, $760.78 with a standard kit and a starting liability policy, and $1,045.51 with a pro kit and the same policy. Gear totals are Amazon.ca prices from October 9, 2026, with 13% Ontario HST.

| Kit | Gear before tax | Gear with 13% HST | Gear with 5% GST | Insurance (per year) | First-year cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $152.14 | $171.92 | $159.75 | Quote before the first paid clean | $171.92 |
| Standard | $275.03 | $310.78 | $288.78 | From about $450 | $760.78 |
| Pro | $527.00 | $595.51 | $553.35 | From about $450 | $1,045.51 |

Alberta and the territories pay 5% GST only; the [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province. House cleaning is one of the cheapest service businesses to open because the client's home already has the sink, the water and often the vacuum. The insurance policy, not the gear, is the largest line in the first year.

## Who should use a house cleaning startup budget in Canada?

This budget fits people who can clean for three to four hours on their feet, reach homes by transit, bike or a car they already own, and want recurring weekly or biweekly clients. It is a poor fit if you cannot carry a caddy and vacuum up stairs or across a parking lot, if you need evening-only hours in a market where clients want weekday daytime cleans, or if you have allergies to common cleaning chemicals.

Transit changes the kit. The lean kit fits in a caddy and a tote bag and assumes the client's vacuum. The standard and pro kits add a vacuum, which is easy in a car and awkward on a bus. Decide how you will travel before you buy anything heavy.

## How much is house cleaning insurance in Canada?

[Zensurance](https://www.zensurance.com/cleaning-insurance) says general liability for a small residential cleaning business may start at around $450 a year for a $2 million limit, with the price depending on services, revenue, location and claims history. The kits above use $450 as the starting point.

Commercial general liability covers a broken heirloom, a scratched counter or a client tripping over your vacuum cord. Two other covers come up with cleaning clients:

- **Bonding (a fidelity bond)** protects clients against theft by the cleaner. Some clients, property managers and offices ask for it; quote it with the liability policy.
- **Care, custody and control** covers damage to items you are handling, which basic liability can exclude. Ask the broker whether it is included.

Companies that compete with you advertise insurance: [NeatNow](https://neatnow.ca/blog/house-cleaning-cost-calgary/) states every clean is covered by $2 million liability insurance and cleaners pass a criminal record check. Matching that is how a solo cleaner answers the first question many clients ask. Compare quote routes in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for house cleaning in Canada?

Each clean has to cover the cleaner, cloth wear, gloves, shoe covers and travel before anything goes toward the kit.

**Sourced inputs:** [NeatNow](https://neatnow.ca/blog/house-cleaning-cost-calgary/) in Calgary publishes a standard clean of a two-bedroom, one-bathroom home at $165 one-time and $140 biweekly, before 5% GST. [SNAM Cleaning](https://snamcleaning.com/house-cleaning-rates-montreal/) in Montreal charges $50 an hour with a $125 minimum visit. [Taskrabbit](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/cost-guides/house-cleaning) lists a $35 national average hourly rate. **Assumed input:** $5 a clean in product, gloves, shoe covers and cloth wear. That leaves $120 at $125 and $135 at $140.

These are company prices. A solo cleaner starting out may charge less, closer to the $35 Taskrabbit average, and pay back slower. Set your own rates with the [house cleaning pricing guide](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-pricing-canada/).

Gaps between clients cost more than gear: two homes in the same building or street on the same day are worth more than a slightly higher price across town.

## Where can house cleaning startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Use the client's vacuum at first.** The lean kit saves $82.99 or more by starting without one. Buy yours when you have a client whose vacuum slows you down.
- **Buy refills, not new bottles.** Fill your trigger bottles from larger jugs once you have a few regular clients.
- **Wash microfibre instead of buying paper towels.** Cloths last months; paper is a cost every clean.
- **Use free invoicing first.** Wave's free plan handles a short client list; <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> or Jobber earn their fee when billing becomes a weekly chore.
- **Skip branded uniforms and signage.** A clean, plain shirt and on-time arrival matter more to the first clients.

## Where should house cleaning spending not be cut in Canada?

- **Liability insurance before the first paid clean.** From about $450 a year, it covers the broken item or the fall that a single clean cannot pay for.
- **Gloves.** Bathroom cleaners and bleach products damage skin over a season of cleans.
- **Product safety.** Never mix bleach with ammonia or acid cleaners. Keep products in labelled bottles, and read the label for ventilation directions.
- **A stable stool.** The $39.90 stool is cheaper than a fall from a client's chair.
- **Separate money.** <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that keeps cleaning payments apart from household spending, so refills and insurance are obvious at tax time.

## What could change these house cleaning numbers?

Your price, your travel and the insurance quote move these totals most. A solo cleaner charging $35 an hour for a three-hour clean earns $105, not $140, which stretches the standard kit's payback from six cleans to eight ($760.78 ÷ $100). Published insurance starting points are a floor; bonding, more staff or commercial contracts raise the premium. The 13% HST versus 5% GST difference moves the pro kit by $42.16. Amazon.ca prices were checked October 9, 2026 and move with sales. Some cities license cleaning businesses or require a business licence; check your municipality before you advertise. ## Which tax rules apply to a house cleaning budget in Canada?

House cleaning income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Cleaning products, gloves, cloths and insurance are ordinary expenses. A vacuum that lasts several years is equipment claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html). If you drive between clients, keep a kilometre log of date, destination, distance and purpose to claim the business share of vehicle costs. Self-employed profit also means paying both shares of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting all your side hustles. House cleaning is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. Once registered, use your province's rate from the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). A quarterly review, like the [side hustle money check-in](/side-hustles/quarterly-side-hustle-money-check-in-canada/), catches the threshold before it surprises you.
