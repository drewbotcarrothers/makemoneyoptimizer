Starting junk removal in Canada, meaning paid hauling of household items to a facility that will take them, costs less in gear than in insurance. What you're budgeting for is gloves, straps and a tarp for the first carload, lifting straps and a dolly once furniture is on the list, a headlamp for basements, and a liability policy before you work in strangers' homes. Amazon.ca prices below were checked on October 9, 2026. The insurance estimate is from Zensurance's junk removal page the same day. Tipping fees are the facility's, per load.

## How much does junk removal cost to start in Canada?

Junk removal in Canada costs $93.76 for a carload kit when you already own a vehicle, $221.20 adding lifting straps, a dolly and a headlamp, $921.20 with a starting general liability policy, or $1,423.76 with moving blankets, a hand truck, spare gloves and invoicing software. Goods and software include 13% Ontario HST. Insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate. None of these include the tipping fee, a trailer, or the vehicle.

## Who should use a junk removal startup budget in Canada?

A junk removal startup budget in Canada fits people who can carry a dresser, who have a legal place to tip the same day, and who will sort paint out of a "misc" pile. The lean kit fits a few items in a car, SUV or pickup you already drive. The standard kit fits sofas, mattresses and appliances, where a second person and lifting straps matter. The pro kit fits regular basement and garage clean-outs with a helper.

Junk removal is a poor fit if you have nowhere legal to unload, if you won't refuse hazardous items, or if the plan is a trailer payment before the first paid load.

## What does disposal cost per load?

Disposal is the repeating cost, and it's local. Two published rates from the pillar guide show the range:

| Facility | Rate | Minimum | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| City of Toronto drop-off depots, garbage and renovation waste | $189.86 per tonne | $19 covers the first 100 kg | [Fees for drop-off depots](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/drop-off-depots/fees-for-drop-off-depots/) |
| City of Calgary landfills, commercial basic waste | $113 per tonne | $25 for loads under 250 kg | [Landfill commercial rates](https://www.calgary.ca/for-business/operations/landfill-commercial-rates.html) |

At those rates, a 300 kg load costs 0.3 × $189.86 = $56.96 in Toronto and 0.3 × $113 = $33.90 in Calgary. Calgary's page adds a $180-a-tonne surcharge for loads with readily recyclable or compostable material, so the same 300 kg mixed with cardboard would be $54.00. Sorting before the scale pays you directly. Toronto lists a separate commercial disposal service, so check which rate applies to you before you quote.

Leftover paint is a separate stream. [Product Care Recycling's paint page](https://www.productcare.org/products/paint/) describes free drop-off for leftover paint in British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Nova Scotia, and Newfoundland and Labrador. Alberta, Quebec and the territories aren't on that list. If your province is missing, use the city's household hazardous waste page.

## Do you need insurance or a licence for junk removal in Canada?

**Insurance.** Zensurance's [junk removal page](https://www.zensurance.com/contractor-insurance/junk-removal) gives about $700 a year as a typical starting point for commercial general liability with a $1 million limit on residential work, and lists the factors insurers weigh. Its example claims include a client who says you removed an appliance they didn't want removed. A written list of what goes, signed before you load, is cheaper than that claim. Ask separately about your auto policy and business use of the vehicle. Compare options in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/). This is not insurance or legal advice.

**Business licence.** Some cities licence haulers or require a business licence for a home-based business. Search your city's bylaws before you advertise. This guide does not state a licence fee.

**Hazardous items.** Paint, batteries, propane tanks, tires and appliances with refrigerant often can't ride in the same pile. Each is a separate stop with its own rules.

### A first-month sketch

**A teaching sketch, not a contract price, a tipping fee, or a survey of Canadian junk removal rates.**

Suppose you buy the lean kit for $93.76 and carry insurance at $700 a year, or about $58.33 a month. You complete three small loads in Toronto at an assumed $250 each, so revenue is $750. Each is 300 kg at the depot rate, $56.96, and an assumed $15 of fuel.

Pay per load = $250 − $56.96 − $15 = $178.04. Three loads = $534.12. After the month's insurance share: $534.12 − $58.33 = $475.79. After the lean kit: $475.79 − $93.76 = $382.03 before a tax set-aside. If the three loads take 9 hours including the drive and the gate, that's about $42.45 an hour in month one. If the client pays the disposal as a separate line, it's in and out: not your income. Do not copy $250. A basement of mixed hazardous waste is not in this sketch.

## What are the ongoing costs?

- **Disposal** on every load, at the facility's posted rate that day, including minimums and commercial rates.
- **Fuel and parking** on every load. Log kilometres if you claim the vehicle.
- **Insurance,** from about $700 a year.
- **Gloves and tarps,** which wear out.
- **Diversion stops** for paint, batteries, electronics and tires. A second stop is time.
- **Unpaid scope.** "It's all trash" that turns out to include paint, and a basement you didn't see before you priced, are how a carload becomes a loss.

## Where can junk removal startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Start with carloads** in a vehicle you already drive.
- **Wait on the dolly** until a real dresser says you need it, and on the hand truck until appliances are regular.
- **Skip the trailer.** It's a payment before the first paid load.
- **Use a reuse centre** when an item is still usable and the centre will take it.
- **Sort before the scale.** On Calgary's rates, recyclables in the pile cost more.
- **A phone note** can split labour from the gate fee until invoicing software is worth it.

## Where should junk removal spending not be cut in Canada?

Don't skip insurance before you enter strangers' homes. Don't skip gloves. Don't skip straps on a roof load.

Don't take what you can't legally drop. Paint, batteries, propane, tires and appliances with refrigerant have their own rules. A ravine, a parking lot or someone else's bin is not the cheap version of a tipping fee. It's illegal dumping.

Don't guess the fee. Look it up the day you quote and write it as its own line.

## Which tax rules apply to a junk removal budget in Canada?

Junk removal income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. Gloves, straps, tarps and tipping fees are generally current expenses. A dolly or hand truck may be a current expense or [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) depending on its cost and life; a vehicle is CCA on the business-use portion. GST/HST uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the scale tickets, the invoices, and a mileage log. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a quarter and over four consecutive quarters. Add other side hustles. Hauling junk is not ride-sharing.

If you are registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) to the extent of commercial use, with receipts that show the tax. Calgary's landfill page says no GST is charged on its rates, so there's nothing to claim on those tickets. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST and do not claim the credit. Confirm the current rules with the CRA or a tax professional.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Quebec charges GST and QST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) and set the tax aside.
