## Who does a micro-SaaS side project in Canada suit?

A micro-SaaS side project in Canada suits someone who already sees a repeated task: a form a shop retypes, a calculation a freelancer does every Friday, a browser click a team repeats. It suits a developer, a bookkeeper, or an operator who can show a working slice to one real user. It is a poor fit if you need this month's rent from the first week, or if you will not maintain the thing after strangers depend on it.

A client project and a public product are different shapes. A script you write inside one company's spreadsheet is [Google Apps Script freelancing](/side-hustles/apps-script-automation-side-hustle-canada/). A public tool has support, a password-reset path, and a fee page. Pick one shape for the first month.

## How do you start a micro-SaaS side project in Canada?

Starting a micro-SaaS side project in Canada means naming the buyer, putting a page where that buyer can see the price, and only then building the smallest version that does the job.

### Step 1: Write the job in one sentence

"A bookkeeper in Canada pastes a CSV and gets a T2125-ready category sheet" is a sentence. "An AI platform for small business" is not. If you cannot name the person, stop.

### Step 2: Name the distribution path before you build

Pick one: a Chrome listing, a Play or App Store listing, a page you host, or a business that will pay you to install it. Ten people who already do the task are more useful than a feature list. If the path is email, read [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) first. A purchased list is not distribution. The compliant version of outreach is [cold email and lead generation under CASL](/side-hustles/cold-email-lead-generation-casl-canada/).

### Step 3: Ship a page that states the price

One screen: the job, the price, and how to pay or how to ask for a pilot. Host it where you can update it. A <AffiliateLink program="hostinger" label="Hostinger" /> plan is one option. Confirm the current price on the host's page. A page on a tool you already pay for is fine.

### Step 4: Build the smallest version that does the sentence

One input, one output, an error the user can read. Leave account deletion and a way to export their data in the first version if you store anything personal. Read the [PIPEDA overview](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/) before you keep names, emails, or customer files. Provincial privacy law can also apply. This is not legal advice.

### Step 5: Charge one real user, or decide not to

A free pilot with a written end date is allowed. An endless free pilot is how the project stays unpaid. If nobody will pay the price on the page, change the sentence or stop. Do not add a second feature to avoid that conversation.

### Step 6: Publish to a store only when the page has a yes

Chrome registration is available in Canada on the [country list](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/register). Play's signup page states the US$25 fee and the age rule. Apple's paid membership is what the programs page ties to App Store distribution. Pay the fee when you are ready to submit, and keep the receipt.

### Step 7: Write the support boundary

What you will fix, how fast, and what is a new project. A side hustle tool that pages you at midnight is a job you did not price. Say the hours in the listing.

### Step 8: Review after the first five payments or the first month with none

Keep the price, raise it, or shut the tool down. Shutting down includes exporting or deleting personal information you no longer need. Tell the users before you turn it off.

## What micro-SaaS mistakes are common in Canada?

Common micro-SaaS mistakes in Canada are building before anyone can find the tool, paying an annual store membership for an idea with no user, and storing customer data in a personal account you also use for other work.

A second mistake is copying a tool that already has a free version from the company whose files it reads. If the job is "make Sheets add a row," the client may need [Apps Script](/side-hustles/apps-script-automation-side-hustle-canada/) or [Zapier](/side-hustles/zapier-automation-side-hustle-canada/), not a new subscription brand.

A third is treating a course that promises app income as the product. Offers that ask you to pay before you can earn are covered in [side hustle scams in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply to a micro-SaaS side project in Canada?

Money you keep from a micro-SaaS project in Canada is self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), in Canadian dollars. Self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) on worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', in a quarter and over four consecutive quarters. Add other side hustles to the same pot. The [$30,000 figure is not an income-tax exemption](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/). This work is not [taxi or ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST. Confirm the rate for the place of supply with the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). A store or processor that collects sales tax as merchant of record does not replace your income report. The rental-versus-business form choice is [T2125 versus T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/). USD payouts are [earning in USD from US platforms](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).
