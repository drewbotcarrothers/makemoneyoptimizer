## What are the best side hustles for nurses in Canada?

The best side hustles for nurses in Canada fit irregular shifts and don't involve delivering nursing care outside your job. The groups below are non-clinical.

### Tutoring nursing students

Nursing students need help with pharmacology, care plans, and exam prep. [Online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/) lets you book sessions around shifts; the [tutoring pricing guide](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-pricing-canada/) helps you set a rate. If you also precept students at work, don't tutor the same students privately for pay; that's a conflict.

### Health and medical writing

Health publishers, clinics, and wellness brands need accurate, plain-language content. Start with [freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/) and pick a niche you know. Don't use patient stories or any information from work, and avoid giving individual medical advice in what you publish.

### Courses and digital products

Study guides, exam-prep courses, and shift planners can sell over time. See [online courses](/side-hustles/online-course-side-hustle-canada/), [printables](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/), and [Notion templates](/side-hustles/notion-templates-side-hustle-canada/).

### Remote admin and care work

Nurses are organised and good at documentation, which suits [virtual assistant work](/side-hustles/virtual-assistant-side-hustle-canada/). Off-shift [babysitting](/side-hustles/babysitting-side-hustle-canada/) is another option; tell parents you're babysitting, not providing nursing care.

## What professional rules apply to nurses with a side hustle?

Nursing is regulated provincially, so your own college's rules apply. Ontario's are a useful public example:

- **Liability protection.** The [College of Nurses of Ontario](https://cno.org/maintain-your-membership/professional-liability-protection-plp) says practising nurses must have professional liability protection (PLP). Nurses in the General, Extended, Temporary, Emergency Assignment and Special Assignment classes must hold it even if they aren't currently practising, and those in non-clinical roles still need it.
- **Minimum amounts.** At least $1 million per claim for the General, Temporary, Emergency Assignment and Special Assignment classes, and $5 million per claim for the Extended Class (NPs). If coverage has a yearly maximum, it must be at least $2 million for those classes and $5 million for NPs.
- **Employer coverage has limits.** CNO says your employer's insurance may cover you as staff, but you're responsible for confirming it meets the minimum and fits your circumstances. It suggests considering your own PLP to avoid gaps. Ask whether it covers anything you do outside that job.

Your employer's code of conduct matters too. Public-sector rules are a good illustration: the federal [Directive on Conflict of Interest FAQ](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/values-ethics/conflict-interest-post-employment/frequently-asked-questions.html) says outside activities should be on your own time, not use employer property, and not use non-public information from your job. Hospitals and health authorities set their own policies, so read yours.

It depends on the hustle, your hours, and your local market. This page doesn't estimate earnings. The worked example below shows the arithmetic only.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers: a nurse tutors 3 nursing students for an hour each on days off, at an assumed $45 per session, for 20 weeks.

- Sessions: 3 × 20 = 60
- Revenue: 60 × $45 = $2,700
- Less an assumed $150 for a video-call subscription and materials: $2,700 − $150 = $2,550 before tax

## What mistakes do nurses make with side hustles?

- **Drifting into clinical advice.** Answering a client's personal health question can blur into practice.
- **Using work information.** Never use patient details or employer materials.
- **Assuming employer PLP covers side work.** Confirm in writing, or get your own.
- **Booking around a schedule you don't control.** Leave buffer for shift changes and overtime.
- **Skipping the tax set-aside.** Side income has no withholding.

## How is a nurse's side hustle taxed in Canada?

Report self-employment income and expenses on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). If net self-employment earnings exceed $3,500, you generally pay both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on that income outside Quebec. GST/HST registration only becomes mandatory above the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Read [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) and [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).
