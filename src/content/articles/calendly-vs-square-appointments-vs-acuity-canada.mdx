Calendly, Square Appointments and Acuity Scheduling are the three booking tools Canadian tutors, music teachers, coaches and personal trainers compare most often. All three give clients a link to pick a time; they differ on whether you can take payment at booking, how many session types you can offer for free, and whether you pay in CAD or USD. ## Which booking tool is cheapest for a Canadian tutor?

Calendly Free and Square Appointments Free both cost $0; the difference is that Square's plan lets clients pay by card at booking (you pay Square's processing fee), while Calendly's free plan schedules but does not collect payment. Acuity starts at $16 USD a month on yearly billing.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not a quote: a tutor charges $50 a session, collected online at booking through Square, for 30 sessions a month.

- Fee per session at 2.8% + $0.30: $50 × 2.8% + $0.30 = $1.70.
- Monthly fees: 30 × $1.70 = $51.00.
- Collecting by Interac e-Transfer instead avoids card fees but takes more admin.

The session price and volume are assumptions for the arithmetic; check Square's live rates.

## What mistakes do tutors make with booking tools?

- **No cancellation policy in the booking page.** Put your 24-hour rule where clients agree to it.
- **Taking card payments without pricing in fees.** Build the fee into the rate or use e-Transfer.
- **Too many session types.** Start with one or two.
- **Forgetting buffers.** Add travel or reset time between sessions.
- **Sharing a personal calendar.** Connect a separate calendar for tutoring.

## Which tax rules apply to tutoring income and booking tools?

Tutoring income is generally business income on Form T2125, and booking software and processing fees are deductible business expenses. Some tutoring and educational services have specific GST/HST treatment; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and confirm your situation.
