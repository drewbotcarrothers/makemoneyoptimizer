Affiliate marketing in Canada is recommending a product or a service you have used, with a link that may pay you a commission, and with a disclosure a reader can see before they click. It suits people who can write a page that still helps if the link pays nothing. It is a poor fit if the plan is a page of buttons with no explanation, or if you will not say that you may earn a commission.

This guide does not publish a commission rate. Programs change their percentages, and many require an application before a tracking link exists. [Amazon Associates Canada](https://affiliate-program.amazon.ca/) is one program with a public join page. Read that program's current operating agreement yourself. A rate in a video is not your agreement. The links in this article that go to merchant pages are marked as placeholders. They are not a promise that this site earns a commission today.

## Who does affiliate marketing in Canada suit?

Affiliate marketing in Canada suits people who already use a tool and can say who it does not fit. A computer and one honest page are enough. The work is a poor fit if you want to be paid for clicks you did not earn, if you will hide the commission, or if the page would copy a manufacturer's description and add a button.

You are not the seller of the product unless you are. The reader buys from the merchant. You might be paid if the program's rules say the click qualified. Many clicks do not. Plan for a month with a zero report.

## How much can affiliate marketing earn in Canada?

Affiliate earnings in Canada cannot be promised as a rate per click or per thousand readers. What you keep depends on the list below. This guide does not publish a national commission.

- Whether the program approved you
- The percentage in your agreement, which you read there
- Whether the buyer completed a qualifying purchase
- Returns and reversed commissions
- Hosting and the cost of the product you bought to test
- Hours to update the page when the price or the feature changes
- Other side-hustle revenue in the same GST/HST pot

A dashboard screenshot from another publisher is not your report. Export your own numbers. A month at zero is a normal early result, not a sign you should add twenty empty pages.

## How do you start affiliate marketing in Canada?

Starting affiliate marketing in Canada means writing one useful page, disclosing the relationship, and applying to a program only when the page exists. The steps below are that sequence.

### Step 1: Pick a product you have used

Name who it fits and who it does not. If you have not used it, do not write the review. Buy it with your own money or wait.

### Step 2: Write the page without the link

Answer the question in the first sentences. Include the drawback. Add the link only where the reader is choosing. A button in the first line, before the answer, is an ad that has not helped yet.

### Step 3: Add a disclosure the reader sees first

The Competition Bureau's [influencer page](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says disclosures should be visible without an extra tap, in plain language, and that linking to an affiliate page is unlikely to be enough on its own. Write "I may earn a commission if you buy through this link, at no extra cost to you" or a sentence that clear. Put it above the link. A footer on another page is a poor hiding place.

### Step 4: Publish on a site you control

One host. Your name on the site. Google's [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) say it does not cost money to be eligible to appear in Search, and that eligibility is not a promise of indexing. Do not buy links.

### Step 5: Read the spam policies

Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) describe scraping and other tactics used to manipulate rankings. A site that exists only to repeat a merchant's catalogue is the pattern those policies are written against. Add your own steps, your own limits, and the date you checked the price.

### Step 6: Apply to one program

Use the program's official join page. Amazon's Canadian program page is linked above as the public application, not as an associate tracking link. Read the rules on what you may say. Some programs forbid income claims. Follow the stricter of the program rule and the Competition Bureau's rule on misleading claims.

### Step 7: Do not email strangers

If you later write to readers, CASL's [three requirements](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm) are consent, identification, and an unsubscribe. A message that only asks for consent is itself a commercial electronic message. The [newsletter guide](/side-hustles/newsletter-side-hustle-canada/) walks through that in more detail. This is not legal advice.

### Step 8: Check the report after 30 days

If it is zero, the page may still be useful. Update the price if it changed. Do not add ten more products to "test faster."

### Step 9: Keep or cut the page

If you no longer use the product, take the recommendation down or rewrite it. A stale "best" label is how a helpful page becomes a misleading one.

## What should the first week of affiliate marketing in Canada include?

The first week should produce one drafted page, a disclosure line, and a program page you have read. A commission is not the goal of the week.

- **Day 1:** Choose the product. Write who it does not fit.
- **Day 2:** Draft the page with no link. Include the drawback and the date.
- **Day 3:** Add the disclosure above where the link will go. Read the Competition Bureau page.
- **Day 4:** Read Google's spam-policy examples on scraping. Cut any borrowed paragraph.
- **Day 5:** Publish on a host you can name. Add your name.
- **Day 6:** Open one program's join page. Read the prohibited claims. Apply only if the page is live.
- **Day 7:** Tell three people who already ask you about the product. Do not buy an email list.

## What affiliate marketing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common mistakes in Canada are hiding the commission, copying a product page, and quoting a commission rate you are not contracted for. The Competition Bureau says a material connection includes commissions, and that an affiliate link alone is unlikely to be enough disclosure. Google's spam policies are the reason a thin site is a bad asset even if a program approves it.

Another mistake is promising income. You do not know the reader's result. AdSense's [eligibility page](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724) is a separate program from affiliate links. Being 18 and having original content is the start of an application, not a wage. Do not stack ads on a page that does not yet help anyone.

Do not recommend a loan, a credit card, or an investment you do not understand. Those products have their own disclosure rules beyond this guide. Stay with a tool you have used.

## Which tax rules apply to affiliate marketing in Canada?

Affiliate commission in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on that activity, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST on your own taxable supplies follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. Recheck program agreements when they change.

Keep the program reports, including reversals. A form from a company outside Canada does not replace your T2125. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Affiliate pages are not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made, if you are the one charging tax. Commission from a platform is often a different fact pattern from a fee you invoice a Canadian client. Confirm the rate table and the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set aside what you may owe. This guide does not tell you that a foreign platform has already handled Canadian tax.

Search your city for a business licence and use that city's fee if there is one. This guide does not state a licence fee from another city.
