In Newfoundland and Labrador, there is no provincial registration for sole proprietorships, so a side hustle usually starts with no provincial paperwork at all. The checks that remain are federal and municipal: 15% HST once taxable sales pass the $30,000 small-supplier test (rideshare from the first fare), a CRA business number only if you need one, and city approval if you run the business from home in places like St. John's. Employees earn at least $16.35 an hour from April 1, 2026. This guide suits anyone in the province starting a service, selling goods, or picking up gig work.

It belongs to the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) set. For depth, read [how to get a CRA business number](/side-hustles/how-to-get-cra-business-number-canada/), [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), and [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

## Do you need to register a side hustle in Newfoundland and Labrador?

Not with the province. The Government of Canada's [sole proprietorship page](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business/start/register-with-gov/register-sole-prop-partner.html) notes that Newfoundland and Labrador has no provincial registration for sole proprietorships. Because there is no provincial registration, you also do not get a CRA business number automatically; the CRA lists Newfoundland and Labrador among the places where you [register for a BN separately](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html).

### Step 1: Decide whether you need a BN at all

You need one when you register for HST or open certain CRA accounts. Many small side hustles never do; see [how to get a CRA business number](/side-hustles/how-to-get-cra-business-number-canada/).

### Step 2: Check your municipality

City approvals are the main local step, covered below.

### Step 3: Open a separate account

Without a provincial registration, some banks ask for other proof of business; [opening a business bank account](/side-hustles/business-bank-account-side-hustle-canada/) covers the options.

## When do Newfoundland and Labrador side hustlers charge HST?

After passing $30,000. The CRA [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) shows 15% HST in the province. Registration is required once worldwide taxable sales exceed $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). [Taxi and ride-sharing drivers](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) register from the start.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Corner Brook snow clearer bills $3,000 a month from December to March: $12,000, under $30,000, so no HST registration. A voluntary registration would make a $40 visit $46.00 with 15% HST, which homeowners cannot claim back.

## Which approvals do home-based side hustles need in St. John's?

St. John's distinguishes a home office from a home occupation. Its [business guide](https://www.stjohns.ca/media/xudpcpvw/guide-to-municipal-regulations-for-business-august-2025.pdf) treats a home office as office use only, and lists starting a home office among the activities that need a permit application. A home occupation, which the city defines as a small business or gainful activity operated by a resident as a secondary use of the home, is applied for with the [home occupation checklist](https://www.stjohns.ca/media-manager/media-pages/applications-forms/home-occupation-secondary-use-of-a-dwelling-checklist/). Other towns set their own rules; search [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/).

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in Newfoundland and Labrador?

Not a platform-specific one. The province announced a [minimum wage of $16.35 an hour](https://www.gov.nl.ca/releases/2026/government-services/0226n02/) from April 1, 2026, with a minimum overtime rate of $24.53, adjusted each April 1 based on the national consumer price index. That applies to employees, not independent contractors on apps.

## What are the common Newfoundland and Labrador side hustle mistakes?

- **Expecting a provincial registration to get a BN.** There is none; register with the CRA if you need a BN.
- **Running from home without checking the city.** St. John's requires approvals for home offices and home occupations.
- **Charging HST before registering.** Only registered businesses add HST.
- **Forgetting rideshare's first-fare rule.** Rideshare drivers register for HST from the start.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in Newfoundland and Labrador?

Report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net earnings, and provincial income tax is calculated on the same return.
