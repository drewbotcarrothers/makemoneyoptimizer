YouTube income in Canada has two parts you price differently. Ad revenue is set by YouTube: 55% of net long-form ad revenue, 45% of your Shorts pool allocation and 70% of memberships and Super Chat. Sponsorships are where you set the price. Influencer Marketing Hub's 2026 guide puts a channel with 10,000 to 100,000 subscribers at $200 to $1,000+ per sponsored video, and sponsorship CPMs at $15 to $30 per 1,000 views. Canadian-audience ad rates sit below American ones (US$9.93 vs US$14.67 CPM in AIR Media-Tech's country data), so sponsorships matter more per view for a Canadian channel.

## What do YouTube sponsorships pay in 2026?

Published benchmarks run from $20 for a nano channel's sponsored video to $50,000+ for channels over a million subscribers. All figures were on each page on October 9, 2026.

### Rates by subscriber tier (Influencer Marketing Hub)

| Creator tier | Subscribers | Typical rate per sponsored video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nano | 1,000–10,000 | $20 to $200+ |
| Micro | 10,000–100,000 | $200 to $1,000+ |
| Mid-tier | 100,000–500,000 | $1,000 to $10,000+ |
| Macro | 500,000–1M | $10,000 to $20,000+ |
| Mega | 1M+ | $20,000 to $50,000+ |

Source: [Influencer Marketing Hub, YouTube influencer rates 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-rates/youtube-influencer-rates/), updated August 31, 2026. The guide says the ranges reflect 2025 and early 2026 benchmark surveys and aggregators, doesn't state a currency, and puts sponsorship CPMs at $15 to $30 per 1,000 views. Its worked example: 500,000 views at a $20 CPM is a $10,000 budget before licensing or cross-posting. It also says integrations usually run 30 to 90 seconds and that audiences in the US, Canada, the UK and Western Europe command higher rates.

## How does YouTube pay creators in Canada?

YouTube pays Partner Program creators in Canada a share of revenue that depends on the format, according to YouTube's [revenue sharing overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902). To join, a channel needs 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million public Shorts views in the last 90 days, per the [YPP eligibility page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851).

| Income stream | What YouTube pays the creator |
| --- | --- |
| Long-form watch-page ads | 55% of net revenue |
| Shorts feed ads | 45% of the revenue allocated to you from the creator pool |
| Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks | 70% of net revenue |

YouTube defines [RPM](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314357) as what you earn per 1,000 video views after the revenue share; CPM is what advertisers pay before the split. Your own RPM is in YouTube Studio under Analytics → Revenue.

## Which sponsorship formats and add-ons should you price?

YouTube sponsorships are usually a flat fee per integration, a higher fee for a dedicated video, or a package, with usage rights and exclusivity priced on top.

| Model | How it works | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Integration (30–90 s) | Segment inside a regular video | Pricing below your production time |
| Dedicated video | Whole video about the product | Replaces a regular upload, so price the lost slot |
| Package | Several integrations over months | Exclusivity that blocks other sponsors |
| Flat fee + affiliate code | Lower fee plus commission per sale | Tracking disputes; get the reporting in writing |
| Affiliate only | Paid per sale | All risk on you |

YouTube sponsorship guides don't publish standard usage percentages, so the closest Canadian benchmark is IF Media's [2026 UGC pricing guide](https://ifmedia.ca/ugc-creator-rates-in-canada-2026-the-complete-pricing-guide/) (May 14, 2026) for brands reusing creator footage:

| Usage the brand wants | IF Media UGC benchmark (% of base) |
| --- | --- |
| Paid ads, 30 days, one platform | +20% to 35% |
| Paid ads, 90 days | +50% to 75% |
| Whitelisting (ads run through your channel) | +25% to 40% per 30 days |
| Buyout, unlimited use | +100% to 200% |

If a Canadian brand books you through [IF Media Connect](https://connect.ifmedia.ca/), the project amount is your quote; the brand pays a default 15% platform fee plus GST/HST, and creators pay a default 5% service fee.

## How do you calculate a YouTube sponsorship price?

Price from the views you can show, check it against your time, then add rights.

```text
Median views  = median 30-day views of your last 10 long-form videos
View price    = median views ÷ 1,000 × sponsor CPM ($15 to $30)
Time floor    = (brief + script + film + edit + revisions) hours × hourly rate
Base fee      = the higher of view price and time floor
Add-ons       = base × usage % + base × exclusivity %
Package       = base × number of videos − package discount
Convert       = USD figure × Bank of Canada rate (1.4240 on Oct 8, 2026)
```

Use median views, not your best video. Choose the CPM inside the $15 to $30 range by niche: finance, software and education toward the top, broad entertainment toward the bottom, in line with AIR's RPM spread and Collabstr's expensive-niche list.

## What do worked YouTube sponsorship quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** Median views, hours, hourly rates, the CPM chosen inside the $15 to $30 range and the usage and exclusivity percentages are my assumptions. Because Influencer Marketing Hub doesn't state a currency, I treat its CPMs as US dollars and convert at 1.4240.

### Quote 1: small Canadian personal finance channel, one integration

- Median views 8,000. CPM US$20: 8 × US$20 = US$160 = **C$227.84**.
- Time floor: 5 hours × $34.62 (Job Bank median) = $173.10. The view price is higher.
- Quote **C$230**. If you're registered and the sponsor is in Ontario: C$230 × 1.13 = C$259.90.
- Compare ads on the same video: 8 × US$2.30 median RPM = US$18.40, about C$26.20.

### Quote 2: tech channel, integration with 30-day paid usage

- Median views 45,000. CPM US$25: 45 × US$25 = US$1,125 = **C$1,602.00**.
- Brand wants to run your segment as an ad for 30 days on one platform; at 30%: C$1,602 × 0.30 = C$480.60.
- Total **C$2,082.60**. Usage starts on the first ad impression and ends on a written date.

### Quote 3: dedicated video for a Canadian outdoor brand

- Median views 20,000. CPM US$30: 20 × US$30 = US$600 = C$854.40.
- Time floor: 20 hours × $45 = **$900**. The time floor wins, as it usually does for dedicated videos.
- 30-day category exclusivity at 20%: $180. Total **C$1,080**. With 5% GST (Alberta sponsor, registered): C$1,134.00.

### Quote 4: three-integration package for a small channel

- Median views 12,000. CPM US$15: 12 × US$15 = US$180 = C$256.32 per integration.
- Three integrations: C$768.96, less a 10% package discount = C$692.06. Quote **C$690** (C$230 each), plus an affiliate code at the brand's standard commission.
- That's close to Collabstr's $255 average final cost per YouTube collaboration.

## Which templates help with YouTube sponsor deals?

A quote that settles the terms that cause disputes:

```text
SPONSORSHIP QUOTE — [Brand]        Date: [date]   Valid 14 days
Deliverable: 60–90 s integration in a long-form video on [topic]
Publish window: [dates]; video stays public at least 12 months
Script: brand talking points by [date]; I write the final script;
  1 revision round; claims limited to my own experience
Links: description link + pinned comment with [tracking link / code]
Disclosure: YouTube paid promotion box + spoken and written disclosure
Usage: organic only [or: paid usage on Meta for 30 days, +30%]
Exclusivity: none [or: no [category] sponsors for 30 days, +20%]
Fee: $[base] + $[add-ons] = $[total] before GST/HST
Payment: 50% on signing, 50% within 15 days of publishing
Reporting: views, watch time and clicks at 7 and 30 days
```

A reply to "what are your rates?":

```text
Thanks for reaching out. My long-form videos average [12,000] views in
the first 30 days, [X]% from Canada and [Y]% from the US. A 60–90 second
integration is $[260] CAD; a dedicated video is $[900]. Paid usage and
exclusivity are priced separately. If you share the brief and timing,
I'll send a quote.
```

A usage-renewal email:

```text
Hi [Name], the 30-day paid usage on the [month] integration ends on
[date]. If you'd like to keep running it, I can extend it 30 days for
$[480.60], the same 30% as the original licence.
```

## How do you raise YouTube sponsorship prices?

- **Recalculate every quarter** from the median views of your last 10 videos; when the median rises, the rate card rises with it for new deals.
- **Move up the CPM range** when brands rebook. A rebooking is evidence your audience converts.
- **Report results.** Sending views and clicks at 7 and 30 days supports a higher CPM next time.
- **Sell rights separately.** Usage and exclusivity priced as add-ons are a raise that doesn't depend on views.
- **Say no more often.** Griffin Milks reported turning down about 95% of sponsor emails; a full calendar is the clearest signal to raise. Scripts for the conversation are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What YouTube sponsorship pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common YouTube sponsorship pricing mistakes in Canada are pricing from your best video instead of the median, giving away paid usage and exclusivity, accepting affiliate-only deals with no tracking terms, and skipping disclosure.

YouTube requires the [paid promotion box](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235) when a video has paid product placements, sponsorships or endorsements. The Competition Bureau says influencers should [disclose all material connections](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) with brands they promote, including free products, and that testimonials should reflect actual experience.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect YouTube pricing in Canada?

YouTube ad revenue and sponsorships in Canada are self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both [CPP shares](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net income. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

How GST/HST applies can differ between payments from YouTube, fees from a Canadian sponsor and fees from a sponsor outside Canada; the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) covers the place-of-supply rules, and the [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration. How much to hold back is in [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

Influencer Marketing Hub's tiers and CPM range come from aggregated benchmarks without a stated currency or sample. Collabstr's averages come from its own marketplace, also without a stated currency, and most of its collaborations are small. AIR's RPM data covers its partner channels, not all of YouTube, and the Business Insider report is from 2021. Job Bank wages are 2023–2024 employee pay. The worked quotes use assumed views, hours and percentages; your own median views and audience split in YouTube Studio replace them.
