## What specs matter when you buy a pressure washer for paid work in Canada?

Flow (GPM), pressure (PSI), power source and pump design matter most, in that order for most residential jobs. Pressure loosens dirt; flow carries it away, so a machine with more GPM finishes a driveway or a wall of siding faster at the same PSI.

| Spec | What it means | What to look for in a side hustle machine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PSI | Water pressure at the nozzle | 2,000+ for concrete; you'll back off with wider tips on wood and siding |
| GPM | Gallons per minute of flow | Compare GPM between machines with similar PSI. 2.0+ GPM is where electric units start to feel quick |
| Rated vs "max" | PWMA-rated or working figures are measured under load; "max" figures are peak | Compare rated to rated. The SPX3000 lists 2030 PSI PWMA rated and 2500 max |
| Cleaning units | PSI × GPM, a rough single number for output | Useful for ranking models, not a promise of job time |
| Power source | Corded electric, battery, or gas | Electric: quiet, light, needs a GFCI outlet. Gas: more flow, more maintenance, winter storage |
| Pump | Axial/wobble vs triplex | Triplex pumps are built to be serviced and run longer; they cost more |
| Fittings | M22 threads and 1/4-inch quick-connect tips | Standard fittings let you add a surface cleaner, longer hose and turbo nozzle later. Check each accessory's connector before buying |
| Hose and cord | Hose length and rating; cord length with GFCI plug | Longer rated hose means fewer machine moves |

## How fast does a pressure washer pay for itself in a side hustle?

Divide the kit cost by what each job leaves after you pay yourself a fair wage and consumables. With the inputs below, the lean electric kit is covered after about 6 double driveways and the gas kit after about 29.

**Illustrative example.** The $110 double-driveway price and the 2-hour electric job time are the labelled teaching example from [pressure washing pricing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-pricing-canada/), not a market average. The wage is Job Bank's national median for building exterior cleaners, $19.23 an hour ([Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/14341/ca)). The $15 of fuel, detergent and wear per job (electric) and $20 (gas) and the faster 1.25-hour gas job time are assumptions.

Formula: **jobs to pay back = kit cost ÷ (price − hours × wage − consumables)**

| Kit | Left per job toward gear | Jobs to pay back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean electric ($322.33) | $110 − (2 × $19.23) − $15 = $56.54 | $322.33 ÷ $56.54 = 5.7, so **6 jobs** |
| High-flow electric ($630.53) | $56.54 | $630.53 ÷ $56.54 = 11.2, so **12 jobs** |
| Gas route ($1,876.44) | $110 − (1.25 × $19.23) − $20 = $65.96 | $1,876.44 ÷ $65.96 = 28.4, so **29 jobs** |

The gas kit takes longer to pay off but frees about 45 minutes per driveway in this example. If your calendar is full, that time is a second job on a Saturday; if it isn't, the electric kit is the better buy.

## What should Canadian buyers know before buying a pressure washer?

Canada adds four things to the spec sheet: freezing, runoff bylaws, outlets and warranty service.

- **Winter storage.** Water left in a pump can freeze and crack it. Drain the pump and hoses, store the machine above freezing if you can, and use a pump protector such as the Simpson Pump Guard above. Gas units also need their fuel stabilized or run dry, per the manual.
- **Runoff.** [Toronto](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says outdoor washwater must not enter a catch basin or storm drain. Most Canadian cities have a similar sewer-use bylaw; check yours before you use detergent on a driveway that slopes to the street.
- **Outlets and GFCI.** [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/garages/wash_clean.html) advises GFCI protection for electric washers and never pointing the spray at a person. Many electric units, including the SPX3000, have a GFCI built into the plug.
- **Certification and warranty.** Look for a Canadian certification mark (CSA, cUL or cETL) on corded electrics, and confirm the warranty is honoured in Canada before buying an imported listing.
- **Recalls.** Search the model on [Recalls and safety alerts](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en) before you buy, especially used.
- **Season.** Amazon.ca stock and prices for pressure washers move around in spring. Buying in the fall, as here, means checking that the listing still ships before the season ends.

## How do taxes treat a pressure washer bought for a side hustle?

A pressure washer used to earn business income is a capital purchase you claim through capital cost allowance on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). CRA's [classes of depreciable property](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance/classes-depreciable-property.html) page puts tools costing less than $500 in Class 12 (100%), and most small tools there are fully deductible in the year of purchase. Tools of $500 or more go in Class 8 at 20% a year, so the SPX3000 and surface cleaners usually land in Class 12, while the Simpson gas machines land in Class 8.

If you register for GST/HST, which is required once your taxable sales pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)), you can generally claim the HST paid on gear back as an input tax credit. See [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/) and [GST/HST registration for side hustles](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
