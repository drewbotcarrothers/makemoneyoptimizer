- **Short answer:** most new pressure washing side hustles in Canada start with a good electric unit for patios, walkways and siding, and move to gas only once paid driveway work shows the electric machine is the bottleneck.
- **The two numbers that matter:** PSI (pressure) and GPM (water flow). Flow does more of the rinsing work than people expect.
- **Check before you buy:** pump type, hose length and rating, the nozzles in the box, and whether a surface cleaner will fit.

## What should you look for in a pressure washer?

Look at flow, pressure, power source and pump before brand or colour. A machine that is pleasant to move and easy to store will get used; one that is too heavy for your car will not.

| Spec | What it means | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PSI | Water pressure at the nozzle | Enough for concrete, with nozzles that let you back off for wood and siding |
| GPM | Gallons per minute of water flow | Higher flow rinses faster; compare GPM between machines with similar PSI |
| Cleaning units | PSI × GPM, a rough way to compare overall output | Use it to compare models, not as a promise of speed |
| Power source | Electric (corded) or gas | Electric: quieter, lighter, needs a GFCI outlet. Gas: more output, more maintenance |
| Pump | Wobble/axial vs triplex | Axial pumps are common on consumer units; triplex pumps are serviceable and built for longer duty |
| Hose and fittings | Length, pressure rating, quick-connect type | A longer rated hose saves moving the machine; standard fittings make upgrades easier |
| Weight and wheels | Can you lift it into your vehicle alone? | Check the listed weight before you buy |

[CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/garages/wash_clean.html) advises never pointing the spray at a person and using a GFCI-protected outlet for electric units.

## What should Canadian buyers know before buying?

- **Winter storage.** Follow the manual on draining and storing the pump; water left inside can freeze and crack it.
- **Runoff rules.** [Toronto](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says outdoor washwater must not reach a catch basin; check your city.
- **Recalls.** Search the model on [Recalls and safety alerts](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en) before buying, especially used.
- **Taxes.** A machine used for your business is generally claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) rather than deducted all at once.
