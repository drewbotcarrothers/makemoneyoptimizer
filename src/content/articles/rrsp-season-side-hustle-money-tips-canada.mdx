RRSP season, the first weeks of the year before the contribution deadline, is when many Canadian side hustlers decide what to do with last year's earnings. Start by setting aside what you owe: side hustle income is taxed at your marginal rate, and self-employed people pay both shares of CPP, 11.9% on pensionable earnings above the $3,500 basic exemption in 2026. With the remainder, an RRSP contribution can lower your taxable income, and your net self-employment earnings count as earned income, which builds RRSP room for the next year at 18% of earned income up to the annual limit ($33,810 for 2026, $35,390 for 2027). A TFSA or, for first-time buyers, an FHSA may fit better depending on your income. This guide explains the rules, not which account or product to choose.

## How does side hustle income affect your RRSP room?

Net self-employment income counts toward earned income, which sets next year's RRSP room. The CRA's [T4040 guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4040/rrsps-other-registered-plans-retirement.html) says earned income adds employment earnings, self-employment earnings, and certain other income, then subtracts specific employment expenses and business or rental losses. Your RRSP deduction limit is generally 18% of the previous year's earned income, up to the annual dollar limit, adjusted for pension adjustments and plus unused room carried forward. Your notice of assessment shows your exact limit.

| Limit | 2026 | 2027 | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RRSP dollar limit | $33,810 | $35,390 | [CRA limits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/registered-plans-administrators/pspa/mp-rrsp-dpsp-tfsa-limits-ympe.html) |
| TFSA dollar limit | $7,000 | Not yet listed | CRA limits |
| CPP earnings ceiling (YMPE) | $74,600 | Not yet listed | CRA limits |
| FHSA first-year room | $8,000 | Not listed | [FHSA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html) |

## When is the RRSP deadline for the 2026 tax year?

Contributions made in the first 60 days of 2027 can generally be deducted on your 2026 return. For the 2025 tax year, the CRA counted contributions made from March 4, 2025 to March 2, 2026 ([RRSP deduction limit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/contributing-a-rrsp-prpp/contributions-affect-your-rrsp-prpp-deduction-limit.html)). By the same 60-day count, the window for 2026 would end March 1, 2027; confirm the date on the CRA's page before relying on it. Contributions that exceed your deduction limit by more than $2,000 are generally taxed at 1% per month.

## What should a side hustler do with money before contributing?

Cover what you owe first, then decide between accounts. In order:

1. **Set aside income tax and CPP** on your net side hustle income; see [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
2. **Check instalments.** If your net tax owing is more than $3,000 ($1,800 in Quebec) in the current year and either of the two previous years, you may have to pay [quarterly instalments](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments.html) on March 15, June 15, September 15, and December 15.
3. **Pay any balance by April 30, 2027.** Self-employed people can file by June 15, but the CRA's [filing rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/important-dates-individuals/filing-dates-tax-return.html) make the balance due April 30.
4. **Then compare an RRSP, TFSA, or FHSA** for any extra money.

## How do the RRSP, TFSA, and FHSA compare for side hustle money?

All three shelter investment growth, but they treat contributions and withdrawals differently.

| Account | Contribution deductible? | Room built by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RRSP | Yes | 18% of prior-year earned income, up to the limit | Withdrawals are generally taxable |
| TFSA | No | Annual dollar limit ($7,000 for 2026) | Check your room in your CRA account ([TFSA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account/contributing.html)) |
| FHSA | Yes | $8,000 in the first year you open it | For first-time home buyers; transfers from an RRSP are not deductible |

### Illustrative example

Assume $10,000 in net side hustle income in 2026, no other earned income changes, and a 30% combined marginal tax rate. The income and rate are assumptions; real rates depend on your province and total income.

- **RRSP room created for 2027 from this income:** 18% × $10,000 = $1,800.
- **Effect of a $2,000 RRSP contribution on 2026 tax, if you have the room:** 30% × $2,000 = $600 less tax.
- **CPP on the side hustle, if your employment income already covers the $3,500 exemption:** 11.9% × $10,000 = $1,190, which an RRSP deduction does not reduce.

## What RRSP-season mistakes do side hustlers make?

The common mistakes are contributing tax money and assuming room without checking.

- **Contributing money owed to the CRA.** Pay tax and CPP first.
- **Guessing your limit.** Use your notice of assessment or CRA account.
- **Over-contributing.** Amounts more than $2,000 over your limit are taxed at 1% a month.
- **Forgetting CPP.** RRSP deductions lower income tax, not self-employed CPP contributions.
- **Missing the window.** Contribute within the first 60 days of 2027 to count for 2026.
