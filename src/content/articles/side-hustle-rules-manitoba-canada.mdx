In Manitoba, most side hustles under $30,000 a year have little to register. A business name costs about $105 in registry fees ($45 reservation plus $60 registration) if you want one, the 7% retail sales tax (RST) does not require registration for small businesses under $30,000 in annual taxable sales, GST has a matching $30,000 test, and Winnipeg does not require a business licence to work from home. The traps are the conditions attached to the RST exception and the handful of activities Winnipeg does license, such as used goods dealing. This guide suits anyone in Manitoba starting a service, reselling, or doing gig work.

## Do you need to register a side hustle business name in Manitoba?

If you operate under a business name, yes, with the Companies Office. Its [business names page](https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/starting_business_name.html) lists a $45 name reservation for a "named" business, then $60 to register. The CRA lists Manitoba among the provinces where a business number is issued automatically when you register ([When you need a BN](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html)).

### Step 1: Decide whether you need a name

Testing a service under your own name keeps costs at zero; a brand name helps once you advertise.

### Step 2: Reserve, then register

Reserve the name, then register within the reservation period. Keep the registration documents for your bank.

## Do Manitoba side hustlers charge RST?

Not until annual taxable sales reach $30,000, if you meet the conditions. Manitoba's [RST page](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/taxes/retail.html) sets the general rate at 7% on the retail sale or rental of most goods and certain services. [Information Notice RST 24-01](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/noticerst2401.pdf), issued April 2024, says small businesses with annual taxable sales under $30,000 are not required to register and collect RST.

Businesses that skip registration must still pay RST on taxable goods and services they buy, including goods bought for resale, keep sales records, and state on invoices that RST is included in the price without itemizing it. The exception is not available if you sell tobacco or liquor, or use out-of-province suppliers that do not collect Manitoba RST. Once sales pass $30,000, you get one month to register and start collecting.

## When do Manitoba side hustlers charge GST?

GST is separate. The CRA [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) shows Manitoba at 5% GST plus 7% PST (the RST). You register for GST once worldwide taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)); rideshare drivers register from the first fare.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Brandon reseller sells $1,800 a month of used furniture: $21,600 a year. That is under $30,000 for both RST and GST, so no registration is required if the RST conditions are met, and invoices say RST is included. If sales reach $2,800 a month ($33,600 a year), both registrations apply: a $200 dresser is then $200 + $14 RST + $10 GST = $224.

## Do you need a business licence in Winnipeg for a side hustle?

Usually not. Winnipeg says [you do not require a business licence if you work from home](https://www.winnipeg.ca/services-programs/permits-licences-0/business-licences), although zoning requirements still apply and questions go to the Zoning Branch. The city licenses 11 activities under its Community Safety Business Licensing By-law, including bicycle dealers, flea markets, used goods dealers, and short-term rental accommodations. If your reselling side hustle starts to look like a used goods business, check with the city. Outside Winnipeg, search [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/).

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in Manitoba?

Not a platform-specific one. Manitoba's [minimum wage fact sheet](https://www.gov.mb.ca/labour/standards/doc,minimum-wage,factsheet.html) lists $16.40 an hour effective October 1, 2026, for employees. App couriers working as independent contractors are paid on the app's terms; [food delivery pay in Canada](/side-hustles/food-delivery-pricing-canada/) shows how to measure your own hourly rate.

## What are the common Manitoba side hustle mistakes?

- **Itemizing RST without being registered.** Unregistered small businesses state that RST is included; they do not add it.
- **Forgetting RST on your own purchases.** The exception still requires you to pay RST on supplies and stock.
- **Buying from out-of-province suppliers that do not collect RST.** That disqualifies you from the exception.
- **Assuming no city rules at all.** Winnipeg licenses specific activities like used goods dealing.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in Manitoba?

Report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net self-employment earnings, and Manitoba income tax is calculated on the same return.
