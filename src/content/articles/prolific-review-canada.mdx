Prolific pays people to complete research studies for universities and companies. For someone in Canada it is one of the few study platforms that names Canada on the participant country list and publishes a minimum hourly reward. It suits adults who can verify an ID, sit through tasks without rushing, and accept that some weeks are quiet. It does not suit anyone who needs a set number of hours. ## Is Prolific available to participants in Canada?

Yes, if you live in Canada. Prolific's participant help, updated September 2, 2026, says the platform is open to most OECD countries, including Canada, as long as you are over 18 and have a verified account. Canada appears in the country list on that page. Eligibility is based on where you currently live, not your nationality. A citizen of an unlisted country who lives in Canada can join. Someone who lives outside the list cannot, unless Prolific invites them ([who can participate](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445007-who-can-participate-in-studies-on-prolific)).

Researchers are not allowed to recruit children. If a study appears to be doing that, Prolific asks you to report it from the support icon.

## How much does Prolific pay?

Prolific does not publish an average participant income, in Canada or anywhere else. It publishes the minimum a researcher must offer.

Researchers set a reward at or above £6 or US$8 per hour. Prolific recommends at least £9 or US$12 per hour. The base reward is a fixed amount. Every approved participant on that study receives the same payment, whether they finish faster or slower than the estimate. The "per hour" figure is that fixed amount divided by the researcher's estimated time. If people consistently take longer than the estimate, the study can be flagged as underpaying and the researcher has to top it up ([payment model](https://researcher-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445230-prolific-s-payment-model), [pricing](https://researcher-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445239-what-is-your-pricing)).

Bonuses do not count toward the minimum. A screen-out through Prolific's custom screening pays a smaller fixed amount, with a minimum of £0.10 or US$0.14. A returned or timed-out submission pays nothing.

Studies are priced in GBP or USD, depending on the researcher's workspace. Your balance for a non-US address is displayed in GBP, and you still hold whatever mix of GBP and USD you actually earned.

### Illustrative example

Assumed study length, using Prolific's published US-dollar floor. A researcher sets a 15-minute study at the US$8 hourly minimum: US$8 × 0.25 = US$2.00. At the Bank of Canada daily average of 1.4254 CAD per US dollar on October 5, 2026 ([daily rates](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/)), US$2.00 is about $2.85 CAD before PayPal converts the currency. The same 15 minutes at the recommended US$12 floor is US$3.00, about $4.28 CAD. These are floors, not typical study pay, and they are not a weekly forecast.

## How do Canadians cash out Prolific earnings?

You connect PayPal, then cash out when the combined approved balance in GBP and USD reaches £6 or US$6. An example Prolific gives is £6 or US$6 in one currency, or a mix such as US$4 plus £3.50. Pending rewards do not count. Both currencies cash out together. PayPal handles conversion. Prolific says the payment arrives in PayPal within 24 hours, often sooner ([how to cash out](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445062-how-do-i-cash-out-on-prolific)).

You can generally cash out once every 24 hours, resetting at midnight UTC. The wait is 72 hours if you recently changed PayPal details or you have made fewer than four cash-outs.

PayPal has to be in your name, verified, and able to receive both GBP and USD. The country on the PayPal address should match the country of residence or birth on Prolific, and the residential address should match ([connect PayPal](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445055-how-do-i-connect-my-paypal-to-my-prolific-account)). Prolific says it pays via PayPal via Hyperwallet and cannot access your PayPal account.

About $8.55 CAD is US$6 at the October 5, 2026 rate above. A £6 minimum is a different currency. Convert pounds with the Bank of Canada GBP rate on the day you are doing the math, not with the USD figure.

## Does Prolific include AI-training studies?

Some studies on Prolific ask you to rate or compare model outputs, because researchers study those systems. Prolific does not publish a share of tasks that are AI-related, so this review will not guess one. The work is still a study with a fixed reward and a screen-out risk. It is not the same product as a contractor seat on [Outlier](/side-hustles/outlier-ai-review-canada/) or [DataAnnotation](/side-hustles/dataannotation-review-canada/), where you take an assessment and then bill project time.

If a study's instructions conflict with Prolific's rules, or the pay looks below the floor once you see the estimated time, return it. Chasing a low-reward task trains you to work for less than the minimum the platform already set.

## What are the pros and cons of Prolific for Canadians?

**Pros**

- Canada is on the published participant list.
- There is a real minimum hourly reward, unlike survey sites that only show points.
- The reward is visible before you accept.
- Cash-out is PayPal, with a low published minimum.

**Cons**

- You cannot force studies to appear. A matched profile still waits on researchers.
- Pay arrives in GBP, USD, or both. Canadians convert to CAD and report CAD.
- ID verification can fail on glare, a VPN, or a document from a country you do not live in.
- The hourly figure is an estimate. If you are slow, you are not paid extra for that study.

## What mistakes do Canadians make on Prolific?

- **Treating the floor as the usual rate, or ignoring it.** US$8 an hour is the minimum researchers must meet, not a promise that your next study pays that much per minute of your real time. Check the reward and the estimated minutes before you start.
- **Cashing out in a currency PayPal is not set to receive.** Enable GBP and USD before the first withdrawal.
- **Speeding.** Fast, low-effort submissions are a listed reason a researcher may reject a study, within Prolific's rejection rules. Rejections remove the pending reward.
- **A VPN during verification.** Turn it off. Use the network where you live.
- **One platform only.** Keep a survey panel or a user-testing profile so a quiet Prolific week is not a quiet month.

## How is Prolific income taxed in Canada?

Approved rewards are income. Report them in Canadian dollars on your return. When this is business income, use [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Convert each payout with the Bank of Canada rate for the day the amount arises, or an average rate for the year when the CRA's currency folio allows it. The walkthrough is [earning in USD from US platforms](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/). Prolific's help pages checked for this review do not say the company files a Canadian tax slip for participants. You still report the income.
