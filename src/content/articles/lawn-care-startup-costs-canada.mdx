**Quick answer:** a lean lawn care kit in Canada costs about **$558** (a 21-inch 170cc gas push mower, a gas can, a rake, glasses, earmuffs, gloves and yard bags, 13% Ontario HST included). A standard kit that adds a 20V string trimmer costs about **$647 plus liability insurance from about $500 a year**, and a pro kit with a self-propelled mower and a cordless leaf blower costs about **$901 plus insurance**. Prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026. At $42 a cut, the low end of one Ottawa company's published weekly rate, the standard kit and insurance pay back after 30 cuts: about four weeks with eight weekly lawns.

How the season works by region, and how to sign the first neighbours, is in the [lawn care side hustle guide](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash: what to buy, what it cost on a specific date, and how many cuts recover it.

## How much does it cost to start lawn care in Canada?

Lawn care costs $557.54 to start with a lean kit, $1,146.79 with a standard kit and a starting liability policy, and $1,400.60 with a pro kit and the same policy. Gear totals are Amazon.ca prices from October 9, 2026, with 13% Ontario HST.

| Kit | Gear before tax | Gear with 13% HST | Gear with 5% GST | Insurance (per year) | First-year cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $493.40 | $557.54 | $518.07 | Quote before the first paid cut | $557.54 |
| Standard | $572.38 | $646.79 | $601.00 | From about $500 | $1,146.79 |
| Pro | $796.99 | $900.60 | $836.84 | From about $500 | $1,400.60 |

Alberta and the territories pay 5% GST only; the [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province. A used mower bought locally can cut the lean total by half or more, but only if you see it start cold before you pay.

## Who is a lawn care startup budget a fit for in Canada?

This budget fits people who can push a mower for several hours in summer heat, have a garage or shed to store a mower and fuel, and live where houses have lawns within walking distance or a short drive. It is a poor fit if you have nowhere to keep gasoline, if your neighbourhood is mostly condos with contracted grounds crews, or if you need income through the winter without a second service.

The season sets the ceiling. Southern Ontario, southern Quebec and the B.C. south coast have long mowing seasons; the Prairies, Atlantic Canada and the North are shorter. Size the kit to the number of cuts your region allows, not to a twelve-month plan.

## How many cuts does it take to pay back lawn care gear?

Each cut has to cover its fuel, oil, line and blade wear before anything goes toward the kit.

**Sourced input:** [Ottawa Lawn Maintenance](https://ottawalawnmaintenance.ca/) publishes weekly mowing at $42 to $65 a visit for residential lots up to 5,000 sq ft, including line trimming. **Assumed input:** $3 a cut in fuel, oil, line and blade wear for a walkable route. That leaves $39 at the low price and $62 at the high price.

| Kit | First-year cash | ÷ $39 (at $42 a cut) | ÷ $62 (at $65 a cut) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $557.54 | 14.30, so 15 cuts | 8.99, so 9 cuts |
| Standard + $500 insurance | $1,146.79 | 29.40, so 30 cuts | 18.50, so 19 cuts |

### Illustrative example: one season on the standard kit

**Assumed inputs:** eight weekly lawns on or near your street, a 22-week season, $42 a cut, $3 a cut in variable costs. **Sourced inputs:** the $42 price and the standard kit and insurance totals above.

- Cuts in the season: 8 × 22 = **176**
- Revenue: 176 × $42 = **$7,392**
- Variable costs: 176 × $3 = **$528**
- Before the kit: $7,392 − $528 = **$6,864**
- After the $1,146.79 kit and insurance: **$5,717.21** for the season, before tax and your time

Eight lawns make 32 cuts in four weeks, so the standard kit and insurance are paid for early in the fifth week. A shorter season or lawns spread across town moves that later: add driving time and fuel for every lawn you cannot walk to. ## Where can a lawn care budget in Canada stay smaller?

- **Buy a used mower you have seen start cold.** The mower is most of the lean kit. A local used mower that starts on the first pulls beats a new one you cannot afford.
- **Walk the route.** Lawns on your own street need no trailer, no truck and no fuel beyond the mower's.
- **Leave clippings where clients and city rules allow.** Mulching saves bags, disposal and time.
- **Wait on the blower.** A rake and a broom work for the first few lawns.

## Where should a lawn care budget in Canada refuse to shrink?

- **Eye and ear protection.** $17.99 and $25.97 protect against the two injuries that build up across a season.
- **Liability insurance before a route.** From about $500 a year, it covers the window, the car or the sprinkler head.
- **A sharp, balanced blade.** A dull blade tears grass and looks sloppy; a loose blade is dangerous. Pull the spark plug wire before you clear a clog.
- **Proper fuel storage.** A spill-proof can and a ventilated shed, never a balcony.
- **Pesticide rules.** Ontario's [cosmetic pesticide rules](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticides-home-lawns-and-gardens) and Quebec's [Pesticides Management Code](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/P-9.3,%20r.%201) restrict lawn pesticides and require licensing for application. Mow, trim and rake; leave spraying to licensed operators.

Keep equipment money and household money apart. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that keeps lawn payments separate so fuel and repairs are obvious.

## What could change these lawn care numbers?

Season length, mower choice and insurance move these totals most. A 16-week season instead of 22 cuts the illustrative revenue by more than a quarter, a local used mower can halve the lean kit, and published insurance starting points run from about $500 to $1,800 a year depending on province, coverage and services. The 13% HST versus 5% GST difference moves the pro kit by $63.76. Amazon.ca prices were checked on October 9, 2026 and move with sales and the season. Noise bylaws and yard-waste rules are municipal; check yours before you set hours or promise bag pickup. This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.

## How do taxes work for lawn care in Canada?

Lawn care income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Fuel, line, bags and blades are ordinary expenses. A mower, trimmer or blower that lasts several seasons is equipment claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use share. If you drive between lawns, keep a kilometre log of date, destination, distance and purpose to claim the business share of vehicle costs. Self-employed profit also means paying both shares of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting all your side hustles, including winter snow work. Lawn care is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. Once registered, use your province's rate from the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).
