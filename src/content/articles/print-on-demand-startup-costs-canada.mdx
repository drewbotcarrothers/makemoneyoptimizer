Starting print-on-demand in Canada means selling your own designs on shirts, hoodies and mugs that a printer makes and ships only after a buyer pays. This page is the cash: what samples, listings, tools and a store cost today, three kits with tax, and how many shirts pay them back. ## How much does print-on-demand cost to start in Canada?

Print-on-demand costs $33.80 to $808.76 to start in Canada, depending on whether you list one tested design on Etsy, add a paid design tool and more samples, or open your own Shopify store. There is no inventory. The money goes on samples, listing fees and subscriptions.

| Kit | What it includes | First-year cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | 1 Printful sample tee with 13% HST, 20 Etsy listings, free Canva | $33.80 |
| Standard | Lean, but 3 sample tees in one order, plus Canva Pro for a year and a phone tripod | $241.51 |
| Pro | Standard, plus a hoodie sample, a mug sample, a Wacom Intuos Small, Shopify Basic for the first year and a domain | $808.76 |

Kits are cumulative. The sample prices assume an Ontario address, where Printful charges 13% HST on the product and shipping. The tripod and tablet are Amazon.ca prices plus 13% HST. Canva, Shopify, Etsy and the domain are shown at their listed prices; your invoice adds GST/HST where the company charges it. Optional e-commerce insurance, about $500 a year, is not in any column.

A Printful sample Bella + Canvas 3001 tee costs $28.10 delivered to an Ontario address, after Printful's 20% sample discount on its free plan and 13% HST. Printful shows catalogue prices in USD, so the figures below convert at the Bank of Canada [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4240 CAD per USD for 8 October 2026.

| 3 tees in one order | 35.76 USD | 28.61 USD ($40.74) | $16.29 ($11.29 + $2.50 + $2.50) | $64.44 |

The tee price is from Printful's public [catalogue for the Bella + Canvas 3001](https://api.printful.com/products/71); 2XL is 13.92 USD and 3XL 15.92 USD. Shipping is from Printful's [shipping page](https://www.printful.com/shipping): $11.29 for the first shirt and $2.50 for each extra one, $13.99 for a hoodie and $10.29 for an 11 oz mug. The sample discount is 20% on the free plan and 25% on Growth, per Printful's [pricing page](https://www.printful.com/pricing).

Two settings change these numbers:

- **Tax by destination.** Printful's [Canadian sales tax page](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264769445905-How-does-sales-tax-work-for-my-Canadian-orders) says it charges tax on the Printful price plus shipping, at the rate for the province the order ships to: 5% in Alberta and the territories, 12% in B.C. and Manitoba, 11% in Saskatchewan, 13% in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia, 14.975% in Quebec and 15% in the other Atlantic provinces. If you are registered for GST/HST, you can add your number under Billing in the Printful dashboard.
- **Billing currency.** Printful [lets you choose](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264577056529-How-do-I-change-my-currency-on-Printful) the currency each store is billed in, including CAD. Billing in CAD avoids your card's foreign-exchange fee, but Printful's CAD price may not match the Bank of Canada conversion above. Compare the checkout total.

Order the sample in the colour and size you photograph. A white tee in a mid-size is the cheapest test of print quality; a dark garment tests how your colours hold up.

## What does each print-on-demand sale cost and earn in Canada?

Each Printful tee sold to an Ontario buyer costs you $31.94 before platform fees: $16.97 for the shirt, $11.29 shipping and $3.67 of HST that you cannot recover unless you are registered. On Etsy at $30 plus $11.29 shipping, about $4.69 is left. At $35, about $9.19.

**Illustrative. The $30 and $35 prices are assumptions, not recommendations or survey results.**

| Per sale, Ontario buyer | Etsy at $30 | Etsy at $35 | Shopify at $30 | Shopify at $35 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer pays (item + $11.29 shipping) | $41.29 | $46.29 | $41.29 | $46.29 |
| Platform and card fees | $4.38 | $4.88 | $1.46 | $1.60 |
| Etsy listing renewal, 0.20 USD | $0.28 | $0.28 | — | — |
| Printful tee + shipping + 13% HST | $31.94 | $31.94 | $31.94 | $31.94 |
| Left per sale | $4.69 | $9.19 | $7.89 | $12.75 |

How the fees are built:

- **Etsy:** 6.5% transaction fee on the item and shipping, 3% + $0.25 CAD payment processing for a Canadian seller's domestic order, and the 0.50% Canadian regulatory operating fee, from Etsy's [fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy), [processing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy) and [regulatory fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) pages. At $41.29: 10% × $41.29 + $0.25 = $4.38. A sale that came through Etsy's Offsite Ads costs another 15% on shops under $10,000 a year.
- **Shopify Basic:** 2.8% + 30¢ for online card payments through Shopify Payments. At $41.29: $1.16 + $0.30 = $1.46. The subscription is counted in the kit, not per sale.

For a market reference, tentree.ca listed graphic tees at $45 and Frank And Oak its Essential Slim Tee at $39 on 9 October 2026. Those are established brands with their own blanks. A new design on a standard blank usually has to justify its price with the art.

## How many sales does it take to break even on print-on-demand?

Print-on-demand breaks even after 8 Etsy tee sales on the lean kit at $30, or 4 at $35. The standard kit takes 52 or 27. The pro kit, selling through its own Shopify store, takes 103 at $30 or 64 at $35.

**Illustrative. Same assumed prices and per-sale margins as the table above.**

| Kit | First-year cost | Sales at $30 | Sales at $35 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean, on Etsy | $33.80 | 8 (7.21) | 4 (3.68) |
| Standard, on Etsy | $241.51 | 52 (51.49) | 27 (26.28) |
| Pro, on Shopify | $808.76 | 103 (102.50) | 64 (63.43) |

The arithmetic is first-year cost ÷ margin per sale, rounded up. Standard at $35: $241.51 ÷ $9.19 = 26.28, so 27 tees. Pro at $30: $808.76 ÷ $7.89 = 102.50, so 103.

Two things stand out. First, the $5 difference between a $30 and a $35 tee roughly halves the number of sales you need, because the base cost does not move. Second, a Shopify store has the better margin per sale but needs about 100 sales in its first year to cover itself at $30, and Shopify does not bring buyers. Etsy's marketplace search does, which is why the lean and standard kits start there.

Change the inputs to yours: your province's tax rate on the Printful order, your actual base product and size, and your price. Price larger sizes separately, since a 3XL tee costs 4 USD more than an XL.

## Where can print-on-demand startup spending be lower, and where should it not be cut?

Print-on-demand spending stays lower when you start with one design on one blank, list it on a marketplace you already use, and skip subscriptions until an order shows you need them. Canva Free handles text designs. A phone by a window handles sample photos. Shopify can wait until you have a way to send people to it, such as an existing following.

Do not cut the sample or the rights check. A sample shows colour, fit and print quality before a buyer does, and a description that does not match the product is a refund and, if it misleads, a problem under the Competition Bureau's guidance on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations). A design that uses a team logo, a character or a phrase someone owns can be removed, and the listing fees and samples spent on it are gone.

Insurance is optional at this size. [Zensurance](https://www.zensurance.com/e-commerce-insurance) gives a starting cost of about $500 a year for a basic policy for a low-risk small e-commerce business. Ask whether product liability covers goods a third party prints and ships for you before you buy.

Keep sale deposits apart from household money. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can hold Etsy and Shopify payouts until the Printful charge and a tax set-aside come out.

## Which tax rules apply to a print-on-demand budget in Canada?

Print-on-demand income in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on the activity for profit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both portions of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule.

The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add all side-hustle revenue together. Under that, registration is optional. If you register, you charge the [rate for the province](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) where the supply is made (use the CRA [calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html)), and the HST Printful charged you on samples and orders can be claimed back as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html). That is why the per-sale table counts the $3.67 of HST as a cost: an unregistered seller pays it and keeps it.

Samples, listing fees, subscriptions and Printful charges are generally current expenses. A drawing tablet or camera that lasts for years may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. Keep order exports, Printful invoices and platform statements. For a set-aside habit, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle in Canada](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
