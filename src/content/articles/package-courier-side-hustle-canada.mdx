## How do you start package courier work in Canada?

Start package courier work in Canada by checking the zone, measuring the cargo area, telling the insurer you will deliver goods for pay, and logging one short offer before you buy a van. A waitlist is a no for this month.

### 1. Check the zone before you buy a dolly

Open Spark if the [Spark Driver Canada](https://www.sparkdriverapp.ca/content/sparkdriverca/en_ca.html) list includes your region, and Amazon Flex if your city is on the [Canada locations page](https://flex.amazon.ca/our-locations). If both are waitlists, write down the date and look at local courier ads. Leave commercial shelving off a waitlist.

### 2. Measure the cargo area

Know how many standard boxes fit with the seats up. Take a photo. Offers that do not fit are declines. A hatchback can do many grocery-sized Spark orders. A hatchback cannot do a route of flat-screen televisions. Believe the measuring tape.

### 3. Call the insurer

Tell the insurer you will deliver store parcels for pay, name the app, and ask what part of the trip the policy covers. Personal policies that exclude carrying goods for compensation stay excluded when the customer prepaid shipping. Platform insurance, if the app provides it, usually sits on top of an active block and still requires you to disclose the work. The longer version of this conversation is in the [food delivery guide](/side-hustles/food-delivery-side-hustle-canada/). Package courier work is the same category of risk: business use of a private car. The rule is the one for the province where the car is plated.

### 4. Apply and wait for the background check

Have licence, insurance, registration, and banking ready. Names must match. The Play listing says the background check is part of Spark signup, and that you are notified when the preferred zone has availability. A waitlisted zone is a no for this month. Use the month.

### 5. Set a loading routine

Heavy items low and against the seatbacks. Fragile up high and wedged. Liquids in a bin. Nothing loose on the rear shelf that becomes a projectile. You are not a moving company, and you should leave parcels closed.

### 6. Take one short offer first

Choose a small number of stops near the store. Time the pickup queue separately from the drive. Practise the failed-delivery flow in the app before you need it: gate code missing, customer not home, business closed.

### 7. Learn the building problem

Buzzers, ice, and “leave in the lobby” notes that building managers forbid are ordinary in Canadian deliveries. Follow the app and the note. If those conflict, use the in-app help. A returned parcel is better than a lost one.

### 8. Add equipment only after you feel the gap

If every heavy stop hurt, buy the dolly before the next block. If boxes slid, buy bins. If the work was fine, leave the cargo van payment for later. A van changes insurance again. Price that on purpose.

### 9. Stack package courier work with other driving only if the rules allow

Some people run food delivery between package blocks. The insurer has to know about all of it. A 7 a.m. warehouse block plus a 6 p.m. dinner shift is a long day of decision-making. Fatigue is how parcels get left at the wrong door.

## What is easy to miss in package courier work in Canada?

The return trip is the silent half of a package courier route in Canada: an offer that ends 40 minutes from home is a longer shift than the app’s estimate. Include the drive home in the hourly math, or line up the end of the route near home on purpose.

Photograph the drop-off the way the app asks, with the house number if you can do it without photographing people. Those photos settle “I never got it” disputes. A photo of only a blurry porch does not settle the dispute.

Winter sidewalks and uncleared steps are a falling hazard. Use boots with grip. A dolly is useless on ice unless you can control it. Carry one heavy box by hand on ice or a narrow stair rather than ride a sliding stack.

Separate pharmacy and age-restricted offers. ID rules are provincial. If the app’s prompt and your judgment disagree, leave the handoff incomplete.

Secure the load. A sudden stop should leave the crate out of the front seat.

## How do taxes and insurance apply to package courier work in Canada?

Package courier income in Canada is generally self-employment income, and GST/HST follows the small-supplier test, which is a different rule from commercial ride-sharing. This guide is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. Insurance for delivering goods is the rule in the province where the car is plated.

Keep offer summaries, fuel receipts, and a kilometre log. Report the income even if a T4A is late, on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with the T1. Plan for the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net self-employment income. A kilometre log supports the business-use percentage of the vehicle. [Capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) spreads a lasting vehicle or trailer over time on that business portion. Bins and gloves are ordinary expenses.

The CRA treats you as a small supplier when worldwide taxable supplies, including associates, are $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Above that, you register. The timing is on the CRA’s [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) page. Package delivery is not the ride-sharing first-dollar rule. Passenger ride-sharing is different from the first fare: see [rideshare driving](/side-hustles/rideshare-driving-side-hustle-canada/) and the CRA’s [taxi and ride-sharing page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html).

The rate depends on the province of the supply. HST, GST plus PST, and Quebec’s GST plus QST are different systems. Confirm the current QST rate with Revenu Québec before you add QST. Nova Scotia’s HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 ([Charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)). Use that table or the CRA [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).

Vehicle documents you may be asked to carry: licence, registration, and insurance. Some cities add commercial parking rules and loading-zone limits. A ticket for stopping in a bus lane belongs in the log. Look up the fee on the city’s page rather than budgeting a number you have not seen.

If a local courier hires you as an employee, the tax story changes: the employer may deduct at source, and the same expense claims may not be available. Ask before you start. The word “contractor” on a poster is not always how the CRA will see the relationship.
