For a Canadian side hustle store, Shopify is the most sales-focused of the three: it lists Canadian-dollar prices, starting at $49 CAD a month for Basic (or $37 a month billed yearly), online card rates from 2.8% plus 30 cents, and a 3-day free trial followed by $1 a month for three months. Squarespace suits sellers who want a portfolio or brand site with a shop attached; its Basic plan is 25 USD a month (19 USD yearly) and adds a 2% online store fee, removed on the Core plan. Wix lets you build a site free and upgrade, with eCommerce on its Core plan and above, but its prices vary by location and are confirmed at checkout. Pick on what you sell and how many orders you expect, not on the headline price.

## Which fees matter most for a small Canadian store?

At low volume the monthly plan matters most; as sales grow, card rates and any platform transaction fee take over.

- **Shopify:** no setup fees, CAD pricing, and in-person card payments at 2.6% if you also sell at markets. The third-party transaction fee only applies if you skip Shopify Payments.
- **Squarespace:** the 2% store fee on Basic adds up as sales grow, so Core can be cheaper overall at higher volume. Courses and memberships carry separate digital content fees of 7% on Basic, 5% on Core, 1% on Plus, and 0% on Advanced.
- **Wix:** the plans page lists features such as Accept payments and Basic, Standard, or Advanced eCommerce by tier. Check the checkout price in CAD and payment rates in your dashboard.

### Illustrative example

Assume $1,000 CAD a month in online sales from 20 orders of $50, on each platform's entry selling plan billed monthly. Order counts and sales are assumptions; Squarespace's USD figures are converted at 1.4246 and treated as if they applied in CAD terms for comparison.

- **Shopify Basic:** plan $49. Card fees: 2.8% × $1,000 = $28, plus 20 × $0.30 = $6. Total about $83 a month.
- **Squarespace Basic:** plan about $35.62. Store fee: 2% × $1,000 = $20. Card fees: 2.9% × $1,000 = $29, plus 20 × about $0.43 = about $8.55. Total about $93.17 a month.
- **Squarespace Core:** plan about $55.56 (39 USD). No store fee. Card fees about $37.55. Total about $93.11 a month.

At this volume the totals are close; the choice depends more on features. Wix is left out of the arithmetic because its prices are confirmed at checkout.

## What mistakes do Canadian sellers make choosing a store builder?

The common mistakes are comparing monthly prices without fees and forgetting currency.

- **Comparing plan prices only.** Add card rates and any platform transaction fee.
- **Ignoring currency.** A USD plan costs more in CAD and moves with the exchange rate.
- **Using a third-party processor on Shopify without noticing the fee.** Shopify adds 2% on Basic in that case.
- **Picking the cheapest Squarespace plan at high volume.** The 2% store fee can outweigh the Core upgrade.
- **Building on a free plan you cannot sell from.** Check which Wix tier includes eCommerce.

## Which tax rules apply to online store sales in Canada?

Store profits are generally business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and plan fees, card fees, and apps are generally deductible. Once your taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters, you generally must [register for GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) and charge it; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). For where to keep store payouts, see [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).
