For a Canadian side hustle store, the decision comes down to three numbers: the monthly plan, the card rate, and whether the platform adds its own fee on each sale. On October 9, 2026, Shopify's Canadian page listed Basic at $49 CAD a month ($37 billed yearly) with online card rates from 2.8% plus 30 cents. Squarespace's page, loaded from Canada, listed Core at CA$45 a month (CA$32 yearly) with 2.9% plus CA$0.30 and no store fee, and Basic at CA$30 (CA$21 yearly) with a 2% store fee. Wix's free plan cannot take payments; selling starts at Core, and Wix Payments charges 2.9% plus 0.30 CAD per card payment in Canada.

At $1,000 a month in sales on yearly billing, the plan-plus-card cost is about $71 on Shopify Basic and $67 on Squarespace Core. The gap is small enough that the deciding factor is what the site is for: a product catalogue with inventory and shipping (Shopify), a portfolio or service site with a shop attached (Squarespace), or a free site you may upgrade later (Wix).

## What does each store builder cost at different sales levels?

Plan prices matter most at low volume; card rates and store fees take over as sales grow. These three examples use the same orders on every platform so only the fees differ.

**Assumed inputs:** $50 average order paid by Visa or Mastercard online, domestic buyers, each platform's own payment processor, no paid apps, no Amex. **Sourced inputs:** the plan prices and card rates above.

### Illustrative example: $1,000 a month from 20 orders

| Plan | Plan (monthly / yearly billing) | Card fees | Store fee | Total, monthly billing | Total, yearly billing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shopify Basic | $49 / $37 | 2.8% × $1,000 + 20 × $0.30 = $34 | $0 | $83 | $71 |
| Squarespace Basic | $30 / $21 | 2.9% × $1,000 + 20 × $0.30 = $35 | 2% = $20 | $85 | $76 |
| Squarespace Core | $45 / $32 | $35 | $0 | $80 | $67 |
| Wix Core | Checkout price | $35 | $0 | $35 + plan | $35 + plan |

Squarespace Core beats Squarespace Basic as soon as 2% of sales exceeds the plan difference: $11 a month on yearly billing, so above $550 a month in sales, or $15 on monthly billing, above $750.

### Illustrative example: $250 a month from 5 orders

- **Shopify Basic, yearly:** $37 + (2.8% × $250 = $7.00) + (5 × $0.30 = $1.50) = **$45.50**
- **Squarespace Basic, yearly:** $21 + $7.25 + $1.50 + (2% × $250 = $5.00) = **$34.75**
- **Squarespace Core, yearly:** $32 + $7.25 + $1.50 = **$40.75**
- **Wix Core:** $8.75 in card fees plus the plan price

At this volume the plan price dominates, and Squarespace Basic is cheapest. Many sellers at $250 a month are better off on a marketplace with no monthly fee at all; see [Etsy vs Shopify](/side-hustles/etsy-vs-shopify-canada/).

### Illustrative example: $4,000 a month from 80 orders

- **Shopify Basic, yearly:** $37 + $112 + $24 = **$173**
- **Shopify Grow, yearly:** $99 + (2.6% × $4,000 = $104) + $24 = **$227**
- **Squarespace Core, yearly:** $32 + $116 + $24 = **$172**
- **Squarespace Basic, yearly:** $21 + $116 + $24 + $80 store fee = **$241**
- **Wix Core:** $140 in card fees plus the plan price

Shopify Grow's 0.2-point lower card rate only covers its extra $62 a month (yearly billing) at about $31,000 a month in online card sales, so Basic stays the cheaper Shopify plan for most side hustles. Squarespace Core and Shopify Basic are within a dollar of each other here; the platform features decide it.

## What does each platform do best for Canadian sellers?

### Shopify

Shopify is the most complete store of the three. The Canadian page lists plan prices and card rates in CAD, so there is no exchange-rate drift on the subscription. Shopify describes Basic as covering sales online, in person and in AI chats. Shopify Payments removes the third-party transaction fee; using another processor on Basic adds 2% of every sale. The trial is short: 3 days free, then $1 a month for three months before the regular price. See [Shopify store startup costs](/side-hustles/shopify-store-startup-costs-canada/) for apps, domain and theme costs.

### Squarespace

Squarespace showed CAD prices to a Canadian visitor, and Squarespace Payments is available in Canada. Every plan can sell products, services and digital products. The trade-off is fees: Basic charges a 2% store transaction fee plus card processing, and courses and memberships pay a separate 7% on Basic. Core removes the store fee and adds automated tax calculation at 0.15% per transaction (maximum CA$1.49), shipping labels and real-time carrier rates. There is no free plan, but the 14-day trial needs no card.

### Wix

Wix is the only one with a free plan, which is useful for building and testing a design. The free and Light plans cannot accept payments; Core adds payments and basic eCommerce, Business adds standard eCommerce, and Business Elite adds advanced eCommerce. Wix Payments' Canadian rates are 2.9% plus 0.30 CAD for cards, 3.5% plus 0.30 CAD for Amex, 2.7% for in-person POS and 0.15 CAD for Interac. International cards add a 1% cross-border fee, and payments in a currency other than your site's main currency add a 2% conversion fee. A chargeback can cost 15 CAD.

## What mistakes do Canadian sellers make choosing a store builder?

- **Comparing plan prices only.** Add card rates and any platform fee; at $4,000 a month, fees are larger than the plan.
- **Paying monthly for a plan you will keep.** Yearly billing saves about 22% to 30% on Squarespace and about 25% on Shopify Basic.
- **Using a third-party processor on Shopify Basic.** That adds 2% of every sale on top of the processor's own fee.
- **Staying on Squarespace Basic past about $550 a month.** The 2% store fee then costs more than upgrading to Core.
- **Building a store on Wix Light.** Light cannot take payments; you need Core or higher.
- **Ignoring foreign buyers.** Wix adds 1% on international cards, and Shopify's international card rate is 3.5% on Basic.

## What could change these numbers?

Plan prices, promotions and card rates change without notice, and Squarespace's annual discounts and Shopify's $1 offer are the parts most likely to move. Wix's Canadian plan price is only confirmed at checkout, so the Wix rows above leave it out rather than guess. Amex, international and in-person rates differ from the standard online rate used in the examples. Prices exclude GST/HST, which is added according to your billing address. ## Which tax rules apply to online store sales in Canada?

Store profits are generally business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and plan fees, card fees and apps are generally deductible. Once your worldwide taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, you must [register for GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) and charge it; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). For where to keep store payouts, see [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).
