Charging for Google Business Profile management in Canada means a one-time fee to set up or fix the profile, then a monthly plan for posts, photos, review replies and hours updates. Six Canadian agencies checked on October 9, 2026 list $297 to $500 for the one-time work and $97 to $300 a month for profile-only management. Build your own prices from the hours each task takes. This work suits someone who can keep a local business's details accurate and follow Google's rules. It's a poor fit for anyone tempted to promise a top spot on Maps.

## Which pricing models work for Google Business Profile management?

| Model | What it covers | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set-up or clean-up fee | Categories, services, hours, description, photos, verification help | Verification can take time outside your control |
| Monthly plan | Posts, photos, review replies, hours updates | Name the number of posts and the reply turnaround |
| Prepaid quarter or year | The monthly plan, discounted | Clients who stop sending photos halfway through |
| Audit only | Written list of fixes | The owner may not act on it |
| Per location | One price per profile for multi-location businesses | Consistency checks across locations take extra time |

## How do you calculate a Google Business Profile price?

```text
Set-up fee     = set-up hours × hourly rate
Monthly plan   = (posts × time each + review replies + photo/hours updates) × hourly rate × 1.10
First year     = set-up fee + 12 × monthly plan
Multi-location = locations × monthly plan, less a small discount for shared work
```

**Time the set-up**, including getting manager access and waiting for the owner. **List monthly tasks with a count**: four posts, replies within two business days, holiday hours. **Add 10%** for messages and approvals. **Then compare** with the agency table: a solo freelancer's price usually lands between IshaanEx's $97 and North SEO's $300 a month.

## How do you raise Google Business Profile prices?

- **Raise at the anniversary** with written notice and a prepay option, as in the template.
- **Sell the prepaid year.** North SEO's annual plan gives two months free; one free month still locks in twelve.
- **Price locations separately.** A second location is more work than a few extra posts.
- **Show the work**, not rankings: review counts, replies sent, posts published, calls from the profile.
- Scripts are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What Google Business Profile pricing mistakes are common?

Promising rankings, logging in with the owner's password instead of manager access, and selling review schemes. Google's [owners and managers page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100?hl=en) explains how owners invite managers, and that only owners can change or remove other owners and managers. Google's [guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) allow one profile per business and say service-area businesses shouldn't show an address customers can't visit. Google's [prohibited content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en) bans incentivized reviews, such as reviews in exchange for payment, discounts or free goods, and posts must follow the [photos, videos and posts policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en).

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect Google Business Profile prices?

Profile management income is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both shares of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, across all your businesses. Once registered, use the rate from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html).
