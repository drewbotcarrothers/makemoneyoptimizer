Google Business Profile management in Canada is usually sold as a set-up or audit fee followed by a monthly plan. Some clients only want the clean-up; others want ongoing posts and review replies.

| Model | What it covers | Watch out for |

## When should you raise Google Business Profile prices in Canada?

Raise Google Business Profile prices in Canada when clients rebook or refer others, when your calendar is full, or when owner back-and-forth regularly takes longer than you priced. Give monthly clients written notice before a change.

Multi-location businesses take more time per profile for consistency checks; price each location rather than offering one flat fee.

## What Google Business Profile pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common Google Business Profile pricing mistakes in Canada are promising rankings, logging in with the owner's password instead of manager access, and selling review schemes that break Google's rules.

Google's [owners and managers page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100?hl=en) explains how owners invite managers, and that only owners can change or remove other owners and managers, so the client keeps control. Google's [guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en) say there should be only one profile per business, the description should not focus on promotions or prices, and service-area businesses should not show an address customers cannot visit. Google's [prohibited content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en) says incentivized reviews, such as reviews in exchange for payment, discounts, or free goods, are not allowed. Post content must follow the [photos, videos, and posts policy](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077?hl=en).
