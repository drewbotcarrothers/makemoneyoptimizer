Dog walking gear for a Canadian side hustle is a sturdy leash, a backup slip lead, waste bags, a treat pouch, and, once the evenings get dark and icy, a high-visibility layer and boots that grip. Most of it costs less than one week of walks, and the owner's own leash and harness stay the default. It suits people who walk in any weather, keep a clean key routine, and will say no to a dog they cannot control.

## How should you choose dog walking gear in Canada?

Choose dog walking gear in Canada by control, visibility, and weather: gear that keeps a dog attached, makes you visible to drivers at 5 p.m. in November, and keeps you upright on ice. These are selection criteria, not product test results.

| Criterion | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Control | Stitching, clip quality, and a leash length you can shorten near traffic | A frayed client leash is not your only option. Carry a backup |
| Owner's instructions | Harness, collar, treats, and anything the owner has ruled out | Allergies and training plans belong to the owner |
| Visibility | Reflective or lit gear on you, not only on the dog | Winter afternoons are dark early across most of Canada |
| Footing | Boots that grip on packed snow and ice | A fall with a leash wrapped around your wrist is the injury to avoid |
| Key security | No address labels on keys; a coded lock box if the owner agrees | A lost labelled key is a security problem for the client |
| Bulk | Bags, treats, and phone fit without hands being full | You need one hand free for the leash and one for the bag |

## Which dog walking gear upgrades matter for a Canadian winter?

The dog walking gear upgrades that matter for a Canadian winter are boots with grip, a high-visibility layer or clip light, and a small human first-aid pouch. A hands-free belt for two familiar dogs comes after a calm month, not on day one with strangers.

| Upgrade item | Typical range (check current price) | Buy it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-visibility layer or clip light | About $15–$40 | Before the clocks change in the fall |
| Winter boots with grip, or traction cleats | About $40–$150 if you do not own them | Before the first icy week |
| Human first-aid basics | About $20–$40 | Once you are out daily. It is for you; the owner's vet is for the dog |
| Hands-free belt for two familiar dogs | About $25–$60 | After a calm month with dogs who walk well together |
| Paw balm, only if owners ask | About $8–$18 | When an owner requests it. Do not apply products they did not approve |

## Does dog walking gear include insurance or a permit in Canada?

Insurance and permits are not gear, but in some Canadian cities they decide how many leashes you can carry. The City of Toronto's [commercial dog walker permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) is for individuals walking four to six dogs commercially. It requires evidence of $2,000,000 of commercial general liability insurance naming the City as an additional insured, and lists a fee of $305.99 for one year from January 1, 2026 (tax not included). Confirm the live page, and check your own city's rules.

If you use an app instead, [Rover Canada](https://www.rover.com/ca/) says pet care providers must pass a background check and that bookings come with the Rover Guarantee. Read Rover's terms for what it covers; it is not the same as your own policy. The [Rover vs independent dog walking](/side-hustles/rover-vs-independent-dog-walking-canada/) comparison covers that choice.

## Which tax rules apply to dog walking gear in Canada?

Dog walking income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. Waste bags, leads, and a business-only clip light are ordinary expenses when used for the business; keep receipts.

GST/HST registration applies once worldwide taxable supplies, including all your side hustles and your associates', pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
