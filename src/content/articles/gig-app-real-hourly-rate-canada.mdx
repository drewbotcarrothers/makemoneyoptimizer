<QuickAnswer>

- **Formula:** real hourly rate = (pay + tips − kilometres × your cost per km) ÷ every hour you were logged in.
- **Floors cover part of the clock:** Ontario guarantees $17.95 an hour of assignment time from October 1, 2026; B.C. guarantees $21.89 an hour of engaged time plus $0.35/km for delivery ($0.45/km for ride-hail). Tips are excluded and the wait is unpaid in both.
- **Car cost:** CAA's figures for a 2025 Nissan Sentra in Ontario work out to about $0.12/km for fuel and $0.26/km with operating costs. The CRA's 73 cents is an employee allowance, not your deduction.
- **Benchmarks:** Job Bank's median for employed food delivery drivers is $20.00 an hour; Amazon Flex says most drivers earn $22 to $27 an hour before costs.

</QuickAnswer>

Your real hourly rate on a gig app in Canada is what the app paid, minus what the kilometres cost, divided by all the time you were logged in. The number on the offer is gross for that trip. Provincial floors in Ontario and B.C. cover assignment or engaged time and leave the wait unpaid. On the illustrative evening below, the same $55 works out to $8.60 to $16.73 an hour depending on the car cost you subtract. This worksheet suits couriers and drivers who will log real shifts before deciding an app is worth it.

## What do the gig apps and provinces publish about pay?

No app publishes an hourly wage for Canada, so the pay pages give you inputs, not a result. What each page said on October 9, 2026:

| [DoorDash](https://help.doordash.com/en-ca/dashers/article/how-is-dasher-pay-calculated) | Base pay of $2 to $10+ per offer, plus promotions and 100% of tips; Earn by Time in select cities pays a guaranteed rate for active time | Per offer, or active time |
| [Uber Eats](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/deliver/) | Pay shown before you accept; no per-km rate card; car delivery requires age 21 | Per offer |
| [SkipTheDishes](https://courier-help.skipthedishes.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360060022453-How-Courier-Pay-Works) | Transit Pay from estimated distance and time; tips not used to subsidize it | Per order |

## What does a kilometre cost?

The car cost is the line that moves the answer most. Three published benchmarks:

| Benchmark | Per km | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CAA](https://carcosts.caa.ca/results/ontario/passenger_mainstream/ice), 2025 Nissan Sentra, Ontario, fuel only | $0.12 | 6.55 L/100 km × $1.82/L |
| CAA, same car, fuel plus operating costs, 15,000–20,000 km a year | $0.26 | $4,549.61 ÷ 17,500 km |
| [CRA travel directive](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/travel-directive/appendix-a-cra-kilometric-rates-jan-2026.html), Ontario, from January 1, 2026 | $0.63 | What the CRA pays its own staff for using their car (B.C. $0.60, Alberta $0.565, Quebec $0.625) |
| [CRA prescribed allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/benefits-allowances/automobile/automobile-motor-vehicle-allowances.html), 2026 | $0.73 first 5,000 km, $0.67 after | A reasonable allowance an employer can pay an employee |

Finance Canada's [January 2026 announcement](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/01/government-announces-the-2026-automobile-deduction-limits-and-expense-benefit-rates-for-businesses.html) set the 73 cents, one cent above 2025. A self-employed driver deducts actual [motor vehicle expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses.html) for the business-use share, from a log, never a per-km rate. Use the benchmarks only until your own receipts give you a number, and label which one you used.

## How do you calculate your real hourly rate?

```text
Gross            = base + promotions + tips + any top-up (from the statement)
Car cost         = all kilometres (including the drive home) × your cost per km
Real hourly rate = (gross − car cost) ÷ logged-in hours
Floor check      = assignment or engaged hours × provincial rate (+ B.C. km amount),
                   compared with pay excluding tips
Offer floor      = minutes ÷ 60 × target hourly + km × cost per km
```

**Use three clocks.** Assignment or engaged minutes are the floor's clock; logged-in minutes are the evening you gave up; the odometer covers the drive home too. **Divide by logged-in time.** Dividing by assignment time hides the wait. **Write a target before the shift.** A [mileage log](/side-hustles/mileage-log-cra-side-hustle-canada/) gives the kilometres.

## What do worked examples look like?

**Illustrative.** Pay, tips, minutes and kilometres are my assumptions, not an app average. Car costs use the CAA and CRA benchmarks above.

### Example 1: an Ontario delivery evening

- $55 including tips, 3 hours logged in, 1.5 hours of assignment time, 40 km.
- Floor on the assignment clock: 1.5 × $17.95 = $26.93, compared with pay excluding tips; the statement shows whether a top-up is owed.
- At $0.26/km: 40 × $0.26 = $10.40; ($55 − $10.40) ÷ 3 = **$14.87 an hour**.
- At fuel only ($0.12): ($55 − $4.80) ÷ 3 = $16.73. At the CRA's $0.73: ($55 − $29.20) ÷ 3 = $8.60.
- Dividing $44.60 by assignment time instead gives $29.73, which describes a different evening than the one you spent.

### Example 2: a B.C. delivery evening

- $40 pay before tips plus $15 tips, 3 hours logged in, 1.5 hours engaged, 28 engaged km, 45 km total.
- B.C. floor: 1.5 × $21.89 = $32.84, plus 28 × $0.35 = $9.80, total $42.64. Pay before tips is $2.64 short of that, so check the weekly statement for a top-up.
- With the top-up: $42.64 + $15 = $57.64. Car: 45 × $0.26 = $11.70. ($57.64 − $11.70) ÷ 3 = **$15.31 an hour**.

### Example 3: an Amazon Flex block

- Block pays $75 for 3 hours ($25 an hour, inside Amazon's stated $22–27). The drive to the station, loading and the drive home make it 3.75 hours and 85 km.
- 85 × $0.26 = $22.10; ($75 − $22.10) ÷ 3.75 = **$14.11 an hour**. Amazon doesn't reimburse mileage, parking or tolls.

### Example 4: an Ontario rideshare day, HST-registered

- Weekly statement: $200 in fares after Uber's service fee, $20 tips, plus GST/HST collected on fares, which belongs to the CRA and stays out of the calculation. 8 hours logged in, 160 km.
- 160 × $0.26 = $41.60; ($200 + $20 − $41.60) ÷ 8 = **$22.30 an hour**. At the CRA's $0.73 stand-in: ($220 − $116.80) ÷ 8 = $12.90.
- Rideshare drivers [register from the first fare](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html); input tax credits on fuel and car costs reduce what you remit.

## What templates help you track your real rate?

```text
SHIFT LOG — [date] — [app]
Logged in: [6:00 pm] to [9:00 pm]        = [3.0] h
Assignment/engaged time (from app)       = [1.5] h
Odometer: start [______] end [______]    = [40] km
Pay before tips [$____] Tips [$____] Promotions [$____] Top-up [$____]
Car cost: [40] km × [$0.26 CAA / my receipts $____] = [$____]
Real hourly rate: ([gross] − [car]) ÷ [logged-in h] = [$____]
Target written before shift: [$____]
```

```text
OFFER CARD (keep on the dash)
Target $[20]/h, car $[0.26]/km
Minimum offer = minutes ÷ 60 × $[20] + km × $[0.26]
  15 min, 5 km   → $6.30
  25 min, 9 km   → $10.67
  40 min, 15 km  → $17.23
```

```text
Hi [platform] support, my statement for [week] shows [1.5] hours of
engaged time and [28] engaged km, and pay before tips of [$40.00].
At [B.C.'s $21.89/h + $0.35/km] that is [$42.64]. Can you confirm
whether a top-up was calculated for that week, and where it appears
on my statement?
```

The offer card arithmetic: 0.25 × $20 + 5 × $0.26 = $6.30; 25 ÷ 60 × $20 + 9 × $0.26 = $10.67; 40 ÷ 60 × $20 + 15 × $0.26 = $17.23.

## How do you raise your real hourly rate?

You can't set the app's price, so the levers are which work you take and what it costs you.

- **Decline below the offer card.** A written minimum stops the slow $3 offer from eating the evening.
- **Cut the unpaid clock.** Log in where and when orders arrive; the wait is the biggest gap between the floor and your logged-in rate.
- **Lower the per-km cost.** A car that costs $0.12/km in fuel instead of $0.20 changes every offer.
- **Check top-ups.** In Ontario and B.C., read each statement against the floor and ask, as in the template, if the arithmetic doesn't match.
- **Compare apps on the same log.** If several normal weeks miss the target, read [when to quit a gig app](/side-hustles/when-to-quit-a-gig-app-canada/).

## What mistakes hide a low hourly rate?

Dividing by assignment time, treating the CRA allowance as a deduction, and judging the app by one boosted night. Also common: leaving out the drive home, and comparing a gross offer with an employee wage that includes a car you don't fuel. Insurance is its own cost: tell the insurer you drive for a platform before the first shift and get the answer in writing.

## Which tax rules apply to gig pay after costs?

Gig pay is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). The vehicle claim is the business share of actual expenses, with the log. Delivery follows the GST/HST [small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): register once taxable supplies pass $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters. Passenger driving is registered from the first fare. Platform reporting is in [tax on reselling and gig apps](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).

Keep payouts in an account that isn't the rent account so the tax share is still there in April. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian option; any separate account works.

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

The app terms and provincial rates are from each page on October 9, 2026; Ontario's rate changes each October and B.C.'s each June. Amazon Flex's $22–27 is the company's own claim before costs. Job Bank wages are for employees in 2023 to 2024. CAA's per-km figures are for one car at one Ontario gas price and don't include platform insurance.
