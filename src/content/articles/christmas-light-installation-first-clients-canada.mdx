## Who should you ask first for Christmas light work in Canada?

Ask lawn clients, snow clients, and near neighbours first for Christmas light work in Canada, because they already know whether you show up. A stranger three neighbourhoods away is a later problem. Travel and daylight are the limit.

## What should the first Christmas light offer in Canada say?

The first Christmas light offer in Canada should name the address, the surfaces, the roof and power-line refusal, who supplies the strings, the install window, the take-down window, the price before tax, the deposit, and how either person can cancel. A sentence that you are not an electrician belongs on the page.

Say whether shrubs and railings are included. Say whether you will plug into an existing outdoor outlet only. A missing GFCI is not your cue to open a wall. The beginner guide separates decorating from regulated electrical work.

Do not offer a free house that becomes a free street. A paid first install, at the written price, teaches both of you whether the timing works. If the property is unsafe, decline it. Ice, a steep roof, an aggressive dog, or a service drop you cannot stay clear of is not a client to collect.
