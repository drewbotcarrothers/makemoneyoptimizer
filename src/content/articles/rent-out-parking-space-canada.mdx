Renting out a parking space in Canada means charging someone to park in a driveway, garage, or stall you control, for a period you both agree. It suits people who have a space they do not use and written permission to rent it. It is a poor fit if the condo, strata, or landlord forbids commercial use of the stall, or if the only "space" is a city street permit that is not yours to sublet.

A garage you want to fill with boxes instead of a car is [renting out storage space](/side-hustles/rent-out-storage-space-canada/). The form question, including a parking example, is [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/).

## Who can rent out a parking space in Canada?

You can rent out a parking space in Canada when you have the right to let someone else use it. That right is not automatic. In Ontario, a condominium corporation's declaration, bylaws, and rules bind owners under the [Condominium Act, 1998](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/98c19). Read them, and get written permission if they require the board's consent. In British Columbia, strata bylaws and the designation of the stall (a strata lot, limited common property, or common property) decide whether you can rent it. A tenant in a purpose-built rental needs the landlord's consent if the lease reserves parking or bans subletting. A municipal on-street permit is a city permission for a vehicle, not a stall you own. Do not advertise a permit you cannot assign.

Zoning can also matter if a home driveway becomes a steady commercial lot. Confirm the use with your municipality. This page does not state a bylaw for every city. ## How do you rent out a parking space in Canada?

### Step 1: Confirm you may rent it

Read the declaration, bylaws, rules, or lease. Email the property manager if the documents are unclear. Save the reply.

### Step 2: Describe the stall honestly

Covered or not, the height limit, EV charging or not, 24-hour access or evenings only, and how the driver gets in. A fob you cannot replace is a reason to pause.

### Step 3: Choose a channel

WhereiPark if it accepts your address. A classified ad if you want a neighbour and will handle payment yourself. SpotHero if you operate a lot it already works with. Skip platforms whose sites describe only the United States.

### Step 4: Price from local stalls, not from another city

Downtown Toronto and a suburban driveway are different markets. Look at a few current listings near you. There is no national monthly rate in this guide.

### Step 5: Put the deal in writing

Who may park, which vehicle, the monthly amount, how access works, what happens if the fob is lost, and how either side ends it. A condo may require its own form. Payment by e-transfer beats cash you cannot trace.

### Step 6: Tell your insurer

Ask the home, tenant, or condo insurer whether a paying parker changes the policy. This guide does not quote a premium. A padlock on a private gate is ordinary hardware if you control the gate. <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="A weatherproof padlock" search="weatherproof padlock" /> is enough for a gate you already have. It does not replace permission from the condo.

## What does a month of parking rent look like?

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch. The rent is assumed, not a city average.

Suppose a reserved stall rents for $120 a month and the parker stays 12 months. Gross is $1,440. Suppose a platform fee you confirmed in writing is 10% , so $144, and a replacement fob costs you $25. Cash left is $1,440 − $144 − $25 = $1,271 for the year, before tax and before any condo fee you were already paying. If the platform fee is different, use that figure. If you were going to pay the condo parking fee anyway, do not pretend the fee appeared because you found a parker.

The CRA's [rental or business](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/rental-income/you-have-rental-income-business-income.html) page lists parking among basic services when you rent space. A single stall with no extra service is the pattern [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) walks through. Adding car washing, valet, or security you provide can push the activity toward a business.

## What mistakes do parking hosts in Canada make?

- **Advertising a stall the bylaws assign to the unit, not to you.** Common property is not your listing.
- **Subletting a municipal street permit.** Read the permit. Many are not transferable.
- **Sending a fob before the first month is paid.** You are handing over access.
- **Using a U.S. app because the logo looks familiar.** Neighbor and Spacer's U.S. site are not proof of Canadian coverage.
- **Ignoring tax because the amount feels small.** Rent is still income. The [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) is the short version.

## Which tax rules apply to parking rent in Canada?

Renting space and providing only basic services is usually income from property, reported with [Guide T4036](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4036/rental-income.html) and Form T776. The CRA page lists heat, light, parking, and laundry as basic services. If you add enough extra services that the operation is a business, the income moves toward Form T2125. GST/HST depends on whether the supply is taxable and on the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). A residential parking stall tied to an exempt residential lease can be treated differently from a standalone commercial stall. Confirm that split for your facts. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
