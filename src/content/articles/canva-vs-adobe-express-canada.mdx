Canva and Adobe Express are browser-based design tools that Canadian freelancers use for social posts, flyers, slide decks and short videos. Both have a real free plan. On October 9, 2026, Canva's Canadian pricing page listed Canva Pro at $142 CAD a year for one person and Canva Business at $260 CAD a year per person, before tax. Adobe's Canadian Express pricing page listed Adobe Express Premium at CAD $12.99 a month, billed monthly with no annual commitment, and Adobe Firefly Pro, which bundles Express Premium with more AI credits and Photoshop on web and mobile, at CAD $25.99 a month.

Over a full year, Canva Pro is about $16 cheaper with Ontario HST. Over a few months, Express Premium is cheaper because there is no annual commitment. If you already pay for Creative Cloud Standard, Creative Cloud Pro or most single-app plans, Express Premium is included and costs nothing extra.

## How much does each tool cost a Canadian freelancer per year?

The arithmetic below uses the published prices and Ontario's 13% HST. If you are registered for GST/HST, you can generally claim the tax back as an input tax credit; if you are not, the tax is part of your cost.

| Plan | Price before tax | Yearly with 13% HST | Yearly with 5% GST | Per month with 13% HST |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canva Pro | $142 a year | $160.46 | $149.10 | $13.37 |
| Adobe Express Premium | $12.99 a month | $176.14 | $163.67 | $14.68 |
| Canva Business | $260 a year | $293.80 | $273.00 | $24.48 |
| Adobe Firefly Pro | $25.99 a month | $352.42 | $327.47 | $29.37 |

### Illustrative example: a full year of client work

**Assumed input:** eight design clients a year. **Sourced inputs:** the prices above.

- **Canva Pro:** $160.46 ÷ 8 = **$20.06 per client**
- **Adobe Express Premium:** $176.14 ÷ 8 = **$22.02 per client**
- **Already on Creative Cloud:** Express Premium adds **$0**, so Canva Pro would be a second subscription.

The difference between the two is $15.68 a year. That is less than one hour of design work, so the deciding factor is which tool you work faster in and which one your clients use.

### Illustrative example: four busy months a year

**Assumed input:** you only need premium templates and background removal for a four-month busy season, such as holiday promotions or wedding season.

- **Adobe Express Premium for four months:** 4 × $12.99 × 1.13 = **$58.71**, then cancel
- **Canva Pro yearly:** **$160.46**, whether you use it for four months or twelve

Canva also sells Pro monthly, and its page says yearly billing saves from 16%, so check Canva's monthly price at checkout before comparing a short season.

## What can you sell or trademark with Canva or Adobe Express content?

You can generally use stock content inside an original design for client work and merchandise, but not as a standalone item for sale, and not as a trademark. Read the licence before you hand a logo to a client.

- **Logos and trademarks.** Canva says you cannot use any Free or Pro content from its library in a trademark, except fonts, basic shapes and lines, because other users can use the same content ([Canva Help](https://www.canva.com/help/trademarks-logo/)). Its logo templates are non-exclusive, so a logo built from one cannot be registered.
- **Merchandise.** Canva allows designs printed on products for sale if you comply with its licence, but its [Content License Agreement](https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/) bars displaying content on a standalone basis on print-on-demand sites, such as a plain stock photo on a t-shirt.
- **Adobe Express premium content** carries a limited licence; Adobe's help page links the licence terms. Read them for the asset type before using it on products or logos.
- **AI output.** Adobe says outputs from Firefly-powered features can generally be used commercially unless a feature is marked as a beta for personal use only.

If a client needs an exclusive mark, draw it from scratch or commission it, then use either tool for the brand kit around it.

## What mistakes do freelancers make with Canva and Adobe Express?

The common mistakes are licence and billing mistakes, not design mistakes.

- **Selling a template-based logo as exclusive.** Canva says its logo templates cannot be registered as trademarks.
- **Putting raw stock on merch.** Standalone stock on print-on-demand products breaks Canva's licence.
- **Paying for two suites.** Check whether your Creative Cloud plan already includes Express Premium.
- **Paying yearly for seasonal work.** Four months of Express Premium cost $58.71 with HST; a year of Canva Pro costs $160.46.
- **Not pricing the subscription in.** Spread the yearly cost across clients; at eight clients it is about $20 each.
- **Losing the receipts.** Keep the invoice for each subscription for your tax return.

## What could change these numbers?

Both companies change plans, AI allowances and prices, and Canva's monthly price and the length of its free trial were not shown on the yearly view used here. The 13% HST figures become 5% in Alberta and the territories and 14% in Nova Scotia. Promotions and student or education pricing can be lower than the list prices above. ## Which tax rules apply to design tools for Canadian freelancers?

Design-tool subscriptions used for client work are generally deductible business expenses on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and design income is generally business income. You must register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Once registered, the GST/HST on your Canva or Adobe subscription can generally be claimed back as an input tax credit.
