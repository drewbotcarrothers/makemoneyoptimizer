## How do you set your own size tiers?

Define tiers by what a client can see, time one driveway in each tier, then turn minutes into prices. Keep the price gaps in line with the time gaps, and check them against the ratios above.

### Step 1: Define the tiers

Clients can't tell you square metres, but they know how many cars fit.

- **Tier 1:** single-car, short driveway.
- **Tier 2:** single-car long, or tandem (two cars nose to tail).
- **Tier 3:** double-wide.
- **Tier 4:** triple-wide, long double, corner lot or shared pad, quoted individually. Blizzard Wizard quotes circular, long and corner-lot layouts individually too.

### Step 2: Time one of each

Clear one driveway in each tier during a normal snowfall and write down the minutes, including setup, the walk and steps if they're part of the base price, and walking to the next house. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) explains measuring and timing. A <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="measuring wheel" search="measuring wheel distance" /> makes long driveways quick to size. Amazon.ca listed the Zozen collapsible wheel at $45.99 and the Komelon ML1212 at $42.50 on October 9, 2026.

### Step 3: Turn minutes into prices

```text
Per-visit price  = (minutes on site + travel) ÷ 60 × hourly target + salt
Seasonal price   = per-visit price × 8 to 10
Tier check       = Tier 3 seasonal ÷ Tier 1 seasonal should be about 1.1 to 1.4
```

Published seasonal prices work out to roughly 7 to 10 one-time visits: BlueGuard's $499 season is about 10 of its $49 visits, and Km Seasonal's $550 is about 7 of its $75 visits.

### Step 4: Add a depth trigger and a heavy-snow price

State the depth that starts a visit, and charge more above a second depth, because deep or wet snow can double your time. Snoweco prices three levels: blow-in, under 8 cm and over 8 cm. A Quebec pricing guide from [Sendwork](https://blog-fr.sendwork.com/deneigement-residentiel-quebec/) suggests a major-storm surcharge of $25 to $50 on a single driveway. Seasonal contracts need a visit or snowfall cap, as covered in the [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/).

## Which add-ons belong on a Canadian price sheet?

Walkway and steps, the public sidewalk where by-laws make it the owner's job, windrow returns, salt and the corner lot are the add-ons Canadian companies charge for. Price each one separately so clients see what they're buying.

- **Walkway and steps.** These are shovel work even if you blow the driveway. Snowforge charges $199 for a front walkway, $99 for steps or $249 for both on its 5 cm plan.
- **Public sidewalk, only where the owner is responsible.** Toronto's [sidewalk page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/streets-parking-transportation/road-maintenance/winter-maintenance/clearing-snow-and-ice-from-your-property/) says the City clears public sidewalks once 2 cm falls, so residents clear only smaller snowfalls. Calgary's [by-law](https://www.calgary.ca/bylaws/snow-ice.html) gives owners 24 hours to clear bordering sidewalks to bare surface, with fines of $250, $500 and $750. Hamilton's [by-law](https://www.hamilton.ca/home-neighbourhood/getting-around/streets-sidewalks/sidewalk-snow-clearing) gives owners 24 hours unless the property is on the City's route.
- **Windrow returns.** Say whether a return trip after the plough is included or extra.
- **Salt or ice melt.** Price per application or include it with a limit, so an ice-storm week doesn't eat the margin.
- **Where the snow goes.** Ottawa's [roads by-law](https://ottawa.ca/en/living-ottawa/laws-licences-and-permits/laws/laws-z/use-and-care-roads-law-no-2003-498) prohibits depositing snow on a road, and Toronto lists a $500 fine plus a $110 victim surcharge for pushing it onto sidewalks or roads.

## How do you raise prices on a price sheet?

Raise the standard price each September and give returning clients an early-booking price, the way Blizzard Wizard's $550 standard price drops to $495 prepaid until October 31. Add a faster-response tier (Blizzard Wizard charges $540 instead of $495 for a 4–6 hour window) and a lower-trigger tier (Snowforge's 2.5 cm plan is $1,149 against $649 at 5 cm). Keep the tier ratios the same when you raise, so double driveways don't drift out of line.

## How should a snow removal quote be written in Canada?

Write down the tier, add-ons, trigger, heavy-snow price, cap and payment terms before the first snowfall. In Ontario, the province says the final price [can't be more than 10% above a written estimate](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) unless the client agrees to a new price. Federally, the Competition Bureau says advertising a price that isn't attainable because of added mandatory fees is [drip pricing](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/drip-pricing), which the law prohibits; government charges like sales tax are the exception. Build any fuel or booking fee into the tier price.

```text
QUOTE — [address] — 2026–27
Tier: 3 (double)           Seasonal: $450 (up to 25 visits)
Add-ons: front walk + steps $100; ice melt on walk $8 per application
Heavy snowfall (over 15 cm): +$15 per visit
Extra visits after 25: $50 each
Total seasonal price: $550 before tax
Payment: $275 on signing, $275 by January 1, 2027
```

## What mistakes do people make with snow removal price sheets?

- **One flat price for every driveway.** Big driveways get subsidized by small ones. Every published list above has at least two tiers.
- **Pricing the sidewalk where the city clears it.** Check the local rule first.
- **No depth trigger or cap.** Clients and you will disagree about what counts as a snowfall, and a record winter wipes out your hourly rate.
- **Advertising a base price, then adding a mandatory fee.** Quote the full price.
- **Tier gaps out of proportion.** If a double takes 1.7 times as long and costs 1.1 times as much, you lose money on doubles.

## How does GST/HST apply to snow removal prices?

You must register for GST/HST once your taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). After that, show prices before tax and add it on the invoice: Tier 3 at $450 becomes $508.50 with Ontario's 13% HST. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/). This is not tax advice.
