## What are the best side hustles for retired professionals in Canada?

The best side hustles for retired professionals in Canada use expertise without needing a practising licence, and let you set your own hours.

Small businesses often need experienced advice for a few hours, not a full-time hire. [AI consulting](/side-hustles/ai-consulting-side-hustle-canada/) and [Zapier automation](/side-hustles/zapier-automation-side-hustle-canada/) are newer niches where business experience counts as much as technical skill. ## What licence rules apply to retired professionals?

Regulated professions are provincial, and retiring usually changes your status:

- **Non-practising classes.** The [College of Nurses of Ontario](https://cno.org/maintain-your-membership/professional-liability-protection-plp) says nurses in the Non-Practising Class don't need professional liability protection, while practising classes do, at minimum amounts set by the college. Being non-practising means you can't offer nursing services, so stick to non-clinical work like tutoring or writing.
- **Public accounting.** In Ontario, [CPA Ontario](https://www.cpaontario.ca/members/regulations-guidance/regulatory-publications/when-is-pal-required-in-ontario) says a Public Accounting Licence is needed to lead assurance engagements, and compilations a third party will rely on that don't use the prescribed report wording. Bookkeeping generally falls outside that, but confirm with your body.
- **Titles.** Some professions restrict titles to current members. If you've resigned or retired, describe what you did ("former engineer," "30 years in accounting") rather than using a designation.

Former employers may also have confidentiality or non-solicitation terms that outlast your job. Check any you signed before consulting for competitors.

## How do OAS and CPP interact with side hustle income?

- **OAS recovery tax.** The [Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/old-age-security/repayment.html) says that if your 2025 net income is over $93,454, you repay 15% of the amount above that threshold, deducted from OAS payments from July 2026 to June 2027. Net self-employment income counts toward net income.
- **CPP Post-Retirement Benefit.** If you continue to work while receiving your CPP retirement pension and you're under 70, your contributions go toward [post-retirement benefits](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/cpp-post-retirement.html), which increase your retirement income.

The [retirees guide](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-retirees-canada/) goes deeper on both.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not anyone's real return: a retiree's 2025 net income from pensions is an assumed $90,000, and they add $8,000 of net consulting income.

- New net income: $90,000 + $8,000 = $98,000
- Amount over the threshold: $98,000 − $93,454 = $4,546
- OAS repayment: $4,546 × 15% ≈ $681.90

## What mistakes do retired professionals make with side hustles?

- **Offering regulated services at a non-practising status.** Check your class.
- **Using a designation you've given up.** Describe your experience instead.
- **Ignoring the OAS threshold.** Plan around net income.
- **Underpricing expertise.** Price for value, not just hours.
- **Forgetting CPP contributions.** Self-employed people under 70 who receive CPP may still owe them.

## How is a retired professional's side hustle taxed in Canada?

Report self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Because pension income already fills the lower brackets, set aside money from each payment; [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) explains the method. If taxable revenue passes $30,000, see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
