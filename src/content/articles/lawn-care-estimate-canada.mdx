To write a lawn care estimate in Canada, measure the yard, time similar lawns, and write a scope that says exactly what each visit includes. Then quote per cut or per season, with add-ons priced separately. A good estimate also says what you won't do, such as spraying where provincial pesticide rules don't allow it. This guide is for new lawn care operators who want quotes they can repeat across a street.

## How do you measure a lawn for an estimate in Canada?

### Step 1: Measure from a map

Google Maps lets you [measure distance between points](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/1628031?hl=en) by clicking along a path. Trace the front and back lawn edges to get rough lengths and widths before you visit. It's a starting point, since trees, gardens and fences don't always show clearly from above. For a tape-free site check, a measuring wheel is the fastest tool: on October 9, 2026, Amazon.ca listed the Komelon ML1212 Meter-Man at $42.50 and a Zozen collapsible wheel at $45.99. Compare <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="measuring wheels" search="measuring wheel" /> if you quote more than a few lawns a week; pacing works for the first five.

### Step 2: Walk the yard

Spend five minutes on site. Note slopes, gates and their width, trees, beds, play equipment, dog waste, and where clippings would go. These change your time more than the square metres.

### Step 3: Compare with lawns you've timed

Match the yard to similar ones you've already cut and timed. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/lawn-care-pricing-canada/) covers timing each lawn and adding fuel, wear and disposal.

## What should a Canadian lawn care estimate include?

### Step 4: Write scope lines

- Mowing front and back, at an agreed height.
- Trimming along fences, beds and obstacles.
- Edging walks and driveways, if offered.
- Clippings: mulched, bagged, or removed.
- Blowing off hard surfaces.

### Step 5: Price add-ons separately

Spring and fall cleanups, leaf removal, aeration, overseeding, and hedge trimming each get their own price. In Toronto, [yard waste pickup](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/yard-waste/) runs every other week from around mid-March to mid-November in kraft paper bags or rigid containers. Say whether you leave bags for pickup or haul them away. The [fall leaf cleanup guide](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/) covers that add-on.

### Step 6: Exclude what you can't legally do

Ontario's [home lawns and gardens page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticides-home-lawns-and-gardens) limits cosmetic pesticide use on lawns to products on an allowable list, with health and safety exceptions. Its [licences page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticide-licences-and-permits) says that, unless exempt, you need a licence to apply pesticides commercially on property you don't own. Other provinces set their own rules, so write "no chemical treatments" unless you've checked yours.

### Illustrative example

Suppose a front and back lawn takes you 35 minutes to mow, trim and blow, plus 10 minutes of travel from your last stop. Your per-cut price would be about 45 minutes of your target rate plus a fuel share. A seasonal option would multiply that by the planned number of cuts, with a clause for extra cuts in wet weeks. Bagging and hauling clippings would be a separate line.

## How should a lawn care estimate be worded in Canada?

Put the scope, price per cut or season, schedule, rain policy, payment terms and change terms in writing. In Ontario, if a written estimate is part of the contract, the province says the [final price can't be more than 10% above it](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) unless the client agrees to a new price and signs a change. Don't advertise a base cut price and then add a mandatory fuel or disposal fee. The Competition Bureau says [unattainable advertised prices](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/drip-pricing) break the law unless the extra is a government charge such as sales tax.

```text
GST/HST added only if registered. Payment: e-transfer within [7] days.
```

## What mistakes do people make with lawn care estimates in Canada?

- **Quoting from a map alone.** Gates, slopes and beds change your time.
- **Including disposal without pricing it.** Hauling takes time and may cost depot fees.
- **Offering treatments you can't legally apply.** Check provincial pesticide rules first.
- **No rain or extra-cut clause.** Fast-growing weeks need an answer in writing.

## How do GST/HST and income tax apply to lawn care estimates in Canada?

You report lawn care income on Form T2125 and register for GST/HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). After registering, show the tax as its own line on each estimate. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), the [mileage log guide](/side-hustles/mileage-log-cra-side-hustle-canada/), and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/).
