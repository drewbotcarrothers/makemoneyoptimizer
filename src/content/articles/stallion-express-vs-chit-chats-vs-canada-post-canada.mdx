- **Short answer:** if you ship small parcels to U.S. buyers regularly, compare Stallion Express and Chit Chats first; Canada Post is the simplest option for occasional domestic parcels and anyone far from a consolidator's drop-off point.

Stallion Express and Chit Chats are Canadian shipping consolidators: you drop parcels at their locations (or book a pickup), they bundle volume and pass on lower postage. Canada Post is the national carrier you can use at any post office. This comparison is for resellers, Etsy and eBay sellers, and small online shops. Rates below are the sample rates each company published on October 7, 2026, in CAD; your price depends on weight, size and destination.

## Which shipping option is cheapest for small parcels?

On their own sample rates, both consolidators were far below the national-carrier figures they quoted: Stallion's 4 oz parcel to New York was $4.39 versus $17.62, and Chit Chats' 85 g necklace within Canada was $3.83 versus $15.29. Get a real quote for your parcel before you switch, because the samples are chosen by each company.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not a quote: a reseller ships 40 small parcels a month and saves $8 per parcel by using a consolidator.

- Monthly saving: 40 × $8 = $320.
- If the round trip to a drop-off point takes an hour a week and you value your time at $25 an hour, that costs about 4 × $25 = $100 a month.
- Net: roughly $220 a month in this example.

The per-parcel saving, volume and time value are assumptions for the arithmetic.

## What shipping mistakes do Canadian resellers make?

- **Comparing headline samples, not your parcel.** Quote your real weight and box size.
- **Ignoring dimensional weight.** A light but bulky box can price as heavier.
- **Skipping tracking on marketplace orders.** Platforms often require proof of delivery for disputes.
- **Forgetting U.S. duties and fees.** Check the current tariff and CUSMA rules on each consolidator's site.
- **Not deducting shipping.** Postage and supplies are business expenses; see [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).
