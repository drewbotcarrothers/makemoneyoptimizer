Getting your first window cleaning clients in Canada means walking a street of ground-floor glass you can reach without a ladder, with a written price, and asking each yes for one neighbour. It suits someone who will knock or leave a note where that is allowed, and who will say no to a third storey. It does not suit someone who needs winter exterior work or who will climb because the client asked.

The tools, the season, and the ladder rule are in [how to start window cleaning in Canada](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/). Per-pane and per-home arithmetic is in [how to price window cleaning in Canada](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-pricing-canada/). Signs, scripts, and the email rules after you leave are in [door-to-door marketing for side hustles in Canada](/side-hustles/door-to-door-marketing-side-hustle-canada/).

## Who should you ask first for window cleaning in Canada?

Ask the nearest ground-floor glass first for window cleaning in Canada. Streets with larger homes can be worth the walk because they have more panes, but only the panes you can reach from the ground with a pole. A three-storey ask is how a first week becomes a fall. The Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety's page on [portable ladders](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) says falls from portable ladders are a major source of serious injury. Your first clients should not depend on climbing.

Storefronts are the other first ask. A shop owner can say yes or no in a minute, and a row of shops is a route. Homes and shops are both in the [start guide](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/).

## What should a first window cleaning offer say in Canada?

A first window cleaning offer in Canada should say exterior or interior, ground floor or a named height, and a price the owner can check against the panes they see. A short script:

> I clean ground-floor exterior windows on this street. I don't use a ladder. For a front like yours I would count the panes and give you one number before I start. Here is a card with my phone number.

Then count, quote from the method in the [pricing guide](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-pricing-canada/), and wait for a yes. Do not start while they are "thinking about the back as well."

Toronto licences mobile salespeople who travel on foot and sell goods door-to-door. The City's [Hawker and Pedlar on Foot](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/street-vending-non-food-hawkerpedlar-assistants/sales-on-foot/) page says those sellers must obtain that licence, and the parent page describes hawkers and pedlars as people who sell goods or provide services such as knife sharpening and shoe shining. A window cleaner leaving a note may or may not be in that category. Confirm with the city before you treat a flyer route as unlicensed, and check the city you actually work in. Bylaw changes for Toronto hawkers and pedlars are described as beginning March 1, 2027. This is not legal advice.

## How do you get a second window cleaning client from the first in Canada?

You get a second window cleaning client from the first in Canada by finishing the glass you priced, asking for one neighbour, and booking that neighbour while you are still on the street.

### Step 1: Finish only the panes on the quote

If they add the side of the house, recount and reprice before you continue.

### Step 2: Ask for one neighbour, not a list

"Is there one house on this block whose windows you think I should offer?" is enough. Do not take phone numbers off mailboxes. A follow-up email or text that promotes the service needs consent under [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en). A paper note does not. The detail is in the [door-to-door guide](/side-hustles/door-to-door-marketing-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 3: Walk to that house the same day

The referral is warmest while your bucket is still on the sidewalk. If nobody is home, leave the note if the door allows it.

### Step 4: Offer the storefronts on the way home

One shop, one price for the front glass, and a day you can repeat. A main street beats a single plaza at the edge of the city.

### Step 5: Write the next date

Spring and fall are when many homes book again. A storefront may want a shorter gap. Put it on a calendar the same day you are paid.

## What should you avoid when looking for window cleaning clients in Canada?

Avoid any job above your height limit, any street with a no-soliciting rule you plan to ignore, and any "buy a route" offer. Paying a stranger for a guaranteed list of window clients is a pattern described in [side hustle scams in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/).

Ontario's page on [signing or cancelling a contract](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) says most agreements for a product or service that costs more than $50 should be in writing, with a door-to-door purchase as one example, and that a written estimate in the contract cannot be exceeded by more than 10% unless the consumer agrees to a new price. A $48 illustrative front from the pricing guide sits near that line. Use a written price when the visit crosses $50, and read the page for the cooling-off wording before you take a large prepayment. This is not legal advice.

## What first-client mistakes are common in window cleaning in Canada?

Common first-client mistakes in window cleaning in Canada are climbing for the first yes, quoting "the whole house" from the sidewalk, and leaving no next date.

- **The ladder exception.** One upstairs pane is how the height limit disappears. The [CCOHS ladder page](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) is the reason to keep the limit.
- **Interior by surprise.** Someone has to be home, and the price is different. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-pricing-canada/) separates it.
- **A free clean "for the photo."** A paid small front is a better sample than a free house.
- **Scattered addresses.** Two clients on one street are a route. Two clients in two suburbs are a driving lesson.
- **No insurance conversation.** [Side hustle insurance](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/) is the question to ask a broker before you work over someone's garden. This is not insurance advice.

## Which tax rules apply to the first window cleaning clients in Canada?

Fees from the first window cleaning clients in Canada are self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both portions of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all of your side hustles.
