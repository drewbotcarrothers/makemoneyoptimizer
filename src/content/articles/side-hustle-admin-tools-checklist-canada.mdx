## Which admin jobs does a Canadian side hustle need tools for?

Go through the list once and tick each job off.

### Step 1: Money in and tax money aside

A dedicated account for business payments and a second one for tax. That pair does more for your records than any app. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> and online banks have low-fee options.

### Step 2: Receipts

The CRA says receipts should show the date, the seller and a description of what you bought ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html)). A photo, saved in a folder by month, covers it.

### Step 3: Invoices

Number them, date them, and include what you did and the amount. Once registered for GST/HST, add your registration number and the tax.

### Step 4: Vehicle log

If you'll claim vehicle expenses, the CRA expects a logbook with the date, destination, purpose and kilometres of each business trip ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses/motor-vehicle-records.html)).

### Step 5: Contracts and quotes

Save each signed quote or contract with the client's name and date.

### Step 6: Calendar and client list

One calendar for all bookings, and a simple client list. If you'll email clients offers, ISED's CASL guidance on consent and unsubscribe applies ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)).

### Step 7: Income and expense tracking

One row per transaction, with a category that matches the T2125 expense lines. Accounting software such as <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can do this from bank feeds when the spreadsheet gets slow.

### Step 8: Tax filing

Use certified tax software that supports Form T2125, or a tax professional. See [reporting side hustle income to the CRA](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

## How do you choose between free and paid admin tools?

Start free and upgrade one job at a time, when it takes more than a few minutes a week or causes mistakes. Paid software doesn't change what the CRA expects; it only changes how long it takes you. For bookkeeping apps specifically, see [free and cheap bookkeeping tools](/side-hustles/bookkeeping-tools-side-hustle-canada/).

## What admin setup mistakes do side hustlers make in Canada?

- **Buying software before having a process.**
- **Receipts in three places:** email, a wallet and a camera roll.
- **No backup** of electronic records.
- **Using a personal account for business payments.**
- **Waiting until April** to build the spreadsheet.

## How does an admin setup help at tax time in Canada?

Each job feeds the return: deposits give gross income, receipts and the mileage log support expenses, and the tax account pays the bill. Paid tools used for the business are generally deductible business expenses; see [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).

## How do you track which side hustles actually pay?

To track which side hustles actually pay in Canada, record income, expenses and hours for each hustle separately, then compare profit per hour once a month. A single spreadsheet with one row per transaction and a "hustle" column is enough. The tracking also does double duty: the CRA asks for a separate Form T2125 for each business or professional activity if you have two or more ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/completing-form-t2125.html)), so splitting your numbers by hustle now saves sorting them at tax time.

### Why track each Canadian side hustle separately?

Because combined totals hide the weak one. A busy delivery week and a slow Etsy month can average out to "fine" while one of them loses money per hour. Separate tracking shows which hustle deserves more of your time.

It's also how the CRA expects the return to look. If you run two or more distinct business activities, each gets its own T2125 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/completing-form-t2125.html)). Shared costs, such as a phone used for both, are split on a reasonable basis.

### How do you set up side hustle tracking in Canada?

Set it up in one sitting and update it weekly.

#### Step 1: Create one sheet with a hustle column

Use the columns in the table above. Free spreadsheet tools are enough; you can move to accounting software later.

#### Step 2: Run business money through one account

A separate account means your bank statement becomes most of your income and expense list. See [separating business and personal money](/side-hustles/business-bank-account-side-hustle-canada/#how-do-you-keep-business-and-personal-money-separate-after-the-account-is-open).

#### Step 3: Log hours as you go

A notes app or calendar entry per session is enough. Include travel, quoting and messaging time.

#### Step 4: Keep vehicle use per hustle

The CRA asks for a logbook of business kilometres to support vehicle expenses ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses/motor-vehicle-records.html)). Note which hustle each trip was for. See [the CRA mileage log guide](/side-hustles/mileage-log-cra-side-hustle-canada/).

#### Step 5: Review monthly

Total each hustle's income, expenses and hours, and calculate profit per hour.

#### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers. Someone runs three hustles in November:

| Hustle | Income | Expenses | Hours | Profit per hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Food delivery | $600 | $180 (fuel, phone) | 35 | $12.00 |
| Dog walking | $480 | $20 (bags, treats) | 20 | $23.00 |
| Etsy printables | $90 | $25 (fees) | 6 | $10.83 |

Delivery brings in the most money, but dog walking pays the most per hour. Printables are low per hour this month, but listings keep selling without new hours, so they review that one over a longer period. The obvious move is to shift a few delivery hours to dog walking if there's demand.

### What should you do with the numbers each month?

Use them to make one decision at a time.

- **Cut or reduce the lowest earner per hour** if it stays lowest for several months. [When to quit a gig app](/side-hustles/when-to-quit-a-gig-app-canada/) walks through this for app work.
- **Raise prices on the hustle with a waitlist.** See [building a waitlist](#how-do-you-run-a-side-hustle-waitlist-under-casl).
- **Watch the combined GST/HST total.** The $30,000 small supplier test adds up all your businesses; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Feed the year-end review.** Twelve monthly totals make [the first-year price review](/side-hustles/first-year-side-hustle-price-review-canada/) quick.

### What mistakes do people make tracking side hustle income?

- **Tracking income but not hours.** Profit per hour needs both.
- **Tracking only gross.** Expenses change the ranking.
- **Mixing personal and business purchases.**
- **No receipts.** The CRA expects documents that support each entry ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html)).
- **Judging long-tail products by one month.** Digital products and listings can earn after the work is done.

## What does a quarterly side hustle money check-in cover?

A quarterly side hustle money check-in is a short routine you do at the end of March, June, September and December: match your records to your bank account, add up taxable sales for the GST/HST small supplier test, top up your tax savings, check whether instalments apply, and see which work is paying. Quarters are the natural unit in Canada because the CRA measures the $30,000 GST/HST threshold over calendar quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)), and instalment due dates fall in each quarter ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments/due-dates.html)).

### What should a quarterly side hustle money check-in cover in Canada?

Block 30 minutes in the first week after each quarter ends and run the same five checks.

#### Step 1: Reconcile records

Match each business account transaction to an invoice or receipt. The CRA expects records that support your income and expenses ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html)). Fix gaps now while you still remember them.

#### Step 2: Run the GST/HST small supplier test

Total this quarter's taxable sales from all your businesses, then add the previous three quarters. The CRA's rules have two triggers ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)):

- **Over $30,000 in a single calendar quarter:** you stop being a small supplier on the sale that takes you over, and must charge GST/HST on that sale.
- **Over $30,000 over four consecutive quarters, but not in one:** you stop being a small supplier at the end of the month after the quarter in which you went over.

If you're getting close, read [GST/HST registration for side hustles](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) before next quarter. Ride-hail drivers register from the first fare regardless.

#### Step 3: Top up tax savings

Multiply this quarter's profit by your set-aside rate and compare it with what's in the tax account. Remember self-employed CPP: both portions, 5.95% each in 2026 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/canada-pension-plan-cpp/cpp-contribution-rates-maximums-exemptions.html)).

#### Step 4: Check instalments

You may have to pay instalments if your net tax owing is more than $3,000 ($1,800 in Quebec) this year and in either of the two previous years ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments/who-pays-instalments.html)). The CRA sends instalment reminders to people likely to have to pay. Due dates are March 15, June 15, September 15 and December 15. See [CRA tax instalments for side hustles](/side-hustles/cra-tax-instalments-side-hustle-canada/).

#### Step 5: Review profit per hour

For each hustle, income minus costs divided by hours. Drop or reprice what isn't working; see [the pricing worksheet](/side-hustles/side-hustle-pricing-worksheet-canada/).

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A freelance writer does a check-in on July 3. Records match, apart from one missing software receipt, which they download. Taxable sales were $6,500 this quarter and $21,000 over the last four, so they're not near $30,000 yet but will watch next quarter. Their tax account holds $3,100; profit this quarter was $5,900 and their set-aside rate is 25%, so they move $1,475 for this quarter. No instalment reminder arrived. ### How does the December check-in differ?

It's the same five checks plus year-end work: gathering a full year of receipts, recording your vehicle's odometer, preparing for platform slips in the new year, and planning your return. [Side hustle tax prep before January](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-prep-before-january-canada/) covers that list in detail.

### What mistakes do people make with quarterly money check-ins?

- **Only checking once a year,** when the GST/HST test and instalments are quarterly.
- **Counting one hustle for GST/HST** instead of all your businesses.
- **Topping up tax savings from personal money** in April instead of from the business account each quarter.
- **Ignoring an instalment reminder.**
- **Skipping the profit review** because the totals look fine.

## How do you build a simple client acquisition system?

A client acquisition system for a Canadian side hustle is a small routine you repeat every week: show up in two channels where your customers already look, record every enquiry in one tracker, reply and follow up on a fixed schedule, and ask happy clients for a referral or review. It doesn't need software. It needs consistency, and a few legal guardrails: Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) governs follow-up emails and texts ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)), and the Competition Bureau expects reviews and testimonials to be based on actual experience ([Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act)).

### How do you build a client acquisition system for a Canadian side hustle?

Set it up once, then run the same loop every week.

#### Step 1: Pick two channels

Choose where your customers already look. Local services can use a [Google Business Profile](/side-hustles/google-business-profile-side-hustle-canada/), community groups and flyers; online freelancers can use a marketplace profile and direct outreach. Two channels done consistently are easier to judge than six done occasionally.

#### Step 2: Make one clear offer

Every channel should say the same thing: what you do, where, for whom, and how to book. [The one-page business plan](/side-hustles/side-hustle-business-plan-canada/) is where you write it.

#### Step 3: Track every lead

A spreadsheet with date, channel, name, request, quote, follow-up date and outcome. After a month, you can see which channel actually produces paying clients.

#### Step 4: Reply fast, quote clearly

A quick, specific reply with a price or a clear next step. Use [the pricing worksheet](/side-hustles/side-hustle-pricing-worksheet-canada/) so your quotes are consistent.

#### Step 5: Follow up once

If someone goes quiet after a quote, one polite follow-up a few days later is reasonable. CASL says implied consent from an inquiry lasts six months ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)); for ongoing promotional emails, ask for express consent and include an unsubscribe.

#### Step 6: Ask for referrals and reviews

After a job goes well, ask: "If you know anyone who needs this, I'd be grateful for a referral," and send your review link. Never write reviews yourself or ask friends to review work they didn't receive.

#### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A new house cleaner picks two channels: a Google Business Profile and a neighbourhood Facebook group. Over six weeks they log 18 enquiries in a spreadsheet: 11 from the group, 7 from the profile. Five group leads book; four profile leads book. They follow up once on every unanswered quote, which wins two more. After each first clean, they send a review link. By week six, they know the profile converts better per enquiry, so they spend more time on photos and reviews there.

### What legal rules apply to finding clients in Canada?

Three sets of rules apply.

- **CASL** covers commercial emails and texts: consent, identifying yourself, and an unsubscribe that's actioned within 10 business days ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)). See [email marketing for side hustles](/side-hustles/email-marketing-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Competition Act** rules on reviews and claims: base reviews and testimonials on actual experience ([Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act)), and don't use fake urgency or scarcity, such as countdowns that reset or untrue claims about limited spots ([Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations)).
- **Privacy:** PIPEDA, or a similar provincial law in Alberta, B.C. and Quebec, applies to personal information you collect in commercial activity ([Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/)). Keep your lead tracker private and collect only what you need.

### What client acquisition mistakes do side hustlers make in Canada?

- **Trying every channel at once** and judging none of them.
- **No tracker,** so you can't tell what works.
- **Slow replies** to enquiries.
- **Following up forever,** or adding enquiries to a newsletter without consent.
- **Fake reviews or fake scarcity.**

## How do you run a side hustle waitlist under CASL?

A side hustle waitlist in Canada is a simple list of people who want your service when a spot opens. Build one when you're consistently full: collect a name, contact details, the service they want and their timing, ask for clear permission to contact them, and fill openings in a fair, stated order. If you'll send them emails or texts that promote your business, Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) applies. ISED says express consent is someone agreeing, verbally or in writing, to receive messages from you, with no time limit unless they withdraw it, and every unsubscribe request must be actioned within 10 business days at no cost ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)).

### When should you start a side hustle waitlist in Canada?

Start one the second or third time in a month that you turn down work you'd have liked. Typical moments are the first snowfall for [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/), spring for [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/), and the weeks before holidays for [pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/). A waitlist also helps freelancers whose calendar is full for the next month.

### How do you set up a waitlist for a Canadian side hustle?

Keep it simple enough to run from a phone.

#### Step 1: Pick one place to collect names

A free form tool, a spreadsheet, or the signup form in an email service. One list beats scattered texts and DMs.

#### Step 2: Ask for consent clearly

Use an unticked checkbox with plain words, such as: "Yes, email me when a spot opens and send occasional updates from Northside Dog Walking. You can unsubscribe any time." ISED's guidance says express consent means the person said yes to being contacted, and that you should clearly identify yourself and your business ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)).

#### Step 3: Say how the list works

State the order (first come, first served, or by route), roughly how often spots open if you know, and that joining doesn't guarantee service.

#### Step 4: Contact people in order and give a deadline

"A Tuesday morning spot opened. Reply by Friday if you'd like it." If they pass, move to the next name.

#### Step 5: Clean the list

Remove people who found someone else, who asked to be removed, or whose consent has run out.

#### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A house cleaner books eight regular clients and has no room for more. Instead of saying no, they reply to each new enquiry with a waitlist link. The form asks for name, email, neighbourhood and preferred day, plus an unticked consent box. Over the next two months, a regular client moves away. The cleaner emails the first person on the list in the same neighbourhood, gives them three days to accept, and fills the spot without advertising. They also note that the list keeps growing, which feeds their next [price review](/side-hustles/first-year-side-hustle-price-review-canada/).

### What does CASL require for a side hustle waitlist in Canada?

CASL covers commercial electronic messages, such as emails and texts that promote your services ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en)). The practical rules for a small waitlist:

- **Consent:** express consent has no time limit unless withdrawn. Implied consent from an existing business relationship can last up to two years, but only six months after an inquiry or application ([ISED](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email)).
- **Identification:** say who you are and how to reach you in every message.
- **Unsubscribe:** include a clear, easy unsubscribe and act on requests within 10 business days.
- **Records:** keep a note of when and how each person gave consent.

### What privacy rules apply to a waitlist in Canada?

PIPEDA sets the ground rules for how private-sector organizations collect, use and disclose personal information in commercial activity. Alberta, B.C. and Quebec have their own substantially similar private-sector privacy laws ([Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/)). For a side hustle, that means collect only what you need, say what you'll use it for, keep it secure, and don't share or sell the list.

### What mistakes do side hustlers make with waitlists?

- **A pre-ticked consent box.** Consent should be an active yes.
- **Adding enquiry emails to a newsletter.** Implied consent from an inquiry is short and doesn't cover every kind of message.
- **No order or deadline.** People feel skipped and spots sit empty.
- **Collecting addresses or details you don't need yet.**
- **Never raising prices.** A long, growing list is a pricing signal.
