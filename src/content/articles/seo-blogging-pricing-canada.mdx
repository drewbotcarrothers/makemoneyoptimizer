Pricing on an SEO blog in Canada is mostly about the one income line you set yourself: sponsored posts. Display ads and affiliate commissions pay at rates the programs publish. Three Canadian blogs and publishers list $350 to $500 for a sponsored post, and Job Bank's median wage for writers gives the floor for the writing time. Selling sponsored posts suits a blog with steady search traffic in a niche brands want to reach. It's a poor fit for a new blog with no traffic to show a sponsor.

Ad networks and affiliate programs pay at rates they publish, and the bigger networks have entry rules.

| Amazon.ca Associates | Fixed rates by category: 10% luxury beauty, 9% pet food and supplies, 6% home and furniture, 4% kitchen, books and sports, 3% toys, 1% computers, 0% gift cards and grocery, 2% for all other categories | [Amazon.ca commission statement](https://associates.amazon.ca/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ) |

Your AdSense report is the only reliable per-view number for your site. A sponsored post that replaces an affiliate box on a ranking page should cost at least what that box earns; Quote 4 shows the arithmetic.

## Which pricing models work for sponsored blog posts?

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New sponsored post | Writing plus publishing, flat fee | Most sponsors | Unlimited edits; set one approval round |
| Sponsor an existing post | A short sponsor blurb on a page that already ranks | Pages with proven traffic | Changing a page readers trust |
| Package | Post + newsletter mention + social share | Blogs with a list | Each channel needs its own disclosure |
| Term-limited placement | The sponsor section stays for a set period | High-traffic pages | "Permanent" placements you'll maintain for years |
| Link-only request | Payment for a followed link and nothing else | Nobody | Link spam under Google's policies; decline |

## How do you calculate a sponsored post price?

```text
Writing fee     = hours (research, draft, images, review, publish) × hourly rate
Placement fee   = what the audience is worth: page views, list size, niche
Package price   = writing fee + placement fee + add-ons (newsletter, social)
Existing post   = placement fee + short writing time, for a set term
Floor check     = never below what the page earns from ads/affiliates over the term
```

**Time the work honestly.** Sponsor review rounds add time your own posts don't have. **Price the hours from Job Bank.** Use your province's median or higher. **Then check against the published rates.** The $350 to $500 range above is what Canadian sponsors already see. **Write the link terms.** Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) treat buying or selling links for ranking as link spam, and paid links are fine marked rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow". The Competition Bureau's [influencer guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says to disclose payment clearly.

## What do worked sponsored post quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** The $45 target rate (above Ontario's $40.38 median), the hours, traffic figures and sales are my assumptions. GST/HST appears only once you're registered.

### Quote 1: new sponsored post, Ontario personal finance blog, 20,000 monthly pageviews

- Writing: 6 hours × $45 = $270.
- Placement: $130, so the total lands inside the $350 to $500 published range.
- Quote **$400**, one approval round, rel="sponsored" link, disclosure at the top. Not registered, so no HST.

### Quote 2: sponsoring an existing ranking post for 12 months

- Writing the sponsor section: 1.5 hours × $45 = $67.50.
- Anchor: Live & Earn Canada lists about $350 to sponsor an existing post.
- Quote **$300 for 12 months**, removed or renewed after that. The placement share is $300 − $67.50 = $232.50.

### Quote 3: package with a newsletter mention, HST-registered in Ontario

- Sponsored post from Quote 1: $400. Newsletter mention to 4,000 subscribers: $120 (Live & Earn lists about $250 to 9,000).
- $400 + $120 = $520. With 13% HST: $520 × 1.13 = **$587.60**.

### Quote 4: when a sponsor wants to replace an affiliate box

- The page sends Amazon.ca buyers who spend $1,500 a month in a 4% category: 4% × $1,500 = $60 a month, $720 a year.
- A 12-month sponsor placement that removes the box must cover $720 plus the writing: $720 + 2 hours × $45 = **$810 at minimum**.
- If the sponsor won't pay that, keep the affiliate box.

## How do you raise sponsored post prices?

- **Update the audience numbers first.** Send screenshots of page views and list size; a sponsor pays for reach they can see.
- **Raise at rebooking.** Hold the old price for anything booked before a date, as in the template.
- **Sell the package, not the post.** Live & Earn Canada's $450 includes a newsletter and social mention; add those before raising the base price.
- **Limit the term.** A 12-month placement can be renewed at the new rate; a permanent one can't.
- Scripts are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What SEO blog pricing mistakes are common?

Common mistakes are selling followed links, pricing only the writing time, and planning around one good month of ad income. Selling followed links can hurt the whole site in search. Never accept a deal that forbids a disclosure or the rel="sponsored" tag, and never quote a page-view number you can't show. The [SEO blogging side hustle guide](/side-hustles/seo-blogging-side-hustle-canada/) covers building pages that keep traffic.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect blog pricing?

Ad revenue, commissions and sponsored posts are business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, across all your businesses. Once registered, charge the rate for the place of supply from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). Payments from foreign ad networks and sponsors outside Canada can be treated differently; confirm with a tax professional once you register.
