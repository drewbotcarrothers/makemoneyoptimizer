Lawn care in Canada is a seasonal side hustle: mowing, trimming, and clearing clippings for people with lawns, from spring until the grass stops. Lawn care suits people who can do physical work in the heat, store a mower, and repeat a local route, and lawn care does not suit anyone who needs grass income in January.

In much of the country the product is weekly cuts, then leaves and, later, snow, or the season simply ends. Operators who stay calm treat lawn care that way. The winter half of the same driveways is [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/). Small repairs along the same street often turn into [handyman work](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/). Indoor recurring work is [house cleaning](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/).

## Who does lawn care in Canada suit?

Lawn care in Canada suits people who can do physical work in the heat, store a mower, and live where people have lawns rather than only balconies. Lawn care also suits early risers who can mow inside municipal noise hours and before the day gets hot, and people who like repeating a route. Lawn care is a poor fit with nowhere to keep fuel and equipment, a body that will not tolerate pushing a mower, a high-rise whose superintendent already has a contract, or a need for income in January from grass.

A riding mower is not step one. A reliable push mower, a string trimmer, and a rake will tell you whether you like the work. Ontario and Quebec restrict cosmetic lawn pesticides. Details are in the pesticide section below. Check the province where you actually mow before you spray anything.

## How long is the lawn care season in Canada?

The lawn care season in Canada follows the region: southern Ontario and southern Quebec stay green long enough to matter, the Prairies and the North are shorter, Atlantic Canada slips in the rain, and British Columbia's south coast is a longer green season with moss. Grass does not follow a national calendar. Noise hours for lawn care are municipal. Check the city's noise bylaw. This guide does not state one city's start time or a leaf-blower ban.

- **Southern Ontario and southern Quebec:** a long enough season to matter, with a spring rush and a hot July that slows growth.
- **Prairies:** a shorter, intense season. Drought can shrink the route. You do not control rain. Price that limit into your expectations.
- **Atlantic Canada:** a decent season, more rain, and schedules that slip. Build slack into the week.
- **British Columbia's south coast:** a longer green season and moss, not the same brown-August story. The interior is closer to a prairie calendar.
- **The North:** a short season. Do not buy southern-Ontario equipment debt for a six-week cut.

Leaves are a second product in many cities. Bagging or hauling them is heavier than mowing and may be limited by municipal yard-waste rules. Know whether you can leave clippings, whether the city takes paper bags only, and where dumping is not allowed. Those rules are on the city's waste page.

## How should lawn care be priced in Canada?

Lawn care prices in Canada should be a per-cut fee for a defined lawn, because a small, flat, fenced front yard is not a corner lot with a ditch, and the guide does not publish a national rate. Walk the property. Note width, hills, gates, and whether you must bag.

Weekly service on a set weekday is easier to sell and easier to deliver than random calls. Biweekly only works when the grass cooperates. In a wet June, biweekly looks shaggy and you will be asked to come early. Put that in the conversation.

You are selling about as many cuts as the climate gives you. If you need twelve equal months of income, pair lawn care with [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) or indoor work. July's revenue does not happen in February.

Get three local flyers or posts and see what maintained lawns actually go for, then check the number against hours and fuel. If the winner's price only works with unpaid help, that price is not your price.

## Which pesticide rules apply to lawn care in Canada?

Lawn care in Canada should be sold as cutting and trimming unless the provincial pesticide rule allows the product you intend to use. In Ontario, [Pesticides (home lawns and gardens)](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticides-home-lawns-and-gardens) says you cannot use certain pesticides, including products that contain 2,4-D, diazinon, or glyphosate, to kill weeds and insects on lawns, and cosmetic use is limited to active ingredients on the allowable list. In Quebec, the [Pesticides Management Code](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cr/P-9.3,%20r.%201) sets the provincial lawn pesticide rules, so read that code before you apply anything.

A hardware-store weed killer is not automatically a lawn care service in Ontario or Quebec. Other provinces and cities may have their own rules. This guide does not describe those other rules. Fertilizer has its own label. Take extra care near water. If you are not sure the product is allowed, sell cutting and trimming only. That is a complete service. "The customer wanted it" is a weak reason to spray.

## How do you start lawn care in Canada?

Starting lawn care in Canada means counting real lawns on your block, checking the municipal noise bylaw and the provincial pesticide rule, and doing one paid cut before you buy a trailer. The steps below are that sequence.

### Step 1: Walk your own block and count real lawns

Not parks. Not condos with a crew. Houses and small rentals whose owners are not already mowing happily. Five possible clients within a walk is a better start than a Facebook ad to the whole region.

### Step 2: Check noise, clipping, and pesticide rules

Cities set hours for mowers and leaf blowers. Read your city's noise bylaw and yard-waste page. This guide does not name a city's hours. For pesticides, use the Ontario and Quebec pages in the section above if you work in those provinces, and read your own province if you work somewhere else.

### Step 3: Buy or borrow the smallest working kit

Mower that starts, trimmer, rake, fuel, protection for ears and eyes, closed boots. Service the mower before the first paid cut. Blade sharpness changes both the look and the strain on you. A shop can sharpen a blade. Ask what that shop charges. This guide does not set a sharpening price.

### Step 4: Write a one-screen offer

Weekly cut and trim, clippings handled the way the city allows, price for a typical yard on your street, and the weekday you will come. Say you do not spray unless the provincial rule allows the product. Say what happens in a rain delay: you come the next dry day, not at 10 p.m.

### Step 5: Knock or post locally once

A short note through the door still works for lawns because the buyer can see the grass. Include your first name, the street you are on, the price range, and a phone number. Skip the five-paragraph origin story.

### Step 6: Do the first cut in daylight and leave edges

Blow or sweep clippings off the walk. That five minutes is the difference between a cut that looks finished and a cut the client will not keep. Take a photo for your own record if the client is fine with it. Do not photograph children, plates, or open garages.

### Step 7: Lock a weekday

"I'll be here Thursdays" is the product. Weather exceptions get a text the night before or the morning of. Silence is how you lose a lawn to the teenager next door.

### Step 8: Track time and fuel per lawn

After four cuts you know which properties are underpriced. Fix those prices for next month rather than subsidizing a corner lot all summer. Offer the client the new number with notice. Some will say no. That is allowed.

### Step 9: Add fall, or do not

If you want leaf cleanup, price it separately and learn the municipal bag rules. If you do not want leaf cleanup, say so in September before clients assume you are their autumn plan. The same conversation is how you introduce snow without being cornered in November. The [snow guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) is the one to follow if you go there.

## What can the first week of lawn care look like in Canada?

The first week of lawn care in Canada can happen in spring, or in early fall if you are selling the rest of this season and next. The week is a counted block, a mower that starts, and one paid cut.

- **Day 1:** Count lawns on two streets. Read the municipal noise bylaw and the pesticide rule for your province.
- **Day 2:** Secure a mower that starts. Price insurance with an honest description: residential cutting, no pesticides unless you are allowed to use a named product, no employees.
- **Day 3:** One-screen offer. Fuel, line, glasses, ears.

- **Day 5:** First paid cut. Time it. Sweep the walk.

## What is easy to miss on a lawn care route in Canada?

Lawn care in Canada looks better with a sharp blade, a dry lawn, and a sidewalk that is swept, and a new property needs a walk-through before the first pass. A dull blade tears grass and tugs the mower.

Do not mow wet grass to stay on schedule. Wet grass clumps, slips, and looks worse. Text the client. A rain delay you explain is professional. A mud streak is not.

Edging and the sidewalk sweep are the visible quality. The middle of the lawn is the easy part.

Rocks, toys, and irrigation heads hide. Walk the lawn once before you mow a new property. A broken sprinkler head can erase several cuts of profit.

Fuel storage follows the can and the municipality: approved can, out of the sun, not in a stairwell, not beside the furnace. Your insurer may have a view as well. Ask. This is not insurance advice.

## Which tax rules apply to lawn care in Canada?

Lawn care fees in Canada are self-employment income reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. A mower that lasts for years may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. GST/HST for lawn care follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep a list of clients, dates, and what you spent on fuel and repairs. Report lawn care on Form T2125 with the return. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, including associates, of $30,000 or less in one calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register. The CRA's when-to-register page has the timing, including the difference between crossing the line in one quarter and crossing it over four. Lawn care is not ride-sharing. Do not apply the taxi first-dollar rule here.

Capital cost allowance spreads the cost of lasting equipment on the business-use portion, in the class the CRA assigns. A simple first year with modest used gear is easier to explain than a trailer. If the amounts get large, ask an accountant. A truck or car used to haul the mower needs a kilometre log if you claim vehicle costs. Do not claim the family car as 100 percent business.

Charge the rate for the province once you are registered. That might be HST, or GST plus a provincial tax. Confirm Quebec and every other province on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).

Business licences are municipal. Pesticide rules in Ontario and Quebec are the pages linked above, and other provinces need their own check. Noise bylaws and yard-waste rules are municipal. Workers' compensation may matter later if you hire. A person you pay to help on Saturdays can still be your worker. Check the board in your province before you scale.
