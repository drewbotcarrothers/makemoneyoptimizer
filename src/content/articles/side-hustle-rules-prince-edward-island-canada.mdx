In Prince Edward Island, a side hustle under your own name usually has nothing to register with the province. If you use a business name, PEI lists a $40 name search and a $90 registration, renewed for $75 every three years. HST is 15%, charged only after you pass the $30,000 small-supplier test, and employees earn at least $17.30 an hour from October 1, 2026, rising to $17.60 on April 1, 2027. This guide suits Islanders starting a seasonal service, selling crafts or food to visitors, or picking up gig work.

It is part of the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) set. For depth, read [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/), [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), and [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

## Do you need to register a side hustle in PEI?

Only if you trade under a business name. PEI's [name rules guide](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/en/information/justice-and-public-safety/guide-to-corporate-and-business-name-rules) states an own-name exemption for sole proprietors. If you use any other name, the province's [new sole proprietorship sheet](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/publications/new_sole_proprietorship.pdf) lists a $40 name search and a $90 registration through the online Corporate and Business Names Registry. Registration is valid for three years and the fee is $75 every three years.

### Step 1: Search the name ($40)

The search confirms the name is available before you pay to register.

### Step 2: Register online ($90)

Keep the registration for your bank and note the three-year renewal.

### Step 3: Check your business number

The CRA lists PEI among the provinces where a BN is issued automatically when you register ([When you need a BN](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html)).

## When do PEI side hustlers charge HST?

After passing $30,000. PEI's HST is 15% on the CRA [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and you register once worldwide taxable sales exceed $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). A busy summer quarter alone can trigger it, which matters on an island where tourism work is concentrated in a few months. Rideshare drivers register from the first fare.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Cavendish-area craft seller makes $28,000 of sales in July to September and $3,000 the rest of the year. The single summer quarter is under $30,000, but four consecutive quarters total $31,000, so registration is required at the end of the month after the quarter in which the four-quarter total passed $30,000. Had the summer quarter alone passed $30,000, registration would apply from the sale that crossed it.

## Which permits do side hustles need in PEI?

Local ones, which vary by municipality, plus activity-specific provincial permits for things like food. Search [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/) by location and business type before you sell at markets or take clients at home. The [holiday market vendor guide](/side-hustles/holiday-market-vendor-guide-canada/) covers the booth side of selling.

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in PEI?

Not a platform-specific one. PEI's [Minimum Wage Order](https://www.princeedwardisland.ca/sites/default/files/89b2/20260331truwww_0.pdf) sets $17.30 an hour from October 1, 2026 and $17.60 from April 1, 2027, for employees. That covers seasonal employees such as event and tourism staff, not independent contractors on apps.

## What are the common PEI side hustle mistakes?

- **Ignoring a big summer quarter.** HST registration can be triggered by one quarter over $30,000.
- **Paying for registration you do not need.** Trading under your own name is exempt.
- **Forgetting the three-year renewal.** Budget $75.
- **Charging HST before registering.** Only registered businesses add it.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in PEI?

Report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net earnings, and PEI income tax is calculated on the same return.
