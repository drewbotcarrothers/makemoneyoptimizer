## Which pricing models work for social media management?

Sell a monthly package by posts, platforms and community hours, quote audits and shoots as projects, and charge a separate fee for running ads.

| Model | How it works | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly package | Posts × platforms + community hours + report | YYC Social: $399 / $599 / $799 |
| Posts per week | Volume tiers by posting frequency | Toronto Digital Solutions: 3 / 5 / daily |
| Per-platform add-on | Extra fee per added channel | YYC Social: $49/month; guide: $300–$1,500 |
| Engagement add-on | Community management as its own line | YYC Social: $449/month |
| Audit or strategy project | One-time fee before monthly work | Guide: $500–$2,500 |
| Ad management | Fee for running ads; spend paid by the client | Makacek: add-on |
| Annual prepay | Discount for paying the year | YYC Social: 20% off |

Write how many posts, which platforms, comment response times, revision rounds and that the client pays ad spend directly to the platform.

## How do you calculate a social media management rate?

Build an hourly rate, estimate the month's hours from posts, platforms, community management, planning and reporting, then price the package.

```text
Hourly rate     = (target pay + CPP + tools per hour) ÷ share of hours billable
Monthly hours   = posts × minutes per post ÷ 60
                  + community hours per platform × platforms
                  + planning + reporting
Package price   = monthly hours × hourly rate
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $40/hour (between the $35.58 median and $57.44 high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $4.76 |
| Canva, scheduler, analytics per hour | $3.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 65% (pitching and admin are unpaid) |
| **Rate** | ($40 + $4.76 + $3) ÷ 0.65 = $73.48, **round to $75** |

## How should you write a social media proposal and invoice?

Name posts, platforms, community hours, approvals, reporting and ad spend.

```text
SOCIAL MEDIA PACKAGE — [client]                    Date: [date]
Package: Plus — 16 posts/month on Instagram and Facebook
  Includes: content calendar for approval by the 25th, captions,
  graphics from your brand template, scheduling, 2 hours/month of
  comments and DMs per platform (replies within 1 business day),
  monthly report
Price: $1,165/month, billed on the 1st
Ad management: $225/month. Ad spend is billed by Meta to your card.
Not included: video shoots ($450 half-day), new platforms
  ($225/month each), paid influencer deals
Revisions: 1 round per monthly calendar
Notice to cancel: 30 days. No follower or sales guarantees.
Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ____]
```

## How do you raise social media management prices?

Raise prices when a client's scope grows past the package, when your calendar is full, or at renewal.

1. **Track hours per client monthly.** A client who sends daily extra requests belongs in a bigger package.
2. **Charge per platform.** YYC Social and the Owls Digital guide price each added channel.
3. **Sell community management separately.** YYC Social's engagement add-on is $449.
4. **Price video and shoots as projects.** Owls Digital puts short-form video in the $1,500+ experienced tier.
5. **Raise new clients first.** Give retainer clients 30 days' notice.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], from [January 1] your Standard package moves from $[750] to
$[825] a month. Posts, platforms and reporting stay the same.
— [Your name]
```

## What social media management pricing mistakes are common?

- **Unlimited posts or revisions.** Name the count and the rounds.
- **Ad spend on your card.** The client pays the platform directly.
- **Free extra platforms.** Each one adds adapting and community hours.
- **Promising follower numbers.** Price the work you control.
- **Ignoring disclosure.** If a client pays creators or gives free products for posts, Canada's Competition Bureau says influencers should [disclose all material connections](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) with the business they promote; build that check into your process.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect social media management prices?

Social media management income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration, and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to hold back.

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
