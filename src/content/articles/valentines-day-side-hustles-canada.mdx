Valentine's Day 2027 is on Sunday, February 14, which makes it a short, early-February side hustle window in Canada. The work that pays is mostly done in January: designing a small gift or printable line, opening date-night babysitting or pet sitting slots, or pitching Valentine promos to local businesses. Set a shipping cut-off using Canada Post's delivery standards so parcels arrive by Friday, February 12, and plan pickup or local delivery for the weekend. Follow the rules that apply to what you sell: fibre labels on handmade textiles, provincial rules for home-baked goods, and consent for promotional emails. Keep it small and pre-sold where you can, since unsold Valentine stock has little value after the 14th.

## Which rules apply to Valentine's products in Canada?

The rules depend on the product.

- **Textiles:** knitted scarves and similar consumer textile articles generally need fibre-content and dealer-identity labels; the Competition Bureau's [guide](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/guide-textile-labelling-and-advertising-regulations) explains alternatives for home-crafted articles.
- **Baked goods and chocolate:** home food sales follow provincial and local rules; see [holiday baking and cottage food rules by province](/side-hustles/holiday-baking-cottage-food-rules-by-province-canada/).
- **Email promotions:** Canada's [anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies to commercial electronic messages, so email only people who opted in.
- **Childcare:** babysitting families will ask about first aid and references; prepare them in January. For a pay benchmark, Job Bank's wages for home child care providers (NOC 44100, 2023–2024) show these hourly medians:

| Region | Low | Median | High |

| Montréal | $16.60 | $20.00 | $25.00 |
| Lower Mainland–Southwest | $18.25 | $20.00 | $28.00 |
| Calgary | $16.24 | $21.00 | $26.00 |
| Ottawa | $17.95 | $18.23 | $22.88 |
| Winnipeg | $16.40 | $18.00 | $23.00 |
| Canada | $15.00 | $19.00 | $28.00 |

Calgary's $21.00 median is the highest of these; Ottawa's $18.23 sits just above Ontario's $17.95 minimum wage. Source: [Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/wagereport/occupation/17173). The [babysitting guide](/side-hustles/babysitting-side-hustle-canada/) covers first aid and rates.

## How should you price a Valentine's product?

Price from materials, your time, and payment fees, and sell a small number of fixed bundles. 

### Worked example

Assume a gift box with $14 in materials, 30 minutes of work at a $24-an-hour target, a $36 price, and in-person card payment on Square's free plan at 2.5% ([Square pricing](https://squareup.com/ca/en/pricing)). The materials, time and price are assumptions.

- **Packaging inside the $14:** a <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="COSCANA 100-pack of cellophane treat bags" asin="B0CCV2QHNL" /> was $7.99 and a <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="Hiceeden 50-pack of windowed boxes" asin="B0BPY589NK" /> $27.99 on Amazon.ca on October 9, 2026, about $0.08 and $0.56 a gift.
- **Cost:** $14 + 0.5 × $24 = $26.
- **Card fee:** 2.5% × $36 = $0.90.
- **Margin per box:** $36 − $26 − $0.90 = $9.10 above your time.
- **30 pre-sold boxes:** 30 × $9.10 = $273 above your time and materials, plus 30 × $12 = $360 for your 15 hours.

## What mistakes do Valentine's Day side hustlers make?

The common mistakes are starting in February and making stock nobody pre-ordered.

- **Starting late.** Listings and bookings need January lead time.
- **Shipping too close to the 14th.** February 14, 2027 is a Sunday; aim for arrival by Friday.
- **Overproducing.** Pre-sell, then make.
- **Unlabelled textile gifts.** Labels apply to handmade items too.
- **Emailing old contacts without consent.** Follow anti-spam rules.

## Which tax rules apply to Valentine's side hustle income in Canada?

Seasonal sales and services are generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and materials, packaging, and payment fees are generally deductible. GST/HST registration is generally required once taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters across all your business activities ([when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)).
