## Do I have to report side hustle income if I only made a small amount?

Yes, if the amount is income. The CRA self-employment pages do not publish a minimum that lets you leave business income off [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) because the year felt small. Whether tax is owing depends on the whole return. The walkthrough is [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

## Is June 15 the day I have to pay tax on a side hustle in Canada?

No. For the 2025 return, the CRA [filing dates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/important-dates-individuals/filing-dates-tax-return.html) page says you generally file by June 15, 2026 if you or your spouse or common-law partner carried on a business, and any balance owing is due April 30, 2026. Filing later does not move the payment date. Confirm the year, including the weekend rule.

## Is $30,000 the income-tax threshold for a side hustle in Canada?

No. $30,000 is the GST/HST small-supplier threshold on the CRA [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) page, measured on taxable supplies in a calendar quarter and over four consecutive calendar quarters. Income tax starts from the income you report, not from that line. The full split is [the $30,000 GST/HST myth](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/).

## When does a side hustle in Canada have to register for GST/HST?

A side hustle has to register when you are no longer a small supplier and you make taxable supplies in Canada, with the dates on the when-to-register page. Under the threshold, registration is usually optional. You can register voluntarily, and you may have to stay registered for at least a year. The timetable, Quebec QST, and the rate table are in [GST/HST registration for side hustlers](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

## Do ride-sharing drivers in Canada wait until $30,000 to charge GST/HST?

No. The CRA [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) page says self-employed taxi and commercial ride-sharing drivers register and charge GST/HST even as small suppliers, from the day the passenger trips start. Food delivery does not use that rule by itself. The work guide is [rideshare driving](/side-hustles/rideshare-driving-side-hustle-canada/).

## Does side income go on Form T2125 or Form T776 in Canada?

Business and professional income goes on Form T2125. Real-estate rental income, when you rent space and provide only basic services such as heat, light, parking, and laundry, goes on Form T776. The CRA [rental or business](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/rental-income/you-have-rental-income-business-income.html) page says extra services such as cleaning, security, and meals can make the operation a business. The comparison is [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/).

## Is selling on Kijiji or Facebook Marketplace taxable in Canada?

It depends on what you sold. Personal belongings you owned to use are personal-use property. If both the cost and the proceeds are $1,000 or less, Schedule 3 says you do not report a gain or a loss. Buying goods to resell is business income on T2125, and the $1,000 rule does not apply. The split is [is selling on Kijiji taxable](/side-hustles/is-selling-on-kijiji-taxable-canada/). Platform reporting since 2024 does not replace your return. That system is in [tax on reselling and platform income](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).

## Are survey rewards taxable in Canada?

They are business income when you are carrying on a business of doing the surveys, including gift cards the CRA's incentive-income page tells a business to report. A one-off lottery-style prize is on the CRA's list of amounts that are not reported, unless it is income from employment, a business or property, or a prize for achievement. There is no small-amount pass for a real business. The nuance is [are rewards and bonuses taxable](/side-hustles/are-rewards-and-bonuses-taxable-canada/).

## Is credit-card cashback taxable in Canada?

The CRA's published loyalty-points policy is about employment. Points an employee collects are not a taxable benefit if the employer does not control them, they are not cashed out, and the plan is not another form of pay. Cashing them out is reportable by the employee. That page does not declare every personal grocery rebate to be income, and it does not declare it tax-free. If the reward reduces a business expense you are deducting, deduct the cost you actually bore. Read the rewards guide before you pick a label.

## Are bank sign-up bonuses taxable in Canada?

Not on one CRA sentence that covers every offer. Report a T5, T4A, or other slip if one arrives. Incentive income in a business is taxable. Most gifts are not reported, but a promotional credit for opening an account is not automatically a gift, especially when it is interest. Ask the institution how it characterizes the payment, and read [are rewards and bonuses taxable](/side-hustles/are-rewards-and-bonuses-taxable-canada/).

## Can I earn side income while I am on EI in Canada?

You must report the hours and the earnings, including self-employment, in the week the work was done. [Working While on Claim](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/ei/ei-list/working-while-claim.html) lets you keep 50 cents of EI benefits for every dollar you earn, up to 90 percent of your previous weekly earnings, then deducts dollar for dollar. A full week of work means no EI that week. Self-employment that is more than minor in extent can mean you are not unemployed and cannot be paid regular benefits. The detail is [side income while on EI](/side-hustles/side-income-on-ei-canada/). EI reports do not replace the tax return.

## Do I pay CPP on side hustle income in Canada?

Self-employed people outside Quebec pay both the employee and employer shares of the Canada Pension Plan on net business income, not on investment earnings. That is the [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) page. The basic exemption and the year's ceiling are in [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/). A hustle under the basic personal amount can still produce a CPP bill. Quebec uses the Quebec Pension Plan. The [Quebec side hustle rules](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-quebec-canada/) point at that return.

## How much tax should I set aside from a side hustle in Canada?

There is no single percentage that fits every province and every other dollar of income. The 2026 [TD1](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/cra-arc/formspubs/pbg/td1/td1-26e.pdf) basic personal amount for a resident is $16,452, and it can be lower at high incomes. It is a credit, not a reporting exemption. Provincial tax sits on top of federal tax. The labelled arithmetic, including CPP, is [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/). <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> can hold that amount in a separate account until you pay. The account does not calculate the rate.

## Which side hustle expenses can I deduct in Canada?

You can deduct the business part of reasonable current expenses you incur to earn the income, and you claim lasting equipment over time as capital cost allowance. Meals are only partly deductible. You cannot deduct the personal portion, a salary you pay yourself, or most personal living costs. The lines are in [side hustle expenses](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).

## Can I claim a home office for a side hustle in Canada?

You can claim the business-use portion of an eligible workspace if you meet the CRA's tests, and the claim cannot create or increase a loss. Mortgage principal is not an expense. Capital cost allowance on a home you live in can affect a later principal-residence claim, which is why many people claim operating costs only. The tests are in [home office expenses](/side-hustles/home-office-expenses-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do I claim a car used for delivery or rideshare in Canada?

You claim the business percentage of eligible vehicle costs, supported by a log of kilometres, not the whole car by default. Parking for the business and supplementary business insurance are the items the mileage guide treats as fully deductible. The log and the percentage are in [vehicle expenses for delivery and rideshare](/side-hustles/vehicle-expenses-delivery-rideshare-canada/) and [how to track mileage](/side-hustles/mileage-log-cra-side-hustle-canada/).

## How long do I keep side hustle records in Canada?

Generally six years from the end of the last tax year the records relate to. The CRA's [how long to keep records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/long-should-you-keep-your-income-tax-records.html) page is the starting point, and capital property can run longer because the clock follows the last year the asset can affect a return. The practical system is [record keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).

## When do CRA tax instalments start for a side hustle?

For 2026, the CRA [who has to pay](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/payments/payments-cra/individual-payments/income-tax-instalments/who-pays-instalments.html) page, as walked through in the instalments guide, requires instalments when net tax owing is more than $3,000 ($1,800 in Quebec) in 2026 and in either 2025 or 2024. The dates are March 15, June 15, September 15, and December 15. That $3,000 is not the GST/HST threshold. The guide is [CRA tax instalments](/side-hustles/cra-tax-instalments-side-hustle-canada/).

## If a platform reports my sales, is the tax already paid?

No. A platform report gives the CRA information. It does not withhold your income tax and it does not pay your CPP. You still file, and you still pay any balance by the payment deadline. The $2,800 goods-seller exclusion is about whether the platform reports you, not about whether the income is taxable. See [tax on reselling, gig apps, and platform income](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).
