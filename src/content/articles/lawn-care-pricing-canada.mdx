## How do you calculate a lawn care price?

Price the stop, not the lawn: time on site plus the drive, times your hourly rate, with a minimum. Biller's guide makes the same point, and suggests a $35 to $40 floor because unloading costs the same on any lawn.

```text
Hourly rate   = (target pay + CPP + equipment, fuel and insurance per hour) ÷ share of hours billable
Per cut       = (minutes on site + travel minutes) ÷ 60 × hourly rate
Price         = max(minimum stop, per cut), rounded to the nearest $5
Monthly plan  = per cut × cuts per month (4.33 weekly), minus 5% for autopay
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $35/hour (about 1.7 times the $20.75 Job Bank median) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $4.17 |
| Mower, trimmer, fuel, insurance per billable hour | $6.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 70% (driving and loading are unpaid) |
| **Rate** | ($35 + $4.17 + $6) ÷ 0.70 = **$64.53, round to $65** |

That sits inside Biller's $35 to $70 per on-site hour. A walking or cycling route with a push mower raises the billable share and lowers costs, so you can charge less and still hit the same pay.

## How should you write a lawn care quote and agreement?

Write the lot, the frequency, what's included, the rain policy, the season dates and payment terms. Keep pesticides off the menu unless you know the rules: Ontario's [pesticides page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticides-home-lawns-and-gardens) says you can't use certain products, including 2,4-D or glyphosate, to kill weeds and insects on lawns, and cosmetic use is limited to an allowable list.

```text
LAWN CARE AGREEMENT — 2026
Client: [name, address]   Lot: ~4,500 sq ft, front and back, gate on side
Service: weekly mow at 3 in., trim around obstacles, edge walks, blow off hard surfaces
Season: [May 4] to [Sept 28], about 22 visits
Price: $45 per cut, billed monthly ($185/month on autopay)
Rain: moved to next dry day; no charge for skipped weeks I cancel
Not included: fertilizer, weed control, leaf cleanup (quoted separately)
Cancellation: 14 days' notice
Prices before GST/HST
```

## How do you raise lawn care prices?

Raise prices before spring sign-ups, not mid-season, and lean on add-ons and minimums.

1. **Set a minimum stop.** Biller's guide suggests $35 to $40; below that a small lawn loses money.
2. **Charge more for bi-weekly and overgrown cuts.** Longer grass takes longer to cut and bag.
3. **Sell the season's extras.** Spring and fall cleanups, aeration and hedges are priced from $85 to $389 on Ottawa Lawn Maintenance's list.
4. **Offer a monthly plan.** Ottawa Lawn Maintenance's $189 Standard Care plan bundles mowing with fertilization and cleanups; a bundle hides the per-cut price.
5. **Densify the route.** Raise prices for far-off clients first; a neighbour of an existing client can get your standard rate.

More scripts are in the [guide to raising side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What lawn care pricing mistakes are common?

- **No minimum.** A 10-minute lawn priced at $20 with a 15-minute drive is $20 for 25 minutes before fuel. A $35 minimum makes the stop worth the drive.
- **Same price weekly and bi-weekly.** Bi-weekly grass is longer.
- **Ignoring the drive.** Biller: "Price the stop, not the lawn."
- **Underpricing cleanups.** They take hours, and Ottawa's starting price for two fall visits is $325.
- **Selling weed control without checking provincial rules.**

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect lawn care prices?

Lawn care income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution outside Quebec. Register for GST/HST once your taxable sales from all businesses, lawn and snow together, pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). Then add the rate where you work ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)): a $45 cut becomes $50.85 with Ontario's 13% HST or $47.25 with Alberta's 5% GST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

See [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) and the [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
