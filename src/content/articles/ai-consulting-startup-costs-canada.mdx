## How much does AI consulting cost to start in Canada?

AI consulting in Canada costs $39.55 for a headset on free AI tiers, $546.00 adding a webcam and a year of Claude Pro and Google Workspace, $796.00 with a basic professional liability policy, or $1,557.15 with a year of FreshBooks, Zapier Professional and a laptop stand. Goods and software include 13% Ontario HST. Insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate. None of these include the laptop.

## Which subscriptions should a new AI consultant pay for?

Pay for one tool you can explain. If you can't say what the paid tier adds to the client's task, stay on the free tier.

**Your AI plan.** Claude's Pro tier was listed at $20 a month billed monthly, or $17 a month on an annual subscription billed as $200 up front, on October 9, 2026. Max plans start at $100 a month. Usage limits apply on every plan. Other chat tools have their own paid tiers; compare the free version first and pick the one the client already uses when you can.

**The client's tools, on the client's account.** When you set up an automation or a shared workspace for a client, the subscription belongs on their card, in their name. That keeps the data, the login and the cost with the business that owns it, and it means a cancelled engagement doesn't leave you paying for their Zapier. Zapier's [pricing page](https://zapier.com/pricing) listed the free plan at 100 tasks a month and Professional at US$19.99 a month billed annually or US$29.99 monthly, for 750 tasks, on October 9, 2026. The pro kit includes a year for your own demos and internal work.

**Business email.** A matching domain address helps a cold email land. It isn't required for the first client found through someone you know.

## Do you need insurance or a licence for AI consulting in Canada?

**Insurance.** AI consulting is advice. If an owner follows your written steps and an automated email goes to the wrong list, the claim is about your advice, which is what professional liability (errors and omissions) covers. Zensurance's [professional liability page](https://www.zensurance.com/professional-liability-insurance) said on October 9, 2026 that a basic policy with a $100,000 limit costs approximately $250 a year, and that the most common coverage business owners and consultants choose is $1 million per claim and $2 million in total, which costs more. Factors include industry, services and years of experience. That's an estimate, not a quote. Some clients will ask for a certificate before they sign. Compare options in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/). This is not insurance or legal advice.

**Privacy.** [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) covers personal information handled in commercial activity in most provinces. The OPC's [generative AI principles](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/gd_principles_ai/) say to know your legal authority for using personal information and to limit collection, use and disclosure to what the purpose needs. A client list pasted into a personal chat account is the risk to plan around, not a shortcut.

**Advertising claims.** A promise to cut a client's costs by a set percentage can fall under the Competition Bureau's guidance on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations). Describe the task, not a result you can't support.

**Business licence.** There's no AI consulting licence. Some cities licence home-based businesses. Search your city's bylaws, and register a business name with your province if you trade under one.

## Where can AI consulting startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Use free tiers** until a client task needs a feature you can name.
- **Pay monthly first,** and switch to annual only after a few months of steady use. Annual Claude Pro saves $3 a month but is $200 up front.
- **Put client tools on client accounts.** You shouldn't be the one paying for their automation.
- **Skip the webcam** until workshops run by video.
- **Skip paid AI certificates** a small business owner won't ask about.

## Where should AI consulting spending not be cut in Canada?

Don't cut the time to check outputs. An output you didn't read becomes the client's error, and your claim.

Don't skip the written privacy boundary or a scope that says what the tool can't do. Don't skip insurance once you're giving written advice to a business.

Keep project fees apart from household money. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can hold a fee until the tax set-aside and next month's subscriptions are covered.

## Which tax rules apply to an AI consulting budget in Canada?

AI consulting income in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on the activity for profit, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. Subscriptions, insurance and a headset are generally current expenses. A computer or other equipment that lasts for years may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep signed scopes, invoices and subscription receipts. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. The pillar guide's high scenario crosses $30,000 in a year.

If you are registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) to the extent of commercial use, with receipts that show the tax. If you are not registered, you do not charge GST/HST and you do not claim the credit.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). For a set-aside habit, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle in Canada](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
