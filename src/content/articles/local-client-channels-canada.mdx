- **Kijiji Services ads are not free.** Kijiji's pricing overview lists a free listing limit of 0 in Services, so a posting fee applies for each 31-day period. The dollar amount is on Kijiji's current fee screen.
- **Nextdoor limits a personal account to one promotional post a week** in the main feed. Business pages and neighbour-services listings are the other tools. Canada has an English business site.

- **None of the three replaces a review from a real job.** After the first clients, the Google review steps are a separate guide.

Nextdoor, Facebook Groups, and Kijiji are three places Canadians look for a local side hustle client. They are not interchangeable. Kijiji is a classified ad you pay for in Services and then reply to yourself. Nextdoor is a neighbourhood feed with a written limit on how often a personal account may promote a service. A Facebook Group is whatever its admins allow. This comparison suits someone who will pick one channel, post one clear offer, and answer the reply. It does not suit someone who wants to paste the same ad into every group in the city.

How to write the Kijiji ad, screen replies, and avoid duplicate listings is [getting clients on Kijiji](/side-hustles/get-clients-on-kijiji-canada/). Handyman-specific tradeoffs, including TaskRabbit, are [TaskRabbit vs Kijiji vs Facebook](/side-hustles/taskrabbit-vs-kijiji-facebook-handyman-canada/). Fitting more than one channel into a week is [a client acquisition system](/side-hustles/side-hustle-client-acquisition-system-canada/).

## How do Nextdoor, Facebook Groups and Kijiji differ for side hustle clients in Canada?

They differ on who sees the post, what it costs, and who is allowed to say you do the work. Every line below is from the platform's own page or, for Facebook Groups, from the fact that each group sets its own rules. Confirm the live help article before you post. Neighbourhood coverage is not national: if Nextdoor has no neighbours on your street, the fee comparison does not matter.

| Check | Nextdoor | Facebook Groups | Kijiji |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What you post | A neighbour offer, or a business page | A post the group allows | One Services ad |
| Price | Personal promotional posts are described as a weekly limit, not a fee. Business tools can be paid. Confirm in the app | No national Canadian insertion fee this guide can cite | Posting fee in Services. Free listing limit for private sellers is 0 |
| How long it lasts | A feed post, not a 31-day classified | Until the admin or the feed moves on | Fee-based listings stay active 31 days |
| Proof | Recommendations neighbours write on a business page | Comments, if the group allows them | Your photos and your replies |
| First-week fit | One offer to people near the address | One group whose rules allow the service | One paid ad in the right Services category |

Sources: [Nextdoor promoting a business](https://nextdoorcrm.my.site.com/s/article/Promoting-a-business-or-service-on-Nextdoor?language=en_CA), [Kijiji listing fees](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141), [how long Kijiji listings stay active](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/how-long-kijiji-listings-stay-active/160).

## What does Nextdoor allow for a side hustle in Canada?

Nextdoor allows a limited promotional post from a personal account, and it points ongoing businesses to its business tools. The [Canada-language help article](https://nextdoorcrm.my.site.com/s/article/Promoting-a-business-or-service-on-Nextdoor?language=en_CA) says personal accounts may post up to one promotional post per week in the main feed. Posts over that limit, or posts Nextdoor treats as spam, are not permitted. It points neighbours who offer a casual service, such as dog walking or babysitting, to the neighbour Made and neighbour Services categories in For Sale & Free. It also says you can claim a business page, where customers can leave recommendations, or create a group people join on purpose.

Nextdoor's [English Canada business site](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-ca/small-business/product/business-posts) describes business posts to nearby neighbourhoods. It also says casual services may need business documents to verify a business page before posting to the newsfeed, and that without those documents you promote from a member account using the neighbour categories. Read that page for your own service before you assume a business page is open.

Recommendations are neighbours writing about a business, including a "Fave" and a short note, or an @mention. Nextdoor's [recommendations article](https://nextdoorcrm.my.site.com/s/article/About-Recommendations) says the business can see and reply. That is not a Google review. Do not ask for one by offering a discount. The Google rules are in [how to get your first Google reviews](/side-hustles/first-google-reviews-side-hustle-canada/).

## What do Facebook Groups allow for a side hustle in Canada?

Facebook Groups allow what the admins wrote in that group's rules. Many neighbourhood groups limit selling to a weekly thread or ban it. This guide will not invent a national limit Meta did not print for every Canadian group. Read the pinned post. If the group says no promotion, do not post the offer there.

A group you join only to paste an ad is how people get removed and how the neighbours stop reading. Introduce the service where the rules allow it, with the area you cover and a way to reply. Selling items, which is a different task from selling a service, has its own listing habits in [Facebook Marketplace listing tips](/side-hustles/facebook-marketplace-listing-tips-canada/).

Do not export the member list and email it. [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies to commercial electronic messages. ISED's [consent page](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) covers express and implied consent, identification, and an unsubscribe path. A reply to someone who asked you for a quote is a different fact from a blast. This is not legal advice.

## What does Kijiji cost for a service ad in Canada?

Kijiji costs a posting fee for a Services ad, including for a private seller. The [pricing overview](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141) lists a free listing limit of 0 in all Services categories, and it says fees are charged per listing for each 31-day period. This guide does not restate a dollar amount the overview does not print in a single national cell. The fee you see in the posting flow is the one to budget. [Listing length](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/how-long-kijiji-listings-stay-active/160) says fee-based listings stay active 31 days, and reposting may require a new fee.

One ad, real photos, a service area, and a fast reply are the rest of the job. The sentences, the duplicate-ad rule, and the scam screen are in [getting clients on Kijiji](/side-hustles/get-clients-on-kijiji-canada/). Do not post the same service in three cities. Kijiji's duplicate rule is in that guide.

## Which channel should you use first for local clients in Canada?

Use the channel where your customers already look, and where you can post this week without breaking a written rule.

| If you are… | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offering a casual service on your own street | Nextdoor neighbour services, one promotional post in the week | The help article is written for that offer, with a frequency cap |
| Ready to pay for a classified that sits for 31 days | Kijiji Services | The ad stays up. You still have to answer it |
| Already in a local group that allows a weekly offer | That Facebook Group, on the day it allows | The admin's rule is the constraint |
| Comparing with an app that sends the job | TaskRabbit, after this table | [TaskRabbit vs Kijiji vs Facebook](/side-hustles/taskrabbit-vs-kijiji-facebook-handyman-canada/) is the app column |

Run one channel until you have a reply you can quote. A second channel is how the first reply sits unanswered. After a few paid jobs, ask every customer for a Google review the lawful way, and keep a simple tracker. The acquisition system guide is the tracker. The review guide is the ask.

## What mistakes do people make looking for local clients in Canada?

People paste one ad into every Facebook Group, post a second Nextdoor promotion the same week, and let a Kijiji ad lapse in the busy month. They also collect phone numbers from a group and email the list. They promise a price in the ad that they have not written down as a quote. And they ask for reviews before they have done the job.

Safety is the same on all three: meet in a way that fits the work, do not refund an overpayment, and do not pay a "client" to get the job. Overpayment patterns are in [side hustle scams](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply to clients from Nextdoor, Facebook or Kijiji in Canada?

Money from local clients is business income if you are carrying on the activity, reported on Form T2125, whether the introduction was a feed post or a classified ad. The platform does not pay you, so it also does not withhold tax. GST/HST follows the $30,000 small-supplier test across your side hustles. The filing map is [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/) and the [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/). Whether a Kijiji sale of used personal goods is a different question from a service you repeat is [is selling on Kijiji taxable](/side-hustles/is-selling-on-kijiji-taxable-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. Recheck Nextdoor's promotion article and Kijiji's fee overview when you post. Group rules change when an admin changes them.
