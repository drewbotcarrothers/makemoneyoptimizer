## What offer gets a first car detailing client in Canada?

A first car detailing client in Canada usually buys one interior, or one exterior on a driveway you have already checked, with a vehicle size and a time window. "Full detail" is hard to purchase. "Interior of a sedan, mats out, one visit, about two and a half hours" is a purchase.

Say what is outside the first job. Paint correction, ceramic coatings, engine bays, and a second vehicle "while you are here" are later quotes. Pet hair and smoke are lines on the note, not surprises at the end. The first yes is easier when the no-list is visible.

If the hose would freeze, sell the interior. If the only drain is a catch basin, sell the interior. Toronto's page says wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach a catch basin. Your city may use different words. Read it before you promise a rinse.

## Where do Canadians find a first car detailing client?

Canadians find a first car detailing client among people who have seen them finish something, then among neighbours whose cars they can already describe. A purchased email list is a weak and often unlawful start.

Use three paths, and use the first one before the third.

People who already know you. A colleague, a family car, a neighbour who asked. Name the price and the time window. Do not add a free second vehicle to fill the silence.

A driveway you can point at. You have looked at where the water would go, and you can say interior-only if the answer is a grate. You are offering a package, not a slogan.

A marketplace or a local listing, if you use one. This guide does not state a platform percentage. Read that site's own page before you rely on a cut you heard second-hand. Put the same package and the same photos on the profile.

If the note is a commercial electronic message, [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies. ISED's page on [getting consent to send email](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) says businesses need consent before commercial emails or texts. Consent is express, or implied in limited cases such as an existing business relationship. That page says implied consent from a relationship may last up to two years, or six months after an inquiry. A commercial message must include your name, a current mailing address, and a phone number, email, or website that stays valid for at least 60 days, plus an unsubscribe mechanism. Unsubscribe requests must be actioned within 10 business days, at no cost. A conversation on a driveway you were invited onto is a different posture from a bought list. This is not legal advice.

## What should a first car detailing note say?

A first car detailing note in Canada should name the package, the vehicle size, the time window, the price, and where the water will go if there is a rinse. Three to six sentences are enough. A generic "I detail cars" note gives the reader nothing to accept.

**Example message.** This is a generic example you can adapt. It is not a note that produced a client, and it is not a claim that any neighbour replied.

> Hello. I am booking one interior detail for a sedan: mats out, vacuum, wipe-down, about two and a half hours, at a price we write down before I start. I do not do paint correction or engine bays. If you want an exterior rinse, I need a place for the water that your city's rules allow. I can send two photos of my own car, labelled as practice. Would Tuesday after 4 p.m. work, or is another day better?

Keep a list of who received a commercial message, and honour a no. Do not discount the package to fill a silent week.

## How do you prove car detailing skill without fake reviews?

You prove car detailing skill in Canada by detailing your own car, timing it, and showing the before photo of damage that was already there. A fake client logo, an invented testimonial, or a photo of someone else's work is a misrepresentation, not a portfolio.

Write "Practice on my own car, not a client" on the photo if nobody hired you. When a real client later allows you to show their car, put that permission in the note. Payment alone is a poor substitute for a sentence about photos.

The timing is the proof that matters. If the interior took four hours on your own sedan, do not tell a stranger it will take ninety minutes.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first car detailing client look like?

A 14-day plan for a first car detailing client in Canada should produce a package, a timed practice car, a wash-water answer, and a short list of real offers. A paid yes is a bonus. Silence in two weeks is a reason to tighten the photos, not a reason to buy a van.

### Days 1 and 2: Fix the offer

Write interior or exterior in one sentence. Read your city's outdoor-washing page. List what you will not do: paint correction, coatings, engine bays, and electrical.

### Days 3 to 5: Practise and photograph

Detail your own car and time it. Photograph the scratch that was already there. Price two vehicle sizes on paper. Ask for a liability quote, and ask whether a home policy covers the business. This is not insurance advice.

### Days 6 to 9: Offer it to people who know you

Talk to five people. If you email, follow ISED's consent page. Book one time or none. Do not discount the package to fill the silence. Confirm water, parking, and the pet-hair line by text if someone says yes.

### Days 10 to 14: Run the visit or improve the package

If someone booked, arrive when you said, stop at the package, and invoice the same day. If nobody booked, tighten the photos and offer the same package to people you have not already asked. Do not add a coating to escape the silence.

## What mistakes block a first car detailing client in Canada?

Common mistakes that block a first car detailing client in Canada are rinsing into a catch basin, promising a coating you have not trained for, and pricing every vehicle as a small sedan. One package is enough until someone pays for a second.

A listing with no photo of your own work asks the client to imagine you. A free second car turns a defined visit into an open afternoon. Scraping addresses, or emailing a purchased list, skips the consent rules on the anti-spam pages above.

Working for "exposure" with no cap is a gift. Call it a gift if you choose it, and cap the vehicle. Do not describe it as a client.

## Which tax rules apply to a first car detailing client in Canada?

Car detailing income in Canada is self-employment income, including a deposit, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the note, the before photo, and the e-transfer. Report detailing on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add cleaning and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. One interior does not by itself cross that line. Car detailing is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the work happens. Confirm it on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside. If you drive, keep a kilometre log. Search your city for a business licence. This guide does not state a fee.
