## What are the best side hustles for graphic designers in Canada?

The best side hustles for graphic designers in Canada mix client work, which pays sooner, with products, which take longer to pay off but don't need you there for each sale.

### Freelance design clients

Logos, social graphics, and print materials for small businesses are the core. Start with [graphic design freelancing](/side-hustles/graphic-design-side-hustle-canada/), and compare marketplaces in [Fiverr vs Upwork](/side-hustles/fiverr-vs-upwork-canada/). Avoid pitching your employer's clients.

### Products you design once

[Print-on-demand](/side-hustles/print-on-demand-side-hustle-canada/) puts your designs on shirts and mugs without inventory, and [Etsy shops](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-side-hustle-canada/) are a common storefront. [Printables](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/), [Notion templates](/side-hustles/notion-templates-side-hustle-canada/), and [Lightroom presets](/side-hustles/lightroom-presets-side-hustle-canada/) are digital and ship instantly. A marketplace like <AffiliateLink program="etsy" label="Etsy" /> or a tool like <AffiliateLink program="canva" label="Canva" /> can get you started.

### Social media and content design

Small businesses often want ongoing graphics. [Social media management](/side-hustles/social-media-manager-side-hustle-canada/) and [faceless Instagram pages](/side-hustles/faceless-instagram-side-hustle-canada/) use the same skills, and [stock photography](/side-hustles/stock-photography-side-hustle-canada/) suits designers who also shoot.

### Simple websites

Designers who can lay out a page can learn [no-code web design](/side-hustles/no-code-web-design-side-hustle-canada/) and offer [website maintenance](/side-hustles/website-maintenance-side-hustle-canada/) as a monthly service.

## What copyright and employer rules apply to designers' side work?

Two areas matter:

- **Who owns the work.** Section 13(3) of the [Copyright Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/section-13.html) says that where an author is employed under a contract of service and makes a work in the course of that employment, the employer is the first owner of the copyright unless there's an agreement to the contrary. Design your side products on your own time and equipment. Don't reuse assets, templates, or fonts licensed to your employer.
- **Your employment contract.** Contracts can include moonlighting, non-solicitation, or IP-assignment clauses that go further than the default. Public-sector policies are similar in spirit: the federal [conflict-of-interest FAQ](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/values-ethics/conflict-interest-post-employment/frequently-asked-questions.html) says outside work should be on your own time, not use government property, and not use non-public information from your job.

For freelance clients, set out in your contract who owns the final files and when ownership transfers.

## How should designers price side work?

Price by project with a set number of revisions, and price products by comparing similar listings. The [design pricing guide](/side-hustles/graphic-design-pricing-canada/) covers both.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not market rates: a designer quotes an assumed $600 logo package with two revision rounds and spends an assumed 10 hours on it.

- Effective hourly rate: $600 ÷ 10 = $60
- If a third revision round adds 3 unpaid hours: $600 ÷ 13 ≈ $46.15

Capping revisions in the quote protects the rate.

## What mistakes do designers make with side hustles?

- **Using employer assets or licences.** Fonts, stock, and templates are often licensed only for work use.
- **Pitching your employer's clients.** Check non-solicitation clauses.
- **Unlimited revisions.** Set a number in writing.
- **Unclear file ownership.** Say who owns the final files and when.
- **Copying trademarks on print-on-demand.** Platforms can remove infringing designs.

## How is a design side hustle taxed in Canada?

Report it on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Software subscriptions, fonts, and a share of equipment can be deductible; see [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/) and [home office expenses](/side-hustles/home-office-expenses-side-hustle-canada/). Register for GST/HST once you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Read [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
