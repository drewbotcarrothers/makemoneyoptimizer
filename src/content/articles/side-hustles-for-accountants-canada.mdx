## What licensing and CRA rules apply to accounting side hustles?

Three rules matter most:

- **Public accounting needs a licence.** In Ontario, [CPA Ontario](https://www.cpaontario.ca/members/regulations-guidance/regulatory-publications/when-is-pal-required-in-ontario) says a Public Accounting Licence (PAL) is required if you're the lead engagement person signing reports for any assurance engagement on financial statements, or for a compilation engagement a third party can reasonably be expected to rely on where the prescribed compilation report wording isn't used. Practising public accounting without a PAL is prohibited under Ontario's Public Accounting Act, 2004. Other provinces have their own rules. Bookkeeping and personal tax returns generally aren't assurance work, but check your province before offering financial statements.
- **Paid tax preparers must e-file.** The CRA says that since January 1, 2024, tax preparers who accept payment to prepare more than 5 T1, T2 or T3 returns a year must file them electronically, with penalties of $25 for each paper-filed T1 or T3 and $100 for each T2 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/digital-services-tax-preparers/mandatory-electronic-filing-tax-preparers.html)). Registered preparers use [EFILE](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/e-services-businesses/efile-electronic-filers.html), which needs yearly renewal.
- **Professional titles.** Only CPA members can call themselves CPAs. If you're not a member, describe your work as bookkeeping and don't imply a designation.

If you work at a firm, read its policy on outside clients. Firms may restrict side work that competes with them.

## How should accountants price side work?

Price monthly bookkeeping as a fixed fee per month based on transaction volume, and tax returns per return. The [bookkeeping pricing guide](/side-hustles/freelance-bookkeeping-pricing-canada/) explains the models.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not market rates: a bookkeeper takes 4 small-business clients at an assumed $250 a month each, and spends an assumed 4 hours per client each month.

- Monthly revenue: 4 × $250 = $1,000
- Monthly hours: 4 × 4 = 16
- Effective hourly rate: $1,000 ÷ 16 = $62.50 before software and tax

## What mistakes do accountants make with side hustles?

- **Signing anything that looks like assurance.** Know where the licensing line is in your province.
- **Paper-filing client returns.** Once you're paid for more than 5, you must e-file.
- **Competing with your employer.** Check your firm's policy first.
- **Taking on tax season without capacity.** April deadlines stack up.
- **Skipping engagement letters.** Put scope and fees in writing.

## How is an accounting side hustle taxed in Canada?

Report it on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Bookkeeping and tax prep are taxable services, so the $30,000 GST/HST small-supplier test applies ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)); see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
