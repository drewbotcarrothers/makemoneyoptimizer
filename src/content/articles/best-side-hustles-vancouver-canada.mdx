## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Vancouver, Canada?

Start with the rules that create work, then add a service you already know.

### Step 1: Drive or deliver under the B.C. app-worker floor

B.C.'s [minimum wage page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage) sets a minimum for ride-hail and delivery app workers. From June 1, 2026, it's $21.89 per hour of engaged time. Delivery work adds $0.35 per kilometre and ride-hail adds $0.45. Workers keep 100% of tips. Engaged time runs from accepting an offer to completing it, so waiting between orders isn't paid. The [food delivery pricing guide](/side-hustles/food-delivery-pricing-canada/) explains how to judge an offer, and [Uber vs Lyft for drivers](/side-hustles/uber-vs-lyft-drivers-canada/) compares the ride-hail apps.

### Step 2: Walk dogs with the right park permit

If you walk clients' dogs in regional parks, Metro Vancouver requires a [commercial use permit for dog walkers](https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/commercial-use-dog-walking-permit). A first application costs $200 plus GST, the permit is $250 per walker plus GST, and you need proof of $5 million in general liability insurance. City parks and other municipalities set their own rules, so check before you build a route. The [dog walking guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) and [Rover vs independent dog walking](/side-hustles/rover-vs-independent-dog-walking-canada/) help you choose an app or your own clients.

### Step 3: Be the on-call snow shoveller

Vancouver gets less snow than prairie cities, but the deadline is strict. The [City's winter page](https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/snow-removal-from-city-streets.aspx) says owners and occupants who don't clear snow and ice by 10 a.m. the day after snowfall may get a $250 fine. If it stays more than 24 hours, the fine can be $750. That suits a short list of clients who pay per visit, such as landlords and owners who travel. Keep the [snow removal guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) for gear and [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) for per-visit rates.

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Vancouver, Canada?

Yes, if you run the business from your home in Vancouver. The City requires a [home-based business licence](https://vancouver.ca/doing-business/home-based-business.aspx). Its [FAQ](https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/home-based-business-web-faq.pdf) says you must live where the business is, customers come by appointment only, and no more than 3 customers or students may visit at one time. Business names are a separate provincial step, covered in [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you deliver on weekday evenings and walk two neighbours' dogs at lunch. You'd first get a City licence, because the business is run from home. You'd log engaged time from each delivery app to check that pay meets the B.C. floor. If the dogs walk in a regional park, you'd need the Metro Vancouver permit and its insurance before the first walk. Keep the walking schedule to streets and city parks if the permit isn't in place yet.

## What mistakes do Vancouver side hustlers make?

- **Skipping the City licence.** A home-based hustle still needs one in Vancouver.
- **Walking in regional parks without a permit.** Metro Vancouver requires it for commercial walkers.
- **Counting waiting time as paid.** B.C.'s minimum applies to engaged time only.
- **Promising snow clearing you can't reach by 10 a.m.** The fine lands on your client, so keep the list short.

## How are Vancouver side hustles taxed?

You report self-employment income on Form T2125. You register for 5% GST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Ride-hail drivers register from their first fare. B.C.'s 7% PST is separate and covers taxable goods, software and some services. [Bulletin PST 003](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/taxes/sales-taxes/publications/pst-003-small-sellers.pdf) exempts small sellers with $10,000 or less in eligible sales. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/).
