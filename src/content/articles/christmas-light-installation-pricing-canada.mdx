## Where should a side hustle price itself?

Price install-only work near The Light Elves' $2 a foot reinstall rate, with a minimum of $200 to $250, and price full packages with your own lights under the $1,100 Toronto starting packages. Companies charge more for two-person crews, custom-cut commercial strands, warranties and storage.

## What formula should you use?

Measure the roofline, multiply by a per-foot rate, apply a minimum, then add takedown and any lights you supply. Peaks and two-storey runs take longer, so price them higher per foot.

```text
Install      = max(minimum, lit feet × install rate per foot)
Peaks        = peak feet × (install rate + premium)   [steeper, slower]
Takedown     = 40% × install
Lights       = lit feet × lights price per foot       [only if you supply them]
Extras       = trees (from a photo), $ per extension cord, timer
Year two     = install + takedown (lights already owned)
```

**Measuring.** Count feet along eaves and gables, plus each peak. The Light Elves estimates from Google Street View and photos and works to 90% to 110% of the client's figure. A <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="measuring wheel" search="measuring wheel distance" /> on the ground gives you eave length; add peaks by counting siding rows or from photos.

**Materials if you supply them (Amazon.ca, October 9, 2026):** Minetom C9 50 ft warm white, $93.99 ($1.88 a foot); Omni all-in-one clips, 100 for $29.99 (30¢ each). ## What does a worked Christmas light quote look like?

**Illustrative.** The feet, times and side-hustle rates are assumptions you replace with your own. The material prices and market anchors are published.

### Quote 1: bungalow, client's own lights

- 120 feet of eaves, no peaks. Install: 120 × $2.00 = $240 (above a $200 minimum).
- Takedown: 40% × $240 = $96.
- **Quote: $240 install + $96 takedown = $336 before tax.** Time on the house, assumed 2.5 hours to install and 1.5 to take down, gives $336 ÷ 4 = $84 an hour.
- Market check: The Light Elves would charge its $250 minimum plus 40% of $240 for takedown, $346.

### Quote 2: two-storey home, you supply C9 lights

- 140 feet of eaves at $2.50/ft (two-storey) = $350. Two 12-foot peaks: 24 × $3.00 = $72. Install total: $422.
- Lights: 164 lit feet. Your cost: four 50 ft Minetom strings = 4 × $93.99 = $375.96, plus 200 clips (two 100-packs) = $59.98, so $435.94 for 200 feet of light. Charge 164 × $3.50 = $574, and keep the leftover 36 feet for repairs.
- Takedown: 40% × $422 = $168.80.
- **Year one: $422 + $574 + $168.80 = $1,164.80 before tax.** That's in line with Shack Shine's "from $1,100" in Toronto and Shine City's $650 to $1,200 first year.
- **Year two: $422 + $168.80 = $590.80**, or 51% of year one, in the 45% to 55% range Shack Shine and LawnSavers use.

### Quote 3: front tree and shrubs

- Photo shows a 10-foot spruce and four shrubs. Assumed 1.5 hours at $70 an hour = $105, plus client's lights. Takedown: $42 (40%).
- **Quote: $105 + $42 = $147.** Trees taller than your ladder's safe reach go to a crew with a boom truck.

## How do you raise Christmas light prices?

The published lists show four levers: early-bird pricing, returning-client terms, prepaid takedown and supplying lights.

1. **Early-bird vs peak.** The Light Elves runs early-bird installs from mid-September to the end of October, prices peak season (November to mid-December) higher, and closes early-bird access on Thanksgiving Monday. Charge 10% to 15% more for November bookings.
2. **Returning clients pay less, but commit.** The Light Elves cuts the deposit from 50% to 25% for returning clients. Year-two pricing at 45% to 55% of year one keeps them, and your margin rises because the lights are already bought.
3. **Prepay the takedown.** The Light Elves says most regular clients prepay takedown. It saves you chasing a January invoice.
4. **Sell the lights.** Supplying custom-cut strings the client keeps turns a $336 install into a $1,000-plus first year and locks in year two.

A renewal note to send in September:

```text
Hi [Name], booking 2026 holiday lights now.
Same lights as last year: install $[422] + takedown $[169] = $[591]
Book by October 31 for early-bird install dates (Oct 20–Nov 8).
November installs: $[650].
Deposit: 25% for returning clients. Takedown in January, prepaid or invoiced.
Reply with a date that works. — [Your name, phone]
```

## What should a Christmas light quote and invoice include?

Write the lit feet, peaks, who owns the lights, the install window, the takedown month and the deposit. The Electrical Safety Authority says to use clips, not staples or nails, to attach outdoor lights ([ESA holiday safety](https://esasafe.com/safety/home-electrical-safety/holiday-safety/)), and that's worth stating on the quote.

Attachment: plastic clips only, no staples or nails

## What pricing mistakes are common?

- **No takedown line.** January takedown is a second visit in worse footing. The Light Elves prices it at 40% of install, on its own line.
- **No minimum.** A 40-foot porch at $2 a foot is $80 for a ladder setup and travel. Use a $200 to $250 minimum.
- **Underpricing two-storey runs and peaks.** They take longer and carry more risk, so price them higher per foot.
- **Supplying lights at cost.** The Light Elves charges $5 a foot for its custom-built lights. A markup over your cost covers cutting, testing and replacements.
- **Double-booking with snow.** If you also clear snow on the same street, check the [snow removal pricing guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) so a storm morning doesn't collide with a takedown.

Ladder work is the main risk. The Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety covers [choosing and using portable ladders](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html), and Health Canada's [holiday safety tips](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/home-safety/tips-holiday-safety.html) cover lights rated for outdoor use. Price jobs you can do safely from a ladder and pass on the rest.

## Which tax rules apply to Christmas light prices?

Christmas light income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution outside Quebec. Register for GST/HST once your taxable sales from all businesses pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)), then add the rate where the work happens ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)): the $1,164.80 first-year quote becomes $1,316.22 with Ontario's 13% HST. Lights you sell are part of the taxable sale once you're registered.
