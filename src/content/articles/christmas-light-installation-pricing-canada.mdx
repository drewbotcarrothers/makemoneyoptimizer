## What should a Christmas light price in Canada include?

A Christmas light price in Canada should name the surfaces, the length of the run, who supplies the strings, the install window, and the take-down date. A single number for "the front of the house" hides whether the job is a bungalow trough or a two-storey gable.

| Factor | What changes the price |
| --- | --- |
| Storeys and ladder moves | More climbs, more time, more reason to decline |
| Length of the run | Clips and minutes scale with metres |
| Who supplies lights | Your receipts, or a box the client already owns |
| Outlet and GFCI | A long cord run takes time. A missing outlet is not your cue to rewire |
| Roof, peak, or power line | Often a refusal, not a premium |
| Take-down | A second visit on colder footing |
| Travel | A far house is not the same hour as the neighbour |

The [Canadian Centre for Occupational Health and Safety](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) says not to use an aluminum ladder near electricity and not to climb in high wind. Those are reasons to reschedule, and the price should allow that. Ontario's [Electrical Safety Authority](https://esasafe.com/safety/home-electrical-safety/holiday-safety/) says to stay at least 3 metres from overhead power lines and to use clips rather than staples or nails. Health Canada says outdoor lights need a Canadian certification mark. If the strings fail that check, the job is not a discount. It is a decline.

## Which pricing models work for Christmas lights in Canada?

Christmas light installation in Canada is usually sold as one package that includes install and take-down, with a higher line when you supply the strings. Per-metre pricing shows the length. Hourly pricing suits a house you have not measured.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Install + take-down package | Two visits, one written price, two lines | Most houses | Forgetting the January visit in the number |
| Per linear metre | Price times the measured run | Comparing two fronts on the same street | A peak or a tree that is not a straight metre |
| Hourly with an estimate | Your rate times timed hours | Unusual houses | A client who wants one number before you visit |
| Client's lights | You hang and remove what they own | Lower material risk | Indoor strings, recalls, and broken sockets |
| Your lights | You supply outdoor-rated strings | Clients who own nothing | Who keeps the lights in January |

Do not copy a fee from a United States video or from another city's flyer. A Prairie two-storey and a coastal bungalow are not the same afternoon.

## How do you calculate a Christmas light price in Canada?

Calculate a Christmas light price in Canada from your own hourly target, the minutes you will actually spend, materials you will buy, and a separate take-down. Then compare the result with what you can finish before dark.

### Step 1: Set an hourly target you can replace

There is no Job Bank page for holiday-light installers. Employed wage tables for other trades are not this job. Pick a target for time on the property that still makes sense after travel, and write it down so the quote is arithmetic rather than a mood.

### Step 2: Measure the run and refuse what you will not climb

Walk the house in daylight. Count metres of trough, railing, or shrubs you will actually clip. Mark the roof, the icy deck, and anything inside the power-line distance as out of scope. CCOHS ladder guidance is the stop rule, not a line item you can upcharge.

### Step 3: Time the install, then time the take-down

Take-down is not free because the clips are already up. January footing is often worse. If you also clear snow on that street, check the [snow removal pricing guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) so a storm morning is not double-booked with a take-down you already promised.

### Step 4: Add only the materials on this job

Clips, a timer, a cord, and strings if you supply them. Shelf ranges to verify locally are in the [startup cost guide](/side-hustles/christmas-light-installation-startup-costs-canada/). Do not bury a whole season of inventory inside one bungalow.

### Step 5: Hold back tax, then round

Self-employed installers report net income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) and pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution. Add GST/HST only after you register.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, a survey, a recommended fee, or a typical invoice in any city.

Suppose you choose $45 an hour as your own target for time on the property. That target is a number you replace. Suppose a front you are willing to do measures 24 metres of eavestrough, and you time the ladder moves, clips, plug-in, and test at 8 minutes per metre. That is 24 × 8 = 192 minutes, or 3.2 hours. Labour in the sketch is 3.2 × $45 = $144.

Suppose you supply clips at an assumed $20 and the client supplies outdoor-rated strings you have checked. The install line is $144 + $20 = $164 before tax.

Suppose take-down takes half as long, 1.6 hours. Labour is 1.6 × $45 = $72. The two-visit sketch is $164 + $72 = $236 before tax. Travel, insurance, and a wind delay are not in these lines.

Suppose you also supply eight strings at an assumed $25 each, or $200. The two-visit total becomes $236 + $200 = $436 before tax, and the agreement says who keeps the lights. Dividing the install line by the measured run is $164 ÷ 24 = about $6.83 per metre before take-down. A flat "front of the house" price hides that length. Do not copy $164, $236, $436, or $6.83.

## When should you raise Christmas light prices in Canada?

Raise Christmas light prices in Canada on the next quote when a house ran long, when a street is full, or when materials cost more than the line you used. This season is short. You usually cannot reopen a November deposit in January without the agreement saying so.

Change the price for new asks first. If take-down weather makes a booked house unsafe, reschedule. Do not "make it up" by rushing a roof you already refused.

## What Christmas light pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common Christmas light pricing mistakes in Canada are quoting a roof, stapling to save clips, leaving take-down out of the price, and treating a busy December gross as profit. A first season can look busy and still lose money once the ladder, the light inventory, insurance, and your hours are counted.

Other misses: copying a price from another city, spending the deposit before take-down, and promising a street you cannot finish in daylight. ## Which tax rules apply to Christmas light prices in Canada?

Christmas light income in Canada is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all of your side hustles, not a special rule for seasonal work. A November deposit is still income to track.
