## How does Mistplay work for Canadians?

Mistplay's beginner guide, updated January 20, 2026, describes a free Android app. You install a new game through Mistplay, play to earn game experience points, collect units at checkpoints, and redeem units in the Shop for gift cards or sweepstakes entries. Games installed outside the app don't count. Gift-card choice depends on your location, and sweepstakes eligibility depends on your region.

The guide also lists daily tasks, buddy quests, boosted games that temporarily speed up earning, and referral bonuses when a friend you invited reaches Checkpoint 5 in a game. The referral article doesn't publish a bonus amount, so this guide doesn't print one.

Two Shop features aren't earnings. The **mystery box** uses units for a chance at a bonus, and **gems** come from in-game purchases. Spending real money in a game to earn gems turns a free reward app into a cost. If you're using Mistplay to earn, redeem units for gift cards and skip both.

The iPhone post says Mistplay for iPhone is rolling out to people in the US and Canada, and that points on one version aren't shared with the other. The VPN article, updated February 13, 2025, says the terms don't allow VPN use and that accounts connecting through a VPN risk a permanent ban.

## How does Freecash work for Canadians?

Freecash's PayPal country article, updated May 28, 2025, includes Canada. That means the PayPal option is available, not that every offer will show up for a Canadian account.

The country-setting article says Freecash knows your location from your IP address, which is why it forbids VPNs. If you travel abroad, the country article says to wait until you return to use Freecash again; Freecash also has a [help article on using it while on holiday](https://freecash.com/academy/en/support/account/restrictions/can-do-offers-surveys-on-holiday).

The PayPal cash-out article, updated August 19, 2026, says:

- PayPal withdrawals are available from $5 to $200 per transaction, and a 5% withdrawal fee applies to all amounts.
- Almost all withdrawals go through Tremendous. You enter your PayPal email, confirm the order is non-refundable, wait out a security hold, then redeem from an email with the subject line "Redeem Freecash PayPal $X USD".
- Your first withdrawal requires identity verification with a government-issued ID and a working camera.

Freecash has a separate help article about a region-by-region threshold for the first withdrawal, so check the cash-out screen before counting on $5 being enough the first time. The ID check, the 5% fee and the hold are why a first payout takes longer than later ones.

The page writes amounts with a dollar sign, but the payout email says USD, so this guide treats them as US dollars. If your cash-out screen shows a different currency, go by the screen.

**Skip gambling-style offers.** Reward sites sometimes list offers from betting or real-money gaming apps, or offers that require a deposit or purchase. This guide doesn't recommend them. Gambling isn't a side hustle, and an offer you pay into can cost more than it returns. Stick to free games, surveys and tasks.

## Who does each app suit?

**Mistplay** suits Android users, or iPhone users in Canada once the iPhone app reaches them, who already play mobile games and are happy with gift cards. It's the lower-effort choice: you play games you might have played anyway.

**Freecash** suits people who want PayPal money and don't mind surveys and offers alongside games, an ID check, and a 5% fee.

**Neither** suits someone who needs reliable income. Neither company publishes a Canadian average, and a quiet week of offers pays nothing.

## How do you use these apps without wasting the week?

### Step 1: Set up on your home connection in Canada

On Android, install games from inside Mistplay so they count. On iPhone, follow Mistplay's download note for Canada and expect a separate balance. Open Freecash for the first time at home, so your profile country is Canada.

### Step 2: Leave the VPN off

Both companies treat a VPN as faking your country. A banned account doesn't pay out the balance you were saving.

### Step 3: Read the reward before you start

Freecash shows an amount on each offer. Mistplay shows units and Shop prices. If an offer needs a purchase or a deposit, it's no longer free. Skip it.

### Step 4: Cash out when you can

A balance in an app isn't money in your account. Freecash's PayPal path has a fee, a hold and an email to redeem. Mistplay's reward is a gift card after you redeem. Note the date you requested each payout.

### Step 5: Stop when the hours stop making sense

If a week of play produces one gift card you didn't particularly want, the app is entertainment with a rebate, not a side hustle. A wider list of phone options is [side hustles you can do from your phone](/side-hustles/phone-side-hustles-canada/).

## What mistakes do Canadians make with game reward apps?

- **Playing a game installed from the app store.** Mistplay only rewards games launched through the app.
- **Using a VPN to see a US catalogue.** Both companies can close the account.
- **Ignoring Freecash's 5% fee.** The balance isn't what PayPal receives.
- **Counting an unredeemed balance as income.** Until a gift card or PayPal payment arrives, you have points.
- **Spending money to earn rewards.** In-game purchases for gems and offers that need a deposit cost real money.
- **Paying someone to "unlock" a balance.** Both apps describe free sign-up and a normal cash-out. A fee to release earnings is a pattern described in [side hustle scams](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply to game-app rewards in Canada?

Rewards you receive for completing tasks can be income, and a gift card is still value. Business income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), reported in Canadian dollars. Whether small consumer rewards are treated like survey pay or like a rebate is covered in [are rewards and bonuses taxable](/side-hustles/are-rewards-and-bonuses-taxable-canada/).
