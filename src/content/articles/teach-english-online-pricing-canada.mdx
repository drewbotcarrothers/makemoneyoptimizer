What you can charge to teach English online from Canada depends first on the channel. Fixed-pay platforms set your rate: Cambly lists $0.17 a minute for adult conversation, about $14.52 CAD an hour of conversation. Marketplaces such as Preply let you set your own rate and take a commission. Direct students pay what you agree, and Canadian schools show the ceiling: Language Trainers Canada charges students from C$39 an hour for online private lessons, and English for Canada charges CAD$69 for a one-hour CELPIP session. A direct rate has to cover prep, scheduling and both halves of CPP.

## What do online English teaching platforms pay?

Platforms either pay a fixed rate or let you set one and keep a commission. Both were on the platforms' own pages on October 9, 2026.

| Channel | Published terms | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cambly (adults) | $0.17 per minute ($10.20 per hour) | [Cambly tutor page](https://www.cambly.com/english/tutors) |
| Cambly Kids | $0.20 per minute ($12 per hour) | [Cambly tutor page](https://www.cambly.com/english/tutors) |
| Cambly payment threshold | Weekly payment when at least US$20.00 is owed | [Cambly tutor agreement](https://www.cambly.com/web/en/tutor/agreement_doc) |
| Preply | You set the rate; 100% commission on each new student's trial lesson, then 33% for new tutors, 28% after 20 hours, falling to as low as 18% | [Preply commission model](https://help.preply.com/en/articles/4171383-preply-commission-model) |
| Direct students | Your own rate | — |

Cambly's agreement states its threshold in US dollars, so treat the per-minute rate as USD and confirm the currency in your account. Cambly pays for minutes in conversation, not time waiting for a student.

### In Canadian dollars

At the Bank of Canada's [daily exchange rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4240 CAD per USD on October 8, 2026, Cambly's US$10.20 hour is about $14.52 CAD and the US$12 Kids hour about $17.09 CAD, before waiting time, transfer fees, tax and CPP. Your payment processor's rate may be lower.

### Wage benchmark (Job Bank)

[Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/24624/ca) lists employed secondary-school-subject tutors at a $20.00 national median hourly wage (2023–2024 data, updated November 19, 2025), with medians of $21.16 in Alberta, $21.67 in British Columbia and $20.00 in Ontario and Quebec. It's a floor check for direct students, not an ESL rate.

## Which pricing models work for teaching English online?

Online English is priced per lesson (30 or 60 minutes), as prepaid packages, or as specialised courses such as test prep at a higher rate. The schools above use all three.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed platform rate | Paid per minute in conversation | Starting with no students | Waiting time is unpaid |
| Marketplace rate | You set a USD rate; commission comes off | Building a student base | The trial lesson pays nothing on Preply |
| Direct per lesson | Your own rate, paid to you | Returning students | You handle scheduling and payment |
| Package | 6 to 12 lessons prepaid | Committed students | Expiry and rescheduling rules |
| Test prep or business English | CELPIP, IELTS, PTE, interviews | Higher-value goals | Overpromising scores |

## How do you calculate an online English teaching rate?

```text
Direct lesson   = CAD target per working hour × (lesson + prep minutes) ÷ 60
                  (for a 60-minute lesson)
Package         = lessons × lesson price × (1 − package discount)
Marketplace     = amount you want to keep ÷ (1 − commission)
USD price       = CAD price ÷ Bank of Canada rate (leave room for spreads)
Platform check  = minutes in conversation × per-minute rate ÷ hours online
```

**Use Job Bank's $20.00 median as a floor, not a target.** You also pay for prep, unpaid messages and both halves of CPP. **Count prep honestly.** Free conversation needs little; CELPIP or IELTS marking can double the time. **Gross up for commission and currency** before you list a marketplace rate.

## What do worked English teaching quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** Targets, prep times, minutes in conversation and discounts are my assumptions. Currency conversions use 1.4240.

### Quote 1: what Cambly pays for three hours online

- 45 minutes in conversation per hour online × 3 hours = 135 minutes.
- 135 × US$0.17 = US$22.95, or $32.68 CAD.
- That's $10.89 CAD per hour online, below Job Bank's $20.00 tutor median. The trade is no marketing at all.

### Quote 2: a direct conversation student, weekly 60-minute lessons

- Target $30 per working hour; each lesson needs 15 minutes of prep and messages: $30 × 75 ÷ 60 = **$37.50 a lesson**.
- 10-lesson package: 10 × $37.50 = $375, less $25 (about 7%) for prepaying = **$350**.
- Hourly check: $350 ÷ 12.5 working hours = $28. The discount costs $2 an hour; the prepaid term is what you buy with it.
- Language Trainers Canada's online private lessons start at C$39 an hour, so $37.50 sits just under a school's price.

### Quote 3: CELPIP speaking and writing prep

- Target $40 per working hour; each 60-minute lesson needs 30 minutes of marking: $40 × 90 ÷ 60 = **$60 a lesson**.
- 6-lesson program: 6 × $60 = $360, less 5% = **$342**, or $38 per working hour across 9 hours.
- English for Canada lists a single CELPIP session at CAD$69 and a 6-class program at CAD$350. Don't promise a band score.

### Quote 4: listing the same lesson on Preply

- Keep the CAD$37.50 target: $37.50 ÷ 1.4240 = US$26.33 to keep.
- List price at 33% commission: US$26.33 ÷ 0.67 = US$39.30, listed as **US$39**.
- At 33%: you keep US$39 × 0.67 = US$26.13, about $37.21 CAD. After 20 hours at 28%: US$39 × 0.72 = US$28.08, about $39.99 CAD.
- Each new student's trial lesson pays nothing, at 100% commission.

```text

Lessons: 60 minutes, weekly, [day/time + time zone]
Focus: [conversation / CELPIP speaking and writing]
Price: $37.50 a lesson, or $350 for 10 prepaid (expires [date])
Cancellations: 24 hours' notice; later cancellations count as taught
Late arrival: lesson ends at the scheduled time
Homework marking: [included / $15 per writing task]

```

## How do you raise English teaching rates?

- **Raise between packages,** never in the middle of one, and give notice before the next purchase.
- **Add a specialty.** Test prep, business English and interview practice support higher prices than conversation, as English for Canada's CELPIP pricing shows.
- **Test higher marketplace rates on new students first;** keep existing students until a natural break.
- **Re-check the exchange rate** if students pay in USD; a move in the rate changes your CAD result. For the scripts, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What online English teaching pricing mistakes are common?

Common mistakes are counting time online as paid time on per-minute platforms, ignoring the commission and exchange rate, and having no cancellation policy. Each makes the hourly result look higher than it is. Track minutes in conversation versus minutes online. Price direct lessons in one currency and say which. Don't promise test scores.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect online English teaching?

Online teaching income earned from Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), converted to Canadian dollars, and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net earnings; see the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/). GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

Place-of-supply rules decide which rate, if any, applies to a registered teacher, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html); students outside Canada and platforms as intermediaries make this more complex, so get advice before you near the threshold. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers the basics.

Direct students paying in USD and CAD are easier to track in one invoicing tool. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> is one option for lesson and package invoices; check its current currency options before relying on them.

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

Platform terms and school prices are what each page listed on October 9, 2026, and they change. School prices are what students pay a business with staff and marketing, not what a solo teacher will get on day one. Language Trainers Canada's package table covers common languages generally, not English only. English for Canada's program prices are labelled introductory. Job Bank's tutor wage is 2023–2024 employee pay for school-subject tutors. The worked quotes use assumed prep times and targets.
