Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip all pay Canadian couriers per offer, plus 100% of tips, and none of them publishes a fixed hourly wage outside the provincial minimums in Ontario and British Columbia. ## How does each delivery app calculate courier pay in Canada?

Each delivery app in Canada shows the offer before you accept it, but the formula behind that number is different, and none of the three is an hourly wage outside the provincial minimums. Read the offer as gross pay for the time from acceptance to drop-off, not including the drive back.

### Uber Eats

[Uber's Canadian delivery page](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/deliver/) says you see how much you will be paid before you accept an order, customers can tip in the app, and 100% of tips are yours. Earnings move to your bank account weekly. Uber does not publish a per-kilometre or per-minute rate card for Canadian couriers on that page, so the offer screen is the number you judge.

### DoorDash

[How Dasher Pay Works](https://help.doordash.com/en-ca/dashers/article/how-is-dasher-pay-calculated) says base pay ranges from $2 to $10+ per offer when you earn per offer, rising for longer, slower, or less popular offers. Promotions such as Peak Pay, Challenges, and Streaks are added on top, and tips never change the base pay. The acceptance screen is described as the minimum guaranteed amount for that offer. DoorDash also offers an Earn by Time mode in select cities, which pays a guaranteed rate for active time instead of per offer.

### Skip

[How Courier Pay Works](https://courier-help.skipthedishes.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360060022453-How-Courier-Pay-Works) says Transit Pay is set by estimated driving distance and time, tips are never used to subsidize Transit Pay, and tips are not pooled. You see the total, the pickup and drop-off, and the estimated distance before you accept. Weekly direct deposit covers the previous Monday to Sunday, and funds usually land by Thursday ([Courier Pay basics](https://insidetrack.skipthedishes.com/faq/courier-pay-basics/)). Fast Cash, where available in your city, cashes out to a supported debit card for $1.99 each time ([Courier Pay basics](https://insidetrack.skipthedishes.com/faq/courier-pay-basics/)).

## What minimum pay rules apply to delivery couriers in Ontario and B.C.?

Ontario requires at least $17.95 an hour as the general minimum wage from October 1, 2026 (up from $17.60), and British Columbia requires $21.89 per hour of engaged time from June 1, 2026, plus at least $0.35 per km for delivery by any transport other than walking. Tips are excluded from both floors, and waiting between offers is not counted. Other provinces do not have an equivalent rule on the pages this guide checked, so outside Ontario and British Columbia the offer is the only number.

- **Ontario:** the [Digital Platform Workers' Rights Act page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers) says workers must be paid at least the general minimum wage set out in the Employment Standards Act, 2000, determined for each work assignment performed or on a pay period basis, with tips excluded. Waiting between offers is not part of a work assignment. The Digital Platform Workers' Rights Act, 2022 came into force on July 1, 2025. Ontario's [minimum wage guide](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage) lists the general minimum wage as $17.95 per hour from October 1, 2026 to September 30, 2027 ($17.60 the year before); it is adjusted every October 1 with the Ontario Consumer Price Index.
- **British Columbia:** the province's [minimum wage page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage) lists $21.89 per hour of engaged time for online platform workers as of June 1, 2026, plus at least $0.35 per km for delivery by any transport other than walking. B.C. adjusts this rate every June 1 by the B.C. Consumer Price Index.

Skip's [Ontario and B.C. FAQ](https://insidetrack.skipthedishes.com/faq/bill-88/) says it reviews average hourly eligible pay for active delivery time each week and automatically adds a top-up if it falls below the applicable minimum wage, with tips excluded.

Both floors apply to every app operating in the province, so they do not make one app better than another. They do mean a slow week can be topped up. Check the weekly statement rather than assuming.

## How should you compare the apps with your own numbers?

Compare delivery apps in Canada with a two-week log of gross pay, tips, hours online, and kilometres, then subtract a per-kilometre vehicle cost you calculate yourself. For the full method, see [how to work out your real hourly rate on gig apps](/side-hustles/gig-app-real-hourly-rate-canada/). The illustrative example below shows the arithmetic, and it is not a forecast.

### Illustrative example

Suppose a courier runs the same Friday dinner window on two apps. On app A, the log shows $62 in offers and $18 in tips over 3 hours and 45 km. On app B, it shows $55 in offers and $24 in tips over 3 hours and 38 km. Suppose the courier's own vehicle cost estimate is $0.30 per km (fuel, wear, and a share of insurance, worked out from their own bills).

- App A: $62 + $18 = $80 gross. 45 km × $0.30 = $13.50. $80 − $13.50 = $66.50, or about $22.17 per hour online.
- App B: $55 + $24 = $79 gross. 38 km × $0.30 = $11.40. $79 − $11.40 = $67.60, or about $22.53 per hour online.

These are assumed numbers for teaching, not a quote, an average, or a typical result. The point is that the app with the bigger offers was not the better shift once kilometres were counted. Tax is not subtracted here.

## What pitfalls do Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip couriers commonly hit in Canada?

Couriers commonly find that the offer on the screen is not what they keep once gas, wear, and the unpaid time between orders are counted. Set your own minimum net hourly before the shift, then decline offers that miss it.

- Treat gross pay as the starting number. The CRA says you can deduct motor vehicle expenses you incur to earn business income, using Chart A on Form T2125, and that a full logbook should show each business trip's destination, reason, and distance ([motor vehicle expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses.html), [motor vehicle records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses/motor-vehicle-records.html)). The worksheet is [how to work out your real hourly rate on gig apps](/side-hustles/gig-app-real-hourly-rate-canada/).
- Write that net hourly down, from your own fuel and wear, before you go online. Decline offers under the line. [How Dasher Pay Works](https://help.doordash.com/en-ca/dashers/article/how-is-dasher-pay-calculated) says the acceptance screen is the minimum guaranteed amount for that offer, so the decline happens before you tap. Uber and Skip also show the offer first. The table above is that comparison.
- A second app is a common way to fill a quiet hour once you already know one zone. Juggling three apps in the first week is still how orders go late. The longer test is [when to quit a gig app](/side-hustles/when-to-quit-a-gig-app-canada/).
- Order volume changes by neighbourhood and by time of day. Confirm the live map for the streets you will actually drive. A busy core in one city is not a promise about another.

## What mistakes do couriers make when choosing between delivery apps in Canada?

Couriers in Canada most often choose the app by sign-up bonus, run three apps at once in week one, or skip the insurance call. A promotion has an end date; the restaurant mix in your zone does not change as fast. More of these traps are in [food delivery mistakes to avoid in Canada](/side-hustles/food-delivery-mistakes-canada/).

- **Stacking apps too early.** Juggling offers from three apps while learning the zone leads to late orders and cancelled offers.
- **Ignoring the city page.** Uber's national 1990 car rule is not the Toronto rule.
- **Not telling the insurer.** Business use of a personal car is a change in risk. Get the answer in writing before the first shift. What to ask is in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).
- **Counting the offer as profit.** Fuel, parking, and the drive home are not in the offer.

## Do Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip report courier earnings to the CRA?

Since the 2024 calendar year, the CRA's [Reporting Rules for Digital Platform Operators](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html) (Part XX of the Income Tax Act) require certain platform operators to collect and report seller information each year, and the CRA lists rideshare and delivery services among the activities whose sellers may be reported. A platform report does not pay the tax for you; couriers still report the income themselves on Form T2125.

How that income is taxed for Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip in Canada is in [how gig-app and platform income is taxed](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply to delivery app couriers in Canada?

Delivery income from Uber Eats, DoorDash, or Skip is generally self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), whichever app you pick. Food delivery follows the CRA's [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) for GST/HST, not the first-dollar rule that applies to drivers carrying passengers.

If you deliver on two apps, add both together for the small-supplier test and for your return. Keep each app's weekly statements. The kilometre log that helped you compare apps is the same log you need for [vehicle expenses for delivery and rideshare](/side-hustles/vehicle-expenses-delivery-rideshare-canada/). How much to hold back is in [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
