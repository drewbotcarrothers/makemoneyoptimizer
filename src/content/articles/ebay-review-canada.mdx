eBay is the oldest online marketplace open to Canadian sellers, and on eBay.ca most items pay a 13.6% final value fee on the total amount of the sale plus a $0.40 per-order fee, with payment processing included ([eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)). ## How much does eBay charge Canadian sellers in 2026?

Two main fees: an insertion fee when you list (free for your first 250 listings each month, then $0.30) and a final value fee when the item sells ([eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)). The final value fee is a percentage of the **total amount of the sale**, which eBay defines as the item price plus shipping, handling and sales tax, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 (orders $10 or less) or $0.40 (over $10). All fees are in Canadian dollars.

| eBay.ca category (no Store, November 2026) | Final value fee | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most categories | 13.6% up to $7,500, 2.35% above | Includes parts, accessories, tools |
| Books & Magazines, Movies & TV, Music (except vinyl) | 15.3% up to $7,500, 2.35% above | Highest common rate |
| Trading cards, comics, collectible card games, coins | 13.25% up to $7,500, 2.35% above | Bullion has its own rule |
| Guitars & Basses | 6.7% up to $7,500, 2.35% above | Insertion is free |
| Men's and women's athletic shoes | 8% at $150 or more (no per-order fee); 13.6% below $150 | Free insertion at $150+ |

Optional extras cost more: a reserve price is $5 or 7.5% of the reserve (whichever is greater, max $250), bold is $2 on auctions or $4 on fixed price, and a subtitle is $1.50 to $6.00. A chargeback you are found responsible for costs $20 ([eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)).

### What extra fees apply when you sell to the US?

If you ship yourself to a US buyer (not through eBay International Shipping), eBay adds a 0.4% international fee, or 1% for other countries. With eBay International Shipping, Canadian sellers pay no international fee. If you list on another eBay site or choose a USD payout, eBay's currency conversion charge is 3.25% ([eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)). For your own US labels, compare [Stallion Express vs Chit Chats vs Canada Post](/side-hustles/stallion-express-vs-chit-chats-vs-canada-post-canada/).

### What raises your eBay fees?

Seller performance. An account rated Below Standard pays an extra 6% on final value fees the next month (7% after four months), and a Very High rate of "item not as described" returns adds 5% in that category. Sharing contact details to sell off eBay is charged a final value fee even if the item doesn't sell ([eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)).

## How much do you keep from an eBay sale in Canada?

Fees are charged on the tax-inclusive total, so they take a bigger bite than the headline 13.6% suggests. eBay's own example: a $400 phone with free shipping to Ontario has a $452 total (13% HST), fees of $62.17, and the seller makes $337.83, about 84% of the price ([eBay Canada](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822)).

### Illustrative example: one thrifted item

**Assumed:** you bought a jacket for $12, list it at $60 plus $15 shipping, it sells to a buyer in Ontario (13% HST), and your shipping label costs $14.

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Total amount of the sale: ($60 + $15) × 1.13 | $84.75 |
| Final value fee: 13.6% × $84.75 + $0.40 | −$11.93 |
| Item price + shipping received | $75.00 |
| Shipping label (assumed) | −$14.00 |
| Item cost (assumed) | −$12.00 |
| **Profit on the sale** | **$37.07** |

### Illustrative example: a used book

**Assumed:** $30 book plus $12 shipping to Ontario, $11 label, $3 cost. Total $47.46; fee 15.3% × $47.46 + $0.40 = $7.66. Profit: $42 − $7.66 − $11 − $3 = **$20.34**. The higher book rate and shipping take a larger share on cheaper items.

### What you keep over a year

If eBay selling is a business (regular buying to resell), profit is self-employment income. Same assumptions, scaled up:

| Line (illustrative) | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| 200 sales × $37.07 profit | $7,414.00 |
| CPP: 11.9% × ($7,414 − $3,500) | −$465.77 |
| Tax set-aside: an assumed 15% | −$1,112.10 |
| **Kept** | **$5,836.13** |
| Per hour at an assumed 30 minutes per item (100 hours) | about $58.36 |

The 30 minutes covers sourcing, photos, listing, packing and the post-office run; slow sellers, returns and unsold stock pull it down. CPP is 11.9% on net self-employment earnings above $3,500 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/canada-pension-plan-cpp/cpp-contribution-rates-maximums-exemptions.html)); see [CPP on side hustle income](/side-hustles/cpp-on-side-hustle-income-canada/). Size your tax share with [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### A pricing formula you can copy

```text
Minimum eBay price (most categories, Ontario-style 13% tax)
  Price ≥ (item cost + label cost + profit you want + $0.40)
          ÷ (1 − 0.136 × 1.13)
Example: ($12 + $14 + $25 + $0.40) ÷ 0.84632 ≈ $60.73 total (item + shipping charged)
```

Change 0.136 to your category's rate and 1.13 to the buyer's tax rate (GST-only provinces are 1.05).

## How do eBay payouts work in Canada?

eBay pays into a linked Canadian chequing account (not savings, not PayPal). Sales show as Available for payout about one day after the buyer's payment is confirmed; daily payouts are initiated about two days after payment, and your bank takes another 1 to 3 business days ([eBay payouts](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814)). You can switch to weekly (Tuesdays), biweekly or monthly, and request available funds on demand on those schedules.

Payouts can be held on new accounts or problem orders; Seller Hub's Payments tab shows holds. Keep the payout account separate from personal spending so bookkeeping is easy; see [business bank accounts for side hustles](/side-hustles/business-bank-account-side-hustle-canada/).

## What sells well on eBay for Canadian sellers?

Items buyers search for by brand, model or part number, that ship in a small box. The fee table above favours some categories. Ideas that suit Canadian resellers:

1. **Sports cards and trading card games**: 13.25% rate, light to ship. See [sports card flipping](/side-hustles/sports-card-flipping-canada/).
2. **Hockey and NHL memorabilia**: Canadian supply, US demand.
3. **Guitars and basses**: 6.7% rate, higher prices.
4. **Athletic sneakers of $150+**: 8% rate, no per-order fee.
5. **Car and truck parts**: searched by part number.
6. **Video games and retro consoles**: search-driven, compact.
7. **Camera lenses and film cameras**: high value per box.
8. **Vintage Canadiana** (Roots, Hudson's Bay, Expo 67): niche searches.
9. **Textbooks**: seasonal demand (15.3% rate).
10. **Vinyl records**: excluded from the 15.3% music rate, so 13.6%.
11. **Lego sets and minifigures**: priced by set number.
12. **Branded outdoor gear** (Arc'teryx, MEC, Canada Goose): resale demand.
13. **Collectible coins and paper money**: 13.25% rate.
14. **Replacement electronics parts** (chargers, remotes): cheap to ship.
15. **Liquidation returns**: tested and described honestly. See [liquidation pallets](/side-hustles/liquidation-pallets-canada/).
16. **Black Friday and Boxing Day buys**: see [holiday reselling](/side-hustles/black-friday-boxing-day-reselling-canada/).

Furniture and big appliances usually do better locally; see [Facebook Marketplace flipping](/side-hustles/facebook-marketplace-flipping-side-hustle-canada/).

## What are the pros and cons of eBay for Canadian sellers?

**Pros**
- Search-driven buyers, including the US market.
- One fee covers payment processing; 250 free listings a month.
- Lower rates for guitars, sneakers over $150, cards and coins.
- Daily payouts to a Canadian chequing account.

**Cons**
- Fees apply to shipping and sales tax, not just the item.
- 15.3% on books, movies and music.
- Performance surcharges of 5% to 7% if your service slips.
- Shipping cost makes cheap or bulky items hard to profit on.

## Who is eBay best for in Canada?

eBay suits Canadians who know a category well enough to spot underpriced items, sell things that ship small, and want US buyers. It is a weaker fit for clothing under about $30, furniture and anything heavy, where [Poshmark](/side-hustles/poshmark-review-canada/), Vinted or local pickup win. If you would rather sell handmade or vintage to a gift-shopping crowd, see the [Etsy review](/side-hustles/etsy-review-canada/).

## What mistakes do new eBay sellers make in Canada?

- **Pricing on the item alone.** The fee hits shipping and tax too; use the formula above.
- **Guessing shipping.** Weigh and measure before you list.
- **Slow handling or vague condition.** Both feed the performance surcharges.
- **Paying for upgrades by habit.** Bold and subtitles rarely pay back on low-priced items.
- **No records.** You need purchase receipts for cost of goods sold.

## How is eBay income taxed in Canada?

Selling your own used belongings occasionally is usually not business income; buying to resell regularly usually is. Our [is selling on Kijiji taxable?](/side-hustles/is-selling-on-kijiji-taxable-canada/) guide walks through the line. Business profit goes on Form [T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), with item costs, fees and shipping as expenses, and you pay CPP on net profit.

eBay is a reporting platform under Canada's digital platform rules. It reports sellers' identity information and the amounts paid or credited, plus fees, to the CRA, and gives you a copy by January 31 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2024/using-app-website-earn-income.html); [what is shared](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms/information-shared.html)). Once your taxable sales pass $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters, the GST/HST small-supplier rule applies ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). See [tax on reselling and gig app income](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/). ## What should you keep in mind about these eBay numbers?

Fee rates, payout timing and surcharges are as listed on eBay.ca on November 3, 2026, for sellers without an eBay Store; Store subscribers, vehicles and eBay Live have different fee pages.
