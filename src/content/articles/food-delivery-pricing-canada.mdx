Food delivery drivers in Canada don't set a price. The app offers pay per delivery, and your hourly result depends on which offers you take, the tips, and what your vehicle costs per kilometre. Two provinces set a floor. Ontario requires at least the general minimum wage, $17.95 an hour from October 1, 2026, for time on work assignments. British Columbia requires $21.89 per hour of engaged time plus at least $0.35 per kilometre. Neither pays for waiting between orders. Employed delivery drivers earn a $20.00 median on Job Bank, and the CRA's 2026 vehicle allowance is $0.73 per kilometre, so a 60 km shift carries roughly $34 to $44 of full vehicle cost at official rates.

<QuickAnswer>

- **How pay works:** base pay per offer plus promotions plus 100% of tips. DoorDash's base pay is $2 to $10+ per offer; Uber Eats and Skip price each offer by estimated time and distance.
- **Provincial floors (October 2026):** Ontario $17.95/hour of assignment time; B.C. $21.89/hour of engaged time plus $0.35/km. Tips don't count toward either. Waiting time isn't covered.
- **Vehicle cost benchmarks:** CRA prescribed allowance $0.73/km (first 5,000 km) and $0.67/km after; CRA travel rates of 56¢ to 63¢/km in the provinces.
- **Employee benchmark:** Job Bank food delivery driver wages $15.00 low, $20.00 median, $29.35 high (Canada, 2023–2024).
- **Use it:** set a per-minute and per-km floor, decline offers below it, and track net pay per logged-in hour.

</QuickAnswer>

## How do DoorDash, Uber Eats and Skip calculate delivery pay in Canada?

All three pay a per-delivery amount based on estimated time and distance, add promotions, and pass on 100% of tips. The offer screen shows the amount before you accept. Pages were checked on October 9, 2026.

| App | How the delivery amount is set | Promotions | Tips | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DoorDash | Base pay $2–$10+ per offer by estimated time, distance and desirability; Earn by Time mode pays a guaranteed hourly rate for active time | Peak Pay, Challenges, Delivery Streaks, Boosts | 100%; base pay never changes with the tip | [DoorDash help](https://help.doordash.com/en-ca/dashers/article/how-is-dasher-pay-calculated), [Dasher pay](https://dasher.doordash.com/en-ca/about/pay) |
| Uber Eats | Upfront price based on estimated distance, travel time and wait time at pickup and dropoff, plus surge or boost | Surge, boost multipliers | 100%; customers can change the tip up to one hour after dropoff | [Uber Canada delivery pricing addendum](https://tb-static.uber.com/prod/reddog/country/Canada/logistics/3f02dd1c-834d-463b-b357-24d7932a466c.pdf) |
| SkipTheDishes | Transit Pay based on estimated time and distance; no fixed per-km rate | Bonuses and incentives by time of day and order type | 100%; tips are never pooled | [Skip Bill 88 FAQ](https://insidetrack.skipthedishes.com/faq/bill-88/) |

DoorDash says the amount on the acceptance screen is the minimum guaranteed for that offer, and gives the example of $6 base pay plus a $5 tip paying $11. Uber's addendum says the upfront price is either the minimum delivery fee before tip or, if the card says "including estimated tip", an estimate with the tip in it. Read which one you're looking at before you compare offers.

## What do Ontario and B.C. require apps to pay?

Ontario and B.C. set legal floors for app delivery workers. Both exclude tips and neither covers time waiting for an offer.

| Rule | Ontario | British Columbia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly floor | General minimum wage: $17.95 from Oct 1, 2026 ($17.60 the year before) | $21.89 per hour of engaged time from June 1, 2026 ($21.43 in 2025) |
| Time counted | Work assignments, generally from acceptance | Engaged time: from accepting an order to completing it or cancellation |
| Distance | No per-km amount | At least $0.35/km for delivery by any means other than walking ($0.45/km for ride-hail) |
| How it's checked | Per work assignment or per pay period | Per pay period |
| Tips | Excluded | Excluded; 100% of tips go to the worker |
| Source | [Ontario DPWRA page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers), [Ontario minimum wage](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage) | [B.C. minimum wage page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage) |

B.C. raises its rates each June 1 by the B.C. consumer price index. Ontario's page says that when an operator tops you up to the minimum for a pay period, it must tell you the extra amount in writing. Skip's FAQ says it reviews average hourly earnings for active delivery time each week in Ontario and B.C. and adds a top-up when they fall below the minimum. Elsewhere in Canada, pay is whatever the offer says.

## What does a car cost per kilometre on delivery?

The CRA's 2026 prescribed allowance is $0.73 per km for the first 5,000 km and $0.67 after, in the provinces ($0.77 and $0.71 in the territories). The CRA says this rate is generally reasonable to cover an employee's main costs of owning and operating their own vehicle ([CRA allowances page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/benefits-allowances/automobile/automobile-motor-vehicle-allowances.html)). The CRA's own travel directive pays 56¢ to 63¢ per km in the provinces from January 1, 2026, taxes included ([Appendix A](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/corporate/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/travel-directive/appendix-a-cra-kilometric-rates-jan-2026.html)).

| Distance in 2026 | At CRA allowance | At Ontario travel rate (63¢) | At Alberta travel rate (56.5¢) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 40 km shift | $29.20 | $25.20 | $22.60 |
| 60 km shift | $43.80 | $37.80 | $33.90 |
| 5,000 km year | $3,650 | $3,150 | $2,825 |
| 10,000 km year | $7,000 ($3,650 + $3,350) | $6,300 | $5,650 |

These are employer reimbursement rates, not your deduction. As a self-employed courier you claim the business share of actual expenses from receipts and a logbook; see the [vehicle expenses guide](/side-hustles/vehicle-expenses-delivery-rideshare-canada/). Use the official rates two ways:

- **Full cost (is the hustle worth it?):** the official rate, because it includes depreciation, insurance and repairs that delivery speeds up.
- **Running cost (should I take this offer?):** your own fuel, tires and maintenance per km from receipts, because you pay the car's fixed costs whether or not you accept.

## How do you calculate your floor for an offer?

Work out the least an offer must pay to cover your time and the kilometres, including the drive back to a busy area.

```text
Offer floor     = (estimated minutes ÷ 60 × target net hourly)
                + (offer km + return km) × running cost per km
Real hourly     = (base + promotions + tips + top-ups − total km × cost per km)
                  ÷ logged-in hours (including waiting)
B.C. minimum    = engaged hours × $21.89 + engaged km × $0.35   (per pay period, before tips)
Ontario minimum = assignment hours × $17.95                     (per assignment or pay period)
```

**Illustrative floor card** at a $20 target and an assumed $0.25/km running cost:

| Offer | Minutes | Km incl. return | Floor | Offer pay | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Short, close | 15 | 4 | $5.00 + $1.00 = $6.00 | $7.00 | Accept |
| Medium | 25 | 8 | $8.33 + $2.00 = $10.33 | $7.50 | Decline |
| Long, suburban | 40 | 18 | $13.33 + $4.50 = $17.83 | $16.00 | Decline unless it ends near demand |

At $20 an hour, every minute must earn $0.33. Write that and your per-km cost on a card on the dash.

## What do worked delivery shifts look like?

**Illustrative.** Pay, tips, minutes and kilometres below are assumed, not averages. Floors and per-km rates are the official ones above.

### Shift 1: Toronto, car, 3-hour Friday dinner

- 8 deliveries: base and promotions $56, tips $34, gross **$90**. 2.25 hours on assignments. 62 km including the drive home.
- Ontario floor: 2.25 × $17.95 = $40.39. Base pay of $56 is above it, so no top-up.
- Full cost at 63¢: 62 × $0.63 = $39.06. Net $50.94, or **$16.98 per logged-in hour**.
- Running cost at an assumed 25¢: 62 × $0.25 = $15.50. Net $74.50, or $24.83 per hour before the car's fixed costs.

### Shift 2: Vancouver, car, 2-hour lunch

- 5 deliveries: base and promotions $27, tips $18. Engaged time 1.3 hours, engaged distance 24 km, 32 km driven in total.
- B.C. minimum for the period: 1.3 × $21.89 = $28.46, plus 24 × $0.35 = $8.40, total $36.86. Base pay was $27, so the top-up is **$9.86**, if this shift were the whole pay period.
- Gross $36.86 + $18 tips = $54.86. Full cost at 60¢: 32 × $0.60 = $19.20. Net $35.66, or **$17.83 per hour**.

### Shift 3: Montreal, e-bike, 3-hour evening

- 9 deliveries: base and promotions $45, tips $30, gross **$75**. 36 km.
- Quebec has no app-worker floor, so pay is what the offers say.
- E-bike running cost assumed at 5¢/km (battery charging, tires, chain): $1.80. Net $73.20, or **$24.40 per hour**.

### Shift 4: Calgary, one long suburban offer

- Offer shows $9.50 for 32 minutes and 11 km, plus 6 km back to the restaurant cluster: 17 km.
- Floor at $20/h and full Alberta cost (56.5¢): $10.67 + $9.61 = $20.27. At running cost (25¢): $10.67 + $4.25 = **$14.92**.
- $9.50 clears neither, so decline. Alberta has no floor to top it up.

```text
               BC: hours × $21.89 + km × $0.35 = $____

Top-up shown on statement: $____   Matches? Y / N
If no: ask the app for the calculation in writing, then
contact Employment Standards in your province.
```

## How can delivery drivers raise earnings per hour?

You can't raise the price, so raise pay per hour with which offers you take and when you drive.

1. **Drive the peaks.** DoorDash's pay page points Dashers to mealtimes, weekends, events and late nights. Peak Pay and boosts apply then.
2. **Hold your floor.** Declining a $7.50 offer that needs $10.33 protects the hourly rate more than any promotion.
3. **Cut kilometres.** Stay in dense zones. In B.C., every engaged km adds $0.35 to the floor.
4. **Check top-ups.** In Ontario and B.C., compare base pay with the minimum each week.
5. **Change vehicle in the core.** A bike or e-bike skips fuel and most of a car's per-km cost, though not the weather.
6. **Compare apps on net per hour.** Run the shift log for a few weeks on each app before deciding. Multi-apping can cut waiting time; check each app's terms.

## What food delivery earnings mistakes are common?

- **Counting gross pay.** A $90 night can be $51 after full vehicle cost.
- **Ignoring waiting time.** Floors don't cover it, so your real rate is per logged-in hour.
- **Misreading tip estimates.** Uber's upfront price can include an estimated tip that the customer changes.
- **No logbook.** Without kilometres you can't claim vehicle expenses or check B.C.'s per-km minimum.
- **Accepting everything for stats.** Offers below your floor lower your hourly rate.

For the insurance, logbook, and tax mistakes couriers make most, and how to fix them, see the [food delivery mistakes guide](/side-hustles/food-delivery-side-hustle-canada/#what-to-watch-out-for).

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect food delivery earnings?

Food delivery income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both [CPP shares](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. Food delivery follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) for GST/HST, counting all your side hustles. The first-dollar registration rule applies to taxi and ride-sharing passenger services, so it only matters if you also carry passengers.

Uber's Canadian delivery addendum says sales tax is calculated and added separately from delivery fees. If you register, the rate depends on the province of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [platform income tax guide](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/) and [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) explain what platforms report and when to register.

Weekly payouts are easier to manage with tax set aside in a separate account. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian option for keeping delivery income and the tax portion apart. The [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers how much to hold back.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

Floors, app pay rules and CRA rates were on official pages on October 9, 2026. B.C. changes its rates every June 1, Ontario every October 1, and the CRA's travel rates are reviewed quarterly. Job Bank wages are for employed drivers (2023–2024 data), not app couriers. The CRA rates are employer allowance and travel rates, not a deduction you can claim. The shifts, tips, kilometres and the 25¢ and 5¢ running costs are illustrative, so replace them with your own logbook and receipts.
