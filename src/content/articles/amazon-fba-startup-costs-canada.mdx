## How much does Amazon FBA cost to start in Canada?

Amazon FBA costs $90.52 to $1,343.97 to start in Canada before inventory, depending on whether you send a few units of an existing product on the Individual plan, run a Professional account with proper boxes and a scale, or launch your own brand with barcodes and a trademark. Inventory is the largest line and is not in any kit.

| Kit | Supplies with 13% HST | Plan and brand costs, first year | First-year total before inventory | With an illustrative $280 of inventory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean (existing products, Individual plan) | $90.52 | $1.49 per item sold | $90.52 | $370.52 |
| Standard (Professional plan) | $199.95 | $359.88 (12 × $29.99) | $559.83 | $839.83 |
| Private label (Professional plan, own brand) | $335.53 | $359.88 + $157.50 GS1 + $491.06 CIPO | $1,343.97 | $1,623.97 |

Kits are cumulative. The supplies column uses Ontario's 13% HST; with 5% GST only, it is $84.12, $185.80 and $311.78. The plan fee, GS1 licence and CIPO fee are shown at their published prices; GS1 says applicable taxes are extra. The $280 is 40 units at an assumed $7 landed cost, which is a teaching number, not a product.

The kits assume you already own a computer, a laser or inkjet printer, and a phone. They leave out inbound freight to the fulfilment centre, which Seller Central quotes per shipment, and paid ads.

## What does Amazon.ca charge to sell with FBA?

Amazon.ca charges a plan fee, a referral fee on each sale, a fulfilment fee per unit and monthly storage. All figures below are from the public [pricing page](https://sell.amazon.ca/pricing), read on 9 October 2026. Amazon's [2026 fee announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/a9383e96-87ef-484e-bbe7-cc07584331bb) said there would be no increases to Canadian referral and FBA fees for 2026. Run the [revenue calculator](https://sell.amazon.ca/pricing/estimate) on the exact product before you buy it.

| Fee | Amazon.ca published rate |
| --- | --- |
| Individual plan | $1.49 per item sold, plus selling fees |
| Professional plan | CAD $29.99 a month, plus selling fees; suggested above 30 units a month, for ads and advanced tools, and for restricted categories. New subscriptions include access to Amazon.com and Amazon.com.mx |
| Referral fee | A percentage of the total price, including shipping and gift wrap, or a per-item minimum, whichever is greater. Home and Kitchen, Office Products, Pet Products, Sports and Outdoors, and Toys and Games are 15%, with a CAD $0.40 minimum on many rows |
| Fulfilment, envelope | First 100 g CAD $4.46; 100 to 200 g CAD $4.71 |
| Fulfilment, standard | First 100 g CAD $5.92; 100 to 200 g CAD $6.12 |
| Fulfilment, oversize | Small oversize first 500 g CAD $15.43; medium CAD $37.78; large CAD $82.20, plus a per-500 g amount |
| Low-price discount | Products under CAD $14 get fulfilment rates CAD $0.80 lower |
| Storage, standard-size | CAD $40 per cubic metre January to September; CAD $77 October to December |
| Storage, oversize | CAD $28 and CAD $49 in the same seasons |
| Extra storage fees | Aged inventory over 180 days and high storage utilization; amounts are in Seller Central, not on the public page |
| Refund administration | The lesser of CAD $5.00 or 20% of the referral fee |
| Lithium batteries and dangerous goods | CAD $0.11 per unit on top of fulfilment |
| Payout | Proceeds go to your bank account every two weeks |

Shipping weight is the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight, so measure the packaged unit. Beauty, grocery, clothing and jewellery use price bands for referral fees. Do not borrow 15% for a category you have not opened.

Individual or Professional? $29.99 ÷ $1.49 = 20.1, so the monthly plan costs less in plan fees once you sell about 20 units a month. Amazon's page still points sellers under 30 units a month to Individual. Choose Professional earlier if you need a restricted category, ads, or the new-seller incentives, which require it.

### Do you need GS1 barcodes and a trademark?

You need a GTIN for a new product, and you need a trademark only for Brand Registry. Amazon's [beginner's guide](https://sell.amazon.ca/beginners-guide) says it uses product IDs, also called GTINs, to match items to product pages; the UPC is the most common, some products need one from GS1, and in rare cases you can apply for a GTIN exemption. Reselling an existing branded product uses its existing barcode, so the lean and standard kits skip GS1.

GS1 Canada licenses 1 barcode for $26.25 a year (gross revenue under $250,000) or 10 for $157.50 a year (under $500,000), and a GTIN is needed for every size, flavour and variation. Its [small business bundles](https://gs1ca.org/small-business-bundles/) add product images and data from $341.25 a year for 10 GTINs.

[Amazon Brand Registry](https://brandservices.amazon.ca/eligibility) asks for a logo with your brand name on the product or packaging and a registered trademark or pending application from the trademark office of a country with an Amazon store. CIPO's online fee in 2026 is $491.06 for the first class of goods and $149.04 for each additional class, rising to $499.41 in 2027. Brand Registry is also the gate for Amazon.ca's [new-seller incentives](https://sell.amazon.ca/programs): 10% back on your first $70,000 of branded sales, then 5% back through your first year up to $1,350,000, plus up to $1,250 in Sponsored Products ad credit. Amazon applies the 10% as a referral-fee credit, so on a 15% category it takes the effective referral fee to about 5% of the price.

## Do Amazon FBA sellers need insurance in Canada?

Amazon FBA sellers in Canada need commercial liability insurance once sales reach $10,000 in a month, or earlier if Amazon asks. [Zensurance's Amazon seller page](https://www.zensurance.com/zensurance-insurance-program) says the policy must carry at least CAD $1 million per occurrence and in aggregate, and that Amazon sellers can expect to pay $400 to $3,000 a year for a $2 million commercial general liability policy, depending on location, products and where they are sourced, revenue, experience, staff and claims. Below $10,000 a month it is not required, so none of the kits above includes it.

What moves the premium most is the product. A kitchen gadget made overseas and a printed notebook are very different risks. Get a quote for the exact product before you place a large order, because a high premium changes the per-unit math. See [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/) for how liability and product cover fit together.

## How many units does it take to break even on Amazon FBA?

Amazon FBA breaks even after about 14 units on the lean kit, 69 on the standard kit and 127 on the private-label kit, in an illustrative $25 product that keeps $6.64 to $10.63 per unit. Inventory is not in those counts, because each unit's cost is already subtracted.

**Illustrative. The $25 price, $7 landed cost and 100 to 200 g standard size are assumptions, not a product recommendation or a typical result.**

| Price | $25.00 | $25.00 | $25.00 |
| Referral fee, 15% | −$3.75 | −$3.75 | −$3.75 |
| New-seller referral credit, 10% of the price | — | — | +$2.50 |
| FBA fulfilment, standard 100–200 g | −$6.12 | −$6.12 | −$6.12 |

| Individual plan fee | −$1.49 | — | — |
| Left per unit | $6.64 | $8.13 | $10.63 |

| Kit | First-year cost before inventory | Units to break even |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $90.52 | 14 (13.63) |
| Standard | $559.83 | 69 (68.86) |
| Private label | $1,343.97 | 127 (126.43) |

The arithmetic is first-year cost ÷ amount left per unit, rounded up. Standard: $559.83 ÷ $8.13 = 68.86, so 69 units. Private label: $1,343.97 ÷ $10.63 = 126.43, so 127. Units that never sell do not count: if 10 of 40 units sit unsold, $70 of landed cost stays in the warehouse and, after 180 days, aged-inventory fees apply on top of the monthly rate.

Storage is small at this scale. If each packaged unit is 0.002 cubic metres, an assumption, 40 units are 0.08 cubic metres: $3.20 a month from January to September and $6.16 a month from October to December at the standard-size rates. A slow product in the fourth quarter is what turns storage from a rounding error into a real cost.

Change the inputs to your product: its referral category, the exact size tier from the revenue calculator, and a landed cost from a real supplier quote.

## Where does the working capital go in an Amazon FBA start?

Working capital for Amazon FBA in Canada is the inventory, the freight to you, the freight into Amazon, and the two weeks or more you wait to be paid. A sale on day one is not cash on day one.

Brand and category approval is the trap that costs the most. The pricing page ties additional and restricted categories to the Professional plan, and some brands limit which sellers may list their goods. Open the listing flow in your own Seller Central account before you buy a case. A tool's "ungated" flag is not approval. If the listing is blocked, the goods are still yours.

## Where can Amazon FBA startup spending be lower, and where should it not be cut?

Amazon FBA spending stays lower when the first shipment is a few units of something you are already approved to sell, the product is light enough for the envelope or standard tier, and you stay on the Individual plan until about 20 sales a month. Merchant-fulfilled shipping, also on the pricing page, keeps the goods at home and saves fulfilment fees in exchange for your packing time. That trade is the point of [reselling](/side-hustles/reselling-side-hustle-canada/) if you do not want a warehouse at all. A trademark and GS1 licence can wait until you have a product of your own that sold.

Do not cut the listing-approval check, the revenue calculator run, or the prep rules. Do not send a lithium or hazmat product without reading the CAD $0.11 surcharge and the dangerous-goods rules. Do not budget October storage at the January rate.

## Which tax rules apply to an Amazon FBA budget in Canada?

Amazon FBA profit in Canada is generally business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Inventory is handled through cost of goods sold, so keep purchase and freight records. Self-employed people pay both portions of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net self-employment income. A label printer or scale that lasts for years may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion rather than a one-year deduction.

GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule. Under the CRA's [digital-economy rules for qualifying goods](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy-gsthst/charge-collect/sales-good.html), a platform generally collects GST/HST for vendors who are not registered, and registered vendors charge it themselves, including on platform sales. If you are registered, tax on business purchases, including the HST on the kits above, can often be an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html). The [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) and [T2125 versus T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) keep this on the business form. Money left after fees is discussed in [what to do with side hustle profits](/side-hustles/what-to-do-with-side-hustle-profits-canada/).
