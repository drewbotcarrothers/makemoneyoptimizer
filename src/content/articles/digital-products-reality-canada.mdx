Selling digital products in Canada means charging for a file a buyer downloads: a printable, a Notion template, a preset pack, or a short ebook. The realistic expectation is a slow ramp, a fee on every sale, and a real chance of few sales or none. It suits someone who can finish one specific file and keep it updated. It does not suit someone who needs this month's rent from a download that does not exist yet.

The hours-before-the-sale version, without these fee lines, is [passive income in Canada](/side-hustles/passive-income-reality-canada/). Why a generic catalogue shows up as a disappointment is [overhyped side hustles](/side-hustles/overhyped-side-hustles-canada/).

## What should you expect from templates, printables and presets in Canada?

Expect to spend the hours before the first dollar, and expect the sales to bunch rather than to arrive on a schedule. This site does not have a survey of Canadian digital-product income, so it will not print a typical month. Sellers commonly find that a vague, generic file sits unsold while a file for a narrow job is the one people can search for. That is a description of the work, not a statistic. One finished product is a better test than a shop of twenty unfinished ones.

## How do platform fees change a digital sale in Canada?

Platform fees change a digital sale by taking a percentage before your time is repaid. Etsy's [fees policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/) is the page to confirm. As this site's Etsy guide records from that policy, a listing fee is 0.20 USD whether or not the item sells, the transaction fee is 6.5% of the displayed price plus delivery and gift wrap, and payment processing depends on the bank-account country. Etsy's [regulatory operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) article is the source for the extra 0.50% on Canadian seller accounts. Confirm both pages. They move.

Etsy's [creativity standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity) are the other gate. Read them before you upload a file you did not make. The printables guide covers what a printable may contain. A borrowed mockup is how a shop gets a complaint.

Notion Marketplace and Gumroad publish their own fee pages. The [Notion template pricing guide](/side-hustles/notion-templates-pricing-canada/) cites those rates. Do not copy an Etsy percentage onto a Notion payout.

### Illustrative example

Teaching sketch. The $12 price and the 15 hours are assumptions. The 6.5% and 0.50% are the published Etsy lines this site cites. Not a quote, a forecast, or a typical shop.

Suppose one file is listed at $12 CAD. Transaction fee: 0.065 × $12 = $0.78. Regulatory operating fee: 0.005 × $12 = $0.06. Those two lines are $0.84, so $11.16 remains before payment processing and before the 0.20 USD listing fee. Suppose you spent an assumed 15 hours and you value that time at an assumed $20 an hour: 15 × $20 = $300. Sales to cover that time value, before processing: $300 ÷ $11.16 = about 27 sales. A processing fee you have not opened on Etsy's payments page makes the count higher. This sketch does not know whether you will make 27 sales, 2 sales, or none. Do not copy $12. The point is that the hours are spent first, and one sale does not retire them.

## How do you test a digital product without a large catalogue?

Test it by shipping one file to one place, telling people who already have the problem, and judging the sales you can count.

### Step 1: Finish one file a stranger can use without you on a call

If they need you on a call, you are selling a service. Price the service. [Freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/) and [graphic design](/side-hustles/graphic-design-side-hustle-canada/) are that invoice.

### Step 2: Read the fee page before you pick the price

Write 6.5% and 0.50% down if the shop is Etsy, then open the payments policy for your processing rate. For Notion or Gumroad, use the pricing guide instead of this paragraph.

### Step 3: Use a photo or a preview you made

Your own test print, your own screen recording, or your own sample photo. Etsy's creativity page and the shop's rules are the constraint, not a mood board you saved.

### Step 4: Give the test a stop date

If the file has no sale by that date, the lesson is the offer or the audience. A second generic file is not a fix. [Best side hustles in Canada](/side-hustles/best-side-hustles-canada/) is the list of work that can pay sooner because you were there.

### Step 5: Keep the payout separate from spending

A first deposit is easy to treat as profit. [Separating business and personal money](/side-hustles/separate-business-personal-money-canada/) is the practical half. What to do after tax is set aside is [side hustle profits](/side-hustles/what-to-do-with-side-hustle-profits-canada/).

## What mistakes do digital-product sellers make in Canada?

Sellers upload a generic file, ignore the fee page, and describe one sale as a business. They also let listings auto-renew on files nobody buys, so the listing fee repeats. They promise updates they will not do. A template for a tool that changes is a maintenance job. The word "passive" skips that job.

Another mistake is buying ads for a file you have not sold once to a person who asked for it. Measure the first sales before you pay for traffic you cannot describe.

## Which tax rules apply to digital product sales in Canada?

Digital product sales in Canada are self-employment income if you are carrying on the activity, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across your taxable supplies, not a special rule for downloads. A printed-book rebate does not make a digital file GST-free. The printables guide points at the CRA page for that distinction.

This is not tax, legal, or financial advice. Recheck Etsy's fees policy and the regulatory operating fee page before you price from the percentages above. The [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) is the short version of the questions people mix up.
