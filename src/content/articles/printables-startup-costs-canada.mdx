Selling printables in Canada is one of the cheapest side hustles to start. The product is a PDF, so there's no inventory and no shipping. What you pay for is design software, paper and ink to test that your file prints properly, and shop fees. This guide prices each item from a named seller on October 9, 2026, builds three kits with HST, and works out how many sales cover them.

How to choose a niche and make a file you have the right to sell is in the [printables side hustle guide](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the budget.

## How much does it cost to start selling printables in Canada?

Selling printables in Canada costs about $25 to start if you already own a computer and printer, about $274 with a new inkjet printer and a year of Canva Pro, and about $452 with a laser printer instead. All three use prices checked on October 9, 2026, 13% Ontario HST on goods and software, and Etsy listing fees converted at the [Bank of Canada's](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) October 8, 2026 rate of 1.4240.

| Kit | Cash to start | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $24.56 | Test paper, Canva Free, 10 Etsy listings |
| Standard | $273.72 | Inkjet printer, paper, cardstock, Canva Pro for a year, 20 listings |
| Pro | $451.76 | Laser printer, paper, cardstock, Canva Pro for a year, 50 listings |

None of the totals include Etsy's one-time set-up fee, ink refills, or your time designing files.

## Who should use a printables startup budget in Canada?

This budget suits people who can design a clean, useful page, such as a budget sheet, a planner, a checklist or a classroom worksheet, and who are willing to make several files before sales pick up. The lean kit suits a first test on equipment you already own. The standard and pro kits make sense once you're designing regularly and want to check how pages look on paper.

Printables are a poor fit if you need money soon. Sales usually build slowly from many listings. They're also a poor fit if your idea relies on someone else's characters, logos or artwork.

## What equipment do you need to sell printables in Canada?

You need a computer, design software and a way to print test copies. Prices below were read on Amazon.ca product pages on October 9, 2026.

| Item | Product and price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computer | One you already own: $0 | A tablet isn't required |
| Test paper | [HP Copy&Print paper, 20 lb, 500 sheets](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B001AFL8GY), $19.21 | Letter size, the size most Canadian buyers print on |
| Cardstock | [Neenah Premium Cardstock, 65 lb, 250 sheets](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B00006IDRW), $25.99 | For testing cards, tags and planner covers |
| Inkjet printer | [HP DeskJet 2955 All-in-One](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0GKJZ864R), $49.99 | Prints colour; ink is a repeat cost |
| Laser printer | [Brother HL-L2465DW monochrome laser](https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B0DBRTG1RJ), $199.99 | Black and white only; suits worksheets and trackers |

**Why test prints matter.** Buyers print at home, usually on letter paper. A file that looks fine on screen can have margins that get cut off, colours that print dark, or text that's too small. Print every file before you list it.

**Inkjet or laser.** The DeskJet costs less up front and prints colour, but ink is an ongoing cost. Check the cartridge model on the printer's own listing before you buy refills. The Brother laser prints only black and white, which suits worksheets, checklists and trackers. If you already own a printer, or can print a few test pages at a library, you don't need either.

## What software do you need to make printables?

You can start with free software. Canva's free plan includes templates, fonts and PDF export. Prices from [Canva's Canadian pricing page](https://www.canva.com/en_ca/pricing/), checked October 9, 2026, exclude tax:

| Plan | Price | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canva Free | $0 | Templates, 5 GB of storage, PDF export |
| Canva Pro | $142 CAD a year, one person | Premium templates and assets, brand kits, background remover, 100 GB of storage |
| Canva Business | $260 CAD a year per person | Team features you don't need when selling alone |

**Read the licence before you sell.** Canva templates and elements come with licence terms. Selling a design to the public as a template is a different permission from making one design for yourself. Read Canva's content licence before you upload a file built from its elements. Your own drawings and your own text are the safest material. The Canadian Intellectual Property Office explains [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright).

## What does it cost to sell printables on Etsy and Gumroad?

Etsy charges per listing and per sale. Gumroad charges only per sale. Fees below were checked on October 9, 2026 and are covered in more detail in the [printables pricing guide](/side-hustles/printables-pricing-canada/).

| Fee | Etsy | Gumroad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To list | US$0.20 per listing, about $0.28, for 4 months | $0 |
| Per sale | 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + $0.25 processing for Canadian and US buyers, and a 0.50% regulatory operating fee in Canada | 10% + 50¢ per sale from your own link or profile ([Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing)) |
| Repeat listing fee | US$0.20 each time a listing sells and renews | None |
| To open the shop | One-time set-up fee, shown during set-up | None |

The [printables pricing guide](/side-hustles/printables-pricing-canada/) works out that Etsy keeps about 10% of the price plus about $0.53 per order, so a Canadian seller nets about **0.90 × price − $0.53**. A $5 file nets about $3.97. Etsy's [fees help page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) says the set-up fee is one-time, non-refundable and varies by location, and it doesn't publish the Canadian amount.

## How many sales does it take to break even on printables?

**Illustrative. The $5, $8 and $12 prices are assumptions for the arithmetic, not typical results. Etsy net per sale uses the printables pricing guide's formula, 0.90 × price − $0.53, which already includes the US$0.20 renewal on each sale. Verify the arithmetic for your own prices.**

| Kit | Cost to cover | Sales at $5 (nets $3.97) | Sales at $8 (nets $6.67) | Sales at $12 (nets $10.27) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $24.56 | 7 | 4 | 3 |
| Standard | $273.72 | 69 | 42 | 27 |
| Pro | $451.76 | 114 | 68 | 44 |

Each figure is the cost divided by the net per sale, rounded up. For example, $273.72 ÷ $3.97 = 68.95, so 69 sales.

**What the table shows.** Fixed fees take a larger share of cheap files: $0.53 is 10.6% of a $5 sale before the percentage fees. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/printables-pricing-canada/) shows published Canadian prices, from $2.99 single templates to $47–$67 bundles. A bundle of related pages usually covers the kit with far fewer sales than single sheets.

**Time isn't in the table.** If designing and testing a file takes 5 hours, that time is a cost too. The break-even here covers cash only, before income tax and CPP.

## What are the ongoing costs of selling printables?

Ongoing costs are Etsy's per-sale fees and renewals, Canva Pro if you keep it ($142 a year), ink and paper for testing new files, and any refunds. Etsy listings expire after 4 months unless they renew, at US$0.20 each time. Offsite Ads can also take a share of some sales; the [printables pricing guide](/side-hustles/printables-pricing-canada/) covers that fee.

Things that don't show on a receipt:

- **Refunds** for files that don't print properly. Test every file.
- **Copyright takedowns.** A file built on someone else's artwork can be removed, and the listing fee is lost.
- **Ads.** Optional. Don't advertise a file you haven't test-printed.

## Where can printables startup costs be lower in Canada?

Printables startup costs stay lower when you use Canva Free, test print on a printer you already own or at a library, and list a small number of files first. Skip mockup subscriptions. A photo of your own test print shows buyers the real result. Open one shop, not two, until the first one is selling.

## Where shouldn't you cut printables costs?

Don't skip the test print, the copyright check, or reading the fee page. A PDF that looks fine on a phone and gets cut off on letter paper leads to refunds. A character, photo or layout you copied can get your listing removed.

If a file is labelled for Canadian buyers, such as a school planner, make sure it fits. Provincial school calendars differ, so point buyers to their own province's calendar rather than copying dates. Don't invent sales numbers or reviews in listings.

## Which tax rules apply to printables startup costs in Canada?

Printables income in Canada is self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net business income. Paper, ink, software and shop fees are generally current expenses. A printer that lasts for years is usually claimed through capital cost allowance on the business-use portion.

You must register for GST/HST once your taxable supplies pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, counting all your businesses. Digital products have their own rules, and marketplaces may collect tax on some sales. Read the CRA's [digital economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html), and check rates with the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) and [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) pages. A printable isn't a printed book, so don't assume the [point-of-sale rebate on books](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-065/point-sale-rebate-on-books.html) applies.

This is not tax or legal advice.
