## Which shovel should a snow removal route in Canada start with?

A snow removal route in Canada should start with a snow shovel you can push, plus a smaller one for steps. [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/ergonomics/snow_shovelling.html) says fresh snow is lighter, so shovel early and often, push rather than lift, and if you must throw, turn your feet instead of twisting at the waist.

Do not throw snow over your shoulder or to the side. Take smaller loads.
