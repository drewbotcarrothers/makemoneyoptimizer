## What should the first house cleaning offer in Canada say?

The first house cleaning offer in Canada should list the rooms, the surfaces you will not touch, who brings products, how long you expect, the first-visit price, the later visit price, and what happens if they cancel the morning of. Say whether you bring supplies or use theirs. Their products make sense when they have a scent limit or a stone surface you should not guess at. A spot test beats a ruined tub.
