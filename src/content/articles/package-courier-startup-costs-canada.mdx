## How do ongoing costs change package courier break-even in Canada?

Ongoing costs for package courier work in Canada are fuel, wear, failed-delivery kilometres, small replacements, phone data, insurance, and unpaid waiting at the store. Hours for package courier work start when you arrive for pickup, not when you tap complete on the last stop. [Spark Driver Canada](https://www.sparkdriverapp.ca/content/sparkdriverca/en_ca.html) says you are paid for each completed delivery and that you choose offers, and the page does not publish a wage.

**Fuel and wear.** Highway blocks burn more than neighbourhood hops. Use your consumption and the price you paid, and include the empty drive back to the store or home. A planning set-aside per kilometre keeps wear money from getting spent. The tax claim, if you make one, is usually actual expenses times a business-use percentage from a log.

**Failed deliveries.** The drive back to return a parcel is unpaid distance. Log it on that block.

**Supplies that disappear.** Straps, gloves, and a blanket wear out. Budget a small replacement, not a new kit every month. Often $0 in a quiet month and $20–$40 when something tears. That band is a range to verify locally.

**Platform pay.** Where tips exist, confirm the current in-app terms. The Play listing says Spark drivers keep 100% of confirmed customer tips. Where Amazon Flex operates, read the amount and the time on the offer in the Canadian app before you accept. The time can run long. Leave U.S. block rates out of a Canadian budget.

**Phone and insurance.** Data add-on $0–$30 if you need it, a range to verify locally. Insurance is the monthly number from your quote, for the province where the car is plated.

**Waiting.** Arrival at the store until the order exists is part of the shift. Waiting has no receipt and still counts in the hourly math.

### 4. Add the one-time startup cash

That cash is the numerator when you ask how many blocks earn the kit back.

### 5. Add the monthly fixed costs

Monthly fixed costs are the insurance difference and the phone.

### 6. Subtract variable cost from the block pay

Variable cost of one block is fuel, a wear set-aside, parking, and the return kilometres. Contribution = pay for the completed block, plus any tip the app actually confirmed, minus variable cost. Blocks to recover gear = startup ÷ contribution.

You spent $240 on a folding dolly, bins, gloves, and a mount. Insurance in this sketch is $60 a month extra. One block pays $85. You drove 70 km including the drive back. At 8.5 L/100 km and $1.55/L, fuel is 70 × 0.085 × 1.55 = $9.22. Wear at $0.12/km is $8.40. No parking fee.

Variable cost = $17.62. Contribution = $85 − $17.62 = $67.38. You were out for 3 hours including a 40-minute wait. This sketch is about $22 per hour before the monthly insurance.

Gear payback = $240 ÷ $67.38, or about four blocks like this one. The $60 insurance takes roughly one such block a month. A block that pays $85 and sends you 140 km, or makes you return three failed stops, may not clear fuel and wear. Decline patterns you have now measured. ## How do taxes and vehicle claims work for package courier work in Canada?

Package courier income in Canada is generally self-employment income, GST/HST follows the small-supplier test, and the vehicle claim is the business portion from a kilometre log. This guide is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. Package delivery is not the ride-sharing first-fare rule.

Keep offer summaries and receipts. A T4A may arrive. You still report the income if a slip does not. The whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution generally applies to net self-employment income. Report the business on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1.

**Log and CCA.** A kilometre log — date, destination, kilometres, purpose — supports the business-use percentage of the vehicle. Apply it to fuel, insurance, and repairs. Leave the family car off a 100 percent claim. Bins and gloves are ordinary expenses. A vehicle, trailer, or other lasting asset is capital. [Capital cost allowance (CCA)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) recovers that cost over time on the business portion, in the class for that year. Leave a full write-off of the car out of the month you deliver the first parcel. Confirm the class when you file.

**GST/HST.** The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters, including associates and your other side hustles. The CRA explains the timing on its [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) page. If you also carry passengers for a platform, those fares follow the first-fare rule on the CRA’s [ride-sharing page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html). Package courier work does not.

**Input tax credits.** Registrants can often claim an input tax credit for GST/HST paid on business purchases used in commercial activity. Keep receipts that show the tax. Non-registrants do not charge GST/HST and do not claim the credit.

Charge the provincial rate once you are registered. Quebec adds QST; confirm the current QST rate with Revenu Québec. Some provinces use HST, including Nova Scotia at 14% as of 1 April 2025 ([Charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)). Others charge GST and a separate PST. Use that table or the CRA [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) and set the tax aside.

Zones, background checks, and any municipal licence are local. Read the Canadian app and your city’s business-licence page. Leave a fee you have not looked up out of the budget. Refresh the Spark list, the Flex locations page, and the tax rates when you next update this budget.
