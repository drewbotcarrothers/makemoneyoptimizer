Etsy is a strong fit for Canadians selling handmade goods, vintage items, craft supplies, and digital downloads to buyers who are already searching for them. As of October 14, 2026, a typical Canadian sale carries a 6.5% transaction fee, a 3% + $0.25 CAD payment processing fee on domestic or US orders, a 0.50% regulatory operating fee, and the 0.20 USD listing fee ([Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy)). Offsite Ads can add 12% to 15% on sales they drive. Etsy does not publish typical shop earnings, and this review does not estimate them.

Fees come from Etsy's help centre, rechecked October 14, 2026. USD amounts are converted at the Bank of Canada rate of 1.4222 CAD per USD for October 13, 2026 ([Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/)); Etsy itself converts at the market rate when the fee posts. For setup and strategy, see the [Etsy shop guide](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-side-hustle-canada/), [Etsy shop pricing](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/), and [Etsy vs Shopify](/side-hustles/etsy-vs-shopify-canada/).

## Is Etsy available to sellers in Canada?

Yes. Canadian sellers open a shop, connect a Canadian bank account, and receive deposits through Etsy Payments. Etsy charges a one-time, non-refundable set-up fee that varies by location and is shown during shop setup. Etsy's [payment account page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015747228-How-to-Manage-Your-Payment-Account) says sellers outside the US must add their bank account within 30 days of their first sale and verify it within 60 days, or the shop will be suspended.

Several small fees per sale, plus optional advertising. As listed on October 14, 2026:

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers: a Canadian seller lists in CAD with a CAD payment account and sells a $40 CAD item with $10 CAD shipping to a buyer in Canada. No Offsite Ads, and processing is calculated on $50 for simplicity (Etsy applies it to the total including any tax you charge).

- Listing: 0.20 USD × 1.4222 ≈ $0.28
- Transaction: $50 × 6.5% = $3.25
- Processing: $50 × 3% + $0.25 = $1.75
- Regulatory operating fee: $50 × 0.50% = $0.25
- Total Etsy fees: $0.28 + $3.25 + $1.75 + $0.25 = $5.53, before GST/HST on those fees
- Left before materials and postage: $50 − $5.53 = $44.47

### What does a shop keep each month after all costs?

**Illustrative example.** Assumed: every order is C$50 including shipping, listed in CAD with a CAD payment account, materials cost C$12 per order and postage C$8 per order. Etsy fees use the sourced rates above, which collapse to 10% of the order plus about $0.53 (processing's $0.25 plus one listing renewal at about $0.28), or **$5.53 per order**. The large shop also has 20% of its orders come through Offsite Ads at the 12% rate that applies once a shop has made $10,000 in the past 365 days.

| Shop size | Orders/month | Sales | Etsy fees | Offsite Ads | Materials | Postage | Kept before tax and your time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 10 | $500 | $55.30 | $0 | $120 | $80 | **$244.70** |
| Medium | 50 | $2,500 | $276.50 | $0 | $600 | $400 | **$1,223.50** |
| Large | 200 | $10,000 | $1,106.00 | $240.00 | $2,400 | $1,600 | **$4,654.00** |

Math: Etsy fees = orders × $5.53; Offsite Ads = 40 orders × $50 × 12% = $240. Fees take about 11% of sales at the light and medium levels and about 13% at the large level once ads kick in. GST/HST charged on Etsy's seller fees is not included, and none of this counts your hours. Etsy does not publish typical shop earnings; these numbers only show how the fees stack.

## How do Etsy payouts work in Canadian dollars?

Etsy deducts fees from your sales and deposits the rest to your bank on your chosen schedule, in your payment account currency. Etsy says new sellers generally have funds available 14 days after a sale, while established shops may be eligible as soon as the next business day. Adding or changing bank details triggers a five-day security hold. If your bank account is in a different currency from your payment account, Etsy still pays in the payment account currency ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015747228-How-to-Manage-Your-Payment-Account)). Listing in CAD with a CAD payment account avoids the 2.5% conversion fee.

## How does Etsy handle GST/HST and PST for Canadian sellers?

Since July 1, 2022, Etsy collects and remits GST/HST on orders delivered to Canadian buyers when the seller has not entered a GST/HST number, and it collects B.C. PST, Saskatchewan PST, and Manitoba RST on applicable orders regardless ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/6633345416215-What-Does-Etsy-Remit-to-Canadian-Tax-Authorities)). If you are registered for GST/HST and enter your number, you handle GST/HST on those sales yourself. Whether you need to register is covered in [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

## What are the pros and cons of Etsy for Canadians?

**Pros**
- Built-in buyer traffic searching for handmade, vintage and digital goods.
- Low cost to start: 0.20 USD per listing.
- Processing priced in CAD for Canadian sellers.
- Etsy handles GST/HST and some provincial taxes on many Canadian orders.

**Cons**
- Fees stack: transaction, processing, regulatory, and possibly Offsite Ads.
- Listing fees are in USD, so the CAD cost moves with the exchange rate.
- New sellers wait about 14 days for funds.
- Heavy competition and Etsy's rules limit how you brand and contact buyers.

## Who is Etsy best for in Canada?

Etsy suits Canadian makers, vintage sellers, and digital product creators who want buyers without building traffic from scratch. ## What mistakes do Canadians make on Etsy?

- **Pricing without every fee.** Add transaction, processing, regulatory and possible Offsite Ads fees; see [Etsy shop pricing](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/).
- **Listing in USD with a CAD payment account.** That triggers the 2.5% conversion fee.
- **Ignoring Offsite Ads.** Know the rate that applies to your shop.
- **Missing the bank verification deadline.** Etsy can suspend shops that don't verify within 60 days.
- **Forgetting shipping costs.** The transaction fee applies to shipping you charge too.

## The caveat: fees change and ads can grow with your shop

Etsy adjusts its fees and policies periodically, and the USD listing fee moves with the exchange rate. Offsite Ads rules depend on your sales over the past 365 days, and whether you can opt out is set by Etsy's Offsite Ads policy, so a growing shop should budget for the 12% rate on some orders rather than assume zero. Recheck the help-centre pages before setting prices.

More pitfalls are in [Etsy shop mistakes](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-side-hustle-canada/#what-mistakes-do-canadian-etsy-sellers-make).

## How is Etsy income taxed in Canada?

Regular Etsy selling is business income reported on Form T2125, with Etsy fees, materials and shipping as expenses. Etsy is a platform operator under Canada's [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html), which require platforms to report seller information and income to the CRA. Etsy's monthly statements and CSV downloads make bookkeeping easier; see [taxes on reselling and platform income](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).
