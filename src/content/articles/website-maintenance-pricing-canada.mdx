## Which pricing models work for website maintenance in Canada?

Website maintenance in Canada is usually sold as tiered monthly plans, hourly work, or a prepaid block of hours. Every provider above uses monthly plans. Sparked Digital also sells hour blocks.

| Model | Example | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly care plan | Tiffany Mark, $59–$169 | Small business sites | List included tasks and edit limits |
| Plan with capped updates | Dot832, 2 or 5 updates | Clients who edit often | Define what one "update" is |
| Prepaid hour block | Sparked Digital, 5 h for $599 | Irregular but steady work | Set a rollover cap |
| Hourly, on demand | Sparked Digital, $150/h | One-off fixes | Clients wait until things break |
| Annual prepay | Jennifer Leigh, 11 months' price | Cash flow and retention | Refund terms if the client leaves |

Hosting and domain renewals are best on the client's own account, so the client keeps the site if you part ways.

## How do you calculate a website maintenance price in Canada?

To price a care plan, work out the hours a site needs each month, multiply by your hourly rate, then add tool costs and a buffer.

**Monthly plan = (routine hours + included edit hours) × hourly rate + tools per site + buffer**

1. **Routine hours.** Count updates, backups and a test restore, uptime and security checks, and a short report. Time a real site for two or three months before you set a price.
2. **Included edit hours.** Cap them, as Jennifer Leigh does with half an hour and WP Expert with one hour.
3. **Hourly rate.** Use something above Job Bank's $38.46 median and within the hourly range above. This guide assumes $75, within DoodleWeb's $50 to $99 range and half of Sparked Digital's on-demand rate.
4. **Tools per site.** The [website maintenance guide](/side-hustles/website-maintenance-side-hustle-canada/) priced WP Umbrella plus its security add-on at US$4.19 per site a month, or $5.97 at 1.4240. ManageWP's core dashboard is free.
5. **Buffer.** Add about 10% of labour for updates that break something.
6. **Tax and CPP.** Self-employed people pay both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html). See the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do you raise website maintenance prices in Canada?

Raise website maintenance prices when a client's tracked hours regularly go over the plan, when tool costs rise, or when your client list is full.

- **Review hours per client every quarter.** A client who always uses three hours on a one-hour plan belongs on a bigger plan.
- **Give written notice.** Jennifer Leigh's plans require 30 days' written notice to cancel. Give clients at least the same notice before you change the price.
- **Watch the exchange rate.** Many tools bill in US dollars, so a weaker Canadian dollar raises your costs.
- **Move store sites to a store plan.** Alma Web Pro's WooCommerce plan starts at five times its basic plan, which reflects the extra risk.

## What templates help with website maintenance pricing?

**Plan proposal:**

> Here's what I'd suggest for [site]. The Standard plan is $[170] a month. It includes WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, backups with a monthly test restore, uptime and security monitoring, a monthly report and up to [1] hour of edits. Extra work is $[75] an hour in 15-minute increments. Unused edit time doesn't roll over. Hosting and the domain stay in your name. If you'd prefer to pay yearly, it's $[1,870] (11 months' price).

**Scope lines for the agreement:**

> Included: [list]. Not included: redesigns, new features, content writing, hacked-site cleanup beyond restoring a backup (billed hourly).
> Emergency means the site is down or showing an error to visitors. Response: within [4] business hours.
> Either party can end the plan with 30 days' written notice.

**Price change notice:**

> Hi [name], from [date], your care plan goes from $[89] to $[99] a month because [tool costs / hours used]. Your last three months averaged [2.1] hours against the 1 hour included. Nothing else changes. Reply if you'd like to talk about a different plan.

## What website maintenance pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common website maintenance pricing mistakes in Canada are unlimited edits with no time limit, no definition of an emergency, and holding the client's hosting and logins in your own name. When providers do offer unlimited updates, they set limits: WP Expert caps each task at 30 minutes, one at a time, and Dot832 limits it to "small" updates.

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security's [top 10 IT security actions](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/top-10-it-security-actions) include patching operating systems and applications and managing administrative privileges. In a care plan, list updates and admin-account reviews as included tasks, not extras. Sites that collect customer details also raise privacy duties. [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) applies to commercial activity, and Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec have substantially similar laws. Test your restores too. A backup you've never restored may not work when you need it.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect website maintenance prices in Canada?

Website maintenance income in Canada is self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). You must register for GST/HST once your taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), either in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters. That total counts all your businesses together. Recurring plans can reach the threshold steadily: 28 clients at $89 is $2,492 a month, or $29,904 a year. Track the running total.

Once you're registered, charge the rate for the client's location, using the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). For example, 13% HST for an Ontario client or 5% GST for an Alberta client ($89 × 1.05 = $93.45).
