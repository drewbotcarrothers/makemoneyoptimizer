## Which pricing models work for website maintenance in Canada?

Website maintenance in Canada is usually sold as tiered monthly plans, hourly work, or a prepaid block of hours. Monthly plans give you recurring income and give the client a known cost.

| Model | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly care plan | Small business sites that need regular updates | List included tasks and hours; unused hours usually do not roll over |
| Hourly | One-off fixes, irregular clients | Clients wait until things break |
| Prepaid hour block | Clients with occasional edits | Set an expiry date |
| Emergency rate | After-hours fixes | Define what counts as an emergency |

Hosting and domain renewals are best on the client's own account, so the client keeps the site if you part ways.

## How do you calculate a website maintenance price in Canada?

Calculate a website maintenance price in Canada from the monthly hours for routine tasks plus included edit time, times your hourly rate, plus the cost of any tools you pay for on the client's behalf.

### Step 1: List the routine tasks

Updates to the platform, themes, and plugins; backups and a test restore; uptime and security checks; a short monthly report.

### Step 2: Time each task per month

Time a real site for two or three months before you fix a price.

### Step 3: Add included edit time

Small text or image changes, usually a set number of hours.

### Step 4: Add tool costs and a buffer

Backup or monitoring tools you pay for, and a buffer for update problems.

### Step 5: Set aside tax and CPP

Self-employed people pay both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose a small business site needs 1 hour a month of updates and checks, 0.5 hours for a backup test and report, and 1 hour of included edits: 1 + 0.5 + 1 = 2.5 hours. At an assumed $50 an hour, between Job Bank's median and high, labour is 2.5 × $50 = $125.

Add $10 a month of tools (assumed) and a 10% buffer on labour ($12.50): $125 + $10 + $12.50 = $147.50, rounded to $150 a month. Edits beyond the included hour bill at $50 an hour.

## When should you raise website maintenance prices in Canada?

Raise website maintenance prices in Canada when your tracked hours regularly exceed the plan, when tool costs rise, or when your calendar is full. Give current clients written notice, such as 30 days, before a monthly fee changes.

Review hours per client each quarter. A client who always uses three hours on a one-hour plan belongs on a bigger plan.

## What website maintenance pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common website maintenance pricing mistakes in Canada are unlimited edits, no definition of an emergency, and holding the client's hosting and logins in your own name.

The Canadian Centre for Cyber Security's [top 10 IT security actions](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/top-10-it-security-actions) include patching operating systems and applications and enforcing the management of administrative privileges. In a care plan, that means listing updates and admin-account reviews as included tasks rather than extras. Sites that collect customer details also raise privacy duties; [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) applies to commercial activity, with substantially similar laws in Alberta, British Columbia, and Quebec. Another mistake is skipping test restores: a backup that has never been restored is untested.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect website maintenance prices in Canada?

Website maintenance income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses. Recurring plans can reach that threshold steadily, so track the running total.
