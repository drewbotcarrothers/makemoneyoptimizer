A 30-day Etsy shop launch plan for Canada uses week 1 to choose products and do fee math, week 2 for stock, photos, and listings, week 3 to open and share the shop, and week 4 to fulfil orders and review margins. It suits makers and designers who can produce consistent items, photograph them well, and keep careful cost records.

## What happens in week 1 of an Etsy launch plan in Canada?

Week 1 is product and money: choose what to sell, cost it, and understand Etsy's fees before setting prices.

### Days 1 and 2: Choose a small product line

Pick 5 to 10 related items you can make or source consistently. A focused shop is easier to describe and photograph than a mixed one.

### Days 3 and 4: Cost every item

List materials, packaging, and your time per item. Include shipping supplies.

### Days 5 to 7: Learn Etsy's fees

Etsy's [fees page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) lists a $0.20 listing fee (fixed fees are published in USD and converted), a listing life of four months, and a 6.5% transaction fee on the order total including shipping and gift wrap. It says payment processing fees vary by country, that a regulatory operating fee applies to sellers in Canada, that a one-time shop set-up fee varies by location, and that Offsite Ads fees are 15% (or 12% for sellers over $10,000 in the past 365 days) when a sale comes from those ads. Build these into your [prices](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/).

## What happens in week 2 of an Etsy launch plan in Canada?

Week 2 is stock and listings: make inventory, photograph it, and write listings and policies.

### Days 8 to 10: Make or source starting stock

Make a small quantity of each item. For made-to-order items, time how long one takes so processing times are honest.

### Days 11 and 12: Photograph

Shoot in daylight on a clean background, with scale and detail shots. Simple graphics for banners can be made in <AffiliateLink program="canva" label="Canva" /> or any free design tool.

### Days 13 and 14: Write listings and policies

Write accurate titles and descriptions with materials, dimensions, and care. The Competition Bureau says the Competition Act prohibits [false or misleading claims](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) in a material respect, so avoid claims you cannot back up. Set processing time, shipping, and return policies.

## What happens in week 3 of an Etsy launch plan in Canada?

Week 3 is opening: set up the shop, publish listings, and tell people who want to hear from you.

### Days 15 and 16: Open the shop

Complete shop setup, payment details, and the about section. Pay any set-up fee Etsy shows for your location.

### Days 17 to 19: Publish listings

Publish all listings, check each on mobile, and fix typos and photo order.

### Days 20 and 21: Share the shop

Share with friends, family, and followers who have asked to hear from you. Local [holiday craft markets](/side-hustles/holiday-etsy-craft-market-shop-canada/) can also send buyers to the shop.

## What happens in week 4 of an Etsy launch plan in Canada?

Week 4 is fulfilment and review.

### Days 22 to 24: Ship first orders well

Pack carefully and ship within your stated processing time. Canada Post says its [Solutions for Small Business](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/small-business.page) membership is free; compare its rates with Etsy labels where available.

### Days 25 and 26: Ask for feedback

Include a thank-you note. Reply to messages quickly and fix any problem a buyer raises.

### Days 27 to 30: Review margins

For each sale, subtract materials, packaging, shipping, and every Etsy fee from the sale price. Raise prices or drop items that do not earn. If sales grow, compare [Etsy vs Shopify](/side-hustles/etsy-vs-shopify-canada/).

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a forecast: an item sells for $30 with $6 shipping charged, so the order total is $36. The 6.5% transaction fee is 0.065 × $36 = $2.34. Add the $0.20 USD listing fee, which Etsy converts at the market rate, plus payment processing and the regulatory operating fee from Etsy's live pages. Subtract materials and actual postage to see what is left.

## What Etsy launch mistakes should you avoid in Canada?

Common launch mistakes are pricing before learning the fees, listing too many unrelated items, using unclear photos, promising processing times you cannot meet, and ignoring postage costs. More are in [Etsy shop mistakes](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-mistakes-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply when launching an Etsy shop in Canada?

Etsy shop profit is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and the self-employed pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. GST/HST registration applies once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single quarter or over four consecutive quarters. Read Etsy's help pages on taxes to see how sales tax is handled on your orders. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
