## What are the best side hustles without a car in Canada?

The best car-free side hustles in Canada either come to your neighbourhood or need no travel at all. Pick based on how you'll get to the work.

### On foot: neighbourhood services

[Dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) is the classic car-free hustle, because clients are the neighbours you can walk to. If you'd rather start on an app, compare [Rover vs independent dog walking](/side-hustles/rover-vs-independent-dog-walking-canada/). [Babysitting](/side-hustles/babysitting-side-hustle-canada/) and [pet and house sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/) also happen at the client's home. Some cities regulate larger groups: Toronto's [commercial dog walker permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) covers walking four to six dogs and requires $2,000,000 of commercial general liability insurance, so check your city's rules if you plan to walk several dogs at once.

### On transit: home services with light gear

[House cleaning](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) works if you build a route near transit and many clients already own a vacuum. [Window cleaning](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) for ground-floor windows and [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) with a shovel on your own street can also work within walking distance.

### By bike or e-bike: delivery

[Bike and e-bike delivery](/side-hustles/bike-courier-side-hustle-canada/) and [food delivery](/side-hustles/food-delivery-side-hustle-canada/) only work where your app offers bike or foot delivery. Uber's [delivery page](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/deliver/) says you can deliver by car, scooter, bike, or on foot in Canada, that bike and foot couriers must be at least 18 or 19 depending on the province, and that delivery people must pass a background screening. If you use an e-bike in Ontario, the province's [e-bike page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/riding-e-bike) says riders must be 16 or older, wear an approved bicycle or motorcycle helmet, and stay within a maximum assisted speed of 32 km/h. Other provinces have their own rules. To compare apps, read [Uber Eats vs DoorDash vs Skip](/side-hustles/uber-eats-vs-doordash-vs-skip-canada/).

### No travel at all: online work

[Freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/), [virtual assistant work](/side-hustles/virtual-assistant-side-hustle-canada/), [online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/), and [graphic design](/side-hustles/graphic-design-side-hustle-canada/) only need a computer and internet. Digital products such as [printables](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/) and [Notion templates](/side-hustles/notion-templates-side-hustle-canada/) sell without any delivery.

### Selling locally without a car

[Reselling](/side-hustles/reselling-side-hustle-canada/) works if buyers pick up from you or you ship small items from a nearby post office. Bulky [furniture flipping](/side-hustles/furniture-flipping-side-hustle-canada/) is hard without a vehicle.

## Which side hustles need a car in Canada?

Side hustles that need a car or truck in Canada are the ones that haul equipment or cover long distances: rideshare driving, most grocery and package delivery, junk removal, moving help with a truck, pressure washing, lawn care with a mower, and car detailing at the client's home. Rideshare and car-based delivery also need insurance that covers the work. If you're getting a car later, those guides start at [rideshare driving](/side-hustles/rideshare-driving-side-hustle-canada/) and [package courier](/side-hustles/package-courier-side-hustle-canada/).

## What mistakes do people make with car-free side hustles in Canada?

The common car-free side hustle mistakes in Canada are taking clients too far away, underestimating winter, and ignoring bike rules.

- **Travel time eats your hourly rate.** Keep a tight radius; unpaid transit time is real time.
- **Winter changes everything.** Snow and cold make walking and biking routes slower; dress for it and price accordingly.
- **E-bike rules vary.** Check the provincial rules before you buy or modify an e-bike.
- **Carrying too much.** Choose services where the client supplies the heavy equipment.
- **No lights or lock.** For bike delivery, a quality <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="bike light set" search="rechargeable bike light set front rear" /> and a solid lock come before speed.

## Which tax rules apply to car-free side hustles in Canada?

Car-free side hustle income in Canada is taxed the same way as any self-employment income: report it on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and register for GST/HST once taxable sales pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Business costs such as delivery bags, cleaning supplies, or a work phone plan may be deductible; see [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/) and [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).
