## Which pricing models work for local SEO?

Sell a fixed-price audit or profile clean-up first, then a monthly retainer per location with a short minimum term.

| Model | How it works | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Profile setup or clean-up | Fixed price to claim, complete and correct a Google Business Profile | Storyteller Media $345; IshaanEx $297 |
| Audit | Fixed price for a written action list | IshaanEx and Cressoft offer free audits as lead-ins |
| Monthly retainer | Set deliverables each month | Keelroom Foundation $499 |
| Tiered retainers | More keywords, pages and content per tier | IshaanEx $397 / $1,000; Keelroom $499 / $997 / $1,497 |
| Setup fee | Charged on the entry tier, waived on upgrade | Keelroom $499 |
| Hourly consulting | Rate × hours, for training an in-house person | First Rank guide: $125–$200 |

Write the deliverables, the minimum term, that the client owns the profile and analytics, and that rankings aren't guaranteed.

## How do you calculate a local SEO rate?

Build an hourly rate, time each deliverable once, and price from the hours.

```text
Hourly rate    = (target pay + CPP + tools per hour) ÷ share of hours billable
Setup or audit = hours × hourly rate
Retainer       = monthly hours × hourly rate
Extra location = extra monthly hours × hourly rate (shared reporting counted once)
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $45/hour (between the $38.46 median and $57.16 high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $5.36 |
| Tools (rank tracking, citations, reporting) per hour | $2.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 70% |
| **Rate** | ($45 + $5.36 + $2.00) ÷ 0.70 = $74.80, **round to $75** |

**Illustrative price sheet** at $75 an hour, before GST/HST:

| Service | Assumed hours | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Business Profile setup or clean-up | 3 h | $225 |
| Local SEO audit with action list | 6 h | $450 |
| Monthly retainer, 1 location | 6 h/month | $450/month |
| Each additional location | 4 h/month | $300/month |
| Location landing page (copy and on-page) | 3 h | $225 |
| Consulting or training | per hour | $75 |

$225 sits below the $297 to $345 published profile setups, and $450 a month sits between IshaanEx's $397 and Keelroom's $499.

## How should you write a local SEO proposal and invoice?

Put deliverables, ownership and term on one page; invoice the retainer at the start of each month.

```text
LOCAL SEO PROPOSAL — [business]                  Date: [date]
1. Google Business Profile clean-up (one-time) ........ $225
   categories, services, hours, service area, photos, description
2. Monthly retainer, 1 location ................ $450/month
   - 4 profile posts, review-request link and replies template
   - 5 citation fixes or new listings
   - 1 location or service page update
   - monthly report: calls, direction requests, top searches
Extra location: $300/month. Extra work: $75/hour, quoted first.
Term: 3 months, then month-to-month, 30 days' notice.
You own your profile, website and analytics; I'm added as a manager.
No ranking guarantees. Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ____]
```

## How do you raise local SEO prices?

Raise prices when you can show results for similar businesses, when your hours are full, or at the end of a term.

1. **Add tiers.** IshaanEx and Keelroom both sell a step up with more keywords and content.
2. **Charge a setup fee on the entry plan.** Keelroom charges $499 and waives it on upgrade.
3. **Price locations separately.** Each one adds reporting, pages and citations.
4. **Stop free audits once booked.** Free audits are a lead-in; charge once your calendar fills.
5. **Raise new clients first.** Give retainer clients a month's notice:

```text
Hi [Name], from [January 1] your retainer moves from $[450] to $[500]
a month. Deliverables and reporting stay the same. — [Your name]
```

For moving retainer clients to a new rate, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What local SEO pricing mistakes are common?

- **Guaranteeing rankings.** You don't control the map pack; price deliverables.
- **Owning the client's profile.** Be added as a manager; the business stays owner.
- **Matching $97 launch rates.** Thinkora's is a 10-client portfolio rate, not a market price.
- **Unlimited "small changes."** Write a monthly scope and an hourly rate for extras.
- **No minimum term.** Results take months; a three-month minimum covers the setup work.

For the access and reporting mistakes local SEO freelancers make most, see the [local SEO mistakes guide](/side-hustles/local-seo-side-hustle-canada/#what-mistakes-do-new-local-seo-freelancers-make).

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect local SEO prices?

Local SEO income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to hold back.

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
