## Which pricing models work for local SEO in Canada?

Local SEO in Canada is usually priced as a fixed-price audit, a fixed-price Google Business Profile setup or clean-up, a monthly retainer per location, or hourly consulting.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Audit | Fixed price for a written review and action list | First contact with a business | Giving the fix list away for free |
| Profile setup or clean-up | Fixed price to complete and correct a Google Business Profile and key listings | Owners who will run it themselves | Ownership of the profile (it stays with the client) |
| Monthly retainer | Set hours for citations, review requests, posts, location pages, reporting | Ongoing clients | Scope creep into web development |
| Per-location pricing | Retainer multiplied by locations, with a discount for extras | Multi-location businesses | Underpricing reporting per location |
| Hourly consulting | Rate × hours | Training an in-house person | Open-ended calls |

Write the deliverables, who owns accounts (the client should), the reporting schedule, and that rankings are not guaranteed.

## How do you calculate a local SEO rate in Canada?

Calculate a local SEO rate in Canada from a target hourly pay, your billable share, tool costs, and the hours each deliverable takes. Then price audits, setups, and retainers from those hours.

### Step 1: Set a target hourly pay

The Job Bank table is your floor check.

### Step 2: Estimate billable share

Prospecting, proposals, and keeping up with search changes are unpaid.

### Step 3: Add tools

Rank tracking, citation, and reporting tools, divided across clients or billed through.

### Step 4: Time each deliverable

Time a real audit and a real profile clean-up before fixing their prices.

### Step 5: Hold back tax and CPP

Self-employed people pay both CPP shares; see [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose your target is $45 an hour, between Job Bank's national median of $38.46 and high of $57.16. Suppose 70% of your working time is billable. The billable rate is $45 ÷ 0.70 = $64.29, which you might round to $64 an hour.

Suppose an audit takes 6 hours: 6 × $64 = $384, quoted as $385. A profile clean-up takes 3 hours: 3 × $64 = $192, quoted as $195. A single-location retainer with 8 hours a month is 8 × $64 = $512, quoted as $510 a month.

For a second location, suppose shared work such as reporting and strategy means the extra location needs 5 more hours: 5 × $64 = $320 more, so two locations are $830 a month.

## When should you raise local SEO prices in Canada?

Raise local SEO prices in Canada when your client list is full, when you can show results for similar businesses, or at contract renewal. Change new-client prices first and give retainer clients notice before the next term.

Re-time your deliverables every few months; tools and process changes alter the hours.

## What local SEO pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common local SEO pricing mistakes in Canada are guaranteeing rankings, keeping client accounts in your name, and free audits that hand over the whole plan. Each costs trust or unpaid time.

Price the work you control. Keep the client as owner of their profile and analytics. Charge for audits, or credit them against a retainer.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect local SEO prices in Canada?

Local SEO income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration.
