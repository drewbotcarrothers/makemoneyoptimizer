Pricing printables in Canada means choosing a price per file or bundle, then checking what's left after platform fees. Canadian shops selling their own downloads price single templates around $3 to $15, planners and trackers around $20 to $50, and large classroom bundles $47 to $67, all in Canadian dollars. On Etsy, a Canadian seller keeps about 90% of the price minus about $0.53 per order; on Gumroad, 90% minus US$0.50; on TPT's Basic plan, 55% minus US$0.30. Because the fixed part of each fee stays the same, a $3 file loses more than a quarter of its price to fees, which is why most of the shops below sell bundles.

## How do you calculate a printables price?

Work out net per sale at a few prices, then divide your design time and costs by that net to see how many sales each price needs.

```text
Etsy net per sale    = 0.90 × price − $0.53         (Canadian or US buyer)
Gumroad net per sale = 0.90 × price − US$0.50       (direct link)
TPT Basic net        = 0.55 × price − US$0.30
Break-even sales     = (design hours × hourly target + tool costs) ÷ net per sale
Bundle price         = 40% to 60% of the singles' total  (assumed range; test it)
```

**Count the design time honestly.** Include research, layout, proofreading, mockup images and listing copy. **Spread tool costs.** Canva Pro costs $142 a year in Canada (checked October 9, 2026); spread across 20 listings, that's $7.10 each.

## What do worked printables quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** Design hours, hourly targets and sales assumptions are mine. Fee rules and the exchange rate are the sourced ones.

### Quote 1: a single planner page on Etsy at $5

- Fees: 6.5% × $5 = $0.325; processing 3% × $5 + $0.25 = $0.40; regulatory 0.50% × $5 = $0.025; listing renewal $0.28. Total $1.03, net **$3.97**.
- Design: 2 hours at a $30 hourly target = $60. Break-even: $60 ÷ $3.97 = 15.1, so **16 sales**.

### Quote 2: ten pages as singles or as one bundle on Etsy

- Ten singles at $4: Etsy net $3.07 each, and the $0.93 fee is 23% of each sale.
- One bundle at $18 (45% of the $40 singles total): fees $1.80 + $0.53 = $2.33 (12.9%), net **$15.67** per bundle.
- A buyer who would have bought 3 singles ($9.21 net to you) is worth more as a bundle buyer ($15.67), and you pay one listing renewal instead of three.

### Quote 3: a classroom resource on TPT, Basic vs Exclusive

- Price US$8. Basic: 0.55 × $8 − $0.30 = **US$4.10** (about $5.84 at 1.4240). Exclusive at the 2027 rate: 0.775 × $8 = **US$6.20** (about $8.83).
- The difference is US$2.10 a sale. US$59.95 ÷ $2.10 = 28.5, so Exclusive pays for itself at **29 sales a year**, but only if you're willing to sell those resources on TPT alone.

### Quote 4: a $30 digital planner on Etsy vs Gumroad

- Etsy: 0.90 × $30 − $0.53 = **$26.47**.
- Gumroad direct link, priced at US$21 (about $29.90 at 1.4240): 0.90 × $21 − $0.50 = US$18.40, about **$26.20**.
- Gumroad Discover at US$21: 70% × $21 = US$14.70, about $20.93.
- The two direct options land within 30 cents; the choice is about where buyers find you, not fees. Etsy brings search traffic; Gumroad suits sellers sending their own audience. For a fuller comparison, see [Teachable vs Gumroad vs Payhip](/side-hustles/teachable-vs-gumroad-vs-payhip-canada/).

## How do you raise printables prices?

- **Raise after adding pages or editable versions,** not on an unchanged file. A new version is the clearest reason for a new price.
- **Move singles into bundles.** Super Simple Sheets prices unit bundles at $13 to $15 and full-year bundles at $47 to $67, so the bigger purchase is the obvious one.
- **Price systems higher than pages.** The Line charges $30 for a digital planner and $49 for a budget tracker; a planner priced like a single page signals a single page.
- **Test one listing at a time** and compare net revenue per week, not units sold.
- **Show a "was" price only if you actually charged it.** BBT and Super Simple Sheets show compare-at prices; keep yours honest.
- **Sell commercial use separately** instead of including it free.

## What printables pricing mistakes are common?

- **Pricing single pages so low that fixed fees take most of the sale.** At $2, Etsy's fees are 36.5% ($0.73 of $2).
- **Forgetting the renewal fee on each sale.** An auto-renewing listing pays US$0.20 again every time it sells.
- **Selling commercial use at personal-use prices.**
- **Comparing your price to US shops in US dollars.** A US$5 listing is about $7.12 in Canadian dollars at 1.4240.
- **Never repricing.** Check fees when Etsy, Gumroad or TPT update them; TPT changed its plans on October 1, 2026.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect printables pricing in Canada?

Printables sales in Canada are self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both [CPP shares](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net income. GST/HST registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all your side hustles.

Who collects tax on a sale depends on the platform and the buyer's location. The CRA explains platform rules in its [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html), and the [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) covers sales you make yourself. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration, and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers how much to keep back.
