Charging for freelance graphic design in Canada usually means a fixed project price (a logo package, a set of social templates, a flyer) built from estimated hours at a billable rate, with a set number of revision rounds and usage rights written in. Job Bank lists employed graphic designers at $20.00 (low), $31.25 (median), and $52.88 (high) an hour nationally. A freelancer prices above that employee wage because software, fonts, unpaid admin, and both halves of CPP come out of the same rate. This guide suits designers who will scope a project before naming a number.

## Which pricing models work for graphic design in Canada?

Graphic design in Canada is usually sold as fixed-price projects with a set number of concepts and revisions, hourly for edits and unclear scope, or a monthly retainer for ongoing design needs. Usage rights and file delivery are part of the price.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed project | One price for defined deliverables, concepts, and revision rounds | Logos, brand kits, flyers | Vague briefs that expand |
| Hourly | Rate × hours | Edits, production work | Clients nervous about open bills |
| Monthly retainer | Set hours or deliverables per month | Small businesses with steady needs | Unused hours rolling over |
| Template packs | One price for a set of editable templates | Social media, presentations | Licensing and font rights |
| Add-ons | Extra concepts, extra revisions, rush, print-ready files | Real extra work | Giving away source files without pricing them |

Say what the client receives (formats, source files or not) and what they may do with it. Fonts and stock images may carry their own licences.

## How do you calculate a graphic design rate in Canada?

Calculate a graphic design rate in Canada from a target hourly pay, your billable share, software and asset costs, and the hours a project really takes, including discovery calls and revisions.

### Step 1: Set a target hourly pay

The Job Bank table is your floor check.

### Step 2: Estimate billable share

Portfolio work, pitching, admin, and learning are unpaid. Measure your own split.

### Step 3: Add software and asset costs

Design software, fonts, stock, and hardware wear, divided across billable hours.

### Step 4: Estimate the project hours

Discovery, research, concepts, revision rounds, and file prep. Pad for the client's first feedback round.

### Step 5: Hold back tax and CPP

Self-employed people pay both CPP shares; see [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose your target is $40 an hour, between Job Bank's national median of $31.25 and high of $52.88. Suppose 65% of your working time is billable. The billable rate is $40 ÷ 0.65 = $61.54, which you might round to $62 an hour.

Suppose a small-business logo package (discovery call, three concepts, two revision rounds, final files in colour and one-colour versions) takes 12 hours. The project price is 12 × $62 = $744, which you might quote as $750 with 50% up front.

Suppose a set of 10 editable social media templates takes 6 hours: 6 × $62 = $372, quoted as $375. A third revision round on the logo, if the client wants it, is billed hourly at $62.

## When should you raise graphic design prices in Canada?

Raise graphic design prices in Canada when your calendar books out, when projects keep running over their hours, or when your portfolio supports bigger clients. Raise package prices for new clients first and give retainer clients notice before the next term.

Track actual hours on every project for the first few months. Packages that always run over are underpriced, not unlucky.

## What graphic design pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common graphic design pricing mistakes in Canada are unlimited revisions, starting without a deposit, and handing over source files and full rights without pricing them. Each gives away the most valuable part of the job.

Write a brief and get it approved before concepts. Take a deposit. Price extra concepts and rush work. Check font and stock licences before delivering to a client.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect graphic design prices in Canada?

Freelance graphic design income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers voluntary registration, and the [expense deductions guide](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/) covers software and equipment.
