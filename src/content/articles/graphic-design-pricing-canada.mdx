## Should you charge by the hour or by the project?

Quote fixed project fees for defined work like logos and brand kits, bill hourly for edits and unclear scope, and use a retainer for ongoing work. The [RGD's guide to what designers charge](https://rgd.ca/hiring-designers/what-designers-charge) says project fees focus on the value of the work, and that the quote should change if the scope changes.

| Model | Use it for | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed package | Logos, brand kits | Casa Media House: $900 with 2 concepts and 2 rounds |
| Tiered packages | Clients who want choice | CHRISPS: $1,250 / $2,500 / $4,000 |
| Priced add-ons | Extra concepts, rounds, print pieces | Casa: $300 per extra concept, $150 per extra round |
| Hourly | Edits, production work, unclear briefs | freel.ca: $50 to $85 mid-level |
| Retainer | Monthly social graphics, ongoing support | Hours a month at a set fee, billed in advance |
| Payment terms | Cash flow | Z Design bills 100% on completion; many freelancers take 50% up front |

## How do you calculate a graphic design rate?

Build an hourly rate from target pay, CPP and software, divided by the share of hours you can bill, then estimate each project's hours including calls and revisions.

```text
Hourly rate   = (target pay + CPP + software, fonts and assets per hour)
                ÷ share of hours billable
Project fee   = estimated hours (discovery + concepts + revisions + file prep) × hourly rate
Extra round   = hours per round × hourly rate
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $35/hour (above the $31.25 Job Bank median) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $4.17 |
| Software, fonts, stock and equipment per hour | $3.00 |
| Share of hours billable (pitching, admin, unpaid) | 60% |
| **Rate** | ($35 + $4.17 + $3) ÷ 0.60 = **$70.28, round to $70** |

$70 sits inside freel.ca's $50 to $85 mid-level estimate and well above Upwork's median range.

## How should you write a design proposal and invoice?

Send a written proposal that lists deliverables, rounds, timeline, payment terms and who owns what. The [first client guide](/side-hustles/graphic-design-first-client-canada/) covers the discovery call.

```text
DESIGN PROPOSAL — [client]   Date: [date]
Project: Logo package for [business name]
Includes: discovery call (45 min), 2 logo concepts, 2 revision rounds on the
  chosen concept, final files (AI, SVG, PDF, PNG; colour, one-colour, reversed),
  mini brand kit (colours, fonts, clear space)
Not included: website, print production, trademark search, extra concepts ($280),
  extra rounds ($140 each)
Timeline: concepts in 7 business days; finals 5 business days after approval
Fee: $850. Deposit $425 to start; $425 before final files
Ownership: on full payment, client owns the final logo; I may show it in my portfolio
Fonts: licensed fonts are the client's to buy if needed beyond the logo
Kill fee: if cancelled after concepts, the deposit is kept
Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ______]
```

```text
INVOICE #2026-018   Date: [date]
Logo package, final payment (50%) ............. $425.00
Extra revision round .......................... $140.00
Subtotal ...................................... $565.00
HST 13% (registered only) ..................... $73.45
Total ......................................... $638.45
```

## How do you raise graphic design prices?

Raise prices for new clients first, give existing clients 30 days' notice, and use the structures studios already publish.

1. **Sell tiers.** CHRISPS's $1,250 / $2,500 / $4,000 ladder moves clients up a level instead of down to a discount.
2. **Cap concepts and rounds, then price extras.** Casa charges $300 per extra concept and $150 per extra round.
3. **Add a brand guide.** Parabolic puts a logo alone at $300 to $1,500 and a full identity at $1,500 to $4,500; the guide is the difference.
4. **Charge for rush work and print production.** Z Design bills print pieces as extra artwork.
5. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], thank you for a great year. Starting [date, 30+ days out],
my hourly rate will be $[75] (from $[70]) and the monthly retainer $[710].
Projects already quoted keep their price. — [Your name]
```

After a year of projects, [the first-year price review](/side-hustles/first-year-side-hustle-price-review-canada/) turns your income, costs and hours into a new rate, and [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/) covers telling existing clients.

## What graphic design pricing mistakes are common?

- **Unlimited revisions.** Every published package above caps concepts and rounds.
- **No deposit.** A cancelled project after concepts is unpaid work.
- **Pricing at marketplace rates.** Upwork's US$15 to US$35 median is a worldwide figure.
- **Unclear ownership.** Write when the client owns the final files and whether you can show them in your portfolio.
- **Forgetting fonts and stock.** Licences cost money; say who pays.

Employed designers should read [side hustles for graphic designers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-graphic-designers-canada/) for copyright and employer rules.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect graphic design prices?

Design income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. Register for GST/HST once taxable sales from all your businesses pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). The rate depends on where the client is ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)): an $850 logo is $960.50 for an Ontario client at 13% HST and $892.50 for an Alberta client at 5% GST. Software, fonts and part of your home office may be deductible; see [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.

See [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) and the [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
