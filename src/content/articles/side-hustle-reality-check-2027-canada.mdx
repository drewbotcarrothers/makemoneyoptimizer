## Which tax numbers should Canadian side hustlers recheck for 2027?

Recheck four federal figures before you copy a 2026 note into a 2027 spreadsheet. Each one lives on a CRA page that gets a new row when the year turns.

| Figure | What the 2026 pages say | What to do in 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GST/HST small supplier | Register after more than $30,000 in taxable supplies over four consecutive calendar quarters, or in a single quarter ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)) | Add every hustle together. The threshold is not per app |
| CPP, outside Quebec | Self-employed rate 11.9% on pensionable earnings. 2026 ceiling $74,600. Maximum self-employed contribution $8,460.90 ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/canada-pension-plan-cpp/cpp-contribution-rates-maximums-exemptions.html)) | Quebec uses the Quebec Pension Plan. Confirm that rate on Retraite Québec |
| CPP2 | 8% for a self-employed person on earnings between the first ceiling and the second. For 2026 the second ceiling is $85,000 and the maximum is $832 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/calculating-deductions/making-deductions/second-additional-cpp-contribution-rates-maximums.html)) | You do not pay CPP on dividends or interest |
| TFSA dollar limit | $7,000 for 2026 ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account/contributing/calculate-room.html)) | The 2027 limit was not on that page. Room is personal. Check the CRA account |

Self-employment profit still goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Renting out property is often Form T776 instead. The choice is [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/). Short answers people mix up are in the [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/). How to file the business half is [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This sketch uses the 2026 self-employed CPP maximum as a teaching number. It is not your tax bill and not a typical side hustle.

Suppose net self-employment income is high enough to hit the 2026 maximum. The CRA table lists that maximum as $8,460.90, before any CPP2. Suppose you also owe income tax on the profit. The CPP line alone is $8,460.90 ÷ 12 = about $705 a month if you spread the year's maximum evenly, which is a budgeting sketch, not how the CRA bills you. You still set cash aside through the year. The point is that a gross payout from an app is not the amount you can spend. Do not copy $705 into a quote.

## What gig-app pay rules are in force, and what they do not cover?

Ontario and British Columbia are the provinces with a published floor for app-based delivery and rides. The floor is not an hourly wage for the whole shift.

- **Ontario:** digital platform workers must be paid at least the general minimum wage for a work assignment or a pay period, tips excluded ([Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers)). The general minimum is $17.95 from October 1, 2026 ([Ontario minimum wage](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage)).
- **British Columbia:** $21.89 per hour of engaged time from June 1, 2026, plus at least $0.35 per kilometre for delivery by transport other than walking ([B.C.](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage)).

Engaged time starts when you accept and ends when you finish or the order is cancelled. Minutes you spend waiting for the next ping are yours. The worksheet is [your real hourly rate on gig apps](/side-hustles/gig-app-real-hourly-rate-canada/). The quit-or-stay test is [when to quit a gig app](/side-hustles/when-to-quit-a-gig-app-canada/). Skip's own zone limits, which are not a wage, are in the [SkipTheDishes courier review](/side-hustles/skipthedishes-courier-review-canada/). Uber Eats is the [Uber Eats courier review](/side-hustles/uber-eats-courier-review-canada/). The three-way comparison is [Uber Eats vs DoorDash vs Skip](/side-hustles/uber-eats-vs-doordash-vs-skip-canada/).

Other provinces do not have an equivalent floor on the pages this guide checked. If you drive there, the offer on the screen is the number, minus your costs.

## What did Canadians report losing to job fraud, and where does a report go?

The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's [top 10 frauds in 2025](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/features-vedette/2026/02/top-fraud-2025-fraudes-plus-courantes-eng.htm) page says the centre received over 112,000 fraud reports involving more than $704 million in reported losses. Job fraud on that page is 2,148 reports, 1,726 victims, and $50.6 million. Those are reported figures, not a complete count of what happened in the country. The pattern, including pay-to-unlock offers, is [side hustle scams](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/) and [task scams](/side-hustles/task-scams-canada/). Where to file is [where to report a side hustle scam](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-scam-canada/): local police, the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at 1-888-495-8501, and your bank if money moved.

## How should you run this check once a year?

Do it in January, or after a province changes a minimum wage, and write the answers down. The newsletter at the bottom of this site is where later updates land. There is no separate ebook to buy for this list.

### Step 1: Add up taxable sales

Put every hustle in one total and compare it with the $30,000 GST/HST line. One app under the line does not keep you under the line.

### Step 2: Separate profit from payouts

Gross deposits minus the costs you paid. For a car, that includes fuel and a share of insurance. For a digital file, that includes the hours, even though the hours are not a CRA deduction of their own.

### Step 3: Read the floor that applies to you

If you deliver in Ontario or B.C., compare your log with the engaged-time floor, then with all-in hours. If you do not, skip the floor and use your own number.

### Step 4: Park the profit on purpose

A payout that sits in the same account as rent gets spent. [What to do with side hustle profits](/side-hustles/what-to-do-with-side-hustle-profits-canada/) is the next decision, after tax is set aside. It is not a reason to buy a course.

### Step 5: Drop one idea that failed the page test

If a pitch cannot show its costs, its permission, or its typical result, it does not get another month. The replacement is a hustle already on the [national list](/side-hustles/best-side-hustles-canada/), not a new funnel.
