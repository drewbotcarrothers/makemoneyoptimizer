## Which pricing approaches work for print-on-demand in Canada?

Most print-on-demand sellers in Canada price from cost plus a target margin, then check the result against similar designs on the same platform. The sales channel matters because marketplace fees are a percentage of the whole order.

| Approach | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target margin | Price so a set share of the sale is left after all costs | Most products | Confusing margin with markup |
| Free shipping built in | Shipping folded into the retail price | Apparel to Canadian buyers | Heavy items or far destinations |
| Premium design tier | Higher price for detailed or limited designs | Artwork with a following | Same blank, same cost: the premium is the design |
| Bundle | Two items, one shipment | Stickers, cards, mugs | Provider ships separately in some cases |

## How do you calculate a print-on-demand price in Canada?

Calculate a print-on-demand price in Canada by adding the provider's product price, extras, shipping, and the tax it charges you, then dividing by one minus your channel's percentage fees and your target margin. Then round.

### Step 1: Price one real product in the catalogue

Open the product you will sell, choose the print areas you need, and note the price your dashboard shows.

### Step 2: Add shipping and extras

Add the shipping rate to your main destination province, plus labels or extra placements.

### Step 3: Account for the sales tax the provider charges

Printful charges Canadian sales tax on product plus shipping. If you are not GST/HST registered, that tax is a cost. If you are registered, you may be able to claim [input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) for the GST/HST portion.

### Step 4: Add your channel's fees

On Etsy, a Canadian seller pays a [6.5% transaction fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy), [3% + CA$0.25 processing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy) on domestic and US orders, and a [0.50% Regulatory Operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee): 10% of the order, plus fixed amounts. Your own store has its own plan and card fees.

### Step 5: Solve for price with a target margin

Price = total cost ÷ (1 − fee percentage − target margin). Margin is profit as a share of the price, not a markup on cost.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose a T-shirt's product price is $17.00 and shipping to Ontario is $6.00, a $23.00 provider subtotal. At Ontario's 13% HST, as listed on Printful's tax page, the tax charged to you is $2.99, so your provider cost is $25.99 if you are not registered.

You sell on Etsy with free shipping and want a 25% margin. Price = ($25.99 + $0.25 + $0.28 listing) ÷ (1 − 0.10 − 0.25) = $26.52 ÷ 0.65 = $40.80, which you might round to $41.

Check at $41: Etsy percentage fees are 10% × $41 = $4.10, plus $0.25 and $0.28, so $4.63. You keep $41 − $4.63 − $25.99 = $10.38, about 25% of the price. If the sale came through Etsy Offsite Ads at 15%, another $6.15 comes off and you keep $4.23. This sketch ignores sales tax collected from the buyer.

## When should you raise print-on-demand prices in Canada?

Raise print-on-demand prices in Canada when the provider changes product or shipping prices, when you add a second print area, or when a channel's fees go up. Re-run the margin formula after any change to the provider's catalogue.

Raising prices on a design that sells steadily is usually safer than cutting them on one that does not. A design that does not sell at a fair margin may need new photos or a new audience, not a lower price.

## What print-on-demand pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common print-on-demand pricing mistakes in Canada are forgetting the sales tax the provider charges, confusing markup with margin, and ignoring channel fees on shipping. Each one turns a sale into a near-zero payout.

Do not price from a US calculator that skips Canadian tax. Do not assume every product ships from the Ontario facility; check where your product is fulfilled before promising delivery dates or no duties. Order a sample before you set a premium price. Use only artwork you own or licence; see the [print-on-demand guide](/side-hustles/print-on-demand-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect print-on-demand prices in Canada?

Print-on-demand income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses.

Once registered, Printful's help page says you can add your GST/HST number in its dashboard, and you may claim input tax credits on the GST/HST you paid. How tax is collected from buyers depends on the channel; on Etsy, see the [Etsy pricing guide](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/).
