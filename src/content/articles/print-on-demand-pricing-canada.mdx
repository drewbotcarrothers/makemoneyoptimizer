A print-on-demand price in Canada has to cover the provider's product price, shipping, the sales tax the provider charges you and your store's fees. On October 9, 2026, Printful's catalogue listed a Bella + Canvas 3001 tee at US$11.92 (about $16.97), and its shipping page charged C$11.29 to send one shirt within Canada. Add 13% HST in Ontario and the tee costs you $31.93 delivered before you've sold it. Canadian brands set the ceiling: Frank And Oak's tees were $39 and $45, tentree's graphic tees $45 and Province of Canada's Canada Tee $58. Sold at $45 on Shopify Basic, that Printful tee leaves $11.51; on Etsy, $8.04.

## Which pricing approaches work for print-on-demand?

| Approach | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target margin | Price so a set share of the sale is left after all costs | Every product, as a floor | Confusing margin with markup |
| Free shipping built in | Shipping folded into the price | Tees to Canadian buyers | Mugs: C$10.29 shipping on a $9.61 product |
| Premium design tier | Higher price for detailed or limited designs | Artwork with a following | Same blank, same cost: the premium is the design |
| Bundle | Two items, one shipment | Mugs and tees (extra item ships for C$2.50–C$6.00) | Items from different facilities ship separately |
| Size pricing | Higher price on 2XL and up | Tees and hoodies | Forgetting the US$2–$4 size upcharge |

## How do you calculate a print-on-demand price?

```text
Provider cost = product (USD × exchange rate) + shipping
Provider tax  = provider cost × province rate (a cost if you're not registered)
Delivered cost = provider cost + provider tax
Price         = (delivered cost + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − channel % − target margin)
```

1. **Price the real product** in your dashboard with the print areas you need.
2. **Add shipping** to your main destination province.
3. **Add the provider's sales tax.** Printful charges it on product plus shipping for orders fulfilled and shipped within Canada, per its [sales tax help page](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017039060-How-does-sales-tax-work-for-my-Canadian-orders). If you're GST/HST registered you may claim [input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) on it.
4. **Add channel fees.** Etsy charges a Canadian seller a [6.5% transaction fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy), [3% + CA$0.25 processing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy) on Canadian and US orders, and a [0.50% Regulatory Operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee): 10% plus $0.25 and a US$0.20 listing fee (about $0.28). [Shopify Basic](https://www.shopify.com/ca/pricing) charges 2.8% + 30¢ on online cards.
5. **Solve for price,** round, and check it against the Canadian anchors.

## What do worked print-on-demand examples look like?

**Illustrative.** Provider prices, shipping, fees and the exchange rate are published (October 9, 2026); the retail prices and target margins are choices. Each example assumes you're not GST/HST registered, so the provider's tax is a cost.

### Example 1: Printful tee on Shopify to Ontario

- Product $16.97 + shipping $11.29 = $28.26. HST 13%: $3.67. Delivered cost **$31.93**.
- Price $45, free shipping. Card fee 2.8% × $45 + $0.30 = $1.56.
- Profit $45 − $31.93 − $1.56 = **$11.51**, a 25.6% margin.

### Example 2: The same tee on Etsy

- Delivered cost $31.93. Price $45.
- Etsy fees: 10% × $45 = $4.50, plus $0.25 and $0.28 = $5.03.
- Profit **$8.04** (17.9%). If the sale came through Offsite Ads at 15% ($6.75), you keep $1.29.

### Example 3: Gildan hoodie on Shopify to Alberta, 30% margin

- Product $32.23 + shipping $13.99 = $46.22. GST 5%: $2.31. Delivered cost **$48.53**.
- Price = ($48.53 + $0.30) ÷ (1 − 0.028 − 0.30) = $48.83 ÷ 0.672 = $72.66. Round to $75.
- At $75: card fee $2.40, profit **$24.07**, a 32.1% margin. tentree's Mountainscape Hoodie is $88.

### Example 4: Mugs on Etsy to Ontario, single and pair

- One 11 oz mug: $9.61 + $10.29 = $19.90, HST $2.59, delivered **$22.49**. At $32, Etsy fees $3.73, profit $5.78 (18.1%).
- A second mug in the same order: $9.61 + $6.00 extra shipping = $15.61, HST $2.03. Pair delivered cost $40.13.
- Sell the pair at $58: fees $5.80 + $0.53 = $6.33, profit **$11.54** (19.9%), double a single sale.

## How do you raise print-on-demand prices?

1. **Re-run the sheet when the provider changes prices** or the US dollar moves. At 1.4240, each US$1 of product cost is $1.42.
2. **Charge more for 2XL and up.** Printful's tee costs US$2 more at 2XL and US$4 more at 3XL.
3. **Add a print area as a premium version** priced for the extra cost, not a free upgrade.
4. **Use bundles** where the second item ships cheaply: +C$2.50 for a second shirt.
5. **Consider Printful Growth** once sales near US$12,000 a year, when it becomes free.
6. **Raise prices on designs that sell,** not on the whole catalogue at once. The wording for repeat buyers is in [how to raise side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What print-on-demand pricing mistakes are common?

- **Forgetting the provider's sales tax.** It's $3.67 on a $45 tee in Ontario.
- **Copying US calculators.** Printful shipping is C$11.29 within Canada but C$6.99 to the US.
- **Free shipping on single mugs.** Shipping is more than the mug.
- **Pricing on Etsy like Shopify.** The same $45 tee earns $3.47 less on Etsy.
- **Selling artwork you don't own.** Use only designs you made or licensed; see the [print-on-demand guide](/side-hustles/print-on-demand-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect print-on-demand prices?

Print-on-demand income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses.

Once registered, Printful's help page says you can add your GST/HST number in its dashboard, and you may claim input tax credits on the GST/HST you paid, which turns the provider tax in the examples back into a credit. How tax is collected from buyers depends on the channel; on Etsy, see the [Etsy pricing guide](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/). If you sell from your own site, <AffiliateLink program="printful" label="Printful" /> is one provider that connects to <AffiliateLink program="shopify" label="Shopify" />; compare at least two providers' catalogue prices before choosing.
