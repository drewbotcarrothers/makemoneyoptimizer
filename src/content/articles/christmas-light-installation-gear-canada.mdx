<QuickAnswer>

- **Short answer:** the core kit is a ladder rated for your weight plus tools, roof and gutter clips, outdoor-rated LED lights with a recognized Canadian certification mark, outdoor extension cords and timers.
- **Electrical rules:** Ontario's ESA says use lights rated for outdoor use, plug outdoor connections into GFCIs and don't overload outlets.
- **Ladders:** CCOHS covers choosing the right ladder and maintaining three points of contact.
- **Prices:** see current price; this is a what-to-look-for guide, not a product review.

## What should you look for in installation gear?

| Item | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extension or step ladder | Duty rating covers you plus tools; right height for the eaves | [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/portable.html) covers choosing and inspecting ladders |
| Ladder accessories | Stabilizer/standoff, levellers | Keeps the ladder off gutters and steady on uneven ground |
| Lights | Outdoor-rated LED, Canadian approval mark | [ESA](https://esasafe.com/safety/home-electrical-safety/holiday-safety/) says use lights rated for outdoor use with a recognized Canadian mark |
| Clips | All-in-one clips for shingles and gutters | No nails or staples through roofing or wire insulation |
| Cords and timers | Outdoor-rated cords, outdoor timers, GFCI | ESA says use GFCIs for outdoor connections |
| Cold-weather gear | Insulated gloves with grip, boots with tread | Fine work on a ladder in the cold |

## What should Canadian installers know?

- **Certification marks.** ESA says choose lights with a recognized Canadian approval mark and buy from trusted retailers; watch for typos that may signal counterfeits.
- **Product safety.** Health Canada has an assessment page on [decorative lighting strings](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/legislation-guidelines/guidelines-policies/information-regulated-parties-enforcement-approach-general-prohibitions/danger-human-health-safety-assessment-decorative-lighting-strings.html).
- **Ladders and ice.** Follow CCOHS on [climbing safely](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/ladders/climbing_safely.html); don't work on icy roofs.
- **Insurance.** Ladder work at clients' homes is a liability risk; compare quotes in [APOLLO vs Zensurance](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/).
