A referral bonus in Canada is cash or account credit a company pays when someone new opens the product and meets a funding or spending rule. It is not a side hustle with hours, and it is not the same thing as a bank sign-up bonus you earn by opening your own account. That separate topic is [bank account sign-up bonuses](/side-hustles/bank-account-sign-up-bonuses-canada/). This page only states amounts that appear on the company's own page, and it tells you to check current terms because every one of these offers says the company can change them.

This is not financial, tax, or legal advice. A bonus is not a reason to move money you need, to borrow, or to open an investing account you do not understand.

## How do you use a referral offer without fooling yourself?

### Step 1: Open the terms on the day you share the link

Wealthsimple's $25, KOHO's "up to $100," and Questrade's $50 were on the pages above on October 6, 2026. Check current terms. A blog, including this one, goes stale the day the company edits the promotion.

### Step 2: Invite someone who actually wants the account

The referee has to fund or spend. A friend who does not want a chequing account, a prepaid card, or a brokerage account should not open one so you can collect $25. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one prepaid account Canadians use for everyday spending. You do not need it, and you do not need to refer anyone, to keep a side hustle's money separate. A no-fee bank account can do that job. The comparison is [Wealthsimple versus a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 3: Use money that can sit still

Wealthsimple requires the bonus to remain 180 days, and it can recover the bonus from a withdrawal if you do not. Questrade pays into the investing account the following month. Do not move rent money to qualify. Do not borrow to hit a deposit. A transfer fee at the old institution is your cost unless that company's reimbursement terms cover it. Wealthsimple's referral article says it reimburses a transfer-out fee when a qualifying transfer is at least $25,000 in a single account. Read that reimbursement page before you assume a smaller transfer is free.

### Step 4: Open the account that receives the bonus

Wealthsimple pays the chequing account. If you never open one, the terms say the bonus expires 90 days after they notify you. Questrade pays the billing account when you have more than one. KOHO shows the reward in the app after the friend's purchase clears.

### Step 5: Do not publish a code on a random site and call it a business

These programs pay for a person who joins and qualifies. They are a poor substitute for work. If you want hours and a client, start from [best side hustles in Canada](/side-hustles/best-side-hustles-canada/), not from a referral link.

### Illustrative example

Teaching sketch using Wealthsimple's published $25, not a forecast of how many friends will qualify. One qualifying referral pays $25 to you and $25 to your friend, after the $100 external deposit and the other terms. Four friends who each qualify would be 4 × $25 = $100 to you, and $0 for any friend who does not deposit. The $100 is still locked to the 180-day hold on each bonus, and it assumes the September 11, 2026 terms are still the ones in force. Check current terms. This is not income you can schedule, and it is not a statement that four people will sign up.

## What mistakes do people make with referral bonuses in Canada?

- **Trusting a dollar amount from a post that is older than the terms.** Questrade's page was updated December 2, 2024. Wealthsimple's current terms start September 11, 2026. They are different dates on purpose.
- **Counting a deposit you moved from another Wealthsimple account.** The terms exclude money that starts inside Wealthsimple.
- **Missing the chequing-account step.** The $25 does not land in a trade account just because that is where the deposit went.
- **Combining codes.** Questrade says a new customer can use one referral code and cannot combine it with other offer codes.
- **Sending a code to strangers with a promise of profit.** You are describing someone else's product. If you are paid to promote it, say so. A referral bonus is not a return on an investment.
- **Treating the bonus as spent on the day you send the link.** It is paid when the terms say it is paid.

## Which tax rules apply to referral bonuses in Canada?

Wealthsimple's own terms say there are tax implications to bonuses of this nature in most instances, that Wealthsimple will not issue a tax slip for a bonus paid to a non-registered account, and that you are responsible for any required reporting. That is the company's statement. It is not a CRA ruling, and it does not by itself tell you which line of the return to use.

This guide does not give a yes or no for every referral bonus. The careful version of survey rewards, cash-back, and bank bonuses is [are rewards and bonuses taxable](/side-hustles/are-rewards-and-bonuses-taxable-canada/). If a T5, a T4A, or another slip arrives, report the slip. If you carry on a business of promoting financial products, incentive income is business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). A single bonus for inviting a friend is not automatically that business. Ask the institution whether it will issue a slip, and ask a tax professional if the amount matters. <AffiliateLink program="wealthsimple-tax" label="Wealthsimple Tax" /> can file a return once you have decided what the amount is. It will not decide the label for you.

This is not tax, legal, or financial advice.
