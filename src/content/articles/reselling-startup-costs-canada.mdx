**Quick answer:** a lean reselling kit in Canada costs about **$66** (100 poly mailers, tape, a soft measuring tape and an 86 lb shipping scale, 13% Ontario HST included). A standard kit that adds a tape gun, a clothes steamer, a storage bin and a rolling rack costs about **$196**, and a pro kit with a Bluetooth 4x6 label printer, labels, a phone tripod and four large bins costs about **$479**. Prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026. Platform fees come out of each sale: eBay.ca takes 13.6% of the total sale plus $0.30 or $0.40 an order in most categories, and Poshmark Canada takes C$3.95 under C$20 or 20% at C$20 and up. A $40 item bought for $12 leaves about $19.50 after fees and supplies, so the lean kit pays back on the fourth sale and the pro kit on the twenty-fifth.

Listing, packing and meeting buyers safely are in the [reselling side hustle guide](/side-hustles/reselling-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash: what to buy, what it cost on a specific date, which fees each sale pays, and how many sales recover the kit.

## How much does it cost to start reselling in Canada?

Reselling costs $66.34 to start with a lean kit, $195.76 with a standard kit and $478.89 with a pro kit. Those are Amazon.ca prices from October 9, 2026 with 13% Ontario HST. Inventory is a separate line: the closet costs nothing, and anything you buy to flip is money you get back only when it sells.

| Kit | Gear before tax | Gear with 13% HST | Gear with 5% GST | Inventory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $58.71 | $66.34 | $61.65 | Items you already own |
| Standard | $173.24 | $195.76 | $181.90 | Closet plus your first thrift or yard-sale finds |
| Pro | $423.80 | $478.89 | $444.99 | A sourcing budget you set and track per item |

Alberta and the territories pay 5% GST only. The [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province, including Nova Scotia's 14% HST since April 1, 2025.

Nothing in these kits is a subscription. eBay.ca, Poshmark, Facebook Marketplace and Kijiji all let you list without a monthly fee; the cost arrives as a per-sale fee or not at all for a cash pickup. A Shopify store is a later decision covered below.

## Who is a reselling startup budget a fit for in Canada?

This budget fits people who have a closet or basement of things worth selling, can photograph a flaw honestly, and are willing to wait a few weeks for the right buyer. It also fits thrift, yard-sale and estate-sale shoppers who already know what sells in one category, such as outerwear, kids' clothes, video games or tools.

It is a poor fit if you need rent money from the first weekend, if you would rather not ship, or if your plan starts with buying a liquidation pallet you have not inspected. Bought inventory ties up cash until it sells, and the gear in these kits does nothing for an item nobody wants.

Your own checkout, later, is [Shopify store startup costs](/side-hustles/shopify-store-startup-costs-canada/). Furniture needs transport and finishing gear; see [furniture flipping startup costs](/side-hustles/furniture-flipping-startup-costs-canada/).

## What fees does each reselling platform charge in Canada?

The kit is a one-time cost. The fee on every sale is the cost that never stops, so it belongs in your price before you list. These are the platforms' own published fees, checked October 9, 2026.

| Platform | Listing fee | Fee when it sells | Shipping |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| eBay.ca (most categories) | First 250 listings a month free, then $0.30 each | 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500, plus $0.30 per order of $10.00 or less or $0.40 over $10.00 | You buy or print the label; shipping charged to the buyer is part of the fee base |
| eBay.ca (books, movies, music) | Same | 15.3% of the total sale up to $7,500 plus the per-order fee | Same |
| Poshmark Canada | Free | C$3.95 flat under C$20; 20% of the listing price at C$20 or more | Poshmark sends a prepaid label; up to 2.5 kg ships with no extra fee |
| Facebook Marketplace, Kijiji (local pickup) | Free to post a basic listing | None on a cash or e-transfer pickup, because no payment passes through the platform | Your drive or the buyer's |
| Shopify Basic (own store) | Not per listing | $49 CAD a month, or $37 CAD a month billed yearly, plus card processing | Your own labels |

Details that change the math on eBay.ca, from its [selling fees page](https://www.ebay.ca/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/selling-fees?id=4822):

- **The fee base includes tax and shipping.** "Total amount of the sale" includes the item price, handling, shipping collected from the buyer and sales tax. eBay's own example: a $400 phone with free shipping sold to an Ontario buyer is a $452 total with 13% HST, so the fee is 13.6% of $452 plus $0.40, or $61.87.
- **Optional upgrades cost extra.** A subtitle on a fixed-price listing is $2.00 up to $150, and bold is $4.00. Neither is needed to sell.
- **Performance matters.** A Below Standard account pays an additional 6% on final value fees the following month.
- **Currency conversion goes up October 14, 2026.** If eBay converts your funds for a sale on another eBay site, the charge for Canadian sellers rises from 3% to 3.25%.
- **Sneakers get a break.** Athletic shoes sold for $150 or more pay 8% with no per-order fee.

Poshmark's [getting started guide](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark) says the 20% covers payment processing, the prepaid label and customer support, and withdrawals to your bank account are free. Its [label upgrade page](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/how_to_get_overweight_shipping_label) says the printed label shows 0.75 kg but you can ship up to 2.5 kg without extra fees; heavier parcels need an upgrade deducted from your earnings. That is why the scale is in every kit.

## How many sales does it take to pay back reselling gear?

Each sale has to cover the item, the platform fee and the mailer before anything goes toward the kit.

### Worked example: one $40 jacket on eBay.ca and on Poshmark

**Assumed inputs:** a jacket bought at a thrift store for $12, listed at $40, $12 of shipping charged to an Ontario buyer on eBay.ca and paid out exactly on the label, one mailer at $0.17 (the $16.75 pack ÷ 100). **Sourced inputs:** the eBay.ca and Poshmark fee rules above.

eBay.ca:

- Total amount of the sale: ($40 + $12) × 1.13 = **$58.76**
- Final value fee: 13.6% × $58.76 = $7.99, plus $0.40 = **$8.39**
- Left after fee, label, item and mailer: $40 + $12 − $8.39 − $12 − $12 − $0.17 = **$19.44**

Poshmark Canada:

- Fee: 20% × $40 = **$8.00**, and the label is prepaid
- Left after item and mailer: $40 − $8.00 − $12 − $0.17 = **$19.83**

A $15 item on Poshmark pays the C$3.95 flat fee, leaving $11.05 before the item's cost. Low-priced items carry a much bigger fee share, which is why many sellers bundle them.

### Sales to pay back each kit

| Kit | Kit cost with HST | ÷ $19.44 (eBay.ca example) | ÷ $19.83 (Poshmark example) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $66.34 | 3.41, so 4 sales | 3.35, so 4 sales |
| Standard | $195.76 | 10.07, so 11 sales | 9.87, so 10 sales |
| Pro | $478.89 | 24.63, so 25 sales | 24.15, so 25 sales |

Closet items change the picture: with no $12 purchase, the same eBay.ca sale leaves $31.44, and the lean kit is paid back on the third sale.

### Illustrative example: a first month on the standard kit

**Assumed inputs:** 15 items sold at an average of $30 on Poshmark, each bought for $8, one mailer each. **Sourced inputs:** Poshmark's 20% fee and the standard kit total.

- Sales: 15 × $30 = **$450**
- Poshmark fees: 15 × $6.00 = **$90**
- Items: 15 × $8 = **$120**
- Mailers: 15 × $0.17 = **$2.55**
- Before the kit: $450 − $90 − $120 − $2.55 = **$237.45**
- After the $195.76 kit: **$41.69** for the first month, and the full $237.45 in each later month at the same pace, before tax and your time

## Do you need a licence or insurance to resell in Canada?

Selling your own belongings online does not usually need a licence. A physical second-hand shop is different: Toronto's [Second Hand Shop licence page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/second-hand-salvage-yards-shops/second-hand-shop/) lists a $242.82 application fee and a $545.51 licence fee, $788.33 in total, with a $384.88 renewal. Other cities set their own rules and fees, so check your municipality before you open a storefront or sell from a fixed location.

Goods you import to resell are commercial imports. The CBSA's [guide to importing commercial goods](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/guide-eng.html) covers the business number, duties and the paperwork.

Home insurance policies often limit or exclude business property and business activity. Ask your insurer before you store a basement of inventory.

## Where can a reselling budget in Canada stay smaller?

- **Sell the closet first.** The first ten listings cost nothing to source and teach you what sells.
- **Use pickups for big, cheap items.** A Marketplace or Kijiji pickup needs no mailer, no label and no platform fee.
- **Reuse clean boxes and mailers.** Buyers care about the item arriving intact, not new packaging.
- **Wait on the label printer.** Poshmark sends a prepaid label you can print on a home printer. A thermal printer earns its $89.99 once you ship several parcels a week.
- **Skip the store subscription.** Shopify Basic at $37 to $49 a month is a cost before any sale. Marketplaces bring the buyers.

## Where should a reselling budget in Canada refuse to shrink?

- **The scale.** A guessed weight becomes a label upgrade, a short-paid parcel or a dispute.
- **Honest photos and measurements.** An "item not as described" return costs the fee, the shipping both ways and possibly a higher eBay fee tier.
- **A record of what you paid.** Every item needs its purchase cost on file, or you cannot tell profit from turnover.
- **Separate money.** <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that keeps sales payouts and sourcing money apart from household spending.

For the product safety, fee and record mistakes resellers make most, see the [reselling mistakes guide](/side-hustles/reselling-side-hustle-canada/#what-to-watch-out-for).

## What could change these reselling numbers?

Fees, item mix and province move these totals most. eBay.ca and Poshmark can change their fee pages at any time, and eBay's currency conversion charge rises on October 14, 2026. A fee of 13.6% on a total that includes tax and shipping takes a bigger share of a cheap item than of an expensive one. The 13% HST versus 5% GST difference moves the pro kit by $33.90. Amazon.ca prices were checked on October 9, 2026 and move with sales. This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.

## How do taxes work for reselling in Canada?

Selling your own used belongings at a loss is generally not business income. Buying items to resell at a profit, regularly, is a business, and the profit goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Ask a tax professional if you are unsure which one you are doing, and keep purchase notes either way.

As a business, the cost of goods sold, mailers, platform fees and shipping labels are ordinary expenses. A label printer or scale that lasts years is claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html). Self-employed profit also means paying both shares of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting all your side hustles. Reselling is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. Once registered, tax you pay on business purchases can be claimed back as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html), and the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) gives each province's rate.
