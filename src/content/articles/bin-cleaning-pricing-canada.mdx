## Which pricing models work for bin cleaning in Canada?

Bin cleaning in Canada is usually sold as a recurring household plan, not as an open hourly visit. The client wants one number. Your drive has to live inside it.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per household, per visit | One price for the carts at that address, on the route day | A tight block | A single house far from the loop |
| Monthly plan | A set number of washes, billed once a month | Predictable curb days | Collection holidays and carts that were not set out |
| Seasonal plan | Spring through fall, or the warm months you will actually wash | Households who hate the summer smell | Winter weeks when water and ice make the job unsafe |
| First wash or restoration | A higher one-time price for a cart that has never been cleaned | The sale that starts the plan | Quoting it at the maintenance price |
| Per cart add-on | Extra when the house has garbage, recycling, and organics | Streets with three carts | Forgetting the organics cart takes longer |

Write what happens if the cart is not at the curb, and what happens if the only drain is a storm grate. A visit you must refuse is worth zero. The [start guide](/side-hustles/bin-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) quotes Toronto's page: wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach a catch basin, and outside garbage areas should not be cleaned with running water. Other cities write their own bylaw. A price that assumes a pressure washer into the street is a price you cannot legally earn in Toronto.

## How do you calculate a bin cleaning rate in Canada?

Calculate a bin cleaning rate in Canada from the minutes per stop, the share of the morning lost to walking and water disposal, and a target hourly pay you choose. There is no Job Bank wage this guide will pretend is the bin-wash occupation.

### Step 1: Set a target for your time

Use a number you can say out loud. Compare it with other outdoor work you already price, such as [lawn care pricing](/side-hustles/lawn-care-pricing-canada/), only as a method. Do not copy a lawn price onto a cart.

### Step 2: Time a clustered stop

Wash, rinse or wipe, put the carts back where the city collects them, and empty dirty water only where the bylaw allows. A stop with a legal drain is a different job from a stop you have to skip.

### Step 3: Count the carts

Two carts are not three. The organics cart is often the slow one. Calgary's green cart takes food scraps and pet waste. Toronto's green bin takes food scraps and pet waste under that city's rules. Smell is why the recurring plan exists. It is also why the first wash takes longer.

### Step 4: Put cleaner and bags in the price

A bottle that lasts ten houses is a few cents per stop. A restoration that uses half a bottle is not the same cost. The [startup cost guide](/side-hustles/bin-cleaning-startup-costs-canada/) separates the kit you buy once from the cleaner you use up.

### Step 5: Bill the route, not the hero house

One household at the end of a 20-minute drive needs a higher price or a no. Four households on one block share the walk.

Monthly invoices are the admin. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can send the same bill each month and show who has not paid. A notebook works for four houses. It will not chase a season of e-transfers.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, a forecast, or a survey. Suppose your target is $30 an hour and 75% of the morning is washing rather than walking between gates. The billable rate is $30 ÷ 0.75 = $40 an hour.

Suppose a house with two carts takes 20 minutes when you are already on the block, and you have permission to empty the bucket in a laundry sink. Labour is (20 ÷ 60) × $40 = $13.33. Suppose cleaner for that stop costs $1. The sketch is about $14, which you might round to $16 per visit before tax. Suppose you wash twice a month. The monthly plan is 2 × $16 = $32. Suppose a first wash of a neglected organics cart takes 40 minutes and $3 of cleaner: (40 ÷ 60) × $40 + $3 = $29.67, which you might round to $35 once, then drop to the $16 maintenance visit. Do not copy $16 or $35. Time the curb. A house you must refuse because the water would reach a catch basin is not a discounted $16. It is a no.

Suppose eight houses on one street take the 20-minute stop each, 160 minutes, plus 20 minutes to deal with water at the end. That is 3 hours. At $16 × 8 = $128. The sketch hourly result is $128 ÷ 3 = about $43 before a tax set-aside, above the $30 target, because the stops were clustered. Move two of those houses across town and the result falls. The route is the product.

## When should you raise bin cleaning prices in Canada?

Raise prices before the next season, when you send the renewal, or when the route is full and a new street would wreck the timing. A waiting list on one block is the clearest signal. A jump in cleaner cost is another. Mid-month surprises make people cancel. Spring renewals are a natural moment: the carts smell worse as the weather warms, and households are already outside. ## What bin cleaning pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common mistakes are one price for every address in the city, a first wash sold at the maintenance rate, and a monthly plan that quietly includes a missed curb day for free. Another is quoting a pressure-wash price in a city where the water cannot go in the street. Another is copying a flyer from a US company. Their sewer rule is not Toronto's.

Charge for the organics cart if it takes longer. Write the skip rule for carts that were not set out. Keep a log of minutes for the first month and change the sheet if the 20-minute assumption was wrong. ## How do GST/HST and income tax affect bin cleaning prices in Canada?

Bin cleaning fees in Canada are self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. Add GST/HST only once you are registered. Registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) on taxable supplies in a quarter or over four consecutive quarters. Lawn care, pet waste, and bin washes share one total if they are all taxable.

When registered, use the rate for the province where you wash, from the CRA's [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) page. Put "plus tax" on the sheet only after that registration. The [tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) and [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) keep this income off the rental form.
