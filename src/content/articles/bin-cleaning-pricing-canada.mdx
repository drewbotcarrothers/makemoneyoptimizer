Pricing garbage bin cleaning in Canada means a flat price per household per visit, a cheaper monthly or seasonal plan, and a higher first wash, with the wash water handled legally. Nine Canadian bin cleaning businesses list one-time cleans from $24.99 for a single bin to $65 for three, and monthly plans from $15.95 a bin to $40 for two bins. The hourly result depends on how many stops sit on one street, so price the route before you print a flyer. Bin cleaning suits someone who will time a real curb first. It's a poor fit if the only customers are single driveways across town.

## Which pricing models work for bin cleaning in Canada?

The businesses above all use the same three pieces: a one-time clean, a cheaper recurring plan with a minimum term, and a per-bin add-on.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per household, per visit | One price for the carts at that address, on the route day | A tight block | A single house far from the loop |
| Monthly plan | A set number of washes, billed once a month, with a minimum term | Predictable curb days | Collection holidays and carts that weren't set out |
| Seasonal plan | Spring through fall, paid up front or monthly | Households who hate the summer smell | Winter weeks when water and ice make the job unsafe |
| First wash or restoration | A higher one-time price for a neglected cart | The sale that starts the plan | Quoting it at the maintenance price |
| Per cart add-on | Extra for the third cart or recycling boxes | Streets with three carts | The organics cart takes longer |

Write what happens if the cart isn't at the curb, and what happens if the only drain is a storm grate. The [start guide](/side-hustles/bin-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) quotes [Toronto's page on outdoor washing](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/): wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach a catch basin. Other cities write their own bylaws. Bin Ninja says its trailer captures the dirty water and hauls it away, and Just Bin Cleaned says it recycles water through a filtration system. That equipment is part of why their prices sit higher than a hand-scrub service.

## How do you calculate a bin cleaning rate?

Price the stop from minutes, then check the route.

```text
Billable rate  = target hourly pay ÷ share of the morning spent washing
Visit price    = minutes per stop ÷ 60 × billable rate + supplies
First wash     = same formula with the longer time and more cleaner
Route check    = (sum of visit prices − supplies) ÷ total route hours
Monthly plan   = visits per month × visit price (minimum term written down)
```

**Pick a target and time a clustered stop.** Wash, rinse, put the carts back where the city collects them, and empty the water only where the bylaw allows. **Count the carts.** Toronto's [green bin](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/what-goes-in-my-green-bin/) and Calgary's [green cart](https://www.calgary.ca/waste/residential/what-can-go-in-green-cart.html) take food scraps and pet waste, which is why the organics cart is the slow one. **Then compare with the local price list.** The formula gives your floor; the businesses above show what households in your area already pay.

## What do worked bin cleaning quotes look like?

- Quote **$25 a visit**, 7 visits from April to October = **$175 for the season**. Not registered, so no HST.

### Quote 2: first wash of a neglected garbage and organics pair, Edmonton

- 40 minutes ÷ 60 × $40 + $3 of cleaner = $29.67.
- Local anchor: Bin Ninja's two-bin single clean is $55 + GST.
- Quote **$45 for the first wash**, then $25 a visit on a monthly plan. If registered for GST: $45 × 1.05 = $47.25.

### Quote 3: what the route does to the hourly result

- Eight houses on one street at $25: $200. Supplies 8 × $1 = $8. Washing 8 × 20 minutes = 160 minutes, plus 20 minutes to deal with water = 3 hours.
- ($200 − $8) ÷ 3 = **$64 an hour**.
- Split the same eight houses across town and add an hour of driving: $192 ÷ 4 = **$48 an hour**. The route is the product.

### Quote 4: three carts every two weeks, Ontario, HST-registered

- Floor: 28 minutes ÷ 60 × $40 + $1.50 = $20.17 a visit.
- Local anchors: Eco Pro Wash lists three bins every two weeks at $51.99 a month in BC; Bin Medic lists all three every four weeks at $50 in Calgary.
- Quote **$30 a visit**, 2 visits a month = $60. With 13% HST: $60 × 1.13 = **$67.80 a month**.

## How do you raise bin cleaning prices?

- **Raise at renewal, not mid-season.** Send the new price with the spring booking message.
- **Fill one street before adding another.** A waiting list on one block supports a higher price for the next block.
- **Charge the first wash properly.** Bin Ninja, Bin Medic and We Clean Garbage Cans all price one-time or extra-dirty cleans above their plan visits.
- **Use minimum terms.** Eco Pro Wash's 4-month and Bin Ninja's 6-month minimums protect the route; copy the idea, not the number.
- For scripts, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/). ## What bin cleaning pricing mistakes are common?

Common mistakes are one price for every address in the city, a first wash sold at the maintenance rate, and a monthly plan that quietly includes a missed curb day for free. Another is quoting a pressure-wash price where the water can't legally go in the street.

Keep a log of minutes for the first month and change the sheet if the 20-minute assumption was wrong. The [pricing worksheet](/side-hustles/side-hustle-pricing-worksheet-canada/) is a blank for that arithmetic, and [lawn care pricing](/side-hustles/lawn-care-pricing-canada/) shows the same route logic for another outdoor service.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect bin cleaning prices?

Bin cleaning fees are self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both shares of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. Add GST/HST only once you're registered. Registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) on taxable supplies in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across your businesses. When registered, use the rate for the province where you wash, from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) and [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) keep this income off the rental form.

Monthly invoices are the admin. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can send the same bill each month and show who hasn't paid. How to send the monthly bill is in [how to invoice clients](/side-hustles/how-to-invoice-clients-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

## What should you keep in mind about these numbers?

The price tables are what each business listed on its own page on October 9, 2026, not a survey, and several pages don't say whether tax is included.
