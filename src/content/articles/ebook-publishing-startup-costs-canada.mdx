## How much does ebook publishing cost to start in Canada?

Ebook publishing in Canada costs about $0 if you already own a computer, $40.67 with a backup drive, $415.82 with Canva Pro and a Kindle to proofread on, or $1,117.52 with a Paperwhite and a year of writer's insurance. Kit totals include 13% Ontario HST on the goods and on Canva. Use your province's rate.

| Kit | What's in it | Before tax | With 13% HST |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0 start | Computer you own, free writing tool, Canva Free, free ISBN if eligible | $0 | $0 |
| Lean | SanDisk 128GB Ultra Flair USB drive ($35.99) | $35.99 | $40.67 |
| Standard | Lean + Canva Pro ($142/year) + Amazon Kindle 16 GB ($189.99) | $367.98 | $415.82 |
| Pro | Lean + Canva Pro + Kindle Paperwhite 16GB ($279.99) | $457.98 | $517.52 |
| Pro with insurance | Pro + writer's insurance at about $50/month (about $600/year) | — | about $1,117.52 |

Insurance is added without HST. Zensurance's [writer and author insurance](https://www.zensurance.com/writer-and-author-insurance) page says $1 million of professional indemnity "costs around $50 per month". That's an estimate, not a quote.

## Who should use an ebook startup budget in Canada?

An ebook budget fits people who know a process well enough to explain it without inventing numbers: a checklist, a walkthrough of a tool, a recipe set, or a guide to a local system. The $0 start fits a first short book. The standard kit fits someone planning several books who wants to see the file as a reader would. Ebook publishing is a poor fit if you need the money before the draft exists, if you'd paste someone else's chapters, or if the book promises income you can't support.

## Do you need to pay for an ISBN in Canada?

No, if you're eligible. Library and Archives Canada's [ISBN page](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn.html) says "ISBN Canada provides free ISBNs to eligible publishers and publications." Its [apply page](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/apply.html) says a high volume of requests means new accounts may take up to two months to process. Start the account early, and don't hold back a direct PDF sale while you wait.

Legal deposit is separate. Section 10 of the [Library and Archives of Canada Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-7.7/section-10.html) says a publisher who makes a publication available in Canada shall, subject to the regulations, provide copies to the Librarian and Archivist. LAC's [legal deposit](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/legal-deposit/about.html) page explains who it treats as a Canadian publisher. This is not legal advice.

These are the published rates checked on October 9, 2026. "$" amounts from Gumroad and Payhip are treated as US dollars.

## What does one sale actually pay?

**Illustrative. The list prices are assumptions within each store's published rules, not typical results. The Kindle examples assume a 2 MB file, Amazon.ca's C$0.15/MB delivery cost (C$0.30), and a list price that excludes sales tax. Verify the arithmetic and the fees on your own dashboard.**

| Example | Arithmetic | You keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon.ca, C$2.99, 70% | 0.70 × (2.99 − 0.30) | C$1.88 |
| Amazon.ca, C$4.99, 70% | 0.70 × (4.99 − 0.30) | C$3.28 |
| Amazon.ca, C$4.99, 35% | 0.35 × 4.99 | C$1.75 |
| Amazon.ca, C$9.99, 70% | 0.70 × (9.99 − 0.30) | C$6.78 |
| Amazon.ca, C$12.99, 70% | 0.70 × (12.99 − 0.30) | C$8.88 |
| Gumroad direct, US$9.99 | 9.99 − 0.999 − 0.50 = US$8.49 × 1.4240 | about C$12.09, before any payment-processing fee |
| Payhip Free, US$9.99 | 9.99 × 0.95 = US$9.49 | before Stripe or PayPal fees |

Two things stand out. At C$4.99, the 70% option pays almost twice the 35% option. And a direct sale through your own link keeps more per copy, but only if you have people to send to the link. ## How many sales does it take to break even?

**Illustrative. Uses the per-sale amounts above (C$3.28 at C$4.99 and C$6.78 at C$9.99 on Amazon.ca's 70% option, and about C$12.09 on a US$9.99 Gumroad sale). Each figure is the kit cost divided by the amount per sale, rounded up. Verify the arithmetic.**

| Kit | Cost to cover | Sales at C$4.99 on Amazon.ca | Sales at C$9.99 on Amazon.ca | Gumroad sales at US$9.99 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $0 start | $0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Lean | $40.67 | 13 | 6 | 4 |
| Standard | $415.82 | 127 | 62 | 35 |
| Pro | $517.52 | 158 | 77 | 43 |
| Pro with insurance | $1,117.52 | 341 | 165 | 93 |

For example, $415.82 ÷ $3.28 = 126.8, so 127 sales. Your writing hours aren't in this table, and they're the biggest cost. The Kindle and Canva Pro can also be reused across several books, so the standard kit's cost is spread across them if you keep publishing.

## What are the ongoing costs?

- **The store's cut,** on every sale, at the rates above.
- **Canva Pro renewal,** $142 a year before tax, if you keep it.
- **Insurance,** about $50 a month if you buy the Zensurance-style policy.
- **Corrections.** A tax figure, a fee or a screen in a tool will move. Date the file and fix it when a rule you cited changes.
- **Refunds.** A file that won't open, or a chapter that copied someone else, comes back. The Canadian Intellectual Property Office explains [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright). Write original text.

When the payout lands, it's income to record, not spending money. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can hold it until you know the tax set-aside. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can record the sales if the store's report isn't enough for your own file. ## Where can ebook startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Skip the e-reader at first.** Free reading apps on a phone you own preview the file, though less faithfully than a dedicated device.
- **Use Canva Free** until you've published something that sells.
- **Use the free ISBN** if you're eligible, rather than buying one elsewhere.
- **Use storage you already pay for** instead of a new drive.
- **Skip ads, a ghostwriter and a bookstore plan.** A short, honest direct PDF can go on sale while you read the ISBN page.

## Where shouldn't you cut ebook spending?

Don't skip the source check, the copyright line or the legal-deposit reading. Pasted chapters aren't a cheap draft. A sales page with invented reviews isn't a launch plan. Don't guarantee income inside a book about making money; label examples, and when a rule changes, date the file and say what changed.

GI-065, dated August 2014, lists electronic and digital books among goods that don't qualify for the [point-of-sale book rebate](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-065/point-sale-rebate-on-books.html) it describes. The rate table in that sheet isn't the current HST table. Use the charge-and-collect page for rates.

## What should you know before relying on these numbers?

Prices, store fees and the KDP price band are what each page said on October 9, 2026, and they change. The sale prices and file size in the examples are assumptions, not typical results. Re-check each link before you buy anything or set a price.

## Which tax rules apply to an ebook budget in Canada?

Ebook sales in Canada are self-employment income, including a store payout, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both portions of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. Equipment like an e-reader used for the business may be deductible in part; keep the receipt.

GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add printables, templates and other side-hustle revenue to the same total. The first-dollar rule in the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) guidance doesn't apply to selling ebooks. If you're not registered, don't charge GST/HST.

Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of April 1, 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table. Once registered, digital supplies can be taxed differently depending on who the buyer is and where the supply is made; confirm with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Quebec charges GST and QST. The CRA's [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) is the further reading when a platform sits between you and the buyer. Gumroad says it has handled sales tax collection and remittance as merchant of record since January 1, 2025.

Search your city for a business licence if you advertise the book as a local business. This guide doesn't state a licence fee.

This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.
