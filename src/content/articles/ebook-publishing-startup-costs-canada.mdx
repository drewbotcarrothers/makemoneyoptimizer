## Which tax rules apply to an ebook budget in Canada?

Ebook sales in Canada are self-employment income, including a shop payout, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table. The CRA's [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) is the further reading when a platform sits between you and the buyer.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the shop reports and the draft with its date. Report the income on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add printables, templates, and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Selling an ebook is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform. This guide does not explain input tax credits. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST.

Once registered, digital supplies can be taxed differently depending on who the buyer is and where the supply is made. Confirm the rate on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Quebec charges GST and QST. Set the tax aside. Do not apply a printed-book rebate story to a download. GI-065's non-qualifying list includes electronic and digital books, and the sheet's 2014 rates are not current.
