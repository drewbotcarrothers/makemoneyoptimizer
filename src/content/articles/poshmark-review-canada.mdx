Poshmark is a simple way for Canadians to resell clothing, shoes, and accessories, especially mid-priced brand-name pieces. As of October 2026, Poshmark Canada charges a flat C$3.95 on sales under C$20 and 20% of the listing price on sales of C$20 or more, and in return it handles payment processing, provides a prepaid shipping label, and acts as customer support for the sale ([Poshmark Canada](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark)). Poshmark does not publish typical seller earnings, and this review does not estimate them.

## Is Poshmark available in Canada?

Yes. Poshmark runs a Canadian marketplace at poshmark.ca with Canadian-dollar pricing and Canada Post shipping. Listing is free; you pay only when an item sells ([Poshmark Canada](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark)).

## What fees does Poshmark charge Canadian sellers?

One commission per sale, plus optional payout fees. As listed in October 2026:

| Item | Amount | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing | Free | [Get started](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark) |
| Sale under C$20 | Flat C$3.95 | Get started |
| Sale of C$20 or more | 20% of the listing price (you keep 80%) | Get started |
| Shipping label | Prepaid label emailed to you | [Shipping](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/how_to_ship) |
| Direct deposit payout | Free | [Getting paid](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/how_to_get_paid) |
| Instant Transfer to a Visa debit card | Flat fee shown in the app | Getting paid |
| PayPal payout | Transfer fee shown in the app | Getting paid |

Poshmark says the commission covers payment processing, including credit card fees, so there is no separate processing charge.

### Illustrative example

Assumed sale prices, using Poshmark Canada's published fee rule:

- A C$15 sale: C$15 − C$3.95 = C$11.05 to you
- A C$50 sale: C$50 × 20% = C$10 fee, so C$40 to you
- On a C$15 sale the flat fee is about 26% of the price (3.95 ÷ 15), which is why many sellers bundle low-priced items

## How do Poshmark payouts work in Canada?

You withdraw your redeemable balance from the app. Poshmark Canada lists three options: free direct deposit to your bank, typically within 2 to 3 business days; Instant Transfer to a participating Visa debit card for a flat fee, typically within 30 minutes; and PayPal for a transfer fee ([Poshmark Canada](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/how_to_get_paid)). Poshmark uses Hyperwallet to deliver payments, and prepaid or expired debit cards are not accepted for Instant Transfer. You can also spend your balance on Poshmark.

## How does Poshmark shipping work in Canada?

After a sale, Poshmark emails a prepaid, pre-addressed shipping label. You print it, pack the item in any box or padded envelope, and drop it in a mailbox or at a Canada Post location ([Poshmark Canada](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/how_to_ship)). Bulky items may need a post office visit.

## What are the pros and cons of Poshmark for Canadians?

**Pros**
- Simple fee rule in Canadian dollars.
- Prepaid Canada Post labels remove postage guesswork.
- Free direct deposit payouts.
- Poshmark handles payment and customer support for the sale.

**Cons**
- 20% is high on expensive items.
- The C$3.95 flat fee eats a large share of cheap items.
- A smaller Canadian buyer pool than some local options.
- Fashion focus; not ideal for furniture or electronics.

## Who is Poshmark best for in Canada?

Poshmark suits Canadians reselling brand-name clothing, shoes and accessories priced roughly C$20 and up, who want shipping handled with a prepaid label. It suits low-priced items and bulky goods less well. ## What mistakes do Canadians make on Poshmark?

- **Pricing under C$20 without noticing the flat fee.** Bundle cheap items or price above C$20.
- **Forgetting the 20% when buying inventory.** Work back from what you'll keep.
- **Paying for Instant Transfer by habit.** Direct deposit is free.
- **Poor photos and measurements.** Clear details reduce returns and disputes.
- **Treating regular flipping as a hobby.** Buying to resell is business income.

## How is Poshmark income taxed in Canada?

Selling your own used clothes occasionally at a loss is generally not business income, but buying items to resell for profit is, and you report it on Form T2125 with Poshmark's fees and your cost of goods as expenses. Poshmark is a platform operator under Canada's [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html), which require platforms to report seller information and income to the CRA.
