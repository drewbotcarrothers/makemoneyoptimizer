Turo host costs in Canada are the share of the trip price you do not keep, the personal insurance you still carry, cleaning and small repairs, parking, and the loan or lease payment that continues on days with no booking. Turo's list-your-car page says there is no sign-up charge and no monthly platform fee. The expensive lines are the car you already own and the plan you pick. There is no national startup total, because a paid-off car in a driveway and a financed car in a downtown stall are different businesses.

How listing works, and which provinces Turo names, is [how to start hosting on Turo in Canada](/side-hustles/turo-host-canada/). Passenger-app budgets are a different set of costs in [rideshare startup costs](/side-hustles/rideshare-startup-costs-canada/).

## How much does it cost to host on Turo in Canada?

Hosting on Turo in Canada can start at the cost of a clean and a phone, and it can fail to cover a car payment even when trips book. Turo does not publish a host startup price list. Use the categories below, then replace every assumed number in the sketch with a quote or a receipt.

| Cost | What to put in the budget | Where the rule lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform fee to join | Turo says listing is free, with no monthly fee | [List your car](https://turo.com/ca/en/car-rental/canada/list-your-car) |
| Share of the trip price you give up | You keep 65%, 75%, or 85%, by plan | Same page, and the [April 2026 plan update](https://turo.com/blog/canada/news/host-protection-plans-update-april-2026/) |

## What does the protection plan change in a Turo budget?

The protection plan changes how much of the trip price you keep and how much damage responsibility you accept. Turo's April 2026 Canada post sets More earnings at 85% and $2,000 of damage responsibility, Balanced at 75% and $250, and More peace of mind at 65% and $0, with a replacement vehicle only on the last of those. A higher percentage is not a higher profit if one claim wipes out several trips. The list-your-car page says the public summary is incomplete. The plan in your account is the one to budget.

British Columbia hosts should also budget the delivery period as uncovered time. Turo's hosting guide says B.C. coverage is active during the reservation period only. Driving the car to a guest in Vancouver is not the same coverage window as a reservation that has started.

## What should you ask your insurer and your lender before you list?

Ask whether the personal policy allows peer-to-peer car sharing, what you pay if it does, and what is excluded while the car is on a trip and while it is not. Turo says you must carry your own insurance and that the insurer must approve participation. A premium change belongs in the monthly sketch as a quote, not as a number from this page.

Ask the lender or lessor whether the contract allows you to rent the car to other drivers. A finance payment does not pause because a week has no booking. If the answer is no, the startup cost of hosting is a different car, or it is zero because you do not list.

## What ongoing costs do Turo hosts in Canada actually pay?

Ongoing costs are cleaning, wear, parking, and the ownership costs Turo's earnings footnote says it does not subtract.

**Cleaning.** Interior wipe-downs, vacuuming, and the occasional car wash. Prices vary by city. A bottle of cleaner and towels are a small kit. <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="Interior cleaner and microfibre towels" search="car interior cleaner" /> cover that kit. A detail shop is optional and easy to overspend on a slow month.

**Wear and repairs.** Brakes, tires, windshields, and winter tires are ownership costs whether or not Turo reimburses a guest-caused incident. Damage responsibility on your plan is the amount you are expected to absorb on a covered incident, on the terms Turo publishes. Exterior wear is not the same as a crash.

**Parking.** A condo stall you could have rented to someone else is a cost of hosting, even when you do not write a cheque. The alternative use of that stall is [renting out a parking space](/side-hustles/rent-out-parking-space-canada/).

**Depreciation.** Extra kilometres and extra drivers can reduce what the car is worth when you sell it. There is no single Canadian depreciation table for hosted cars. Track kilometres and keep the purchase papers so you can see the change yourself.

**Your time.** Handoffs and messages are unpaid unless you build them into the daily price. A late return that makes you miss work is a real cost.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers. It is not a quote, a forecast, or Turo's median.

Suppose guest trip prices in a month add up to $800. Suppose you are on the 75% plan, so your share of trip price is $600. Suppose your insurer adds $40 that month because you disclosed car sharing. Suppose washes and supplies are $60. Suppose you set aside $80 for wear. Suppose the stall would have rented for $150, so you count $150 as a cost of keeping the car there. Cash left before the loan and before depreciation is $600 − $40 − $60 − $80 − $150 = $270.

Suppose the car payment is $450. Then $270 − $450 = −$180 before depreciation and before the hours you spent on handoffs. A paid-off car skips the $450 line and the sketch looks different. Change any input and the result changes. Turo's published median of $657 is a separate figure, described in the footnote as not reduced for maintenance and insurance. Do not paste $657 into this sketch and call it profit.

## How do you sketch break-even for a Turo car in Canada?

### Step 1: Write the trip-price share you actually keep

Use 65%, 75%, or 85% from the plan in your account, not from memory.

### Step 2: Add the bills that exist with zero bookings

Insurance, the loan or lease, parking, and winter storage if the car sits. These are the bills a quiet January still has.

### Step 3: Add a per-trip cleaning and wear amount from your own receipts

Use what you paid last month. A guess from a forum is not a receipt.

### Step 4: Subtract those costs from the share you keep

If the remainder is negative, the daily price is too low, the car is booked too little, or the payment is too large for this use. Hosting does not make a payment smaller.

## What mistakes show up in Turo host budgets?

- **Counting the trip price, not the plan percentage.** The guest's total is not your deposit.
- **Leaving the car payment out because "the car was already bought."** The payment continues.
- **Ignoring a stall you could have rented.** An empty stall has a value. See [parking rental](/side-hustles/rent-out-parking-space-canada/).
- **Skipping the insurer's answer.** An unapproved listing can be a coverage problem that costs more than a month of trips.
- **Treating a reimbursement for fuel as income.** Turo says hosts are reimbursed for fuel and for mileage beyond the limit. A reimbursement replaces money you spent.

## Which tax rules apply to Turo host costs in Canada?

Net hosting income, if the activity is a business, goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). A vehicle used to earn income may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion, under the class and first-year rules for that year. This guide does not assign a class. Personal kilometres stay out. Self-employed net income is also subject to both shares of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html). GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) for taxable supplies. Keep the trip records with [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/). This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
