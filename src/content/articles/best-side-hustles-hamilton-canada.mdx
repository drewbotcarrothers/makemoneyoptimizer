## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Hamilton, Canada?

### Step 1: Clear sidewalks and driveways against the 24-hour rule

The City's [sidewalk snow clearing page](https://www.hamilton.ca/home-neighbourhood/getting-around/streets-sidewalks/sidewalk-snow-clearing) says Snow and Ice By-law 03-296 requires owners and occupants to clear snow and ice within 24 hours after the end of a snow event. That covers adjacent sidewalks and corners, plus roofs that overhang a City sidewalk. If the property is on the City's sidewalk clearing route, the City handles the sidewalk. Ask each client whether they're on the route, then price sidewalk and driveway together. See [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) and the [snow removal first clients guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-first-clients-canada/).

### Step 2: Keep handyman work inside the licence line

Hamilton's [business licence reference guide](https://www.hamilton.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/businesslicence-reference-guide-jan2026.pdf) lists Building Repair Contractor under Schedule 29 of By-law 07-170. It covers additions, prefabricated structures, roofing, siding, decks, chimneys and fireplaces, porches, replacement windows, insulation and framing. A new licence costs $92 to process (including HST) plus a $341 licence fee. Furniture assembly, mounting, small repairs and painting aren't on that list. If you want deck or window jobs, get the licence first. The [handyman side hustle guide](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/) and [handyman pricing](/side-hustles/handyman-pricing-canada/) cover the rest.

### Step 3: Walk dogs, but don't run a kennel by accident

The same guide defines a kennel as any lot, building or structure used for "the registered pure-bred breeding, raising, keeping, training, or boarding of four or more dogs", which must also meet zoning. Walking a few dogs isn't covered. If you plan to board several dogs at home, ask the City whether the kennel licence applies before you take bookings. Start with [dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) and [Rover vs independent dog walking](/side-hustles/rover-vs-independent-dog-walking-canada/).

## Which apps work in Hamilton, Canada?

Hamilton is on [TaskRabbit's list of locations](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/locations), which suits assembly, mounting and moving help; the [TaskRabbit review](/side-hustles/taskrabbit-review-canada/) explains fees. ## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Hamilton, Canada?

Only if your activity is on the City's list. The [reference guide](https://www.hamilton.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/businesslicence-reference-guide-jan2026.pdf) sets out every licensed category under By-law 07-170, with fees. For side hustlers, the ones to check are building repair contractors, kennels, and food shops or food service vehicles. If you use a name other than your own, [register a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/) with Ontario.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you offer handyman help and winter clearing in your neighbourhood. You'd list furniture assembly, TV mounting, small repairs and painting as services. You'd leave decks and replacement windows off until you hold a building repair contractor licence. In winter, you'd ask each client whether the City clears their sidewalk. Then you'd quote either driveway only or driveway plus sidewalk within the 24-hour window.

## What mistakes do Hamilton side hustlers make?

- **Building decks without a licence.** Decks, porches, roofing and siding are licensed building repair work.
- **Boarding four or more dogs at home without asking.** It can fall under the kennel licence.
- **Forgetting overhanging roofs.** The by-law includes roofs that overhang a City sidewalk.
- **Clearing sidewalks the City already clears.** Check the route before you charge for it.

## How are Hamilton side hustles taxed?

You report self-employment income on Form T2125 and register for HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from their first fare. Ontario's HST rate is 13%.
