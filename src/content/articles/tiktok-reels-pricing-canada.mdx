Charging for TikTok and Reels videos in Canada means a price per finished clip or a monthly package for a business, built from the real hours behind each clip, plus a fee when clips run as paid ads. Canadian studios publish prices from $299 a reel in Ottawa to $1,200 a reel in BC's Fraser Valley, and monthly packages from $800 for four reels in Toronto. Job Bank's $34.62 median hourly wage for employed video editors is a floor for the time part of the price, because a freelancer also carries equipment, unpaid pitching and both halves of CPP. ## Which pricing models work for TikTok and Reels clients?

Short-form video for businesses is sold per reel, as a monthly package, as a filming session with editing priced per clip, or as raw footage, with usage terms on top. The published prices above use all four.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per reel | One price per finished clip, set number of revisions | First jobs, one-off promotions | Unlimited revisions |
| Monthly package | Set number of reels per month, filmed in one or two sessions | Local businesses that post weekly | Scope creep into posting and replies |
| Filming session + editing | Session on site, edit priced per clip | Restaurants, events, shops | Travel time and parking |
| Raw footage only | Unedited clips for the client's team | Businesses with an editor | Usage terms still apply |
| Usage add-on | Extra fee when clips run as paid ads or beyond a set period | Clips used in ads | Not writing the period down |

Find You Media's prices show how ownership is priced: its exclusive reel with full rights is $600 more than its standard collaborative reel ($1,800 − $1,200), or 50% more.

## How do you calculate a TikTok and Reels price?

Price each clip from your hours, then add a percentage for any paid-ad use, with the period written beside it.

```text
Hours per clip = planning + share of shoot + share of travel
                 + editing + captions/music + 1 revision
Clip price     = hours × hourly rate + equipment share
Package        = clips × clip price  (batching in one shoot lowers hours)
Posting        = scheduling hours × hourly rate  (only if included)
Usage fee      = clip price × usage %  (per clip, per period, per platform)
Check          = package ÷ total hours  ≥  your hourly target
```

**Set the hourly target above Job Bank's median.** A target of $45 to $50 an hour sits between the $34.62 median and the $50.00 high for employed video editors. **Time one real clip** from brief to export, including a reshoot and captions. **Price usage separately.** Calgary-area agency IF Media's [2026 UGC guide](https://ifmedia.ca/ugc-creator-rates-in-canada-2026-the-complete-pricing-guide/) adds 20% to 35% of the base for 30 days of paid ads on one platform, 50% to 75% for 90 days, and 100% to 200% for a buyout. Those percentages are written for UGC creators, but the logic is the same when a business runs your reel as an ad.

## What do worked TikTok and Reels quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** Hours, hourly targets, equipment shares and the usage percentages chosen inside IF Media's ranges are my assumptions. GST/HST lines apply only once you're registered, at the rate for the client's province.

### Quote 1: one reel for an Ottawa café

- Planning 0.5 h, filming 0.5 h, travel 0.5 h, editing and captions 0.75 h, one revision 0.25 h = 2.5 hours.
- 2.5 × $45 = $112.50, plus a $10 equipment share = $122.50, rounded to **$125**.
- With 13% HST: $125 × 1.13 = $141.25.
- Hourly check: $125 ÷ 2.5 = $50 an hour. Ottawa Reels lists $299 for one reel, so $125 is a beginner's price with room to rise.

### Quote 2: four reels a month for a Toronto restaurant, one shoot

- Planning 1 h + shoot 2 h + travel 1 h + editing 4 × 1.25 h (5 h) + revisions 4 × 0.25 h (1 h) = 10 hours.
- 10 × $45 = $450, plus a $40 monthly equipment share = $490, rounded to **$495 a month**, or $123.75 a reel.
- With 13% HST: $495 × 1.13 = $559.35.
- Manisoft's comparable four-reel package is $800 + HST; the gap is what a studio's track record and planning help add.

### Quote 3: eight reels plus posting for a Calgary gym

- Planning 2 h + two shoots 4 h + travel 2 h + editing 8 × 1.25 h (10 h) + revisions 8 × 0.25 h (2 h) = 20 hours × $50 = $1,000.
- Scheduling posts on TikTok and Instagram: 4 h × $50 = $200. Equipment share: $40.
- Total **$1,240 a month**. With 5% GST (Alberta): $1,240 × 1.05 = $1,302.
- Hourly check: $1,240 ÷ 24 h = $51.67. Ottawa Reels lists 10 reels for $1,799 without posting; Monk Creatives' starter social packages begin at $1,200.

### Quote 4: the restaurant runs two reels as paid ads

- Clip price from Quote 2: $123.75.
- Paid ads on Instagram for 30 days at 30%: 2 × $123.75 × 0.30 = **$74.25**.
- Paid ads for 90 days at 60%: 2 × $123.75 × 0.60 = **$148.50**.
- A full buyout at 150%: 2 × $123.75 × 1.50 = **$371.25**, with no renewal fee later. For comparison, Find You Media charges 50% more for its full-rights reel.

## How should you write a TikTok and Reels package agreement?

Put the clip count, shoot dates, revisions, posting and usage in one page before the first shoot.

```text
SHORT-FORM VIDEO PACKAGE — [Business]     Date: [date]
Deliverables: 4 vertical reels per month, up to 45 s, captions included
Shoot: 1 session per month, up to 2 hours, at [address]; extra
  locations quoted separately
Revisions: 1 round per reel; extra rounds $25
Posting: [not included / scheduled on TikTok and Instagram, +$200]
Not included: replying to comments or DMs, running ads, ad spend
Usage: organic on [business]'s own accounts. Paid-ad use is +30% per
  clip per 30 days per platform, agreed in writing before the ad runs
Price: $495 per month, paid before each month's shoot
Term: month to month; 30 days' notice to cancel or change price
Prices before GST/HST [or: plus 13% HST, # ______]
```

## How do you raise TikTok and Reels prices?

- **Track hours per reel.** If a package that was priced at 10 hours is taking 13, the hourly check has dropped below your target and the price should change.
- **Move toward the published range.** Ottawa Reels' $299 single reel and Manisoft's $200-a-reel package are reference points once your portfolio shows results for local businesses.
- **Price usage every time.** Clips that start running as ads are a fee, not a favour.
- **Give monthly clients 30 days' notice** and change new-client prices first. For the scripts, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).
- **Re-quote when scope grows.** More locations, shoots or revisions need a new package price, not just a new month.

## What TikTok and Reels pricing mistakes are common?

Common short-form video pricing mistakes are pricing only the editing time, leaving usage open-ended, and agreeing to post and reply to comments for free. Each one turns a package into a part-time job at a low rate.

If you post a paid clip on your own account, the Competition Bureau's [influencer guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says to disclose material connections such as payment or free products, clearly and in the post itself. Put revisions, usage and what "posting" includes in writing. The [TikTok and Reels startup costs guide](/side-hustles/tiktok-reels-startup-costs-canada/) helps you count the equipment share.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect TikTok and Reels prices?

Video income from clients is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net earnings. GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses. When registered, use the rate from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html), such as 5% GST in Alberta or 13% HST in Ontario.
