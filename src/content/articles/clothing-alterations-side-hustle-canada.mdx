A clothing alterations side hustle in Canada hems pants, takes in waists, replaces zippers and shortens dresses from home, priced per job. Canadian alteration shops that publish price lists charge about $15 to $28 for a pant hem, $35 to $50 to take in a waist, and $30 to $60 or more per layer to shorten a dress. November and December bring holiday-party dresses and suits, so the season starts now. Prices below were read on each shop's own page on October 19, 2026.

<QuickAnswer>

- **Pant hems:** from $14.99 plus tax (Ottawa), $18 standard and $20 to $28 for jeans (Topology, Vancouver), $20 to $26 (Calgary Seamstress).
- **Bigger jobs:** dress pant waist $35 to $40, jeans waist $50, jacket zipper $55 to $85, men's suit sleeves $55, dress shortening $30 to $60 per layer (Calgary Seamstress).

- **Shops price after a fitting:** the Ottawa shop says every garment is inspected and fitted in person before the final price is confirmed.

## How does an alterations side hustle compare with a tailoring job?

An alterations side hustle pays per job, while a tailoring job pays an hourly wage. Job Bank's [tailor wage page](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/21596/ca), updated November 19, 2025 from 2023-2024 Labour Force Survey data, gives a useful floor:

| Region | Low | Median | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canada | $15.50 | $19.00 | $26.00 |
| British Columbia | $18.25 | $20.00 | $35.00 |
| Ontario | $17.95 | $20.00 | $28.00 |
| Alberta | $15.00 | $19.00 | $26.00 |
| Quebec | $16.60 | $18.00 | $23.00 |

If a $18 hem takes you 30 minutes including the fitting, that is $36 an hour before costs, nearly double the national median wage. If it takes an hour while you learn, it is $18, below it. Speed is the business.

## Which alteration jobs sell in November and December?

The alteration jobs that sell in November and December are the ones tied to holiday parties, gifts and winter clothes. These 15 jobs suit a home sewer, each with why it sells:

1. **Party dress hems.** One date, one dress, a deadline.
2. **Suit trouser hems for office parties.** Quick, repeatable work.

4. **Taking in a dress at the sides.** Fitted holiday dresses.

6. **Shortening winter coat sleeves.** New coats bought in fall sales.
7. **Jeans hems for gifted jeans.** Gifts in the wrong length after December 25.
8. **Hoodie and jacket zipper inserts.** Calgary Seamstress lists hoodie zipper inserts at $40.
9. **Snow pants and kids' gear repairs.** Rips and broken snaps.
10. **Curtain shortening before guests arrive.** Per panel pricing.
11. **Tablecloth hems for holiday dinners.** Simple straight seams.
12. **Costume and theatre adjustments.** School concerts and pageants.
13. **Uniform alterations for seasonal staff.** Retail and hospitality hires.
14. **Wedding party fittings for winter weddings.** Book weeks ahead.

## Illustrative example: one holiday-season week from home

This is an illustrative example. The starting prices come from the shop pages above; the job mix, your times and thread costs are assumptions.

| Job (assumed count) | Your price (assumed, under shop prices) | Revenue | Assumed minutes each |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 pant hems | $16 | $128 | 30 |
| 3 dress hems (one layer) | $28 | $84 | 50 |
| 2 jacket sleeve shortenings | $45 | $90 | 75 |
| 1 coat zipper replacement | $55 | $55 | 90 |
| **Total** | | **$357** | **8 × 30 + 3 × 50 + 2 × 75 + 90 = 630 minutes** |

- Hours: 630 ÷ 60 = 10.5 hours of sewing, plus an assumed 2 hours of fittings and pickups = 12.5 hours.
- Assumed materials: $2 thread per job (14 jobs = $28) and $15 for the zipper = $43.
- Left before tax: $357 − $43 = $314, or $314 ÷ 12.5 = about $25 an hour.
- The machine: $209.99 ÷ $314 = 0.67, so under one week of this mix covers the Brother XM2701M.

At the same mix, cutting the pant hem from 30 to 20 minutes saves 80 minutes and lifts the hourly figure to about $28. Fitting and pinning accurately the first time is where that time comes from.

## Which tax rules apply to an alterations side hustle in Canada?

Alterations income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), with thread, zippers, a share of the machine through capital cost allowance and home-office costs as possible expenses. GST/HST registration is required once taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive calendar quarters, under the CRA's [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) rules. Home workspace claims are explained in the [home office expenses guide](/side-hustles/home-office-expenses-side-hustle-canada/). This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
