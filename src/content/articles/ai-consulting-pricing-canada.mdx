## What do AI consultants earn per hour in Canada?

Employed AI consultants in Canada earn a median of $46.15 an hour, according to [Job Bank's AI consultant wage page](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/24508/ca) (NOC 21211, data scientists, reference period 2023–2024). Those are employee wages; a freelance rate should sit above the median once you add unbilled time and costs.

| Area (Job Bank, AI consultant) | Low | Median | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canada | $30.00 | $46.15 | $69.74 |
| Ontario | $31.25 | $47.69 | $71.79 |
| British Columbia | $32.45 | $48.08 | $88.94 |
| Alberta | $28.00 | $43.27 | $57.69 |
| Quebec | $31.73 | $46.00 | $64.84 |

The NOC group covers data scientists, so the high end reflects technical work such as building models. A side hustle that teaches small businesses to use existing tools will often price nearer the median at first.

## Which pricing models work for AI consulting in Canada?

AI consulting in Canada is usually sold as a fixed-scope project, hourly work, or a monthly retainer. Fixed scope suits small businesses, because the owner knows the total before you start.

| Model | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed-scope project | Audit, workshop, one workflow set-up | Scope creep: list what is not included |
| Hourly | Open-ended questions, troubleshooting | Clients may cut sessions short to save money |
| Monthly retainer | Ongoing help, updates as tools change | Define hours or requests included |
| Workshop fee | Training a team in one session | Preparation time is often longer than delivery |

Tool subscriptions the client uses should be on the client's own account, so they keep access and pay the vendor directly.

## How do you calculate an AI consulting price in Canada?

Calculate an AI consulting price in Canada from your hourly rate times the hours for every stage, including the discovery call, research, testing, documentation, and a follow-up. Then add a buffer for tools changing mid-project.

### Step 1: Set your hourly rate

Start from Job Bank's median, then add unbilled sales and admin time and both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).

### Step 2: List each stage of the project

Discovery, research, testing on the client's real tasks, written instructions, and handover.

### Step 3: Estimate hours per stage

Testing usually takes longer than expected, because outputs vary.

### Step 4: Add a buffer

A share of hours for revisions and tool changes.

### Step 5: Round and write the scope

State the tools, tasks, deliverables, and what is excluded.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose a small accounting office wants help drafting client emails with an AI tool. You estimate 1 hour of discovery, 3 hours of testing prompts on sample emails, 2 hours writing a short guide, and 2 hours of staff training: 1 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 8 hours.

At an assumed $65 an hour, between Job Bank's median and high, labour is 8 × $65 = $520. Add a 15% buffer: $520 × 0.15 = $78, for $598, rounded to $600. A 30-day follow-up call of 1 hour is listed as included.

## When should you raise AI consulting prices in Canada?

Raise AI consulting prices in Canada when clients rebook, when your calendar is full, or when projects regularly run past your estimates. Raise prices for new quotes and give retainer clients written notice.

Track actual hours against quoted hours for every project. If testing always runs long, the fix is a higher estimate, not unpaid evenings.

## What AI consulting pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common AI consulting pricing mistakes in Canada are quoting before you know what data the client will use, promising results the tools cannot guarantee, and leaving privacy out of the scope.

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner's [principles for generative AI](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/gd_principles_ai/) cover legal authority and consent, limiting collection, openness, and accountability when personal information goes into AI tools. [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) applies to private-sector organizations in commercial activity; Alberta, British Columbia, and Quebec have their own substantially similar laws. Ask clients whether customer data will go into a tool, and price the time to plan for it. Other mistakes: paying for client tool subscriptions on your card and selling "AI strategy" without a deliverable.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect AI consulting prices in Canada?

AI consulting income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses.
