## Which pricing models work for AI consulting in Canada?

AI consulting in Canada is usually sold as a fixed-scope project, hourly work or a monthly retainer. Every firm above publishes fixed prices, because small business owners want the total before work starts.

| Model | Best for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed-scope project | Audit, workshop, one workflow set-up | Scope creep: list what isn't included |
| Paid discovery (like Remolda's $490 review) | First step with a new client | Credit it toward the project if they go ahead |
| Hourly | Open-ended questions, troubleshooting | Clients cutting sessions short to save money |
| Monthly retainer | Ongoing help as tools change | Define hours or requests included |
| Workshop fee | Training a team in one session | Preparation often takes longer than delivery |

Tool subscriptions the client uses go on the client's own account, so they keep access and pay the vendor directly.

## How do you calculate an AI consulting price?

Set an hourly rate from a wage benchmark adjusted for unbilled time, multiply by the hours for every stage, and add a buffer for testing and tool changes.

```text
Hourly rate = wage benchmark × (total working hours ÷ billable hours)
Hours       = discovery + research + testing on real tasks + documentation
              + training + follow-up
Price       = hours × hourly rate × (1 + buffer 10% to 20%)
Retainer    = monthly hours × hourly rate × (1 − commitment discount)
Tax         = + GST/HST at the client's provincial rate, once registered
Check       = price vs Remolda $490 / $2,900 and chamber $79–$98 per seat
```

**Illustrative rate.** If one in three working hours goes to unpaid sales and admin, total ÷ billable = 1.5, and $46.15 × 1.5 = $69.23. Rounded up, $75 an hour, which sits between Job Bank's national median and high for employed AI consultants.

## Which templates help scope and quote AI work?

A discovery checklist that sets the price:

```text
DISCOVERY — [Business]                         Date: [date]
1. Which 3 tasks take the most staff time each week? (hours each)
2. Which tools do you already pay for? (accounts in whose name?)
3. Will customer or employee personal information go into an AI tool?
   If yes: which data, consent basis, and who approves?
4. Who will use the workflow, and how comfortable are they with it?
5. What does "done" look like? (time saved, a written guide, trained staff)
6. Budget range and decision date
```

A fixed-scope quote:

```text
QUOTE — [Business]   Valid 30 days
Scope: set up [tool] to draft [task] for [team], using [data source]
Included: discovery (1 h), testing on 20 real examples (3 h),
  written guide (2 h), 2 h training session, 30-day follow-up call
Not included: tool subscriptions, integrations with [system],
  work on additional tasks (billed at $75/h on approval)
Data: no personal information in tools without your written approval;
  test data supplied by you with names removed
Price: $690 [+ HST 13% = $779.70 if registered]
Payment: 50% to book, 50% on delivery
```

A change request when the scope grows:

```text
Hi [Name], adding [new task] is outside the current scope. It would take
about [3] hours, so $[225] at my $75 rate. Reply "approved" and I'll
start after the current work is delivered.
```

## How do you raise AI consulting prices?

- **Track actual vs quoted hours** for every project. If testing always runs long, raise the estimate, not your unpaid evenings.
- **Sell a paid first step.** Remolda prices discovery as a $490 product; charge for yours and credit it toward the project.
- **Package what repeats.** Once you've built the same workflow three times, price it as a fixed package above your hourly maths.
- **Raise for new clients first,** and give retainer clients 30 days' written notice.
- **Add written deliverables** (guides, policies, a one-page AI use policy) that justify a higher fixed price. Scripts for the conversation are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What AI consulting pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common AI consulting pricing mistakes in Canada are quoting before you know what data the client will use, promising results the tools can't guarantee, paying for client subscriptions on your own card, and leaving privacy out of the scope.

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner's [principles for generative AI](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/gd_principles_ai/) cover legal authority and consent, limiting collection, openness and accountability when personal information goes into AI tools. [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) applies to private-sector organizations in commercial activity; Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec have their own substantially similar laws. Price the time to plan for it.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect AI consulting prices in Canada?

AI consulting income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net earnings. GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses. Once registered, charge the rate for the client's province, as the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) explains. How much to hold back is in [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
