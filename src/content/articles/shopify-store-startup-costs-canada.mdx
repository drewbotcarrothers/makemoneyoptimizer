**Quick answer:** a lean Shopify store in Canada costs about **$49** in its first month: $1 for Shopify's introductory month plus a phone tripod and 100 poly mailers at **$48.30** with 13% Ontario HST. A standard setup that adds a shipping scale, a light box, a tape gun and a custom domain costs **$156.18** in gear and domain, and about **$1,100 in the first year** once the Basic plan at its standard price and a starting e-commerce insurance policy of about $500 are included. A pro setup with a thermal label printer costs **$385.16** in gear and about **$1,342** in the first year. Gear prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026; plan prices are from Shopify's Canadian pricing page the same day. At an assumed $40 product that costs $12 to make or buy, the standard first year pays back after 42 orders.

How to list one product and get to the first order is in the [Shopify store side hustle guide](/side-hustles/shopify-store-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash: what Shopify charges, what to buy to ship, what each item cost on a specific date, and how many orders recover it.

## How much does a Shopify store cost to start in Canada?

A Shopify store costs $49.30 in its first month on the lean setup, and $492.30, $1,100.18 or $1,341.98 across the first year for the lean, standard and pro setups. Plan prices are from [Shopify's Canada pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/ca/pricing) on October 9, 2026; gear is Amazon.ca on the same date, with 13% Ontario HST.

| Setup | Gear with 13% HST | Gear with 5% GST | Custom domain | Basic plan, first 12 months | Insurance (per year) | First-year cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $48.30 | $44.88 | None (myshopify.com address) | $444 | None for a digital or very low-risk product | $492.30 |
| Standard | $143.36 | $133.21 | $12.82 | $444 | About $500 | $1,100.18 |
| Pro | $385.16 | $357.89 | $12.82 | $444 | About $500 | $1,341.98 |

The plan line is three $1 introductory months plus nine months of Basic at $49, which is $3 + $441 = $444. Paying yearly from day one at $37 a month is also 12 × $37 = $444, so the first-year plan cost is the same either way; the yearly route gives up the cheap test months, and the monthly route keeps the option to stop. Alberta and the territories pay 5% GST only on gear; the [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province.

The plan is the largest line in every setup. The lean and standard gear each cost less than four months of Basic at $49, which is why the first decision is whether you have a product people will buy, not which printer to get.

## Who should use a Shopify startup budget in Canada?

This budget fits people who already have a product: something they make, a line they already sell at markets or on a marketplace, or a digital file they can support. It suits sellers who want their own checkout, their own customer list and no marketplace listing fees, and who can bring their own traffic from social media, email or local customers.

It is a poor fit if the product is still "something trending," if you have no way to send people to the store, or if $49 a month would hurt before the first order. A marketplace brings buyers with it; Shopify does not. The trade-off is laid out in [Etsy vs Shopify in Canada](/side-hustles/etsy-vs-shopify-canada/), and website builders that also sell are compared in [Shopify vs Squarespace vs Wix](/side-hustles/shopify-vs-squarespace-vs-wix-canada/). Selling secondhand goods on marketplaces with no subscription is priced in [reselling startup costs](/side-hustles/reselling-startup-costs-canada/).

## What does Shopify charge in Canada?

Shopify's Canadian pricing page listed these prices in CAD on October 9, 2026:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Yearly billing (per month) | Online card rate (Shopify Payments) | Third-party payment fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | $49 | $37 | 2.8% + 30¢ | 2% |
| Grow | $132 | $99 | 2.6% + 30¢ | 1% |
| Advanced | $517 | $389 | 2.4% + 30¢ | 0.6% |
| Plus | From $3,400 | | 2.15% + 30¢ | |

New stores get 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months, then the standard price. On Basic, American Express and international cards are 3.5% + 30¢ CAD online. In-person card payments on Basic are 2.6% with no fixed fee, and in-person Interac is 15¢. The third-party fee applies only if you take payments through a provider other than Shopify Payments, on top of that provider's own charge.

Hosting, a TLS certificate, a myshopify.com address and free Shopify Academy courses are included in every plan. Basic includes buying and printing shipping labels from the admin. Grow adds 5 staff accounts and lower card rates; neither matters for a one-person store with a few orders a week. POS Pro, for selling in person with Shopify's tools, is $119 a month per location and is not part of a startup budget.

A custom domain is optional. Shopify's [domains page](https://www.shopify.com/ca/domains) says domains bought through Shopify typically start at $9 USD a year, including WHOIS privacy, and renewal pricing can differ from the first year. At the Bank of Canada's October 8, 2026 rate of 1.4240 CAD per USD, $9 USD is $12.82 CAD. Some extensions cost more; the checkout shows the real price.

### Costs that are not gear

- **Product samples.** If someone else makes your product, order one to your own address before you publish a delivery time. The sample costs whatever the supplier charges and shows you the real packaging and shipping time.
- **Inventory.** Buy the smallest batch the supplier allows. Stock that has not sold is cash you cannot spend on the plan.
- **Business name.** Selling under your own legal name needs no registration in most provinces. A trade name in Ontario is $60 to register online for five years, per the [Ontario government fee page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/cost-time-required-to-register-change-search-for-business-name-corporation-not-for-profit). Other provinces are in [how to register a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).
- **Import costs.** Stock you bring in from outside Canada is a commercial import, with duties and taxes set out in the CBSA [guide to importing commercial goods](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/guide-eng.html). Add them to the product cost before you set the price.

## How much is e-commerce insurance in Canada?

[Zensurance](https://www.zensurance.com/e-commerce-insurance) says a low-risk small e-commerce business can expect a starting cost of approximately $500 a year for a basic policy, with high-risk or expensive products costing more. The standard and pro totals above use $500.

Two covers matter for an online store. **Product liability** covers injury or property damage caused by something you sell, including items you did not make yourself; Zensurance's example is a phone charger bought from a Shopify store that overheats. **Cyber liability** covers a breach of customer data, which matters more if you keep customer details outside Shopify in spreadsheets or email tools. A digital-only store selling templates or guides has a different exposure, which is why the lean column leaves insurance at a quote rather than a fixed number. Compare quote routes in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for a Shopify store in Canada?

Every order has to cover the card fee, the product, the mailer and the label before anything goes toward the plan and the gear.

### Illustrative example: one physical order

**Sourced inputs:** Basic's 2.8% + 30¢ online card rate, the $16.75 for 100 mailers and $29.99 for 500 labels. **Assumed inputs:** a $40 product price, $12 to make or buy the item, and shipping charged to the customer at what it costs you.

- Card fee: 0.028 × $40 = $1.12, plus $0.30 = **$1.42**
- Mailer: $16.75 ÷ 100 = **$0.17**
- Label: $29.99 ÷ 500 = **$0.06**
- Left per order: $40 − $1.42 − $12 − $0.17 − $0.06 = **$26.35**

| Setup | First-year cash | ÷ $26.35 per order | Orders to break even |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $492.30 | 18.68 | 19 |
| Standard | $1,100.18 | 41.75 | 42 |
| Pro | $1,341.98 | 50.93 | 51 |

Once the introductory months end, Basic at $49 a month needs $49 ÷ $26.35 = 1.86, so 2 orders a month just to cover the plan; at the yearly $37 it is 1.40, still 2 orders. A store selling fewer than two orders a month at this margin loses money on the plan alone.

### Illustrative example: a digital product

**Assumed inputs:** a $20 template or guide, no product cost, no shipping. **Sourced input:** the 2.8% + 30¢ card rate.

- Card fee: 0.028 × $20 = $0.56, plus $0.30 = **$0.86**
- Left per sale: $20 − $0.86 = **$19.14**
- First year on the lean plan with only the tripod: $444 + $29.37 = $473.37
- Break-even: $473.37 ÷ $19.14 = 24.73, so **25 sales** in the year

Lower-priced files carry a bigger share of the 30¢ fixed fee. A $5 file leaves $5 − $0.44 = $4.56, and the same first year needs 104 sales.

## What could change these Shopify numbers?

Your product margin moves the break-even more than any gear choice. At a $30 price and the same $12 cost, each order leaves $30 − $1.14 − $12 − $0.23 = $16.63, and the standard first year needs 67 orders instead of 42. Shopify's card rates and plan prices change; the introductory offer especially is set by Shopify and can be different when you sign up. Insurance quotes depend on the product: Zensurance names fireworks and luxury watches as examples that cost more. Amazon.ca prices were checked October 9, 2026 and move with sales. The Ontario 13% HST and 5% GST columns bracket most provinces; the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) gives the rest. ## Where can Shopify startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Use the introductory months to build, not to wait.** Three $1 months are enough to list products, set shipping rates and place a test order. Launch before month four.
- **Stay on Basic.** Grow costs $83 a month more on monthly billing; its lower card rate saves 0.2 percentage points, which is $2 on $1,000 of sales.
- **Keep Shopify Payments.** A third-party provider adds a 2% fee on Basic on top of its own rate.
- **Skip paid apps and themes at launch.** The free themes and built-in editor run a one-product store.
- **Photograph in daylight first.** The light box earns its place once you list many items or sell through winter.
- **Print labels on paper until volume justifies a thermal printer.** The $89.99 printer saves time, not postage.

## Where should Shopify spending not be cut in Canada?

- **The standard price in your plan.** Budget from the $49 or $37 figure, not the $1 months.
- **A real product photo and an honest delivery time.** The Competition Bureau's guidance on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) applies to product claims and shipping promises on your store.
- **Product liability once you ship physical goods.** About $500 a year is cheaper than one claim from a product that injures someone.
- **A returns buffer.** A refund returns the sale but not the outgoing postage or card fee. Keep enough cash for at least one refund before you advertise.
- **Separate money.** A separate account for store payouts makes the T2125 easy. Options are compared in [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/); <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is another no-fee option.

## Which tax rules apply to a Shopify budget in Canada?

Shopify store income in Canada is business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both shares of [Canada Pension Plan contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. The plan, card fees, mailers, labels, apps and the cost of goods sold are generally current expenses. A label printer or light box that lasts several years is equipment claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion.

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting all your side hustles. A store is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. Digital products sold to buyers outside Canada raise their own questions, covered on the CRA [digital economy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) pages.

Once registered, charge the rate for the buyer's province from the [CRA rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html), and claim [input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) for the GST/HST on your plan and gear. Shopify can calculate tax at checkout, but turning that on does not decide whether you must be registered. Below the threshold you do not charge GST/HST and do not claim the credits. This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.
