## How do you get your first TaskRabbit job in Canada?

### Step 1: Open real availability blocks

TaskRabbit's [get more tasks page](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260519260699-How-Do-I-Get-More-Tasks) says that if you mark yourself unavailable, you won't show up in client searches or get invitations. Taskers usually need three consecutive hours of availability to show up. Set only times you can actually work, such as two weekday evenings and a full Saturday.

### Step 2: Widen your work area and skills

The same page suggests extending your Work Area Map and adding every skill you're comfortable with. Early on, a slightly bigger area and more categories mean more invitations. Narrow it again once you're busy.

### Step 3: Write a quick pitch for each category

TaskRabbit says the [quick pitch](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260464215707-How-Do-I-Write-an-Effective-Quick-Pitch) is the client's first impression and can be set per category. A good one shows your experience and any certifications, lists your tools, explains why you're the best choice, and names your limits. For example, a furniture assembly pitch might say: "Assembled flat-pack furniture for friends and family for years. I bring a drill, bits and a level. I'll recycle the boxes on request. No wall anchoring into brick."

### Step 4: Price to get the first reviews

The app's pricing guidance in "Skills & Rates" is based on your experience level and what clients book in your area. Start near it to win first bookings, then raise rates as reviews build. [Odd-jobs apps pricing](/side-hustles/odd-jobs-apps-pricing-canada/) explains how to work out a floor that covers travel and tax.

### Step 5: Turn on Same Day invitations

TaskRabbit says the Same Day Invitations toggle resets at midnight each night. Turn it back on whenever you can take same-day work. Quick same-day jobs are a common way to get early reviews.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not real earnings. Suppose you sign up in Ottawa for furniture assembly, TV mounting and help moving. You'd open Tuesday and Thursday 5 to 9 p.m. and Saturday 9 to 5, write a pitch for each category, and set rates near the app's guidance. You'd turn on Same Day each evening. After five good reviews in assembly, you'd raise that rate and drop the category you liked least.

## What billing rules should new Taskers know in Canada?

TaskRabbit's [rates and minimum hours policy](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260502667035-Tasker-Rates-Minimum-Hours-Policy) says Taskers set their own hourly rates in most categories. All hourly tasks have a one-hour minimum, and tasks over an hour are invoiced in 15-minute increments. You can choose a 2-hour minimum, but you must note it in your category description and in chat. A few categories are fixed-price, paid on completion rather than hours. Joining costs a one-time, non-refundable [$25 registration fee](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260480151579-What-s-the-Registration-Fee). You must meet the [Tasker requirements](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260520394651-What-s-Required-to-Become-a-Tasker), including being at least 19.

## What does Elite status take on TaskRabbit in Canada?

Elite is a later goal, not a first-month one. TaskRabbit's [Elite requirements](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260521586715-What-s-Required-to-Become-an-Elite-Tasker) list 7 tasks a month, 40 lifetime tasks in the category, 150 lifetime tasks, and a top-35% Performance Score Ranking.

## What mistakes do new Taskers make in Canada?

- **Short availability windows.** Under three consecutive hours, you may not appear in searches.
- **One generic pitch for every category.** Clients read the pitch for the task they need.
- **Starting far above local pricing.** No bookings means no reviews.
- **Forgetting Same Day.** The toggle resets every night.

## How is TaskRabbit income taxed in Canada?

TaskRabbit income is self-employment income reported on Form T2125. Under the CRA's [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html), platforms report sellers' earnings to the CRA. The $25 registration fee, tools and work driving are business expenses if you keep records.
