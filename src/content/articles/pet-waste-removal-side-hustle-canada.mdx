## Who does pet waste removal in Canada suit?

Pet waste removal in Canada suits people who can treat an unpleasant job as a route: same streets, same day, short visits. Dog walkers, students, and retirees who already walk a neighbourhood are the usual fit, because the client already trusts someone with a gate and a dog. It is a poor fit if you are uncomfortable with dogs behind a fence, if you cannot commit to a weekly day, or if you need the work in January in a city where yards disappear under snow.

The visit is the yard, not a walk. Walking is its own service, with its own gear, in the [dog walking gear guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-gear-canada/). Do not fold a walk into a scoop price unless you have timed both and written both.

## Where does pet waste go in Canadian cities?

Pet waste in Canada goes where that municipality says it goes, and two large cities do not use the same bag. Read the page for the city you work in before you buy a case of bags.

On Toronto's [green bin page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/what-goes-in-my-green-bin/), accepted items include pet waste (animal droppings, clay and wood-pellet cat litter, and cage bedding). The same page says you may line the kitchen catcher or the green bin with any plastic bag, and that compostable plastic bags are not necessary. Pet fur, hair, and feathers are listed with items that do not belong in the green bin. In parks, Toronto says it has installed green bins in dog off-leash areas, and that where a park has no green bin, residents should use a garbage bin or take the waste home for the green bin.

Calgary's [green cart page](https://www.calgary.ca/waste/residential/what-can-go-in-green-cart.html) says all food scraps, yard waste, and pet waste can go in the green cart, and that pet waste and bedding must be bagged in a certified compostable bag or a paper bag, rolled or tied closed, for the collector's safety. Calgary's [pet waste and kitty litter page](https://www.calgary.ca/waste/what-goes-where/pet-waste-and-kitty-litter.html) says biodegradable bags are not accepted in the green cart program, because biodegradable and plastic bags do not break down and contaminate the compost. Those two labels are not the same product. Buy the bag the live page names, and re-check it, because the wording is easy to mix up.

Other cities write their own rule. Some still want pet waste in the garbage. A hired service should set the waste out in the resident's cart, on that property, only if that city allows it. Do not haul a route's waste to a park bin, and do not assume a residential cart accepts commercial loads. If the city is silent, call 311 or the waste department before you sell the service.

## How do you start pet waste removal in Canada?

Starting pet waste removal in Canada means learning the bin rule, practising on a yard you have permission to use, and selling a weekly visit on streets you can walk.

### Step 1: Read the waste page for your city

Write down which cart takes pet waste and which bag is allowed. If you work in two cities, keep two bag types and do not mix them.

### Step 2: Practise the visit

Time a full scoop, including the walk to the cart and handwashing. A tidy yard and a neglected yard are different jobs. Note gates, dogs, and where the cart sits.

### Step 3: Buy the small kit

Scooper, the right bags, gloves, and sanitizer. Skip a trailer. The work fits in a bucket or a small tote.

### Step 4: Write two prices

A recurring visit, and a one-time cleanup for a yard that has been left. The arithmetic is in the [pricing guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-pricing-canada/). Do not publish a price you have not timed.

### Step 5: Ask on one walked loop

Neighbours, dog walkers you know, and a short note on doors that allow it. The ask itself is in the [first clients guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-first-clients-canada/), and the bylaw side of knocking is in [door-to-door marketing for side hustles in Canada](/side-hustles/door-to-door-marketing-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 6: Put the waste in the right cart that day

Do not leave bags on the lawn for the owner to "deal with later" unless that is the written deal. Tie the bag the way the city asks.

### Step 7: Keep a route list

Street, gate code if the owner chooses to share one, dog notes, cart type, and the next date. ## What mistakes are common in pet waste removal in Canada?

Common pet waste removal mistakes in Canada are using the wrong bag, pricing a neglected yard like a weekly visit, and selling a winter route you cannot see.

- **The wrong bag.** A plastic bag that Toronto allows can be the bag Calgary tells you not to use. Read the page for the address.
- **Pet fur in the organics cart.** Toronto lists pet fur with items that stay out of the green bin. Scoop waste. Leave fur unless the city says otherwise.
- **Hauling waste to a park.** Toronto's park note is for residents at the park, not a business emptying a route into a public bin.
- **One price for every yard.** A spring cleanup of a winter's worth of waste is a longer visit. Price it on its own.
- **Loose gate codes in a group chat.** Keep access notes private. See [side hustle scams](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/) for the wider habit of not handing strangers a way into someone's routine.
- **Ignoring snow.** If you cannot find the waste, you cannot honestly charge for a full scoop. Pause or narrow the promise.

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, a forecast, or a survey. Suppose the kit is a $25 scooper, $15 of bags the city accepts, and $12 of gloves and sanitizer: $52. Suppose eight nearby yards each pay an assumed $18 for one weekly visit. Fees that week are 8 × $18 = $144. Suppose bags used that week cost $6. Amount left after those bags is $144 − $6 = $138. The $52 kit is covered inside that one assumed week, with $86 left before tax, insurance, and your time. If each yard takes 15 minutes on site, eight yards are 2 hours on site, and $138 ÷ 2 = $69 an hour in this sketch before walking between gates and before a tax set-aside.
