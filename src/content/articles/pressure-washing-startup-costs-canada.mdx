## How much does pressure washing cost to start in Canada?

Pressure washing costs $311.98 to $587.18 to equip in Canada with 13% HST, depending on whether you start with a wand and a bottle of cleaner, add a surface cleaner for flat concrete, or step up to a 2,500 PSI machine. Insurance is the biggest single line, about $450 a year. Prices below were checked on Amazon.ca on 9 October 2026 and move often, so treat them as a dated snapshot.

| Kit | Before tax | With 13% HST | With 5% GST only | First year with ~$450 insurance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean wand kit | $276.09 | $311.98 | $289.89 | $761.98 |
| Standard driveway kit | $404.55 | $457.14 | $424.78 | $907.14 |
| Pro electric kit | $519.63 | $587.18 | $545.61 | $1,037.18 |

The HST column fits Ontario. The GST column fits Alberta and the territories. Provinces with PST add it on top of GST, and Nova Scotia's HST is 14%. Kits are cumulative: standard includes everything in lean, and pro includes everything in standard, with the machine swapped for the bigger Westinghouse.

All three kits are electric. They assume you already own a garden hose that reaches the driveway and a car with room for the machine. A gas washer, a trailer, a water tank and a water-recovery system are not in any column. They are the purchases that turn a $450 start into a loan.

### What these prices leave out

A gas machine is absent on purpose. A VEVOR 3,400 PSI, 2.6 GPM gas washer was $364.99 on Amazon.ca on 9 October 2026, and a Champion 100780 3,100 PSI gas unit was listed at $1,574.57. ## How much is pressure washing insurance in Canada?

Pressure washing insurance in Canada starts around $450 a year for a basic commercial general liability policy with a $2 million limit, which is the figure [Zensurance publishes](https://www.zensurance.com/general-liability-insurance) for a small business. Its [pressure washing page](https://www.zensurance.com/contractor-insurance/pressure-washing) gives no fixed price and lists what moves the premium: the services you offer, your location and your clients' locations, staff, years of experience, revenue and claims history. Treat $450 as a starting point. A quote for your services can be higher.

Three questions to settle before the first paid driveway:

- **What surfaces are covered.** A policy written for ground-level concrete may exclude roofs, second-storey siding or commercial lots. Describe exactly what you will wash.
- **Damage to the client's property.** Etched concrete, stripped paint on a car parked too close, water forced under a door sill. Ask whether these are covered and what the deductible is.
- **Your vehicle.** Hauling a washer to jobs is business use. Tell your auto insurer before you rely on a personal policy.

Compare a direct online quote with a broker in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/), and see [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/) for how liability, tools and auto cover fit together.

## How many paid driveways does it take to break even on pressure washing?

Pressure washing breaks even after about 7 driveways for the standard kit plus a year of insurance, and about 8 for the pro kit, at an assumed $150 one-car driveway with $15 of cleaner, electricity and fuel per job. At $100 a driveway it rises to 11 and 13.

**Illustrative. The $150 and $100 prices and the $15 per-job cost are assumptions, not quotes or survey results.**

For a market reference, DT Cleaning in Toronto lists a one-car driveway from $150 and a two-car from $300, and AnyClean's seven Toronto jobs averaged $225, both drawn from [how much to charge for pressure washing in Canada](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-pricing-canada/). The $100 column shows a new operator pricing well below shops.

| Start | First-year cost | Driveways at $150 ($135 margin) | Driveways at $100 ($85 margin) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean kit, no insurance | $311.98 | 3 (2.31) | 4 (3.67) |
| Lean kit + $450 insurance | $761.98 | 6 (5.64) | 9 (8.96) |
| Standard kit + $450 insurance | $907.14 | 7 (6.72) | 11 (10.67) |
| Pro kit + $450 insurance | $1,037.18 | 8 (7.68) | 13 (12.20) |

Round up, because a partial driveway does not pay a bill. The arithmetic is first-year cost ÷ margin per job. Standard at $150: $907.14 ÷ $135 = 6.72, so 7 driveways. Pro at $100: $1,037.18 ÷ $85 = 12.20, so 13.

The surface cleaner in the standard kit costs $46.49, about a third of one $150 driveway, and it pays for itself the first time it saves you an hour on a two-car pad. The pro kit costs one extra driveway at $150, and only makes sense when stained concrete is a regular request.

Change the inputs to your own: your price, your kilometres and how much cleaner a stained driveway uses. Note how many driveways each jug of Oxy Solve covers, and replace the $15 with your own number after five jobs.

## What ongoing costs does pressure washing have in Canada?

Ongoing pressure washing costs in Canada are cleaner, electricity, nozzles and O-rings you lose, fuel to reach driveways, and insurance that does not shrink in a frozen January. Concrete cannot be washed when it freezes, so an annual premium is paid from a shorter working season.

**Water and drains.** Toronto's page on [washing cars and outdoor surfaces](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/water-environment/water-sewer-related-permits-and-bylaws/sewers-by-law/washing-cars-outdoors-surfaces/) says wastewater from outdoor washing must not reach catch basins, including from sidewalks, alleys and building washes, that power washing with water alone can still dislodge contaminants, and that the person hiring and the mobile wash business are both responsible. Your city's rule may be worded differently. If you cannot say where the rinse water goes, do not take the job.

**Winter storage.** An electric washer left with water in the pump can crack in a Canadian winter. Follow the manual's storage steps in the fall, before the first frost, not after.

**Kilometres.** Log the trips if you plan to claim them. A route of neighbours is cheaper than a route across the city.

**Unpaid scope.** A walkway "while you are here," a set of steps or the back patio are how a priced driveway becomes an open afternoon. Write the add-ons before you arrive. Per-surface and per-square-foot prices are in the [pricing guide](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-pricing-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply to pressure washing startup costs in Canada?

Pressure washing income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. Cleaner, nozzles and safety glasses are current expenses. A pressure washer or surface cleaner that lasts for years is usually claimed as [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business portion, over time rather than all in the first year. Buying the ePX3500 in December does not make it a full write-off that year.

GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): worldwide taxable supplies of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive quarters, counting every side hustle together. Pressure washing is not ride-sharing, so the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule does not apply. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST. Once registered, charge the rate where the work happens, using the CRA [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table and the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).

Search your own city for a business licence requirement. A licence is a separate question from the sewer bylaw. If you pay a helper, check your province's workers' compensation rules first.

## Where can pressure washing startup spending be lower, and where should it not be cut?

Spend less by starting with the lean kit, the hose you already own and driveways within a short drive. If you already own a pressure washer that delivers at least 1.5 GPM, the lean kit drops to $106.42 before tax, $120.25 with HST. Wait on the surface cleaner until a two-car driveway takes you more than an afternoon.

Do not cut the boots, the safety glasses, the wide-fan tip for first passes, or the insurance before the first stranger's driveway. A zero-degree tip on interlock or a painted surface is how a $150 job becomes a repair you pay for. Test every surface on a hidden spot, and never run a washer off a cord the manual does not allow.
