## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for pressure washing in Canada?

Ongoing pressure washing costs in Canada are cleaner, electricity, insurance that does not shrink in a frozen January, and fuel only after a gas machine exists, and the break-even sketch further down keeps this guide's assumed fees. **Cleaner.** The repeating supply. A stained double driveway uses more than a short city walk. Follow the label rate. A homemade mix is not a saving if it etches the surface or breaks a rule.

**Power and water.** Electric machines use the client's outlet or yours. A cord that is too light is a failure, not a discount. Water still has to go somewhere the municipality allows.

**Vehicle.** $0 if the jobs are close enough to carry the unit in a car you own. Log kilometres if you claim them.

## Which tax rules apply to a pressure washing budget in Canada?

Pressure washing income in Canada is self-employment income, including a deposit, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. Cleaner you use up is a current expense, while a pressure washer that lasts for years is usually [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business portion. GST/HST for pressure washing uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule, and Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the notes, the e-transfers, and the receipts for cleaner. Report the work on Form T2125 with the T1. Capital cost allowance recovers the cost of a lasting machine over time on the business portion, not necessarily in full on the receipt date. If you drive, keep a kilometre log and claim the business portion only.

The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add detailing, lawn care, and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Pressure washing is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform. This guide does not explain input tax credits. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the work happens. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Quebec charges GST and QST. Confirm the rate on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Set any tax you must collect aside.
