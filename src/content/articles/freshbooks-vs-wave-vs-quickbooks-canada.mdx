FreshBooks, Wave and QuickBooks are the three accounting apps Canadian freelancers compare most often, and they suit different stages. Wave is the free starting point, FreshBooks is built around invoicing and client work, and QuickBooks is the fuller accounting system that bookkeepers and accountants use every day. All three bill Canadians in CAD on their Canadian pricing pages, and all three can add GST/HST to invoices once you are registered.

FreshBooks' page also lists add-ons: Advanced Payments at $25 a month and team members at $13 a month per user. Wave lists optional add-ons such as receipt capture and payroll, and its Pro plan waives the $0.60 fixed card fee on the first ten card transactions each month.

## Which app is cheapest for a Canadian side hustle in 2026?

Wave Starter is the cheapest because it is $0 and includes unlimited invoices; you only pay card fees when clients pay online. Among the paid plans, QuickBooks Lite and Wave Pro sit near the bottom of the range and FreshBooks Lite's regular price is $26 CAD a month.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, using the listed regular prices above, for one year of software only (no card fees, no promotions):

- Wave Starter: $0.
- Wave Pro billed yearly: $250 CAD.
- FreshBooks Lite at the regular monthly price: $26 × 12 = $312 CAD.
- FreshBooks Plus at the regular monthly price: $42 × 12 = $504 CAD.

This is a teaching sketch, not a quote. FreshBooks and QuickBooks both showed introductory discounts on October 7, 2026, so your first-year cost may be lower and your second-year cost higher. Check the renewal price before you pay.

## How do the three handle GST/HST and CRA records?

All three let you add GST/HST to an invoice and track tax collected, which matters once you register. You must register once your taxable sales pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) over four consecutive calendar quarters (or in a single quarter), or earlier if you choose to. Our [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers the steps.

The CRA does not require any particular software. It asks you to keep complete, readable [business records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html) that support your income and expenses. Whichever app you choose, set its expense categories to match the lines on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) so year-end reporting is a copy-paste job. See [record-keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/) for what to keep.

Pick by how you work, not by brand. The rule below follows from the features and prices on each pricing page; it is not a ranking.

You may not need any of them yet. A spreadsheet with an income tab, an expense tab and a mileage tab is enough for many side hustles with a few transactions a week.

## What mistakes do Canadian freelancers make choosing accounting software?

The common mistakes are paying for features you will not use, and choosing on an introductory price.

- **Ignoring the renewal price.** Intro offers end; budget for the regular price.
- **Hitting client limits.** FreshBooks Lite allows invoices to 5 clients; a busy month can push you to Plus.
- **Mixing personal and business transactions.** Any app is easier with a separate account; see [separating business and personal money](/side-hustles/separate-business-personal-money-canada/).
- **Turning on online card payments without pricing them in.** At 2.9% + $0.60, card fees on small invoices add up.
- **Switching mid-year.** Moving books between apps in March is painful; switch at year-end.

## Which tax rules apply when you use accounting software in Canada?

Software subscriptions you use for your business are generally deductible business expenses on Form T2125, as covered in [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/). The software does not file your taxes for you; you still [report side hustle income on T2125](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

This is not tax or accounting advice.
