FreshBooks, Wave and QuickBooks are the three accounting apps Canadian freelancers compare most often, and they suit different stages. Wave is the free starting point, FreshBooks is built around invoicing and client work, and QuickBooks is the fuller accounting system that bookkeepers and accountants use every day. All three bill Canadians in CAD on their Canadian pricing pages, and all three can add GST/HST to invoices once you are registered.

## What would each cost at three business sizes?

**Illustrative example.** Assumed business profiles; plan prices are the regular prices above, with no promotions, for one year of software only.

| Size | Profile (assumed) | Wave | FreshBooks | QuickBooks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 3 regular clients, a few invoices a month, enters expenses by hand | Starter **$0** | Lite **$312** | Lite **from $240** |
| Medium | 15 clients, wants bank import and receipt capture | Pro **$250** | Plus **$504** | EasyStart **$360** |
| Heavy | 60+ clients or a helper, tracks projects or inventory | Pro **$250** (limited projects and inventory) | Premium **$864** (+ $13/mo per team member) | Plus **$1,320** |

Math: monthly price × 12. At the medium level the gap between Wave Pro and FreshBooks Plus is $504 − $250 = $254 a year, which is what you pay for FreshBooks' proposals, retainers and client-facing polish. At the heavy level QuickBooks costs the most but is the only one of the three with five users, inventory and project profitability on the plan.

### Card fees on Wave: Starter vs Pro

**Illustrative example.** Assumed: 8 client invoices a month of $500 each, all paid by Visa or Mastercard through Wave.

- **Starter:** 8 × ($500 × 2.9% + $0.60) = 8 × $15.10 = **$120.80 a month**.
- **Pro:** all 8 fall within the first 10 transactions, so 8 × ($500 × 2.9%) = **$116.00 a month**, saving $4.80 a month or $57.60 a year, which covers part of the $250 Pro fee.

On larger invoices the percentage dominates, so the $0.60 waiver matters most if you take many small payments. Interac e-Transfer avoids card fees altogether. FreshBooks and QuickBooks payment rates are on their own payment pages; FreshBooks also sells Advanced Payments at $25 a month for in-person and phone card payments.

## How do the three handle GST/HST and CRA records?

All three let you add GST/HST to an invoice and track tax collected, which matters once you register. You must register once your taxable sales pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) over four consecutive calendar quarters (or in a single quarter), or earlier if you choose to. Our [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers the steps.

The CRA does not require any particular software. It asks you to keep complete, readable [business records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html) that support your income and expenses. Whichever app you choose, set its expense categories to match the lines on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) so year-end reporting is a copy-paste job. See [record-keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/) for what to keep.

## Who should pick FreshBooks, Wave or QuickBooks?

Pick by how you work, not by brand. The rule below follows from the features and prices on each pricing page; it is not a ranking.

| If you… | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send fewer than a few invoices a month and want $0 | <AffiliateLink program="wave" label="Wave Starter" /> | Unlimited invoices on the free plan |
| Bill by the hour or project and send estimates and proposals | <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> | Invoicing, estimates and time tracking are its focus |
| Have, or plan to hire, a bookkeeper or accountant | QuickBooks Online | The accounting depth accountants expect |
| Want bank feeds without paying much | Wave Pro or QuickBooks Lite | Both are near the low end of paid plans |
| Run a product business with inventory | QuickBooks Plus | Inventory tracking is listed from Plus ($110/month regular) |
| Need several users on the books | QuickBooks Plus or Advanced | 5 or 25 users included |

You may not need any of them yet. A spreadsheet with an income tab, an expense tab and a mileage tab is enough for many side hustles with a few transactions a week.

## What mistakes do Canadian freelancers make choosing accounting software?

The common mistakes are paying for features you will not use, and choosing on an introductory price.

- **Ignoring the renewal price.** Intro offers end; budget for the regular price.
- **Hitting client limits.** FreshBooks Lite allows invoices to 5 clients; a busy month can push you to Plus.
- **Mixing personal and business transactions.** Any app is easier with a separate account; see [separating business and personal money](/side-hustles/business-bank-account-side-hustle-canada/#how-do-you-keep-business-and-personal-money-separate-after-the-account-is-open).
- **Turning on online card payments without pricing them in.** At 2.9% + $0.60, card fees on small invoices add up.
- **Switching mid-year.** Moving books between apps in March is painful; switch at year-end.

## The caveat: introductory prices hide the real cost

All three companies change plans and promotions often, and two of them were discounting heavily when this was checked. A $1-a-month first year or 90% off for six months can turn into $312 or $1,320 a year at renewal. Prices here are the regular CAD prices on each Canadian pricing page on October 14, 2026, before GST/HST; check the live page and the renewal date before you enter a card.

## Which tax rules apply when you use accounting software in Canada?

Software subscriptions you use for your business are generally deductible business expenses on Form T2125 (deduct what you actually paid, including promotional prices, plus any GST/HST you cannot claim back as an input tax credit), as covered in [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/). The software does not file your taxes for you; you still [report side hustle income on T2125](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).

This is not tax or accounting advice.
