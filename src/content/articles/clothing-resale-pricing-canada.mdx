## How do you calculate a clothing resale price?

Start from sold comps, cap them at the brand's own resale price, then check what you keep against the consignment alternative.

```text
List price    = median of 3–5 sold comps (same brand, item, size, condition)
                capped below the brand's own resale price, if one exists
You keep      = Poshmark: sale × 0.80 (or sale − 3.95 under $20)
                Depop:    (sale + shipping charged) × 0.90
                          − PayPal 2.9% + $0.30 − your shipping cost
Profit        = you keep − cost of the item − cleaning/repairs
Floor         = lowest sale where profit ≥ your target
Beat the store = you keep > consignment payout (e.g. 40% of sale)
```

## What do worked clothing resale examples look like?

**Illustrative.** Buy prices, comps, sale prices and the cost of cleaning are my assumptions. Fees, consignment shares and Arc'teryx ratios are from the sources above.

### Example 1: wool sweater on Poshmark.ca

- Bought $8, cleaning $2: cost **$10**. Sold comps around $40; listed at $45.
- Sold at $45: fee 20% = $9, you keep $36, profit **$26**.
- Floor $30: fee $6, keep $24, profit $14. At $18 the flat C$3.95 applies: keep $14.05, profit $4.05.

### Example 2: Arc'teryx Beta SL jacket found at a thrift store

- Bought $40. Arc'teryx's own resale price for an Excellent women's Beta SL is $480 (retail $640), so list below it: $380, with a $330 floor.
- Sold at $350 on Poshmark: fee $70, you keep **$280**, profit **$240**.
- Alternatives: Arc'teryx trade-in credit would be 30% × $640 = $192, as a gift card. Front & Company's 40% of a $350 sale is $140 cash.

### Example 3: graphic T-shirt on Depop vs Poshmark

- Bought $3. Listed at $25 on Depop, buyer pays $8 shipping: total $33.
- Depop 10% of $33 = $3.30; PayPal 2.9% × $33 + $0.30 = $1.26; Chit Chats label $5.33. You keep $33 − $3.30 − $1.26 − $5.33 = **$23.11**, profit $20.11.
- Same tee at $25 on Poshmark: fee $5, label included, you keep $20, profit $17.
- New tees from Canadian brands were $39 to $55, so $25 used is a believable price for a good-condition branded tee.

### Example 4: consign or sell yourself in Ottawa?

- A $150 dress: The Recollective pays 30% cash ($45) or 40% credit ($60). On Poshmark you keep $120. Self-selling earns $60 to $75 more.
- A $250 coat: The Recollective pays 50% ($125). Poshmark: you keep $200. The $75 difference pays for photos, listing and shipping time.
- A $750 designer bag: The Recollective pays 70% ($525). Poshmark: you keep $600. A $75 gap on a higher-risk item (authentication, returns) can make consignment the better call.

## Which templates help price and list clothing?

A sourcing check before you buy at a thrift store:

```text
SOURCING CHECK — [brand, item, size]
New price today: $____ (brand site)   Brand resale price: $____ (if any)
Sold comps: $___  $___  $___   → expected sale $____
Fee at that price: $____ (Poshmark 20% / Depop 10% + PayPal)
Max buy = expected keep − target profit − cleaning = $____
Condition flags: pilling / stains / odour / zip / missing tag
```

A listing that answers the questions buyers ask:

```text
[Brand] [item] — [colour] — size [X]
Condition: [Excellent / Great / Good] — [specific flaws, with photos]
Measurements (flat): pit to pit __ cm, length __ cm, sleeve __ cm
Material: [from care label]
Retail: $[price] at [brand] on [date]
Ships from [city] within 2 business days. Bundle 3+ items for 15% off.
```

Offer replies and a markdown schedule:

```text
Thanks for the offer! The lowest I can do is $[floor]. It's in
[condition] with [detail]. Happy to send more photos.
```

```text
MARKDOWN SCHEDULE (per item)
Day 0    list at comps
Day 30   −10% (Front & Company marks consigned items down 20% at day 30)
Day 60   −10% again, or move to a bundle
Day 90   floor, consign, or donate; record the result
```

## How do you raise clothing resale prices?

- **Price premium brands from the brand's own resale data.** Arc'teryx's 75% of retail for Excellent condition tells you where the top of the market is; list a few points below it.
- **Grade honestly and photograph flaws.** Arc'teryx's grade gap (75% Excellent vs 68% Great) is the price of condition.
- **Bundle low-ticket items** so the flat C$3.95 Poshmark fee or shipping cost is spread across several sales.
- **Sell in season.** List coats and insulated jackets in autumn, when buyers are shopping for them, rather than cutting prices in spring.
- **Pick the platform per item.** Under C$20, Depop with a low-cost label keeps more when the buyer pays shipping; above it, Poshmark's included label is simpler. For general raise scripts, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What clothing resale pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common clothing resale pricing mistakes in Canada are pricing from active listings instead of sold ones, pricing above a brand's own resale store, ignoring the flat fee on small sales, and not recording cost, which hides whether items make money.

Children's clothing has safety rules. Health Canada's [guide to second-hand products](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/reports-publications/industry-professionals/industry-guide-second-hand-products-including-children-products/guidance-document.html) says the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act applies to sellers of second-hand products and covers children's products and recalls; read it before pricing kids' items, because some can't be sold at any price. Counterfeit items should never be listed.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect clothing resale in Canada?

Regular clothing resale for profit in Canada is business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html); occasionally selling your own used clothes at a loss generally isn't. Keep receipts for what you buy, as the CRA's [keeping records page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/keeping-records.html) explains. GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters.
