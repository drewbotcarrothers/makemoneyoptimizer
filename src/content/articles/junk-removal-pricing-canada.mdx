Canadian junk removal companies publish volume prices of $149 to $469 for a quarter load and $449 to $799 for a full load in 2026. Toronto's We-Haul Disposal charges $200 for a quarter load and $550 for a full one. In Calgary, Mighty Junk charges $175 and $475. Metro Van Junk lists $449 to $599 for a full truck before GST. Disposal is the cost that moves your price: Toronto's depots charge $189.86 a tonne, Metro Vancouver $182, Calgary $113 and Edmonton $102 for commercial loads. A side-hustle hauler with a trailer can price a half load at about $270 in Toronto by adding labour, a helper, the scale fee and vehicle cost.

## Which pricing models work for junk removal?

Price household junk by volume, heavy debris by weight, and single items flat, with a minimum for every visit.

| Model | How it works | Published example | Watch out for |

| Volume | 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 or full load | We-Haul: $200 to $550 | Heavy loads that hit weight limits first |
| Fine volume steps | 1/8 load increments | Local Lads: 12 steps, $99 to $499 | Arguing over fractions on site |
| Single item | Flat price per piece | ClearPath: sofa $99, fridge $129 | Stairs and tight doorways |

## How do you calculate a junk removal price?

Add your labour at a billable rate, the helper's pay, the scale fee and the vehicle cost, then round to a load-fraction price.

```text
Billable rate  = target hourly pay ÷ share of hours billable
Job price      = (hours × billable rate) + (hours × helper wage)
               + disposal (tonnes × city rate, at least the minimum)
               + vehicle (fuel + wear for the round trip)
Load sheet     = job price for each fraction, rounded; heavy loads by weight
```

**Illustrative rate build:** a $30 target and 70% of hours billable gives $30 ÷ 0.70 = **$42.86 an hour**. A helper at the $22 Job Bank median adds $22 an hour.

**Illustrative Calgary price sheet** at $42.86, a $22 helper and $113 a tonne, single-axle utility trailer:

## How should you quote and invoice junk removal?

Quote from photos with a range, confirm on site before loading, and put the scale fee on the invoice.

```text
JUNK REMOVAL QUOTE — [client]                    Date: [date]
Items from photos: couch, 2 dressers, 10 bags, old desk
Estimated volume: 1/2 trailer ........................ $230
Included: loading by two people, ground floor and one flight,
  disposal up to 550 kg, sweep-up
Extras if found on site: stairs beyond one flight +$16 per heavy item;
  fridge/freezer +$42; mattress +$[city fee]; heavy debris by weight
Not accepted: paint, propane, chemicals, asbestos (municipal programs)
Final price confirmed before loading. Prices before GST/HST.
```

## How do you raise junk removal prices?

Raise prices when your city raises tipping fees, when scale tickets show heavier loads than you priced, or when your calendar fills.

1. **Track fee changes.** Metro Vancouver's current rates started July 1, 2026, and Toronto added a mattress fee the same day. Update the sheet the week a new rate posts.
2. **Charge for heavy debris by weight.** Local Lads prices dense loads by truck-bed fraction.
3. **Add a rush rate.** Junk Pros GTA adds 20% for same-day pickup.
4. **Bill long jobs by the hour.** J4T includes the first hour and charges $100 an hour after for two workers.
5. **Sort before the scale.** Calgary's $180 surcharge on loads with recyclables is $67 a tonne more than its $113 basic rate.
6. **Notify repeat clients:**

```text
Hi [Name], from [January 1] my half-load price moves from $[230] to
$[250] because the landfill rate went up. Everything else stays the same.
— [Your name]
```

For moving repeat clients to a new rate, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What junk removal pricing mistakes are common?

- **Volume prices on heavy debris.** Shingles and concrete hit weight limits long before the trailer looks full.
- **Unsorted loads.** Calgary charges $180 a tonne instead of $113 when recyclables are mixed in.
- **No minimum.** A short pickup still costs the drive and Edmonton's $40 or Calgary's $25 minimum.
- **Forgetting item fees.** Refrigerant appliances and mattresses carry their own charges.
- **"Anything goes" quotes.** Refuse hazardous waste and point the client to the municipal program.

For the insurance, vehicle and dumping mistakes new haulers make most, see the [junk removal mistakes guide](/side-hustles/junk-removal-side-hustle-canada/#what-mistakes-do-new-junk-haulers-make). ## How do GST/HST and income tax affect junk removal prices?

Junk removal income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and disposal fees and fuel are business expenses. You pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, counting all your side hustles.

Once registered, charge your province's rate from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html), generally on the whole invoice, including the disposal portion built into your price. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), [vehicle expenses guide](/side-hustles/vehicle-expenses-delivery-rideshare-canada/) and [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) cover the related records.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
