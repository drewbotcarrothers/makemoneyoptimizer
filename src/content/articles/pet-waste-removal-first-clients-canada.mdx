## Who should you ask first for pet waste removal in Canada?

Ask the nearest dog yards first for pet waste removal in Canada, especially people who already know you will show up. Five weekly yards on two streets beat a list of maybes across the city. A dog walker who does not want to scoop is a better introduction than a stranger's group post, because the owner has already handed that person a key.

If you walk dogs, offer the yard as a separate service with its own price. Walking stays in the [dog walking guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/). Do not tell a walking client the scoop is included unless the written price says so.

## What should a first pet waste removal offer say in Canada?

A first pet waste removal offer in Canada should name the day, the price per visit, what "done" means, and which cart you will use. Leave out slogans. A useful note fits on a small card:

> Weekly yard scoop on Tuesdays. $15 a visit in this sketch, billed once a month, or a one-time cleanup priced after I see the yard. Waste goes in the organics cart only if your city allows it. Text this number. I do not need your email.

The $15 is the assumed weekly figure from the [pricing guide's illustrative card](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-pricing-canada/), not a price you should print. Replace it. ## How do you turn a cleanup into a weekly pet waste client in Canada?

You turn a cleanup into a weekly pet waste client in Canada by pricing the cleanup on its own, doing it, and asking for the schedule while the owner can see the yard. The weekly price should be lower per visit than the cleanup, because the yard is now maintained. Say the next two dates out loud and write them down.

### Step 1: See the yard before you quote the cleanup

A photo helps. A gate you cannot open is not a client yet.

### Step 2: Do only the cleanup you priced

Do not add the side path, the alley, or a walk unless it is on the note.

### Step 3: Offer the weekly day you already walk

"Tuesdays, with the other yards on this block" is a route. "Whenever I can" is how the cleanup never becomes income.

### Step 4: Ask for one introduction

One neighbour with a dog is enough. Ask the client, do not take a phone number from a mailbox. If you later email or text a promotion, Canada's anti-spam law requires consent first. The rule is in the [door-to-door guide](/side-hustles/door-to-door-marketing-side-hustle-canada/) and on ISED's [CASL page](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en).

### Step 5: Invoice the month, not each gate

A short monthly note with the visits and the total is enough at the start. Keep the e-transfer memo matched to the month.

## What should you avoid when looking for pet waste clients in Canada?

Avoid buying a route, posting your home address, and emailing people who did not agree to hear from you. Also avoid any pitch that sounds like the paid-to-start schemes in [side hustle scams in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/). A real yard service does not ask you to pay a stranger for a list of clients.

Respect a no-soliciting sign and a condo rule. Ontario's page on [signing or cancelling a contract](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) says most agreements for a product or service over $50 should be in writing, and it uses a door-to-door purchase as one example. A single weekly visit under that amount may be a simple note. A season of visits billed up front can cross it. Read the page before you take a large prepayment. This is not legal advice.

## What first-client mistakes are common in pet waste removal in Canada?

Common first-client mistakes in pet waste removal in Canada are scooping a yard you have not priced, promising every day of the week, and leaving bags the city will not collect.

- **No written day.** "I'll come by" is how Tuesday gets skipped.
- **The wrong cart.** Confirm organics versus garbage before the first bag is tied.
- **Keys and codes labelled with the address.** Keep access notes private.
- **A free month.** A short first cleanup at the cleanup price is clearer than a free month that trains the client to wait.
- **Scattered yeses.** Three yards in three neighbourhoods teach you the work and lose the hour to travel.

## Which tax rules apply to the first pet waste removal clients in Canada?

Fees from the first pet waste removal clients in Canada are self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), including a small e-transfer.
