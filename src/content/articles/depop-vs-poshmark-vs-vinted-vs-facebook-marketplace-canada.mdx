For Canadian resellers in October 2026 the choice is between three platforms, not four: Vinted closed its Canadian marketplace on February 12, 2024. Poshmark Canada charges a flat C$3.95 on sales under C$20 and 20% on sales of C$20 or more, and that fee includes card processing and a prepaid shipping label the buyer pays for. Depop charges sellers outside the U.K., U.S. and Australia 10% of the item price and the shipping charge, and payment processing goes through PayPal on top. Facebook Marketplace is a local, meet-up channel for Canadians: Meta's shipping and checkout help describes buying and selling across the continental United States, so Canadian sales are settled between you and the buyer, with no platform fee.

Every fee below is from the platform's own page on October 9, 2026. All amounts are Canadian dollars; no currency conversion is involved because Poshmark Canada, Depop and PayPal all charge Canadian sellers in CAD.

The biggest difference is not the headline percentage but **who pays for shipping and whether the fee applies to it**. On Poshmark the buyer pays a flat shipping price and the seller prints a free label; the 20% applies only to the item. On Depop the seller charges shipping, pays for the postage, and the 10% fee and the PayPal fee both apply to the item plus shipping.

## What does each app charge Canadian sellers in October 2026?

### Poshmark Canada

Poshmark's [getting-started guide](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/get_started_on_poshmark) says that for sales under C$20 it charges a flat C$3.95, and for sales of C$20 or more it charges 20% and you keep 80%. In return it handles the payment, including card fees, sends a prepaid, pre-addressed shipping label, and acts as customer support. Withdrawals to your bank account are free.

Shipping is paid by the buyer. Poshmark's [May 26, 2026 announcement](https://blog.poshmark.com/2026/05/26/canada-c6-99-shipping-is-here-purolator-pick-up/) says buyers choose between **C$12.49 Canada Post Home Delivery**, which is always on, and **C$6.99 Purolator Pick Up**, which sellers can turn on under Seller Tools > Shipping Settings if they have a Purolator drop-off location nearby. Buyers have 5 business days to collect a Purolator parcel. Poshmark's [Canada FAQ](https://blog.poshmark.com/poshmark-canada-faq/) says the C$12.49 label covers packages under 2.5 kg; heavier parcels need a label upgrade paid by the seller.

### Depop

Depop's [fee page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) has two regimes. Sellers in the U.K., U.S. and Australia pay no selling fee, only payment processing. Sellers outside those three countries, selling in another currency such as CAD, pay a **10% selling fee** on the item price and, if they did not use a Depop shipping label, on the shipping cost. Payment processing for those sellers runs through PayPal, and Depop says PayPal's fee varies with your location and account. Boosted listings cost **8%** outside the U.K. and U.S.

PayPal Canada's [merchant fee table](https://www.paypal.com/ca/business/paypal-business-fees), last updated July 15, 2026, lists **2.90% + C$0.30** for domestic commercial transactions, plus 0.80% when the buyer is in the U.S. and 1.00% for other countries. If you refund a sale, PayPal does not return its original fee; Depop reverses its own fees on refunds, and for PayPal sellers outside the U.K., U.S. and Australia that means raising a ticket with Depop support.

On bundles, Depop charges its selling fee on each item and the processing fee once per transaction. On tax, Depop's [tax help page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/18276924278033-Will-I-have-to-pay-for-tax-or-customs-on-my-order) says it collects GST/HST from Canadian buyers on sales within Canada, plus applicable taxes in Quebec, Saskatchewan, British Columbia and Manitoba.

Canadian sellers set a shipping price on each listing and buy postage themselves. Canada Post's [flat rate box](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/personal/sending/parcels/flat-rate-box.page) prices on October 9, 2026 were **$18.99** extra small (22.5 × 15.5 × 7.62 cm), **$21.99** small, **$24.99** medium and **$32.99** large, each up to 5 kg. Discounted courier labels are compared in [Stallion Express vs Chit Chats vs Canada Post](/side-hustles/stallion-express-vs-chit-chats-vs-canada-post-canada/).

### Facebook Marketplace

Meta's [shipping and checkout help](https://www.facebook.com/help/796066857221106) describes buying and selling across the continental United States with shipping and checkout. In Canada, Marketplace works as a local listing board: the buyer pays you in cash or by Interac e-Transfer at pickup, nothing passes through Facebook, and there is no selling fee.

### Vinted

Vinted's [Canadian site](https://www.vinted.ca/) still says the Canada platform closed on February 12, 2024. Any guide that recommends Vinted to Canadian sellers is out of date.

## How much do you keep on each app in Canada?

The fee structures cross over around $39. Below that, Poshmark's flat rules keep more for the seller; above it, Depop's lower percentage wins even after PayPal and the fee on shipping.

### Illustrative example: one item at three prices

**Sourced inputs:** Poshmark's C$3.95 and 20% fees, Depop's 10% fee on item and shipping, PayPal's 2.9% + C$0.30, and Canada Post flat rate box prices. **Assumed inputs:** the three item prices, a Depop seller who charges the buyer exactly the box price for shipping ($18.99 extra small for the $15 and $30 items, $21.99 small for the $80 item), and sales tax left out because the platforms collect it from the buyer.

**$15 T-shirt**

- Poshmark: $15 − $3.95 = **$11.05**. The buyer pays $15 + $12.49 = $27.49, or $21.99 with Purolator.
- Depop: buyer pays $15 + $18.99 = $33.99. Depop fee 10% × $33.99 = $3.40. PayPal 2.9% × $33.99 + $0.30 = $1.29. Postage $18.99. You keep $33.99 − $3.40 − $1.29 − $18.99 = **$10.31**.
- Marketplace pickup: **$15.00**.

**$30 jacket**

- Poshmark: 20% × $30 = $6, so you keep **$24.00**. The buyer pays $42.49, or $36.99 with Purolator.
- Depop: buyer pays $48.99. Fee $4.90, PayPal $1.72, postage $18.99. You keep **$23.38**.
- Marketplace pickup: **$30.00**.

**$80 boots**

- Poshmark: 20% × $80 = $16, so you keep **$64.00**. The buyer pays $92.49, or $86.99 with Purolator.
- Depop: buyer pays $80 + $21.99 = $101.99. Fee $10.20, PayPal 2.9% × $101.99 + $0.30 = $3.26, postage $21.99. You keep **$66.54**.
- Marketplace pickup: **$80.00**.

| Item price | Poshmark keeps | Depop keeps | Marketplace pickup keeps | Higher shipped payout |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $15 | $11.05 | $10.31 | $15.00 | Poshmark |
| $30 | $24.00 | $23.38 | $30.00 | Poshmark |
| $80 | $64.00 | $66.54 | $80.00 | Depop |

Where they cross: Depop keeps more than Poshmark when 0.071 × price is larger than 0.129 × shipping + $0.30. At an $18.99 shipping charge that is (0.129 × 18.99 + 0.30) ÷ 0.071 = **about $38.73**. A cheaper courier label lowers the crossover; a boosted Depop listing raises it, because the extra 8% applies to the item and shipping. On the $30 jacket, boosting costs 8% × $48.99 = $3.92 and leaves $19.46.

The buyer's total matters as much as your payout. The $30 jacket costs the buyer $36.99 to $42.49 on Poshmark and $48.99 on Depop at Canada Post box prices, and a higher total can mean a slower sale or a lower offer.

## What mistakes do Canadian resellers make choosing an app?

- **Using U.S. fee tables.** Depop charges U.S. sellers no selling fee and Poshmark U.S. uses U.S. shipping prices; Canadian sellers pay the Canadian fees above.
- **Comparing 10% with 20% and stopping there.** Depop's 10% applies to the shipping charge too, PayPal adds 2.9% + C$0.30, and the seller pays the postage.
- **Listing $5 items on Poshmark.** The C$3.95 flat fee takes most of the sale.
- **Shipping overweight parcels on a Poshmark label.** Over 2.5 kg needs a paid upgrade; weigh first.
- **Planning around Vinted.** It is not open to Canadian sellers.
- **Selling recalled products.** Check car seats, cribs, toys and children's items against the Government of Canada [recalls and safety alerts database](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en) before listing.
- **Meeting strangers carelessly.** For local sales, meet in a public place in daylight.

Every fee and price above was read on October 9, 2026, and all of them change. PayPal's 2.9% + C$0.30 is its standard published rate; Depop says your PayPal fee depends on your account. Canada Post flat rate boxes are one postage option among several, and a cheaper courier label changes the Depop column. Check each platform's page before you price a batch of listings. ## Which tax rules apply to resale app sellers in Canada?

Selling your own used belongings occasionally is different from buying items to resell for profit, which is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Platforms can report seller information to the CRA, as explained in [tax on reselling and gig apps](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).

If you resell as a business, add sales from every app together for the GST/HST [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Depop and Poshmark collect tax from buyers where they are required to, but that does not decide whether you must register. Keep receipts for inventory, postage and platform fees, as covered in [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).
