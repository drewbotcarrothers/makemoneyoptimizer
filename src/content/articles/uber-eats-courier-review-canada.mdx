## Is Uber Eats available to couriers in Canada?

Yes. Uber's [delivery page](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/deliver/) says Uber Eats operates in major Canadian cities including Toronto, Ottawa, Edmonton, and Vancouver, plus hundreds of other cities. You can deliver by car, bike, or scooter, and on foot in select cities. Couriers use the same Driver app as rideshare drivers and are independent contractors.

## What do you need to deliver with Uber Eats in Canada?

Requirements depend on how you deliver. For car delivery, Uber lists:

| Requirement | Detail (as listed October 2026) |
| --- | --- |
| Age and licence | At least 21; licence requirements vary by location |
| Vehicle | 2-door or 4-door car, 1990 or newer |
| Insurance | Proof of vehicle insurance showing the delivery person's name, not expired |
| Work eligibility | Canadian passport, study or work permit, Canadian birth certificate, PR card or citizenship card |
| Background screening | Criminal history check through ISB and Triton; car couriers also get a driving history check |

Bike and scooter couriers need proof of work eligibility and the background screening. Uber notes that scans and photocopies of documents are not accepted.

## How does Uber Eats pay couriers in Canada?

Uber shows the payout for each delivery request before you accept, and 100% of in-app tips go to you. Uber does not list a fixed courier commission on its Canadian delivery page. For rides, Uber's Canadian [upfront offers announcement](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/blog/more-transparency-with-upfront-offers-in-canada/) explains that offers are shown net of Uber's service fee, which varies trip to trip, with a weekly summary of what Uber collected; check your Driver app for how delivery offers are presented in your city.

### Ontario and B.C. pay floors

- **Ontario:** the [Digital Platform Workers' Rights Act](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers) entitles delivery platform workers to at least the general minimum wage per work assignment or pay period, excluding tips. Ontario's general minimum wage is $17.95 from October 1, 2026 ([Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage)).
- **British Columbia:** at least $21.89 per hour of engaged time from June 1, 2026, plus $0.35 per km for delivery using transport other than walking ([B.C.](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage)).

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, using Ontario's published minimum: an Ontario courier spends 20 minutes on an assigned delivery, from accepting it to drop-off.

- Minimum for that assignment: (20 ÷ 60) × $17.95 = $5.98, excluding tips
- Ontario lets the operator meet the minimum per assignment or across a whole pay period, so this is a floor check, not a per-order guarantee
- Time waiting between orders is not part of a work assignment

## How do Uber Eats payouts work in Canada?

Earnings are transferred automatically to your bank account every week. For faster access, Uber's [Instant Pay](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/drive/driver-app/instant-pay/) sends earnings to a debit card for $1.49 per cash-out, up to five times a day; Uber says funds usually arrive right away but can take a few business days depending on the bank. Most Visa, Mastercard, and Discover debit cards are accepted.

## What are the pros and cons of Uber Eats for Canadian couriers?

**Pros**
- Available in many Canadian cities, with car, bike, and scooter options.
- See pay before accepting; 100% of in-app tips.
- Weekly deposits, or Instant Pay up to five times a day.
- Legal pay floors in Ontario and B.C.

**Cons**
- No published courier commission, so pay per order is hard to predict.
- Vehicle, fuel, insurance and phone costs are yours.
- Waiting time between orders is unpaid.
- Weather and traffic risk, especially for bike couriers in winter.

Uber Eats suits Canadians who want flexible, short shifts with no scheduling, especially students and people with a car, e-bike or bike in a dense city. It suits people who need predictable hourly income less well. ## What mistakes do new Uber Eats couriers make?

- **Accepting every offer.** Check distance and pay before tapping accept.
- **Not logging kilometres.** You need a log to deduct vehicle costs.
- **Skipping the insurance conversation.** Ask your insurer whether your policy covers delivery use.
- **Cashing out daily by habit.** Each Instant Pay costs $1.49; weekly deposits are free.
- **Forgetting tax.** No tax is withheld.

More are in [food delivery mistakes](/side-hustles/food-delivery-mistakes-canada/).

## How is Uber Eats income taxed in Canada?

Couriers are independent contractors, so earnings are self-employment income reported on Form T2125, with the business share of vehicle and phone costs deductible.
