## How much does freelance bookkeeping cost to start in Canada?

Freelance bookkeeping in Canada costs about $40.67 for a backup drive if you already have a computer, $187.56 with a headset and a second monitor, $487.56 with a starting E&O policy, or $1,057.08 with a year of invoicing software. Goods and FreshBooks include 13% Ontario HST; insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate.

## What do accounting tools cost a bookkeeper in Canada?

The client pays for their own ledger. Your access as their bookkeeper is free on the two biggest platforms, as of October 9, 2026:

- **Xero.** Xero's [partner program page](https://www.xero.com/ca/partner-programme/) says "the Xero partner program is free and there are no ongoing costs," and includes a free Xero subscription to run your practice. Its [certifications page](https://www.xero.com/ca/certifications/) says Xero certification is free. Partners need someone to complete Level 1 certification within 30 days, and Xero's partner agreement asks for at least one paid client subscription within 60 days.
- **QuickBooks.** Intuit's [accountants page](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/ca/accountants/) says QuickBooks Online Accountant will be discontinued on December 31, 2026, as firms move to Intuit Accountant Suite, and that the Core version "has no monthly recurring fee and remains free for your firm to use." Paid tiers exist above Core.

**Correcting an outdated recommendation.** Many startup guides still tell new bookkeepers to sign up for QuickBooks Online Accountant. Intuit's page now says it's being discontinued at the end of 2026, so start on Intuit Accountant Suite Core instead. It's still free.

For your own invoicing, <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> lists Canadian-dollar plans. Don't buy the client's subscription for them in week one and hope to bill it back.

## Do you need a licence, title or insurance to bookkeep in Canada?

Titles are provincial, and Ontario is the example this guide can cite. Ontario's [Public Accounting Act, 2004](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/04p08) says a person is not required to be licensed under that Act for providing services as a bookkeeper, or for engaging in bookkeeping, solely by virtue of those activities. Ontario's [Chartered Professional Accountants of Ontario Act, 2017](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/17c08) restricts designations such as Chartered Professional Accountant and the initials CPA. The same Act says it doesn't interfere with the right of a non-member to practise as an accountant. Don't use a protected title you don't hold. Other provinces write their own statutes. This is not legal advice.

**Insurance.** Zensurance's [bookkeeper insurance page](https://www.zensurance.com/bookkeeper-insurance) says the average cost for a bookkeeper starts around $300 a year for an errors and omissions policy with a $500,000 limit, and varies with training, certifications, location, revenue, services and staff. That's an estimate, not a quote. Ask about cyber cover if you'll hold client files. Compare options in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

**CRA access.** Don't use a client's CRA password. If the engagement needs access, use the CRA's [authorize a representative](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/representative-authorization/how.html) process, which costs nothing.

**Business name and licence.** If you trade under a name that isn't your own, most provinces require a registration; in Ontario that's $60 online, per the province's [fee page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/cost-time-required-to-register-change-search-for-business-name-corporation-not-for-profit). Search your city for a home-business licence.

### A first-client sketch

**Illustrative example, not a quote or a survey of Canadian fees.** Suppose a solo cleaner pays an assumed $200 for one month of categorizing and a bank reconciliation, and it takes five hours including chasing receipts. Suppose you'd already bought the standard kit. Month one: $200 − $187.56 = $12.44 left before tax. Hour sketch for month two onward: $200 ÷ 5 = $40 an hour before tax and software. Filing their return isn't in the $200, and neither is a CRA login using their password. If the shoebox takes twelve hours, the next engagement changes. ## What are the ongoing costs?

- **Software you pay and can't bill,** like your own invoicing tool. The client's ledger is theirs.
- **Insurance,** from about $300 a year.
- **Chasing.** Receipts a client promised and didn't send are time. The engagement should say what happens when the month can't close.
- **Scope creep.** Payroll, sales-tax filings and "quick" tax advice are different engagements. Don't tell a client they're under a threshold unless you're engaged, and qualified, to say so.
- **Quiet months.** A subscription you opened for a client who never sent a statement is still a subscription.

## Where can freelance bookkeeping startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Use a spreadsheet** for your first invoices, and the client's existing ledger for their books.
- **Use the free accountant tiers** from Xero and Intuit rather than paying for your own ledger.
- **Wait on the headset and monitor** until a call or a second client is booked.
- **Skip a course you'd describe as a designation.** Skip receiving a client's tax instalments "to make it easier."

## Where shouldn't you cut freelance bookkeeping spending?

Don't skip the backup, the six-year record habit, or the line that says you're not their CPA. A lost laptop with the only copy of a year isn't a saving. Don't sign an audit or a review. Ask a broker before assuming a home policy covers holding other people's financial records. Keep your fees in their own account; <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian option. This is not insurance or legal advice.

## Which tax rules apply to a freelance bookkeeping budget in Canada?

Your bookkeeping fees in Canada are self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both portions of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. The client's GST/HST is their registration, not yours. Your own GST/HST on your fees uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule.

The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add virtual-assistant and other side-hustle revenue to the same total. Once registered, charge the rate for the province where your supply is made, using the [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table and the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Quebec charges GST and QST. If you're registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases like the monitor or FreshBooks can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html).

Working papers follow the six-year rule on the CRA's [business records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html) page.

This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.
