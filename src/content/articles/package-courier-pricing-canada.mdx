- **App blocks:** Amazon Flex says most drivers earn $22 to $27 an hour before costs; Job Bank's median for employed courier drivers is $23.00 an hour. After the drive to the station and car costs, the illustrative block below nets $12.96 an hour.
- **Your own customers:** local Canadian couriers list $7 to $22.75 per delivery under 10 km in the GTA, $15 same-day with 15 km included at Q's Couriers in Toronto, and $9 to $20 by distance in Calgary, Vancouver and Ottawa.
- **Floors:** Ontario's $17.95 an hour of assignment time from October 1, 2026; B.C.'s $21.89 an hour of engaged time plus $0.35/km.
- **Formula:** block floor = total hours × target hourly + total km × cost per km.

</QuickAnswer>

Package couriers in Canada are paid two ways: per block or per parcel by an app or delivery partner, or per delivery by local businesses they sign themselves. App pay is set for you, so the work is deciding which blocks clear your floor. Local same-day couriers publish prices from $7 to $22.75 a delivery, and those set the ceiling for your own rate sheet. Courier work suits someone with a reliable car and a log of kilometres. It's a poor fit if the station or the customers are a long, unpaid drive away.

## How do you calculate a courier rate?

```text
Car cost        = total km (home → station or first pickup → route → home) × cost per km
Block floor     = total hours × target hourly + car cost
Delivery price  = minutes for the run ÷ 60 × target hourly + round-trip km × cost per km
Route price     = drops × price per drop, checked against (route hours, route km)
```

**Get a per-km cost.** Until you have receipts, CAA's figures for a 2025 Nissan Sentra in Ontario work out to $0.12/km for fuel alone and $0.26/km with operating costs ($4,549.61 a year over 17,500 km), from its [Driving Costs Calculator](https://carcosts.caa.ca/results/ontario/passenger_mainstream/ice). **Count the unpaid time.** The drive to the station, the wait to load and the drive home belong in the hours. **Then compare.** The block floor tells you which app blocks to accept; the local price table tells you what a shop will pay you directly.

## What do worked courier quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** The $20 and $25 targets, the $0.26/km cost, block pay, minutes and kilometres are my assumptions. GST/HST appears only once you're registered.

### Quote 1: an Amazon Flex block in Ontario

- Block pays $72 for 3 hours. With the station drive, loading and the drive home: 3.75 hours, 90 km.
- Car: 90 × $0.26 = $23.40. Net: ($72 − $23.40) ÷ 3.75 = **$12.96 an hour**.
- Floor at a $20 target: 3.75 × $20 + $23.40 = **$98.40**. The block pays $72, so decline at this target.

### Quote 2: a Toronto florist's same-day delivery

- One delivery, 12 km each way, 40 minutes: 40 ÷ 60 × $25 + 24 × $0.26 = $22.91.
- Local anchors: Q's Couriers $15 same day; 1Courier $14.75 for a 2-hour delivery under 10 km. A single run at $23 is above the market.
- Quote **$16 per delivery when 3 or more go out on one afternoon run**. Five drops on one route: 2 hours, 45 km. 5 × $16 = $80; ($80 − 45 × $0.26) ÷ 2 = ($80 − $11.70) ÷ 2 = **$34.15 an hour**.

### Quote 3: a Calgary bakery's weekly route

- Eight drops, most under 20 km from the shop. Anchor: Trexity's Calgary table at $10 (0–10 km) and $12 (10–20 km).
- Quote **$11 per drop**: 8 × $11 = $88. Route: 2.5 hours, 60 km. ($88 − 60 × $0.26) ÷ 2.5 = ($88 − $15.60) ÷ 2.5 = **$28.96 an hour**.

### Quote 4: a monthly invoice, HST-registered in Ontario

- 40 deliveries at $15 = $600. With 13% HST: $600 × 1.13 = **$678**.
- Not registered: $600, no tax line. Registration is required once taxable supplies pass $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters.

## How do you raise courier earnings?

- **On apps, pick blocks by the floor.** You can't set block pay; you can choose stations near home and dense routes.
- **On your own customers, batch.** Quote 2's single run doesn't work at market prices; the five-drop route does.
- **Price speed and weight.** Every published sheet above charges more for a 2-hour window, after-hours runs, heavy parcels or re-delivery.
- **Raise at the anniversary**, with the reason, as in the template. Scripts are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).
- **Lower the per-km cost.** A fuel-efficient car lowers the floor on every block and route.

## What package courier mistakes are common?

Judging pay by the block length instead of total time, ignoring the drive to the station, and not logging kilometres. Each makes a gross figure look like take-home pay. Don't compare an app's hourly claim with an employee wage that comes with a company van. When delivering for your own customers, check that your auto policy covers commercial delivery before the first run.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect courier earnings?

Courier income is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both shares of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all your businesses; the first-fare rule is for passenger transport, not parcels. Once registered, charge the rate from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [platform income tax guide](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/) covers what platforms report.

Block payouts arrive in batches; moving the tax share aside each time avoids a spring surprise. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account option for that.
