## What does TaskRabbit cost a Tasker in Canada?

TaskRabbit costs a Canadian Tasker a one-time $25 registration fee and nothing per job. The help centre says that beyond the registration fee, "all fees are charged to Clients, not Taskers." Clients see your hourly rate with TaskRabbit's service fee built in, and a separate Trust & Support fee on the receipt, calculated as a percentage of the hourly rate. TaskRabbit does not publish either percentage on its Canadian help pages. It also charges GST, HST or QST on those two fees where required.

What you keep is your rate times billed time, plus 100% of tips and approved expenses. The rules that shape that number:

- **Billing.** Every hourly task has a one-hour minimum, then 15-minute increments. You may set a two-hour minimum, but you must say so in your category description and confirm it with the client in the chat.
- **Fixed-price tasks.** A few categories pay a set amount for the job, not the hours. You may not charge those clients a different amount.
- **Expenses.** Clients can reimburse up to $100 a task for materials left at the job site, $150 for dump fees, with a receipt in the chat. Parking, gas, tolls and tool use are not allowed as expenses, so build them into your rate.
- **Vehicles.** Moving categories that need your truck or van now expect the vehicle cost inside your hourly rate; surcharges are banned.
- **Cancellations.** If a client cancels within 24 hours or is not there, you are eligible for one hour at your rate, unless you have had a terms violation in the last 90 days.
- **Payment.** All payments go through the app. TaskRabbit tells clients not to exchange phone numbers or emails, and off-platform payment can get an account suspended. A good repeat client stays a TaskRabbit client.

TaskRabbit pays by direct deposit to a Canadian chequing account; savings accounts and prepaid cards are not accepted. Elite status needs 7 tasks a month, 40 lifetime tasks in a category, 150 lifetime tasks and a top-35% performance score. It brings a badge in search results, not a lower fee.

### Where is TaskRabbit available in Canada?

TaskRabbit's [city list](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/locations) names 93 Canadian locations, many of them neighbourhoods of the same metro. They cover the Lower Mainland, Victoria, Nanaimo, Squamish and Whistler in BC; Calgary, Edmonton and nearby towns in Alberta; Winnipeg; most of southern Ontario from Windsor to Ottawa and Cornwall; Montréal, Québec City, Gatineau, Sherbrooke and Trois-Rivières; and Halifax. No city in Saskatchewan, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island or Newfoundland and Labrador is on the list. Outside those areas, Kijiji and Facebook are the realistic options.

### What does TaskRabbit's Taskprotect cover?

Taskprotect is TaskRabbit's own goodwill program, not insurance. Under its terms, TaskRabbit "may, in its sole discretion, offer up to $1,000" for property damage, bodily injury or theft caused by negligence on a task booked and paid in the app. Claims are due within 30 days. Exclusions include water damage, vehicles, wall beds, and damage beyond the specific area. Clients must claim on their own home or renter's insurance first.

For a handyperson drilling into walls, $1,000 does not go far. [Zensurance's handyman page](https://www.zensurance.com/contractor-insurance/handyman) says a small residential handyman business "typically starts at $450 per year" for $2 million in commercial general liability. ## What does Kijiji cost for handyman ads?

Kijiji charges a posting fee for every Services ad a private seller places. Its [category pricing overview](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141), updated 21 May 2026, sets the free listing limit for private sellers in Services, all categories, at 0. Fees apply per listing for each 31-day listing period. Business sellers pay posting fees in every fee-based category. Kijiji does not publish the dollar amount on that page; you see it when you start the ad, and it can vary by category and city.

Treat it as a monthly advertising cost. One ad kept up all year is twelve posting fees. A second ad in another Services subcategory is another twelve. Track which ad brings calls and drop the ones that do not. Kijiji takes nothing from the job, so you keep the full price, minus any card fee if you take cards. More on writing the ad is in [getting clients on Kijiji](/side-hustles/get-clients-on-kijiji-canada/).

## Can you advertise handyman services on Facebook Marketplace in Canada?

No. Facebook's [Marketplace rules](https://www.facebook.com/help/130910837313345) say offering services, with house cleaning as the example, "is only allowed on Jobs on Marketplace". Its [jobs page](https://www.facebook.com/help/2203257093390031) says Jobs "is a new feature currently being tested for selected people in selected cities in the US". It is also built for employers hiring staff, not for tradespeople offering work. A handyman listing posted as a Marketplace item in Canada can be rejected.

What works in Canada:

- **A free Facebook business Page** with your service area, prices or minimums, and before-and-after photos.
- **Local community and buy-and-sell groups** whose rules allow service posts. Many admins allow them only on certain days, or only when someone asks for a recommendation. Read the rules first.
- **Paid ads or boosted posts** at a daily budget you set, aimed at your postal codes.

Facebook takes nothing from the job, so the full price is yours. Most direct clients pay by e-Transfer, which costs nothing on many personal accounts.

## How much do you keep from the same job on each platform?

The same three-hour job pays the same rate on all three; what changes is who pays the fees and what you spend to get the job.

**Illustrative. The $50 hourly rate, the job lengths and 20 jobs a year are assumptions, not typical earnings. Kijiji's posting fee is shown as "K" because Kijiji does not publish it.**

| Three-hour shelf and TV-mount job at $50/h | TaskRabbit | Kijiji | Facebook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You bill | 3 × $50 = $150 | $150 | $150 |
| Platform fee from you | $0 | $0 | $0 |
| Card fee if paid by Square invoice | Not applicable | $4.50 (2.8% + 30¢) | $4.50 |
| You keep, e-Transfer or app | $150.00 | $150.00 | $150.00 |
| What the client pays | $150 plus TaskRabbit's two fees and tax on them | $150 | $150 |
| 20 such jobs a year | $3,000 − $25 = $2,975 | $3,000 − 12K for one ad kept up all year | $3,000 |

The [Square Canada](https://squareup.com/ca/en/pricing) invoice fee is 2.8% of $150, $4.20, plus 30¢, for $4.50. Square charges 2.5% on a credit card tapped in person and 0.75% + 7¢ on debit.

Two shorter jobs show where the minimums matter:

- **A 20-minute curtain-rod job.** TaskRabbit bills the one-hour minimum: $50. Direct, you charge whatever minimum or call-out charge you have set, or nothing extra.
- **A 75-minute job.** TaskRabbit bills one hour plus one 15-minute increment: $62.50. Direct, you round however your quote says.

The real difference is how you find clients. TaskRabbit's fees are added to what the client pays, so your $50 shows up higher in search beside cheaper Taskers. On Kijiji and Facebook, the price is what the client sees, but every hour spent posting, answering messages and quoting is unpaid. Most handypeople who start on TaskRabbit add direct channels once they have photos and reviews, and keep app clients in the app.

TaskRabbit suits people in a covered city who want booked work now; Kijiji and Facebook suit people building a client list they own.

## What mistakes do handypeople make on these platforms?

- **Posting services as a Facebook Marketplace item.** It is not allowed in Canada. Use a Page and groups.
- **Assuming Kijiji Services ads are free.** Private sellers pay for every Services ad, every 31 days.
- **Taking a TaskRabbit client off the app.** The payment policy forbids it, and the account is worth more than one job.
- **Billing parking or gas as a TaskRabbit expense.** Only materials left at the site and dump fees qualify. Price your travel into your rate.
- **Treating Taskprotect as insurance.** It is discretionary, capped at $1,000 and excludes water damage.
- **Skipping deposits on direct jobs.** For Kijiji and Facebook clients, agree the price and materials in writing, and take a deposit before buying materials.

## Which tax rules apply to TaskRabbit, Kijiji, and Facebook handyman income in Canada?

Handyman income is generally business income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), whichever channel it comes from. TaskRabbit [reports to the CRA](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260454972315-Income-Tax-Act-Requirements-for-Taskers) every January under the [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html): your name, address, date of birth, SIN, bank account, total earnings, fees deducted and amounts paid per quarter. It suspends payouts until you provide the information. Kijiji and Facebook jobs paid by e-Transfer or cash are not reported by a platform but are just as taxable.

Kijiji posting fees, ad spend, insurance, materials and business kilometres are business expenses. GST/HST registration follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across all channels combined. Keep receipts and see [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/), [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/), and [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/). Tools bought on <AffiliateLink program="amazon-ca" label="Amazon.ca" search="cordless drill driver kit" /> or elsewhere are deductible or depreciable when used for the work.
