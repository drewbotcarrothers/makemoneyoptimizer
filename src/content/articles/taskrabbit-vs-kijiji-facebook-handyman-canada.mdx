For handyman work in Canada, TaskRabbit is the most structured option: you pay a one-time, non-refundable $25 registration fee, set your own hourly rate in most categories, and TaskRabbit charges its service fee to the client and handles payment. Kijiji and Facebook are classifieds and community channels rather than booking apps. Kijiji lists Services as a category with no free listings for private sellers, so posting fees apply per 31-day listing. Facebook says offering services is allowed only on Jobs on Marketplace, though local groups and a business Page are other ways to be found. Most new handypeople use TaskRabbit for booked work and Kijiji or Facebook for direct, repeat clients.

## How do TaskRabbit, Kijiji, and Facebook compare for handyman work?

TaskRabbit is a booking platform with set rules; Kijiji and Facebook are places to advertise where you run the job yourself. Details are from each company's Canadian help pages on October 4, 2026.

| Factor | TaskRabbit | Kijiji | Facebook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost to start | One-time, non-refundable $25 registration fee ([registration fee](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46260480151579-What-s-the-Registration-Fee)) | Private sellers' free listing limit in Services is 0, so posting fees apply ([pricing overview](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141)) | No listing fee stated; services only on Jobs on Marketplace ([Marketplace rules](https://www.facebook.com/help/130910837313345)) |

## How do fees and costs work on TaskRabbit, Kijiji, and Facebook?

On TaskRabbit, your up-front cost is the $25 registration fee and the platform's fee is billed to the client; on Kijiji you pay to post in Services; on Facebook the main cost is your time.

- **TaskRabbit:** the client sees your hourly rate inclusive of the service fee. You receive 100% of any reimbursements, such as materials you buy for the task. Paying the registration fee does not guarantee approval.
- **Kijiji:** posting fees are charged for each 31-day listing period in fee-based categories. Business sellers pay posting fees in all listed categories. Kijiji's overview does not list the dollar amount, so check it before you post.
- **Facebook:** regular Marketplace listings cannot offer services. Jobs on Marketplace, a business Page, and local community groups (subject to each group's rules) are the usual routes.

### Illustrative example

Assume a three-hour shelf and TV-mount job at a $50 hourly rate. The rate, hours, and the 20 jobs a year are assumptions; Kijiji's posting fee is left as a variable because Kijiji does not publish it on the overview page.

- **TaskRabbit:** 3 × $50 = $150 for you; the client pays the service fee on top. Over 20 such jobs, $3,000 before tax, minus the one-time $25 registration fee = $2,975.
- **Kijiji:** 3 × $50 = $150 from the client directly. Over 20 jobs, $3,000 minus your posting fees (charged per listing, per 31 days).
- **Facebook:** $150 per job and $3,000 over 20 jobs, with no posting fee for a Jobs on Marketplace post or group post, but every lead depends on your own follow-up.

## Which option should a new handyperson choose?

TaskRabbit suits people who want booked jobs without marketing; Kijiji and Facebook suit people building a local client list. This is a decision rule from the fee structures, not a ranking.

| If you… | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Want work without writing ads | TaskRabbit | Clients book through the app |
| Live outside a TaskRabbit city | Kijiji or Facebook | TaskRabbit requires an active city |
| Want to keep every repeat client | Kijiji or Facebook | No platform between you and the client |
| Want the platform to collect payment | TaskRabbit | Payment runs through the app |
| Prefer quoting by the job | Kijiji or Facebook | TaskRabbit hourly tasks use hourly billing |

## What mistakes do handypeople make on these platforms?

The common mistakes are posting services in the wrong place and not accounting for the minimum-hour rules.

- **Listing services as a Marketplace item.** Facebook only allows services on Jobs on Marketplace.
- **Ignoring Kijiji posting fees.** Services is a fee-based category for private sellers.
- **Setting a TaskRabbit 2-hour minimum without saying so.** TaskRabbit requires Taskers to note it in their category description and in client chats.
- **Charging off-app on TaskRabbit fixed-price tasks.** TaskRabbit says Taskers may not charge a different amount for fixed-price tasks.
- **Skipping deposits on direct jobs.** For Kijiji and Facebook clients, agree the price in writing before buying materials.

## Which tax rules apply to TaskRabbit, Kijiji, and Facebook handyman income in Canada?

Handyman income is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), whether it comes through an app or from a classified ad. Under the CRA's [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html), platforms that facilitate personal services may report seller information to the CRA, while cash and e-Transfer jobs from classified ads are not reported by the platform but are still taxable. Keep receipts for tools and materials, track mileage, and see [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/), [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/), and [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/).
