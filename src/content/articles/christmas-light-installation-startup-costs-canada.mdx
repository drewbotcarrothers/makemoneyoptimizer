## How much does Christmas light installation cost to start in Canada?

Christmas light installation in Canada starts with clips and a cord when a safe ladder is already in the garage, and it costs more when the ladder, insurance, and light inventory are still to buy. Canada does not publish a startup fee for this work. Verify every shelf price locally. Insurance is whatever a broker quotes after you describe ladders and other people's homes.

## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for Christmas lights in Canada?

Ongoing Christmas light costs in Canada are replacement strings, fuel or transit to the route, insurance that does not shrink because December was mild, and your unpaid time measuring houses. The break-even sketch below is a method. It is not a contract price.

**Insurance.** The seasonal premium is fixed whether you hang ten houses or two. Get the quote before you spend a deposit.

**Materials that disappear.** Clips break. Clients keep strings, or they do not. Write that down so January is not an argument.

**Vehicle.** $0 if you walk. If you drive, the kilometres are a cost. Log them.

**Weather.** CCOHS says not to climb in high wind. A price that assumes every booked day happens will be wrong in an ice week.

**Inventory.** The common first-season miss is a high gross that still loses money once the ladder, the lights you bought, insurance, and labour are counted. Judge the season on what is left, not on the deposit total.

### Step 1: Add the cash you must spend before the first paid climb

Clips, cord, timer, and a ladder only if you do not already own a suitable one. Leave insurance as "quote," not as a guessed premium.

### Step 2: Do not add unsold light inventory

Buy strings for a house that has agreed, unless you have already decided you are in the business of owning displays. That is a bigger bet than a side season.

### Step 3: Separate gear cash from your hours

Money you pay yourself for ladder time is not "left over" until the gear and the insurance quote are covered. The pricing guide's hourly target is how you pay the hours. This page is how you get the tools back.
