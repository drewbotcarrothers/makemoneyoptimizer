## What offer gets a first moving help client in Canada?

A first moving help client in Canada usually buys a few hours of carrying, on their vehicle, with stairs and addresses written down. "Full service movers" is a different business. "Two hours, boxes and a sofa, your van, no piano" is a purchase.

Say what is outside the first job. Pianos, safes, appliances you have not agreed to, and a second address are later quotes. In Ontario, the [Hiring a mover](https://www.ontario.ca/page/hiring-mover) page says moving companies are not licensed by the province, and that consumer contracts for moving services worth more than $50 must be in writing, with the mover's name and contact information, a description and itemized prices, the total, dates, and payment terms. The page also says a mover cannot charge more than 10 percent above the estimate unless the customer agrees to additional supplies or services. Other provinces publish their own pages. Ontario is an example, not a national code. Write the note even when you are under that threshold.

## Where do Canadians find a first moving help client?

Canadians find a first moving help client among people who are actually moving and who already know them, then through a local post that names labour-only. A scraped list of apartment buildings is a poor start.

People who already know you. A friend of a colleague, a neighbour with a date. Ask what floor, what vehicle, and what they will not ask you to carry.

A move you can describe. Elevator or walk-up, distance from the door to the vehicle, and whether a second person is required for one piece. You are offering that morning, not a fleet.

A listing, if you use one. This guide does not state a platform fee. Put "client's vehicle" in the first line so you are not hired as a truck you do not have.

If you email, [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies to commercial messages. ## What should a first moving help note say?

A first moving help note in Canada should name the hours or the load, both addresses, the vehicle, the pieces you refuse, and the price.

**Example message.** This is a generic example you can adapt. It is not a note that produced a client.

> Hello. I can help carry on Saturday for two hours. You supply the vehicle. I will move boxes and one sofa. I do not move pianos, safes, or appliances unless we add them in writing before I start. The price is a number we both have before the first box. Stairs and both addresses need to be in the note. Does that match the morning you have?

If the job grows at noon, stop and write the extra before you do it. Where Ontario's 10 percent rule applies, an unagreed jump is not a rounding error. Name both addresses, the floor, and whether an elevator is part of the plan. A walk-up that was described as "a couple of steps" is how a two-hour block becomes a morning you did not price.

## How do you prove moving help skill without fake reviews?

You prove moving help in Canada with a clear no-list and a plan for the landing, not with a stock photo of a truck. Invented testimonials and a logo that implies a fleet are misrepresentations.

If you have helped someone who agrees you may say so, say what you actually carried. Do not upgrade "helped with boxes" into "full-service mover." Practise a blanket wrap on a sofa you own so the first client's sofa is not the lesson.

## How should you price the first moving help job in Canada?

Price the first moving help job in Canada as a block of hours or a small defined load, with stairs in the note, and replace any sample number with one you can defend on those stairs. This guide does not publish a national moving rate. An elevator studio and a third-floor walk-up are different jobs.

| Path | What the client buys | What you watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Labour block | Hours, their vehicle, named pieces | A piece that will not turn, a broken elevator |
| You supply the truck | A later offer | Insurance and the province's rules for that use |

Parking, a broken elevator, and a hallway that will not turn a sofa are pricing factors even when the note says two hours. Write the floor before you accept. A labour-only morning that becomes "can you also drive the van" is a different offer, with that province's rules for the vehicle and an insurance conversation you have not had. Keep the first yes inside the block you wrote.

**Illustrative example.** Suppose you assume $200 for a two-hour labour block. That $200 is an assumption for the arithmetic, not a market rate. Suppose the morning runs three hours because a sofa would not turn, and you did not write an overtime line. The sketch is $200 ÷ 3 = about $67 an hour before a tax set-aside, which is worse than the two-hour plan of $100 an hour. Parking is not in these figures. These totals are assumptions, not a quote, a forecast, or a typical result.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first moving help client look like?

A 14-day plan for a first moving help client in Canada should produce a labour-only offer, a written note, and a few asks to people who are actually moving. A paid morning is a bonus. Silence is not a reason to finance a van.

### Days 1 and 2: Write the no-list

Hours or a small load. Client's vehicle. No piano, no safe. Read Ontario's hiring-a-mover page if you work there, and your own province's consumer page if you do not.

### Days 3 to 5: Gather the small kit

Gloves, a few blankets, straps. Practise a wrap. Ask for a liability quote that mentions stairs and household goods. This is not insurance advice.

### Days 6 to 9: Ask people with a date

Five conversations. If you email, follow the consent page. Confirm floors and the vehicle before you agree.

### Days 10 to 14: Carry what you named, or wait

If someone books, stop at the list and invoice the same day. If nobody books, do not add a truck to the offer. Ask again only among people you have not already contacted.

## What mistakes block a first moving help client in Canada?

Mistakes that block a first moving help client in Canada are advertising a truck you do not have, skipping the written note, and accepting a piano to be polite. One labour block is enough.

An unstrapped load is a road hazard, not a favour. A second address that appears at noon is a new job. Emailing a purchased list skips anti-spam consent. Calling a friend an employee without checking that province's workers' compensation rules is a separate mistake.

## Which tax rules apply to a first moving help client in Canada?

Moving help income in Canada is self-employment income, including a deposit, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.
