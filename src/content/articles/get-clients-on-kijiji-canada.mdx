Kijiji isn't a booking app like TaskRabbit. You find, quote, schedule and collect from clients yourself, so the ad and your reply do the selling. This guide is for anyone offering cleaning, lawn care, snow clearing, handyman or moving help locally.

## How much does it cost to advertise a side hustle on Kijiji in Canada?

Kijiji's [category pricing overview](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/category-pricing-overview-listing-fees/141) lists a free listing limit of 0 for private sellers in all Services categories. Posting fees apply per listing and are charged for each 31-day listing period. Kijiji's [listing length page](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/how-long-kijiji-listings-stay-active/160) says fee-based listings stay active for 31 days, and reposting may require a new insertion fee. Bump Up is a separate paid feature that moves an active ad closer to the top. The fee is shown when you post, so check it then and count it as a monthly marketing cost. Log it as a business expense, as covered in [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/).

## How do you write a Kijiji ad that gets replies in Canada?

### Step 1: Pick one category and one ad

Kijiji's [duplicate ads policy](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/posting-duplicate-ads-policy/190) says not to post the same ad in more than one category or location. In Services, combine related offerings into one comprehensive listing. The [Services Code of Conduct](https://community.kijiji.ca/t/services-code-of-conduct/52) allows separate ads only for clearly different services, such as carpentry and plumbing. So "house cleaning and move-out cleaning" is one ad, while "lawn care" and "house cleaning" can be two.

### Step 2: Write a headline a client would search

Name the service and the area: "Weekly lawn mowing, east end, small yards." Skip vague words like "best" or "cheap." Clients scan headlines, so the service and the place should come first.

### Step 3: Fill the body with what clients ask anyway

- What's included and what isn't.
- Your service area, by neighbourhood or postal code prefix.
- Your availability, such as weekday evenings and Saturdays.
- A price starting point or how you quote, such as "free quote after a 10-minute visit."
- Whether you bring supplies and equipment.
- Insurance if you carry it; see [side hustle insurance](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

### Step 4: Add real photos

Use photos of your own work: a finished lawn edge, a cleaned stove, a cleared driveway. Skip stock images. Before-and-after pairs show the result faster than any sentence.

### Step 5: Reply fast and move to a written quote

Answer within a few hours, ask two or three scoping questions, and send a written quote. In Ontario, the province says the final price [can't be more than 10% above a written estimate](https://www.ontario.ca/page/your-rights-when-signing-or-cancelling-contract) that's part of the contract unless the client agrees to a new price. That's a good reason to quote carefully everywhere. The [house cleaning quote walkthrough](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-quote-walkthrough-canada/) and [lawn care estimate guide](/side-hustles/lawn-care-estimate-canada/) show how.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real ad. Suppose you offer snow clearing and lawn care. You'd post one Services ad for "Driveway snow clearing, seasonal or per visit, north end" in winter, with photos of cleared driveways. In spring you'd let it expire and post a lawn care ad instead, rather than running both at once. Each reply would get the same three questions: address, driveway or yard size, and how often. A written quote would follow the same day.

## How do you avoid scams and bad clients on Kijiji in Canada?

- **Overpayment offers.** A "client" who sends too much and asks for the difference back is a classic scam. Report it to the [Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre](https://www.antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/index-eng.htm).
- **Requests to pay through odd links or gift cards.** Take payment by methods you control, after the work or on a deposit you set.
- **Vague jobs.** Get the address and scope before you drive anywhere.
- **No written terms.** A short agreement prevents most disputes; see [freelance contracts](/side-hustles/freelance-contract-canada/).

## What mistakes do Kijiji side hustlers make in Canada?

- **Posting the same ad in several categories.** Kijiji removes duplicates.
- **Letting the ad lapse in peak season.** Fee-based listings last 31 days, so diarize the repost.
- **No price signal at all.** A starting point or quote method filters out mismatched clients.
- **Slow replies.** The first reasonable reply often wins the job.

## How are Kijiji clients taxed in Canada?

Money from Kijiji clients is business income reported on Form T2125, the same as any other client. You register for GST/HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters.
