## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Winnipeg, Canada?

### Step 1: Sell windrow and driveway clearing

The City's [snow FAQ](https://legacy.winnipeg.ca/publicworks/snow/snow-FAQ.stm) says removing ridges of snow that block private approaches is the owner's job. Windrows left by back lane plowing are the homeowner's responsibility too. The City does remove windrows taller than 20 cm on streets when it can. You can't move snow from private property onto a City right-of-way, or dump it on another property. Plan where each client's snow goes before you quote. Clients often want a visit right after the plow passes. [Snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) covers per-visit and seasonal rates, and the [snow removal 30-day plan](/side-hustles/snow-removal-30-day-plan-canada/) walks through a first month.

### Step 2: Shovel residential sidewalks

The same FAQ says the City clears Downtown and other sidewalks but not those alongside residential streets. Owners who want a clear walk or boulevard path need to do it themselves or hire it out. Bundle it with driveway clearing for one visit price. The [snow removal guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) and [snow removal tools](/side-hustles/snow-removal-tools-canada/) help you equip for it.

### Step 3: Work the winter festival

[Festival du Voyageur](https://heho.ca/en/) takes place February 12 to 21, 2027. Look for [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/) shifts, and offer [winter pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/) to people heading south for a break. Bilingual French-English skills help here; see [bilingual side hustles](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-bilingual-french-english-speakers-canada/).

## What seasons drive Winnipeg side hustle income?

- **November to April:** windrows, driveways and sidewalks; residential parking bans make timing matter.
- **February:** Festival du Voyageur shifts and holiday pet sitting.
- **May to June:** spring yard cleanup and [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Summer:** [moving help](/side-hustles/moving-help-side-hustle-canada/), [pressure washing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-side-hustle-canada/) and [house cleaning](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Fall:** [leaf cleanup](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/) and [Christmas light installation](/side-hustles/christmas-light-installation-side-hustle-canada/).

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Winnipeg, Canada?

Usually not to work from home. The City's [business licences page](https://www.winnipeg.ca/services-programs/permits-licences-0/business-licences) says you don't need a business licence to operate from home, but zoning rules still apply. The City licenses a short list of activities, including used goods dealers. Flipping furniture or clothing at volume should be checked against that list before you advertise. A business name other than your own is registered with Manitoba, as covered in [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you sign ten homes on two streets for windrow and driveway clearing. Your terms would say you'll come after the City plows their street. You'd clear the windrow and driveway and pile snow on the client's lot, never on the street or a neighbour's yard. Sidewalk clearing would be an add-on for clients on residential streets. Your phone would carry the City's Know Your Zone app so you can plan around residential plowing.

## What mistakes do Winnipeg side hustlers make?

- **Pushing snow onto the street.** The City says snow can't be moved from private property onto a City right-of-way.
- **Dumping snow next door.** Snow can't be taken from one property and dumped on another.
- **Clearing before the plow comes.** The windrow comes back, so price a return visit.
- **Assuming reselling is unlicensed.** Used goods dealers are on the City's licensed list.

## How are Winnipeg side hustles taxed?

You report self-employment income on Form T2125 and register for 5% GST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from their first fare. Manitoba's 7% retail sales tax applies to taxable goods and some services. [Notice RST 24-01](https://www.gov.mb.ca/finance/taxation/pubs/bulletins/noticerst2401.pdf) removed the registration requirement for small businesses under $30,000 in annual taxable sales. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/).
