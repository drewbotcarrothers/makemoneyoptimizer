## What fees does Etsy charge Canadian sellers in 2026?

Etsy charges Canadian sellers a listing fee, a transaction fee, a payment processing fee, and a Canada-specific Regulatory Operating fee, with Offsite Ads fees on some orders. The rates below come from Etsy's help centre; open the live pages before you rely on them, because Etsy can change them.

| Fee | Rate for a Canadian seller | Charged on | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listing | US$0.20 per listing; listings expire after 4 months | Each listing and each auto-renewal | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Transaction | 6.5% | Total order, including shipping and gift wrap | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Payment processing | 3% + CA$0.25 (domestic or US orders); 4% + CA$0.25 (international) | Total sale price, including shipping and any sales tax | [Etsy Payments fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Regulatory Operating fee | 0.50% | Item price plus shipping and gift wrap | [Regulatory Operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) |
| Offsite Ads | 15%, or 12% once you made at least $10,000 on Etsy in the past 365 days; never more than $100 per order | Order total, only on sales attributed to those ads | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% | When your listing currency differs from your bank account currency | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |

Etsy also lists a one-time set-up fee that "varies by location" and is shown during shop set-up. Fixed fees such as the listing fee are published in US dollars and converted at the market rate when they are charged. At the Bank of Canada's [daily exchange rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4246 CAD per USD on October 2, 2026, US$0.20 is about $0.28. Listing in Canadian dollars with a Canadian bank account avoids the 2.5% conversion fee.

## Which pricing approaches work for an Etsy shop in Canada?

Most Etsy sellers in Canada use cost-plus pricing built from materials and timed labour, then check the result against sold listings for similar items. Choosing between free shipping and charged shipping changes the number, not the method, because Etsy's percentage fees apply to shipping too.

| Approach | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost-plus with labour | Materials + packaging + timed labour + fees | Handmade items you can time | Forgetting photo, listing, and packing time |
| Shipping built into price | One price, "free shipping" to Canadian buyers | Light items with predictable postage | Heavier orders to remote addresses |
| Charged shipping | Item price plus a shipping profile | Heavy or bulky items | Fees still apply to the shipping amount |
| Tiered (small, standard, custom) | Same item in sizes or with personalization | Prints, jewellery, signs | Custom work that takes twice as long |
| Market check | Compare sold, not just listed, items | Every listing | Copying a price without knowing its costs |

## How do you calculate an Etsy price in Canada?

Calculate an Etsy price in Canada by adding your costs and target pay, then dividing by one minus Etsy's percentage fees so the fees are covered. Round, and test what an Offsite Ads sale would leave.

### Step 1: Count materials and packaging per item

Divide a batch of supplies by the number of items it makes. Include mailers, tissue, labels, and tape.

### Step 2: Time the item and set an hourly rate

Time making, photographing, listing, and packing one item. Multiply by the hourly pay you want.

### Step 3: Price shipping from a real label quote

Weigh and measure the packed item and price the label you will actually buy. A guess hurts most on heavy items.

### Step 4: Divide by one minus the percentage fees

For a domestic or US order the percentage fees are 6.5% + 3% + 0.50% = 10%. Price before fixed fees = costs ÷ 0.90. Then add the CA$0.25 processing fee and the converted listing fee.

### Step 5: Check the Offsite Ads case and set tax aside

Work out what you keep if 15% of the order goes to Offsite Ads. Then hold back a share of profit for income tax and CPP.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose a candle costs $8.00 in materials and $1.50 in packaging, takes 45 minutes in total, and ships for $10.00, which you charge as shipping. You want $24 an hour, so labour is 0.75 × $24 = $18.00. The order total is the item price plus $10.00 shipping.

Costs to cover are $8.00 + $1.50 + $18.00 + $10.00 = $37.50. Dividing by 0.90 gives $41.67. Add $0.25 processing and $0.28 for the listing fee: $42.20 for the whole order. Subtract the $10.00 shipping and the item price is about $32.20, which you might round to $34.

Check the result at $34 + $10 shipping = $44 order total. Transaction fee: 6.5% × $44 = $2.86. Processing: 3% × $44 + $0.25 = $1.57. Regulatory fee: 0.50% × $44 = $0.22. Listing: $0.28. Fees total $4.93. You keep $44 − $4.93 − $10.00 − $9.50 = $19.57 for 45 minutes, or about $26 an hour. If the same order came from Offsite Ads, 15% × $44 = $6.60 more comes off, leaving $12.97, or about $17 an hour. This sketch ignores sales tax.

## When should you raise Etsy prices in Canada?

Raise Etsy prices in Canada when materials or postage go up, when your time per item is higher than you priced, when Etsy changes a fee, or when items sell out faster than you can make them. Change one listing at a time and watch views and sales for a few weeks.

Re-run Step 4 whenever you pass $10,000 in sales over 365 days, because the Offsite Ads rate drops to 12%. Re-run it if you register for GST/HST, because how tax appears in your price changes (below).

## What Etsy pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common Etsy pricing mistakes in Canada are leaving out your own time, forgetting that fees apply to shipping, and ignoring the Offsite Ads rate. Each one makes a "good" price lose money on a busy week.

Do not price from listed prices; check sold items. Do not forget the Canadian Regulatory Operating fee, which most US pricing calculators skip. List in Canadian dollars if your bank account is Canadian, to avoid the 2.5% conversion fee. Price custom work from timed hours, not from a standard item. ## How do GST/HST and income tax affect Etsy prices in Canada?

Etsy income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once your taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses.

Etsy's page on [what it remits to Canadian tax authorities](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/6633345416215-What-Does-Etsy-Remit-to-Canadian-Tax-Authorities) says that if you have not added a GST/HST ID, Etsy collects GST/HST at checkout on physical items shipped from a Canadian seller to a Canadian buyer and remits it. If you add a GST/HST ID, Etsy stops collecting it on those sales, and you include the tax in your listing price and remit it yourself, so your prices need to change. Etsy also charges GST/HST on seller fees such as listing and ad fees.

Self-employed people pay both shares of CPP on net self-employment earnings; see [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
