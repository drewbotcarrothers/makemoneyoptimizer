To price an Etsy item in Canada, add materials, packaging, the postage you'll pay and your time, then divide by 0.90 so Etsy's roughly 10% in percentage fees comes out of the buyer's price, not yours. Add about $0.53 in fixed fees per order. In October 2026, a Canadian seller pays 6.5% transaction, 3% + $0.25 processing on Canadian and US orders, a 0.50% Regulatory Operating fee and a US$0.20 listing fee, all charged on shipping too. Offsite Ads take another 15% on the orders they bring in.

<QuickAnswer>

- **Formula:** order total = (materials + packaging + labour + postage + $0.53) ÷ 0.90. Then check what's left if Offsite Ads take 15%.
- **Etsy fees for a Canadian seller (verified October 9, 2026):** US$0.20 per listing (about $0.28), 6.5% transaction, 3% + CA$0.25 processing (4% + $0.25 outside Canada and the US), 0.50% Regulatory Operating fee, 2.5% currency conversion if you list in another currency.
- **Postage (Canada Post, 2026):** oversize Lettermail $2.61 up to 100 g; flat rate boxes $18.99 to $32.99; Small Packet USA Air $9.11 for 100 g. Chit Chats lists $8.45 for an 85 g necklace and $10.61 for a 425 g mug to New York.
- **Labour benchmark:** craftspersons earn a $20.00 median nationally ($15.60 low, $28.00 high), per Job Bank.

## What fees does Etsy charge Canadian sellers in 2026?

Etsy charges Canadian sellers a listing fee, a transaction fee, a payment processing fee and a 0.50% Regulatory Operating fee, plus Offsite Ads fees on orders those ads bring in. Every rate below was on Etsy's help centre on October 9, 2026.

| Fee | Rate for a Canadian seller | Charged on | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listing | US$0.20 per listing; listings expire after 4 months. Another US$0.20 when a sold listing auto-renews or for each extra quantity sold | Each listing and renewal | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Transaction | 6.5% | Total order, including shipping and gift wrap | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Payment processing | 3% + CA$0.25 (orders from Canada or the US); 4% + CA$0.25 (other countries) | Total sale price, including shipping and any sales tax | [Etsy Payments fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Regulatory Operating fee | 0.50% | Item price plus shipping and gift wrap | [Regulatory Operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) |
| Offsite Ads | 15%, or 12% once you've made at least $10,000 on Etsy in the past 365 days; never more than $100 per order | Order total, only on sales attributed to those ads | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% | When your listing currency differs from your bank account currency | [Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) |
| Share & Save (credit) | 4% of the order total refunded | Orders placed within 30 days of a click on your trackable link | [Share & Save](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/16981332744087-How-to-Save-on-Etsy-Fees-with-the-Share-Save-Program) |

Etsy also charges a one-time set-up fee that "varies by location," shown during shop set-up. Fixed fees are published in US dollars and converted when charged. At the Bank of Canada's [daily exchange rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4240 CAD per USD on October 8, 2026, US$0.20 is $0.28. Listing in Canadian dollars with a Canadian bank account avoids the 2.5% conversion fee.

Lettermail has no tracking, so it suits low-value flat items like cards, stickers and prints. Chit Chats' prices exclude taxes and change, so quote from its calculator before you set a shipping profile.

| Area | Low | Median | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canada | $15.60 | $20.00 | $28.00 |
| British Columbia | $18.25 | $23.00 | $33.00 |
| Ontario | $17.95 | $21.00 | $27.50 |
| Quebec | $17.00 | $20.75 | $28.00 |

Self-employed makers pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) (11.9% outside Quebec) and aren't paid for photographing, listing, answering messages or packing. Price your labour above the median and count every one of those minutes.

**Illustrative labour rate:** $25 an hour target pay (between the $20.00 median and the $28.00 high) + 11.9% CPP ($2.98) = $27.98, rounded to **$28 an hour**. The worked listings below use it.

## Which pricing approaches work for an Etsy shop?

Most sellers use cost-plus pricing built from materials and timed labour, then check the result against sold listings for similar items. Free shipping versus charged shipping changes where the postage shows up, not the math, because Etsy's fees apply to shipping either way.

| Approach | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cost-plus with labour | Materials + packaging + timed labour + postage, ÷ 0.90 | Handmade items you can time | Forgetting photo, listing and packing time |
| Shipping built into price | One price, "free shipping" | Light items with predictable postage | Heavier orders and US addresses |
| Charged shipping | Item price plus a shipping profile | Heavy or bulky items | Fees still apply to the shipping amount |
| Tiered (small, standard, custom) | Same item in sizes or with personalization | Prints, jewellery, signs | Custom work that takes twice as long |
| Bundles | Two or three small items in one order | Cards, stickers, small prints | The fixed $0.53 per order eats small orders |

## How do you calculate an Etsy price?

Add your costs and labour, add the fixed fees, and divide by one minus Etsy's percentage fees. Then round, and check what an Offsite Ads sale would leave.

```text
Costs         = materials + packaging + (minutes ÷ 60 × hourly rate) + postage you pay
Order total   = (costs + $0.25 processing + $0.28 listing) ÷ 0.90
Item price    = order total − shipping you charge
Offsite check = what you keep − 15% of the order total
```

For orders from outside Canada and the US, processing is 4%, so divide by 0.89.

## What do worked Etsy listings look like?

**Illustrative.** These use the $28 labour rate and assumed materials and making times. Fees and postage are the published 2026 figures above; each fee is rounded to the cent.

### Listing 1: greeting card pack, shipped in Canada by Lettermail

- Costs: materials and envelope $2.50 + 10 minutes labour $4.67 + oversize Lettermail $2.61 = $9.78.
- Order total needed: ($9.78 + $0.53) ÷ 0.90 = $11.46. Price **$9.50 + $2.75 shipping = $12.25**.
- Fees on $12.25: transaction $0.80 + processing $0.62 + regulatory $0.06 + listing $0.28 = $1.76.
- You keep $12.25 − $1.76 − $2.61 − $2.50 = **$5.38** for 10 minutes ($32.28 an hour). An Offsite Ads sale takes $1.84 more, leaving $3.54. Small orders are where bundles help.

### Listing 2: necklace to a US buyer by Chit Chats

- Costs: materials and packaging $6.00 + 30 minutes $14.00 + Chit Chats $8.45 = $28.45.
- Needed: ($28.45 + $0.53) ÷ 0.90 = $32.20. Price **$24 + $9 shipping = $33**.
- Fees on $33: $2.15 + $1.24 + $0.17 + $0.28 = $3.84.
- You keep $33 − $3.84 − $8.45 − $6.00 = **$14.71** for 30 minutes ($29.42 an hour); $9.76 after a 15% Offsite Ads fee ($4.95).

### Listing 3: ceramic mug to a US buyer by Chit Chats

- Costs: clay, glaze, firing share and box $9.00 + 1 hour $28.00 + Chit Chats $10.61 = $47.61.
- Needed: ($47.61 + $0.53) ÷ 0.90 = $53.49. Price **$42 + $12 shipping = $54**.
- Fees on $54: $3.51 + $1.87 + $0.27 + $0.28 = $5.93.
- You keep $54 − $5.93 − $10.61 − $9.00 = **$28.46** for the hour; $20.36 after Offsite Ads ($8.10).

### Listing 4: custom wood sign, shipped in Canada in a small flat rate box

- Costs: wood, paint and packing $22.00 + 2 hours $56.00 + small flat rate box $21.99 = $99.99.
- Needed: ($99.99 + $0.53) ÷ 0.90 = $111.69. Price **$92 + $22 shipping = $114**, or $114 with free shipping.
- Fees on $114: $7.41 + $3.67 + $0.57 + $0.28 = $11.93.
- You keep $114 − $11.93 − $21.99 − $22.00 = **$58.08** for 2 hours ($29.04 an hour). If the buyer came through your Share & Save link, Etsy credits back $4.56 (4%). If the buyer came from an Offsite Ad, $17.10 comes off instead, leaving $40.98.

## How should you write a custom-order quote?

Custom orders take longer than standard ones, so quote them from timed hours and take payment before you start, through a private listing (US$0.20 if purchased).

```text
CUSTOM ORDER QUOTE — [buyer username]   Date: [date]
Item: 24" x 8" pine sign, "The [Family Name]s", white on walnut stain
Materials and packing: $22
Making time: 2 hours, includes one proof photo and one round of changes
Extra changes after the proof: $14 per half hour
Price: $92 + $22 shipping (Canada Post flat rate box, tracked) = $114
Ships: 7–10 business days after payment
Payment: through a private Etsy listing I'll send today
Prices include GST/HST [or: Etsy adds GST/HST at checkout]
```

Etsy produces the receipt, so you don't need a separate invoice. Do keep a monthly record of sales, fees, postage and materials for tax time. A tool such as <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can track fees and expenses once a spreadsheet gets crowded.

## How do you raise Etsy prices?

Raise prices when materials or postage go up, when an item takes longer than you priced, when Etsy changes a fee, or when items sell out faster than you can make them. Change one listing at a time and watch views and sales for a few weeks.

1. **Re-run the formula when costs change.** Canada Post updates its price guide (the current version is dated September 7, 2026), and Chit Chats' [rate update](https://chitchats.com/blog/annual-rate-increases) lists temporary US holiday increases of US$0.50 and up from October 4, 2026 to January 17, 2027.
2. **Raise the small items first.** The card pack keeps $5.38 against $58.08 for the sign; a $1 increase matters most where margins are thinnest.
3. **Bundle low-priced items.** Three card packs in one order pay the $0.53 fixed fees once.
4. **Charge for personalization and rush orders** as separate listing options.
5. **Drive your own traffic.** Share & Save returns 4% of the order total, which partly offsets the 6.5% transaction fee.
6. **Re-run the Offsite Ads check at $10,000 in sales**, when the rate drops to 12%.

## What Etsy pricing mistakes are common?

- **Leaving out your time.** Photographing, listing and packing are part of each item.
- **Forgetting fees apply to shipping.** A $22 shipping charge carries about $2.20 in percentage fees.
- **Skipping the Regulatory Operating fee.** Most US pricing calculators leave out Canada's 0.50%.
- **Pricing from listed items, not sold ones.** Check what sells.
- **Ignoring Offsite Ads.** A 15% fee on a thin margin can wipe out most of a small order's profit.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect Etsy prices?

Etsy income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies across all your businesses pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters.

Etsy's page on [what it remits to Canadian tax authorities](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/6633345416215-What-Does-Etsy-Remit-to-Canadian-Tax-Authorities) says that if you haven't added a GST/HST ID, Etsy collects GST/HST at checkout on physical items shipped from a Canadian seller to a Canadian buyer and remits it. Once you add your ID, Etsy stops collecting it on those sales, and you include the tax in your listing price and remit it yourself at your province's [rate](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). A $114 tax-included order to an Ontario buyer then contains $13.12 of HST (13/113 of the price), so registered sellers need to raise prices to keep the same margin. Etsy also charges GST/HST on its seller fees.

Self-employed makers pay both shares of CPP on net earnings; see the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
