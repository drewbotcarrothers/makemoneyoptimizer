Etsy listing SEO for Canadian sellers means filling every field Etsy uses to match a search, accurately and in the words buyers use. That's the title, tags, attributes, category, description and photos. Etsy's help centre sets the limits: 140-character titles, 13 tags of up to 20 characters, and up to 20 photos. It also explains how Google reads your listing page. A few extras matter more when you sell from Canada, such as shipping details and Canadian terms. This guide is for makers who have a shop and want listings that get found.

## How do you write an Etsy title that ranks in Canada?

### Step 1: Say what it is, first

Etsy's [listing guide](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing) says a title can be up to 140 characters, but suggests fewer than 15 words. Clearly state what the item is and its key details. Avoid repetition and subjective words such as "perfect," and leave out price and shipping. Etsy's [SEO page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663987-Search-Engine-Optimization-SEO-for-Shop-and-Listing-Pages) adds that search engines show only the first 50 to 60 characters of a page title. Put colour, material and size up front, for example "Hand-knit merino wool toque, forest green, adult."

### Step 2: Get the first title right

Etsy says a listing's URL is based on the title you enter when you first publish, and it doesn't change later. If you change what you're selling, Etsy suggests a new listing instead.

## How should Canadian sellers use Etsy tags and attributes?

### Step 3: Fill every attribute

Attributes such as colour, material and size change by category. Etsy's [tags page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000336307-How-to-Use-Tags-to-Get-Found-in-Search) says tags are most helpful when there's no matching attribute. So fill attributes first, then use tags for everything else.

### Step 4: Use all 13 tags, with variety

You can add up to 13 tags, each up to 20 characters, using letters, numbers and spaces. Etsy recommends a diverse set of accurate phrases, including buyer-minded terms such as "gift for her." Multi-word phrases like "wool toque" or "baby shower gift" match how people search.

### Step 5: Use the words Canadian buyers use

If your buyers are in Canada, include the terms they type: "toque" as well as "beanie," and Canadian spellings in your description where they fit. Adding both spellings in tags can catch both searches.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real listing. Suppose you sell hand-knit hats. Your title would lead with the item, material and colour. Your attributes would cover colour, material and size. Your 13 tags would mix phrases like "wool toque," "knit beanie," "winter hat women," "gift for skier," and "chunky knit hat." The description's first sentence would say exactly what the hat is and who it fits. Photos would show the hat worn, flat, up close on the stitching, and with a measuring tape.

## What makes Etsy photos and descriptions work for search?

- **Photos:** Etsy allows up to 20 photos and 2 videos of 5 to 15 seconds, and recommends images at least 2000 pixels wide.
- **Alt text:** Etsy's SEO page says to describe each photo clearly and accurately, including colour, texture and material.
- **Description:** make the first sentence say exactly what you sell, then cover variations, personalization and care. Use the words shoppers search without repeating the same phrase over and over.
- **Shop page:** a tagline of up to 55 characters, a full About section, and complete policies help Google understand your shop.
- **Shipping details:** Etsy requires an origin postal code and a processing time on every listing, so set realistic ones for Canadian and cross-border orders.

## What Etsy SEO mistakes do Canadian sellers make?

- **Keyword-stuffed titles.** Etsy suggests under 15 words and no repetition.
- **Fewer than 13 tags.** Each empty tag is a missed match.
- **Repeating attributes as tags.** Fill attributes, then use tags for other phrases.
- **Buying backlinks.** Etsy warns that buying link placements may hurt your Google rankings.

## How are Etsy sales taxed in Canada?

Etsy profit is business income reported on Form T2125. Etsy's [fees page](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy) lists listing, transaction and processing fees, which are business expenses.
