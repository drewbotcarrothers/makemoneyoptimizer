### What sits on each plan

- **Thinkific Basic:** unlimited courses, one community, up to five digital products, checkout with order bumps and coupons, abandoned-cart emails, custom domain. **Start** adds live cohorts and coaching, certificates, assignments, bundles, payment plans and memberships. **Grow** removes Thinkific branding and adds API access, invoicing and phone support.
- **Kajabi Basic:** courses, coaching, memberships, website, community, unlimited emails, landing pages and funnels, 2,500 contacts. **Growth** adds cohort courses, advanced automations, an affiliate program, 25,000 contacts and a discounted Kajabi Payments rate. **Pro** adds a branded app and 100,000 contacts. Extra contacts cost $125 a month per 25,000.
- **Podia Mover:** 50 products, 500 videos, 25 spaces, 100 email subscribers and a 5% transaction fee. **Shaker** removes the fee and raises limits to 150 products and 500 subscribers, adding affiliates, upsells, PayPal and Zapier. **Earthquaker** is unlimited products with 1,000 subscribers.

## What do transaction and processing fees add?

Two layers of fees apply to every sale:

1. **Platform fee.** Podia Mover takes 5%. Thinkific and Kajabi state no revenue share; Kajabi notes an extra processing fee if you use a third-party processor where Kajabi Payments is offered.
2. **Card processing.** Podia's FAQ cites 2.9% + 30¢ through Stripe or PayPal. Stripe's standard Canadian rate is 2.9% + C$0.30. Thinkific and Kajabi run their own payments products whose rates vary by country.

For a like-for-like comparison, the examples below apply 2.9% + C$0.30 to every platform. On a C$100 course that is **C$3.20** per sale, plus **C$5.00** on Podia Mover.

## What would each cost at three sales levels?

**Illustrative example.** Assumed: a C$100 course, all sales processed at 2.9% + C$0.30, annual billing, and the cheapest plan that fits. Sourced: plan prices and Podia's 5% fee. The heavy level moves Thinkific to Start for payment plans and keeps Podia on Shaker, which caps email at 500 subscribers.

| Level | Sales/month (revenue) | Thinkific | Kajabi | Podia |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 5 (C$500) | Basic $56.89 + $16.00 = **$72.89** | Basic $203.37 + $16.00 = **$219.37** | Mover $59.73 + $16.00 + $25.00 = **$100.73** |
| Medium | 30 (C$3,000) | Basic $56.89 + $96.00 = **$152.89** | Basic $203.37 + $96.00 = **$299.37** | Shaker $119.46 + $96.00 = **$215.46** |
| Heavy | 150 (C$15,000) | Start $116.62 + $480.00 = **$596.62** | Basic $203.37 + $480.00 = **$683.37** | Shaker $119.46 + $480.00 = **$599.46** |

Math: processing = sales × $3.20 (5 × 3.20 = 16; 30 × 3.20 = 96; 150 × 3.20 = 480). At the medium level, Podia Mover would have cost $59.73 + $96 + $150 = $305.73, so Shaker is cheaper. At the heavy level, Kajabi's gap narrows to about $87 a month, and it also replaces a separate email tool, which the other two only partly do.

### Break-even: when does Podia Shaker beat Mover?

The plan gap is $119.46 − $59.73 = $59.73 a month in CAD. Mover's 5% on a C$100 sale is $5. So $59.73 ÷ $5 ≈ 11.9: at 12 or more C$100 sales a month, Shaker costs less. For a C$50 product the break-even doubles to about 24 sales.

## Common mistakes when choosing a course platform

- **Paying for Kajabi before validating the course.** Pre-sell on a cheaper plan first; see [course funnel red flags](/side-hustles/course-funnel-red-flags-canada/).
- **Ignoring the 5% fee.** It looks small until sales pick up.
- **Forgetting the email tool.** Thinkific and Podia have limited email; budget for a separate tool if your list grows past Podia's caps.
- **Paying monthly by default.** Annual billing saves 25% on Thinkific and 20% on Kajabi.
- **Locking content in.** Keep source videos and worksheets in your own storage so you can migrate.

## How are course sales taxed in Canada?

Course revenue is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and platform subscriptions and processing fees are generally deductible; record USD charges in CAD at the rate on the payment date. Once your worldwide taxable sales pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, you generally must register for and charge GST/HST ([CRA guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). Sales to non-residents can be zero-rated under specific rules. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). This is general information, not tax advice.
