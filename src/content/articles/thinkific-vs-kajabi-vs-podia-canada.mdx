## Which course platform is cheapest for a first course?

Podia's Mover plan has the lowest sticker price at $42 a month billed yearly, but it adds a 5% transaction fee. Thinkific Basic is $40 a month billed yearly and Thinkific says sellers using its built-in Thinkific Payments (listed at 2.9% processing) avoid the extra third-party gateway fee. Kajabi's cheapest plan is $143 a month billed yearly.

### Illustrative example: when do Podia's 5% fees outgrow Shaker?

Assumed numbers, not a quote: compare Podia Mover and Shaker for a creator selling a $100 course.

- Mover vs Shaker price gap: $84 − $42 = $42 a month (yearly billing).
- Mover's 5% fee on one $100 sale: $5.
- Break-even: $42 ÷ $5 = 8.4, so at roughly 9 or more $100 sales a month, Shaker costs less.

Payment processor fees apply on both plans. The course price is an assumption for the arithmetic.

## What should Canadian course creators check before choosing?

- **Total cost, not the headline.** Add monthly price, platform fees and processor fees.
- **Currency.** If the platform bills in USD, your monthly cost in CAD moves with the exchange rate.
- **GST/HST on digital sales.** Once you pass the $30,000 small supplier threshold you generally need to register; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and the [CRA's registration page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html).
- **Migration.** Moving students later is work; pick a platform you can live with for a year.
- **Trial timing.** Build the course outline before you start a 14- or 30-day trial.
