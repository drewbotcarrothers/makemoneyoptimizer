## How should you choose lawn care tools in Canada?

Choose lawn care tools in Canada by reliability, lawn size, transport, and noise. A mower that starts on the first few pulls matters more than horsepower on small urban lots.

| Criterion | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reliability | Watch a used mower start and cut before you pay | A non-starter costs you the morning and the client |
| Lot size and gates | Deck width that fits the narrowest gate on your route | Many city backyards are reached through a side gate |
| Power type | Gas, corded, or battery. Battery runtime against your route | A battery that dies mid-lawn is a half-finished job |
| Transport | Can it be secured in your car, or walked between nearby lawns? | Loose machines in a car are a hazard |
| Guards and controls | [CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/landscaping/rotary.html) says shields, the rear drag shield, and discharge deflector should be in place and blade-stopping controls effective | Thrown objects are the main injury and damage risk |
| Noise | Local noise bylaw hours | Early-morning mowing can bring complaints |

## What does CCOHS say about using lawn care tools safely?

[CCOHS](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/landscaping/rotary.html) says to clear the yard of rocks and debris, keep people and pets away because a mower can throw objects in any direction, not to mow wet grass, and to mow across slopes with a push mower. It says to turn off the engine before fuelling, fill it when cool, restart at least 8 metres from where you refuelled, and disconnect the spark plug wire before tipping the mower or touching the blade.

It also says never to point the discharge chute at people, animals, buildings, windows, or glass doors, and to stop immediately and inspect the blade if it hits a hard object. Those rules shape the kit: guards in place, a stick for clearing clogs, and gloves for blade work.

## Should lawn care tools include a pesticide sprayer in Canada?

A pesticide sprayer should not be part of a starter lawn care kit in Canada. Ontario's [pesticides page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticides-home-lawns-and-gardens) says you cannot use certain pesticides, including 2,4-D, diazinon, and glyphosate, to kill weeds and insects on lawns, gardens, patios, and driveways, and only products whose active ingredients are on the allowable list may be used for cosmetic purposes. Ontario's [pesticide licences page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/pesticide-licences-and-permits) also says that, unless exempt, you need an exterminator licence to apply pesticides commercially on property you do not own. Other provinces and cities have their own rules. Confirm locally before offering any treatment.

## What pre-route checklist can you copy?

Built from the [CCOHS rotary mower guidance](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/safety_haz/landscaping/rotary.html):

```text
BEFORE EACH LAWN
[ ] Walk the lawn: rocks, sticks, toys, dog waste picked up
[ ] People and pets out of the area
[ ] Grass dry enough to cut? If wet, reschedule
[ ] Guards, rear drag shield, discharge deflector in place
[ ] Blade-stop control works
[ ] Gas: engine off and cool before fuelling; restart 8 m away
[ ] Battery: charge level ___ %, spare battery packed
[ ] Hearing + eye protection on; safety-toe boots
[ ] Slopes: push mower ACROSS the slope
[ ] Blade hit something hard? Stop, disconnect, inspect
AFTER: clippings blown/swept off walks, gate latched, photo sent
```

## Which tax rules apply to lawn care tools in Canada?

Lawn care income is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people make the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. Fuel, line, and blades are ordinary expenses. A tool that costs less than $500, such as the $379 mower or the trimmer, falls in Class 12 of the CRA's [capital cost allowance classes](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance/classes-depreciable-property.html) and is generally fully deductible in the year of purchase; a $899.99 mower is Class 8, claimed at 20% a year as [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html). If you drive between lawns, the CRA's [motor vehicle records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses/motor-vehicle-records.html) page says to log the date, destination, purpose, and kilometres for each business trip.

GST/HST registration applies once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a quarter or over four consecutive quarters. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
