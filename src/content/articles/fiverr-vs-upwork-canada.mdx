Fiverr and Upwork both let Canadians freelance for clients worldwide, but they charge differently. Fiverr keeps a flat 20%, so you earn 80% of each order including extras and tips, and buyers come to your listed "gigs". Upwork charges a freelancer service fee of 0% to 15% per contract, shown before you propose and locked for that contract, and you pay for Connects (0.15 USD each) to send proposals unless your free monthly allotment covers it. Both price in U.S. dollars. Pick Fiverr if you can package a repeatable service at a fixed price; pick Upwork if you sell custom, hourly, or longer projects and are comfortable writing proposals.

## How do Fiverr and Upwork fees compare for Canadian freelancers?

Fiverr takes 20% of every order, while Upwork's fee varies from 0% to 15% by contract and adds the cost of Connects. Amounts are in USD from each company's help and pricing pages, rechecked October 9, 2026, and converted at the Bank of Canada [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4240 CAD per USD for October 8, 2026.

| Factor | Fiverr | Upwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fee on earnings | 20%; you earn 80% of the purchase amount, including extras and tips ([earnings page](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/9234443621137-Your-earnings-page)) | 0% to 15% per contract, shown before you propose and locked ([service fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee)) |

| Paid plan | Not required | Freelancer Plus at 19.99 USD (about $28.47 CAD) a month with 100 Connects |
| Direct contracts | Not applicable | 5% on Basic; 0% on Freelancer Plus |
| Faster payout | Early Payout fee of 1% | Instant Pay is for U.S. freelancers only (2.00 USD) |
| Withdrawal fees | PayPal 0 USD (1 USD minimum); bank transfer via Payoneer 1 USD (about $1.42 CAD, 20 USD minimum); Payoneer account 3 USD (about $4.27 CAD, 10 USD minimum); up to 5,000 USD per withdrawal ([withdrawals](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010530058-Withdrawing-your-earnings-managing-payout-methods)) | Direct to Local Bank in CAD: 0.99 USD (about $1.41 CAD) per withdrawal, arriving within four business days ([fees and timing](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060578-What-are-the-fees-limits-and-timing-of-Direct-to-Local-Bank-payments)); U.S. dollar wire 50 USD; PayPal and Payoneer also offered ([payout methods](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060918-How-to-get-paid-on-Upwork)) |
| Canadian specifics | Tax compliance forms required for Canadian freelancers | Canada (CAD) is a supported Direct to Local Bank currency |

## How do Fiverr and Upwork differ in how you find work?

On Fiverr, buyers search and order the packages you list, so the work is in designing gigs and earning reviews. On Upwork, you mostly browse job posts and send proposals, so the work is in choosing jobs and writing pitches, and each pitch spends Connects.

| Factor | Fiverr | Upwork |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | You list fixed packages; buyers order | Clients post jobs; you propose, or clients invite you |
| Pricing | Fixed price per package, plus extras | Fixed price or hourly |
| Best for | Repeatable, well-defined tasks | Custom, larger, or ongoing projects |
| Effort before income | Building gig pages and reviews | Writing proposals and spending Connects |

## How much do you keep on a project on Fiverr versus Upwork?

On a single project, you keep 80% on Fiverr and 85% to 100% on Upwork before Connects. The labelled example shows the arithmetic.

### Illustrative example

Assume a 500 USD project. The price and the Upwork fee rate are assumptions; Upwork shows your actual rate before you propose.

- **Fiverr:** 20% × 500 USD = 100 USD fee, so you keep 400 USD (about $569.60 CAD).
- **Upwork at a 10% contract fee:** you keep 450 USD (about $640.80 CAD).
- **Upwork at a 15% contract fee:** you keep 425 USD (about $605.20 CAD).
- **Upwork Connects:** if you buy 60 Connects to send proposals before landing the project, 60 × 0.15 USD = 9 USD (about $12.82 CAD).

At a 15% fee with 9 USD of Connects, Upwork leaves 416 USD, still a little more than Fiverr's 400 USD, but proposals that do not win cost Connects and time. Your bank or payout provider may also apply its own USD-to-CAD conversion rate.

### Illustrative example: is Freelancer Plus worth 19.99 USD a month?

**Assumed inputs:** you would otherwise buy 100 Connects a month and bill some clients you found yourself through direct contracts. **Sourced inputs:** Connects at 0.15 USD, Plus at 19.99 USD with 100 Connects, and direct-contract fees of 5% on Basic and 0% on Plus.

- 100 Connects bought on Basic: 100 × 0.15 = **15.00 USD**
- The remaining 4.99 USD of the Plus fee is covered once direct-contract billing saves that much: 4.99 ÷ 5% = **99.80 USD** of direct-contract earnings a month
- If you send few proposals and have no direct contracts, Basic plus occasional Connects is cheaper

### What does a month of 2,000 USD in billings look like?

**Assumed inputs:** 2,000 USD billed in a month, one withdrawal, 60 Connects bought on Upwork Basic.

| | Fiverr | Upwork at 10% | Upwork at 15% |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Platform fee | 400.00 USD | 200.00 USD | 300.00 USD |
| Connects | 0 | 9.00 USD | 9.00 USD |
| Withdrawal | 1.00 USD (bank via Payoneer) | 0.99 USD (Direct to Local Bank) | 0.99 USD |
| **You keep** | **1,599.00 USD (about $2,276.98 CAD)** | **1,790.01 USD (about $2,548.97 CAD)** | **1,690.01 USD (about $2,406.57 CAD)** |

The gap is the fee rate, not the extras: on 2,000 USD, Fiverr's 20% costs 100 to 200 USD more than Upwork's 10% to 15%. Your bank or payout provider may apply its own USD-to-CAD conversion rate, which is why the CAD figures use the Bank of Canada rate.

## Who should pick Fiverr or Upwork in Canada?

Pick Fiverr for packaged services and Upwork for custom projects. This is a decision rule from the fee and work structures, not a ranking.

| If you… | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sell a repeatable task, such as a logo, a resume edit, or a short video edit | Fiverr | Buyers order fixed packages |
| Prefer not to write proposals | Fiverr | No Connects or pitching |
| Sell hourly or multi-week work | Upwork | Hourly contracts and long engagements |
| Bring your own clients to a platform for payments | Upwork | Direct contracts are 0% on Freelancer Plus and 5% on Basic |
| Want to test both | Both | Fiverr gigs and Upwork proposals can run side by side |

## What mistakes do Canadian freelancers make on Fiverr and Upwork?

Canadian freelancers most often price in CAD by accident, ignore the cost of Connects, or forget the platforms report income.

- **Forgetting prices are in USD.** Set prices in USD and convert your target hourly rate first.
- **Burning Connects on poor-fit jobs.** Each proposal spends paid Connects.
- **Pricing without the fee.** On Fiverr, a 100 USD gig pays 80 USD.
- **Skipping the tax forms.** Fiverr requires tax compliance forms for Canadian freelancers.
- **Losing track of USD income.** Record the CAD value of each payout for your tax return.

## What could change these Fiverr and Upwork numbers?

Your Upwork fee is set per contract between 0% and 15%, so the same job can cost 0 USD or 75 USD on a 500 USD project; check the rate shown on each proposal. Both platforms pay in USD unless you withdraw to a CAD bank account, so the exchange rate on the day you withdraw moves your Canadian income. Fees were read from the companies' pages on October 9, 2026, except Fiverr's withdrawal fees, which were read on October 3, 2026. This is not tax or legal advice.

## Which tax rules apply to Fiverr and Upwork freelancers in Canada?

Freelance income from Fiverr or Upwork is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), converted to Canadian dollars, and the platforms' fees and Connects are generally deductible business expenses. Platforms can report seller and freelancer earnings to the CRA, as covered in [tax on reselling and gig apps](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/). Invoicing and expense tracking software such as <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can help, and [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers saving for the bill. Services to clients outside Canada may be treated differently for GST/HST; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
