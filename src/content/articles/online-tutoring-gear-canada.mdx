## What should an online tutor buy first?

Buy a headset first, then a pen tablet if you teach anything with equations or diagrams, then a better webcam. Students forgive a soft picture but not echoing audio, and writing a math step with a mouse is slow.

| Priority | Why | Budget pick | Upgrade pick |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Headset | Clear voice, no echo for the student | Logitech H390, $24.99 | Poly Blackwire 3320, $63.99 |
| 2. Pen tablet | Handwriting on a whiteboard app | Wacom Intuos Small, $39.97 | Wacom One Small Bluetooth, $124.76 |
| 3. Webcam | Sharper image than a laptop camera | Logitech Brio 101, $59.99 | Logitech C920x, $99.99 |
| 4. Stand and light | Eye-level camera and an even face light | Aluminum laptop stand, $19.99 | Elgato Key Light Neo, $99.99 |

## Which webcam should a tutor buy?

A 1080p webcam with automatic light correction is enough for tutoring. Pay more only for sharper low-light images or auto framing if you move between a desk and a whiteboard.

## What should Canadian tutors check before buying?

- **Your laptop's ports.** Several picks above are USB-A; many newer laptops only have USB-C. Choose the H391 or Brio 300 or budget for an adapter.
- **Chromebooks.** If students or you use a Chromebook, pick a tablet whose listing names Chromebook support, such as the Wacom Intuos Small or Huion H640P.
- **Evening light.** Winter lessons after school run past sunset for much of the year, so a front light matters more than a better webcam.
- **Third-party sellers.** Some listings are third-party offers; check the seller and return terms on the product page.

## How do taxes treat tutoring gear?

Equipment you use for tutoring is generally deductible as a [business expense](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses.html) or through capital cost allowance. CRA's [classes of depreciable property](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance/classes-depreciable-property.html) page excludes electronic data processing equipment from Class 12, so computer peripherals usually follow the computer-equipment rules; claim only the business-use share of anything you also use personally. See [home office expenses](/side-hustles/home-office-expenses-side-hustle-canada/). This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
