## Which is cheaper for a new podcaster?

Both have free starting points. Descript's Hobbyist plan is $16 a month billed yearly, and Riverside's Pro plan is billed $288 a year, which works out to $24 a month. If you only need one, choose by your bottleneck: guest audio quality (Riverside) or editing time (Descript).

### Illustrative example: a freelance podcast editor's software cost

Assumed numbers, not a quote: an editor charges $60 per episode and edits 12 episodes a month.

- Monthly revenue: 12 × $60 = $720.
- Descript Creator at $24 a month billed yearly is about 3% of that revenue.
- Ask clients to record in their own Riverside account so the recording cost stays with them.

Rates are assumptions; see [podcast editing](/side-hustles/podcast-editing-side-hustle-canada/) for how Canadian editors price.

## What mistakes do podcasters make with these tools?

- **Recording on a laptop mic.** Software cannot fully fix bad source audio.
- **Paying for both before you need both.** Start with the tool for your bottleneck.
- **Exceeding media hours.** Check monthly caps against your volume.
- **No backup.** Download raw tracks after every session.
- **Forgetting the client's ownership.** Agree who owns raw files and project files.
