- **Short answer:** Riverside is built around recording remote guests on separate high-quality tracks; Descript is built around editing audio and video by editing a transcript. Many shows record in Riverside and edit in Descript, but each can now do both.
- **Descript (October 14, 2026):** Free $0 (1 media hour a month, 720p export). Hobbyist $16 per person a month billed annually ($24 monthly), Creator $24 ($35), Business $50 ($65).

### What the limits actually mean

- **Descript media hours** count uploaded and recorded media whether or not you transcribe it. **AI credits** cover Underlord, Studio Sound, Green Screen, Eye Contact and AI-generated media. Creator and Business can buy top-ups.
- **Descript recording** (its own remote recorder): 2 hours on Free, then 5, 15 and 25 hours a month on Hobbyist, Creator and Business.
- **Riverside recording** is unlimited. The cap is on downloading each participant's separate studio-quality tracks: 15 hours a month on Pro, 20 on Grow and 25 on Webinar. Past the cap you keep access but can't download new separate tracks until the next cycle.
- **Riverside AI credits:** Pro starts with 2,000 for features such as AI translation and AI B-roll. Magic Clips, Magic Audio and the AI co-creator don't use credits.

## What would each cost at three usage levels?

**Illustrative example.** Assumed: hours of raw media per month and the plan choices shown. Sourced: plan prices and limits above (annual billing, CAD at 1.4222).

| Usage level | Raw media per month | Lowest-cost Descript fit | Lowest-cost Riverside fit | Both together |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light: one solo episode | 1 hour | Free, C$0 (720p export) | Pro, ≈ C$34.13 | Not needed |
| Medium: weekly interview show | 4 × 1.5 h = 6 hours | Hobbyist, ≈ C$22.76 | Pro, ≈ C$34.13 (6 h of the 15 h tracks) | ≈ C$56.89 |
| Heavy: editor with 3 client shows | 3 × 6 h = 18 hours | Creator, ≈ C$34.13 (30 h) | Grow, ≈ C$48.35 (20 h tracks) | Clients usually record on their own plan |

Math: episodes × hours = media hours, then pick the cheapest plan whose cap is at or above it. 4 × 1.5 = 6 hours fits Hobbyist's 10; 3 × 6 = 18 fits Creator's 30.

### A record-to-publish workflow

```text
1. Send guest the Riverside studio link and a mic/headphone checklist.
2. Record (host + guest on separate local tracks).
3. Download separate tracks (counts toward the monthly download cap).
4. Import to Descript; run Studio Sound and Remove Filler Words.
5. Edit by transcript; cut tangents; add intro/outro.
6. Export audio + 1080p video; make 3 short clips.
7. Publish; send the client a time-stamped change list.
```

## What mistakes do editors make with Descript and Riverside?

- **Paying for both on day one.** One tool covers a solo show; add the second only when guests or clients require it.
- **Forgetting the track-download cap.** Riverside records unlimited hours but caps separate-track downloads.
- **Burning media hours on raw dumps.** Descript counts uploads even if you never transcribe them; trim before import.
- **Recording guests on laptop mics.** Software can't fully fix room echo; send a gear checklist.
- **Billing monthly for a year-round show.** Annual billing is cheaper on both.
