Wealthsimple Tax, TurboTax and H&R Block are the three tax programs Canadian side hustlers ask about most, and any of them can handle a return that includes a [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) for business or professional income. The real differences are price, how much guidance you get on expenses, and whether you can reach a tax expert. Software does not change what you owe; your records do.

## Do side hustlers need a paid edition?

Not always. Wealthsimple's Basic plan is pay-what-you-want, and its FAQ says there are no restrictions on what you can file, which includes self-employment income. TurboTax Free and H&R Block Free are aimed at simpler returns, so business income generally pushes you to their self-employed tiers. Paid tiers add things like audit protection, more returns per account, and access to an expert.

## What should side hustlers check before filing?

Check three things: your T2125 totals, GST/HST status, and any instalments. If your taxable sales passed the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), you also file GST/HST returns, which these personal tax programs do not do for you; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/). Platform income from apps and marketplaces is covered in [tax on reselling and gig apps](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).

## What mistakes do side hustlers make with tax software?

- **Leaving T2125 blank and adding income as "other income."** Business income belongs on T2125 with its expenses.
- **Claiming personal costs as business expenses.** Only the business-use share counts.
- **Forgetting CPP on self-employment earnings.** The software calculates it, but plan for the cash.
- **Paying for an upgrade you don't need.** Expert help is useful if you have questions, not by default.
- **Missing the June 15 self-employed filing date versus the April 30 payment date.** Any balance owing is still due April 30.

## Which tax rules apply to filing with side hustle income?

Self-employed individuals and their spouses generally have until June 15 to file, but any balance owing is due April 30 ([CRA filing dates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/important-dates-individuals/filing-dates-tax-return.html)). Keep records for six years from the end of the tax year they relate to ([CRA record retention](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/keeping-records/where-keep-your-records-long-request-permission-destroy-them-early.html)). This is not tax advice; for anything unusual, ask a professional.
