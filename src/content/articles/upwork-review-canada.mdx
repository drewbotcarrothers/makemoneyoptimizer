Upwork works for Canadian freelancers, and it suits people who sell custom, hourly, or longer projects and are willing to write proposals. ## Is Upwork available to freelancers in Canada?

Yes. Canadians can join as freelancers or agencies, and Upwork has Canada-specific help pages for indirect tax and the CRA's platform reporting rules. Joining is free: Upwork's [pricing breakdown](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free) says the Basic plan is free and may include 10 Connects a month, which you spend to submit proposals. Canadians who are not US persons complete Form W-8BEN in their tax information ([Upwork W-8BEN](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211063938-How-to-complete-Form-W-8BEN-or-W-8BEN-E)).

## What fees does Upwork charge Canadian freelancers?

The main cost is the freelancer service fee, plus Connects if you send many proposals, plus sales tax on those fees. As listed in October 2026:

| Cost | Amount (USD) | About CAD | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Freelancer service fee | 0% to 15% per contract | Same percentage | Shown on the offer or proposal; fixed once the contract begins ([Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee)) |
| Connects beyond the free allotment | 0.15 each | $0.21 each | Basic plan may include 10 a month ([Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free)) |
| Freelancer Plus (optional) | 19.99 a month | $28.48 a month | Includes 100 Connects a month |
| Direct to Local Bank withdrawal | 0.99 per withdrawal | $1.41 | Plus any incoming fee your bank charges ([Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060578-What-are-the-fees-limits-and-timing-of-Direct-to-Local-Bank-payments)) |
| GST/HST, PST or QST on Upwork's fees | 5% to 15% of the fee, by province | Same | Not charged on your earnings ([Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4401838411283-How-indirect-taxes-work-for-freelancers-in-Canada)) |

Upwork says the service fee is refunded if money is returned to your client, and that freelancers on Direct Contracts with clients they bring to Upwork pay a reduced fee.

## How does Upwork charge GST/HST to Canadians?

Upwork collects Canadian sales tax on freelancer service fees, Connects purchases, and memberships, not on what clients pay you. Its [Canada indirect tax page](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4401838411283-How-indirect-taxes-work-for-freelancers-in-Canada) lists the rates it applies: 5% GST in Alberta and the territories, 13% HST in Ontario, 15% HST in the Atlantic provinces, GST plus PST in B.C. (12%), Manitoba (12%) and Saskatchewan (11%), and GST plus QST in Quebec (14.975%).

If you enter a valid GST/HST or QST number, Upwork stops collecting those taxes, though it still collects PST in B.C., Saskatchewan and Manitoba. Upwork notes that a business number on its own does not qualify, and that charging GST/HST to your own clients is your responsibility. Whether you need to register is covered in [GST/HST registration for side hustles](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do Upwork payouts work in Canadian dollars?

Client payments and Upwork's fees are in USD. You withdraw to a payout method; Direct to Local Bank costs 0.99 USD per withdrawal, should arrive within four business days, and has a minimum of 5 USD or more depending on location. Upwork says sending funds in your local currency may reduce or eliminate incoming bank fees ([Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211060578-What-are-the-fees-limits-and-timing-of-Direct-to-Local-Bank-payments)). The exchange rate on conversion is set by the payment provider, not the Bank of Canada, so compare the CAD amount you actually receive.

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers: an Ontario freelancer, not registered for GST/HST, finishes a 500 USD fixed-price contract with an assumed 10% service fee, then withdraws to a Canadian bank.

- Service fee: 500 × 10% = 50 USD
- HST on the fee: 50 × 13% = 6.50 USD
- Net in Upwork: 500 − 50 − 6.50 = 443.50 USD
- Withdrawal fee: 443.50 − 0.99 = 442.51 USD
- At the Bank of Canada reference rate: 442.51 × 1.4246 ≈ $630.40 CAD, before any spread your bank or the provider applies

## What are the pros and cons of Upwork for Canadians?

Upwork's strengths are client volume and payment protection; its weaknesses are proposal costs and fee variability.

**Pros**
- Large pool of clients posting custom projects, including long-term hourly work.
- Fee shown up front and locked for each contract.
- Payment protection for hourly and fixed-price contracts, per Upwork's help centre.
- Direct Contracts let you bill clients you found yourself at a reduced fee.

**Cons**
- Proposals cost Connects once the free allotment runs out.
- The fee varies by contract (0% to 15%), so it is harder to plan than a flat rate.
- Sales tax on fees adds 5% to 15% on top for Canadians who are not registered.
- USD earnings mean exchange-rate swings and conversion spreads.
- Upwork can restrict withdrawals if your Canadian tax information is incomplete.

## Who is Upwork best for in Canada?

Upwork suits Canadians who already have a marketable skill, can write a clear proposal, and want custom or ongoing work rather than packaged gigs. Writers, VAs, developers, designers, video editors, and bookkeepers fit well. It suits beginners less well if they have no samples, because proposals compete on proof. ## What mistakes do Canadians make on Upwork?

- **Quoting a rate without the fee.** Upwork's own example shows charging 22.22 USD an hour to take home 20 USD at a 10% fee. Add the sales tax on the fee if you are not registered.
- **Burning Connects on vague job posts.** Choose posts with clear scope and a verified payment method.
- **Leaving tax information incomplete.** Upwork says missing or invalid tax information can block withdrawals.
- **Ignoring currency.** Price in USD with the CAD conversion in mind.
- **Taking work off-platform with an Upwork client.** Read Upwork's terms first; this review does not cover them in detail.

## How is Upwork income taxed in Canada?

Upwork income is self-employment income, reported in Canadian dollars on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Upwork's service fees, Connects, and memberships are business expenses. Under Canada's [reporting rules for digital platform operators](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html), Upwork says it reports all payments to Canadian freelancers to the CRA annually, with no minimum, and its RDPO form lists totals in USD ([Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/35984193505939-How-to-understand-and-comply-with-Canada-s-RDPO-on-Upwork)), so convert to CAD when you file.
