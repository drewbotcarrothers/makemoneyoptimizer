A photo booth side hustle in Canada rents a staffed camera, printer and backdrop to holiday parties, weddings and corporate events by the hour. Canadian booth companies that publish prices charge about $400 to $800 for two hours, and the core gear (a dye-sublimation printer, its media, a camera and booth software) can be bought for roughly $2,200 at the Canadian prices below. Every price in this guide was read on the company's or retailer's own page on October 19, 2026.

<QuickAnswer>

- **What booths charge (2 hours):** $399.97 (Photo Booth Of Toronto), $475 (Little Black Box, Vancouver), $550 (The Photo Booth Guy, Toronto), $610 (Twisted, Calgary), $775 (Analog Squad, Calgary).
- **December is a peak month:** Winnie Chan Photobooth in Calgary sets a $520 minimum on Friday to Sunday bookings in August and December.
- **Pro printer:** Continent-Wide in Toronto lists the DNP DS620A at $1,419, and a 4x6 media kit (2 rolls, 800 prints) at $179, about 22 cents a 4x6 print.
- **Budget printer:** Canon Canada lists the SELPHY CP1500 at $179.99 (sale) and its KP-108IN set (108 prints) at $54.99, about 51 cents a print.
- **Software:** LumaBooth for Windows (dslrBooth) is US$17 a month billed annually or US$49.99 month to month.

</QuickAnswer>

### Illustrative example: how many prints does one busy hour use?

Inputs: 40 sessions an hour is Fun Kliks' published average; the 4x6 media cost is the DNP pack price in the gear table below ($179 for 800 prints, about $0.22 a print). The number of busy hours is assumed.

| Scenario (assumed) | Sessions | Prints (1 per session) | Media cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2-hour party, 50% busy | 40 | 40 | about $9 |
| 3-hour party, 75% busy | 90 | 90 | about $20 |

Duplicate prints double media use, which is why Fun Kliks charges $40 an hour for them. If you offer LIT-style "one print per person", a group of four uses four prints per session, so price that option like duplicates.

## How does a photo booth side hustle make money?

A photo booth side hustle makes money from booth hours, with prints, extra hours, idle time and custom templates sold as add-ons. The add-ons in the published packages above give a ready menu:

- **Extra hour:** $90 (Winnie Chan) to $185 (Twisted, Analog Squad).
- **Unlimited prints:** $60 an hour (GTA Photo Booths) or $85 an hour (Analog Squad).
- **Idle time** between sessions, such as during dinner and speeches: $50 an hour at Analog Squad.
- **Glam black-and-white lighting:** $75 at GTA Photo Booths.
- **Custom template:** $30 for each extra layout at Winnie Chan.

December adds two things to that list: corporate holiday parties that book weeks ahead, and weekend minimums. Winnie Chan's page is the clearest public example of a company pricing December weekends higher.

## Which events should a holiday photo booth target?

A holiday photo booth should target events where people dress up, arrive in groups and stay for at least two hours. These 15 event types fit that description, each with the reason it suits a one-booth operator:

1. **Office holiday parties.** Booked by one organiser, often weeks ahead, and paid by invoice.
2. **Restaurant and bar holiday nights.** The venue already has a crowd and wants a reason to post.
3. **Community centre family nights.** Short bookings, many kids, simple props.
4. **School winter concerts and dances.** Parent councils book by committee; quote early.
5. **Church and cultural association holiday events.** Recurring every year once you do one well.
6. **Holiday markets.** A branded booth for the market's social channels; see the [holiday market vendor guide](/side-hustles/holiday-market-vendor-guide-canada/) for how markets run.
7. **New Year's Eve parties.** The highest-demand single night; take a deposit.
8. **Condo and apartment building socials.** The property manager books, residents use it.
9. **Sports team and league banquets.** Group photos with the trophy are the product.
10. **Retail store openings and holiday shopping nights.** Branded prints with the store logo.
11. **Wedding receptions in December.** Longer bookings; the wedding page above is the comparison.
12. **Milestone birthdays during the holidays.** Family members travel home, so the guest list is big.
13. **Real estate and mortgage client appreciation events.** Branded keepsakes are the point.
14. **Charity galas and fundraisers.** Ask whether the booth can be a sponsored line item.
15. **Ugly sweater parties.** Props and a simple template do most of the work.

## Illustrative example: what does one holiday booth cost, and how many events pay it back?

This is an illustrative example. The printer, media, camera and software prices are sourced from the pages above. The event price, print count, and the stand, lighting and backdrop budget are assumptions you replace with your own numbers.

**Kit cost (sourced unless marked):**

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| DNP DS620A | $1,419.00 |
| 4x6 media kit (800 prints) | $179.00 |
| Canon EOS R100 kit | $629.99 |
| Stand, lights, backdrop, props (assumed) | $400.00 |
| **Total before tax** | **$2,627.99** |
| LumaBooth for Windows, 12 months billed annually | US$17 × 12 = US$204 (in US dollars; your card converts it) |

**Per event (assumed):** a 3-hour office holiday party at $550, below the 3-hour prices of $625 (Little Black Box) to $785 (Twisted) above because you are new. Guests print 300 strips, which is 150 4x6 sheets cut in two.

- Media: 150 × ($179 ÷ 800) = 150 × $0.224 = $33.60.
- Assumed travel and parking: $30.
- Left after media and travel: $550 − $33.60 − $30 = $486.40.
- Events to cover the kit: $2,627.99 ÷ $486.40 = 5.4, so the sixth event is the first one that adds to profit, before software, insurance and your own time.

| Event price (assumed) | Left per event | Events to cover $2,627.99 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $400 (low) | $336.40 | 7.8, so 8 events |
| $550 (middle) | $486.40 | 5.4, so 6 events |
| $750 (high) | $686.40 | 3.8, so 4 events |

Hours matter too. A 3-hour booking is closer to five or six hours of your evening once you add an assumed hour of setup, an hour of teardown and the drive. At $486.40 for six hours, that is about $81 an hour before software, insurance and tax, and only after the kit is paid off.

The budget printer changes the math. With a SELPHY CP1500 at $179.99 the kit drops by $1,239.01, but each print costs about $0.51 and the printer is a consumer model, so sell it as a digital booth with limited prints rather than as unlimited strips.

## What should you check before the first booking?

Before the first booking, check what the venue requires, what your insurance covers, and how you will power and secure the gear. Use this checklist with each venue contact:

```
Venue checklist
[ ] Load-in time, elevator or stairs, parking
[ ] Power outlet within reach of the booth (and who supplies the extension cord)
[ ] Wi-Fi for digital sharing, or plan to work offline
[ ] Floor space: about the size of your backdrop plus a line for guests
[ ] Does the venue require proof of liability insurance? Ask for it in writing
[ ] Start and end of booth hours, and any idle time during speeches
[ ] Who approves the print template (logo, date, event name)
[ ] Deposit received, balance due date, cancellation terms
```

A written agreement that states hours, idle time, the deposit and what happens if the event is cancelled saves the December argument. The [freelance contract guide](/side-hustles/freelance-contract-canada/) covers the clauses in plain language.

## What does a 30-day plan look like for a holiday booth?

A 30-day plan for a holiday booth buys the gear in the first week, runs two free or discounted test events, and spends the rest of the month booking December dates. Start in late October or early November, because December weekends fill first.

| Days | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 to 5 | Buy the printer, media and software; set up the camera and print a test template at home |
| 6 to 10 | Run the booth at a friend's party or a community event for free; time setup and teardown |
| 11 to 14 | Build two templates (corporate logo, family holiday) and a price sheet with 2, 3 and 4 hours |
| 15 to 20 | Message 20 local venues and office managers with the pitch below and a few sample prints |
| 21 to 25 | Follow up once; offer one weekday discount to fill a slow night |
| 26 to 30 | Confirm bookings in writing, collect deposits, and order a second media kit if bookings justify it |

### A pitch you can copy

```
Subject: Photo booth for your holiday party

Hi [name],

I run a small photo booth in [city] and I'm booking December holiday parties.
A 3-hour booking is $[price] and includes an attendant, instant 2x6 prints
with your logo and date, props, and a backdrop. Extra hours are $[price].

I can send sample prints from a recent event. Do you have a date set yet?

[Your name], [phone]
```

## Which tax rules apply to a photo booth side hustle in Canada?

Photo booth income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), with the gear claimed through capital cost allowance and media, software and travel as expenses. GST/HST registration is required once your taxable sales pass $30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, under the CRA's [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) rules. The published prices above show both approaches: SelfieBooth quotes "+ HST" and Twisted asks clients to add 5% GST. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. More detail is in [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
