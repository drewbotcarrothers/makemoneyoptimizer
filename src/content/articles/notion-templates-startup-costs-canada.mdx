## How much does it cost to start selling Notion templates in Canada?

Selling Notion templates in Canada costs $0 on Notion Free with a per-sale checkout, $51.97 with a USB mic for the setup video, $405.52 adding a year of Canva Pro and Notion Plus, or $1,296.79 with a yearly Shopify Basic store, a headset for paid setup calls and a starting liability policy. Goods and software include 13% Ontario HST. Insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate.

| --- | --- | --- |
| $0 start | Notion Free, Canva Free, laptop mic, Gumroad direct link or Notion Marketplace (fees per sale only) | $0 |
| Lean | FIFINE K669B USB condenser mic ($45.99) | $51.97 with HST |
| Standard | Lean + Canva Pro for a year ($142) + Notion Plus for a year (US$120, about C$170.88) | $405.52 with HST |
| Pro | Standard + Shopify Basic billed yearly ($37/month, $444) + Logitech H390 headset ($35.00) + professional liability at Zensurance's starting freelancer estimate (about $350/year) | $1,296.79 |

Notion's price is converted at the Bank of Canada's rate of 1.4240 on October 8, 2026, from its [daily exchange rates](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) table. Notion and Gumroad may convert at their own checkout.

The arithmetic: lean is $45.99 × 1.13 = $51.97. Standard is ($45.99 + $142 + $170.88) × 1.13 = $358.87 × 1.13 = $405.52. Pro adds ($444 + $35.00) × 1.13 = $541.27, plus $350, so $405.52 + $541.27 + $350 = $1,296.79.

## Who should use a Notion template startup budget in Canada?

A Notion template startup budget in Canada fits people who already use the workflow and can explain the duplicate step without a call. The $0 column fits one file. The lean kit fits someone whose laptop mic makes the setup video hard to follow. The standard kit fits a seller with several templates, a consistent cover style and features that need Plus. The pro kit fits someone with a catalogue, an audience that would buy from their own store, and paid setup sessions.

Selling templates is a poor fit if you have never used the system, if you dislike support email, or if rent depends on the first upload.

## Do you need insurance or a licence for Notion templates in Canada?

There's no licence to sell digital templates in Canada. Search your city for a home-based business licence if you present yourself as a local business.

**Insurance.** A file sale with a clear refund rule is a small risk. Paid setup work, where you get access to a client's workspace and build their system, is professional work. Zensurance's [freelancer insurance page](https://www.zensurance.com/freelancer-insurance) lists consultants among the freelancers it covers and says freelancer insurance generally starts from $350 to $500 a year for professional liability with a $1 million limit. That's an estimate, not a quote, read on October 9, 2026. It sits in the pro kit only because the pro kit includes setup calls. This is not insurance or legal advice.

**Licences inside the file.** Icons, images and fonts in a template you sell to many buyers need a licence that allows resale or redistribution. Client-use rights are not resale rights.

## What are the ongoing costs?

- **Per-sale fees.** 10% + US$0.50 on Gumroad direct, 30% on Discover, 8% + US$0.40 on Notion Marketplace. A month with no sale is $0 of fees.
- **Software you kept.** Notion Plus about C$14.24 a month, Canva Pro $142 a year, Shopify $37 to $49 a month.
- **Refunds.** A buyer who cannot duplicate the file is a cost you can prevent with a clearer setup page. Notion Marketplace refunds remove the template from the buyer's workspace.
- **Updates.** When Notion changes a feature you used, the file needs a pass. That hour is part of the price.
- **Support hours.** A file that generates an hour of email per sale is priced too thin, or the page is unclear.

Keep payouts where you can see the fee that was taken. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can hold template income separately until you know what the platform paid and what you still owe in tax.

## Where can Notion template startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Ship on Notion Free.** Plus is for a template that needs Plus features, not for you.
- **Use a per-sale checkout.** Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Payhip's free plan and Notion Marketplace all charge $0 a month.

## Where should Notion template spending not be cut in Canada?

Do not skip the setup page, a test duplicate in a second account, or a refund rule you can explain. A pretty cover on a file only you can operate is a personal note. The buyer paid for the saved time.

Do not promise Notion features your plan cannot hand off. Do not film someone else's client data in the demo. Do not treat a merchant-of-record sentence as a reason to skip your own income records.

If you also install the system on a client's workspace for a fee, that is hourly work with access you must be able to end. Price it separately, and that's when insurance belongs in the budget.

## Which tax rules apply to a Notion template budget in Canada?

Notion template income in Canada is income to record. When you are carrying on the activity for profit, self-employment income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. A Notion subscription and platform fees are generally current expenses. A computer that lasts for years is usually [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion. GST/HST on your own taxable supplies uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Digital sales can also sit under the CRA's [digital-economy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) pages when a platform is involved. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Gumroad's pricing page says it handles sales-tax collection as merchant of record, and Notion says the same for Marketplace. That is each platform's description of its role. It does not, by itself, tell you whether your supplies count toward the small-supplier test. Keep payout reports. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a quarter and over four consecutive quarters. Add other side hustles.

If you are registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) to the extent of commercial use, if the receipt shows the tax. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST yourself and do not claim the credit. Confirm how a platform's collection interacts with your registration before you add tax a second time.

Once you must charge, use the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Quebec charges GST and QST. Confirm with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).
