A holiday Etsy or craft-market shop in Canada is a short run of goods you make or finish in time for buyers who need them before Christmas, sold online with a ship-by date or in person at a market table. The work suits someone who can finish a small batch in October, who will look up Canada Post's own tools instead of a blog's cutoff list, and who will read Etsy's fee page without treating a screenshot as permanent.

Digital files skip the mail sack. That path is [printables](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/). Used goods are [reselling](/side-hustles/reselling-side-hustle-canada/). A booth that wraps other people's gifts, rather than selling yours, is [holiday gift wrapping](/side-hustles/holiday-gift-wrapping-side-hustle-canada/).

## How does a holiday shop work in Canada?

A holiday shop in Canada sells a finished object with a date you can keep. Online, the date is a ship-by promise tied to Canada Post's calendar and the delivery-standards tool. At a market, the date is the morning the organizer assigned you a table. Etsy's fees for a Canadian seller are set out below.

| Choice | What you control | What you confirm on a live page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Etsy listing | Photos, price, processing days, ship-from location | [Etsy fees](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/) |
| Canada Post service | Which product you buy at the counter or online | [Delivery standards](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/tools/delivery-standards.page) and the [holiday schedule](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/support/kb/other-products-services/post-office/find-out-operating-hours-on-holidays.page) |
| Craft market | What fits on the table and what you can carry home | The organizer's vendor terms and fee |
| Digital file | The download | The same Etsy fee page, with no parcel |

There is no national price for a handmade ornament; the worked example later is arithmetic on your own costs.

## When should you make holiday stock in Canada?

Make holiday stock in Canada early enough that drying, curing, and a second firing still finish before your ship-by date. October is the production month for a December delivery promise. Starting the batch on December 10 is a craft-market plan only if the buyers are standing in front of you and you already finished the goods.

### Step 1: Write the buyer's need date, then walk backward

A buyer who wants an object in hand on December 20 needs it delivered before that day, not shipped on that day. Canada Post's [delivery hours page](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/support/kb/receiving/delivery-faq/what-are-the-mail-and-parcel-delivery-hours.page) says residential and business delivery is Monday to Friday and that no deliveries are made on statutory holidays such as Christmas Day or New Year's Day. Christmas Day in 2026 is a Friday. A parcel that would have been delivered that day does not move.

### Step 2: Use Canada Post's holiday schedule, not a third-party cutoff list

Canada Post's [holiday schedule](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/support/kb/other-products-services/post-office/find-out-operating-hours-on-holidays.page) says the corporation is closed on national, provincial, and territorial holidays, with no collection or delivery on those days, aside from a note that regular collection and delivery occurs on provincial Family Day holidays, Louis Riel Day in Manitoba, and Islander Day in Prince Edward Island. The 2026 column lists Christmas Day on December 25 and Boxing Day on December 28. The page says that when a holiday falls on a Saturday or Sunday, it may be applied to the next business day, and that post offices will be closed with no collection or delivery. December 26, 2026, is a Saturday, which is why the table shows December 28. That is Canada Post's operating calendar. It is not a province's employment-standards list.

On 20 December 2024, Canada Post published a [closure and collection notice](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/our-company/news-and-media/corporate-news/closures-and-service-interruptions/2024-12-20-holiday-closures-and-mail-collection-from-mailboxes-and-post-offices-during-the-holidays) for that year. It said collection on December 24 and 31 could be earlier than usual, with community mailboxes and street letter boxes cleared as early as 8:00 a.m. local time and post offices as early as noon, and that items deposited after collection would be processed the next business day. Treat it as a 2024 example until the 2026 closure note is posted. Do not copy a shipping blog's "last day to mail" onto your shop banner.

### Step 3: Look up transit on the delivery-standards tool

Canada Post's [delivery standards tool](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/tools/delivery-standards.page) is how you see ordinary transit between an origin and a destination for the services it covers. Run it for the cities you actually ship between. Add days you are willing to promise on top of that result, because a standards page describes the service in ordinary conditions and your processing time is extra. Put the sum into the listing as processing days plus the service you will buy. If you cannot hit a date, close the listing. A refund after Christmas is a worse outcome than a sold-out button in November.

### Step 4: Separate market day from mail day

A craft-market application is the organizer's form. The table fee, the rain plan, and the hours are whatever that organizer wrote. This guide does not state a table fee. In-person sales that day do not need a parcel, and they do not use Etsy unless you also take the order online. Count the fee as a cost you cover with sales that day or with sales you are willing to make later. Unsold stock is still yours.

### Step 5: Stop taking custom orders before you are out of days

Custom work has a conversation inside the production time. A name on an ornament, a size, or a colour approval can add a week. Publish a last day for custom orders that is earlier than your last day for ready-made stock. Say what happens if the buyer replies late. Ship ready-made first.

## What does a worked holiday product price in Canada look like?

A worked holiday product price in Canada starts from your materials and time, then subtracts Etsy's fees from the table above. The materials, time, price and table fee below are assumptions, not a forecast of sales.

**Worked example.** Suppose one ornament uses $6 of materials and 40 minutes of your time. Suppose you choose $30 an hour as a labour target. Labour is (40/60) × $30 = $20. Materials plus labour in the sketch are $6 + $20 = $26 before fees, shipping, and tax. Suppose you list it at $28. One sale brings $28. Ten sales bring 10 × $28 = $280. Materials on ten are 10 × $6 = $60. The $280 minus $60 is $220. Etsy's fees on one $28 order from a Canadian buyer, with no shipping charged, are 6.5% ($1.82) + 3% + $0.25 ($1.09) + 0.5% ($0.14) + the US$0.20 listing (about $0.28) = about $3.33, so ten sales leave about $220 − $33.30 = $186.70 before the label and before your time is treated as paid. If the buyer pays shipping on top, the label is not inside the $28. If you offer free shipping, the label comes out of the $28. Look up the label before you choose free shipping.

Suppose a market table fee is $80. Three sales at $28 are $84, which covers the assumed fee by $4 before materials. Materials on three are 3 × $6 = $18. The sketch is $84 − $80 − $18 = −$14 before your time. Five sales are 5 × $28 = $140, minus $80, minus $30 of materials, which is $30 before time. Twenty ornaments made and three sold means seventeen still in the bin.

## What can you sell at a holiday market in Canada without creating a new problem?

Sell holiday goods in Canada that you have the right to make, that are safe to hand to a stranger, and that fit the market's rules. The Canadian Intellectual Property Office's [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright) pages are the starting point for characters, lyrics, and art you did not create. A popular movie character on an ornament is not a small-business loophole. This is not legal advice.

If you sell decorative light strings, Health Canada's [assessment of decorative lighting strings](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/legislation-guidelines/guidelines-policies/information-regulated-parties-enforcement-approach-general-prohibitions/danger-human-health-safety-assessment-decorative-lighting-strings.html) says certain strings that do not conform to the criteria it describes, tied to CSA C22.2 No. 37 or the equivalent, likely pose a danger to human health or safety. Advertising or selling a consumer product you know is a danger is a separate prohibition on that same policy path. Buy marked products from a traceable seller, or do not sell lights. The install side of outdoor lights is the [Christmas light installation guide](/side-hustles/christmas-light-installation-side-hustle-canada/), which is a service, not a product listing.

Food, cosmetics, and children's jewellery have their own federal and provincial rules. This guide does not cover them. If the object is soap, jam, or a toy, read the page for that product before the market, not after a inspector asks.

A market in a city is not a home-based business by itself. Making the goods at home can be. Vancouver's [home-based business page](https://vancouver.ca/doing-business/home-based-business.aspx) says you may need a licence, with area and employee limits. Confirm it if you produce in Vancouver. Toronto and other cities publish their own pages. Do not copy Vancouver's floor-area limit into another city.

## How should an Etsy shop and a market table work together?

An Etsy shop and a market table in Canada can share one product and still need two last dates. The table sells what you can carry. The shop ships what you can pack before Canada Post's closure days. Update the listing's quantity when the table sells the last of a size. A buyer who pays online for an item you sold in person that morning is a refund you caused.

Take a card reader or exact cash, and write market cash into the same book as Etsy deposits. If you are registered for GST/HST, the in-person price needs the same tax treatment as the online price. Say whether the sticker is tax-included.

Weather and a cancelled market are the organizer's risk and yours. Ask, before you pay the table fee, what the refund rule is if the event is called off. Inventing it in the parking lot does not help.

## What mistakes do holiday sellers make in Canada?

Holiday sellers in Canada promise a delivery date copied from a United States carrier or from a blog that is not Canada Post. They open custom orders in mid-December. They forget to subtract the fee on Etsy's page and then call the payout a surprise. They sell a character they do not own. They mail a fragile object in a soft mailer because the mailer was cheaper.

Another mistake is treating a 2024 collection notice as the 2026 clock. Use the live holiday schedule and the delivery-standards tool, then watch for the current year's closure note.

## Which tax rules apply to a holiday shop in Canada?

Holiday shop income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Market cash and Etsy payouts are in the same pot, along with any other hustle. Under the test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. A marketplace payout line is not a full answer to your registration test. If you are registered, charge the rate for the province of the supply. Confirm it on the [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) page and with the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). If you are not registered, do not add GST/HST.

Keep material receipts, label receipts, and the market application. Recheck Etsy's fee page and Canada Post's holiday schedule before you reuse this listing next year.
