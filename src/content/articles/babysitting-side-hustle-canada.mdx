## Who does babysitting in Canada suit?

Babysitting in Canada suits people who are patient, dependable on the nights they promise, and comfortable saying "your parents said no" to a seven-year-old. It is a poor fit if you need a fixed weekly schedule from day one, if you are unsure what to do when a child is hurt or sick, or if you would rather not be alone in charge of someone else's children.

Babysitting is relationship work. Families tend to rebook the sitter whose evening went exactly as planned: the bedtime held, the allergy rule was followed, the house was as tidy as it was left. That reliability is the product.

## Is babysitting regulated in Canada?

Occasional babysitting in a family's own home is generally treated differently from running child care for several families in your home, which each province regulates. Once you look after children from more than one family at your place, provincial limits on unlicensed child care apply, and the numbers differ a lot between provinces.

The three provinces below are summarized from their official pages as fetched in October 2026. For other provinces and territories, check the provincial child care ministry before you take children from more than one family at once.

| Province | Unlicensed limit, from the official page | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ontario | Up to 5 children under 13; no more than 3 under age 2; your own children under 4 count | Must tell parents in writing that the program is not licensed by the Government of Ontario and keep the notice for 2 years. [Ontario.ca](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-child-care-and-unlicensed-child-care-how-many-children-are-allowed) |
| British Columbia | Two children or one sibling group not related to you | Licence-not-required providers are not monitored or inspected. [Gov.bc.ca](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/family-information/licensed-unlicensed-child-care) |
| Alberta | Up to 6 children, not including your own | The province can investigate complaints and issue stop orders above that number. [Alberta.ca](https://www.alberta.ca/understand-albertas-childcare-system-for-parents-and-families) |

Sitting for one family in their home on a Friday night is the simplest version and the one this guide focuses on. If you plan to watch a few neighbours' children together at your place, read your province's page first, count carefully, and remember that in Ontario your own young children count toward the limit.

## How much can babysitting earn in Canada?

Babysitting earnings in Canada depend on local norms and the job, and this guide does not publish a national rate. What you take home depends on:

- The number and ages of the children, and whether one is an infant
- Evening, overnight, or holiday timing
- Extra tasks the parents ask for, such as cooking dinner or school pickup
- Your training and experience, and how many families rebook you
- Travel time and who pays for the ride home

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, forecast, or market rate. Suppose a sitter spends $90 on a first-aid course and $25 on a kit: $90 + $25 = $115. ## How do you start babysitting in Canada?

Starting babysitting in Canada means training first, offering a clear service to people who already know you, and running the first evenings by the parents' written instructions. The steps below follow that order.

### Step 1: Take a first-aid course

The Canadian Red Cross offers a Babysitting course for youth aged 11 to 15, and adults can take standard first aid with CPR from several providers. Keep the certificate and note its expiry date.

### Step 2: Decide what you will and won't do

Write it down: ages you'll care for, maximum number of children, whether you'll do overnights, whether you'll drive children anywhere (most beginners shouldn't), and whether you'll give medication. A short "no" list protects you and the family.

### Step 3: Check the provincial rule if care happens in your home

If you'll ever look after children from more than one family together at your place, read your province's page: [Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-child-care-and-unlicensed-child-care-how-many-children-are-allowed), [B.C.](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/family-social-supports/caring-for-young-children/family-information/licensed-unlicensed-child-care), or [Alberta](https://www.alberta.ca/understand-albertas-childcare-system-for-parents-and-families). Count your own children where the rule says to.

### Step 4: Build a one-page sitter sheet

Your name, training, availability, references, and what you charge. Leave space on the back for each family's details: parents' cell numbers, allergies, bedtime, screen rules, a nearby contact, and the home address for a 911 call.

### Step 5: Offer it to people who already know you

Neighbours, family friends, and parents from community groups are where most first jobs come from. Two references from people who have watched you with children carry more weight than any flyer.

### Step 6: Meet the family before the first evening

A short visit lets the children meet you and lets you walk through the house: exits, the first-aid kit, the thermostat, and the door locks. Ask the parents to write down anything medical.

### Step 7: Run the first evening by the sheet

Arrive early, keep your phone charged, follow the bedtime and food rules exactly, and write a two-line note for the parents: what the children ate, when they went down, anything unusual. Ask to be paid by e-transfer so you have a record.

## What mistakes are common when babysitting in Canada?

Common babysitting mistakes in Canada are taking more children than you can safely watch, skipping the allergy conversation, and leaving payment vague. Each one is easy to prevent with a written note before the evening starts.

- **Saying yes to too many children.** If care happens at your home, the provincial limit applies; even in the family's home, know your own limit.
- **Guessing about allergies or medication.** If it isn't written down, ask. Don't give medication you weren't told to give.
- **Driving children without a plan.** Car seats, insurance, and parental consent all need to be sorted first. Many sitters simply don't drive children.
- **Leaving the price unspoken.** Agree the hourly rate, what happens after midnight, and who pays for the ride home before the evening.
- **Posting photos of the children.** Never post a child's photo without the parents' permission.

## Which tax rules apply to babysitting in Canada?

Babysitting income in Canada is generally self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1 return. Self-employed people pay both halves of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net self-employment income above the basic exemption.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

GST/HST follows the CRA's [small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html): $30,000 in taxable supplies in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, counting all your side-hustle revenue together. Child care has its own GST/HST treatment: the CRA's page on [collecting GST/HST on your daycare services](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/daycare-your-home/collecting-goods-services-tax-harmonized-sales-tax-on-your-daycare-services.html) says care and supervision you provide in your home to children aged 14 or under, for periods usually less than 24 hours a day, is free of GST/HST, so you can't add the tax. For sitting in the family's home or for older children, confirm the treatment with the CRA before you charge tax. Parents may claim [child care expenses on line 21400](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-21400-child-care-expenses.html) when the care lets them work or study, so write a dated receipt with your name, the dates, and the amount when a family asks for one, and keep a copy. A full tax walkthrough is in [how to report side hustle income to the CRA](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).
