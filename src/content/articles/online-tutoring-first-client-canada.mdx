Getting a first online tutoring client in Canada means booking one paid session, in one subject you can already teach, with a parent or adult learner who knows the time, the price, and how to cancel. Online tutoring suits people who can explain slowly, keep a quiet room, and start on time.

The first client is a capped trial that might become a short pack of hours. A higher test score is not something you can sell. The setup for the room, the no-list, and the gear is in [how to start online tutoring in Canada](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/).

| Fact | Detail for a first online tutoring client in Canada |
| --- | --- |
| Offer | One subject, one level, a session length, and a cancellation rule |
| Who books | A parent or guardian when the student is a child. An adult learner books for themselves |
| Proof | A one-minute explanation you recorded, your real education, and a session note template |
| Screening | Provincial. Ontario: [police record checks](https://www.ontario.ca/page/police-record-checks). British Columbia: [Criminal Records Review Program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/crime-prevention/criminal-record-check/law-policy) |
| Commercial email | [Getting consent to send email](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) |
| GST/HST | Small-supplier threshold of $30,000. [When to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) |

Writing the same explanation as a document is [freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/). Recording lessons for strangers to watch later is a [YouTube channel](/side-hustles/youtube-side-hustle-canada/).

## What offer gets a first online tutoring client in Canada?

A first online tutoring client in Canada is easier to book when the offer is one subject, one age band, and a session length, plus a written rule for no-shows. "All subjects, all ages" asks a parent to guess whether you can teach their child.

Name the topic the way that province labels it when you know the province. Course names differ. "Linear equations for a high-school student in Ontario" is a sentence. Say whether the session is in English or in French.

Write the no-list in the same place as the price. You will not write the student's assignment, guarantee a grade, provide therapy, or supervise a child as if this were licensed child care. A private lesson stays on the lesson side of that line.

## Where do Canadians find a first online tutoring client?

Canadians find a first online tutoring client among families and adult learners who already know the tutor, in local communities that allow a services post, and on tutoring platforms whose fee and conduct pages the tutor has actually read. This guide does not state a platform commission.

People who already know you can teach are the cleanest first path. A coworker, a community you belong to, or a family that has seen you explain the subject. Offer two trials with a time cap. Do not imply that a school endorsed you.

Local boards and classifieds, including Kijiji, are a second path when the category allows tutoring and you follow that site's current posting rules. Read those rules in the product before you copy a format from a video. Describe the subject you teach and the credentials you have.

A tutoring marketplace can introduce families and will publish its own fee. Use one path for the first month. Two inboxes is how a student gets forgotten.

If you email parents, [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) can apply to a commercial message. ISED's [getting consent to send email](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) page says you need consent before commercial emails or texts. Express consent means the person agreed. Implied consent is limited, including some existing relationships, and that page says it may last up to two years, or six months after an inquiry. The message needs your name, a mailing address, a phone number or email or website valid for at least 60 days, and an unsubscribe mechanism acted on within 10 business days at no cost. Do not buy a list of parents. This is not legal advice.

School-age students need an adult in the booking. Send the time, the price, and a way to reach you to the parent or guardian, not only to the child.

## What should a first tutoring message say?

A first tutoring message in Canada should name the subject, the session length, the cancellation rule, and whether a police-check conversation is welcome when the student is a child. It should not promise a grade.

**Example message.** This is a generic example. It is not a message that booked a student.

> Hello. I tutor [subject] for [level] students, in [language], online. A first session is [30 or 45] minutes at [price], and I send a short note of what we covered. Cancellations with less than [your window] notice follow the rule I will paste under this note. I do not write the student's assignment or promise a mark. If you would like a trial, reply with two times that work and the province the course is in.

Put the cancellation rule in the first message. Inventing it after a no-show makes the parent the editor of the business.

## How do you show tutoring proof without fake reviews?

You show tutoring proof in Canada with a one-minute recording of you explaining one problem, a session outline, and the education or experience you actually have. A fake parent review, a stock photo of a child, or another tutor's credentials is a misrepresentation.

Record the minute in the room you will use. No student's face and no student's name. Watch it. If the kitchen is louder than the explanation, fix the sound before you send the file.

A reference is a person who has seen you teach and agrees to be asked. Do not write a quotation they did not say. A practice trial with a friend can be described as practice. It cannot be described as a client result.

When learners are children, screening is provincial. Ontario's [police record checks](https://www.ontario.ca/page/police-record-checks) page describes checks for people who work or volunteer in a position of trust or authority over children or other vulnerable persons. In British Columbia, certain organizations must use the [Criminal Records Review Program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/crime-prevention/criminal-record-check/law-policy). The [applicant process](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/crime-prevention/criminal-record-check/applicants/process) page is where the current fee is listed. Confirm it. A parent hiring you directly may still ask for a check. In other provinces, ask the police service that covers your address. This is not legal advice.

## How should you price the first tutoring sessions in Canada?

Price the first online tutoring sessions in Canada as a single session and a small prepaid pack, with an expiry on the pack and a no-show rule you will enforce. This guide does not publish a Canadian market rate. Ask what is normal in your city, then use a number you can say out loud.

| Path | What the family buys | What you watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single session | One hour, paid before or at the session | A one-off that never books again, which is still useful information |
| Four-session pack | Four hours, prepaid, with an expiry | Hours claimed a year later because you never wrote an end date |

Prepaid packs need an expiry. A platform cut, if you use a platform, comes off the platform's fee page and is not in the sketch below.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first tutoring client look like?

A 14-day plan for a first online tutoring client in Canada should produce a subject sentence, a cancellation rule, a one-minute recording, and a handful of real conversations. A paid booking is not guaranteed by the calendar.

### Days 1 and 2: Name the lesson

Write the subject, the level, the language, and the no-list. Read your province's police-check page if the learners are children. Record one minute and decide whether the headset comes before the first call.

### Days 3 to 5: Write the rules and the note

Pick a cancellation window you will enforce. Draft the five-line session note: date, topic, what the student could do, and what to try next. Price one session and a four-pack on paper, and put an expiry on the pack.

### Days 6 to 9: Talk to people who can say yes or no

Message people who already know you, with the example shape above, adapted to the subject. Post once in a community whose rules allow it. If you email strangers, read ISED's consent page first. Do not message children directly.

### Days 10 to 14: Teach the trial or tighten the offer

Run one capped trial. Send the note the same day. If nobody books, change the subject sentence or the recording, not the font on a flyer. Open two real time slots and leave the rest of the week alone.

## What do days 15 to 30 look like for a first online tutoring client in Canada?

Days 15 to 30 turn the first 14 days into a 30-day plan: one more round of asks, one finished job or a clear reason there was none, and numbers you can review on day 30.

### Days 15 to 21: Deliver, then ask for the next piece of work

Deliver the first sessions with a short note to the parent or student on what you covered on the date you agreed, and invoice the same day. In the same message, ask whether they want a fixed weekly time slot. Send the same offer to five new contacts you have not asked yet.

### Days 22 to 26: Compare the hours with the quote

Write down how long the work really took, including revisions and calls. If preparation took as long as the session, count it in your hourly rate next time.

### Days 27 to 30: Review the month

Fill in the P&L below with what you invoiced, the payment fee, and what you paid for software. Decide one change for month two: the price, the scope, or the channel.

### Script: the follow-up after the first job

> Thanks for booking the first two sessions. I can keep the same time every week at the same rate, and send a two-line note after each session on what we covered.

This is a template, not a quote from a client. Change the details to match the job you actually did.

## What mistakes block a first online tutoring client in Canada?

Mistakes that block a first online tutoring client in Canada are promising a mark, teaching every subject to fill a calendar, and skipping the adult when the student is a child. Parents book clarity and a person who shows up.

Doing the assignment for the student trains the wrong person and can breach a school's rules. A profile that copies another tutor's degree is a false credential. Buying a semester of software before the first paid hour spends the money the headset needed.

A marketplace bio with no cancellation rule fills the week with empty chairs. Write the rule before the first yes.

## Which tax and screening rules apply to a first tutoring client in Canada?

Online tutoring income in Canada is self-employment income, including prepaid packs, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST for online tutoring follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the booking messages, the e-transfers, and the session log. Report tutoring on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Online tutoring is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Confirm Quebec and every other province on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside.

Screening stays provincial. Use the Ontario and British Columbia pages linked above, and the police service where you live for other provinces. A private lesson is not automatically licensed child care.
