## What are the best side hustles for stay-at-home parents in Canada?

The best side hustles for stay-at-home parents in Canada are asynchronous (no fixed meeting times), can pause without losing a client, and need no childcare to do. The groups below follow that logic.

### Desk work you can do in 30-minute blocks

[Virtual assistant work](/side-hustles/virtual-assistant-side-hustle-canada/) covers inbox, scheduling, and simple spreadsheets for small businesses. [Freelance bookkeeping](/side-hustles/freelance-bookkeeping-side-hustle-canada/) and [freelance writing](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/) run on deadlines rather than fixed hours. Set clear response times with clients so a sick day doesn't become a crisis.

### Make once, sell many times

[Printables](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/), [Notion templates](/side-hustles/notion-templates-side-hustle-canada/), and [ebooks](/side-hustles/ebook-publishing-side-hustle-canada/) take time up front and then sell without you being present. Sales are unpredictable, so treat these as slow builds rather than quick income.

### Turn outgrown gear into cash

[Reselling](/side-hustles/reselling-side-hustle-canada/) and [clothing resale](/side-hustles/clothing-resale-side-hustle-canada/) start with what your kids have outgrown, and [Facebook Marketplace flipping](/side-hustles/facebook-marketplace-flipping-side-hustle-canada/) works with local pickups at your door.

### From your kitchen

[Home baking](/side-hustles/home-baking-side-hustle-canada/) can fit school hours, but food rules vary by province; check [cottage food rules by province](/side-hustles/holiday-baking-cottage-food-rules-by-province-canada/) first.

### Caring for other children

[Babysitting](/side-hustles/babysitting-side-hustle-canada/) or home child care pays you for something you're already doing. In Ontario, the province's [home child care page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/home-child-care-and-unlicensed-child-care-how-many-children-are-allowed) says an unlicensed provider can have at most 5 children under 13, no more than 3 under age 2, and must count their own children under 4. Unlicensed providers must tell parents in writing that the program is not licensed by the Government of Ontario. Other provinces set different limits, so confirm locally.

## How does side hustle income affect the Canada child benefit?

Side hustle income can reduce your Canada child benefit because the CCB is based on adjusted family net income (AFNI). The CRA's [CCB calculation page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/child-family-benefits/canada-child-benefit-overview/canada-child-benefit-we-calculate-your-ccb.html) says AFNI is your family net income from line 23600 of your return, plus your spouse's or common-law partner's, with a few adjustments. For payments from July 2026 to June 2027, based on 2025 income, the CRA says you get the maximum per child if AFNI is under $38,237, and payments gradually decrease above that. Payments are recalculated every July.

Two points follow. First, it's net self-employment income that counts, so legitimate business expenses on your T2125 reduce the figure. Second, the effect shows up a year later: 2026 income affects payments starting July 2027. Use the reduction tables on that page to estimate your own number.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a calculation of anyone's benefit. Suppose a couple's 2025 family net income is $60,000 and one parent adds $5,000 of net self-employment income. Their AFNI becomes $60,000 + $5,000 = $65,000. Because that's above $38,237, their CCB was already being reduced; the extra $5,000 increases the reduction by an amount that depends on the number of children. Check the CRA's tables for the exact rate before planning around the number.

## Can parents deduct child care costs while running a side hustle in Canada?

Possibly. The CRA's [line 21400 page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/line-21400-child-care-expenses.html) says child care expenses are amounts paid to have someone else look after an eligible child so you could earn income, go to school, or do research under a grant. Eligibility and who can claim have their own rules on that page, so read it before counting on the deduction.

## What mistakes do stay-at-home parents make with side hustles in Canada?

The common mistakes are promising fixed availability, ignoring the CCB effect, and spending on gear or courses before the first sale.

- **Over-promising availability.** Offer turnaround times, not "always on."
- **Forgetting the one-year lag.** Higher income this year changes CCB from next July.
- **Buying a course first.** Most picks above can be tested for $0.
- **Skipping home child care rules.** Count your own children in the provincial limit.

## Which tax rules apply to parents' side hustles in Canada?

A stay-at-home parent's side hustle income in Canada is generally taxable. Report self-employment income and expenses on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). If you're over 18 and net self-employment earnings exceed $3,500, you generally pay both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration only becomes mandatory above the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). If you work from a corner of your home, read [home office expenses for side hustles](/side-hustles/home-office-expenses-side-hustle-canada/).
