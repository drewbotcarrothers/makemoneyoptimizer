A spring pet waste cleanup in Canada is a one-time yard visit after snowmelt, when waste that was buried all winter is on the grass. It is not the weekly scoop. It suits people who already walk a neighbourhood and can book a short season of longer visits, then convert some of those yards to a route. It is a poor fit if you need the work in January under deep snow, or if you will quote the weekly price for a yard nobody has scooped since November.

## Who does a spring pet waste cleanup in Canada suit?

A spring cleanup suits dog walkers, students, and anyone who can spend a few weekends on yards they can reach on foot. The [start guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-side-hustle-canada/) already says snow cover is why many routes shrink in winter. Spring is the week that reason flips: the waste is visible, the owner is embarrassed, and the lawn person is about to arrive. It is a poor fit if you cannot tell waste from mud, if you are unwilling to kneel in a wet yard, or if you would rather sell mowing. Mowing is a different tool and a different price.

The visit is the yard. A walk that day is [dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/). Do not fold it into the cleanup price unless you timed both.

## When should you sell spring pet waste cleanups in Canada?

Sell them when the ground in your city is visible and before weekly mowing starts. On the Prairies and in much of Ontario and Quebec, that is often March into April, and a late storm can push it. On the coast it can be earlier. Watch the yards you would actually service. A national "book by March" date is a publishing habit, not a weather normal.

The offer is timely for about two to six weeks on a given street. After the first mow, waste gets chopped into the grass and the cleanup is harder and less honest to sell as a surface scoop. Before the melt, you are guessing under snow. The [fall leaf cleanup](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/) guide is the other end of the outdoor year. Leaves hide waste in October the way snow hides it in February.

Say the week you can start, and say that a dump of new snow can move the appointment. A written window is better than a promise of "the first warm Saturday."

## How is a spring cleanup different from a weekly scoop?

A weekly scoop is maintenance. A spring cleanup is a reset of a yard that may hold months of waste, plus litter, plus clumps that froze to the soil. It takes more bags, more minutes, and a rake or a small shovel the weekly visit may not need. The [pricing guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-pricing-canada/) tells you to charge more for that visit than for the weekly price, and its dollar line is labelled as a teaching sketch. Use the method. Do not treat that sketch as the Canadian rate.

| | Weekly scoop | Spring cleanup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | You know the spots | Snowmelt, mud, and a season of unknown piles |
| Time | Short, once the route exists | Longer. Time it. Do not guess from the weekly minutes |
| Bags | A few, the type the city accepts | More. The bag cost belongs in the price |
| Next sale | The visit is the product | The visit is the introduction to a weekly plan |
| Lawn | Leave the grass alone | Do not promise aeration, seeding, or a repair. That is [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) |

Put the waste in the household cart the city allows, on that property. Toronto's green bin page lists pet waste, including animal droppings and certain cat litters. Pet fur, hair, and feathers are listed with items that do not go in. Calgary requires a certified compostable bag or a paper bag, tied closed, and says biodegradable and plastic bags contaminate the compost. Buy the bag the live page names. Other cities still want pet waste in the garbage. If the city is silent, call the waste department before you sell the service.

A cart that is already full is the owner's problem to solve with you before you start, not a reason to drive the waste away. The [bin cleaning guide](/side-hustles/bin-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) is a separate service if they also want the cart washed. Washing it into the street is a sewer issue, not a favour.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, a forecast, or a survey. Suppose the rake, a pack of the correct bags, and gloves cost $15 + $18 + $12 = $45, and you already own a scooper. Suppose a neglected yard takes 70 minutes after melt, including finding the cart. Suppose your target is $30 an hour. Labour is (70 ÷ 60) × $30 = $35. Suppose bags used on that yard cost $6. The sketch price is $35 + $6 = $41, which you might round to $50 because the first visit also includes the gate, the dog, and the spots the owner warns you about. Suppose four yards in a weekend each take that hour and pay the assumed $50. Fees are $200. Bags for the four are 4 × $6 = $24. ## How do you start spring pet waste cleanups in Canada?

### Step 1: Learn the bin rule on your street

Open the city page. Buy those bags. If you work in more than one municipality, you may need two kinds of bags.

### Step 2: Walk the yards you could reach in one morning

A cleanup route is still a route. Four far-apart yards will not fit the sketch above.

### Step 3: Offer the reset and the weekly plan as two prices

Say what the cleanup includes. Say it does not include mowing, de-icing, or hauling waste off the property. The [first clients guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-first-clients-canada/) is how the note and the referral work for the ongoing service.

### Step 4: Book a weather window

"After the yard is clear of snow, before the first mow" is a sentence a client understands. A fixed Saturday in a blizzard is not.

### Step 5: Leave the gate as you found it

A photo of the cleared yard is the weekly pitch. A gate left open is the end of the pitch.

## What mistakes do spring cleanup sellers make in Canada?

Common mistakes are selling the cleanup at the weekly rate, scooping before the melt and missing half the yard, and using the wrong bag because "biodegradable" sounded like Calgary's certified compostable bag. Another is filling a park bin or a car trunk. Another is promising to fix the brown patches. Those patches may be lawn damage. Quote [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) separately or decline.

A further mistake is waiting until May because a blog said "spring." On your street, May may be the third mow. Sell when you can see the ground.

## Which tax rules apply to spring pet waste cleanups in Canada?

Cleanup fees are self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) across taxable side hustles, including a weekly pet-waste route and other outdoor work. Seasonal does not mean invisible. When registered, charge the rate on the CRA's [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) page.

The [tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) and [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) keep this on the business form. Bags are a current expense. Keep the receipts.
