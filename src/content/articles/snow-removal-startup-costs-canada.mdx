**Quick answer:** a lean snow removal start in Canada costs about **$187** (shovel, ice chopper, cleats, headlamps and a bag of melt, Ontario HST included), a standard shovel route about **$358 in gear plus a liability policy that starts around $1,000 a year**, and a pro start with a cordless blower about **$990 in gear plus insurance from about $1,800 a year**. Prices below are Amazon.ca shelf prices checked on October 9, 2026, with 13% Ontario HST added. One Toronto seasonal driveway contract at $550 pays for the lean kit; the pro kit needs about six.

## How much does snow removal cost to start in Canada?

Snow removal costs about $187 to start with a shovel kit, about $1,358 for a shovel route with a $1,000 liability policy, and about $2,790 with a battery blower and a $1,800 policy. All gear prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026, with Ontario's 13% HST added. Insurance is shown separately because premiums are quoted, not shelf-priced.

| Kit | Gear before tax | Gear with 13% HST | Insurance (per year) | First-season cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean shovel route | $165.91 | $187.48 | None yet (get a quote) | $187.48 |
| Standard shovel route | $316.94 | $358.14 | From about $1,000 | $1,358.14 |
| Pro start with a battery blower | $875.94 | $989.81 | From about $1,800 | $2,789.81 |

In a GST-only province (Alberta, the territories) or a 5% GST plus provincial sales tax province, the tax line changes; the [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists the GST/HST rate for each province. At 5% GST, the pro gear total is $919.74 instead of $989.81.

## Who should use a snow removal startup budget in Canada?

This budget fits people who can get up early, are steady on ice, and live where snow repeats every winter: the Prairies, Ottawa, Montreal, northern and central Ontario, and much of Atlantic Canada. Southern Ontario away from the snowbelts gets fewer storms, and a mild coastal B.C. street should not finance a blower. It is a poor fit with a heart or back condition, a day job that cannot move on storm mornings, or a plan to finance a machine before anyone has signed.

Summer on the same street is [lawn care startup costs](/side-hustles/lawn-care-startup-costs-canada/). Repairs after the melt are [handyman startup costs](/side-hustles/handyman-startup-costs-canada/). If the 5 a.m. start is the dealbreaker, [house cleaning startup costs](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-startup-costs-canada/) do not depend on the sky.

## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for snow removal in Canada?

Break-even is the point where seasonal fees cover what you spent. The formula is:

**Driveways to break even = (gear total + insurance) ÷ seasonal price per driveway**

For a seasonal price, [The Blizzard Wizard's 2026–27 Toronto contract](https://theblizzardwizard.ca/services/seasonal-snow-removal-contracts-toronto) lists $550 for a one-car driveway with a 6–10 hour response (November 1 to April 30, 2 cm trigger, before tax). [HomeStars' cost guide](https://www.homestars.com/gardening-outdoors/price-guides/snow-removal-cost) puts a driveway at $30 to $90 per visit and seasonal contracts at $350 to $1,000 or more.

| Kit | First-season cash | ÷ $550 per seasonal driveway | Driveways needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $187.48 | 0.34 | 1 |
| Standard | $1,358.14 | 2.47 | 3 |
| Pro | $2,789.81 | 5.07 | 6 |

### Illustrative example: a six-driveway pro route

**Assumed inputs:** six one-car driveways at $550 each, 20 cleared visits per driveway in the season, and $2.80 of ice melt per visit (one 25 lb bag at $27.99 spread over 10 visits). **Sourced inputs:** the $550 price, the $989.81 pro gear total and the $1,800 insurance starting point above.

- Revenue: 6 × $550 = **$3,300**
- Ice melt: 6 × 20 × $2.80 = **$336**
- First season: $3,300 − $989.81 gear − $1,800 insurance − $336 melt = **$174.19**
- Second season (gear already owned): $3,300 − $1,800 − $336 = **$1,164.00**

The first winter mostly buys the equipment. The second is where the route pays. Battery charging adds a little to the electricity bill, and you will eventually replace batteries and shovel blades; neither is in this sketch.

### Illustrative example: a lean per-visit start

**Assumed input:** $45 per visit, inside HomeStars' $30 to $90 range. $187.48 ÷ $45 = 4.17, so the fifth paid visit puts the lean kit in profit. At $30 per visit it takes seven visits ($187.48 ÷ $30 = 6.25).

Ongoing costs that do not shrink in a light winter: insurance, phone, and any software subscription. Costs that grow in an icy winter: melt, cleat replacements and return trips for the plough windrow if your contract includes them. ## Where can snow removal startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Start with one good shovel and a pusher, not three shovels.** The Shovelution bundle plus a 30-inch pusher covers steps, walks and a double driveway.
- **Buy melt by the bag as the season goes.** One 25 lb bag in November is enough to learn your usage; buying a pallet before you have clients ties up cash.
- **Use the boots you have if they grip.** Cleats ($28.98 to $39.52) turn a decent winter boot into a safe work boot.
- **Run invoicing free at first.** <AffiliateLink program="wave" label="Wave" /> has a free plan that handles a few seasonal invoices. Move to <AffiliateLink program="jobber" label="Jobber" /> when you are scheduling routes, not neighbours.
- **Skip the blower until a route is too long to shovel before work.** A used single-stage machine from a local dealer is the common first upgrade.

## Where should snow removal spending not be cut in Canada?

- **Traction.** A fall on someone else's driveway is the most likely way to end a season.
- **Liability insurance once you have more than a couple of neighbours.** Slip-and-fall claims are the reason commercial clients ask for proof of coverage before signing. The [Apollo vs Zensurance comparison](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/) shows the two quote routes; <AffiliateLink program="zensurance" label="Zensurance" /> brokers snow and landscaping policies.
- **A machine that starts in the cold.** If you do buy a blower, read the [snow blower buying guide](/side-hustles/snow-blower-buying-guide-canada/) first; a cheap machine that will not start at −20°C is not a saving.
- **A written agreement.** The [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/) covers triggers, windrows and what counts as an extra visit.

## What mistakes make snow removal startup costs higher?

1. **Buying the pro kit in September.** The blower and policy cost $2,789.81 together; with no signed driveways, a mild winter turns that into debt.
2. **Comparing tool-only blower prices to kits.** The $499.99 tool-only machine needs a battery and charger the $559.00 kit already includes.
3. **Pricing below the gear.** At $30 a visit with no contracts, the lean kit takes seven visits to pay back; a seasonal contract pays it in one.
4. **Forgetting the windrow.** If the agreement includes plough-ridge return visits, your visit count, and melt use, goes up.
5. **Mixing the float with wages.** Seasonal prepayments arrive in October and November, but melt and repairs land in February. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can keep that money separate.

## What could change these snow removal numbers?

Three things move these totals more than any product choice: your province's sales tax (13% HST in Ontario versus 5% GST in Alberta), the insurance quote you actually receive (the published starting points are $1,000 and $1,800 a year for a $1 million limit, and a $2 million limit or a truck costs more), and the winter itself, which changes how many visits a seasonal contract absorbs. Shelf prices were checked on October 9, 2026 and Amazon.ca reprices often, especially once the first snow hits. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

## Which tax rules apply to a snow removal budget in Canada?

Snow removal income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your personal return. Melt, cleats and small supplies are ordinary expenses. A blower is capital equipment, claimed over time through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion, not deducted in full in year one.

You must register for and charge GST/HST once your worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting every side hustle together. Below that, you are a small supplier and do not charge it. Snow removal is not covered by the special [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. Self-employed profit also brings both halves of [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html). <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> keeps receipts and seasonal invoices in one place once you pass a handful of clients.
