## How should you choose food delivery gear in Canada?

Choose food delivery gear in Canada by what the apps in your zone require, how you travel, and winter conditions. A bag that meets the platform's size rule and a mount that does not drop your phone at minus 20 matter more than branded extras. These are selection criteria, not test results.

| Criterion | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform requirements | The app's own bag rules | [Uber](https://help.uber.com/driving-and-delivering/article/getting-started-with-insulated-bag-orders?nodeId=8bdb32b1-e400-4324-a5d1-de4759cb1a44) says any insulated bag that meets its size requirements is accepted, branded or not |
| Vehicle type | Car seat, bike rack, or backpack | Uber's page says bicycle deliveries may benefit from a more specialized bag |
| Mount type | Dashboard or other fixed mount, not a flimsy vent clip | Cold makes plastic brittle and vents blow heat on the phone |
| Battery | Cable length, charger output | Maps plus the courier app drain batteries |
| Cleanability | Wipeable lining | Spills happen. Restaurants notice a dirty bag |
| Visibility (bike and foot) | Lights and reflective layers | Dark winter evenings |

## How do insulated bag orders work for food delivery in Canada?

On Uber, insulated bag orders are orders where restaurants or stores require a bag for hot or cold food. Uber's [help page](https://help.uber.com/driving-and-delivering/article/getting-started-with-insulated-bag-orders?nodeId=8bdb32b1-e400-4324-a5d1-de4759cb1a44) says there are standard, pizza, and large bag order types, that you add a bag in the Driver app under Account, Work Hub, Insulated Bags, that review typically takes about a day, and that you can upload up to three bags to qualify for different order types. Its Canadian [handling orders page](https://www.uber.com/ca/en/deliver/basics/tips-for-success/handling-food/) says people delivering with the Uber Eats app are not required to use insulated bags.

Other apps set their own rules. The [Uber Eats vs DoorDash vs Skip comparison](/side-hustles/uber-eats-vs-doordash-vs-skip-canada/) is the place to compare platforms; read each app's live requirements before buying a second bag.

## Which tax rules apply to food delivery gear in Canada?

Delivery income is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and the self-employed pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. Bags, mounts, and cables used for deliveries are business expenses. For a car, the CRA's [motor vehicle records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses/motor-vehicle-expenses/motor-vehicle-records.html) page says to log the date, destination, purpose, and kilometres of each business trip and record the odometer at the start and end of the fiscal period.

On GST/HST, the CRA's [registration page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) says commercial ride-sharing drivers who supply passenger transportation must register even if they are small suppliers. Food delivery alone is not passenger transportation, so the general $30,000 small-supplier test applies; combine all your side hustles when you count. If you also drive passengers, the ride-sharing rule applies to you. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. Ask your insurer whether delivery use is covered before your first shift.
