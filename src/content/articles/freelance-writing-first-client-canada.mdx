Getting a first freelance writing client in Canada means selling one defined document, such as a service page, to a business you can describe, with a sample they can read before they pay. Freelance writing suits people who can revise, hit a date, and explain a subject they already know.

The first client is a paid test with a word cap and a revision cap. It is a poor target if you need rent from the first email, or if you will not send the note until the sample feels perfect. ## What offer gets a first freelance writing client in Canada?

A first freelance writing client in Canada usually buys one page with a length, a date, and one round of edits, aimed at a business type you can name. "Content writer" is hard to purchase. "A service page for a house-cleaning company" is a purchase.

Pick a pair you can see in the world: service pages for trades, a help article for a tool you use, or a short explainer for a clinic topic you understand. Write the pair in one sentence. If you cannot list ten real businesses that need that sentence, the offer is still a costume.

Say what is outside the first job. Interviews, a full website, and a monthly blog are later quotes. The first yes is easier when the no-list is visible.

## Where do Canadians find a first freelance writing client?

Canadians find a first freelance writing client among businesses whose sites they can already describe, among people who know their work, and on marketplaces only after reading that marketplace's own fee page. A purchased email list is a weak and often unlawful start.

Use three paths, and use the first one before the third.

Local businesses with a visible gap. Open the site. Note the missing service page, the outdated hours, or the paragraph that does not say what the company does. You are offering to fill a gap you can point at.

People who already know you can write. A former coworker, a community group you belong to, or a business owner who has seen you finish something. That is a conversation, not a scrape.

A marketplace profile, if you use one. This guide does not state a marketplace percentage. Read the platform's seller page before you rely on a cut you heard second-hand. Put the same niche sentence and the same sample on the profile. A profile with no sample is a wish.

If the note is a commercial electronic message, [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies. ISED's page on [getting consent to send email](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) says businesses need consent before commercial emails or texts. Consent is express, meaning the person agreed, or implied in limited cases such as an existing business relationship. That page says implied consent from a relationship may last up to two years, or six months after an inquiry or application. It also says a commercial message must include your name, a current mailing address, and a phone number, email, or website that stays valid for at least 60 days, plus an unsubscribe mechanism. Unsubscribe requests must be actioned within 10 business days, at no cost to the recipient. Look for a "do not contact" line before you use an address that was published online. A public contact form the business put on its own site is a different posture from a bought list. This is not legal advice.

## What should a first freelance writing note say?

A first freelance writing note in Canada should name the gap on that business's site, point at one sample, state a capped paid test, and ask one question. Three to six sentences are enough. A generic "I write content" note gives the reader nothing to accept.

**Example message.** This is a generic example you can adapt. It is not a note that produced a client, and it is not a claim that any business replied.

> Hello. Your site explains the company and does not yet have a page for [the service I can see you offer]. I wrote a sample service page in that kind of business, labelled as a sample, and I can send it. A first piece would be one page, about 700 words, one revision, delivered on a date we write down, at a project fee we agree before I start. Would you like the sample?

Keep a list of who received a commercial message, and honour an unsubscribe or a "no." Do not rewrite the offer for each silence.

## How do you prove freelance writing skill without fake reviews?

You prove freelance writing skill in Canada with one finished sample in the niche you are selling, written from public information, with anything you assumed labelled as an assumption. A fake client logo, an invented testimonial, or a borrowed paragraph is a misrepresentation, not a portfolio.

Six hundred to nine hundred words is enough. Use a headline a business could publish. If a number is not in a source the reader can open, leave it out. The Canadian Intellectual Property Office explains [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright) in plain language. Do not paste someone else's page and change the city.

On the sample itself, write "Sample, not a client project" at the top if nobody hired you to write it. When a real client later allows you to show the piece, put that permission in the agreement. Payment alone is a poor substitute for a sentence about who may publish the work and whether you may show it.

## How should you price the first freelance writing job in Canada?

Price the first freelance writing job in Canada as a project fee for a defined page, with rounds of edits and a date written down, and replace any sample number with one you can defend to that client. This guide does not publish a national per-word rate. A figure copied from a foreign job board ignores the budget in front of you.

| Path | What the client buys | What you watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid test | One page, one revision, a date | Scope that arrives in a chat after the draft |
| Open monthly retainer | "Write what we need" | Hours you did not cap, and a fee that never matches them |

A paid test is the cleaner first sale. A retainer can wait until you have the hours from the test written down.

**Illustrative example.** Suppose a local business asks for one service page and you assume a project fee of $200. That $200 is an assumption for the arithmetic, not a market rate. Suppose the work takes four hours, including one revision. The sketch is $200 ÷ 4 = $50 an hour before tax set-aside. Suppose you already have a computer, so gear is $0. A headset at $30, if you buy one for the call in the same week, makes the sketch $170. A platform fee is not in these figures. If you are registered for GST/HST, tax may sit on top of the $200, and that tax is not extra profit. These totals are assumptions, not a quote, a forecast, or a typical result.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first freelance writing client look like?

A 14-day plan for a first freelance writing client in Canada should produce a niche sentence, one sample, a one-page agreement, and a short list of real notes. A paid yes is a bonus. Silence in two weeks is a reason to improve the sample, not a reason to buy a list. The clauses for that one-page agreement are in [simple freelance contracts](/side-hustles/freelance-contract-canada/).

### Days 1 and 2: Fix the offer

Write the document and the client type in one sentence. List fifteen businesses with a page you can describe. Read [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright) far enough to know the agreement needs an ownership line.

### Days 3 to 5: Build the sample and the note

Draft 600 to 900 words. Cut any fact you cannot source. Label the file as a sample. Write the one-page agreement: deliverable, length, revision rounds, date, price, payment, and what happens if the client goes quiet.

### Days 6 to 9: Send a small batch

Send five notes that name a real gap. On day 8, send five more, or follow up once if someone asked a question. If the message is commercial email, follow the consent, identification, and unsubscribe points on ISED's [getting consent](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) page. Log every send.

### Days 10 to 14: Do the paid test or improve the sample

If someone says yes, send the agreement before you start, deliver inside the cap, and invoice the same day. If nobody has replied, edit the sample once and send a new batch only to businesses you have not already contacted. Do not add a second niche to escape the silence.

## What mistakes block a first freelance writing client in Canada?

Common mistakes that block a first freelance writing client in Canada are offering every format, inventing facts, and doing extra revision rounds because a no feels rude. One document type is enough until someone pays for a second.

For the copyright, GST/HST, and tax mistakes writers make most, and how to fix them, see the [freelance writing mistakes guide](/side-hustles/freelance-writing-side-hustle-canada/#what-to-watch-out-for).

A marketplace profile with no sample asks the client to imagine you. Unlimited tweaks turn a defined page into an open week. Scraping addresses, or emailing a purchased list, skips the consent rules on the anti-spam pages above.

Working for "exposure" with no cap is a gift. Call it a gift if you choose it, and cap the length. Do not describe it as a client.

## Which tax rules apply to a first freelance writing client in Canada?

Freelance writing income in Canada is self-employment income, including a deposit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST for freelance writing follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the agreement, the draft you were paid for, and the e-transfer. Report writing on Form T2125 with the T1. An agency may send a T4A. Your own record still matters. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add tutoring, virtual-assistant, and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Freelance writing is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Confirm Quebec and every other province on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside.
