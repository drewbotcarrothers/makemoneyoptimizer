<QuickAnswer>

- **Short answer:** a direct thermal printer that takes 4×6 labels is the standard setup once you ship more than a few parcels a week; it needs no ink or toner.
- **Pair it with:** a postal scale, 4×6 thermal labels, poly mailers or boxes, and tape.
- **Check before buying:** label width, connection (USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi), driver support for your computer, and whether your shipping platform prints 4×6.
- **Prices:** see current price; this guide covers features, not specific models.

</QuickAnswer>

A label printer is one of the first upgrades most resellers make: it turns printing, cutting and taping paper labels into peel-and-stick. ## What should you look for in a shipping label printer?

| Feature | What it means | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Print type | Direct thermal uses heat-sensitive labels; inkjet/laser use paper | Direct thermal: no ink or toner to buy |
| Label size | 4×6 inch is the common shipping label size | Make sure it handles 4×6 rolls or fan-fold stacks |
| Connection | USB, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi | USB is simplest; wireless helps if you print from a phone |
| Compatibility | Drivers for Windows, macOS or ChromeOS; works with your marketplace | Check your shipping platform's printer list |
| Label feeding | Internal roll holder vs external stack | External feeding accepts more label brands and sizes |
| Footprint | Desk space and weight | Small enough to leave set up |

## What should Canadian resellers check?

- **Your carrier's label format.** Canada Post, Chit Chats and Stallion Express all generate printable labels through their own systems; confirm 4×6 output in your account settings.
- **Weight accuracy.** Underpaid postage can lead to adjustments; weigh every parcel.
- **Deductions.** Printers, labels, scales and packaging used for your business are generally [deductible business expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses.html); larger equipment may be claimed over time.
- **GST/HST.** Once you pass $30,000 in taxable sales, see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).
