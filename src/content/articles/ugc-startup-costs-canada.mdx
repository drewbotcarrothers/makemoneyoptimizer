## How much does UGC creation cost to start in Canada?

UGC creation in Canada starts at $0 with a phone and items you own, about $40–$150 with basic gear, and about $150–$450 with more lighting, props, and software. No follower count is required, and no platform fee applies to a direct brand deal in this guide's scope.

| How you start | First-month cash, about | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $0 | The phone you already own, window light, the phone's editor, and three sample clips of products you already bought |
| Typical | $40–$150 | A phone tripod ($15–$40), a clip-on microphone ($20–$50), and a small LED light ($25–$80) |
| Fuller | $150–$450 | The typical kit plus a second light, two simple backdrops, props, and an optional paid editing or caption app |

## Who should use a UGC startup budget in Canada?

A UGC startup budget in Canada fits people who can deliver clean, on-brief files and negotiate written usage terms. The lean column fits a first portfolio. ## How do ongoing costs and break-even work for UGC creation in Canada?

UGC break-even in Canada is the fee per brief minus gear spread over jobs, props, and any app, divided by your hours. Usage rights, meaning where and how long the brand may run the file, change what a fair fee is, so write them before you price.

### Step 1: Film three sample clips with what you own

Products you already bought, filmed in good light, with a clear hook and a plain claim.

### Step 2: Fix the worst problem in the samples

Usually sound. Then light. Then framing.

### Step 3: Write usage terms on every quote

Where the file may run, for how long, and whether paid ads are included.

### Step 4: Refuse invented claims

The Competition Bureau's guidance on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) applies to advertising claims. Its [influencer marketing page](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says reviews and testimonials should reflect actual experience. A brief that asks you to say a product did something it did not do is one to decline.

### Step 5: Spread gear across jobs

A $50 light on one job is a big cost. On ten jobs it is small.

### Illustrative example

**A teaching sketch, not a quote, a forecast, or a typical UGC rate.**

Suppose a Canadian skincare brand agrees in writing to pay $200 for two 30-second clips with organic use on its own accounts for 3 months, an assumption, not a market rate. You buy a clip-on microphone for $25 and a small light for $40, charged against this first job: $65. The product is supplied by the brand. Remainder: $200 − $65 = $135 before a tax set-aside. Suppose the job takes 6 hours including scripting, filming, editing, and one revision: $135 ÷ 6 = $22.50 an hour inside these assumptions. A second job with the same gear starts at $200 before costs. These totals are assumptions. Do not copy $200.

## Where can UGC startup spending be lower in Canada?

UGC startup spending in Canada stays lower when samples use products you already own, light comes from a window, and the portfolio is a free shared folder or simple page. Skip paid UGC courses and platforms that charge to be seen until you have pitched brands directly.

## Where should UGC spending not be cut in Canada?

UGC spending in Canada should not cut clear audio, a written agreement with usage terms, or backups of delivered files. A brand that cannot hear the hook will not rebook. A file with no usage terms can run in ads for years with no extra fee.

## Which tax rules apply to a UGC budget in Canada?

UGC income in Canada is self-employment income when you are carrying on the activity for profit, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution, both portions, on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) first-dollar rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep signed briefs, usage terms, invoices, and gear receipts. Report the activity on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. UGC work is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Supplies, platform fees, and subscriptions used for the business are generally current expenses. Equipment that lasts for years, such as a computer or a camera, may be [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion rather than a one-year deduction. If you are registered, GST/HST you paid on business purchases can often be recovered as an [input tax credit](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) to the extent of commercial use, with receipts that show the tax. If you are not registered, you do not charge GST/HST and you do not claim the credit.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made, unless a platform rule you have read says the platform collects. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Use the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). For a set-aside habit, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle in Canada](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

Products a brand sends you may have tax consequences depending on the arrangement. Ask a tax adviser if you receive products as payment.
