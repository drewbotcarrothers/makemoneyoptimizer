<QuickAnswer>

- **Short answer:** start with what you own and learn what sells; upgrade to an interchangeable-lens camera when agency feedback or your own files show noise, softness or limited low light.
- **Biggest upgrades:** a sharp lens and a sturdy tripod usually matter more than the camera body.
- **Do not skip:** fast storage cards, a second backup drive and model/property releases.
- **Prices:** see current price; this guide is about what to look for, not specific models.

</QuickAnswer>

Stock agencies review files for focus, noise, exposure and legal releases, so gear is mostly about delivering clean, sharp files consistently. This guide lists what to look for, with links to browse on Amazon.ca. The products were not tested by this site. ## What should you look for in a stock photography camera?

| Feature | What it means | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sensor size | Larger sensors usually handle low light with less noise | APS-C or full frame for a step up from a phone |
| Resolution | Megapixels | Enough to meet agency minimums with room to crop |
| Interchangeable lenses | Swap lenses for different subjects | A mount with affordable used lenses in Canada |
| Video specs | Resolution and frame rates | If you plan to sell stock footage, check 4K and log profiles |
| Weather sealing | Resistance to moisture and dust | Useful for Canadian winters and outdoor shoots |
| Battery life | Shots per charge | Cold drains batteries; carry spares |

## What should Canadian stock contributors know?

- **Releases.** Recognizable people and many private properties need signed releases before agencies accept commercial use.
- **Backups.** Keep two copies, one off-site or in the cloud.
- **Cold-weather shooting.** Let gear warm up slowly in a bag to limit condensation.
- **Taxes.** Cameras and lenses used for business are generally claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html); smaller supplies are [business expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses.html). Agency payouts are often in USD; see [USD platform income tax](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).
- **Used gear.** Check items on [Recalls and safety alerts](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en), especially batteries and chargers.
