## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Halifax, Canada?

### Step 1: Clear private property, not sidewalks

Halifax's [snow clearing page](https://www.halifax.ca/transportation/winter-operations/snow-clearing) sets sidewalk timelines by priority. Main arterials and downtown are cleared within 12 hours of the end of a weather event, transit routes within 18 hours, and residential streets and walkways within 36 hours. Storms over 30 cm take longer. Because the municipality handles sidewalks, sell driveways, steps, walkways and the plow windrow. Use [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) for rates and the [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/) to write terms that cover big storms.

### Step 2: Sell at markets with the right licence

Halifax's [licences page](https://www.halifax.ca/business/licences) explains how to get a vendor's licence to sell food or handmade crafts in the municipality. Before you book a street spot or pop-up, check whether your setup needs one. Indoor markets run by an organizer may handle permits differently, so ask. The [holiday market vendor guide](/side-hustles/holiday-market-vendor-guide-canada/) and [home baking guide](/side-hustles/home-baking-side-hustle-canada/) cover the selling side.

### Step 3: Build a home services list

[House cleaning](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/), [dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) and [handyman work](/side-hustles/handyman-side-hustle-canada/) don't depend on the season. A client list in one or two neighbourhoods keeps travel time down. Use [house cleaning first clients](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-first-clients-canada/) to land the first few.

## What seasons drive Halifax side hustle income?

- **December to March:** private snow clearing and [winter pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/).
- **April to June:** spring cleanup, [window cleaning](/side-hustles/window-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) and [pressure washing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-side-hustle-canada/).
- **Summer:** [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/), [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/) and market vending.
- **Fall:** [moving help](/side-hustles/moving-help-side-hustle-canada/) around the school year, then holiday markets.

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Halifax, Canada?

Halifax licenses specific activities rather than every business. Its [licences page](https://www.halifax.ca/business/licences) covers vendors and sidewalk cafes, among others. Most mobile home services aren't on that list, but zoning rules for home-based businesses still apply, so ask the municipality if clients will come to your home. If you use a business name other than your own, Nova Scotia requires you to [register a sole proprietorship](https://novascotia.ca/register-sole-proprietorship). See [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you offer driveway and walkway clearing on a residential street. The municipality may take up to 36 hours to clear that street's sidewalk. Your service covers the driveway, steps and path to the door. Your terms would say when you arrive after a storm and how storms over 30 cm are handled. In summer, the same clients could book lawn care or window cleaning.

## What mistakes do Halifax side hustlers make?

- **Selling sidewalk clearing the municipality already does.** Focus on private property.
- **Vending without a licence.** Food and handmade craft vendors in public spaces need one.
- **Quoting HST at the old rate.** Nova Scotia's HST is 14% since April 1, 2025.
- **Promising fixed times in big storms.** Write a storm clause into your terms.

## How are Halifax side hustles taxed?

You report self-employment income on Form T2125. You register for HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers register from their first fare. The [CRA rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) lists Nova Scotia's HST at 14% from April 1, 2025. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/), and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/).
