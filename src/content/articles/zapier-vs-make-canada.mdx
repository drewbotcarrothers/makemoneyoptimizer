## Is Zapier or Make cheaper?

On the free tier, Make gives more room: 1,000 credits a month against Zapier's 100 tasks. Paid plans depend on volume. Zapier Professional starts from $19.99 a month at its lowest task tier, and Make's paid prices are on its pricing page. Model your expected monthly runs before choosing.

### Illustrative example: pricing an automation for a client

Assumed numbers, not a quote: you build a lead-intake automation for a local business.

- Setup: 4 hours × $75 an hour = $300.
- Monthly care plan: 1 hour × $75 = $75, plus the client pays their own Zapier or Make plan.
- Keeping the subscription in the client's name avoids you carrying their software cost.

Rates are assumptions; see [Zapier automation pricing](/side-hustles/zapier-automation-pricing-canada/) for how Canadian freelancers set them.

## What automation mistakes do freelancers make?

- **Building in your own account.** Build in the client's account so they own it.
- **No error alerts.** Turn on failure notifications.
- **Ignoring task or credit burn.** A loop can use a month's allowance quickly.
- **Moving personal data carelessly.** Know what client data flows where; Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) applies to many businesses.
- **No documentation.** Leave a one-page map of every automation.
