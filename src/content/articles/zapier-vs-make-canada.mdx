### How the billing units differ

- **Zapier task:** one successful action step. Triggers, polling and Zapier's built-in tools (Formatter, Filter, Paths, Delay, Looping, Digest, Storage, Tables, Forms) cost nothing. Failed steps are not counted.
- **Make credit:** one module action, including reads, searches and built-in transformations. Most actions use 1 credit; some AI features use more. Routers and error handlers are free.

So a "new form entry → add to CRM → send Slack message → create invoice draft" flow uses **3 tasks** on Zapier (the trigger is free) and roughly **4 credits** on Make (the trigger module reads data). Count your flows this way before comparing price tags.

## What does Make cost?

Make's pricing page (October 14, 2026) confirms a **Free plan with 1,000 credits a month**, 1 MB of data storage and one data store. Paid plans are **Core, Pro and Teams**, plus Enterprise. Their prices scale with the credit volume you pick and were not shown in the page text when checked; the live figures are on [Make's pricing page](https://www.make.com/en/pricing). Rules that are published there:

- Extra credits are sold in bundles of 1,000 or 10,000 at a price set by your plan, and Core, Pro and Teams can auto-buy 10,000 credits before you run out.
- Credits expire at the end of the term (monthly or annual).
- Annual prepayment is cheaper than monthly.
- Organisations under 20 employees can apply for a Start-up Plan at 50% off Business.
- If you run out, scenarios stop; incoming webhooks queue up to your storage limit and run once credits are added.

## What would each cost at three usage levels?

**Illustrative example.** Assumed: each automation run uses 3 Zapier tasks or 4 Make credits (the CRM flow above). Sourced: Zapier tier prices and the Make Free allowance.

| Usage level | Runs per month | Zapier tasks needed | Cheapest Zapier fit (annual) | Make credits needed | Make fit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light (your own admin) | 30 | 90 | Free (but two-step only) or Pro 750 at ≈ C$28.43/mo | 120 | Free |
| Medium (one small client) | 200 | 600 | Pro 750 at ≈ C$28.43/mo | 800 | Free, if it fits 1,000 credits and the free limits |
| Heavy (agency, several clients) | 1,500 | 4,500 | Pro 5,000 at ≈ C$126.58/mo | 6,000 | Paid Core or Pro tier; check the 10,000-credit price |

Math: runs × steps = units. 200 × 3 = 600 tasks (fits 750); 1,500 × 3 = 4,500 tasks (fits 5,000). Zapier's Free tier would cover 90 tasks, but this flow has three actions, and Free allows only one trigger and one action, so a light user with multi-step needs still lands on Pro or on Make Free.

## How do you price an automation for a client?

**Illustrative example.** Assumed numbers, not a quote: you build a lead-intake automation for a local business.

- Setup: 4 hours × $75 an hour = $300.
- Monthly care plan: 1 hour × $75 = $75.
- The client pays their own Zapier Pro 750 plan (≈ C$28.43 a month billed annually, October 14, 2026) or Make Free.
- Your first-year revenue: $300 + (12 × $75) = $1,200. Your software cost: $0, because the subscription is in the client's name.

Rates are assumptions; [Zapier automation pricing](/side-hustles/zapier-automation-pricing-canada/) covers how Canadian freelancers set them.

### A copyable discovery checklist

```text
1. Which tools do you already pay for (CRM, email, invoicing, calendar)?
2. Which task do you repeat more than 5 times a week?
3. How many times a month does it happen? (sets the task/credit tier)
4. Who should get an alert when something fails?
5. What personal information moves between tools?
6. Whose account will own the automation and the subscription?
```

## What automation mistakes do freelancers make?

- **Building in your own account.** Build in the client's account so they own the work and the bill.
- **No error alerts.** Set failure notifications; Zapier's paid plans add autoreplay and per-Zap error settings.
- **Ignoring task or credit burn.** A loop can eat a month's allowance; Make scenarios stop when credits run out.
- **Paying monthly by default.** Zapier's monthly prices are 50% higher than annual ones for the same tier.
- **Moving personal data carelessly.** Many Canadian businesses fall under [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/); know what client data flows where.
- **No documentation.** Leave a one-page map of every automation.

## How are automation tools and income taxed in Canada?

Software you use to earn business income is generally a deductible expense on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and fees billed in US dollars are converted to Canadian dollars for your records. Income from setup and care plans is self-employment income; see [how to report side hustle income to CRA](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/), [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and [USD platform income](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).
