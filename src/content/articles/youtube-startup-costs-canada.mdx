**Quick answer:** a lean YouTube channel in Canada costs **$53.86**: a BOYA BY-M1 clip-on microphone and a UBeesize phone tripod from Amazon.ca with 13% Ontario HST, filmed on the phone you own and edited in free DaVinci Resolve. A desk setup with a DJI Mic Mini, a Logitech Litra Glow light and a 60-inch tripod costs **$194.96**, or **$355.42** for the first year with Canva Pro. A pro kit with a two-transmitter mic, a key light, studio headphones and a USB voiceover mic costs **$475.50**, and about **$1,136** for the year once Canva and a starting creator insurance policy are added. Gear prices are Amazon.ca listings checked October 9, 2026. YouTube charges nothing to upload or to join the Partner Program; at the 300-channel median ad rate of US$2.30 per 1,000 views, the lean kit pays back after about 16,400 monetized views, and the desk first year after about 108,500.

How to pick a niche, read RPM by topic and plan the first 90 days is in the [YouTube side hustle guide](/side-hustles/youtube-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash: what each item cost on a specific date, what the software and insurance cost, and how many views or sponsorships recover it.

## How much does a YouTube channel cost to start in Canada?

A YouTube channel costs $53.86 to start on the lean kit, $194.96 on the desk kit and $475.50 on the pro kit, with 13% Ontario HST. Across the first year the desk setup is $355.42 and the pro setup about $1,136. All gear prices are Amazon.ca listings on October 9, 2026.

| Setup | Gear before tax | Gear with 13% HST | Gear with 5% GST | Software, first year | Insurance (per year) | First-year cash |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $47.66 | $53.86 | $50.04 | $0 (DaVinci Resolve, free Canva) | None | $53.86 |
| Desk | $172.53 | $194.96 | $181.16 | $160.46 (Canva Pro $142 + HST) | None | $355.42 |
| Pro | $420.80 | $475.50 | $441.84 | $160.46 (Canva Pro) | About $500 | $1,135.96 |

The lean kit assumes you already have a phone and a computer. Alberta and the territories pay 5% GST only; the [CRA rate page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) lists every province. The pro first year is $475.50 + $160.46 + $500 = $1,135.96.

The two numbers that matter more than the gear are the Partner Program thresholds and the hours. The gear is the small number. The large costs are the hours each video takes to plan, film and edit, and the months before any of it pays.

## Who should use a YouTube startup budget in Canada?

This budget fits people who can explain one subject again and again, publish on a schedule for at least six months before ad money arrives, and edit a rough cut. It suits teachers, tradespeople, hobbyists and anyone with a skill viewers search for, especially in education and how-to topics, where ad rates are highest.

It is a poor fit if you need income from the next invoice, if a quiet month would make you stop, or if the plan is a camera bought on credit against views that do not exist yet. Paid work this month is faster through [online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-startup-costs-canada/) or managing a business's accounts as a [social media manager](/side-hustles/social-media-manager-startup-costs-canada/). Short-form-first creators should read [TikTok and Reels startup costs](/side-hustles/tiktok-reels-startup-costs-canada/) alongside this page, because YouTube's Shorts revenue rules change in February 2027.

## What does YouTube charge, and when does it start paying in Canada?

YouTube charges nothing to upload, to apply for the Partner Program, or to earn from it. It takes its share from the revenue instead. These rules were on YouTube's help pages and blog on October 9, 2026:

| Tier | What you need | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expanded Partner Program (Canada is eligible) | 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3 million Shorts views in 90 days | Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, Jewels, Shopping |
| Full Partner Program (until January 31, 2027) | 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days | Ad revenue and YouTube Premium revenue |
| Full Partner Program (new applicants from February 1, 2027) | 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days | Ad revenue and Premium revenue; fan-funding thresholds unchanged |

Sources: the [Partner Program overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en), the [expanded Partner Program page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en), which lists Canada, and YouTube's [August 10, 2026 announcement](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/). The announcement says the higher entry bar does not affect channels already in the program, and that from February 1, 2027 only channels with 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days share in Shorts ad and subscription revenue; others stay in the program and keep earning on long-form videos.

Once accepted, YouTube's [earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902) says partners keep **55%** of net ad revenue on long-form videos and **45%** of the revenue allocated to them from the Shorts pool. Meeting a threshold starts a review that YouTube says typically takes about a month, and channels that have not uploaded or posted for 6 months or more can lose monetization. Watch hours from Shorts in the Shorts feed do not count toward the long-form watch-hour threshold.

For a budget, that means two things. The lean kit is the right size for the period before any threshold. And a channel starting now that will not reach 4,000 hours before February 1, 2027 should plan for 8,000.

### What is deliberately not in any kit

- **A camera body.** A recent phone films in 4K. Buy a camera when the phone's limits, such as low light or background blur, are a problem you have seen in your own footage.
- **Paid music.** YouTube's free Audio Library in YouTube Studio covers background music at no cost. Using commercial music without a licence is how videos lose monetization.
- **A course.** YouTube's own Creator resources at youtube.com/creators are free and are linked from the Partner Program page.

## How much is creator insurance in Canada?

[Zensurance](https://www.zensurance.com/media-and-marketing-insurance/social-media-influencer) says a social media influencer with a small following (100,000 followers) can expect a starting cost of approximately **$500 a year** for a policy package with a $1,000,000 commercial general liability limit. The price depends on experience, the services and products you provide, revenue and employees. The pro first-year total uses $500.

A channel that films in your own home office about a hobby has little liability exposure. The risk rises when you film in clients' homes or businesses, demonstrate tools or food that could injure someone, run sponsored content with claims a brand relies on, or use other people's footage. A home insurance policy may not cover business activity, so ask your insurer. Quote routes are compared in [side hustle insurance in Canada](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).

## How do break-even and ongoing costs work for YouTube in Canada?

Ad revenue is views ÷ 1,000 × RPM, where RPM is what you earn per 1,000 views after YouTube's share. The best public dataset, AIR Media-Tech's [2026 study](https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/how-much-does-youtube-really-pay-in-2026-real-rpm-data-from-300-channels) of 300 monetized channels (3,595 channel-months, May 2025 to May 2026, read from YouTube Studio), puts the median RPM at **US$2.30**, from US$0.33 for kids' content to US$10.22 for education and science. Its sample runs from 10,000 to 50 million subscribers, so a brand-new channel is outside it.

### Illustrative example: views to pay back each kit

**Sourced inputs:** the US$2.30 median and US$10.22 education RPM, and the Bank of Canada rate of 1.4240 CAD per USD on October 8, 2026. **Assumed input:** every view counted is on a monetized long-form video after the channel is accepted into the Partner Program.

- Median RPM in CAD: 2.30 × 1.4240 = **C$3.28** per 1,000 views
- Education RPM in CAD: 10.22 × 1.4240 = **C$14.55** per 1,000 views

| Setup | First-year cash | Views at C$3.28 (median) | Views at C$14.55 (education) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $53.86 | 53.86 ÷ 3.28 × 1,000 ≈ 16,400 | ≈ 3,700 |
| Desk | $355.42 | ≈ 108,500 | ≈ 24,400 |
| Pro | $1,135.96 | ≈ 346,800 | ≈ 78,100 |

The views only count after acceptance, and reaching acceptance is its own bar. **Assumed input:** an average view lasts 6 minutes. 4,000 watch hours is 240,000 minutes, or 240,000 ÷ 6 = **40,000 views** of long-form video in 12 months. Under the February 2027 rule, 8,000 hours is **80,000 views**. A channel that needs 80,000 views to qualify and another 108,500 monetized views to pay back the desk year has to be a plan you would run for a year with no ad income.

### Illustrative example: one sponsorship instead

**Assumed input:** a local business pays $150 to mention its offer in one video. That fee is an assumption, not a market rate. The desk first year needs $355.42 ÷ $150 = 2.37, so **3 sponsorships**; the lean kit is covered by the first one. Sponsorships, memberships and your own products often arrive before ad revenue, and they do not need the 1,000-subscriber bar. Rate cards and how to price an integration are in [YouTube sponsorship pricing in Canada](/side-hustles/youtube-pricing-canada/).

## What could change these YouTube numbers?

Every figure here has a date and most of them move. YouTube's Partner Program changes on February 1, 2027 for new applicants and for Shorts revenue; check the [overview page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en) when you apply. RPM varies three to eight times within a niche in AIR's data, swings by season (AIR's median fell to US$1.56 in January and peaked at US$2.35 in November) and depends on where viewers live. ## Where can YouTube startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Film on the phone.** It already shoots in 4K; the camera body is the most common early overspend.
- **Buy the mic first, then the light.** A $21.67 lav fixes more videos than a $99.99 light.
- **Use a window.** Daylight is free and flattering; buy a light only if you film after dark.
- **Edit in free DaVinci Resolve.** The free version handles 4K at 60 fps; Studio's C$445 is for later.
- **Stay on Canva Free until thumbnails slow you down.** Canva Pro's $142 a year is the first upgrade worth paying for.
- **Use the Audio Library in YouTube Studio.** Paid music subscriptions can wait until the channel earns.

## Where should YouTube spending not be cut in Canada?

- **Clear audio.** A noisy room is the one problem viewers will not sit through.
- **Rights to everything in the video.** Music, clips and images you do not have rights to can cost you monetization regardless of subscriber count; YouTube's overview page says channels lose monetization when they break its monetization policies.
- **A publishing habit.** YouTube may turn off monetization on channels inactive for 6 months or more. A cheap kit you use every week beats an expensive one you use twice.
- **Insurance once you film on someone else's premises** or demonstrate anything that could injure a viewer who copies you.
- **Separate money.** A separate account for AdSense and sponsor payments makes the T2125 easier. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian option; when a sponsor pays by invoice, <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> keeps those records in one place.

## Which tax rules apply to a YouTube budget in Canada?

YouTube income in Canada, from AdSense, sponsors or memberships, is business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay both shares of [Canada Pension Plan contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. Microphones, lights and software subscriptions are generally deductible; equipment that lasts several years, such as a camera or computer, is usually claimed through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business-use portion.

You register for GST/HST once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000 in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), counting every side hustle. A YouTube channel is not covered by the [taxi and ride-sharing rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) that requires registration from the first dollar. How GST/HST applies to payments from a foreign platform is covered on the CRA [digital economy](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) pages; a sponsor invoice to a Canadian business is a different supply from an AdSense payout. Once registered, claim [input tax credits](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/calculate-prepare-report/input-tax-credit.html) on the HST in the kits above and charge the [provincial rate](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) on your invoices. The US tax form in AdSense is covered in the [YouTube side hustle guide](/side-hustles/youtube-side-hustle-canada/). This is not tax, legal or insurance advice.
