Starting dog walking in Canada, meaning paid walks for other people's dogs, usually a 30-minute midday walk sold by the walk or as a weekly package, costs less than most side hustles. A leash, bags and a treat pouch are the whole lean kit. The costs that matter more arrive with the route: winter traction and a light for 4:30 p.m. sunsets, liability insurance for direct clients, and, in Toronto, a permit if you take more than three dogs into a city park. Amazon.ca prices below were checked on October 9, 2026; insurance, platform and permit figures are from each company's or city's page the same day.

How to find the first owners and run a meet-and-greet is the [dog walking side hustle guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/). This page is the cash. Choosing each item is in [dog walking gear in Canada](/side-hustles/dog-walking-gear-canada/).

## How much does dog walking cost to start in Canada?

Dog walking in Canada costs about $69.27 for a lean kit, $146.26 for a winter-ready kit, $246.26 with a starting liability policy, or $675.44 for a Toronto group walker with the City's permit. Goods include 13% Ontario HST; insurance is added without HST. Use your province's rate.

| Kit | What's in it | Total |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $0 start | Walk on Rover using the owner's leash and harness, bags you already have | $0 plus Rover's $49 profile review fee if you list there |
| Lean | PetSafe 6 ft nylon leash ($10.79) + lynxking 6 ft slip lead ($15.49) + Amazon Basics 300 bags with dispenser ($15.03) + Kurgo treat pouch ($19.99) | $69.27 with HST |
| Winter-ready | Lean + Chiwo reflective vest 2-pack ($12.59) + ICETRAX V3 tungsten ice grips ($23.59) + Black Diamond Astro 300 headlamp ($31.95) | $146.26 with HST |
| Winter-ready with insurance | Winter-ready + Zensurance's example of about $100 a year for $1 million general and $1 million professional liability | about $246.26 |
| Toronto group walker | Winter-ready with insurance + SparklyPets hands-free leash ($23.95) + Master Lock 5422D portable key safe ($49.87) + Commercial Dog Walker Permit ($305.99 + HST = $345.77) | about $675.44 |

The Toronto permit needs $2 million of liability insurance, double the limit in Zensurance's $100 example, so expect a higher quote if you walk groups. Treat the group column as a floor.

## Who should use a dog walking startup budget in Canada?

A dog walking budget fits people who like dogs more than they mind weather, can be at the same door at the same time every weekday, and will say no to a dog they can't control. The lean kit fits a few one-dog walks booked through an app. The winter-ready kit fits a daily route from November to March. The group column fits an experienced walker in Toronto with dogs who already walk well together.

Group walks add gear and liability. They're a later upgrade after the same two dogs have gone well for a month. ## What do Rover, insurance and permits cost dog walkers in Canada?

As listed on each company's or city's page on October 9, 2026:

- **Rover.** Its Canadian help centre says new sitter profiles pay a [$49 profile review fee](https://support-ca.rover.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360036579152-Is-Rover-free) (which may vary by location), and Rover keeps a [20% service fee](https://support-ca.rover.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360037446911-What-are-the-service-fees) on each booking. Sitters in or near Saskatoon, Regina, Québec, Nanaimo, Calgary, Vancouver and Ottawa-Gatineau are in a [pilot](https://support-ca.rover.com/hc/en-ca/articles/46452793506836-New-ways-for-sitters-to-earn-more-with-repeat-clients) with tiered fees per client: 30% up to $499 of that client's bookings, 15% from $500 to $999, and 10% from $1,000.
- **Rover Guarantee.** Rover's [Canadian guarantee terms](https://www.rover.com/ca-en/terms/guarantee/) say it isn't insurance. It covers eligible vet expenses up to $25,000 per incident, with a $250 minimum contribution, on bookings paid through Rover, and says sitters remain responsible for carrying insurance that meets legal requirements.
- **Insurance for direct clients.** Zensurance's [pet services insurance page](https://www.zensurance.com/pet-services-insurance) says a dog walker can anticipate spending about $100 a year for $1 million of commercial general liability and $1 million of professional liability. Premiums vary with training, services and revenue. That's an example, not a quote. Ask whether a home policy covers paid dog walking; most don't. Compare options in [APOLLO vs Zensurance vs a local broker](/side-hustles/apollo-vs-zensurance-side-hustle-insurance-canada/) and the [side hustle insurance guide](/side-hustles/side-hustle-insurance-canada/).
- **Toronto permit.** The City's [Commercial Dog Walker Permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) is required to control more than three dogs, up to six, in a city park. From January 1, 2026 it costs $305.99 a year, $153.00 for six months or $76.50 for three months, before tax, and requires $2 million of liability insurance.

**Correcting an outdated line on this page.** An earlier version of this guide said it wouldn't quote a platform fee. Rover's Canadian help centre publishes its fees, so they're listed above with a date. A $25 walk on Rover pays you $20 at the standard 20%.

The full platform comparison is [Rover vs independent dog walking](/side-hustles/rover-vs-independent-dog-walking-canada/).

## How long does it take to break even?

**Illustrative. The $20.73 walk price is Rover's published Toronto average for a 30-minute walk (September 2025 data), cited in the [dog walking pricing guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-pricing-canada/). The Rover column subtracts the 20% standard fee ($16.58 net) and doesn't include the one-time $49 profile fee. Each figure is the kit cost divided by the net walk price, rounded up. These aren't typical earnings. Verify the arithmetic.**

| Kit | Cost to cover | Direct 30-minute walks at $20.73 | Rover walks at $16.58 net |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean | $69.27 | 4 | 5 |
| Winter-ready with insurance | $246.26 | 12 | 15 |
| Toronto group walker | $675.44 | 33 | 41 |

For example, $246.26 ÷ $20.73 = 11.88, so 12 walks. A group outing of four dogs at an assumed $22 each brings $88 for the hour, as in the [pillar guide's example](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/), so the Toronto group kit is covered by about 8 group hours ($675.44 ÷ $88 = 7.68).

### A first-week sketch

**Illustrative example, not a forecast or a survey of Canadian walk prices.** Suppose you buy the lean kit for $69.27 and land two direct clients at an assumed $22 a walk, five weekdays each, for ten walks in the week. Fees = $220. Remainder after the kit = $220 − $69.27 = $150.73 before tax and any insurance. If each walk takes 40 minutes including the door and the bags, that's about 6.7 hours, or about $22.60 an hour in week one and $33 an hour once the kit is paid. A cancelled week and a sick dog are part of the real number. Don't copy $22.

## What are the ongoing costs?

- **Bags,** the one supply you'll rebuy most. 300 bags for $15.03 is about 5 cents a bag.
- **Insurance,** from about $100 a year for $1 million limits; more for Toronto's $2 million requirement.
- **Permit renewals** where a city requires one.
- **Platform fees,** 20% of each Rover booking, or the pilot tiers.
- **Footwear and outerwear** wear out faster on a daily route.
- **Transit or fuel** if clients aren't within walking distance of each other. Driving a dog is a separate insurance question.

**Payments.** If clients pay by e-transfer, there's usually no fee. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can send recurring invoices once you have regulars. Keep fees in a separate account; <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian option.

## Where can dog walking startup spending be lower in Canada?

- **Use the owner's leash and harness** and carry only a backup.
- **Start on Rover** for the first clients, where the Rover Guarantee covers eligible vet costs on Rover bookings, then buy insurance before going direct.
- **Wait on the headlamp and grips** until October. A summer start can push them back.
- **Skip the group-walk gear and permit** until you have dogs who walk well together.

## Where shouldn't you cut dog walking spending in Canada?

Don't cut the backup lead, enough bags, winter traction, or insurance before direct clients. A dog that slips its collar on a busy street costs more than any lead. A fall on ice ends a route. Don't walk more dogs than the city allows, and don't take a dog you can't hold.

## Which tax rules apply to a dog walking budget in Canada?

Dog walking fees in Canada are generally self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole Canada Pension Plan contribution on the net, as described under [Contributions to the Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html). Waste bags, treats you paid for, and a small leash are ordinary expenses on that form, while lasting equipment is recovered through [capital cost allowance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance.html) on the business portion. GST/HST for dog walking uses the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule, and Nova Scotia’s HST is 14% on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table.

This is not tax, legal, veterinary, or insurance advice.

Keep receipts whether fees arrive by e-transfer or an app. An app may send a T4A. Your spreadsheet still matters. If you claim a car, keep a kilometre log: date, destination, kilometres, purpose. Claim the business portion only. Confirm capital cost allowance treatment for the year you file. This guide does not explain input tax credits. If you register, confirm those rules with the CRA or a tax professional. If you are not registered, do not add GST/HST to the walk fee.

The small-supplier line is $30,000 of worldwide taxable supplies — you and associates — in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Under that test, registration is optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA’s when-to-register page. Confirm the rate for the province where the walk happens, including Quebec, on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html).

Search the city for a business licence, and read a building’s visitor rules before you promise a lobby full of dogs. This guide does not state a national licence fee. Leash and stoop-and-scoop bylaws are on the city’s own pages. Follow the ones posted for your route.
