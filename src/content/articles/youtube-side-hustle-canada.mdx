A YouTube side hustle in Canada means publishing videos on one topic on a schedule you can keep, growing to YouTube's Partner Program thresholds, and then earning from ads, memberships, sponsorships, affiliate links and your own products. A realistic starter kit costs about $48 to $173 on Amazon.ca before tax. Across 300 monetized channels, the median ad payout was about US$2.30 per 1,000 long-form views. It suits people who can explain one subject well and keep publishing for months before the first payment arrives.

<QuickAnswer>

You can apply at 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days and 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views), which unlocks memberships and Super Thanks. Ad revenue starts at 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 watch hours in 12 months, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
- **Pay rates:** YouTube pays you 55% of long-form watch-page ad revenue, 45% of your Shorts pool allocation and 70% of memberships and Super Chat. AIR Media-Tech's 300-channel study (May 2025 to May 2026) found a median RPM of US$2.30, from US$0.33 (kids) to US$10.22 (education).

## How does YouTube pay creators in Canada?

YouTube pays Canadian creators through AdSense for YouTube after they join the YouTube Partner Program (YPP). Your share is 55% of net ad revenue on long-form videos, 45% of the Shorts revenue allocated to you, and 70% of net revenue from memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers and Super Thanks.

Those percentages come from YouTube's [partner earnings overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902?hl=en), which ties each rate to a module you accept in Studio:

| Module you accept | What it pays | Your share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watch Page Monetization | Ads on public long-form videos, including embeds | 55% of net revenue |
| Shorts Monetization | Shorts Feed ads, paid from a creator pool by your share of views | 45% of your allocation |
| Commerce Product | Channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks | 70% of net revenue |

Two metrics decide what lands in your account. YouTube's [ad revenue analytics page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314357?hl=en) defines **CPM** as what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions *before* YouTube's share, and **RPM** as your total revenue per 1,000 views *after* YouTube's share, counting views that showed no ad. RPM is the number to plan with. RPM is always lower than CPM.

The payment schedule is fixed. Finalized earnings for a month post to AdSense between the 7th and 12th of the next month and pay out between the 21st and 26th, once your balance passes the threshold. For a CAD account that threshold is **C$100**, per Google's [payment thresholds table](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1709871?hl=en).

Video length matters. Mid-roll ads can be turned on for monetized videos that are **8 minutes or longer** ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006?hl=en)), so a 9-minute explainer can carry more ads than a 6-minute one.

## What are the YouTube Partner Program requirements in Canada in 2026?

Canada is on YouTube's list for the expanded Partner Program. A Canadian channel can apply at 500 subscribers for fan funding and Shopping, then earn ad revenue at 1,000 subscribers with 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views.

| Tier | Subscribers | Plus one of | Plus | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expanded YPP (Canada eligible) | 500 | 3,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months, **or** 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days | 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days | Memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks, Jewels, Shopping |
| Full YPP | 1,000 | 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months, **or** 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days | n/a | Ad revenue sharing and YouTube Premium revenue |

Sources: [YPP overview & eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en) and [expanded YPP overview](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240?hl=en), both read October 9, 2026.

The fine print also comes from those two pages:

- **Before you apply:** follow the channel monetization policies, have no active Community Guidelines strikes, turn on 2-Step Verification, have advanced features access, and link one AdSense for YouTube account (create it inside Studio).
- **What counts:** only public long-form videos count toward watch hours. Private, unlisted, deleted and ad-campaign views don't count, and Shorts-feed watch hours don't count toward the 4,000.
- **Review:** meeting a threshold queues a human and automated review, typically about 1 month. If you're rejected, you can appeal within 21 days or reapply after 30 days, and after 90 days for later rejections.
- **Stay active:** YouTube may turn off monetization on channels that haven't uploaded or posted in 6 months.
- **Coming change:** YouTube is introducing YPP updates effective **February 1, 2027**. To keep full monetization, review and accept the updated terms in YouTube Studio by **January 31, 2027**.

Click **Get notified** under Earn in YouTube Studio and YouTube will email you when you qualify.

### Why Canadian viewers pay less than American viewers

Advertisers pay by audience country. AIR's [country CPM table](https://air.io/en/monetization/how-much-does-youtube-pay-for-1-million-views) (3,000+ channels, published June 2025) lists **Canada at US$9.93** per 1,000 ad impressions. The figure for the **United States is US$14.67**, Australia US$13.30 and the United Kingdom US$8.91. A channel that mostly reaches Canadians earns less per view than an identical channel that mostly reaches Americans. Canadian creators who cover cross-border topics, such as tools, software and hobbies, often pick up US viewers as well. The audience report in YouTube Analytics shows your split.

### What else moves your RPM

- **Season:** AIR's median RPM fell to US$1.56 in January and peaked at US$2.35 in November. Business & Finance saw the biggest Q4 lift, about 60%.
- **Share of views with an ad:** the median channel monetized only 53% of its views. Education monetized 77% and Gadgets & Tech 34%.
- **Format:** Shorts earned 3–14% of long-form RPM in AIR's 274-channel Shorts study. Most channels need about 7,000–33,000 Shorts views to match what 1,000 long-form views earn.
- **Mid-rolls and length:** videos of 8 minutes or more can carry mid-roll ads, and AIR's median monetized view carried 1.55 ads.

## What could a Canadian YouTube channel earn? (worked math)

The formula is **ad revenue = views ÷ 1,000 × RPM**. Convert USD to CAD at the Bank of Canada rate. The [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) was **1.4240 CAD per USD on October 8, 2026**.

**Illustrative example.** The view count is an assumption. The RPMs are the sourced figures above.

| Monthly long-form views (assumed) | RPM input (source) | Ad revenue | In CAD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20,000 | US$2.30, all-niche median (AIR) | 20 × 2.30 = US$46.00 | US$46.00 × 1.4240 = **C$65.50** |
| 20,000 | US$2.05, gaming median (AIR) | 20 × 2.05 = US$41.00 | **C$58.38** |
| 20,000 | US$10.22, education median (AIR) | 20 × 10.22 = US$204.40 | **C$291.07** |
| 20,000 | CA$14, Griffin Milks' finance RPM (Business Insider) | 20 × 14 = **C$280.00** | already CAD |
| 100,000 | US$2.30, all-niche median | 100 × 2.30 = US$230.00 | **C$327.52** |

At the median, ad revenue alone won't replace a paycheque until views reach the hundreds of thousands a month. That's why the income streams later in this guide matter as much as AdSense does.

**Illustrative example: how many views to reach 4,000 watch hours?** 4,000 hours × 60 = 240,000 minutes. If your average view lasts 4 minutes (assumed), you need 240,000 ÷ 4 = **60,000 long-form views in 12 months**, about 5,000 a month. At a 6-minute average view, you need 40,000. Raising average view duration is the fastest lever on the threshold.

**Illustrative example: the Shorts route.** 10,000,000 Shorts views ÷ 90 days = about **111,000 Shorts views a day** for three months. Most part-time channels reach the long-form route first.

## What does a 90-day YouTube launch plan look like?

A 90-day plan for a part-time Canadian channel publishes one long-form video a week, and one Short for every two or three long-form uploads. That ratio produced the best subscriber and revenue outcomes in AIR's 274-channel Shorts study. Griffin Milks told Business Insider he recommends about three videos a week for a year. Treat one a week as the floor for a side hustle.

| Week | Focus | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pick the niche, write 30 titles, set up the channel | Channel art, a one-sentence "who this is for", 2-Step Verification on, account verified (needed for custom thumbnails) |
| 2 | Gear test and script template | A 60-second test clip in your real room. You've listened back with headphones and chosen the mic |
| 3 | Film and publish video 1 | Video 1 is live with chapters, captions checked, and a custom thumbnail |
| 4 | Video 2. First review | Note the CTR and average view duration for video 1 in Studio. Video 2 is live |
| 5 | Video 3 plus first Short | A Short cut from video 3's best 30–45 seconds, linked to the full video |
| 6 | Video 4. Build a playlist | Videos 1–4 sit in a playlist named for the viewer's problem |
| 7 | Video 5. Title and thumbnail testing | A/B test titles or thumbnails on your best older video (needs advanced features) |
| 8 | Video 6. Double down | Video 6 is a follow-up to whichever earlier video held viewers longest |
| 9 | Video 7 plus second Short | Description template with timestamps and links saved for reuse |
| 10 | Video 8. Money groundwork | Apply to Amazon.ca Associates if you review products. Draft a one-page sponsor sheet (audience, average views, rates) |
| 11 | Video 9. Collaboration | Pitch one creator in your niche for a swap or joint video |
| 12 | Video 10. Bookkeeping | A spreadsheet or app tracking gear receipts, hours and any income. Start a tax set-aside account |

## What filming and editing workflow works for a part-time channel?

Batch the work. Script on one evening, film two videos in one session, and edit in two passes. Below are the templates to copy.

### Script template

```text
HOOK (0:00–0:15): The problem in one sentence + the result they'll get.
  "Your TFSA room isn't what your bank app says. Here's how to find the real number in 3 minutes."
PROOF (0:15–0:30): Why you're worth listening to (experience, not hype).
STEPS (0:30–8:00): 3–7 steps, one idea each. Show, don't describe.
  For each step: what to do → why → common mistake.
PAYOFF: Recap the result. Show it working.
NEXT VIDEO: Point to one related video by name (not "subscribe").
```

### Filming checklist

- Phone or camera at eye level on the tripod, lens wiped, 4K or 1080p at the same frame rate every time
- Light in front of you, window or lamp, never behind
- Mic clipped about a hand's width below your chin. Record 10 seconds of room silence for noise removal
- Airplane mode on, notifications off, furnace or fan noise checked
- Film each step twice. Clap on camera before each take so you can find it in the edit

### Edit passes

1. **Rough cut:** remove false starts and dead air. In Descript, use filler-word and retake removal.
2. **Tight cut:** cut anything that doesn't serve the title's promise. Add on-screen text for key numbers.
3. **Audio:** normalize levels, light noise reduction, music low under the voice, using only tracks you have the rights to.
4. **Final check:** watch once on a phone at normal speed. Fix anything you skipped through.

### Upload checklist

- Title (see templates below) and custom thumbnail, 16:9
- First two lines of the description restate the promise. Add timestamps/chapters, links and disclosures
- **Paid promotion** box ticked if anything was sponsored or gifted ([YouTube policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en))
- Audience set correctly (made for kids: yes or no)
- Captions reviewed, end screen pointing to one related video, video added to its playlist
- Mid-rolls on if the video is 8+ minutes and you're monetized

## How do you write YouTube titles and thumbnails that get clicked?

Write titles that name the viewer's problem and the result, add "Canada" or a province when the answer is local, and test up to three versions with YouTube's built-in A/B tool. Thumbnails should be readable on a phone.

**Title templates with Canadian examples:**

| Template | Example |
| --- | --- |
| How to [result] in [Canada/province] ([year]) | How to Open an FHSA in Canada (2026 Walkthrough) |
| [Number] mistakes [audience] make with [topic] | 7 Mistakes First-Time Renters Make in Toronto |
| [Option A] vs [Option B]: which is better for Canadians? | Winter Tires vs All-Weather Tires: Which Is Better in Ontario? |
| What [thing] actually costs in [city] | What a Kitchen Reno Actually Costs in Ottawa |
| [Topic] explained in [time] | GST/HST for Side Hustles Explained in 10 Minutes |
| The [year] beginner's guide to [topic] | The 2026 Beginner's Guide to Canoe Camping in Algonquin |
| I [did specific thing] for [time]: here's what happened | Only for things you actually did. Never invent the experiment |

**Thumbnail specs and rules:**

- YouTube recommends **3840 × 2160 pixels** for video thumbnails, a minimum width of 640 pixels, a 16:9 ratio and JPG or PNG. The upload limit is 50 MB on desktop and 2 MB on mobile ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431?hl=en)). Custom thumbnails need a verified account.
- Use one face or one object, plus 3–4 words at most that add to the title instead of repeating it.
- Check it at phone size before uploading.
- **A/B testing:** in Studio on desktop, you can test up to 3 titles, 3 thumbnails or both. The version with the highest watch time wins, and tests take up to two weeks ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714?hl=en)). Shorts can't be tested. Thumbnails below 1280 × 720 get downscaled in tests.

## How else can a Canadian YouTube channel make money?

Most creator income comes from beyond ads. Justin Tse's reported revenue was about 92% brand deals. Reni Odetoyinbo listed seven streams. Build a second stream before you hit 1,000 subscribers.

### Memberships and fan funding

At 500 subscribers in the expanded YPP, Canadian channels can turn on channel memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers and Super Thanks, and YouTube pays 70% of net revenue. **Illustrative example:** 20 members at an assumed $4.99 a month is $99.80 gross. Your share is about 70%, and YouTube calculates it on net revenue after taxes such as GST/HST, so expect a little under $69.86.

### Sponsorships

Influencer Marketing Hub's [2026 rate guide](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-rates/youtube-influencer-rates/) (updated August 31, 2026) gives these benchmark ranges per sponsored video:

| Creator tier | Subscribers | Typical rate per sponsored video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nano | 1,000–10,000 | $20 to $200+ |
| Micro | 10,000–100,000 | $200 to $1,000+ |
| Mid-tier | 100,000–500,000 | $1,000 to $10,000+ |
| Macro | 500,000–1M | $10,000 to $20,000+ |
| Mega | 1M+ | $20,000 to $50,000+ |

The same guide says sponsorship CPMs often fall between **$15 and $30 per 1,000 views**. **Illustrative example:** an integration in a video averaging 10,000 views (assumed) prices at 10 × $15 = $150 to 10 × $30 = $300. YouTube's own Shopping affiliate program **isn't open to Canadian creators**. Its [eligibility page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en) lists 14 countries, including the US but not Canada. Sell what your videos already teach: a template, checklist, ebook or course. Since January 1, 2025, Gumroad acts as merchant of record and collects and remits sales tax where it has tax obligations. **Illustrative example:** a $19 checklist sold through your own link costs $1.90 + $0.50 = $2.40 in fees, leaving $16.60. ## Which tax rules apply to a YouTube side hustle in Canada?

YouTube income is self-employment income in Canada. Report it on Form T2125, pay both halves of CPP, register for GST/HST once you pass the $30,000 small-supplier threshold, and give Google your US tax information so it doesn't withhold the maximum.

- **Income tax:** report AdSense, sponsorships, affiliate commissions and product sales, minus expenses, on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Keep the AdSense payment reports and sponsor invoices.
- **CPP:** self-employed people pay the full **11.9%** contribution on net self-employment earnings between $3,500 and the 2026 ceiling of $74,600, per [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html). Quebec residents contribute to the QPP instead.
- **GST/HST:** you're a small supplier until your worldwide taxable supplies, across all your businesses and associates, pass **$30,000** in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). After that you must register. Add YouTube, sponsorships and any other side hustle together. Once registered, charge the rate for the place of supply: 13% HST in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia since April 1, 2025, and GST plus PST or QST elsewhere ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html)). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) walks through it.
- **Digital products:** the CRA's [digital-economy rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) (in effect since July 1, 2021) put GST/HST obligations on some platforms and non-resident sellers. A platform collecting tax at checkout doesn't settle your own registration question.

**US tax info in AdSense (Form W-8BEN).** Every monetizing creator must submit US tax info, wherever they live. Google withholds US tax only on earnings from **US viewers**, at 0–30% depending on treaty status. Without tax info, Google may withhold **24% of your total worldwide earnings** ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10391362?hl=en)). Individuals outside the US generally file **Form W-8BEN** (W-8BEN-E for entities) in AdSense under Payments → Payments info → Manage settings → United States tax info. That form is where you claim treaty benefits, and the tool detects the Canada–US treaty ([Google](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10390801)). File by **December 10**. Forms expire after three full calendar years, and Google issues Form 1042-S by April 14 for the previous year's withholding.

**Illustrative example:** in an assumed month you earn US$100, of which US$40 comes from US viewers. With no tax info on file, Google can withhold 24% × US$100 = **US$24**. With a valid W-8BEN, withholding applies only to the US$40, at the rate AdSense shows after you submit the form. Google's own method is US revenue × your rate. Ask a tax professional how US tax withheld is treated on your Canadian return.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. For how much to put aside each month, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
