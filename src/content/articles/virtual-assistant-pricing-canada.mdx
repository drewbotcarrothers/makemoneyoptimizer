## Which pricing models work for virtual assistants?

Bill hourly at first, then move regular clients to monthly packages with a no-rollover rule. Price specialist and rush work higher.

| Model | How it works | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hourly | Rate × tracked time, invoiced monthly | VIVA: $39, due within 5 business days |
| Monthly package | Prepaid hours, discount for more hours | VIVA: 20 h $735 to 85 h $3,015 |
| Flat-rate tiers | Same hourly rate, fixed hours | Wendy Harris: 5 / 10 / 20 h at $45 |
| Rush and after-hours | Higher rate for short notice | VIVA: $46 vs $39 |
| Trial block | Small first package | finbuzz: $150 for 5 hours |
| Specialist rate | Higher rate for skilled work | Guide: $45–$75 for specialized VAs |

Packages should say how many hours are reserved, the response time, whether unused hours expire (VIVA's do), and the overage rate.

## How do you calculate a virtual assistant rate?

Build an hourly rate, then discount it slightly for packages and mark it up for rush and specialist work.

```text
Hourly rate     = (target pay + CPP + software, headset per hour)
                  ÷ share of hours billable
Package price   = hours × hourly rate × (1 − package discount)
Effective rate  = package price ÷ hours actually used
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $29/hour (between the $26.44 median and $36.88 high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $3.45 |
| Software, headset, computer per hour | $1.50 |
| Share of hours billable | 75% |
| **Rate** | ($29 + $3.45 + $1.50) ÷ 0.75 = $45.27, **round to $45** |

## How should you write a VA agreement and invoice?

Write the hours, response time, working hours, rollover rule and overage rate into a one-page agreement.

```text
VIRTUAL ASSISTANT PACKAGE — [client]                Date: [date]
Package: 20 hours per calendar month ........... $840 (prepaid on the 1st)
Tasks: inbox triage, calendar, client follow-ups, CRM updates,
  monthly listing paperwork
Hours: Mon–Fri, 9–5 [time zone]. Replies within 1 business day.
Unused hours: expire at month end (no rollover).
Extra hours: $45/hour, billed with the next package.
Rush (same day) and evenings/weekends: $55/hour.
Specialist work (automations, bookkeeping support): $60/hour, quoted first.
Access: you share logins through a password manager; I don't store
  passwords in email. Notice to end: 30 days.
Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ____]
```

```text
INVOICE #2026-064   Date: [date]
20-hour package, November (prepaid) ............... $840.00
Extra hours, October: 3 × $45 ..................... $135.00
Subtotal .......................................... $975.00
HST 13% (registered only) ......................... $126.75
Total ............................................. $1,101.75
Due within 5 business days. e-Transfer to [email].
```

## How do you raise virtual assistant prices?

Raise prices when your hours are full, when you add a specialist skill, or at the annual review in your agreement.

1. **Move clients to packages.** Prepaid hours with no rollover protect your week.
2. **Split specialist work.** Remote Work Canada puts specialized VAs at $45 to $75.
3. **Charge for rush and after-hours.** VIVA charges $46 against $39.
4. **Pass on card fees.** VIVA adds 3.4% + $0.15 when clients pay by card.
5. **Raise new clients first.** Existing clients get a month's notice.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], from [January 1] your 20-hour package moves from $[840] to
$[900] a month ($45 an hour). Hours, tasks and response times stay the
same. — [Your name]
```

For moving retainer clients to a new rate, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/). To see which clients and services actually pay per hour, use [tracking which side hustles pay](/side-hustles/track-which-side-hustles-pay-canada/).

## What virtual assistant pricing mistakes are common?

- **Copying offshore rates.** VirtualAssistants.ca's 160 hours for $1,699 isn't your market.
- **Unlimited rollover.** A package becomes a prepaid block with no end date.
- **Specialist work at admin rates.** Price automations and bookkeeping support separately.
- **Free rush work.** Charge a rush rate, as VIVA does.
- **No time tracking.** Track from day one, even on packages.

For the access, privacy and tax mistakes VAs make most, see the [virtual assistant mistakes guide](/side-hustles/virtual-assistant-side-hustle-canada/#what-to-watch-out-for). Don't take on regulated work, such as tax filing for others, without the right training and coverage.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect virtual assistant prices?

Virtual assistant income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration, the [home office guide](/side-hustles/home-office-expenses-side-hustle-canada/) covers workspace claims, and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to hold back.

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
