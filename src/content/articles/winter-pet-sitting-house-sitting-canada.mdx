Winter pet sitting and house sitting in Canada are paid stays in someone else's home, or drop-in visits, while the household is away, often around holiday travel. Pet sitting covers the animal. House sitting covers the dwelling, which may or may not include a pet. The work suits people who can sleep in a stranger's house or visit on a strict clock, who will write down medications and alarm codes, and who will not promise snow clearing unless that is a separate job.

Neighbourhood walks in ordinary weeks are the [dog walking guide](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/). A driveway full of snow is [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/), not a free extra on a sit. The rest of the season is the [winter side hustles roundup](/side-hustles/best-winter-side-hustles-canada/).

## How do winter pet sitting and house sitting work in Canada?

Winter pet sitting and house sitting in Canada fill the days a household is away, with a written list of tasks and a price per night or per visit. Holiday weeks book earlier than a random Tuesday because flights and family dinners are dated. Holiday dates are fixed, so the one rule is not to take two households for the same night.

| Service | Where you are | What to price separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drop-in | Their home, short visit | Meds, a walk, and a second visit the same day |
| Overnight pet sit | Their home, you sleep there | Extra animals, holiday dates, and plants |
| House sit without pets | Their home | Mail, plants, lights, and any snow you did not include |
| Hosting the pet at your home | Your dwelling | Zoning, your own animals, and an escape plan |

[Rover](https://www.rover.com/ca/) lists walking and sitting in many Canadian cities. It is optional. A neighbour who already trusts you does not need an app. Read Rover's live help articles before you set a rate around its fees.

## Who is winter sitting a fit for in Canada?

Winter sitting in Canada fits someone who is calm with animals, available on the dates they sell, and willing to leave a party because a dog needs a medication at 9 p.m. It is a poor fit if you travel the same week, if you have allergies you have not tested, or if sleeping in someone else's house feels unsafe. Decline the sit. A drop-in route can still work.

House sitting without a pet is closer to a presence in the home: lights, plants, mail, and a pipe that should not freeze if the household asked you to watch the thermostat. You are not a security guard, a plumber, or a snow contractor unless the note says so and you actually do that work. Frozen-pipe advice belongs to the homeowner and a plumber. Your job is to follow the written thermostat instruction and to call the person they named if the house will not heat.

## How should you book a holiday sit in Canada?

Book a holiday sit in Canada with a meet-and-greet, a task list, a key plan, and a price that names the nights. Do that in November for late December. A message on December 22 is a favour you may refuse.

### Step 1: Meet the animal and walk the house

Do a visit while the owner is home. Confirm vaccines the owner can show you, the vet's name, and any bite or escape history. The dog walking guide's first-walk questions apply to a sit. Walk the route you will use in the dark. Ice and early sunset are the winter difference. If you are uncomfortable with the animal, do not take the holiday week to "see how it goes."

### Step 2: Write keys, alarms, and backups

Agree how you enter. Do not label a key with the street address. Agree who else has a key, including a neighbour, and who you call if you are sick. An alarm code stays in a password manager or a sealed note, not in a group chat. If the household uses a lockbox, test it before they fly.

### Step 3: List medications, food, and the vet

Write dose, time, and where the medication is stored. Ask what you may not feed. Name the vet and an emergency clinic the owner accepts, and ask whether you may approve treatment up to an amount they write down. You are not the veterinarian. If you cannot reach the owner, the written amount is what you have. Above it, you are spending your own money or waiting. Say that out loud at the meet-and-greet.

### Step 4: Separate house tasks from pet tasks

Mail brought inside, plants, a thermostat setting, and lights on a timer can be part of a house sit. Shovelling the driveway is [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) with its own price, trigger, and insurance. A sidewalk the city assigns to the owner is still a municipal rule. Do not promise it inside a pet sit because the owner assumed "you'll handle the outside."

### Step 5: Decide where the animal sleeps if you cannot stay

Hosting a client's pet in your home is a different product. Your lease, your own pets, and your city's home-business rules can forbid it. Vancouver's [home-based business page](https://vancouver.ca/doing-business/home-based-business.aspx) says you may need a licence to operate a business from home, with limits on floor area and on non-resident employees. Its [FAQ](https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/home-based-business-web-faq.pdf) says a licence is required for a home-based business and that home insurance typically does not cover commercial activities. A client's dog in your Vancouver apartment is a fact pattern to check against those pages and against your lease, not a casual extra. Other cities publish different rules.

## How should you price winter sitting in Canada?

Price winter sitting in Canada by the night or the visit, then add animals, medications, holiday dates, and any house task you included. There is no national nightly rate on this site. A drop-in two blocks away and a seven-night sit with injections are different products.

| Factor | What to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Nights versus visits | Overnight is a calendar you cannot double-book |
| Number of animals | A second dog is not automatically half price |
| Medications | More skill and a stricter clock |
| Holiday dates | You may be giving up your own travel; price that or decline |
| Your travel | A suburb away is time, even when the night is quiet |
| House tasks | Plants and mail are small; snow is another business |
| Where the pet stays | Their home, or yours, with the zoning and lease questions |

## What does a worked sitting price in Canada look like?

A worked sitting price in Canada multiplies your own nightly rate by the nights and adds per-animal extras. The rates below are assumptions, not a Rover price or a survey.

**Worked example.** Suppose you choose $75 a night for an overnight in the client's home, one calm dog, medications already practised at the meet-and-greet. Four nights are 4 × $75 = $300 before tax. Suppose a second dog adds $15 a night in your own menu. Four nights with two dogs are $300 + (4 × $15) = $360 before tax.

Suppose a drop-in is $25 a visit in the same sketch, two visits a day, for five days. That is 10 × $25 = $250 before tax. The overnight sketch is more money and it uses your nights. The drop-in sketch leaves you sleeping at home and still requires you to show up on ice. Pick the product in writing. Do not arrive for a drop-in and stay the night for the drop-in price.

If the booking is on Rover, subtract the fee the live help article describes before you compare $360 with a direct booking. If you are registered for GST/HST, tax may sit on top of the price you advertised. Say which.

Insurance is not in the $360. A broker's quote can be larger than one holiday week. Price the quote across the sits you realistically expect, or do not take the sits. Three direct nights at $75 are $225. If a quote is higher than the season you will actually work, the sits do not fund the policy by themselves. That comparison is a reason to ask the broker, not a prediction.

## What insurance questions apply to sitting in Canada?

Sitting in Canada raises liability for a bite, an escape, a broken object, and a house you were the last person to lock. The Insurance Bureau of Canada says you should tell your home insurer if you run a business from home, that personal liability on a homeowner's policy would typically not cover business injury or property damage, and that commercial liability is the coverage to discuss for amounts you are legally obligated to pay. Ask a broker and describe pets, keys, and overnights in other people's homes. This is not insurance advice. This page does not state a coverage limit.

If you drive a dog, ask your auto insurer before you transport animals for pay. Staying on foot avoids that question. The dog walking guide says the same thing. Winter sidewalks do not make the car safer.

Rover's guarantee, if you booked on Rover, has its own terms, minimum contribution, and exclusions. Read them. Then decide whether you still want a policy in your own name for sits that are not on the platform, and for risks the guarantee does not take.

## What mistakes do new sitters make in Canada?

New sitters in Canada double-book holiday nights, accept a dog they have not met, and include shovelling in a pet price. They label keys with addresses. They host a client's animal in a rental that forbids it. They treat a Rover help-centre percentage they saw last year as this year's fee.

They also leave for their own dinner without a backup person the owner agreed to. A holiday sit fails when you are the only key and you are an hour away. Write the backup down, or do not take the night.

## Which tax rules apply to pet sitting and house sitting in Canada?

Sitting fees in Canada are generally self-employment income reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule.

This is not tax, legal, veterinary, or insurance advice.

Track app payouts and e-transfers. An app may send a slip. Your own record still matters. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Dog walking and sitting share that pot. Under the test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. If you are registered, confirm the rate for the province where the supply is made on the [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) page and with the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). If you are not registered, do not add GST/HST.
