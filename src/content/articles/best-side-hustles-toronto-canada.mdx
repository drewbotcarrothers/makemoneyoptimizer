## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Toronto, Canada?

Look for work the City hands back to residents. Three show up clearly on toronto.ca.

### Step 1: Sell winter clearing of private property

Toronto's [sidewalk clearing page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/streets-parking-transportation/road-maintenance/winter-maintenance/clearing-snow-and-ice-from-your-property/) says City crews clear all public sidewalks once 2 cm or more has fallen. Residents clear their own sidewalk only when less than 2 cm falls, within 12 hours. The bigger job is private property. Owners must clear driveways, parking spaces, steps, ramps and landings within 24 hours after snowfall ends. Snow can't be pushed onto sidewalks, roads or bike lanes, and the page lists a $500 fine plus a victim surcharge. So your service is driveways, steps and walkways, with snow piled on the client's own lot. The [snow removal side hustle guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) covers equipment and [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) covers per-visit and seasonal rates.

### Step 2: Walk dogs within the permit rules

The City's [commercial dog walker permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) applies to walking four to six dogs. It costs $305.99 a year and requires $2,000,000 of commercial general liability insurance. With one to three dogs, you're under the permit threshold. Read [how to start dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) and the [Rover review](/side-hustles/rover-review-canada/) before choosing between an app and your own clients.

### Step 3: Work the yard waste calendar

Toronto picks up [yard waste](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/yard-waste/) every other week from around mid-March to mid-November, in kraft paper bags or rigid open-top containers. Leaf raking, garden cleanup and bagging cluster at both ends of that window. The [fall leaf cleanup guide](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/) shows how to quote it, and [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) fills the summer between.

## Which apps work in Toronto, Canada?

Toronto is on [TaskRabbit's list of locations](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/locations), so furniture assembly, mounting and moving help can come through the app. The [TaskRabbit review](/side-hustles/taskrabbit-review-canada/) covers fees and how jobs are assigned. For delivery, compare apps in [Uber Eats vs DoorDash vs Skip](/side-hustles/uber-eats-vs-doordash-vs-skip-canada/). Ontario's [Digital Platform Workers' Rights Act](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers) has been in force since July 1, 2025. It sets a pay floor equal to the general minimum wage for each work assignment, and tips don't count toward it. Without a car, the [no-car side hustle guide](/side-hustles/side-hustles-without-a-car-canada/) suits transit and bike couriers.

## What seasons drive Toronto side hustle income?

- **November to March:** snow clearing on private property, plus [winter pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/) around holidays.
- **April to May:** spring yard cleanup and [pressure washing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-side-hustle-canada/).
- **June to September:** lawn care, [moving help](/side-hustles/moving-help-side-hustle-canada/), and [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/).
- **October to November:** leaf cleanup, then [Christmas light installation](/side-hustles/christmas-light-installation-side-hustle-canada/).

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Toronto, Canada?

Usually not for a small personal service, but check your category. The City's [business regulations page](https://www.toronto.ca/business-economy/new-businesses-startups/business-regulations/) lists licensed categories. One that catches resellers: a business that sells second-hand goods from a shop, or goes house to house buying them, needs a [Second Hand Shop licence](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/second-hand-salvage-yards-shops/second-hand-shop/). The total fee is $788.33. Selling your own items online isn't that. If you use a name other than your own, [register a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/) with Ontario.

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you sign six neighbours on your street for seasonal driveway clearing. Your plan has three parts: a route order, a written promise to clear within the City's 24-hour window, and a rule to pile snow on each client's lawn rather than the road. When a light snowfall of under 2 cm leaves sidewalks to residents, add a per-visit sidewalk option. ## What mistakes do Toronto side hustlers make?

- **Pushing snow onto the road.** It's illegal under the City's rules and your client can be fined.
- **Walking four dogs without a permit.** The permit kicks in at four.
- **Reselling as a "shop" without a licence.** Pop-up second-hand stores and door-to-door buying are licensed.
- **Quoting yard work after the last collection.** Pickup stops around mid-November, so plan disposal before then.

## Which other Toronto side hustles come up often?

Parking, a room used for a photo or video shoot, a paid focus group, and a serving shift sit beside the outdoor work above. This site does not have a separate photo-shoot rental guide. The City's film pages are the rule for a commercial shoot, and the rental guides that do exist are linked below.

- **Parking.** A stall you are allowed to rent is [renting out a parking space](/side-hustles/rent-out-parking-space-canada/). A garage or shed is [renting out storage space](/side-hustles/rent-out-storage-space-canada/). A legal room or basement is [renting out a room or basement suite](/side-hustles/rent-out-room-basement-suite-canada/).
- **A space for a photo or video shoot.** The City's [plan your shoot](https://www.toronto.ca/business-economy/industry-sector-support/film/plan-your-shoot/) page says a production needs the property owner's permission. If all filming, including vehicle parking and arrival and departure, stays on private property and between 7 a.m. and 11 p.m., a Film Office permit is not required. Work between 11 p.m. and 7 a.m. needs a permit and a guideline exemption even on private property. Special effects, pyrotechnics, fire, or simulated gunfire need a permit even on private property. [Guidelines for filming](https://www.toronto.ca/business-economy/industry-sector-support/film/guidelines-for-filming-in-toronto/) say permits follow Toronto Municipal Code Chapter 459, Filming, and that the Film Office also permits commercial still photography on city streets and sidewalks. The [resident page](https://www.toronto.ca/business-economy/industry-sector-support/film/resident-information/) says the Film Office will not tell you what to charge, and that an owner who wants productions to find the property can register it with Ontario Creates. This is not legal advice.
- **Focus groups.** A research session is not a film permit. Company pages and scam checks are in [paid focus groups](/side-hustles/paid-focus-groups-canada/). The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre's [job fraud](https://antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca/scams-fraudes/job-emploi-eng.htm) page, with an alert dated May 30, 2024, lists web surveys and online-task jobs among variations that target people looking for work, including cases that ask you to deposit funds. Do not pay someone to "unlock" a study.
- **Serving and bartending.** In Ontario, the AGCO says valid certification is legally required for every person involved in the sale or service of liquor, and [Smart Serve](https://www.agco.ca/en/alcohol/responsible-liquor-sale-service-and-delivery-training) is the approved program for bars and restaurants. The shift guide is [bartending and serving](/side-hustles/bartending-serving-side-hustle-canada/).

## How are Toronto side hustles taxed?

Toronto income is taxed like any other self-employment income. You report it on Form T2125 and register for HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers are the exception and register from their first fare. Ontario's HST rate is 13%. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
