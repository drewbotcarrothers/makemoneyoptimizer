## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Toronto, Canada?

Look for work the City hands back to residents. Three show up clearly on toronto.ca.

### Step 1: Sell winter clearing of private property

Toronto's [sidewalk clearing page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/streets-parking-transportation/road-maintenance/winter-maintenance/clearing-snow-and-ice-from-your-property/) says City crews clear all public sidewalks once 2 cm or more has fallen. Residents clear their own sidewalk only when less than 2 cm falls, within 12 hours. The bigger job is private property. Owners must clear driveways, parking spaces, steps, ramps and landings within 24 hours after snowfall ends. Snow can't be pushed onto sidewalks, roads or bike lanes, and the page lists a $500 fine plus a victim surcharge. So your service is driveways, steps and walkways, with snow piled on the client's own lot. The [snow removal side hustle guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) covers equipment and [snow removal pricing](/side-hustles/snow-removal-pricing-canada/) covers per-visit and seasonal rates.

### Step 2: Walk dogs within the permit rules

The City's [commercial dog walker permit](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/commercial-dog-walker-permit/) applies to walking four to six dogs. It costs $305.99 a year and requires $2,000,000 of commercial general liability insurance. With one to three dogs, you're under the permit threshold. Read [how to start dog walking](/side-hustles/dog-walking-side-hustle-canada/) and the [Rover review](/side-hustles/rover-review-canada/) before choosing between an app and your own clients.

### Step 3: Work the yard waste calendar

Toronto picks up [yard waste](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/yard-waste/) every other week from around mid-March to mid-November, in kraft paper bags or rigid open-top containers. Leaf raking, garden cleanup and bagging cluster at both ends of that window. The [fall leaf cleanup guide](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/) shows how to quote it, and [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada/) fills the summer between.

## What seasons drive Toronto side hustle income?

- **November to March:** snow clearing on private property, plus [winter pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/) around holidays.
- **April to May:** spring yard cleanup and [pressure washing](/side-hustles/pressure-washing-side-hustle-canada/).
- **June to September:** lawn care, [moving help](/side-hustles/moving-help-side-hustle-canada/), and [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/).
- **October to November:** leaf cleanup, then [Christmas light installation](/side-hustles/christmas-light-installation-side-hustle-canada/).

## Do you need a business licence for a side hustle in Toronto, Canada?

Usually not for a small personal service, but check your category. The City's [business regulations page](https://www.toronto.ca/business-economy/new-businesses-startups/business-regulations/) lists licensed categories. One that catches resellers: a business that sells second-hand goods from a shop, or goes house to house buying them, needs a [Second Hand Shop licence](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/permits-licences-bylaws/second-hand-salvage-yards-shops/second-hand-shop/). The total fee is $788.33. Selling your own items online isn't that. If you use a name other than your own, [register a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/) with Ontario.

## What mistakes do Toronto side hustlers make?

- **Pushing snow onto the road.** It's illegal under the City's rules and your client can be fined.
- **Walking four dogs without a permit.** The permit kicks in at four.
- **Reselling as a "shop" without a licence.** Pop-up second-hand stores and door-to-door buying are licensed.
- **Quoting yard work after the last collection.** Pickup stops around mid-November, so plan disposal before then.

## How are Toronto side hustles taxed?

Toronto income is taxed like any other self-employment income. You report it on Form T2125 and register for HST once you pass $30,000 in four consecutive calendar quarters, under the [CRA's small supplier rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Rideshare drivers are the exception and register from their first fare. Ontario's HST rate is 13%.
