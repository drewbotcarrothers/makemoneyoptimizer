In-person tutoring in Canada is paid teaching in a home, a library room, or another agreed place, in a subject you can already explain, for a student a parent or an adult learner has booked. It suits people who can travel a short distance, keep a session private, and say which ages they will teach. It is a poor fit if you need students this week, if you cannot pass the check a parent asks for, or if you would rather teach on a video call. The video version is [online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/).

## Who does in-person tutoring in Canada suit?

In-person tutoring in Canada suits people who can teach one subject slowly, in a room, and who will travel only as far as the hour still pays. It suits teachers, retired teachers, university students in that subject, and parents who already explain homework. It is a poor fit if your evenings are already sold, if you dislike repeating the same explanation, or if you plan to tutor every subject from kindergarten through calculus.

A teaching certificate is not a national licence to tutor privately. Employment in a publicly funded school is a different status. Do not imply a certificate, a faculty of education, or a school-board job you do not hold. Teachers who tutor on the side should read [side hustles for teachers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-teachers-canada/) for conflict-of-interest rules at their board. Care of a child that is not teaching is [babysitting](/side-hustles/babysitting-side-hustle-canada/), with its own provincial child-care limits.

## Which police checks apply to in-person tutoring in Canada?

Police checks for in-person tutoring in Canada depend on the province and on who is hiring you. There is no single national tutor badge.

The [RCMP](https://rcmp.ca/en/criminal-records/criminal-record-checks/vulnerable-sector-checks) says a vulnerable sector check is a police information check plus a check for a record suspension for sexual offences. It also says:

- It is an offence to conduct a vulnerable sector check if the position does not meet the Criminal Records Act.
- The hiring organization must make the request. The person being checked consents. They do not make the request themselves.
- No federal law requires every organization to conduct these checks. Ask the province.
- Results go only to organizations in Canada.
- Children, for this federal page, are anyone under 18. A vulnerable person is someone who, because of age, disability, or other circumstance, is more vulnerable than others.
- The position, not the person, must be one of trust or authority. Contact with children is not enough by itself.
- In British Columbia, follow the Criminal Records Review Program. Elsewhere, contact the local police service where you live.

Ontario's [police record checks](https://www.ontario.ca/page/police-record-checks) page, updated 27 April 2026, authorizes three products: a criminal record check, a criminal record and judicial matters check, and a vulnerable sector check. People in a position of trust or authority over children or other vulnerable persons can be asked for the vulnerable sector check. A child in that Ontario page is anyone under 18. Vulnerable sector checks must be completed by the local police service. Some other checks can go through an authorized third party. The Ministry of the Solicitor General does not issue the checks and does not license those third parties. A police service may ask for a letter from the organization describing the position. Volunteers can get some checks free from a police service; a vulnerable sector check can still carry a police fee. A paid tutor is not a volunteer.

British Columbia's [Criminal Records Review Program](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/safety/crime-prevention/criminal-record-check/law-policy) page, updated 26 June 2026, says the Criminal Records Review Act applies to certain organizations. Those organizations must ensure that people who work with vulnerable adults or children under 19 complete a criminal record check through the program. A child, on that page, is under 19. That is a different age line from the federal page's under 18. A self-employed tutor should ask whether the organization hiring them is one of the organizations the Act covers, and should not assume a parent's request and a school board's request follow the same form.

Edmonton Police describes a [vulnerable sector check](https://www.edmontonpolice.ca/CommunityPolicing/OperationalServices/PoliceInformationCheck/PICSFAQ/VulnerableSectorCheck) for positions responsible for children, the elderly, people with disabilities, or other vulnerable populations. The police service verifies that the position qualifies. Edmonton can issue the check only for approved jurisdictions; other applicants use their local RCMP detachment. Proof of residency is required. Treat that as Edmonton's process, not Alberta's statute in one paragraph.

Quebec City police explain a [vérification for people working with vulnerable clients](https://www.ville.quebec.qc.ca/citoyens/police/intervention/services/filtrage-securite/verification-antecedents-judiciaires-clientele-vulnerable.aspx). That page says that in Quebec, educational childcare services have a legal obligation to do this screening, and that other organizations are not under the same legal obligation, though the check is recommended. A protocol with the police service is required for organizations. A private tutor is not a daycare. A parent may still ask for a record check. Use the police service where you live, and do not buy a "background check" from a random website.

This is not legal advice. Ask the parent or the school which document they want, then get that document from the police service that can issue it.

## How do you set a tutoring rate ladder in Canada?

A tutoring rate in Canada should rise with the specificity of the course and with evidence you have taught it, and it should start from numbers parents in your city already see. This site does not publish a national tutor wage. Three local tutors who print a price are a better check than a screenshot from another country.

| Rung | What you are selling | How to think about the price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First students | One subject, homework help, a short trial | The low end of local listings you can point to. You are buying references |
| Course tutor | The course the student is actually in, with the province named | Higher, because the curriculum match is the product |
| Specialist | A named exam or credit course you have taught more than once | Higher again, still without a promise of a mark |
| Out of scope | Every subject, or a result you cannot control | Do not sell it |

Test preparation that is really a general study-skills course can be a different GST/HST product from tutoring in the school course. The tax section below is that distinction. Do not fold a mark guarantee into any rung.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, a survey, or a market rate. Suppose three tutors near you publish $35, $45, and $70 an hour, and you ignore the $70 because you cannot yet show the same course list. Suppose you are on the first rung and you charge an assumed $35. Suppose a parent books four sessions. Gross is 4 × $35 = $140. Suppose the bus or the drive, parking, and a printed worksheet cost an assumed $18 that week. Amount left is $122 before tax set-aside and before the 20 minutes of prep you did not bill. If you later move to the course-tutor rung at an assumed $50, four sessions are $200 before those costs. Replace both rates with listings you have actually read. Do not copy $35 or $50.

A notes app can hold two students. Keep deposits out of the account you spend as wages. <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one Canadian account that can separate them.

## How do you find in-person tutoring students in Canada?

You find in-person tutoring students in Canada through parents who already need the subject, not through a blast to a class list you do not have permission to use.

### Step 1: Name the offer in one sentence

Subject, level, city, and where the session happens. "Grade 10 academic math, Ontario curriculum, at the student's home in the east end" is a sentence. "All subjects" is not.

### Step 2: Ask the places that already see parents

A library notice board, a community centre, a sports club, and families you already know. A school may keep a community tutor list or may forbid staff from recommending a paid tutor. Ask what that school allows. Do not expect a counsellor to advertise you, and do not email a class list. A parent who asks the office "who do other families use?" is a different conversation from you asking the office to recruit.

### Step 3: Write the cancellation rule before the first yes

What you charge if they cancel the morning of the session. Where you will not go. Which ages you teach. A trial session with a short note afterward is easier for a parent to buy than a ten-pack.

### Step 4: Offer the check they asked for

Bring the police product they named, from the service that can issue it. A screenshot of a third-party site is not an Ontario vulnerable sector check.

### Step 5: Keep one channel for the first month

In-person or online, not both inboxes for the same student. The [first online client guide](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-first-client-canada/) is the same discipline on a video call. January packages are in the [exam-season guide](/side-hustles/exam-season-winter-tutoring-canada/).

## What mistakes do in-person tutors make in Canada?

Common mistakes are promising a mark, tutoring in a bedroom with the door shut and no other adult in the home when the student is a child, and skipping the check because "it's just homework." Another is pricing from a US marketplace. Another is treating a school counsellor as a sales channel.

A further mistake is one rate for every request. Elementary reading and a provincial exam are different products. So are a session at the kitchen table and a session that costs you 40 minutes of driving. Track the minutes. ## Which tax rules apply to in-person tutoring in Canada?

In-person tutoring income in Canada is still income. Report it on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Self-employed people pay both shares of the [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. A GST/HST exemption does not remove income tax or CPP.

[GST/HST Memorandum 20-6](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/20-6/tutoring-equivalent-services.html) explains section 9 of Part III of Schedule V. A supply of tutoring or instructing an individual in a course approved for credit by a school authority, or that follows a curriculum designated by a school authority, can be exempt. The memorandum says this is about elementary and secondary courses. It says the exemption does not apply to post-secondary courses. It says there must be a direct connection to that course or curriculum. It says a general study-skills course, too broad to tie to a particular school course, is not exempt under that section. Music lessons are the prescribed equivalent in the memorandum. The parent can be the person who pays; the tutoring still has to be rendered to an individual.

Taxable tutoring, including supplies that do not meet that section, counts toward the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html). Exempt supplies are not taxable supplies. If every hour you sell is exempt, those fees may not push you over the threshold. If you also sell taxable work, that work shares one total with your other side hustles. Confirm the live page, and call GST/HST Rulings if you need a ruling on your sessions. The memorandum itself gives 1-800-959-8287. This is not tax advice.

The [tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) and [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) sit beside the return. [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) is the form choice: tutoring is business income, not rent.
