## Which survey sites are US-only?

**InboxDollars** says it welcomes individuals living within the United States (50 states), that members must be 18 or older, and that members must reside in the United States while using the account. Hiding your country with a VPN, private IP, or proxy is prohibited, and the country on the profile must be the country you live in and log in from ([Who can join](https://help.inboxdollars.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044247092-Who-Can-Join-InboxDollars)). A Canadian address will not make that account valid.

Other names that show up in US survey roundups, including MyPoints and Pinecone Research, were not confirmed on an official Canada page for this guide. If the signup asks for a US ZIP code or a US phone number and offers no Canadian alternative, stop. The wider list of apps with the same pattern is [US-only side hustle apps](/side-hustles/us-only-side-hustle-apps-canada/).

## Why do Canadians get fewer survey invites?

Panels sample people who match a study, then often randomize within that group so the same members are not picked every time. Leger's FAQ says surveys go to panelists whose demographics match, and that within a profile group the distribution is random. It also says you may receive a few surveys on a quarterly, monthly, or weekly basis, and that the first questions can screen you out if your profile does not fit that study. That is the product working, not a penalty.

A complete, truthful profile is the only lever you control. AskingCanadians says it identifies the topic, the reward, and the approximate length before you start, and that you can take as many or as few as you like. You cannot force a brand to need your age or your city this week.

## How do you join survey sites in Canada?

### Step 1: Pick two Canadian panels and one study platform

Join Leger or AskingCanadians, plus Ipsos iSay or YouGov, plus Prolific if you want longer studies. Swagbucks can sit beside them. Five more panels rarely add five times the mail.

### Step 2: Confirm the email and finish the profile

Leger says an unconfirmed email leaves the account from being fully activated, so later invites dry up. Use an inbox you actually check. Answer demographic questions once, consistently.

### Step 3: Read the reward before you start

If the page shows points, length, and a topic, decide then. A 25-minute survey for a tiny point total is optional. Screen-out questions at the start are normal. They are not a payment.

### Step 4: Track approved rewards only

Write down the date, the panel, and the reward that actually cleared. Points still sitting in an account are not cash until the panel's own cash-out rule is met. Caddle's rule is a $20 balance, paid by Interac e-Transfer. Swagbucks redeems through its rewards catalogue. The dollar value of a point is whatever that catalogue says on the day you redeem, so check it there rather than trusting an old blog.

### Step 5: Move up when surveys feel too small

Recorded tests and screened interviews pay more per session and reject more applicants. Compare [UserTesting, User Interviews, and Respondent](/side-hustles/user-testing-sites-canada/). Writing and rating AI answers is a different job again: [AI training](/side-hustles/ai-data-annotation-side-hustle-canada/).

## What mistakes do Canadians make on survey sites?

- **Joining through a page that asks for money.** These panels are free. A fee, a "verification deposit," or a request to buy gift cards is the pattern the [scams guide](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/) tells you to leave.
- **One person, several accounts.** Panels prohibit duplicate accounts. A second email does not create a second eligible Canadian.
- **A VPN set to the United States.** You may see more US surveys for an hour and then lose the account. InboxDollars states the ban directly. Others fail ID checks when the address and the connection disagree.
- **Quitting after one quiet week.** Leger's own FAQ says the cadence can be quarterly. Judge a panel after a month of a finished profile, not after a day.
- **Ignoring the cash-out currency.** A PayPal payout in USD still has to be reported in Canadian dollars. See [USD platform income](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).

### Illustrative example

Assumed numbers, not a panel's published rate. Suppose three completed surveys in a month credit $4, $6, and $5 in rewards, and two more screen you out at question three and pay nothing. Completed rewards are $4 + $6 + $5 = $15. The screen-outs add time and $0. If the panel holds payment until $20, that $15 stays in the account. This sketch is only here to show why approved rewards and the cash-out minimum both matter. It is not an average month.

## Which tax rules apply to survey rewards in Canada?

Money you are paid for surveys and studies is income. Report it in Canadian dollars. When the activity is a business, the form is [T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Gift cards and points are easier to mishandle: some are ordinary income, and some consumer rebates are argued differently. Do not treat a blog's flat answer as a ruling. The careful version is [are rewards and bonuses taxable](/side-hustles/are-rewards-and-bonuses-taxable-canada/), and the reporting steps are [how to report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/).
