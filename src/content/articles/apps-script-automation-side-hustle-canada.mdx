## How do you start Apps Script freelancing in Canada?

Starting Apps Script freelancing in Canada means automating one task you can point at, documenting how to stop it, and selling that shape of project to businesses already in Google tools.

### Step 1: Pick a task with a trigger and a result

Examples: a form adds a row; a menu item archives old rows; a custom function labels a status. Write it in one sentence. "Automate our Google" is not a sentence.

### Step 2: Build it with fake data

Use sample names. Run the happy path and one broken path, such as a blank email. If the script would email people, stop and read the consent rules before any real address is in the sheet.

### Step 3: Write the runbook

What it does, what it does not do, how to pause the trigger, and who receives errors. The client should be able to stop it without you.

### Step 4: Read privacy and anti-spam before real people are involved

If the script collects or moves personal information in a commercial setting, start with the [PIPEDA overview](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/). Provincial privacy law may apply. If it sends a commercial email or text, read [getting consent to send email](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email). A convenient trigger is not consent. This is not legal advice. The longer version for anyone selling outreach is [cold email and lead generation under CASL](/side-hustles/cold-email-lead-generation-casl-canada/).

### Step 5: Define a fixed first project

One script, a fake-data test, a live test with the client watching, the runbook, and a short window for fixes that match the original sentence. New ideas are a new quote.

### Step 6: Offer it where the sheet already exists

Bookkeepers, shops, and coordinators who live in Google Sheets. Ten notes, each naming the task. If you email, the message itself has to follow the consent rules. A conversation with someone who asked you for help is a different fact from a scraped directory.

### Step 7: Work in a copy, then in the client's file

Duplicate the sheet. Share it from their account. Remove your access when the window closes. Do not keep a personal copy of their customer rows "for reference."

### Step 8: Test live with the client, then stop

Watch one real run. Confirm the error notice goes to them. Send the invoice and the runbook the same day. [How to invoice clients in Canada](/side-hustles/how-to-invoice-clients-canada/) is the paperwork around that fee.

### Step 9: Review after two projects

Note the hours you did not quote. Raise the next fixed price or shrink the sentence. Add a paid check-in only if they want someone to watch errors, and cap those hours.

## What Apps Script mistakes are common in Canada?

Common Apps Script mistakes in Canada are editing the live customer sheet first, hiding the script in your own Drive, and quoting a build without a failed test.

A script that emails a purchased list can be an anti-spam problem for you and for the client. The runbook should say why the business is allowed to send the message.

Scope creep arrives as "can it also update the CRM?" That is a second sentence and a second price.

Leaving yourself as the only person who can open the script means the client is stuck when you are away, and you are holding data you no longer need.
