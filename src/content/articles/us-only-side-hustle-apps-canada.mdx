<QuickAnswer>

- **Documented US limits:** InboxDollars (50 states), Ibotta (app functions outside the US, US phone to withdraw), Checkout 51's public homepage (United States members only), the US Spark Driver site (50 states and US work authorization), and Grubhub's driver FAQ (US state ID and US markets).
- **Closed everywhere:** Amazon Mechanical Turk says it permanently closed on September 30, 2026.
- **Not US-only:** Swagbucks lists Canada. Prolific lists Canada. Rakuten has a separate Canadian site that pays CAD.
- **Do not VPN in.** Several of these rules say the country you log in from has to match the country on the account.

</QuickAnswer>

A lot of side-hustle lists are written for the United States and then read in Canada. The app installs, the signup asks for a ZIP code or a Social Security number, and the account never pays. This page lists only the limits that the companies publish, plus the Canadian product to use instead. If a name is missing, this guide did not find an official country rule, so the honest step is the signup screen, not a guess.

The cluster hub is [beermoney apps](/side-hustles/best-beermoney-apps-canada/). Delivery apps that do run in Canadian cities are a different article: [Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip](/side-hustles/uber-eats-vs-doordash-vs-skip-canada/).

## Which famous apps are not US-only?

Saying "everything American is blocked" is how people skip a Canadian door.

- **Swagbucks** lists Canada among countries whose residents above age 13 may redeem rewards. It is the same company family as InboxDollars, and the country rules are not the same.
- **Prolific** lists Canada for participants over 18 who pass verification.
- **User Interviews** lists Canada. **UserTesting** lists Canada as a standard contributor country. **Respondent** lists Canada as a primary market. Details are in [user testing sites](/side-hustles/user-testing-sites-canada/).
- **DoorDash, Uber Eats, SkipTheDishes, Instacart, and Amazon Flex** operate in Canadian cities, with their own waitlists. Start with the [Instacart shopper review](/side-hustles/instacart-shopper-review-canada/) and the [Amazon Flex review](/side-hustles/amazon-flex-review-canada/).
- **TaskRabbit and Rover** have Canadian coverage in some cities. See the [TaskRabbit review](/side-hustles/taskrabbit-review-canada/) and the [Rover review](/side-hustles/rover-review-canada/).
- **Outlier** lists Canada on its full-stack coding role. **DataAnnotation** did not publish a live country list on the pages checked for the [DataAnnotation review](/side-hustles/dataannotation-review-canada/). Do not file it under "US-only" or under "open" until the form says which.

## What about apps this guide did not confirm?

Fetch Rewards, Venmo, Cash App, Thumbtack, and Mercari are often described as US products. This guide did not open an official page that states the Canadian rule in a sentence worth quoting, so they are not in the table. If the signup demands a US Social Security number, a US ZIP code, or a US phone number and offers no Canadian path, stop. A VPN that makes you look American is the failure mode InboxDollars states outright, and it is the same failure ID checks on study platforms are built to catch.

Gig work that needs a car in a Canadian city is a different decision from an app that will not create the account. Compare pay after fuel in the delivery guides rather than importing a US hourly claim.

## How do you pick a Canadian alternative?

### Step 1: Match the job, not the logo

If the US article was about surveys, open [survey sites](/side-hustles/survey-sites-canada/). If it was about receipt scanning, open [cashback apps](/side-hustles/cashback-apps-canada/). If it was about delivery, open the Canadian courier comparison. If it was about Mechanical Turk, that product is closed. Use Prolific or an AI-training platform instead.

### Step 2: Read the country line on the page you are joining

"Available worldwide" on a blog is not the rule. The rule is the help-centre sentence that names countries. Save the link with the date you read it. These lists move.

### Step 3: Use the Canadian domain when one exists

Rakuten.ca, not Rakuten.com. A .ca site that pays CAD saves you a conversion and a second tax note. USD payouts you do receive, from Prolific or Respondent or a US store, still get reported. The method is [earning in USD from US platforms](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).

### Step 4: Skip anything that charges you to withdraw

A real panel does not charge a fee to release a balance. That script is the same in both countries. See [side hustle scams](/side-hustles/side-hustle-scams-canada/).

## What mistakes do Canadians make with US app lists?

- **Installing first and reading second.** The country check takes one minute on the help page and saves the ID photos.
- **Assuming a sister brand has the same countries.** InboxDollars and Swagbucks do not.
- **Using the US Spark or US Rakuten signup** because the icon looks identical.
- **Treating a closed site as a back door.** MTurk's help page is about final payments and refunds, not new tasks.
- **Importing US pay screenshots.** Even on a shared brand, Canadian fee schedules and incentives differ. Use the Canadian page's number.

### Illustrative example

Assumed choice, not a pay comparison. You see a US article about a receipt app. You skip Ibotta because its help centre does not support a foreign phone number for withdrawal. You use Caddle, which pays by Interac once credits reach $20, or Rakuten.ca, which pays CAD once confirmed cash back reaches $5.01. The dollars you receive are Canadian, on a published schedule, instead of a US balance you cannot withdraw. No rate in this paragraph is a promised return. The minimums are the companies' rules, from the [cashback guide](/side-hustles/cashback-apps-canada/).

## Which tax rules apply when you do get paid in US dollars?

An app that blocks Canadians does not create a tax issue. An app that pays you in USD does. Convert the amount to Canadian dollars and report the income. Business income goes on Form T2125. The steps, the Bank of Canada rate, and Form W-8BEN are in [earning in USD from US platforms](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).
