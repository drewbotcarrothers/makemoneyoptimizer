## How do Etsy and Shopify fees compare for Canadian sellers?

Etsy charges Canadian sellers per listing and per sale, while Shopify charges a monthly plan plus card processing; every figure below was rechecked on the company's own pages on October 9, 2026. USD amounts are converted at the Bank of Canada's [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4240 CAD per USD for October 8, 2026.

| Cost | Etsy (bank account in Canada) | Shopify Basic (Canada) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly fee | None | $49 CAD monthly, or $37 CAD a month billed yearly ([Shopify pricing](https://www.shopify.com/ca/pricing)) |
| Trial | Not applicable | 3 days free, then $1 a month for 3 months on most plans |
| Set-up fee | One-time, varies by location ([Etsy fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115014483627-What-are-the-Fees-and-Taxes-for-Selling-on-Etsy)) | None |
| Listing fee | 0.20 USD (about $0.28 CAD) per listing, renews every 4 months | None |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of the order, including shipping and gift wrap | None with Shopify Payments; 2% if you use a third-party processor on Basic |
| Payment processing | 3% + $0.25 CAD for Canadian or U.S. orders; 4% + $0.25 CAD international ([processing table](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy)) | Online cards 2.8% + 30¢ CAD; Amex and international 3.5% + 30¢ CAD; in person 2.6%; Interac in person 15¢ |
| Other platform fees | 0.50% Canada [regulatory operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) | Apps and themes you choose |
| Advertising | Offsite Ads: 15% of an attributed order if you made under 10,000 USD in the past 365 days, 12% at or above; capped at 100 USD per order | Whatever you spend on your own ads |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% if listing currency differs from your bank's | Depends on settings |
| Traffic | Etsy's marketplace search | Only what you bring |

Etsy's processing fee is charged on the total sale including shipping and any sales tax, so the effective rate is a little higher than the headline. Shopify's Grow plan ($132 CAD monthly or $99 CAD a month yearly) lowers online card rates to 2.6% + 30¢.

## How do Etsy and Shopify differ beyond fees in Canada?

Beyond fees, Etsy and Shopify differ on who finds the buyer, who owns the customer relationship, and what you are allowed to sell. On Etsy, Etsy's search and its policies decide whether shoppers see you. On Shopify, you build the store and every visitor is one you attracted.

| Factor | Etsy | Shopify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who brings buyers | Etsy search and Etsy Ads | You, through social media, email, search, or ads |
| What fits | Handmade, vintage, craft supplies, and some digital items under Etsy's policies | Almost any legal product, including your own brand |
| Brand control | Shop page inside Etsy | Your own domain and design |
| Selling in person | Etsy's Square integration | Shopify POS on every plan |
| Work to start | Photos, listings, and shop policies | Theme, domain, payments, shipping settings, and marketing |

## How do Etsy and Shopify fees compare on one Canadian order?

On a small order with no ads, Etsy's per-sale fees are higher than Shopify's card fee, but Shopify only wins once enough orders cover its monthly plan. The illustrative example shows the arithmetic.

### Illustrative example

Suppose a seller in Canada sells a $40 CAD item with $10 CAD shipping to a Canadian buyer, with no Offsite Ads and no tax, to keep the arithmetic simple.

- **Etsy:** transaction fee 6.5% × $50 = $3.25. Processing 3% × $50 + $0.25 = $1.75. Regulatory fee 0.50% × $50 = $0.25. Listing fee about $0.28. Total about $5.53.
- **Shopify Basic:** card fee 2.8% × $50 + $0.30 = $1.70, plus the monthly plan.
- **Difference per order:** $5.53 − $1.70 = $3.83.
- **Orders to cover the $49 CAD monthly plan:** $49 ÷ $3.83 ≈ 12.8, so about 13 orders a month. On the $37 yearly-billed price: $37 ÷ $3.83 ≈ 9.7, so about 10.

**Inputs:** the $40 item and $10 shipping are assumed; every fee is from the pages above. The order total is used for both platforms and sales tax is left out.

### How does the break-even change with order size?

Etsy's fees collapse to one formula for a Canadian seller with no ads: 6.5% + 3% + 0.50% = 10% of the order, plus $0.25 processing and about $0.28 for the listing, so **10% × order + $0.53**. Shopify Basic's online card fee is **2.8% × order + $0.30**.

| Order total (item + shipping) | Etsy fees, no ads | Shopify Basic card fee | Etsy costs more by | Orders/month to cover $49 plan | Orders/month to cover $37 (yearly) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $20 | $2.53 | $0.86 | $1.67 | 30 | 23 |
| $50 | $5.53 | $1.70 | $3.83 | 13 | 10 |
| $100 | $10.53 | $3.10 | $7.43 | 7 | 5 |

Below those order counts Etsy is cheaper in fees; above them Shopify Basic is, before you count what it costs to bring buyers to a Shopify store.

### Illustrative example: one month of 20 orders at $50

**Assumed inputs:** 20 orders a month at $50 each ($1,000 in sales), three of them attributed to Etsy Offsite Ads.

- **Etsy, no ads:** 20 × $5.53 = **$110.60**
- **Etsy with three Offsite Ads orders:** $110.60 + 3 × (15% × $50 = $7.50) = **$133.10**
- **Shopify Basic, monthly billing:** $49 + 20 × $1.70 = **$83.00**
- **Shopify Basic, yearly billing:** $37 + 20 × $1.70 = **$71.00**

At this volume Shopify saves $27.60 to $62.10 a month in fees, but only if those 20 buyers find the store without paid ads. If you spend $40 a month on ads to get them, the monthly-billed Shopify store costs $123.00 and Etsy without Offsite Ads is cheaper.

### When does Shopify Grow beat Basic?

Grow costs $132 CAD a month ($99 billed yearly) and cuts online card rates from 2.8% to 2.6% + 30¢. The 0.2-point saving covers Grow's extra $83 a month only above **$41,500 a month** in online card sales ($83 ÷ 0.002), or $31,000 a month on yearly billing ($62 ÷ 0.002). A side-hustle store belongs on Basic.

## Who should pick Etsy or Shopify in Canada?

Pick Etsy when you need buyers to find you and Shopify when you can bring them yourself. The verdict below is a decision rule from the fee structures, not a ranking.

| If you are… | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting with no audience, selling handmade or vintage | <AffiliateLink program="etsy" label="Etsy" /> | Marketplace search; no monthly fee |
| Testing whether a product sells at all | Etsy | Pay per listing, not per month |
| Already selling 15+ orders a month with your own followers | <AffiliateLink program="shopify" label="Shopify" /> | The plan can cost less than Etsy's per-sale fees at that volume |
| Selling at markets and online | Either; Shopify POS is on every plan | Shopify's in-person card rate is 2.6% on Basic |
| Selling items Etsy's policies do not allow | Shopify | Etsy limits what fits its marketplace |
| Selling to many international buyers | Compare both | Etsy adds 4% + $0.25 CAD on international orders; Shopify adds 3.5% + 30¢ on international cards |

Some sellers run both: Etsy for discovery and Shopify for repeat customers and a mailing list. If you do, keep inventory synced so the same item does not sell twice.

## What mistakes do Canadian sellers make choosing between Etsy and Shopify?

Canadian sellers most often compare only the headline percentages, leave Etsy's Offsite Ads on without understanding them, or open a Shopify store with no plan for traffic.

- **Forgetting the listing renewals.** Each Etsy listing costs 0.20 USD and renews every four months, sold or not.
- **Ignoring Offsite Ads.** At 15% of an attributed order, one ad sale can wipe out the margin.
- **Paying for Shopify before you have buyers.** A store nobody visits costs $49 CAD a month.
- **Using a third-party processor on Shopify Basic** without counting the 2% third-party transaction fee.
- **Pricing in USD by accident.** Etsy's fixed fees are in USD and a 2.5% conversion fee applies when currencies differ.

## What could change these Etsy and Shopify numbers?

Three inputs move the result more than anything else: whether Etsy's Offsite Ads attribute your orders (15% of the order under 10,000 USD in yearly Etsy sales, 12% above, capped at 100 USD per order), what you spend to drive traffic to a Shopify store, and the exchange rate, because Etsy's listing fee is set in USD and converted at the market rate when charged. Both companies change fees with notice; the figures here were read from their pages on October 9, 2026. This is not tax or legal advice.

## Which tax rules apply to Etsy and Shopify sellers in Canada?

Sales on Etsy or Shopify are generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and platform fees are generally business expenses. Selling on both means adding both together for the GST/HST [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html).

Keep each platform's monthly fee statement; the fees are deductible, as covered in [side hustle expense deductions](/side-hustles/side-hustle-expense-deductions-canada/). Marketplaces can report seller information to the CRA, explained in [tax on reselling and gig apps](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/).
