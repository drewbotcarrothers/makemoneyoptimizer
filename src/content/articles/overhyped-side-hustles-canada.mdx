## What mistakes do people make with overhyped side hustles in Canada?

People buy the ads or the course before a single sale, count a dashboard's gross as profit, and copy a monthly total from a post that does not show its arithmetic. Another mistake is treating a platform account as property. The file you made and the mailing list you have permission to email are yours. The account is rented, and the rule page can change. YouTube's Partner Program page says meeting a number is followed by a review, not an automatic payout ([eligibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)).

Also common: starting three of these at once so none of them ships. Pick one test, write a stop date, and keep the receipts.
