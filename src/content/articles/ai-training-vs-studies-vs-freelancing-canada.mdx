<QuickAnswer>

- **Studies** such as Prolific pay a fixed reward for a task, with a published researcher floor of £6 or US$8 an hour. Canada is on Prolific's participant list if you are over 18 and verify.
- **AI training** pays project rates after an assessment. Outlier lists Canada on a coding role and shows the rate before the task. DataAnnotation's FAQ does not name Canada and gives you one starter assessment.
- **Freelancing** pays when a client accepts a gig. You set a price, you wait for the first order, and the platform fee comes off the top. Fiverr's help centre describes a 20% service fee.
- **Pick on the gate, not the headline rate.** An unpaid assessment, a screener, and a client who never replies are three different ways to earn $0 in week one.

</QuickAnswer>

AI training, paid studies, and freelancing are three ways to earn from a laptop in Canada, and they fail in different weeks. Studies need a profile and a match. AI training needs an assessment and a project that has not gone quiet. Freelancing needs a client. The right one is the gate you can stand, not the largest number on a marketing page.

Start from [how to start AI training](/side-hustles/ai-data-annotation-side-hustle-canada/), the [Prolific review](/side-hustles/prolific-review-canada/), and the [Fiverr review](/side-hustles/fiverr-review-canada/) or the [Upwork review](/side-hustles/upwork-review-canada/). Assessment tactics are [how to pass AI training assessments](/side-hustles/ai-training-assessment-tips-canada/).

## How do the three kinds of work differ in Canada?

| | Paid studies | AI training projects | Freelancing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example | Prolific, and the panels in [survey sites](/side-hustles/survey-sites-canada/) | Outlier, DataAnnotation | Fiverr, Upwork, or a direct client |
| Canada | Prolific lists Canada for participants over 18 who pass verification | Outlier's full-stack coding role lists Canada. DataAnnotation's live FAQ does not name a country | The platforms serve Canadian sellers. You still invoice in the currency the order uses |
| What you do | A fixed task. Some ask you to judge an AI answer | Rank, rewrite, or review model output on a project | A scoped deliverable for a named client |
| Gate | Profile and screeners. Many studies will not match you | DataAnnotation: one starter assessment, about an hour, no retake described. Outlier: 30–90 minutes of onboarding and a selective screen | A gig, samples, and a client who hires you |
| Pay language | Prolific's researcher floor is £6 or US$8 an hour. You are paid the fixed reward, not a wage that changes if you finish early | "Starting at" or "up to" bands. You see the task rate after you are in. DataAnnotation's FAQ does not label CAD or USD | You set the price. Fiverr's earnings page says you keep 80% of the order, so Fiverr keeps 20% |
| Who decides the hours | You accept a study or you don't | You accept a task or you don't. The project can end when the customer ends it | The client and your calendar |
| Dry spells | Normal. A quiet week is how panels work | Outlier says projects may not match your field | Normal until reviews exist |

## Who does each one suit in Canada?

Paid studies suit someone who can do a focused task in the gaps and will not budget rent against them. Prolific pays the reward the researcher set, at or above the published floor. Screen-outs pay nothing. The [Prolific review](/side-hustles/prolific-review-canada/) covers cash-out and the AI-training studies that sometimes appear there. Those are still studies with a fixed reward. They are not the assessment platforms.

AI training suits someone who can show the degree or the work history the platform asks for and who can write carefully for an hour. Outlier's FAQ says at least an associate degree, contractor status, and no visa sponsorship. DataAnnotation's FAQ asks for a bachelor's or equivalent experience on generalist work and higher bars on specialist tracks. The [assessment guide](/side-hustles/ai-training-assessment-tips-canada/) is the practical page. If the signup form does not accept Canada, that platform is closed to you today.

Freelancing suits someone who can describe an offer, send it, and revise it when the first clients are slow. A writing gig, a design gig, or a bookkeeping gig is a client relationship. [AI consulting](/side-hustles/ai-consulting-side-hustle-canada/) is the version where a local business pays you for a project. That is not the same job as ranking model outputs on someone else's dashboard. Developers comparing both should read [side hustles for software developers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-software-developers-canada/).

## What does a week look like if you mix them?

### Illustrative example

Assumed hours, not a survey. Suppose one week contains:

- Two Prolific studies at the published US$8-an-hour floor, each estimated by the researcher at 20 minutes. Reward each is US$8 × 20/60 = US$2.67. Two studies are US$5.34.
- Three accepted AI-training hours at an assumed US$20 an hour, after an assessment you already passed. That is US$60. The US$20 is not a platform's published typical rate.
- No freelance order. The gig is live and nobody buys it. That line is US$0, which is a real outcome in the first weeks on [Fiverr](/side-hustles/fiverr-review-canada/).

Gross is US$65.34. At the [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) rate of 1.4254 CAD per US dollar on October 5, 2026, that is about $93.14 CAD before PayPal conversion and before any platform fee on a freelance order that did not happen. If the AI-training dashboard is empty and the studies screen you out, the week is $0 plus the time. If a freelance order of US$100 arrives, Fiverr's 20% fee is US$20 and you receive US$80 before conversion, and that US$100 was also an assumption. None of these lines is an expected week.

## How do you choose in Canada?

### Step 1: Open the study account first if you want something this week

Prolific's country rule is published. A profile you can verify is a smaller bet than an assessment you cannot retake. Add one Canadian survey panel from [survey sites](/side-hustles/survey-sites-canada/) if you want a second inbox.

### Step 2: Take the AI assessment only when you can give it the hour

Read [how to pass the assessments](/side-hustles/ai-training-assessment-tips-canada/). Apply to the Outlier role that lists Canada if that is your background. On DataAnnotation, confirm the signup screen accepts you before you spend the only starter attempt.

### Step 3: Put a freelance offer up if you want a client, not a dashboard

One clear gig on [Fiverr](/side-hustles/fiverr-review-canada/) or [Upwork](/side-hustles/upwork-review-canada/) is enough to learn the fee and the wait. Price it so the fee still leaves you a number you would accept. The Fiverr review walks through the 20%. Do not pause the studies while you wait for the first order.

### Step 4: Record every payout in CAD

Studies, project pay, and client orders can all land in USD. One ledger beats three inboxes at tax time. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can do that once the amounts are real. A spreadsheet is enough at the start. The conversion method is [earning in USD from US platforms](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).

### Step 5: Drop the one that only produces screen-outs

After two weeks, keep the inbox that paid you for completed work and stop refreshing the one that did not. A headline rate you never received is not a reason to stay.

## What mistakes do Canadians make when they compare these?

- **Adding the top of every range into one monthly figure.** "Up to $50" and a Prolific floor and a Fiverr price are not three salaries you hold at once.
- **Quitting a job after a busy dashboard fortnight.** Outlier says the project lasts as long as the customer needs it.
- **Ignoring the freelance fee.** The price the buyer sees is not your deposit.
- **Using a VPN to reach a platform that does not list Canada.** Identity checks compare the document and the network.
- **Calling studies "freelancing."** A fixed reward is not a client. You cannot raise the rate by sending a better proposal.

## Which tax rules apply to all three in Canada?

All three are generally income you report in Canadian dollars. Business and professional income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). You are not an employee of Prolific, Outlier, or DataAnnotation on the pages those companies publish. No Canadian employer is withholding income tax, CPP, or EI unless a slip you actually receive says so. The GST/HST small-supplier test is $30,000 of worldwide taxable supplies across your activities, not a tax-free allowance. See [the $30,000 myth](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/).
