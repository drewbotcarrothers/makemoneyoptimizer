AI training, paid studies and freelancing are three ways to earn from a laptop in Canada, and they fail in different ways. Studies need a profile and a match. AI training needs a passed assessment and a project that's still running. Freelancing needs a client. The right one is the gate you can get through, not the biggest number on a marketing page. Platform pages below were checked on October 9, 2026. US-dollar and pound amounts are converted at the [Bank of Canada's](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) October 8, 2026 rates of 1.4240 and 1.8822.

## How do AI training, paid studies and freelancing compare in Canada?

Paid studies are the easiest to start but pay the least per hour. AI training can pay more, but only after a one-shot assessment and only while projects last. Freelancing has no ceiling, but the first client can take weeks, and the platform keeps a share of every order.

## What changed in the published rates and rules?

Earlier versions of this comparison said DataAnnotation didn't publish rates and that its generalist work required a bachelor's degree. Its FAQ, checked October 9, 2026, now lists starting rates by project type and describes the requirements by tier:

| DataAnnotation project type | Published starting rate | Requirement on the FAQ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General | $25–$50+ an hour | Strong writing and critical thinking |
| Multilingual | $20+ an hour | Translation and localization skills |
| Coding | $40–$150+ an hour | Programming experience |
| STEM | $40–$125+ an hour | Master's or PhD, or a bachelor's plus 10+ years of professional experience |
| Professional | $40–$125+ an hour | Licensed credentials in law, finance or medicine |

The FAQ doesn't say which currency these rates are in. If they're US dollars, US$25 is about C$35.60 and US$40 about C$56.96. Treat them as starting rates for accepted contractors on available projects, not as what a new sign-up will earn.

Fiverr's fee is unchanged at 20%. Upwork moved from a flat fee to a variable 0% to 15% fee per contract, shown before you propose. Its details are in [Fiverr vs Upwork](/side-hustles/fiverr-vs-upwork-canada/).

## Who does each one suit in Canada?

**Paid studies** suit someone who can do a focused task in short gaps and won't count on the money for bills. Prolific pays the reward the researcher set, at or above its minimum. The reward is fixed, so finishing quickly doesn't cut your pay, and taking longer doesn't raise it. Screen-outs pay nothing. The [Prolific review](/side-hustles/prolific-review-canada/) covers cash-out and the AI-related studies that sometimes appear there. Those are still studies with a fixed reward, not AI training projects.

**AI training** suits someone with the degree or work history the platform asks for, who can write carefully for an hour without rushing. Outlier's FAQ asks for at least an associate degree, with some projects needing a bachelor's, master's or PhD, and it doesn't sponsor work visas. DataAnnotation's general projects focus on writing and reasoning. Because its Starter Assessment can be taken only once, read the [assessment guide](/side-hustles/ai-training-assessment-tips-canada/) first. If a sign-up form doesn't accept Canada, that platform isn't open to you.

**Freelancing** suits someone who can describe an offer, send it, and keep improving it while the first clients are slow. Writing, design and bookkeeping gigs are client relationships. [AI consulting](/side-hustles/ai-consulting-side-hustle-canada/) is the version where a local business pays you for a project, which is different from rating model outputs on someone else's dashboard. Developers comparing both should read [side hustles for software developers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-software-developers-canada/).

## What does each pay with real numbers?

**Illustrative. The study lengths, hours, order sizes and Upwork fee below are assumptions to show how the published rates and fees work, not typical results. Conversions use 1.4240 CAD per US dollar. Verify the arithmetic for your own payouts.**

**Example 1: two Prolific studies at the minimum.** Two studies estimated at 20 minutes each, paid at the US$8-an-hour minimum, give US$8 × 20 ÷ 60 = US$2.67 each, or US$5.34 for both. That's about C$7.60. You can cash out once your approved balance reaches $6 or £6.

**Example 2: three hours of general AI training.** If you pass DataAnnotation's assessment and are paid its lowest published general rate, and that rate is in US dollars, 3 hours × US$25 = US$75, about C$106.80. If no project is available that week, it's $0.

**Example 3: a US$100 freelance order.** On Fiverr you keep 80%: US$80, about C$113.92. On Upwork, at an assumed 10% fee for that contract and 10 Connects spent on proposals (10 × US$0.15 = US$1.50), you keep US$100 − US$10 − US$1.50 = US$88.50, about C$126.02.

**Example 4: a mixed week.** Two studies (US$5.34), three AI training hours (US$75) and one Fiverr order (US$80) total US$160.34, about C$228.32. In a first week, the freelance line is often $0 because no one has ordered yet, which leaves US$80.34, about C$114.40.

None of these figures include income tax, CPP, or PayPal's currency conversion costs.

## How do you choose in Canada?

### Step 1: Open a study account first if you want something this week

Prolific's country rule is published, and verification is a smaller bet than a one-time assessment. Add one panel from [survey sites](/side-hustles/survey-sites-canada/) if you want a second source.

### Step 2: Take an AI assessment only when you can give it your full attention

Read [how to pass the assessments](/side-hustles/ai-training-assessment-tips-canada/). DataAnnotation's Starter Assessment can't be retaken, so confirm the sign-up accepts you and set aside an uninterrupted block.

### Step 3: Put up a freelance offer if you want clients, not a dashboard

One clear gig on [Fiverr](/side-hustles/fiverr-review-canada/) or [Upwork](/side-hustles/upwork-review-canada/) is enough to learn the fee and the wait. Price it so you'd still accept what's left after the fee. Keep doing studies while you wait for the first order.

### Step 4: Record every payout in CAD

Studies, project pay and client orders can all arrive in US dollars or pounds. Keep one record. <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can do that once the amounts are real. A spreadsheet is enough at the start. Conversion methods are in [earning in USD from US platforms](/side-hustles/usd-platform-income-tax-canada/).

### Step 5: Drop the one that only produces screen-outs

After two weeks, keep what paid you for completed work and stop refreshing what didn't.

## What mistakes do Canadians make when they compare these?

- **Adding the top of every range into one monthly figure.** "$25–$50+", a Prolific minimum and a Fiverr price aren't three salaries you hold at once.
- **Rushing a one-time assessment.** DataAnnotation doesn't allow a retake.
- **Quitting a job after a busy fortnight.** Projects end when the customer's work ends.
- **Ignoring the freelance fee.** The price the buyer sees isn't your payout.
- **Using a VPN to reach a platform that doesn't accept Canada.** Identity checks compare your documents and your network.
- **Calling studies "freelancing."** A fixed reward isn't a client, and you can't raise the rate with a better proposal.

## What should you know before relying on these figures?

Rates and fees are what each platform published on October 9, 2026. DataAnnotation's are starting rates without a stated currency, and none of these platforms publishes a Canadian average. Exchange rates move daily. Treat every figure as a ceiling to check against the task or order in front of you, not as expected earnings.

## Which tax rules apply to all three in Canada?

All three are generally income you report in Canadian dollars. Business income goes on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). The companies describe you as a participant or contractor, not an employee, so no Canadian employer withholds income tax, CPP or EI unless you actually receive a slip that says so. Self-employed people pay both halves of CPP on net earnings. The $30,000 GST/HST test counts all your businesses together; see [the $30,000 GST/HST myth](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/).
