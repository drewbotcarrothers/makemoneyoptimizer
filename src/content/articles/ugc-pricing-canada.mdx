## Which pricing models work for UGC in Canada?

UGC in Canada is usually sold as a base rate per video with usage add-ons, or as a bundle of videos for one product. Usage terms change the price more than the clip length does.

| Item | What it covers | Why it costs extra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base rate | One edited video, one revision, organic use on the brand's accounts for a set period | Your time and equipment |
| Paid-ad usage | Brand runs the clip as an ad | Ads reach far more people and replace a paid production |
| Longer usage period | Use beyond the base period | More value from the same clip |
| Exclusivity | You agree not to film for competitors for a period | You give up other work |
| Raw footage | Unedited clips for the brand's editor | More material for more ads |
| Hooks and variations | Two or three alternate openings | Lets the brand test ads |
| Whitelisting | Ads run through your own account | Uses your name and audience |

## How do you calculate a UGC video price in Canada?

Calculate a UGC price in Canada from your hourly rate times the hours per clip, then add percentage-based fees for each usage right the brand wants. Write the period beside every right.

### Step 1: Time one clip

Reading the brief, scripting, filming, editing, captions, and one revision.

### Step 2: Set the base rate

Hours × your hourly rate, plus a share of equipment. Include organic use for a set period, such as 3 months.

### Step 3: Add usage fees as percentages of the base

Paid ads, longer periods, and exclusivity are often priced as a percentage of the base so they scale with the clip.

### Step 4: Price variations and raw footage

Extra hooks take minutes once you are filming; raw footage takes minutes to export but gives the brand more material.

### Step 5: Set aside tax and CPP, then round

Self-employed people pay both CPP shares; see [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, average, or survey. Suppose a 30-second product video takes 2.5 hours and your target is $45 an hour, between Job Bank's median and high. Labour is 2.5 × $45 = $112.50. Add $10 as an equipment share: $122.50, rounded to a $125 base rate including 3 months of organic use.

The brand wants paid-ad usage for 3 months (an assumed 50% of base: $62.50), two extra hooks (an assumed $25 each: $50), and 3 months of category exclusivity (an assumed 30% of base: $37.50). The total is $125 + $62.50 + $50 + $37.50 = $275. These percentages are teaching assumptions you set yourself, not market figures.

## When should you raise UGC prices in Canada?

Raise UGC prices in Canada when brands rebook, when clips are running as ads, when your calendar is full, or when briefs take longer than you priced. Change the base rate for new briefs and keep current quotes as agreed.

A portfolio with clips that ran as ads supports a higher paid-usage fee. Ask brands whether a clip ran as an ad before you quote the next one.

## What UGC pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common UGC pricing mistakes in Canada are giving unlimited usage for the base rate, agreeing to exclusivity without a fee, and scripting claims you cannot back up. The last one is a legal risk, not just a pricing one.

The Competition Bureau's [influencer guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says reviews and testimonials should be based on actual experience, and its page on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) covers performance claims. If you post a brand's video on your own account, disclose the connection. Put usage, period, revisions, and payment date in a written agreement.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect UGC prices in Canada?

UGC income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Free products you keep can also have tax consequences; ask a tax professional. GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses.
