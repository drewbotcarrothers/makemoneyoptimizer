Charging for UGC videos in Canada means a base rate per finished clip plus separate fees for how the brand may use it. IF Media, a Calgary-area agency, published 2026 Canadian ranges of $150 to $300 for a beginner's 15 to 30 second video and $300 to $600 for a mid-tier creator, for organic use only, with paid-ad usage adding 20% to 75% depending on platforms and length. Marketplace data sits at the bottom of that range: Insense says UGC campaigns start at $100 per video, and Collabstr's 2026 report puts the average listed UGC package at $180. Job Bank's $35.58 median hourly wage for employed social media content specialists is the floor for the time part of your price.

## What do usage rights and add-ons cost?

Usage is where UGC differs from ordinary video work. IF Media's published percentages are of the base rate:

| Usage or add-on | IF Media guide (CAD or % of base) |
| --- | --- |
| Organic use on the brand's own feed | Included in base |
| Paid ads, 30 days, 1 platform | +20% to 35% |
| Paid ads, 30 days, multiple platforms | +35% to 50% |
| Paid ads, 90 days | +50% to 75% |
| Whitelisting / Spark Ads | +25% to 40% per 30 days |
| Buyout (unlimited use, all platforms) | +100% to 200% |
| Extra hook variation | $50 to $150 each |
| Alternate call-to-action version | $50 to $100 each |
| Raw footage | $75 to $200 |
| Extra revision round | $50 to $100 |
| Rush delivery (under 72 hours) | +25% to 50% of the total |

The same guide suggests a multiplier by client: base rate or slightly below for a local business with under 10 employees, base for a regional brand, 1.5× for a national Canadian brand, and 1.75× to 2× for a US brand targeting Canada. It says French-fluent creators can charge 20% to 30% more for bilingual deliverables.

## How do you calculate a UGC video price?

Price the clip from your hours, then add a percentage for each usage right, with a period beside every right.

```text
Base        = (brief + script + film + edit + 1 revision hours) × hourly rate
              + equipment share
Usage fee   = base × usage %   (per right, per period, per platform)
Total       = base + Σ usage fees + add-ons
Rush        = total × 25% to 50%   (if under 72 hours)
Deposit     = 50% of total before filming
Check       = total ÷ hours  ≥  your hourly target
```

**Set the hourly target above Job Bank's median.** A target of $45 to $60 an hour sits between the $35.58 median and the $57.44 high for employed specialists. **Time a real clip,** including reading the brief, a reshoot and captions; first clips usually take longer than you think.

## What do worked UGC quotes look like?

**Illustrative.** Hours, hourly targets and the percentages chosen inside IF Media's ranges are my assumptions. GST/HST lines apply only once you're registered.

### Quote 1: beginner, one 30-second video for a Calgary café, organic only

- 3 hours × $45 = $135, plus a $15 equipment share = **$150**, the bottom of IF Media's beginner range.
- Local small business: no multiplier.
- With 5% GST: $150 × 1.05 = $157.50. Deposit 50%: $75.
- Hourly check: $150 ÷ 3 = $50 an hour.

### Quote 2: mid-tier, national skincare brand, one video with 30-day paid ads on TikTok and Instagram

- Base $450 (mid-tier 30–60 second range).
- Paid ads, 30 days, two platforms at 40%: $450 × 0.40 = $180.
- Two extra hooks at $75: $150.
- Total **$780**. With 13% HST (Ontario client): $780 × 1.13 = $881.40. Deposit 50%: $390.
- Hours: 5, so $780 ÷ 5 = $156 an hour. Most of the value is the usage, not the filming.

### Quote 3: three-video bundle with 90-day paid usage and raw footage

- Base $300 per video × 3 = $900, less an assumed 10% bundle discount = $810.
- Paid ads for 90 days at 60%: $810 × 0.60 = $486.
- Raw footage for all three: $150.
- Total **$1,446**. With 13% HST: $1,446 × 1.13 = $1,633.98.

### Quote 4: whitelisting for 60 days vs a buyout

- Base $450. Whitelisting / Spark Ads at 30% per 30 days × 2 periods = 60% = $270. Total **$720** for 60 days.
- If the brand asks for a buyout instead, at 150% of base: $450 × 1.50 = $675, total **$1,125**, with no renewal income later.
- IF Media's own example: a $450 video run as a TikTok Spark Ad for 60 days adds about $225 to $340, for $675 to $790.

### Same clip through a marketplace

On Insense, a $100 creator fee costs the brand $100 plus a 7% to 20% marketplace fee, $107 to $120. You receive $100. Marketplaces are useful for first portfolio clips; direct clients are where the usage fees above get paid.

## How should you write a UGC quote and usage terms?

Put the deliverables, the usage, the period, revisions and payment in one page before you film.

```text
UGC QUOTE — [Brand]   Date: [date]   Valid 14 days
Deliverables: 1 × 30–45 s vertical video, 2 extra hooks, captions burned in
Script: [brand talking points]; creator writes final wording; only claims
  based on my own use of the product
Revisions: 1 round included; extra rounds $75
Usage: organic on [brand]'s Instagram and TikTok for 30 days; paid ads on
  Meta and TikTok for 30 days from first ad spend
Exclusivity: none [or: no [category] brands for 90 days, +30%]
Price: base $450 + paid usage 40% ($180) + 2 hooks ($150) = $780
Payment: 50% ($390) to book; 50% on delivery, net 7
Renewal: usage expires [date]; renewal at the same % per 30 days
Prices before GST/HST [or: plus 13% HST, # ______]
```

## How do you raise UGC prices?

- **Raise after every few projects.** IF Media suggests raising rates after every three to five projects instead of staying at beginner pricing.
- **Ask whether clips ran as ads.** A portfolio with clips that ran as paid ads supports a higher usage fee.
- **Sell renewals.** Usage licences expire; a renewal email when one ends is a raise with no new filming.
- **Use brand-size multipliers** for national and US brands, as IF Media's guide suggests.
- **Charge for French or bilingual deliverables,** which IF Media says command 20% to 30% more.
- **Change the base for new briefs only;** keep accepted quotes as agreed. For the scripts, see [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What UGC pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common UGC pricing mistakes in Canada are giving unlimited usage for the base rate, agreeing to exclusivity without a fee, and scripting claims you can't back up. The last one is a legal risk, not just a pricing one.

The Competition Bureau's [influencer guidance](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/influencer-marketing-and-competition-act) says reviews and testimonials should be based on actual experience, and its page on [false or misleading representations](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) covers performance claims. If you post a brand's video on your own account, disclose the connection. Put usage, period, revisions and payment date in a written agreement.

Watch view-based deals too. If a deal is a flat fee plus a per-view bonus, find the sentence that caps the bonus and says how views are counted; if it's missing, ask for it in writing. A CPM with no cap or floor isn't a price you can plan on.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect UGC prices in Canada?

UGC income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on net earnings. Free products you keep can also have tax consequences; ask a tax professional. GST/HST registration is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your businesses. Then charge the rate for the client's province, such as 5% GST in Alberta or 13% HST in Ontario. How much to hold back is in [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/), and whether quarterly payments start is in [CRA tax instalments](/side-hustles/cra-tax-instalments-side-hustle-canada/).
