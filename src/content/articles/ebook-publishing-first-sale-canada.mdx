The first sale is one file and one buyer who is not you. A launch with fake blurbs is a later embarrassment. ## What ebook should you sell first in Canada?

The first ebook to sell in Canada is a short file about a process you can demonstrate, with a date on the draft and a sentence that results vary if the topic is money. A checklist for a local service, or a plain walkthrough of a tool you use, is a product. A pasted chapter is not.

The Canadian Intellectual Property Office explains [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright). Write original text. A sales page is not a registration. This is not legal advice.

An ISBN is a product identifier. Library and Archives Canada's [apply page](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/apply.html) says eligible Canadian publishers create an account, and that new-account review may take up to about two months when volume is high. You can sell a PDF directly while you read that page. The identifier does not finish a thin file.

## Where do Canadians find a first ebook buyer?

Canadians find a first ebook buyer among people who asked for the explanation, and through one checkout whose fee they understand. A launch thread to strangers, before a reader has finished the draft, is a weak start.

Ask one person who has the problem to read for broken steps. They are not a blurb you will fake. If they agree you may say they read a draft, say that, and do not upgrade it into a result.

Put the file on one checkout. [Gumroad's pricing page](https://gumroad.com/pricing) is one place to read a cut. This guide does not restate it. Confirm the live page before you call a price profit.

If you email, follow ISED's [getting consent](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) page: consent, identification, and an unsubscribe path acted on within 10 business days. This is not legal advice.

Legal deposit is separate from the sale. Section 10 of the [Library and Archives of Canada Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-7.7/section-10.html) sets a duty, subject to the regulations. LAC's [legal deposit](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/legal-deposit/about.html) page explains who it treats as a Canadian publisher. Read both. This is not legal advice.

## What should a first ebook sales page say?

A first ebook sales page in Canada should say who the file is for, what it covers, the price, the date of the draft, and what it does not promise.

**Example sales page.** This is a generic example. It is not a page that sold.

> A short guide to closing one month of records for a solo service business in Canada. It explains a checklist. It does not file your return, and it does not promise a refund or a profit. Figures in the examples are labelled as examples. The file is dated. If a rule I cited changes, I will say what changed in the next version. The checkout fee is the one on the vendor's page, not a number I hid. Downloaded files are hard to "return." A file that will not open is replaced.

Write that refund sentence before the first buyer.

## How do you prove an ebook is worth buying without fake reviews?

You prove an ebook in Canada by letting a reader finish it and by linking the official page for every rule you state. A star rating you wrote, a buyer count you guessed, and a testimonial you drafted for a friend are not proof.

Delete invented stories. If a number is not in a source the reader can open, take it out. A plain cover you have the right to use is enough. A photo you do not own is not a better cover.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first ebook sale look like?

A 14-day plan for a first ebook sale in Canada should produce a short honest draft, one reader, a sales page, and a checkout whose fee you have read. A sale in this window is not guaranteed. A second book waits until this file has survived a reader.

### Days 1 and 2: Name the reader

Write who the file is for and what it will not promise. Outline the steps. Mark every number that needs a source.

### Days 3 to 5: Draft and cut

Write the short version. Delete copied passages and invented stories. Read the ISBN and legal-deposit pages. Decide whether this file goes on sale while an account is in review.

### Days 6 to 9: One reader, then a page

Send the draft to one person with the problem. Fix the step they could not follow. Publish a sales page with a date. If you email anyone else, follow ISED's consent page.

### Days 10 to 14: Sell the file you can stand behind, or wait

If the draft is honest and short enough, put the price on the checkout. If it is not, do not announce it. Do not buy ads for a file that still has an unsourced number.

## What mistakes block a first ebook sale in Canada?

Mistakes that block a first ebook sale in Canada are fake testimonials, waiting forever for an ISBN, and telling buyers a download is tax-free. A smaller, truer file is the product.

GI-065, dated August 2014, lists electronic and digital books among goods that do not qualify for the point-of-sale book rebate it describes. Its rate table is not today's HST table. Use the charge-and-collect page for rates.

A book that guarantees income will fail the week a reader asks for the source. Ads do not repair that. A purchased email list skips anti-spam consent.

## Which tax rules apply to a first ebook sale in Canada?

Ebook sales are self-employment income, including a shop payout, reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table. The [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) is the further reading when a platform sits in the middle.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

One small file does not by itself cross the small-supplier line. If you are not registered, do not charge GST/HST. Once registered, confirm the rate with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Quebec charges GST and QST. Do not apply a printed-book rebate story to a download.
