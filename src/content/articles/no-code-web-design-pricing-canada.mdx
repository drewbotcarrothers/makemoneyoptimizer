## Which pricing models work for no-code web design?

Quote a fixed price by pages and features with capped revisions, take a deposit, bill changes hourly, and sell a care plan.

| Model | How it works | Published example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed package by pages | Set pages, features and revision rounds | TIBY: 1–2 pages from $499, up to 5 from $799 |
| Template build vs custom | Lower price on a builder template | Goodwin: $2,200 template vs $4,200 custom |
| Design days | Fixed price for a few days of updates | Goodwin: from $1,800 |
| Care plan | Monthly fee for upkeep and set change time | Rekode: $175 with 1 hour of changes |
| Hourly | Changes outside scope | Your rate, quoted in writing first (FLXW's approach) |

Write who supplies copy and photos, the revision rounds, the deposit, what happens if content is late, and that the client owns the builder account and domain.

## How do you calculate a no-code web design rate?

Build an hourly rate, estimate hours per page and feature, and turn the hours into a fixed price.

```text
Hourly rate   = (target pay + CPP + software, fonts, stock per hour)
                ÷ share of hours billable
Project price = (discovery + build per page + features + revisions + launch)
                × hourly rate
Care plan     = monthly hours × hourly rate
```

**Illustrative rate build.** Replace each input with your own:

| Input | Assumed value |
| --- | --- |
| Target pay | $45/hour (between the $33.65 median and $57.69 high) |
| CPP at 11.9% | $5.36 |
| Software, fonts, stock photos per hour | $3.00 |
| Share of hours billable | 70% |
| **Rate** | ($45 + $5.36 + $3) ÷ 0.70 = $76.23, **round to $75** |

**Illustrative price sheet** at $75 an hour, before GST/HST:

| Service | Assumed hours | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-page site from a template | 8 h | $600 |
| Five-page small-business site, 2 revision rounds | 22 h | $1,650 |
| Extra page | 2.5 h | $190 |
| Booking or contact form integration | 2 h | $150 |
| Online store setup, up to 20 products | 10 h | $750 |
| Care plan, 1 hour of changes a month | 1 h/month | $75/month |
| Care plan, 2 hours of changes a month | 2 h/month | $150/month |
| Changes outside scope | per hour | $75 |

The five-page site's 22 hours assume 3 for discovery, 14 for design and building, 3 for revisions and 2 for launch and training. Builder plan and domain are billed to the client.

## How should you write a website quote and invoice?

List pages, features, revision rounds, content deadlines and ownership in the quote.

```text
WEBSITE QUOTE — [client]                          Date: [date]
Platform: Squarespace, on an account in your name (you pay the plan
  and domain directly)
Pages: Home, About, Services, Team, Contact (5)
Features: booking integration, contact form, Google Maps, basic SEO
  titles and descriptions, mobile check
Revisions: 2 rounds on the full site
Price: 5-page site $1,650 + booking integration $150 = $1,800
Deposit: 50% ($900) to book; 50% on launch
Content: you supply text and photos by [date]. If content is more than
  14 days late, the project pauses and launch moves.
Not included: copywriting, logo, extra pages ($190 each), changes after
  launch ($75/hour or care plan)
Prices before GST/HST [or: + HST 13%, # ____]
```

```text
INVOICE #2026-022   Date: [date]
Website project, 50% deposit .................... $900.00
HST 13% (registered only) ....................... $117.00
Total ........................................... $1,017.00
Balance of $900 + HST due on launch.
```

## How do you raise no-code web design prices?

Raise prices when your calendar books out, when your portfolio covers the client's industry, or when projects run over their hours.

1. **Track quoted versus actual hours.** If five-page sites keep taking 28 hours, the price is $2,100, not $1,650.
2. **Sell care plans with every launch.** Rekode and Minimist+ build recurring revenue from $75 to $749 a month.
3. **Split template and custom.** Goodwin charges $2,200 for a template build and $4,200 for custom.
4. **Price extras.** Extra pages, stores and integrations are line items.
5. **Move toward market.** If TIBY starts at $799 and Goodwin at $2,200, a portfolio of finished sites moves you up that range.
6. **Send the notice:**

```text
Hi [Name], from [January 1] my care plan moves from $[150] to $[165]
a month, still with 2 hours of changes. Nothing else changes.
— [Your name]
```

New quotes get new prices; signed projects keep their agreed price.

## What no-code web design pricing mistakes are common?

- **Unlimited revisions.** TIBY and Rekode name one or two rounds.
- **No deposit.** Take 50% to book the slot.
- **Free copywriting.** Price it or have the client supply text by a date.
- **Holding the client's builder account.** Build in an account they own.
- **No care plan.** Changes after launch become unpaid favours.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect no-code web design prices?

No-code web design income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both halves of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) outside Quebec. GST/HST registration becomes required after you pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles.

When registered, the rate depends on the place of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) covers registration, and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to hold back from each payment.

This is not tax, legal, or accounting advice.
