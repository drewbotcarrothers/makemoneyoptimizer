## What template should you sell first in Canada?

The first Notion template to sell in Canada is one workflow you have used, aimed at one kind of buyer, with instructions that do not require a call. A client tracker, a content calendar, or a listing log is a product. A pretty cover on an empty page is not.

Name the job in one sentence: who duplicates it, what they fill in, and what they can ignore. If you have never run the workflow, you will not be able to answer the first question. Build from your own system. Copying someone else's file and changing the title is not a product you can stand behind.

Leave your real client names out. Example rows should look fake on purpose: "Sample client A," not a person you invoice.

## Where do Canadians find a first Notion template buyer?

Canadians find a first Notion template buyer among people who already do the job the file serves, in places those people asked to hear about tools, and through a checkout whose fee you understand. A stranger's inbox is a poor first shelf.

Show the file to people who share the workflow and who agreed to look: a peer group, a class, a client who asked what you use. Ask them to duplicate it and tell you where they got stuck. That is a test, not a review you can invent later.

A public listing on a checkout you control, or on a marketplace that allows digital files, is the shelf. This guide does not state Gumroad, Etsy, or Notion gallery fees. Those companies publish their own pricing, and it changes. Confirm the live page before you subtract a percentage. Shopify's [Canada pricing](https://www.shopify.com/ca/pricing) page is the source if you want your own store. Basic is listed there at $49 CAD a month, or $37 CAD a month billed yearly, with a short trial and a $1-a-month introductory period described on the page. Confirm it. A subscription can cost more than early sales. A simpler checkout is a valid first sale.

If you email people about the template, [Canada's anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies to commercial messages. ISED's [getting consent to send email](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en/getting-consent-send-email) page says you need consent before commercial emails or texts. Express consent means they agreed. Implied consent is limited, and that page says a relationship may support it for up to two years, or six months after an inquiry. The message needs your name, a mailing address, contact details valid for at least 60 days, and an unsubscribe mechanism acted on within 10 business days at no cost. Do not scrape members of a community that forbids promotion. This is not legal advice.

## What should a first template listing say?

A first Notion template listing in Canada should say what job the file does, what the buyer must already have, how long setup takes in your own test, and how long you will answer questions. It should not say the template will make them money.

**Example listing.** This is a generic example. It is not a listing that sold.

> Client tracker for a solo service business. Duplicate the page, replace the sample rows, and follow the one-page setup note. You need a Notion account that can duplicate a template. Setup took me about 20 minutes on a fresh page. Questions about these instructions are included for 14 days. This file does not include bookkeeping, and it does not promise clients. Sample rows are fake.

State the refund rule you will actually follow. A digital file that has been duplicated is hard to "return." Write that before the first buyer, not after a dispute.

## How do you prove a template works without fake reviews?

You prove a Notion template in Canada by duplicating it into a fresh account, finishing the setup page yourself, and showing that path with sample data. A star rating you wrote, a buyer count you guessed, and a testimonial from a friend posted as a customer are not proof.

Screenshot the duplicate, not your real workspace. Circle nothing that contains a real customer's name. If a person tests it and agrees you may say they tried the setup, say that, and do not upgrade "tried the setup" into "grew their business."

The setup page is part of the product. If a stranger cannot tell which database to touch first, the file is not ready to list.

## What does a 14-day plan for a first template sale look like?

A 14-day plan for a first Notion template sale in Canada should produce one finished file, a setup test on a fresh page, a listing, and a few asks to people who do the job. A sale in this window is not guaranteed. A second template waits until the first one has survived a stranger's questions.

### Days 1 and 2: Name the job

Write who the file is for and what they will stop doing by hand. Strip real names out of your working copy. Decide the question window, such as 14 days, and the refund sentence.

### Days 3 to 5: Duplicate and instruct

Duplicate into a fresh account. Time the setup. Write the one-page note from that timing. If you get stuck, the buyer will too. Fix the file before you write the listing.

### Days 6 to 9: List it and tell the right people

Publish on a checkout whose fee you have read. Send the listing to people who agreed to hear about it, or post where the community allows a tool share. If you email, follow ISED's consent page. Do not invent a launch count.

### Days 10 to 14: Support the buyer or fix the setup

If someone buys, answer inside the window you wrote, and note which step confused them. If nobody buys, improve the setup page and show it again to people who do the job. Do not cut the price in public every day. One change, then wait.

Mistakes that block a first Notion template sale in Canada are selling a system you have never used, hiding the setup work, and decorating the listing with buyer counts you do not have. The file has to work when a stranger duplicates it.

## Which tax rules apply to a first Notion template sale in Canada?

Notion template income in Canada is self-employment income, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST on your taxable supplies follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep payout reports and refund notes. Report the income on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Template sales are not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, charge the rate for the province where the supply is made. Some provinces use HST. Others add PST on top of GST. Confirm Quebec and every other province on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside.

Digital products add a layer the small-supplier test does not erase. The CRA's [digital-economy overview](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) describes GST/HST rules, in effect since 1 July 2021, for certain non-resident suppliers and distribution platform operators. The [cross-border digital products](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy-gsthst/charge-collect/cross-border.html) page explains how collection can fall on the supplier or the platform. A checkout tax line is not a substitute for your own test or Quebec's QST.
