For a Canadian selling ebooks, printables, templates, or courses, the cheapest of the three depends on your monthly sales. Gumroad has no monthly fee and takes 10% + $0.50 per sale through your own links (30% when its Discover marketplace finds the buyer), and it has acted as merchant of record for sales tax since January 1, 2025. Payhip's free plan takes 5% per sale, with paid plans at $29 a month plus 2% or $99 a month with no transaction fee, and PayPal or Stripe fees on top. Teachable is built for courses and starts at $39 a month (or $29 a month billed yearly) with a 7.5% transaction fee on Starter, dropping to 0% on Builder at $89 a month. Pick Gumroad or Payhip's free plan to test a first product, Payhip for low fees as sales grow, and Teachable when courses are the main product.

Gumroad charges only per sale, Payhip combines a monthly plan with a falling transaction fee, and Teachable charges a monthly plan with a 7.5% fee only on its entry plan. Figures are from each company's pricing page on October 3, 2026. ## What does "merchant of record" mean for a Canadian digital seller?

A merchant of record is the seller of record to the buyer, so it handles sales tax collection and remittance on those sales. Gumroad says it took on that role on January 1, 2025, which means it manages sales tax on sales where it has obligations. That is about the tax charged to buyers; you still report your own income to the CRA.

## How much do you keep each month on each platform?

At low sales, the no-monthly-fee options keep more; as sales grow, flat plans start to win. The labelled example shows where the lines cross for one assumed month.

### Illustrative example

Assume 30 sales of a $20 product in one month, so $600 in sales, all through your own links. The product price and volume are assumptions, and Payhip and Teachable processing fees are left out because they depend on the processor.

- **Gumroad (direct):** 10% × $600 = $60, plus 30 × $0.50 = $15, so $75 in fees and you keep $525.
- **Payhip Free:** 5% × $600 = $30, so you keep $570 minus processing.
- **Payhip Plus:** $29 + 2% × $600 = $29 + $12 = $41, so you keep $559 minus processing.
- **Payhip Pro:** $99, so you keep $501 minus processing.
- **Teachable Starter (monthly):** $39 + 7.5% × $600 = $39 + $45 = $84, so you keep $516 minus processing.
- **Teachable Builder (monthly):** $89, so you keep $511 minus processing.

At this volume Payhip's free plan keeps the most before processing. Payhip Plus beats Free once 3% of monthly sales is more than $29, which is about $967 a month in sales; Pro beats Plus once 2% of sales is more than the $70 difference, about $3,500 a month. Recompute with your own prices.

## What mistakes do Canadian digital sellers make choosing a platform?

Canadian digital sellers most often pay a monthly plan before they have sales, ignore processing fees, or assume someone else handles all their tax.

- **Paying monthly too early.** A $39 plan with no sales is a $39 loss.
- **Forgetting processing fees.** Payhip says PayPal and Stripe charge their standard rates on every plan.
- **Ignoring the per-sale minimum.** Gumroad's $0.50 per sale is 25% of a $2 product.
- **Assuming merchant of record covers income tax.** It covers sales tax to buyers, not your own return.
- **Using unlicensed fonts or images.** Design in a tool whose licence covers commercial use, such as <AffiliateLink program="canva" label="Canva" />, and read the terms.

## Which tax rules apply to digital product sellers in Canada?

Digital product sales are generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), converted to Canadian dollars, and platform fees are generally deductible. Whether you need to register for GST/HST depends on your worldwide taxable sales passing the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) and how sales through a merchant of record are treated; see [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and [record keeping](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/).
