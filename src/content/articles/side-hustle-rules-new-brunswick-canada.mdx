In New Brunswick, a side hustle that uses a business name registers it with Service New Brunswick within two months of starting ($112, including the Royal Gazette notice), charges 15% HST only after passing the $30,000 small-supplier test, and checks local permits for the use of a home. Employees are covered by a $15.90 minimum wage from April 1, 2026. This guide suits anyone in New Brunswick, in English or French, starting a service, selling goods, or picking up gig work.

It is part of the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) set. For depth, read [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/), [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), and [side hustles for bilingual French-English speakers](/side-hustles/side-hustles-for-bilingual-french-english-speakers-canada/).

## Do you need to register a side hustle business name in New Brunswick?

If you use one, yes. Service New Brunswick's [business names guide](https://www.pxw1.snb.ca/snb7001/b/1000/CSS-FOL-SNB-45-0003B.pdf) says registration of a business name must be done within two months of starting business, costs $112 ($100 registration plus $12 for publication in the Royal Gazette), and must be renewed every five years for $62. The federal [sole proprietorship page](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business/start/register-with-gov/register-sole-prop-partner.html) notes some sole proprietors using their own name do not need to register; confirm with Service New Brunswick if you plan to trade under your name.

### Step 1: Pick a bilingual-friendly name if you serve both markets

Your name appears on invoices and in the Royal Gazette notice, so choose one you are happy to keep for five years.

### Step 2: File the Certificate of Business Name within two months

Missing the two-month window is the most avoidable error.

### Step 3: Check for your business number

The CRA lists New Brunswick among the provinces where a BN is issued automatically on provincial registration ([When you need a BN](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html)).

## When do New Brunswick side hustlers charge HST?

Only after passing $30,000. The CRA's [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) shows New Brunswick at 15% HST. The [small supplier rule](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) means registration is required once worldwide taxable sales exceed $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive quarters. Rideshare drivers register from their first fare.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Moncton tutor bills $1,600 a month for ten months: $16,000 a year, under $30,000, so no HST registration. If they register voluntarily, a $50 session becomes $57.50 with 15% HST, which matters if your clients are families who cannot claim the tax back.

## Which permits do side hustles need in New Brunswick?

Local ones, which vary by municipality. Fredericton says it [issues permits for the development, construction and use](https://www.fredericton.ca/business-development/permits-licenses) of residential as well as commercial properties, so ask the city before clients start visiting your home. Elsewhere, search [BizPaL](https://bizpal-perle.ca/) by location and business type.

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in New Brunswick?

Not a platform-specific one. New Brunswick's [wage page](https://www.gnb.ca/en/topic/jobs-workplaces/labour-market-workforce/employment-standards/wage-pay.html) lists $15.90 an hour from April 1, 2026, for employees paid by salary, commission or piece work, adjusted with the province's consumer price index. Independent contractors on delivery apps are paid on the app's terms; [food delivery pay in Canada](/side-hustles/food-delivery-pricing-canada/) shows how to measure it after costs.

## What are the common New Brunswick side hustle mistakes?

- **Missing the two-month registration window.** Register soon after you start using the name.
- **Forgetting the five-year renewal.** It costs $62.
- **Charging HST before registering.** Only registered businesses add HST.
- **Ignoring local use permits.** Client visits to your home can change what the city expects.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in New Brunswick?

Report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net earnings, and New Brunswick income tax is calculated on the same return.
