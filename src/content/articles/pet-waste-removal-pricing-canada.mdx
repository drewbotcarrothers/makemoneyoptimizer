Pricing pet waste removal in Canada means a flat price per visit, a lower per-visit price for a longer commitment, more for each extra dog, and a separate price for the first or spring cleanup. Five Canadian scooping businesses checked on October 9, 2026 list weekly service for one dog from $13 to $30 a visit and spring cleanups from $79 to $170. The hourly result depends on how many yards sit on one street. Pet waste removal suits someone who can build a dense weekly route. It's a poor fit if every client is a separate drive.

## Which pricing models work for pet waste removal?

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly visit | Same yard, same day, one price | Daily-use yards, multiple dogs | A small-lot price copied onto a corner lot |
| Commitment plan | Lower weekly price for 12, 26 or 52 weeks, often prepaid | Filling a route through the year | Winter weeks when snow hides the yard |
| Biweekly visit | Higher per visit than weekly | One light dog | A yard that really needs weekly |
| Initial or spring cleanup | One longer visit, priced by dogs and yard size | Every new client after snowmelt | Quoting it at the weekly price |
| Per-dog and yard-size add-ons | A few dollars per extra dog or larger yard | Fair pricing across very different yards | Absorbing a third dog without repricing |

Toronto's [green bin page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/what-goes-in-my-green-bin/) accepts pet waste as organics; other cities differ, and the bag that city requires belongs in the price. The city-by-city rules are in the [start guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do you calculate a pet waste removal price?

```text
Visit floor    = (minutes on the yard + minutes to the next yard) ÷ 60 × target hourly + bags
Cleanup floor  = same formula with the cleanup minutes and more bags
Route check    = (sum of visit prices − bags) ÷ total route hours (including the drive)
Plan price     = visits × visit price, minus a small discount for a long commitment
```

**Time a real yard**, both maintained and neglected, including the gate, the walk to the cart and handwashing. **Count the walk or drive to the next gate.** Eight yards on two streets share the trip; one across the city doesn't. **Then compare with the local table.** Your floor is the minimum; the businesses above show what households in your area already pay.

## How do you raise pet waste removal prices?

- **Raise at the plan renewal or in spring**, in writing before the next billing date, and change new-client prices first.
- **Reprice the yard, not the whole route.** A new dog or a bigger fenced area is a reason to change one client's price.
- **Sell commitment plans.** A Cut Above and PooPickers both price long plans lower; that trades a dollar a visit for a full route.
- **Add frequency, not discounts.** Twice-weekly service at 10% off still raises the client's monthly bill.
- Scripts are in [how to raise your side hustle rates](/side-hustles/how-to-raise-side-hustle-rates-canada/).

## What pet waste removal pricing mistakes are common?

One price for every yard, a spring cleanup sold at the weekly rate, and a biweekly price set below weekly. Also common: no rule for locked gates, add-ons thrown in free (a bin rinse has its own water rules, in [how to start bin cleaning](/side-hustles/bin-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/)), and tax added before you're registered. If a written estimate is part of a contract signed in an Ontario home and the job is over $50, Ontario limits how far the final bill can move above it; see [door-to-door marketing](/side-hustles/door-to-door-marketing-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect pet waste removal prices?

Pet waste removal income is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and you pay both shares of [CPP](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) on the net. Add GST/HST only after you register, which is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single calendar quarter or over four consecutive calendar quarters, across all your businesses. The [myth guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/) explains why that figure isn't an income-tax cutoff.

When registered, use the rate for the province where you work from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) and the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Monthly invoices are the admin; <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> can send a repeating invoice once a spreadsheet isn't enough. [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) and the [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/) cover the forms.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
