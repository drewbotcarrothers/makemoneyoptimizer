Pricing pet waste removal in Canada means a written price per visit, set from the time that yard actually takes, with a separate number for a one-time cleanup. ## Which pricing models work for pet waste removal in Canada?

Pet waste removal in Canada is usually sold as a flat fee per visit, with weekly and biweekly schedules and a higher one-time or spring price. Hourly billing is a poor fit for a 15-minute yard, because the client wants one number and your travel has to live inside it.

| Model | How it works | Works well for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly visit | Same yard, same weekday, one price | Yards with one or more dogs used every day | A price copied from a tiny city lot onto a large corner lot |
| Biweekly visit | Every second week, often a bit more per visit than weekly | Light use, one small dog | A yard that is actually dirty enough to need weekly service |
| Monthly visit | One visit a month | Rarely, and only if you have seen the yard | Waste that has sat for weeks and now takes a cleanup, not a maintenance visit |
| Initial or spring cleanup | One longer visit to reset a neglected yard | New clients after winter, or a yard that was never on a schedule | Quoting it at the weekly price |
| Add-on | A de-ice path, a bin rinse, or a walk | Only if you already do that work and have timed it | Throwing a walk in "for free" |

Toronto's [green bin page](https://www.toronto.ca/services-payments/recycling-organics-garbage/houses/what-goes-in-my-green-bin/) includes pet waste among accepted organics. The bag and the cart are part of the visit, not an extra you spring at the door. If the city you work in wants a different bag, that bag cost belongs in the price. The city-by-city difference is in the [start guide](/side-hustles/pet-waste-removal-side-hustle-canada/).

## How do you calculate a pet waste removal price in Canada?

Calculate a pet waste removal price in Canada from the minutes on that yard, the minutes to the next yard, the bags you will use, and the pay you need per working hour. Then round to a number you can say at the door.

### Step 1: Time a real yard

Include opening the gate, the scoop, the walk to the cart, and handwashing. Do this on a maintained yard and on a neglected one. They are not the same job.

### Step 2: Count the walk between yards

A route of eight houses on two streets shares the trip. One yard across the city does not. Price the isolated yard higher or decline it.

### Step 3: Add bags and sanitizer

Divide a month of bags by the visits that month. A spring cleanup uses more bags than a weekly visit.

### Step 4: Hold back tax and CPP

Self-employed people pay both CPP shares. See the [CPP contributions page](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Step 5: Write the schedule in the price

"Weekly, $X a visit, billed monthly" is clearer than a vague "service." If you are not registered for GST/HST, write "before tax" only if you might register later, and do not add tax you are not registered to collect.

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a quote, an average, or a survey. Suppose your target is $30 for each working hour, including the walk between gates. Suppose a maintained yard takes 12 minutes and the walk to the next gate takes 3 minutes, so one stop is 15 minutes, or 0.25 hours. Labour for that stop is 0.25 × $30 = $7.50. Suppose bags and sanitizer for that visit are $1. The visit is $8.50 before you round.

A price of $8.50 is awkward at the door and leaves nothing for a dog that was out all weekend. Suppose you round the weekly visit to $15. Suppose the biweekly visit takes 20 minutes on site plus the same 3-minute walk, 23 minutes, or about 0.38 hours. Labour is 0.38 × $30 = $11.40, plus $1.50 of bags, $12.90, which you might round to $18 because the yard is dirtier.

Suppose a spring cleanup of a yard that was left all winter takes 50 minutes plus a 3-minute walk, 53 minutes, or about 0.88 hours. Labour is 0.88 × $30 = $26.40, plus $4 of bags, $30.40, which you might round to $45 because the first visit also includes finding the gate, the cart, and the spots the owner warns you about. Do not copy $15, $18, or $45. Time the yard in front of you.

## When should you raise pet waste removal prices in Canada?

Raise pet waste removal prices in Canada when the route is full, when bags cost more, or when a yard now takes longer than the price assumed. Tell recurring clients in writing before the next billing date, and change new-client prices first.

A second dog, a new fence line, or a winter that leaves a backlog is a reason to reprice that yard, not a reason to apply one percentage to every client. Offer a pause for months when snow hides the yard, instead of charging a weekly fee for a visit you cannot do.

## What pet waste removal pricing mistakes are common in Canada?

Common pet waste removal pricing mistakes in Canada are one price for every yard, a spring cleanup sold at the weekly rate, and a monthly plan that quietly becomes a cleanup every time.

- **Copying a flyer.** You do not know whether that number includes a cleanup, two dogs, or a yard next door.
- **Free add-ons.** A walk, a bin wash, or "while I'm here, the front path" is another service. Bin washing has its own water rules in [how to start bin cleaning in Canada](/side-hustles/bin-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/).
- **No cancellation rule.** A gate that is locked every Tuesday still cost you the walk. Write what happens if you cannot get in.
- **Tax added by habit.** Charge GST/HST only after you are registered.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect pet waste removal prices in Canada?

Pet waste removal income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Add GST/HST only after you register, which is required once taxable supplies pass the [$30,000 small-supplier threshold](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters, across all your side hustles. The [myth guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-30000-threshold-myth-canada/) explains why that figure is not an income-tax cutoff.

When registered, use the rate for the province where you work from the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) and confirm it with the [GST/HST calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html). Service income stays on T2125. [T2125 vs T776](/side-hustles/t2125-vs-t776-canada/) is the form choice. Short answers are in the [side hustle tax FAQ](/side-hustles/side-hustle-tax-faq-canada/).
