## Which side hustles have the most local demand in Montreal, Canada?

### Step 1: Build a July 1 moving help calendar

The [Tribunal administratif du logement](https://www.tal.gouv.qc.ca/en/news/detail?code=july-1-moving-day) says most Quebec housing leases end on June 30, so many people move then each year. That bunches demand for loading, carrying, packing and cleaning into a few days. Book clients weeks ahead, set firm start times, and add a [move-out cleaning](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada/) option. [Moving help pricing](/side-hustles/moving-help-pricing-canada/) shows how to quote by the hour and crew size. [Junk removal](/side-hustles/junk-removal-side-hustle-canada/) picks up what movers leave behind.

### Step 2: Clear snow the way Montreal allows

The City's [snow page](https://montreal.ca/en/how-to/ask-permission-to-put-snow-street) says that in most boroughs it's prohibited to put snow in the street, on the sidewalk or on public property. Some boroughs allow permits in specific cases, and a snow removal contractor can apply on a client's behalf in some of them. Rules differ by borough, so check the client's borough before you quote. Plan where snow will be piled on the client's lot. The [snow removal guide](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada/) covers gear, and the [snow removal contract checklist](/side-hustles/snow-removal-contract-checklist-canada/) lists the terms to write down.

### Step 3: Offer services in French, and in English if you can

The [Office de la protection du consommateur](https://www.opc.gouv.qc.ca/en/consumer/topic/illegal-practice/compliance-charter-french-language) says the Charter of the French language gives consumers the right to be served and informed in French in Quebec. Write your listing, quote and invoice in French, and add English if you serve both. Bilingual skills also suit [online tutoring](/side-hustles/online-tutoring-side-hustle-canada/) and [freelance translation](/side-hustles/freelance-translation-side-hustle-canada/).

## What seasons drive Montreal side hustle income?

- **November to April:** snow clearing within borough rules, and [winter pet sitting](/side-hustles/winter-pet-sitting-house-sitting-canada/).
- **May to June:** move-out cleaning, packing and spring yard work.
- **Late June to early July:** the moving day peak.
- **Summer:** [event staffing](/side-hustles/event-staffing-side-hustle-canada/) and delivery.
- **Fall:** [leaf cleanup](/side-hustles/fall-leaf-cleanup-side-hustle-canada/) and back-to-school tutoring.

## Do you need a permit for a side hustle in Montreal, Canada?

For most mobile services done at the client's home, the permit question is about the work, not your address. If you use part of your home for professional purposes, the City says you need an [occupancy permit](https://montreal.ca/en/how-to/get-occupancy-permit-commercial-industrial-or-professional-activity). Its page allows an office, artist's or artisan's studio, or personal care in part of a dwelling, with limits on floor area. It also says that section needs its own exterior entrance. Rules are applied by borough. Quebec registration with the Registraire des entreprises depends on whether you use a name other than your own; see [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real client. Suppose you and a friend offer moving help in late June. You'd publish a calendar of half-day slots for June 28 to July 2 in French and English. You'd take a deposit to hold each slot and add an optional move-out clean. In November you'd switch to snow clearing for the same clients. You'd check each client's borough first, because most don't allow snow onto the street.

## What mistakes do Montreal side hustlers make?

- **Pushing snow into the street.** Most boroughs prohibit it.
- **Overbooking moving day.** Leave travel time and a buffer between jobs.
- **English-only listings.** Clients have a right to service in French.
- **Forgetting QST.** Quebec has its own sales tax on top of GST once you register.

## How are Montreal side hustles taxed?

Quebec residents file a federal return and a Quebec return. Self-employment income goes on Form T2125 federally and on Revenu Québec's TP-80 form provincially. [Revenu Québec](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/citizens/consumption-taxes/taxable-zero-rated-or-tax-exempt-goods-and-services/gst-and-qst/other-situations/small-suppliers/) says small suppliers with $30,000 or less in taxable sales don't have to register for the GST and QST. Its [rate table](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/consumption-taxes/gsthst-and-qst/basic-rules-for-applying-the-gsthst-and-qst/tables-of-gst-and-qst-rates/) lists 5% GST and 9.975% QST. [Taxi and ride-hail drivers](https://www.revenuquebec.ca/en/businesses/sector-specific-measures/taxi-business-operators/) register from the start. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/).
