In Canada, a sole proprietor is not legally required to have a business bank account. Your side hustle income is your personal income, and you can run it through any account you own. A dedicated account still makes record-keeping, GST/HST, and tax time much simpler, and some banks require a business account for business activity. To open one as a sole proprietor, you usually need government ID and, if you trade under a registered name, your provincial business name registration. A corporation, by contrast, must have its own business account.

Whether a bank business account or a separate personal account suits you better is covered in [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/). This guide is the how-to: the documents, the fees to compare, and the setup. It sits between [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/) and [separating business and personal money](/side-hustles/separate-business-personal-money-canada/).

## Do you need a business bank account for a side hustle in Canada?

Not by law if you are a sole proprietor, but your bank may require one and a corporation always needs one. RBC's [business account guide](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/business/advice/open-a-business-account.html) (published August 3, 2026) says RBC requires even sole proprietors operating under their own name to have a business account, that other institutions may only require one if you operate under a business name, and that a corporation is legally required to have a business account.

The CRA's concern is records, not account type. Its [business records](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-records.html) page says you are required by law to keep records of all your transactions to support your income and expense claims. A separate account is the simplest way to show which deposits are business income. [Record-keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/) covers what to keep and for how long.

| Registered trade name ("Northside Dog Walking") | Usually yes, so clients can pay the business name |

## What documents do you need to open a business bank account in Canada?

Personal ID for everyone, plus business documents that depend on your structure. RBC's [checklist](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/business/advice/open-a-business-account.html) lists, for sole proprietors operating under a registered trade name, ID for the owner plus either the Trade Name Registration or a Master Business Licence (Ontario's registration document). Partnerships add a registered declaration of partnership or partnership agreement, and corporations add articles of incorporation and other proof.

For ID, the Financial Consumer Agency of Canada explains that a bank must be able to confirm your identity and that you must provide original ID, not photocopies ([FCAC](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/banking/opening-bank-account.html)). The FCAC's "right to open an account" protections apply to personal accounts; business account requirements are set by each institution, so check the bank's own list.

## How do you open a business bank account step by step?

Decide on your name, register it if required, then apply online or in a branch.

### Step 1: Decide whether you will use a business name

If clients will pay a brand name, you need to [register that name](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/) first in most provinces, because the bank will ask for the registration.

### Step 2: Compare monthly fees and transaction limits

Look at the monthly fee, included transactions, e-Transfer fees, cash and cheque deposit limits, and whether the account connects to your bookkeeping app. As one example, RBC's page lists its Digital Choice Business Account at a $6 monthly fee as of October 2026. Credit unions and online banks publish their own fee tables, so compare at least two.

### Step 3: Apply and verify your documents

RBC describes applying online and then visiting a branch to verify documents. Other banks may verify online. Bring originals.

### Step 4: Point all business money at the new account

Update your invoice payment details, platform payouts (Upwork, Etsy, Uber, and so on), and any subscriptions you pay for the business. [How to invoice clients](/side-hustles/how-to-invoice-clients-canada/) shows where payment details go.

### Step 5: Add a tax savings account

A second account for the tax you will owe keeps you from spending it. [How much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/) suggests percentages, and <AffiliateLink program="koho" label="KOHO" /> is one low-fee option some people use for a separate savings bucket.

## What mistakes do side hustlers make with business bank accounts?

Most mistakes are about mixing money after the account is open.

- **Opening the account but still taking payments in the personal account.** Update every payout source.
- **Paying personal bills from the business account.** Transfer yourself a draw instead; see [separating business and personal money](/side-hustles/separate-business-personal-money-canada/).
- **Ignoring fees.** A monthly fee can eat a big share of a small side hustle's profit. Compare before signing up.
- **Using a business name before registering it.** Banks may refuse the account, and some provinces require registration.

## How does a business bank account affect your taxes in Canada?

It does not change how much tax you pay; a sole proprietor still reports profit on Form T2125. Account fees used for the business generally fall under the "interest and bank charges" category on the CRA's [business expenses](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/business-expenses.html) list. If you run your account through bookkeeping software such as <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" />, transactions can feed straight into your income and expense reports; see [free and cheap bookkeeping tools](/side-hustles/bookkeeping-tools-side-hustle-canada/).
