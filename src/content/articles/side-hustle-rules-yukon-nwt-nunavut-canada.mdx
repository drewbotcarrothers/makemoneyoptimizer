In Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut, a side hustle under your own exact legal name needs no territorial name registration, there is no territorial sales tax, and GST (5%) applies only after the $30,000 small-supplier test. What does apply more often than in the provinces is a business licence: Whitehorse requires one for home-based businesses, and the NWT issues licences either through the territory or the local community. Minimum wages for employees are $18.51 in Yukon, $17.20 in the NWT and $20.17 in Nunavut. This guide suits anyone in the North starting a service, selling crafts, or freelancing online.

The territories are grouped here because the rules are short; the [side hustle rules by province](/side-hustles/side-hustle-rules-by-province-canada/) hub compares them with every province. For depth, read [registering a sole proprietorship](/side-hustles/register-sole-proprietorship-canada/), [how to get a CRA business number](/side-hustles/how-to-get-cra-business-number-canada/), and [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/).

## Do you need to register a side hustle business name in the territories?

Only if you use a name other than your own, and the NWT reads "own name" strictly.

- **Yukon:** you file a declaration of business name for a trade name. The [Yukon page](https://yukon.ca/en/file-declaration-business-name) lists a name reservation at $40 through the Yukon Corporate Online Registry or $60 in person, then $25 to file the declaration, valid for three years and renewable.
- **Northwest Territories:** the [NWT registry](https://www.justice.gov.nt.ca/en/business-names/) says you are only exempt if you use your own, exact legal name. "John Smith Consulting" requires registration because "Consulting" is not part of the legal name.
- **Nunavut:** anyone who uses a business name other than their own must [file a registration within 60 days](https://www.nunavutlegalregistries.ca/cr_partnerships_en.shtml) after the name is first used.

None of the three issues a CRA business number automatically on registration; the CRA lists Yukon, the Northwest Territories and Nunavut among the places where you [register for a BN separately](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html).

## Do territorial side hustlers charge sales tax?

Only GST, once registered. The CRA [rate table](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html) shows all three territories at 5% GST with 0% provincial or territorial sales tax. You register once worldwide taxable sales exceed $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). Once registered, the rate follows the CRA's place-of-supply rules, so an online freelancer in Whitehorse billing a client in an HST province may have to charge that province's HST rather than 5% GST.

### Illustrative example

A teaching sketch with assumed numbers. A Yellowknife web designer bills $2,000 a month, all to clients in the NWT: $24,000 a year, under $30,000, so no GST registration. At $2,800 a month ($33,600) registration is required, and a $1,000 local project becomes $1,050 with 5% GST.

## Which business licences do side hustles need in the territories?

Licensing is more common in the North than in many provinces.

### Step 1: Whitehorse and other Yukon municipalities

Whitehorse says anyone [conducting any kind of endeavour for profit or gain](https://www.whitehorse.ca/business-in-whitehorse/doing-business/business-license/) within the city needs a business licence, including home-based businesses, which also need a home-based business development permit. Garage sales, temporary fundraising and registered charities are exempt.

### Step 2: NWT communities and the territory

The GNWT [business licensing page](https://gnwt.ca/business-and-economy/business-licences-taxes-and-regulations/business-licensing) says the territory issues licences to businesses outside community boundaries and in communities that do not issue their own; all other communities issue their own. Applicants fill out an application, pay a fee and provide proof of Workers' Safety and Compensation Commission coverage.

### Step 3: Nunavut communities

Ask your hamlet or city office first, then the Government of Nunavut, before you start selling to the public.

## Is there a minimum wage for gig app workers in the territories?

No platform-specific rule. The territories set employee minimum wages: [$18.51 in Yukon](https://yukon.ca/en/doing-business/employer-responsibilities/find-minimum-wage-yukon) from April 1, 2026, [$17.20 in the NWT](https://www.gov.nt.ca/en/newsroom/nwt-minimum-wage-set-increase-sept-1-2026) from September 1, 2026, and [$20.17 in Nunavut](https://www.gov.nu.ca/en/newsroom/nunavuts-minimum-wage-increases-september-1-2017-hour-2026-06-30) from September 1, 2026, the highest in Canada. These apply to employees, not independent contractors.

## What are the common side hustle mistakes in the territories?

- **Adding "Services" to your name and skipping registration.** In the NWT that requires registration.
- **Missing the Whitehorse licence.** Home-based businesses need one.
- **Assuming 5% applies to every client.** Once registered, check the place-of-supply rules for out-of-territory clients.
- **Forgetting WSCC proof in the NWT.** It is part of the licence application.

## Which tax rules apply to side hustles in the territories?

Report profit on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) with your T1, pay both halves of CPP on net self-employment earnings, and territorial income tax is calculated on the same return.
