Rideshare drivers in Canada do not set fares; the app sets the fare and your share, so what you earn per hour depends on trips per hour, tips, and what your car costs per kilometre. Two provinces set floors: British Columbia requires $21.89 per hour of engaged time plus at least $0.45 per kilometre for ride-hail from June 1, 2026, and Ontario requires at least the general minimum wage set out in the Employment Standards Act, 2000 ($17.95 from October 1, 2026), determined for each work assignment performed or on a pay period basis ([Ontario](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers)). Waiting for a ping is not covered, and rideshare drivers must handle GST/HST from the first fare. This guide suits drivers who want a net hourly number they can trust.

## How does rideshare pay work in Canada?

Rideshare pay in Canada is a per-trip amount set by the platform from time and distance, plus surge or promotions, plus tips. Provincial rules in BC and Ontario add a minimum for time on trips.

| Pay or rule | Figure | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BC ride-hail minimum | $21.89 per hour of engaged time from June 1, 2026, plus at least $0.45/km while operating a motor vehicle | [BC minimum wage page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/wages/minimum-wage) |
| BC tips | Not included in the minimum wage calculation; 100% of tips to the worker | [BC overview (PDF)](https://engage.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/121/2026/07/Overview-of-Minimum-Standards-and-Protections-for-App-based-Workers.pdf) |
| Ontario platform work rule | General minimum wage under the ESA ($17.95/h from Oct 1, 2026), determined for each work assignment performed or on a pay period basis, tips and other gratuities excluded | [Ontario DPWRA page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/rights-and-protections-digital-platform-workers), [Ontario minimum wage](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage) |
| Employed taxi drivers (benchmark) | $15.00 low, $19.00 median, $30.00 high per hour, Canada | [Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/marketreport/wages-occupation/10557/ca), 2023–2024, updated Nov 19, 2025 |
| GST/HST | Commercial ride-sharing drivers must register even as small suppliers | [CRA taxi and ride-sharing page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) |

In BC, engaged time runs from accepting a trip to completing or cancelling it. Ontario's page says a work assignment generally starts when you accept it. Rules differ by province; confirm where you drive. This guide does not publish average rideshare earnings because no platform publishes a Canadian average we could verify.

## What do rideshare drivers earn per hour after costs in Canada?

What a rideshare driver in Canada keeps per hour is trip earnings plus tips, minus vehicle costs and any GST/HST you owe, divided by all logged-in hours including waiting.

### Step 1: Record trip earnings and tips

From the app's weekly statement, noting any tax amounts shown separately.

### Step 2: Record kilometres

All kilometres while logged in, plus the drive to your starting area. The [vehicle expenses guide](/side-hustles/vehicle-expenses-delivery-rideshare-canada/) covers the logbook and the business-use split.

### Step 3: Set your cost per kilometre

Fuel, maintenance, tires, the rideshare insurance difference, and depreciation, from your own records. This guide does not supply a figure.

### Step 4: Separate GST/HST

If GST/HST is collected on your fares, that money is owed to the CRA. Check how your platform shows it on your statement before treating it as pay.

### Step 5: Divide by logged-in hours, then hold back income tax and CPP

Self-employed drivers pay both CPP shares; see [CPP contributions](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) and the [tax set-aside guide](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch with assumed numbers, not a forecast, average, or survey. Suppose a 3-hour Friday evening shift has 6 trips. Suppose trip earnings before tax total $90 and tips total $12, so $102. Suppose you drove 110 km and your own cost is $0.30 per kilometre: 110 × $0.30 = $33.

Net before income tax is $102 − $33 = $69, and $69 ÷ 3 = $23 per logged-in hour, or $69 ÷ 6 = $11.50 per trip.

Suppose the same shift in BC had 2 hours of engaged time and 80 engaged kilometres. The provincial floor would be 2 × $21.89 = $43.78, plus 80 × $0.45 = $36.00, for $79.78 before tips. Trip earnings of $90 exceed it, so no top-up would be owed in this sketch. The floor matters most on slow nights with short trips.

## How do you decide which rideshare trips to accept in Canada?

Decide which rideshare trips to accept in Canada with a floor that covers your target hourly pay for the pickup plus trip minutes and your cost per kilometre for pickup plus trip distance. Long pickups for short trips are where hourly pay leaks.

**Illustrative example.** Suppose your target is $22 an hour net and your cost is $0.30 per kilometre. A trip with a 5-minute, 3 km pickup and a 20-minute, 10 km ride must pay at least (25 ÷ 60) × $22 + 13 × $0.30 = $9.17 + $3.90 = $13.07.

| Trip (illustrative) | Minutes incl. pickup | Km incl. pickup | Floor at $22/h + $0.30/km | Trip pay shown | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Downtown, short pickup | 18 | 7 | $6.60 + $2.10 = $8.70 | $10.00 | Accept |
| Long pickup, short trip | 25 | 13 | $9.17 + $3.90 = $13.07 | $8.50 | Decline |
| Airport run, empty return | 70 | 60 | $25.67 + $18.00 = $43.67 | $40.00 | Decline unless you can get a return trip |

## How can rideshare drivers raise earnings per hour in Canada?

Rideshare drivers in Canada raise earnings per hour by driving demand peaks, staying near busy areas, declining long pickups, and lowering cost per kilometre. You cannot set the fare, so the levers are when, where, and which trips.

Track net per hour by day and time for a month and keep the best windows. A more fuel-efficient car lowers the cost that every trip has to cover.

## What rideshare earnings mistakes are common in Canada?

Common rideshare earnings mistakes in Canada are treating GST/HST collected on fares as income, ignoring waiting time, and not logging kilometres. Each one inflates the hourly number.

For the GST/HST, insurance, and licence mistakes drivers make most, and how to fix them, see the [rideshare driving mistakes guide](/side-hustles/rideshare-driving-mistakes-canada/).

Register for GST/HST from the start. Keep a logbook and separate personal from business kilometres. Do not compare gross fares with a taxi employee wage as if they were the same thing.

## How do GST/HST and income tax affect rideshare earnings in Canada?

Rideshare income in Canada is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Unlike most side hustles, commercial ride-sharing drivers must register for GST/HST even if they are small suppliers, per the CRA's [taxi and ride-sharing page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html); the usual [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) does not exempt you.

The rate depends on the province of supply, per the CRA's [charge and collect page](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html). Input tax credits on fuel and vehicle costs can offset some of what you remit. The [GST/HST registration guide](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) and [platform income tax guide](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/) cover the details.
