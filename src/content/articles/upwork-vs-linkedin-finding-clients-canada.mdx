Upwork and LinkedIn are the two places Canadian freelancers most often look for clients, but they work in opposite ways. Upwork is a marketplace: clients post jobs, you send proposals that cost Connects, and Upwork runs the contract and payment in exchange for a freelancer service fee of 0% to 15% per contract. LinkedIn is a professional network: you can set up a free Service Page, clients can message you for free, and you agree scope and payment directly, so there is no platform fee but also no built-in payment protection. Upwork suits people who want inbound job posts and escrow-style payment; LinkedIn suits people with a professional history and a network to tap.

## How do Upwork and LinkedIn compare for Canadian freelancers?

Upwork charges per contract and per proposal; LinkedIn charges nothing for a Service Page and sells Premium for extra reach. Amounts are in USD from Upwork's pages on October 4, 2026, converted at the Bank of Canada [daily rate](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/) of 1.4246 CAD per USD for October 2, 2026; conversions are approximate.

| Factor | Upwork | LinkedIn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How clients find you | Clients post jobs; you propose, or clients invite you | Your profile, posts, and a Service Page; clients send a request for proposal or a message |
| Fee on your earnings | 0% to 15% per contract, shown before you propose and locked ([service fee](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee)) | None; you agree terms and payment outside LinkedIn ([get started](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550345)) |
| Cost to pitch | Connects at 0.15 USD (about $0.21 CAD) each; the free Basic plan may include 10 a month ([Is Upwork free?](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free)) | Free to reply to service requests and to message clients who contact you |
| Paid plan | Freelancer Plus at 19.99 USD (about $28.48 CAD) a month with 100 Connects | Premium Business (and Sales Navigator or Recruiter Lite) unlock extra Service Page features; price shown when you sign in ([LinkedIn Premium](https://premium.linkedin.com/)) |
| Payment protection | On eligible contracts ([Is Upwork free?](https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free)) | None from LinkedIn; use your own contract, deposit, and invoice |
| Bringing your own client | Direct contracts at 5% on Basic, 0% on Freelancer Plus | Not applicable |
| Currency | USD | Whatever you invoice in |

## How do you find work on Upwork versus LinkedIn?

On Upwork you choose posted jobs and pay to pitch; on LinkedIn you make yourself findable and respond to inbound requests or reach out to people you know.

- **Upwork:** you see the fee for a job before you propose. Each proposal spends Connects, so pick jobs that fit.
- **LinkedIn:** a Service Page showcases your services and can be found through LinkedIn search and search sites such as Google ([Service Pages FAQs](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a569534)). Clients send a request for proposal; you respond with a proposal and manage requests from the Service Page admin view ([get started](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550345)). LinkedIn displays your average response time if you reply within 24 hours, and your response rate if you answer at least 80% of inquiries.
- **Outreach rules:** if you email prospects outside either platform, Canada's [anti-spam legislation](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-anti-spam-legislation/en) applies to commercial electronic messages.

## How much do you keep on a project from Upwork versus LinkedIn?

On a single project, LinkedIn keeps nothing, while Upwork keeps its contract fee and the Connects you spent. The cost of LinkedIn is time and the risk of unpaid invoices.

### Illustrative example

Assume a 1,000 USD project. The project price, the Upwork fee rate, and the Connects used are assumptions.

- **Upwork at a 10% fee:** 1,000 − 100 = 900 USD (about $1,282.14 CAD). If you spent 40 Connects winning it, 40 × 0.15 = 6 USD, leaving 894 USD (about $1,273.59 CAD).
- **Upwork at 15%:** 1,000 − 150 = 850 USD, minus 6 USD in Connects = 844 USD (about $1,202.36 CAD).
- **LinkedIn lead, invoiced directly:** 1,000 USD (about $1,424.60 CAD) before your payment processor's fee and any currency conversion by your bank.

The LinkedIn figure assumes the client pays on time. Ask for a deposit and write a short contract.

## Who should pick Upwork or LinkedIn in Canada?

Many freelancers use both. This is a decision rule from the fee and work structures, not a ranking.

| If you… | Lean toward | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Have no network yet | Upwork | Inbound job posts and payment protection |
| Have years of industry experience and contacts | LinkedIn | Free Service Page and warm introductions |
| Want the platform to handle payment | Upwork | Contracts and payment run through Upwork |
| Want to keep the whole fee | LinkedIn | No platform fee |
| Sell to Canadian businesses in CAD | LinkedIn | You set the invoice currency |

## What mistakes do Canadian freelancers make on Upwork and LinkedIn?

The common mistakes are spending Connects carelessly on Upwork and working without a contract from a LinkedIn lead.

- **Proposing to every job.** Connects cost money; pick jobs that match your samples.
- **Pricing in CAD on Upwork.** Upwork prices in USD; convert your target rate first.
- **No contract from a LinkedIn lead.** LinkedIn does not protect payment; use a deposit and a written scope.
- **A blank Service Page.** Add services, an About section, and samples so requests have context.
- **Cold-emailing without consent.** Check Canada's anti-spam rules before bulk outreach.

## Which tax rules apply to Upwork and LinkedIn freelancers in Canada?

Freelance income from either source is generally business income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html) in Canadian dollars, and Upwork fees, Connects, and LinkedIn Premium used for the business are generally deductible. Platforms can report earnings to the CRA, as explained in [tax on reselling and gig apps](/side-hustles/tax-reselling-gig-apps-platform-income-canada/). For direct LinkedIn clients you send your own invoices; invoicing software such as <AffiliateLink program="freshbooks" label="FreshBooks" /> or a template both work. See [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/) for the small-supplier rules and services to clients outside Canada, and [Wealthsimple vs a bank business account](/side-hustles/wealthsimple-vs-bank-business-account-side-hustle-canada/) for where to keep client payments.
