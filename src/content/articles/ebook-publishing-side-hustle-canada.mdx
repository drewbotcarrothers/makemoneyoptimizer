Publishing an ebook suits people who can complete a draft, who will not invent a case study, and who can sell the file without waiting for a perfect cover.

The money, when an ebook pays, comes from a reader who needed that explanation. People who do this well ship a narrow guide. People who stall outline a life story, add fake quotes, and never export the PDF.

## Who does ebook publishing in Canada suit?

Ebook publishing in Canada suits people who already know a process well enough to explain it without inventing numbers. A checklist for a local service, a plain-language walkthrough of a tool you use, or a short guide for a hobby you can demonstrate is a concrete book. Ebook publishing is a poor fit if you need the money before the draft exists, if you will paste someone else's chapters, or if the book itself promises income you cannot support.

## Do you need an ISBN and legal deposit for an ebook in Canada?

An ISBN is a product identifier, and a first PDF can go on sale while you read the ISBN rules, because the identifier is not a substitute for a finished file. Library and Archives Canada's [apply page](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/apply.html) says eligible Canadian publishers create an account to request ISBNs, and that new-account review may take up to about two months when volume is high. Read that page for who qualifies. This guide does not restate a fee, because the page you need is theirs.

Legal deposit is a separate duty. Section 10 of the [Library and Archives of Canada Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/L-7.7/section-10.html) says a publisher who makes a publication available in Canada shall, subject to the regulations, provide copies to the Librarian and Archivist. Library and Archives Canada's [legal deposit](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/legal-deposit/about.html) page explains who it treats as a Canadian publisher and what it collects. Read both before you assume a small PDF is outside the program. This is not legal advice.

Copyright is explained by the Canadian Intellectual Property Office on its [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright) page. Write original text. A sales page is not a registration.

## How do you start publishing an ebook in Canada?

Starting an ebook in Canada means outlining a short how-to and exporting it, then reading the ISBN and deposit pages with the file in hand. The steps below are that sequence.

### Step 1: Name the reader and the job

One sentence: who it is for, and what they can do after the last page. If the sentence needs three audiences, you have three unfinished books. Pick one.

### Step 2: Outline eight to twelve short sections

Steps, a worked example labelled as an example, and a list of sources for any rule. Cut the memoir. Cut the quote you cannot place.

### Step 3: Write the draft in your own words

If a number is a rule, link the page that publishes it. If a number is your assumption, say so in the sentence. The Canadian Intellectual Property Office's [copyright](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright) page is the reminder not to lift someone else's chapter.

### Step 4: Ask one reader to try the steps

They are checking whether a step is missing. They are not a testimonial unless they agree to be named and you quote them accurately. Invented praise comes out.

### Step 5: Export a PDF and read it on a phone

Fix headings, page numbers, and the title. A plain cover you made is enough. A cover made from a photo you do not own is not.

### Step 6: Read the ISBN and legal-deposit pages

Open the [ISBN apply page](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/isbn/apply.html) and the [legal deposit](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/publishers/legal-deposit/about.html) page. Decide whether this file waits for an account or goes on sale while the account is in review. Do not skip the reading.

### Step 7: Put up one sales page

What the file covers, who it is for, the price, and what it does not promise. If the book is about earning money, say that results vary. Link your sources in the file itself.

### Step 8: Tell people who asked for the explanation

Ten notes to people with the problem beat a launch thread to strangers. Take the payout path you already priced, after reading the cut.

### Step 9: Correct the file when a rule changes

A tax figure, a fee, or a screen in a tool will move. Put a date on the file. When you change it, say what changed. Buyers of a how-to are buying a file you will stand behind, at least for the version you sold.

## What should the first week of ebook publishing in Canada include?

The first week of ebook publishing in Canada should produce an outline, a chunk of draft, and a decision about ISBN timing. A finished file in seven days is a bonus if the topic is truly short.

- **Day 1:** Write the reader sentence and the outline.
- **Day 2:** Draft the first half. Mark every number that needs a source.
- **Day 3:** Draft the rest. Delete invented stories.
- **Day 4:** Read the ISBN and legal-deposit pages. Note what you still do not know.
- **Day 5:** Export a rough PDF. Read it on a phone.
- **Day 6:** Send it to one reader. Ask which step broke.
- **Day 7:** Fix that step. If the draft is honest and short enough, put a price on a private link. If it is not, do not announce it.

## What ebook mistakes are common in Canada?

Common ebook mistakes in Canada are fake testimonials, copied chapters, and waiting for an ISBN before a single reader has seen the draft. The identifier does not improve a thin file. The LAC apply page is where you learn the timeline. The draft is still your job.

Telling buyers a digital book is tax-free because "books are zero-rated" misreads the rebate rules. CRA info sheet [GI-065](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-065/point-sale-rebate-on-books.html), dated August 2014, lists electronic and digital books among goods that do not qualify for the point-of-sale book rebate it describes. The rate table in that sheet is not the current HST table. Use the charge-and-collect page for rates.

A book that guarantees income will embarrass you the week a reader asks for the source. Label examples. Link the official page. Ship a smaller, truer file.

## Which tax rules apply to ebook sales in Canada?

Ebook sales in Canada are self-employment income, including a shop payout, reported on [Form T2125, Statement of Business or Professional Activities](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and self-employed people pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on the net. GST/HST for these supplies follows the [$30,000 small-supplier test](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html), not the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) rule. Nova Scotia's HST is 14% as of 1 April 2025 on the CRA [charge and collect the GST/HST](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html) table, and Quebec charges GST and QST.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.

Keep the shop reports and the draft with its date. Report the income on Form T2125 with the T1. The small-supplier test is worldwide taxable supplies, yours and your associates', of $30,000 or less in a single calendar quarter and over the last four consecutive calendar quarters. Add printables, templates, and other side-hustle revenue to the same pot. Under that test, registration is usually optional. Over it, you register on the timetable on the CRA's when-to-register page. Selling an ebook is not ride-sharing, so the first-dollar rule on the taxi page does not apply unless you also carry passengers for a platform.

Once registered, digital supplies can be taxed differently depending on who the buyer is and where the supply is made. Confirm the rate on the charge-and-collect table and with the [GST/HST calculator (and rates)](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html), and set the tax aside. Do not apply a printed-book rebate story to a download. GI-065's non-qualifying list includes electronic and digital books, and the sheet's 2014 rates are not current.

Copyright, the ISBN application, and legal deposit sit beside the invoice. Search your city for a business licence if you advertise the book as a local business. This guide does not state a licence fee.
