An Etsy shop in Canada is an online store on Etsy's marketplace where you sell items you make, design or curate, such as handmade goods, digital downloads or vintage finds. Etsy charges Canadian sellers US$0.20 per listing, then 6.5% + 3% + C$0.25 + 0.5% on each order, which comes to about 11% on a $50 sale. It suits makers and designers who can photograph well, price for fees and shipping, and keep listing new products for months.

<QuickAnswer>

- **Fees for a Canadian seller (Etsy Help, October 2026):** US$0.20 per listing, a 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + C$0.25 payment processing on Canadian and US orders (4% + C$0.25 on other international orders), and a 0.50% Regulatory Operating fee. Offsite Ads add 15%, or 12% once a shop passes US$10,000 in 12 months.
- **Real results:** Vancouver's Desiree Sandison reported CA$39,300 in 2023 from one digital-template shop (verified by Business Insider). Mississauga's Lilian Chen told the Financial Post in 2025 she averages about $1,000 a month from Etsy and markets.
- **US shipping changed:** the US de minimis exemption ended August 29, 2025. Postal parcels to the US now have duties collected even on CUSMA-qualifying goods, while couriers can still apply CUSMA (CFIB, March 2026).
- **Gear (Amazon.ca, October 9, 2026):** label printers run from the Rongta Bluetooth 4x6 at $53.99 to the Rollo USB at $293.99.
- **Tax:** report on Form T2125, pay 11.9% CPP on net earnings above $3,500, and register for GST/HST after $30,000 in taxable supplies.

## How does an Etsy shop make money for Canadians?

An Etsy shop makes money from the margin between what buyers pay (item plus shipping) and what you spend on materials, packaging, postage and Etsy's fees. Etsy deposits your sales, minus fees, into your Canadian bank account in Canadian dollars.

Etsy's own help pages break the fees down for Canadian sellers:

| Fee | Rate for a Canadian seller | Charged on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing | US$0.20 per listing | Every listing published or renewed, plus each extra item sold from a multi-quantity listing |
| Transaction | 6.5% | Item price + shipping + gift wrap |
| Payment processing | 3% + C$0.25 (Canada and US orders); 4% + C$0.25 (other countries) | Total sale price, including shipping and any sales tax |
| Regulatory Operating | 0.50% | Item price + shipping + gift wrap |
| Offsite Ads (when a sale is attributed) | 15%, or 12% for shops past US$10,000 in any 365 days | The order, capped at US$100 |

Sources: [fee basics](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics), [payment processing](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015628847-What-are-Payment-Processing-Fees-for-Selling-on-Etsy), [Regulatory Operating fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500011073202-What-is-a-Regulatory-Operating-Fee) and [Offsite Ads](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000338367-How-Etsy-s-Offsite-Ads-Work), all read October 9, 2026. The full rules are in Etsy's [Fees & Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/).

Two things to note. The listing fee is in US dollars and gets converted, while the processing flat fee is in Canadian dollars. And a listing renews (and charges US$0.20 again) after it sells if auto-renew is on.

## What can you sell on Etsy from Canada?

Etsy allows items you make, items you design (including made by a production partner you disclose), vintage items at least 20 years old, and craft supplies. Etsy's [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity) set those categories, and the [Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers) covers accurate descriptions and shop rules. Reselling mass-produced items you didn't design isn't allowed outside the vintage and supplies categories.

That leaves four business models for a Canadian side hustle:

| Model | What you list | Upfront cost | Shipping work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Handmade | Items you make (candles, jewellery, ceramics, knits) | Materials and tools | Yes, every order |
| Digital downloads | Templates, planners, patterns, printables | A laptop and design software | None |
| Designed by you, made by a partner | Print-on-demand apparel, mugs, art prints | None per item until it sells | The partner ships |
| Vintage | Items 20+ years old from thrift stores and estate sales | Inventory | Yes |

Digital is where Sandison built her shop. Print-on-demand is covered in the [print-on-demand guide](/side-hustles/print-on-demand-side-hustle-canada/), and digital printables in the [printables guide](/side-hustles/printables-side-hustle-canada/).

## What do Etsy fees cost on a Canadian sale? (worked math)

On a typical Canadian or US order, Etsy's standard fees take about 10% to 11% of the order total before any Offsite Ads fee. The formula is **listing + 6.5% × order + (3% × order + C$0.25) + 0.5% × order**.

**Illustrative example.** The prices are assumptions. The fee rates are Etsy's published rates above, and the listing fee is converted at the Bank of Canada rate of [1.4240 CAD per USD on October 8, 2026](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/). Sales tax is left out to keep the arithmetic simple; processing is also charged on any tax collected.

| Order (item + shipping, assumed) | Listing (US$0.20) | Transaction 6.5% | Processing 3% + $0.25 | Regulatory 0.5% | Total fees | Share of order |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $15 digital download | $0.28 | $0.98 | $0.70 | $0.08 | **$2.04** | 13.6% |
| $40 + $10 shipping = $50 | $0.28 | $3.25 | $1.75 | $0.25 | **$5.53** | 11.1% |
| $90 + $15 shipping = $105 | $0.28 | $6.83 | $3.40 | $0.53 | **$11.04** | 10.5% |

If the $50 order came through an Offsite Ad, add 15% × $50 = $7.50, for $13.03 in total fees (26.1%). Price your items so an Offsite Ads sale is still profitable, or opt out while you're below US$10,000 a year.

**Illustrative example: a full margin.** A $40 candle with $10 shipping charged. Assumed costs: wax, wick, jar and fragrance $9.00, box and filler $2.00, postage $12.00. Margin = $50 − $5.53 fees − $9.00 − $2.00 − $12.00 = **$21.47**, or 43% of the order. Undercharging shipping by $5 cuts that margin by almost a quarter.

## How do you set up an Etsy shop in Canada, step by step?

Setting up takes an afternoon: create an account, choose a shop name, add listings, set up Etsy Payments with a Canadian bank account and verify your identity. The steps below add the decisions that save you money later.

### Step 1: Choose a narrow product line

Pick one product type for one buyer, such as "onboarding templates for virtual assistants" or "soy candles that smell like Canadian forests." Narrow shops are easier to describe, photograph and find in search.

### Step 2: Do the fee and shipping math before you set a price

Use the fee table above. Weigh a packed sample on a kitchen scale and price postage to Canada and the US before you list. Decide now whether you'll ship to the US through Canada Post or a courier (see the shipping section below).

### Step 3: Open the shop

Sign up at Etsy, choose CAD as your shop currency, link a Canadian bank account for deposits, and add a credit card for fees. Etsy may ask for ID verification. Read the [Seller Policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers) once.

### Step 4: Create 10 to 20 listings

Sandison's two shops had 11 and 23 products. Each listing needs several clear photos, a title buyers would search for, every tag slot filled, and a description that answers size, materials, care and processing time.

### Step 5: Write shop policies

Processing time, returns, exchanges, damaged items and custom orders. For digital files, state clearly that downloads can't be returned once accessed. Avoid any claim you can't support, which the Competition Bureau treats as [false or misleading representation](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) ("handmade" when it isn't, "organic" without proof).

### Step 6: Decide on Offsite Ads

Under US$10,000 in sales, you can opt out in Shop Manager → Settings → Offsite Ads. Keep it on only if your prices still leave a margin after a 15% fee.

### Step 7: Drive the first visits yourself

Share listings on Pinterest, Instagram or a local community group. Pinterest works well for decor, printables and wedding items; see the [Pinterest affiliate guide](/side-hustles/pinterest-affiliate-side-hustle-canada/) for how the platform drives traffic.

### Step 8: Review stats every two weeks

In Shop Manager → Stats, find listings with views but no sales (fix photos and price) and listings with no views (fix titles and tags).

## What listing templates and workflows should you copy?

Treat each listing as a small sales page: a searchable title, clear photos, honest details and a shipping promise you can keep. Copy and edit these.

### Listing title template

```text
[Main product phrase buyers search] | [Key feature] | [Use or recipient] | [Size/variant]
Example: Soy Candle Gift Set | Pine & Cedar Scent | Cottage Housewarming Gift | 3 x 4 oz Tins
Example: Virtual Assistant Onboarding Template Bundle | Canva | Client Intake Form, Welcome Packet
```

### Description template

```text
WHAT YOU GET: [item, size, quantity, what's included]
MATERIALS: [list]. Made by me in [city, province].
HOW TO USE / CARE: [2-3 lines]
PROCESSING TIME: Ships in [X] business days from [city]. Tracking included.
SHIPPING TO THE US: [duties prepaid at checkout / shipped by courier] - see shop policies.
PERSONALIZATION: [how to request, deadline]
QUESTIONS? Message me before ordering if you need a custom size.
```

### Photo checklist

- One clean main photo on a plain background, in daylight from a window
- Scale shot (in a hand, on a table) so buyers understand size
- Detail shot of texture or finish
- Lifestyle shot showing the item in use
- For digital files, a mock-up of the finished design on a screen or printed page

### Order-fulfilment workflow

1. Order notification → confirm personalization within 24 hours
2. Make or pick the item; check it against the listing photos
3. Pack with a thank-you card; weigh the sealed parcel
4. Buy the label (Etsy, Canada Post or a consolidator) and print it
5. Mark as shipped with tracking the same day
6. Log the sale, materials and postage in your bookkeeping sheet

### Pricing formula

`Price = (materials + packaging + your hourly rate × hours) × 2 for wholesale headroom, then check that price − 11% fees − postage still leaves a margin`

The formula and examples are in [how to price on Etsy](/side-hustles/etsy-shop-pricing-canada/).

## How does shipping from Canada to the US work for Etsy sellers in 2026?

Shipping to the US got harder after August 29, 2025, when the US ended its de minimis exemption for parcels under US$800. CFIB reports that parcels sent through Canada Post now have US duties collected even when the goods qualify under CUSMA, while commercial couriers such as UPS, FedEx and DHL can still claim the CUSMA exemption ([CFIB, March 2, 2026](https://www.cfib-fcei.ca/en/tools-resources/us-de-minimis-exemption-cusma)).

What that means for a small Etsy shop:

- **Canadian-made goods** may qualify for CUSMA duty-free treatment, but only through a courier or a consolidator whose network applies it. Keep a CUSMA statement on the commercial invoice.
- **Goods made with imported components** may face US duties regardless of carrier. Check the HS code with a customs broker.
- **Consolidators** like Chit Chats and Stallion Express move parcels across the border in bulk, and duty treatment depends on the network they use for final delivery (CFIB).
- **Prices and policies** need to say who pays duties. Buyers who get a surprise bill at the door leave bad reviews.
- **More tariffs are possible.** CBC reported on August 18, 2026 that a new round of US tariffs on Canadian goods was set to take effect within days unless a deal was reached, and quoted Calgary jewellery seller Cindy Baldassi, whose Etsy customers were about three-quarters American, saying she had shut down US shipping and was raising prices ([CBC News](https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/tariff-deadline-small-businesses-brace-9.7310021)).

Digital downloads avoid all of this, which is one reason digital shops suit Canadian sellers. Domestic orders are another: Lilian Chen told the *Financial Post* her American customers dropped from about 10 a month to one or two in 2025.

**Lilian Chen (Mississauga, Ontario): pins, stationery and apparel.** Chen started Duluduludesign in 2019 selling outsourced enamel pins and stickers, then added cardigans, bags and clocks in 2022. The common thread is product focus, bundles or higher-margin items, and a plan for where customers come from.

## Which tax rules apply to an Etsy shop in Canada?

Etsy income is business income in Canada. Report it on Form T2125, pay both halves of CPP on your net profit, and register for GST/HST once your taxable supplies pass $30,000.

- **Income tax:** report gross sales and subtract Etsy fees, materials, packaging, postage, software and a share of home-office costs on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html). Download your monthly Etsy statements (Shop Manager → Finances) for your records.
- **CPP:** self-employed people pay the full **11.9%** on net self-employment earnings between $3,500 and the 2026 ceiling of $74,600 ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html)). Quebec residents pay into the QPP.
- **GST/HST:** you're a small supplier until your worldwide taxable supplies, across all your businesses, pass **$30,000** in one calendar quarter or over four consecutive quarters ([CRA](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html)). After that you must register and charge the rate for where the buyer receives the item: 13% HST in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia since April 1, 2025, or GST plus PST or QST in other provinces ([CRA rates](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html); [calculator](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculator.html)). The first-dollar rule in the [taxi and ride-sharing](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-specific-situations/taxi-ride-sharing-drivers.html) guidance doesn't apply to Etsy sellers.
- **Platform tax collection:** the CRA's [digital-economy rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/digital-economy.html) put GST/HST obligations on some marketplace platforms. A platform collecting tax at checkout doesn't settle your own registration question.
- **Inventory:** if you hold materials or vintage stock at year end, track opening and closing inventory so you only deduct the cost of goods you sold.

This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice. For set-aside amounts, see [how much tax to set aside for a side hustle](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).
