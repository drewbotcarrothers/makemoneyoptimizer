## How should you choose reselling tools in Canada?

Choose reselling tools in Canada by the platforms you sell on, what you ship, and how many items you list per week. Weight and measurements drive shipping labels and size claims, so a scale and tape come before anything fancy.

| Criterion | What to check | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform rules | Label weight limits and how upgrades work | [Poshmark Canada](https://poshmark.ca/posh_guide/how_to_get_overweight_shipping_label) says its printed label weight is 0.75 kg, you can ship up to 2.5 kg without extra fees, and heavier packages need a label upgrade deducted from earnings |
| What you sell | Clothing, books, electronics, or bulky items | Bulky items push you to local pickup and a different kit |
| Accuracy | Measurements and flaw photos | The [Competition Bureau](https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/deceptive-marketing-practices/types-deceptive-marketing-practices/false-or-misleading-representations) says the Competition Act prohibits claims that are false or misleading in a material respect |
| Volume | Listings per week | A label printer saves time at volume, not at five items a month |
| Space | Where listed items live | A second room of stock usually means you bought inventory too soon |

## How can Canada Post tools help a reseller?

Canada Post says its [Solutions for Small Business](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/cpc/en/small-business.page) membership is free with no hidden fees and advertises savings of up to 32% on domestic shipments and up to 58% internationally. It describes Snap Ship as a free online shipping tool for comparing services and discounted rates, preparing multiple labels, and scheduling pickups. The "up to" figures are Canada Post's maximums, not what every parcel will save, so compare the live rate for your own package.

Marketplaces that provide their own prepaid labels, like Poshmark, use their own label rules instead. Mixing the two is where a scale earns its keep. The [Depop vs Poshmark vs Vinted vs Facebook Marketplace](/side-hustles/depop-vs-poshmark-vs-vinted-vs-facebook-marketplace-canada/) comparison covers which platforms handle shipping for you.

## Which tax rules apply to reselling tools in Canada?

Reselling as a business is self-employment income on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), and the self-employed pay the whole [Canada Pension Plan](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/publicpensions/cpp/contributions.html) contribution on net income. Supplies such as mailers, tape, and labels are business expenses, and a scale or printer under $500 is generally Class 12 under the CRA's [CCA classes](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/sole-proprietorships-partnerships/report-business-income-expenses/claiming-capital-cost-allowance/classes-depreciable-property.html), fully deductible in the year of purchase. They count when used for the business; keep receipts and record what you paid for inventory.

GST/HST registration applies once worldwide taxable supplies pass [$30,000](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) in a single quarter or over four consecutive quarters, counting all your businesses. Selling a few personal items is different from running a resale business; [record keeping for side hustles](/side-hustles/record-keeping-side-hustle-canada/) helps you show which is which. This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
