## Who should you ask first for handyman work in Canada?

Ask people who already know your work first for handyman jobs in Canada: a relative, a former coworker, a household whose lawn or walk you already maintain. A stranger’s “quick electrical” is how the first week becomes a licensed-trade problem.

Lawn and snow clients on the same street often mention a gate or a shelf. Those seasons are [lawn care](/side-hustles/lawn-care-side-hustle-canada) and [snow removal](/side-hustles/snow-removal-side-hustle-canada). Indoor clients who want a clean, not a repair, are [house cleaning](/side-hustles/house-cleaning-side-hustle-canada). Do not fold a repair into a cleaning visit without a separate written price.

## What should the first handyman offer in Canada say?

The first handyman offer in Canada should name the tasks you will do, the tasks you will not do, who buys materials, and whether the price is fixed or a capped look. A useful yes-list is furniture you have assembled, caulking, a sticking door, a shelf into a stud, a fence board, a faucet aerator, and weatherstripping. A useful no-list is panels, gas, roofs, structural changes, and anything that needs a permit you do not understand.

In Ontario, [Skilled Trades Ontario](https://www.skilledtradesontario.ca/) is the public starting point for whether a repair is a compulsory trade. This guide does not list every trade. Other provinces have their own authorities. A task that is legal to advertise in one province can be reserved in the next. Check before you print the flyer. If you open a wall and the job is bigger, stop and say what you found. Do not keep going into wiring or pipe you did not quote.

Permits for decks and many plumbing or electrical jobs are a city or technical-authority question. A magnetic sign does not replace that check. Business licences are often municipal. Search your city. This guide does not state a fee.

Evenings and Saturdays are when owners are home to point at the door that sticks. A job that needs the house empty all day is a different schedule. Say which one you are selling. Bring only the tools for the yes-list. A van full of trade equipment you are not allowed to use is a costume, not a quote.

Parts you buy before the client agrees are your risk. A bracket, a tube of caulk, or a hinge can sit in the bag if the visit is cancelled. Write “client buys materials” or “included up to $10 of hardware” on the note so a $40 special-order piece is not a surprise. Parking at the building is part of the visit. A garage that will not open is a reason to reschedule, not a reason to block a lane.

## What does an example handyman note say?

An example handyman note in Canada is generic. It is not a note that produced a job, and it is not a claim that anyone hired the sender.

**Example flyer.**

> Small household repairs on [these streets]: furniture assembly, caulking, sticking doors, and shelves into studs. I do not do electrical panels, gas, plumbing rough-in, or structural work. You get a written price before I start. Text [number]. I do not publish my home address.

**Example message.** For someone you know.

> Hello. I am taking small repair jobs on evenings this month: one shelf or one door, not a renovation. I will send a price and a no-list before I book. I am not asking for a public review. Want the note?

## How should you price the first handyman jobs in Canada?

Price the first handyman jobs in Canada as a fixed fee for a task you can see, or as a short capped diagnostic when you cannot, and say who buys materials. Travel is part of the price. This guide does not set a national hourly rate.

Factors: have you done this exact task, how long it took last time, parts, stairs, parking, and the chance the wall is not what you both thought. Efficiency should help you on a fixed price. An open hourly “I’ll see what I find” should be capped in writing, then stopped.

**Illustrative example.** Suppose a shelf into a stud takes one hour and you assume $90, including basic hardware you assume at $10. If the client buys the bracket, say so and remove that $10. Suppose the drive is 30 minutes each way. The visit is then two hours for $80 of labour in this sketch, or $40 an hour before insurance and a blade you broke. Do not copy $90. If you cannot see the problem, a diagnostic cap you assume at $60 for 45 minutes is a different product, and it ends when the cap ends. These figures are assumptions, not a quote.

## What does a 14-day plan for first handyman clients in Canada look like?

A 14-day plan for first handyman clients in Canada should produce a yes-list, a no-list, one practised task, and a few real notes. A paid job is a bonus. This plan does not promise bookings.

### Days 1 and 2: Write the lists and check the trade

Put four tasks on the yes-list only if you have finished them. Read the provincial trade authority. In Ontario that starting point is Skilled Trades Ontario. Ask an insurer whether paid handyman work is covered. This is not insurance advice.

### Days 3 to 5: Ask people who have seen the work

Send the example note to people who know you. Do not email a scraped list. Offer one task, not a renovation.

### Days 6 to 9: Do one paid job inside the note

If someone says yes, send the price, who buys parts, and the stop-line before you arrive. Photograph the finished task only with permission. If nobody replies, fix the first sentence. Do not add electrical to the flyer.

### Days 10 to 14: A second job, or a tighter map

Accept a second job only if it is on the yes-list and on streets you can reach after work. Publish a service-area listing with the same no-list. If the fortnight is quiet, that is the result. Do not buy a van.

## What should you write down before you leave the first handyman job in Canada?

Before you leave the first handyman job in Canada, write what you finished, what you refused, who bought the parts, and the time from arrival to packing up. The client’s memory and yours will not match a month later. The note is the record.

### Step 1: Restate the scope in a text

“Shelf in the stud, bracket you bought, no electrical.” Ask them to reply yes if that is what they saw. A wave from the doorway is a weak record.

### Step 2: Photograph only with permission

A picture of the finished shelf helps the next quote. A picture of their living room, shared in a group chat, is not a portfolio. Label a practice piece as practice if nobody hired you.

### Step 3: Stop when the wall changes

If you find wiring, pipe, or rot, the visit ends at the sentence you already wrote. Offer a name of a licensed trade if you know one. Do not become the person who “just tried.”

**Example job note.** This blank is not a real invoice.

> Date and cross streets. Task: __ . Parts, and who bought them: __ . Arrived and left: __ . Stopped because: __ . Price charged: $__ .

## What first-client mistakes are common in handyman work in Canada?

Common first-client mistakes in handyman work in Canada are advertising every trade, starting without a written price, and continuing after the job changes into licensed work. A platform profile or a car magnet does not create a certificate.

Underpricing travel, then resenting the drive, is the next mistake. So is a cash side deal that skips the note you both signed. Memory is a bad invoice. No photos of the scope means the “quick look” has no edge.
