A strong Upwork proposal for Canadian freelancers is short and specific to the job. It opens with the client's problem, shows one relevant proof, gives a short plan, asks one sharp question, and suggests a next step. Upwork's own steps for a proposal cover your terms, a cover letter, screening answers, work samples and an optional boost. Every proposal costs Connects, so a template that you tailor beats a long generic letter sent widely. This guide is for writers, VAs, designers and other freelancers sending their first Upwork proposals from Canada.

## What should an Upwork proposal include for a Canadian freelancer?

Upwork's [proposal steps](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062998-How-to-submit-a-proposal-on-Upwork) are: set your terms, estimate the timeline, and write a cover letter. In the letter, describe what you can do, ask questions, and suggest next steps such as a chat or video call in Upwork Messages. Then answer screening questions, add work samples, decide whether to boost, and proofread. Some job posts ask for an AI video interview instead of a cover letter.

## What is a good Upwork cover letter template?

Replace each bracket with specifics from the job post. Keep it under about 150 words.

### Step 1: Restate their problem in one line

"You need [the outcome from the post, in their words] by [their deadline]."

### Step 2: Give one proof

"I recently [did a closely related task] for [type of client], which [result you can show]. Sample attached."

### Step 3: Offer a short plan

"Here's how I'd approach it: [step one], [step two], [step three]. I'd deliver the first draft by [date]."

### Step 4: Ask one sharp question

"Quick question: [the one detail that changes scope, such as word count, tool access or brand guidelines]?"

### Step 5: Suggest a next step

"Happy to do a 10-minute call in Upwork Messages this week. I'm in [time zone], which overlaps with [their stated hours]."

### Illustrative example

This is a teaching sketch, not a real proposal. Suppose a client wants four blog posts a month about home renovation. Your letter would restate the need, link one renovation article you wrote, and propose a topic list for week one with the first draft by Friday. You'd ask whether they have a style guide. You'd close by offering a short call and noting your Eastern Time hours. You'd also answer both screening questions directly, with no copy-paste.

## How do Connects and boosting work on Upwork in Canada?

Upwork's [Connects page](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062898-Understanding-and-using-Connects) says the number of Connects a proposal needs varies by job, based on project size, scope and demand, and can change while the job is posted. Connects cost 0.15 USD each and are sold in bundles. They're returned if a client cancels before a contract is made, or if Upwork removes the post for breaking its terms, but not when a post simply expires. Returned Connects are credits, not cash. [Boosting](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406395531795-How-to-boost-your-proposal) lets you bid extra Connects to move your proposal up the client's list. Spend Connects on jobs that closely match your samples.

## How should Canadians set rates in an Upwork proposal?

Bids are in USD terms. Work backward from the Canadian dollars you want, after the freelancer service fee. Upwork's [service fee page](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211062538-Learn-about-the-Freelancer-Service-Fee) says the fee ranges from 0% to 15% per contract, is shown before you propose, and is fixed once the contract starts. Upwork's [Canadian tax page](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/4401838411283-How-indirect-taxes-work-for-freelancers-in-Canada) explains that it charges sales tax on its fees unless you add a GST/HST or QST number. Your rate also needs room for income tax; see [how much tax to set aside](/side-hustles/how-much-tax-set-aside-side-hustle-canada/).

## What mistakes do Canadians make with Upwork proposals?

- **Long, generic letters.** Clients skim; specifics win.
- **Asking to move off Upwork.** Upwork says [asking to be paid outside Upwork](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/40444003500435-Is-asking-to-be-paid-outside-of-Upwork-a-violation) is a violation even if the request isn't completed.
- **Skipping screening questions or answering vaguely.** They're often read first.
- **Bidding on everything.** Connects cost money; aim at your best-fit jobs.

## How is Upwork income taxed in Canada?

Upwork earnings are self-employment income reported on Form T2125. The CRA's [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/programs/about-canada-revenue-agency-cra/compliance/reporting-rules-digital-platforms.html) mean platforms report sellers' income to the CRA. Upwork fees and Connects are business expenses. See [report side hustle income](/side-hustles/report-side-hustle-income-cra-canada/), [GST/HST registration](/side-hustles/gst-hst-registration-side-hustle-canada/), and the free [side hustle tax checklist](/free-side-hustle-tax-checklist/). <AffiliateLink program="wealthsimple-tax" label="Wealthsimple Tax" /> handles T2125 at filing time.
