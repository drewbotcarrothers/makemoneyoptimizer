A mobile tire swap side hustle in Canada drives to customers' homes in October and November and swaps their winter tires, already mounted on rims, onto the car, then does the reverse in April. Canadian mobile services that publish prices charge $65 to $99.95 for an on-rim swap of four wheels. Two provincial deadlines create the rush: Quebec requires winter tires from December 1 to March 15, and B.C. requires winter tires or chains on most designated highways from October 1. Prices and rules were read on the official and company pages on October 19, 2026.

<QuickAnswer>

- **What mobile services charge (on-rim, 4 wheels):** $65 and about 45 minutes (Titan Auto Care, Ottawa); $99.95 regular, $79.95 early bird (On The Go, Ottawa, GTA and Calgary).
- **Off-rim jobs are a different trade:** On The Go lists an off-rim change (mounting and balancing) at $249.95, $199.95 early bird. That needs a tire machine and balancer.
- **Quebec:** winter tires are required December 1 to March 15 on Quebec-registered vehicles; fines are $200 to $300 ([Québec](https://www.quebec.ca/en/transports/traffic-road-safety/winter-road-safety/requirements-for-winter-tires)).
- **B.C.:** winter tires or chains are required on most designated routes from October 1 to April 30 (some end March 31), and a legal winter tire needs at least 3.5 mm of tread ([B.C.](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/driving-and-cycling/traveller-information/seasonal/winter-driving/about-winter-tires)).
- **Add-ons in published menus:** tire storage $79.95 a season, two- and three-car pricing (On The Go).
- **CAA member prices (on-rim, before tax):** $70 to $90 (CAA Manitoba), $79.95 to $99.95 (CAA North & East Ontario, CAA Niagara), $80 to $120 (CAA South Central Ontario), read October 19, 2026.

## What service terms do CAA clubs set, and which should you copy?

The CAA pages list the conditions their technicians work under, and most of them translate directly into terms for a one-person service:

- **Tires on rims and accessible.** All four clubs require tires already mounted and placed beside the car or just inside the garage (CAA Niagara and CAA South Central Ontario say this explicitly).
- **Residential driveway, clear and safe.** CAA South Central Ontario serves residential driveways only, with space on all sides and no ice, snow or debris; CAA Niagara makes the member responsible for shovelling and removing hazards.
- **Customer on site.** CAA South Central Ontario, CAA Manitoba and CAA Niagara require the member to stay for the whole service.
- **Vehicle limits.** CAA South Central Ontario services cars, SUVs and trucks up to 3/4-ton, not commercial vehicles, trailers or RVs, and asks for the locking-nut key.
- **Time window.** CAA South Central Ontario says one set can take up to 90 minutes.
- **Re-torque.** CAA South Central Ontario and CAA Manitoba recommend re-torquing wheels within 100 to 150 km after the swap.
- **Workplace bookings.** CAA Niagara requires written permission from the property owner before working on a commercial lot.
- **Payment at the end.** The clubs charge the card or take payment when the job is done and issue an invoice.

## Which provincial rules drive the fall rush?

The provincial rules that drive the fall rush are Quebec's mandatory winter tire period and B.C.'s designated-route requirement. Other provinces leave the choice to drivers, so demand there follows the first snowfall rather than a date.

**Quebec.** The province's [winter tire page](https://www.quebec.ca/en/transports/traffic-road-safety/winter-road-safety/requirements-for-winter-tires) (last updated October 5, 2026) says vehicles registered in Quebec must be winter-ready from December 1 to March 15, including rentals, with fines of $200 to $300. A compliant tire carries the mountain-and-snowflake pictogram or is studded. Studded tires are allowed from October 15 to May 1 on passenger vehicles. The page recommends waiting a few weeks after March 15 before switching back, and says winter groove depth should be at least 4.8 mm (legal minimum 1.6 mm).

**British Columbia.** B.C.'s [designated routes page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/driving-and-cycling/traveller-information/seasonal/winter-driving/winter-tire-and-chain-up-routes) says winter tires or chains are required on most routes from October 1 to April 30, ending March 31 on select routes outside mountain passes and high-snowfall areas. The [passenger vehicle page](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/driving-and-cycling/traveller-information/seasonal/winter-driving/about-winter-tires) says a legal winter tire needs at least 3.5 mm of tread and an M+S or three-peaked mountain/snowflake marking, and at least two matching winter tires on the primary drive axle.

That gives you two sales lines: "booked before December 1" in Quebec, and "legal for the Coquihalla" style highway trips in B.C.

## Illustrative example: one fall weekend of on-rim swaps

This is an illustrative example. The price anchors are sourced from the two companies above; your price, the number of bookings, travel time and costs are assumptions.

- Assumed price: $60 per car, just under Titan's $65.
- Assumed bookings: 6 cars on Saturday and 6 on Sunday = 12 cars.
- Revenue: 12 × $60 = $720.
- Assumed time: 45 minutes per car (Titan's published estimate) + 20 minutes of driving = 65 minutes. 12 × 65 = 780 minutes = 13 hours.
- Assumed costs: $5 a car in fuel = $60.
- Left before tax and insurance: $720 − $60 = $660, or $660 ÷ 13 = about $51 an hour.

Clustering bookings on one street cuts the 20 minutes of driving, which is why On The Go prices two- and three-car bookings at checkout. Ask the first customer on a street to tell a neighbour.

## What equipment and checks does a swap need?

A safe on-rim swap needs a jack rated for the vehicle, jack stands, wheel chocks, a breaker bar, a torque wrench, a tread depth gauge and a tire pressure gauge. Price these from your own receipts; buy for the heaviest vehicle you will accept. Use this checklist on every car:

```
On-rim swap checklist
[ ] Confirm the customer's tires are mounted on rims and the right size for the car
[ ] Park on level, hard ground; chock the wheels; engage the parking brake
[ ] Lift at the jack points in the owner's manual; use stands, never only the jack
[ ] Check tread depth (B.C. winter legal minimum 3.5 mm; Quebec recommends 4.8 mm)
[ ] Check for the mountain/snowflake symbol; note any damage to the customer
[ ] Tighten in a star pattern; torque to the value in the owner's manual
[ ] Set pressures to the door-jamb placard, cold
[ ] Tell the customer when to have the torque re-checked, and write it on the invoice
[ ] Bag and label the removed set if they go to storage
```

Refuse jobs that need off-rim work, have damaged studs or seized nuts, or sit on a slope. Those go to a shop.

## What does a first season look like?

A first season starts selling in early October, runs hardest from mid-October to the first snow, and books spring swaps before the last car is done.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Early October | Buy and test the gear on your own and a friend's car; time each swap |
| Mid-October | Post a price, the checklist and your service area; offer a two-car price |
| Late October to November | Run weekend routes; cluster by street |
| Quebec: before December 1 | Push the deadline in your messages |
| December | Ask every customer to pre-book an April swap |
| April | Run the spring routes; offer a reminder for next October |

## Which tax rules apply to a tire swap side hustle in Canada?

Tire swap income is self-employment income reported on [Form T2125](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t2125.html), with tools through capital cost allowance and fuel and a share of vehicle costs as expenses. GST/HST registration is required once taxable sales pass $30,000 in a calendar quarter or four consecutive calendar quarters, under the CRA's [when to register](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html) rules. Vehicle records are in the [mileage log guide](/side-hustles/mileage-log-cra-side-hustle-canada/). This is not tax, legal, or insurance advice.
