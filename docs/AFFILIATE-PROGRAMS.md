# Canada-eligible affiliate programs — Make Money Optimizer reference

Living shortlist of programs that are **usable for Canadian audiences / Canadian publishers** (or global programs that pay CA creators and sell to Canadians).

**Brand fit:** side hustles, freelancing, ecommerce, gig work, tutoring, tools & gear — practical, disclosed, no income guarantees.

**How to use**
1. Prefer **Priority A** until you have approvals + a few converting articles.
2. Join the **networks** once (Impact, PartnerStack, Awin, Rakuten Advertising, Fintel Connect) — many CA retail/finance brands live there.
3. Confirm current terms, cookie windows, and **Territory** in each dashboard before promoting (programs change).
4. Track status in `AFFILIATES.md`.

**Disclaimer:** Not legal or financial advice. Investing/fintech offers may have extra disclosure rules (e.g. Wealthsimple). Lending offers need careful compliance (FCAC / cost-of-borrowing). Always disclose material connections.
